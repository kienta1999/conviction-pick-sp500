## Per-name thesis (top 5)

**1. MU ($877.55).** The shortage is contracted, not spot: ~$150B RPO under 26 take-or-pay SCAs with ~$32B of customer commitments, HBM sold out through 2026 and the vast majority of 2027 committed, from one of only three HBM makers on earth. Category leadership is oligopoly membership plus an HBM4E co-design lock-in with Nvidia. The honest caveat: HBM share slipped 21% to 18% and CXMT presses conventional DRAM — share risk, not in-sourcing risk.

**2. NVDA ($210.96).** ~$500B Blackwell+Rubin visibility through 2026 and ~$1T of orders through 2027 sit on CUDA/NVLink lock-in for frontier training, with FY28 growth guided ~70% against a ~44% Street. The trap is real but bounded: all four top customers ship their own inference ASICs, capping share of wallet without displacing training. Earnings-quality flags (accruals, receivables) were researched as mostly hypergrowth working capital plus ecosystem financing — cash conversion bears watching.

**3. LLY ($1273.46).** The only name pairing 9/10 shortage with 9/10 irreplaceability: 60.9% U.S. incretin share, Mounjaro+Zepbound $27.7B in H1 2026 (+88%), 11% category penetration, and a patented molecule no customer can in-source. The caveat is price, not moat: only +4.6% to the mean target and a binary Q3 print on 2026-10-29 — see the EV flag below.

**4. FCX ($65.18).** A ~330kt 2026 refined-copper deficit widening toward a ~30% shortfall by 2035, 16-17-year mine lead times, and Grasberg recovering from 67% of normalised throughput on a published schedule into realised prices guided above $6.50/lb. Nobody can in-source an ore body. Caveat: pure commodity cyclicality plus single-asset concentration.

**5. SNDK ($1238.03).** The purest NAND shortage expression: all 2026 capacity pre-sold, bits on allocation beyond 2027, $93.9B of NBM minimum contracted revenue at floor pricing. Caveat: #5 share (~11.4% and slipping) in a standards-based input customers can re-source at renewal — the substitution version of the trap — and NBM ceilings cap the upside the shortage creates.

One line each for the rest: ISRG — deepest medtech moat, frozen price predates the post-print de-rating the dossier flags; VRTX — monopoly floor with a dated catalyst, no shortage; CF — real chokepoint shortage, priced at target with urea already off its peak; META — shortage score 3 by construction, it is the demand side; AME — quality backlog compounder without an explosive leg.
