### MU-4. Moat claim: only three DRAM makers exist on earth (Micron, SK hynix, Samsung)

So the claim is true for at-scale / advanced-DRAM / HBM production, but false if read as "no other DRAM maker exists anywhere." Publishable only with the "at scale / advanced" qualifier.

**Overall verdict — MU: NOT PUBLISHABLE as written.** Two fixes required before publication: (1) MU-2 must be re-dated — the "50% to two-thirds" quote is from the 17 Dec 2025 fiscal Q1 2026 call (repeated Mar 2026), not the 30 Sep 2026 fiscal Q4 call; the Q4 call's verified statement is "much tighter in fiscal 2027 and 2028 than in 2026." MU-1 is confirmed with its HBM-vs-total-output scope distinction noted above.
