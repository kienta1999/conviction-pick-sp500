## Findings per claim

### 1. KLAC backlog — CONFIRMED
- KLA's FY2026 10-K (filed early August 2026) disclosed backlog of **$12.57B as of June 30, 2026**, up from $7.86B in FY2025. Source: stocktitan.net SEC-filing summary of the KLAC 10-K (page updated ~52 days before 2026-09-27, i.e. early Aug 2026).
- At the Citi Global TMT Conference (2026-09-10), CEO Rick Wallace said verbatim: "we're just short of $13 billion in our 10-K that we filed back at the beginning of August. And the order funnel has been very strong, and **I expect that backlog to continue to grow**." Source: semiconalpha.substack.com, "KLA Corporation (KLAC) @ Citi Global TMT Conference. Key Takeaways & Full Transcript," published 2026-09-10 (updated 17 days before verification date).

### 2. KLAC 74% share + WFE quote — CONFIRMED with material caveat
- The ~74% figure: ainvest.com ("Metrology Spending Is Outpacing the Equipment Cycle – and KLA Has Built a Moat Around It," published ~May 2026) states "KLA commands **73.8 percent of the global metrology and inspection market**, up from roughly 50 percent in 2010." This matches the panel's ~74% figure.
- **Caveat / discrepancy:** KLA's own investor-facing materials cite a different figure — tickeron.com (KLAC forecast page, updated 2026-09-21) notes "Management cites industry research showing KLA's process-control share has risen roughly 360 basis points since 2021 and is **about seven times larger than the next closest competitor**," consistent with ~56–58% process-control share from KLA's own Investor Day material. A third source (dataintelo.com market report, crawled 2026-09) gives KLA only 38–42% of the "metrology and inspection" market as defined by that research firm. The ~74% figure exists in reputable secondary financial press, but the number is definition-dependent; KLA's self-reported share is ~58% of process control. Dominance itself is not disputed — only the precise numerator.
- WFE quote: **confirmed verbatim**, attributed to CEO Richard Wallace on the **KLA Q3 2026 earnings call (2026-04-29)**, not the Citi conference: "the **WFE is literally just as fast as we can go as an industry**. That's kind of what we're seeing when we talk to our customers… And the reason '26 isn't bigger is because they can't build enough fast enough." Source: fool.com earnings-call transcript (2026-04-29). The quote is real and the attribution to Wallace is correct; the venue date is April 2026.
