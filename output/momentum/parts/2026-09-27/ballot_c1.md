# LENS C Ballot — Quality/moat & irreplaceability — RUN_DATE 2026-09-27

## Ballot 1 — Single pick

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** MSFT
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLAC is the only true monopoly in the semiconductor equipment stack: ~73.8% of the global metrology-and-inspection market (~80% in patterned-wafer and reticle inspection), with a moat that is algorithmic, not just optical — ~25 years of cross-customer defect-classification data no newcomer can replicate. Demand visibly exceeds supply: management's own words are "WFE is literally just as fast as we can go as an industry," with 2H CY2026 revenue up ~20% vs 1H only because supply availability improved — demand was waiting on supply. The backlog was just short of $13B and management expects it to keep growing, with advanced-packaging process-control revenue up >70% Y/Y to ~$1.1B as HBM and hybrid-bonding capacity gets built. And the entry reopened: shares corrected ~40% from the July $307 peak to ~$187 on WFE digestion fears — a monopoly at a momentum discount.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Backlog "just short of $13 billion" (10-K, early August 2026) with CEO Wallace stating "I expect that backlog to continue to grow" (Citi Global TMT Conference, 9/10/2026), supporting visibility into late 2026 and 2027 — paired with advanced-packaging revenue +70% Y/Y because "customers are adding capacity more quickly, especially for HBM and hybrid bonding."
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Low. Hyperscalers have zero path to in-source this — KLAC's customers are foundries, not hyperscalers, and process control is the one equipment category fabs "cannot run without." Fabs tune recipes layer-by-layer on KLA baselines, so switching vendors requires re-baselining entire processes; competitors compete only in narrow niches. Irreplaceability scored 10/10 in the dossier, the highest of the 14.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base — the $13B backlog converts through 2027 at record margins, low-20s% growth per management's own 2027 guidance, and the stock recovers roughly half the drawdown: ~$187 → $260–280 (~40–50%). Bull — CY2027 WFE lands at $200–225B ("strongest equipment cycle ever contemplated"), advanced packaging keeps compounding, and KLAC re-rates past the $307 peak to ~$340+ (~80%+).
- **Top risk:** WFE cyclicality on a premium multiple — P/E ~51 and a 41.7% operating-margin, high-fixed-cost business means a 2027 capex pause hits brutally (the 40% July→September correction is both the opportunity and the warning). China/export controls also cost $300–350M of CY2026 revenue.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 — single most compelling moat/backlog data point + scenario

**KLAC.** The moat datum: ~73.8% share of global metrology-and-inspection (up from ~50% in 2010), ~80% in patterned-wafer and reticle inspection, plus 52 consecutive quarters of Y/Y services growth — a monopoly whose 24% of revenue is recurring and doesn't participate in equipment downturns. The shortage datum: a near-$13B backlog still growing into a supply-constrained industry where 2H revenue is rising only as supply frees up. Base ~$260–280 (+40–50%), bull past the $307 peak to ~$340+ (+80%+) on a $200–225B CY2027 WFE.

**MSFT.** The irreplaceability datum: ~464M Microsoft 365 commercial seats plus Active Directory/identity and Azure-as-default — there is no realistic path for an enterprise to route around that distribution stack; Azure crossed $100B annual revenue and commercial RPO hit $678B (+84% YoY) with >$200B landing in FY2027. Base: RPO converts, Copilot climbs from 6.5% penetration of the seat base, and $497.93 re-rates toward the $565–575 analyst average (+15–20%); bull: 45%+ Azure growth sustains, Street-high $700 territory (+40%).

**LRCX.** The moat datum: ~45–50% share of plasma etch, >20 years as dry-etch leader, an installed base of >102,000 chambers with qualification lock-in — fabs co-optimize recipes on Lam chambers; first to put cryo HAR etch into volume production (2019). The demand datum: record $6.72B quarter (+15% Q/Q, +30% Y/Y) with a guide implying >20% Q/Q growth and CY2026 WFE raised to the low-$150Bs. Base: record September quarter lands and the memory-etch wedge keeps compounding (~30–40%); bull: WFE runs to $200B+ in 2027 and LRCX retraces multiples toward prior-cycle premium pricing.

### Deliberately left out of the top 10

- **SNDK** — irreplaceability 5/10: #5 of 5 NAND makers with a commodity product and the price curve already flattening at the top of a spike; supply position is not a moat.
- **APH** — irreplaceability 6/10: #2 in a fragmented connector market; hyperscalers qualify second sources and co-design around vendors (Foxconn–NVIDIA), so the 1.23 book-to-bill is demand, not a moat.
- **CSCO** — irreplaceability 5/10: NVIDIA took the #1 DC Ethernet seat (21.5% vs Cisco 17.8%), margins are diluting (−210bps), and the hyperscaler layer is actively being routed around it.
- **NEM** — irreplaceability 3/10: gold is fully fungible; no process-tech or certification lock-in exists over an ounce, and the metal sits 21% off its January record.
