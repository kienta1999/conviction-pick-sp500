## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** AMAT
- **Runner-up:** KLAC
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Applied Materials is the #1 wafer-fab-equipment company and #1 in deposition (~35%), epitaxy (>85% in key applications), CMP (>60% at leading nodes), and ion implantation (~50%) — the broadest toolbox in the AI fab, which matters because advanced nodes increasingly demand co-optimization across process steps that no narrower rival can match. Its $5B EPIC Center co-innovation model (KIOXIA, Intel, UCLA partnerships signed Sep–Oct 2026) locks customers in years ahead of production. With most leading-edge logic and DRAM fabs running at full capacity, 10+ new fabs announced, and customer contracts already being negotiated for 2030, AMAT has multi-year demand visibility into a capex wave it is structurally required for. Record FQ3 (quarter ended Jul 26, 2026): $9.12B revenue (+24.8% YoY), 34% operating margin, 13th straight quarter of gross-margin expansion.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Management on the August 2026 earnings call: "most leading edge logic and DRAM fabs are running at full capacity" while customers "have started negotiating customer contracts for **2030**" — a tool supplier with 2030 visibility (Fool, Sep 29, 2026, summarizing the Aug call).
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — only a handful of firms on earth can build leading-edge process tools (decades of plasma-physics IP), multi-year fab qualification means switching vendors requires re-qualifying the entire process, and TSMC/Samsung/Intel cannot in-source tools; substitute-technology risk is near zero for the core processes.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base — WFE lands in the low-$150B range, DRAM equipment revenue (+52% YoY in the last quarter) and advanced-packaging equipment (+70% guided for 2026) keep converting, stock grinds up with the cycle. Bull — the 10+ announced fabs and 2030 contract negotiations pull WFE above $150B+ and extend the cycle into 2028–29; AMAT's record 34% operating margin and 80–100% of FCF returned to shareholders compound the move.
- **Top risk:** WFE is the most cyclical link in semiconductors — record tool sales may mark the cyclical peak; China export-control exposure is the sector's #1 tail risk; stock is +141% in a year at ~44x earnings, so the multiple assumes the cycle lasts.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

### Top 3 deep notes

**1. AMAT** — The single most compelling moat datum: Applied is the only equipment vendor covering deposition, epitaxy, CMP, ion implantation, and etch at leading positions, and customers are negotiating contracts out to 2030 — fabs commit to a vendor's roadmap four years ahead, which is qualification lock-in in its purest form (Aug 2026 earnings call, via Fool, Sep 29, 2026). 12–18mo: base — low-$150B WFE year, record margins hold, stock compounds with backlog conversion; bull — HBM/advanced-packaging intensity pushes WFE past $150B and the 10+ new fabs keep the cycle running into 2028–29, justifying today's ~44x multiple and then some.

**2. KLAC** — The moat datum: FY2026 10-K (filed Aug 5, 2026) shows backlog of $12.57B vs $7.86B a year earlier (+60% YoY) with order visibility into calendar 2027, behind a ~52% inspection / ~85% reticle-inspection share position and a defect-classification data moat that compounds with every installed tool — fabs standardize on KLA as the reference platform, so switching breaks process comparability. 12–18mo: base — H2 2026 revenue ~20% above H1 per CFO guide, advanced-packaging process-control revenue ~$1.1B (>70% YoY), services floor (~23% of revenue) cushions; bull — cycle extends, backlog keeps growing into 2027, premium multiple holds on near-monopoly economics.

**3. LRCX** — The moat datum: #1 in etch — a process where a single tool drills billions of holes 50x deeper than wide per wafer, a capability only ~3 firms on earth possess, with multi-year qualification lock-in once a process of record is set; management says "most leading edge logic and DRAM fabs are running at full capacity" and customers are "working to secure equipment for future clean-room capacity" (Aug 2026 call via marketbeat, Jul 29, 2026). 12–18mo: base — raised low-$150B WFE outlook, advanced packaging flagged at >70% YoY growth, momentum carries; bull — memory capex super-cycle plus Micron's $32B customer commitments (TradingView, Oct 1, 2026) read straight through to Lam's etch franchise and the ~56x multiple keeps expanding on record tool sales.
