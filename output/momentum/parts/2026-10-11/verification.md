## MU — Micron Technology

**MU-1. ~$150B RPO under 26 take-or-pay SCAs at minimum/floor pricing (Sept 30, 2026 FQ4 call) — CONFIRMED**
CFO Mark Murphy: remaining performance obligations under agreements with defined pricing frameworks totaled approximately $150 billion, based on committed volumes and minimum pricing — "inherently conservative." 26 strategic customer agreements (up from 16 in June), all with take-or-pay volumes; >35% of estimated revenue through 2030, some extending to 2031. Customer financial commitments $32B, mostly cash deposits.
Sources: aistockwire.com (Sept 30, 2026 recap); baseballnewssource.com (Oct 3, 2026, citing call); finance.biggo.com (Sept 30, 2026); momoview.com (Sept/Oct 2026); indmoney.com (Oct 2026); thecerbatgem.com (Oct 2, 2026).

**MU-2. HBM3E/HBM4 sold out through calendar 2027, order books extending into 2028 — CONFIRMED**
"HBM3E and HBM4 chips are sold out through calendar 2027, with customers already locking in supply for 2028"; HBM4 twelve-high ramping ~2x as fast as HBM3E; >$1B of HBM4 shipped; CEO said supply will likely stay tight past 2027. The June Q3 call had already stated "fully booked through calendar 2027, with demand extending into 2028."
Sources: startupfortune.com (Oct 1, 2026); mexc.com (Sept 2026 earnings preview quoting Q4 call); ainvest.com (Aug 2026, Q3 call); fool.com (Sept 22, 2026, Q3 call).

**MU-3. $12.7B of customer deposits received to date ($12.3B in Q4 alone) — CONFIRMED**
Customer financial commitments total ~$32B ("vast majority" cash deposits); $12.7B actually received to date; $12.3B arrived in Q4 alone. FY2026 cash-flow statement shows $12.747B of "proceeds from customer contract liability deposits"; noncurrent customer contract liabilities rose from $568M to $12.895B.
Sources: aistockwire.com (Sept 30, 2026); momoview.com (Sept/Oct 2026).

**MU-4. Netlist ITC fight (new action Sept 29, 2026 vs HBM3E/HBM4/HBM4E; 337-TA-1523 on DDR5 RDIMMs instituted Sept 23, 2026) — CONFIRMED as having occurred, with a MATERIAL subsequent change: SETTLED**
The actions were real: Netlist's new Sept 29, 2026 ITC complaint alleged US Patents 12,308,087 and 12,646,537 cover Micron's HBM3E/HBM4/HBM4E, seeking exclusion and cease-and-desist orders vs Micron plus downstream NVIDIA, Broadcom, Google (Reuters first reported the confidential filing Sept 25). The earlier Aug 11, 2026 complaint (DDR5 RDIMMs/MRDIMMs, 4 patents; respondents Micron, Supermicro, HPE, Lenovo) was instituted as Investigation 337-TA-1523 on Sept 23, 2026.
**Post-dossier change:** On October 6, 2026, Netlist and Micron announced Patent License and Settlement Agreements ending ALL pending disputes: Micron pays $600M over 5 years ($30M/quarter, Q4 2026–Q3 2031) for a worldwide, non-exclusive 5-year license to Netlist's portfolio (server DIMMs + HBM); all lawsuits dropped; Micron also buys 10M Netlist shares for $1M. The agreement "settles all pending legal proceedings between the two parties."
Sources: tradingkey.com (Sept 29, 2026); bitcoinversus.tech (Sept 29, 2026); igorslab.de; ad-hoc-news.de (Oct 2026); **Reuters (Oct 6, 2026); Morningstar/Dow Jones (Oct 6, 2026); tradingview.com/Stocktwits (Oct 6, 2026); startupfortune.com (Oct 6, 2026).**
⚠️ The settlement was announced Oct 6 — before the 2026-10-10 dossier research but not reflected in it. The ITC exclusion-order tail risk the pick treats as live ("Swing factor #2", "thesis-break exit trigger", bear scenario at $640) is now RESOLVED. This materially changes the MU risk picture: bear-case driver #1 and key thesis-break trigger no longer apply. The panel should treat the $600M license cost (a predictable ~$30M/quarter) rather than an open-ended import-ban risk.
