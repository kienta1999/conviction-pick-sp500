### Top 3 deep notes

**1. Base/bull (12–18mo): ~$950–1,050 re-rating as the backlog converts and the Texas/NY regulatory scare fades; ~$1,200+ if gigawatt-scale deals keep landing and the 2027–29 capacity ramp executes.

**2. MU.** The cleanest "skepticism still priced in" name in the dossier: HBM3E/HBM4 "completely sold out through end-2027" (FQ3 call, June 2026) with a ~$150B take-or-pay RPO "at minimum/floor pricing" (FQ4 call, 2026-09-30) — yet the stock trades at ~7x forward P/E per Melius and finished a monster beat-and-raise quarter roughly flat. Base/bull (12–18mo): ~$1,300–1,400 (+25–30%) as 2027 contracts repriced higher flow through; ~$2,000+ if shortage pricing persists into 2028 (D.A. Davidson PT $3,000, Oct 7).

**3. Yes, P/E ~39–41 prices expectations — but the thesis compounds via pipeline, not just the current shortage. Base/bull (12–18mo): ~$1,350–1,450 (+15–25%) on continued share gains and a third guidance raise; ~$1,700+ on Foundayo oral dominance and retatrutide data strength.
