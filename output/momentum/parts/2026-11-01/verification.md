The one unverified item is APH's "~50 weeks" fiber lead time: it rests on the September 2026 Edgewater channel report cited in batch 2, which this verification could not independently retrieve; industry-wide lead-time evidence points the same way (toward about a year) but does not confirm the Amphenol-specific figure.

- CDNS's EDA moat is a three-vendor oligopoly (Synopsys, Cadence, Siemens EDA), not a two-vendor duopoly alone; the dossier's Synopsys-only framing is shorthand, not an error in the oligopoly claim itself.
