## MU (Micron Technology)

### 1. HBM sold out through 2026; most of 2027 output already committed — CONFIRMED

- Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra on the fiscal Q4 2026 earnings call (30 Sep 2026): HBM capacity sold out through calendar 2026; "a large part of the volume is already sold out for 2027 for HBM, and the prices are much higher than 2026 prices"; most of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply already under contract.
- More than 75% of Micron's overall fiscal-2027 output already committed under long-term contracts (same call, reported 1 Oct 2026).
- Corroboration: at the 16 Oct 2026 opening of the AATT2 HBM packaging plant in Taichung, Micron President/COO Manish Bhatia said "the entire planned 2027 capacity is already sold out" for that plant.
- Sources: Micron fiscal Q4 2026 earnings call 30 Sep 2026 (via Tech Times 16 Oct 2026; Tech Insider 1 Oct 2026); Startup Fortune 26 Oct 2026.
- Note: the company said "large part"/"large majority" for 2027 HBM, not 100% of all 2027 HBM — the dossier's "most of 2027 output" is a fair paraphrase.

### 2. ~$32B customer prepayments/commitments vs ~$100B binding multi-year AI memory contracts — CONFIRMED (with update: the $100B figure is stale; the current figure is ~$150B)

- **$32B CONFIRMED exactly:** Micron disclosed on 30 Sep 2026 that customer financial commitments under its 26 strategic customer agreements (SCAs) reached $32B, "mostly in cash deposits" / "the vast majority in cash deposits." (Zacks, 9 Nov 2026; VLSI, 15 Oct 2026; Micron Q4 FY26 release/call.)
- **~$100B is the JUNE figure, superseded:** in the fiscal Q3 2026 disclosure (June 2026) the figure was ~$22B of commitments and ~$100B of minimum contracted revenue across 14 of 16 SCAs. By 30 Sep 2026 the corresponding figure was **~$150B in remaining performance obligations** (RPO) linked to SCAs at minimum pricing (Zacks 9 Nov 2026; VLSI 15 Oct 2026). The dossier's ~$100B therefore **understates** the current contract book; it is not a fabrication, but the memo should cite ~$150B RPO as of 30 Sep 2026. This strengthens, not weakens, the thesis.
- 26 SCAs, estimated to cover >35% of revenue through 2030, some extending into 2031 — confirmed (same sources).

### 3. Only three firms on earth make DRAM at scale (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) — CONTRADICTED as literally worded

- The concentration fact is real: Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron together hold ~90–95% of global DRAM revenue (Counterpoint/TrendForce via Motley Fool, 27 Jul 2026; CryptoBriefing). Q1 2026 shares: Samsung ~38–39%, SK Hynix ~29%, Micron ~22%.
- But the literal claim "only three firms on earth make DRAM at scale" is false: **TrendForce's Q2 2026 DRAM revenue table puts China's CXMT (ChangXin Memory Technologies) at 9.5% share** (Samsung 39.4%, SK Hynix 24.9%, Micron 23.3%) — a fourth producer at meaningful scale (PacificTape, Oct 2026; Hikrobot, 3 Nov 2026). Smaller DRAM makers also exist.
- CXMT's rise is not new news to the market — it was the driver of a Micron sell-off in Oct 2026 — but the dossier's absolute wording overstates the moat. Correct framing: "a highly concentrated oligopoly: the top three hold ~90% of DRAM revenue, with CXMT emerging as a fourth producer at ~10%."
- **Pick impact: none on the ranking.** The oligopoly economics (pricing power, sold-out HBM) stand; only the "only three on earth" phrasing must be corrected in the memo.

### 4. Q4 FY26 revenue ~$54.23B, +379% YoY; FY26 revenue ~$133.19B, +256% YoY — CONFIRMED

- Micron's own release, 30 Sep 2026 (quarter ended 3 Sep 2026): Q4 revenue **$54.23B** vs $11.32B a year earlier (+379.3% by arithmetic; release states +379%); FY26 revenue **$133.19B** vs $37.38B in FY25 (+256.3%). GAAP net income $37.70B (Q4) / $84.97B (FY26). Source: investors.micron.com press release, 30 Sep 2026; corroborated by SignalStack/8-K Exhibit 99.1 and Motley Fool 26 Oct 2026.
- Caveat for the memo (not a contradiction): Q4 FY26 was a **14-week quarter** (FY26 had 53 weeks), so the +379% YoY compares 14 weeks against 13 weeks. The figures themselves are exact.

### 5. Q4 FY26 earnings call 30 Sep 2026; next earnings 2026-12-23 — UNVERIFIED (date half only)

- **30 Sep 2026 call CONFIRMED:** Micron reported results and hosted the conference call on Wednesday, 30 Sep 2026 at 2:30 p.m. MT (Micron IR release, 30 Sep 2026).
- **23 Dec 2026 NOT company-confirmed:** TipRanks, MarketBeat and FMP-based previews all list 23 Dec 2026 as the **estimated** next report date, explicitly marked "TBA (Not Confirmed)" / "estimated based on past reporting schedules." Micron has not announced the date. One aggregator (FundSpec, 11 Nov 2026) instead lists 16 Dec 2026, and a 5 Nov 2026 analysis notes the date was unconfirmed then and that last year's Q1 report was 17 Dec 2025. The memo must label 23 Dec 2026 as an **estimate**, not a confirmed catalyst date.
- **Pick impact: none.** The catalyst exists (Q1 FY27 report in mid/late Dec); only its exact day is unconfirmed.

## CDNS (Cadence Design Systems)

- Q3 2026 release (26 Oct 2026, Cadence IR): "Quarter-end backlog was $8.2 billion and revenue expected to be recognized in the next 12 months from remaining performance obligations was $4.2 billion." - Arvy (updated 19 Oct 2026): "The EDA market is dominated by two giants controlling 74% of it: Synopsys holds a 38% share, and Cadence 36%… backed by near-100% retention rates and an 80–85% recurring revenue model."

## CF (CF Industries)

### 8. ~9M metric tons of urea supply removed since Middle East hostilities began (Feb 2026); urea exports roughly halved (~800k t/month vs normal 1.5–1.7M t/month) — CONFIRMED

- **9M tons CONFIRMED from the company:** on CF's Q3 2026 earnings call (5 Nov 2026), management (Frost) "estimated that global markets have lost approximately 9 million metric tons of urea supply since hostilities began in the Middle East in late February" (American Banking News, 7 Nov 2026; TradingKey call summary).
- **"Roughly halved" is directionally confirmed and, if anything, conservative:** independent trade data show larger drops — urea exports from the seven Hormuz-dependent economies fell **83% YoY in April 2026** (ITC/WTO/UNCTAD); West Asia chemical-fertilizer exports fell to 994k tons in Sep 2026 vs 3.41M tons a year earlier (**−70–75%**, Kpler via PressTV, 31 Oct 2026); Argus puts Middle East urea exports at ~20 Mt/yr (~35% of seaborne trade), with >17 Mt/yr loading behind Hormuz or from Iran.
- The specific monthly pair (~800k t/month vs a normal 1.5–1.7M t/month) was **not independently reproduced** from a single source in this check; the third-party data above support the magnitude and direction but use different scopes (all-fertilizer vs urea-only, regional vs Hormuz-dependent). Treat the monthly pair as company/market-context figures rather than independently verified.
- Hostilities start date confirmed: US–Israeli strikes on Iran began **28 Feb 2026**, closing Hormuz to commercial traffic (multiple sources).

### 9. Ammonia capacity ~97% utilisation YTD; Q3 2026 UAN shipments highest quarterly volume of the decade — CONFIRMED

Footnote: "available" capacity excludes the idled Yazoo City complex (outage since Nov 2025).
- Earnings call (5 Nov 2026), CEO Chris Bohn: "Since the beginning of the year, we have operated our available ammonia capacity at 97% utilization… Our UAN fill program also helped us ship nearly two million tons in the third quarter, our largest quarterly UAN volume this decade." (Motley Fool transcript, 5 Nov 2026; TradingKey.)
- Sources: CF Industries 8-K Exhibit 99.1, 4 Nov 2026; CF Q3 2026 earnings-call transcript, 5 Nov 2026.

### 10. Q3 2026 adjusted EBITDA ~$833M (reported 4 Nov 2026) — CONFIRMED

- CF Industries 8-K / press release, 4 Nov 2026: "Third quarter 2026 net earnings of $436 million, or $2.87 per diluted share, EBITDA of $822 million, and adjusted EBITDA of $833 million" (vs $667M in Q3 2025). Nine-month adjusted EBITDA $3.01B. Source: CF Industries Form 8-K dated 4 Nov 2026 (Exhibit 99.1); corroborated by Minichart, Kalkine, Morningstar/Business Wire same day.
