## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: CDNS
- Runner-up: CF
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Cadence is the one name in this field where the market has *not* priced the story: the stock is flat over 12 months (+0.1%) and sits 20.9% below its 52-week high, while the business just posted its third consecutive beat-and-raise, revenue +22% YoY, and a record $8.2bn backlog with $4.2bn of RPO to be recognised in the next 12 months (+20% YoY). The "shortage" here is not units on a shelf but EDA capability itself — a duopoly with Synopsys controlling ~74% of the market, near-100% client retention, and certification lock-in that makes switching mid-design near-impossible — so every AI accelerator, custom hyperscaler ASIC and chiplet design start flows through this tollbooth whether or not memory prices stay hot. With ~72% of next year's revenue already contracted and analyst upside of +25.1% still on the table, the explosive leg is a re-rating back toward its high on a compounding ~21% grower, not a bet that a commodity price spike lasts forever. Downside is cushioned by the same backlog: this is a contracted revenue stream, not a spot-price call.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Record backlog of $8.2bn at 30 Sep 2026 (+17% YoY), with $4.2bn of remaining performance obligations due in the next 12 months (+20% YoY) — roughly 1.5x annual revenue already booked in a light renewal year.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — Cadence and Synopsys form a duopoly with foundry-certified flows, decades of process-design kits and IP libraries, and near-100% retention; no hyperscaler builds its own EDA, and even the biggest custom-silicon customers buy Cadence tools to design their chips. The residual risks are second-order: open-source EDA is nowhere near parity at advanced nodes, and the July 2026 AI-design scare (9–10% knock) is more likely to raise design starts than to replace the tools.
- 12-18 month return scenario: Base case roughly +25–35% (analyst mean target implies +25.1%; steady ~21% growth plus partial multiple repair from 20.9% below the high). Bull case roughly +50–70% if the market re-rates the duopoly back to its 52-week high and beyond on continued beat-and-raise quarters and Maestro AI-agent adoption (>100 customers already).
- Top risk: It is not a physical shortage, so there is no price-spike kicker — if the market keeps treating EDA as expensive (forward PEG ~4.13 per the dossier, FCF yield ~1.99%) the re-rating may not come; plus China export-control exposure (~15.6% of revenue) and Hexagon integration cost (GAAP operating margin fell to 26.2% in Q3 2026 from 31.8% a year earlier).
- Conviction (1-10): 7
