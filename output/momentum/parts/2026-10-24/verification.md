## MU — Micron

### 1. Backlog / bookings — CONFIRMED, with a wording conflation on the HBM/75% element

- **~$150B backlog:** CONFIRMED. Micron's FYQ4 2026 prepared remarks (reported 2026-09-30) describe 26 strategic customer agreements (SCAs, up from 16 in June) and approximately $150B of remaining performance obligations, measured at committed volumes and *minimum* prices for agreements with determined pricing frameworks; CFO Mark Murphy called the figure "inherently conservative." Sources: Beancount analysis of the FY2026 Q4 call/filings, 2026-10-02; AI Stock Wire / CNBC-sourced coverage, 2026-10-01; SignalStack summary of the Q4 8-K/remarks, Oct 2026.
- **>$100B take-or-pay:** CONFIRMED. All SCAs carry take-or-pay volumes (customers pay whether or not they take delivery). The ~$100B figure was the June 2026 cumulative minimum-price total for 14 of the then-16 agreements; the $150B September figure supersedes it on a different measurement basis, so the two are not like-for-like. Customer financial commitments were separately put at ~$32B, "the vast majority" cash deposits. Sources: AI Stock Wire, 2026-10-01; TechTimes, "Micron Posts Record $54B Quarter, Locks Hyperscalers Into $100B Memory Contracts," 2026-10-01.
- **$12.747B customer deposits in FY26:** CONFIRMED — primary. FY2026 cash-flow statement shows $12,747M of customer contract-liability deposits as a financing inflow; non-current customer contract liabilities were $12.90B at 2026-09-03 (vs $568M at May 28). Sources: Micron FY2026 cash-flow statement via Beancount, 2026-10-02; SignalStack, Oct 2026.
- **HBM sold out through 2027 / ~75% booked through 2027:** CONFIRMED in substance, CONFLATED as written. What management actually said on the 2026-09-30 call: (a) **more than 75% of Micron's *total* 2027 output** is already committed — that 75% is not an HBM-specific figure; (b) separately, agreements are complete for **the "vast majority" of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply**, at prices well above 2026; (c) calendar-2026 HBM was entirely committed/sold out. No source found states HBM is exactly 75% booked, nor literally 100% "sold out through 2027." The dossier should read ">75% of total 2027 output committed; vast majority of 2027 HBM bits contracted," not "HBM sold out through 2027 with ~75% booked." Sources: MindCron summary of the 2026-09-30 call, Oct 2026; Stocktwits/TheFly coverage of the call, Oct 2026; Particle News, Oct 2026.

### 2. Shortage driver — CONFIRMED (attribution note)

- **Demand exceeds supply / tighter in 2027–28:** CONFIRMED verbatim from the FYQ4 call. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra, 2026-09-30: "In calendar 2027 as well as 2028, we see demand exceeding supply. In fact, we see greater tightness in the industry in 2027 and in 2028 versus 2026… We do not have line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance." Source: The Register, 2026-10-01.
- **Only 50–66% of key customer demand can be met:** CONFIRMED, but the quote is older than the dossier implies. Mehrotra first stated it in earlier FY2026 calls and reiterated it on the FYQ2 call (2026-03-18): asked if the ~70% ballpark still held, he replied Micron is "able to fulfill only 50% to 2/3 of their demand in the medium term. Yes, that still remains the case." The FYQ4 call's forward language (above) is consistent with it, but the 50–66% line itself was not a new FYQ4 disclosure. Sources: MarketBeat FYQ2 2026 call transcript, 2026-03-18; JPMorgan analyst commentary via TechTimes, 2026-10-01.

### 3. Catalyst date: next earnings 2026-12-23 — UNVERIFIED as an announced date

- 2026-12-23 is an **estimator's date, not a company-announced date**. No Micron investor-relations announcement of the FYQ1 2027 results date was found as of 2026-10-24.
- MarketBeat lists "Dec. 23 — After Market Close — **Estimated**" for upcoming Q1 FY27 earnings. TipRanks is internally contradictory: its table labels Dec 23, 2026 "(Confirmed)" while its own footnote says the date "is estimated for December 23, 2026, based on past reporting schedules." Other calendars disagree outright: Public.com says **Dec 16, 2026**; Finviz says **Dec 30, 2026 (Est)**.
- **Implication:** treat 2026-12-23 as a plausible screen/consensus estimate only. Do not present it in the final memo as a hard, announced catalyst date; the catalyst is "FYQ1 2027 print, expected mid-to-late December 2026, date unannounced at verification time."
- Sources: MarketBeat MU earnings page, accessed 2026-10-24; TipRanks MU earnings page, accessed 2026-10-24; Public.com MU earnings page, accessed 2026-10-24.

### 4. Moat: only ~3 firms can make HBM / leading DRAM — CONFIRMED (qualified "at scale")

- Multiple independent sources state only three companies manufacture HBM at scale: SK hynix, Samsung and Micron, and that the same trio controls ~90–95% of global DRAM (Counterpoint, cited in CryptoBriefing, 2026: Samsung ~38%, SK hynix ~29%, Micron ~22% of DRAM in Q1 2026). Sources: Nasdaq, "How HBM Memory Chip Dethroned Samsung in the AI Race" (Bernstein HBM shares cited: SK hynix ~57%, Samsung ~27%, Micron ~16%); AInvest, "The Memory Gatekeepers," Aug 2026; TechTimes, 2026-10-09 ("High-bandwidth memory is manufactured at meaningful scale by only three companies — Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron").
- Qualification the dossier already carries and should keep: China's CXMT (~9.5% DRAM share, entering server DRAM) and YMTC exist in conventional memory, so the claim is correct for *leading-edge HBM / leading DRAM at scale*, not for all DRAM production on earth.

## NVDA — Nvidia

### 5. Backlog / visibility: $500B through end-2026, doubled to $1T through 2027 at GTC 2026 — CONFIRMED

- **$500B:** CONFIRMED. Huang first gave visibility to >$500B of Blackwell + Rubin revenue through the end of calendar 2026 at GTC Washington DC (Oct 2025), repeated in CFO Colette Kress's Q3 FY26 call language ("visibility to $0.5 trillion in Blackwell and Rubin revenue from the start of this year through the end of calendar year 2026"). Source: MarketWatch, Mar 2026 ("Last fall, CEO Jensen Huang said at the company's Washington, D.C. iteration of GTC that it had visibility into more than $500 billion in revenue from the Blackwell and upcoming Rubin platforms through the end of 2026").
- **$1T at GTC 2026:** CONFIRMED. At the GTC 2026 keynote (2026-03-16), Huang raised this to at least $1 trillion of Blackwell + Vera Rubin demand/purchase orders through 2027: "I see through 2027 at least $1 trillion." Sources: MarketWatch, Mar 2026; Motley Fool, 2026-04-02; AcadeResearch GTC 2026 recap, 2026.
- Caveat (already in the dossier, and correct): this is management *visibility / purchase-order demand*, not a contractual RPO/backlog line in a filing, and the doubling also **extends the window** (2025–26 → 2025–27), so it is not a like-for-like doubling of demand for the same period. Source: BTW Media analysis of NVIDIA's GTC 2026 materials, 2026.

### 6. Moat: ~70–80% share of AI accelerators — CONFIRMED as an analyst-estimate range

- No filing states this share; it is an analyst/industry estimate, and the 70–80% range sits inside the 2026 consensus spread. TechTimes (2026-05-26): "Nvidia still holds roughly 70–80% of the AI accelerator market by revenue." Silicon Analysts' TrendForce/TSMC-based tracker projects ~75% for 2026, down from an ~87% peak in 2024; other 2026 trackers put revenue share at 80–85%. Sources: TechTimes, 2026-05-26; SiliconAnalysts AI accelerator share tracker, 2026; COR Advisors, Jun 2026 (~70–80%).
- Methodology matters: revenue share (favours NVIDIA's premium pricing) runs higher than unit share, and including hyperscaler custom ASICs in the denominator lowers it. The dossier's "~70–80% (multiple analyst sources, 2026)" phrasing is accurate; do not upgrade it to a measured fact.

## SNDK — SanDisk

### 7. Backlog — MIXED: $93.9B NBM, capacity shares and sold-out 2026 CONFIRMED; $41.6B RPO CONTRADICTED as an FYQ4 figure (it is stale)

- **RPO $41.6B / $41.2B unbilled: CONTRADICTED on timing, correct as an older figure.** $41.6B (of which $41.2B unbilled, $0.4B in contract liabilities, ~15% to be recognised in the next 12 months) is from SanDisk's **10-Q for the period ended 2026-04-03** — i.e. FYQ3, not "disclosed around FYQ4 earnings, Aug 2026" as the dossier states. By fiscal year-end the number had moved materially: the **FY2026 10-K (period ended 2026-07-03) reports RPO of $59.8B** ($58.7B unbilled, $1.1B in contract liabilities, ~19% within 12 months, entirely NBM-related), and management said on the Aug 2026 call that RPO **would be $91.1B including the two NBMs signed after the quarter closed**. Sources: SanDisk 10-Q ended 2026-04-03 (SEC filing text); SanDisk FY2026 10-K summaries (TickerScout / Dividend-Line X-Ray, Oct 2026); FYQ4 call transcript summary (Stock Alarm Pro). **Correction for the final memo: use $59.8B reported / $91.1B pro-forma, not $41.6B — the error understates SNDK's book, so it does not flatter the pick, but the date attribution is wrong.**
- **NBM contracted minimums $93.9B: CONFIRMED — primary (management).** CFO Luis Visoso, FYQ4 call 2026-08-05: "The total expected revenue from all our NBMs we have signed is a minimum of $93.9 billion, assuming floor pricing… We believe actual revenue will be above that minimum," across agreements with eight data-center/edge customers, weighted-average duration over four years, backed by $16.5B of financial guarantees (cash deposits + financial instruments). Note the dossier's ">$11B in financial guarantees" is also stale — the Aug figure is $16.5B. Sources: FourWeekMBA summary of the 2026-08-05 call and annual report, Oct 2026; AInvest, Aug 2026; SanDisk Investor Day (2026-08-13) coverage.
- **>50% of FY27 and ~2/3 of FY28 capacity committed: CONFIRMED.** Visoso, 2026-08-05: NBMs expected to represent "more than 50% of our bits in fiscal year 2027 and approximately 2/3 of our bits in fiscal year 2028." Repeated at the 2026-08-13 Investor Day. Sources: FourWeekMBA, Oct 2026; Counterpoint Research Investor Day note, Aug 2026. (Precisely: share of *bits*, which the dossier renders as "capacity" — acceptable shorthand, bits is the company's word.)
- **All 2026 NAND capacity sold out: CONFIRMED as industry reporting, not a SanDisk filing.** Phison's CEO confirmed in March 2026 that every major NAND manufacturer had told customers its 2026 supply was sold out; SanDisk was separately reported to have sold out its total manufacturing capacity for the remainder of 2026, with 2027 capacity being pre-booked. Sources: AInvest, "SanDisk: The NAND Supply Crunch Is Structural, Not Cyclical," Aug 2026; Trefis, 2026-03-10. Keep the dossier's "reportedly" hedge — this is a supplier/press claim, not a line SanDisk itself filed.

### 8. Category: #5 in NAND, 11.4% share Q2 2026 (TrendForce) vs Samsung 29.3% — CONFIRMED

- TrendForce press release, 2026-08-18 ("Combined Revenue of Top Five NAND Flash Brands Rises 77% QoQ in 2Q26; Micron Moves Up to Third Place"): Samsung #1 with revenue ~$23.06B and share **edging down to 29.3%**; SK hynix Group (incl. Solidigm) #2 (~$14.27B); Micron #3 ($11.85B, +99.2% QoQ, highest growth); Kioxia #4 (~$10.72B, share **13.6%**); SanDisk #5, revenue growth of only 50.7% QoQ (slowest of the five) to ~$8.97B — which against the TrendForce market total is the **11.4%** share shown in the release's ranking table. The ordering, Samsung's 29.3% and Kioxia's 13.6% are in the release text; SanDisk's 11.4% is in its table/figure.
- Cross-check: Counterpoint's Q2 2026 tracker is close but not identical (Samsung 28%, SanDisk 11%, YMTC 14% on a broader denominator) — a methodology difference, not a contradiction of the TrendForce-specific claim as cited. Source: Counterpoint Research, Global NAND Memory Market Share: Quarterly, updated 2026-10-06.
