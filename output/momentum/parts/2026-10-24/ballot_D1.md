# Panelist D — Contrarian / Risk Skeptic Ballot — 2026-10-24

## (1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** META
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The market is pricing Micron at fwd P/E 4.1 and SNDK at 4.6 as if 86-87% gross margins are a one-quarter peak about to collapse, yet management says it can satisfy only 50-66% of key customers' demand and sees *greater* tightness in 2027 and 2028 than in 2026, with no line of sight to balance. That disbelief is the opportunity: unlike a spot cycle, this one is contracted — >$100B of take-or-pay agreements, 26 LTAs covering >35% of revenue through 2030, 75% of capacity booked through end-2027, and customers prepaying $12.7B in cash deposits in FY2026. Micron is also the diversified way to own the shortage — HBM, conventional DRAM and NAND simultaneously, as the only US maker — rather than SNDK's single-product, #5-share NAND bet. If even part of the contracted pricing duration holds, earnings do not need to grow for the multiple to re-rate from peak-cycle distress toward normal.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Customers wired $12.747B of contract-liability deposits in FY2026 ($12.9B non-current) to lock supply years ahead, alongside a reported ~$150B backlog / ~$32B commitments and entire 2026 HBM output committed under long-term contracts.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — 8/10 irreplaceability in the dossier. Hyperscalers cannot fab HBM/DRAM, and their in-sourcing actually helps Micron: every custom TPU, Trainium and Maia still needs merchant HBM stacked on it, including Micron's custom HBM4E co-designed with NVIDIA for Vera Rubin. The real risk is share loss to SK hynix (~70% of NVIDIA HBM4 orders), not disintermediation.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +40-60% (partial re-rating plus contracted 2027 pricing, vs +82.4% analyst upside on screen); Bull +80-100% if 2027 HBM pricing increases hold and the market stops capitalising earnings as a peak.
- **Top risk:** Cyclical reversal from 2028 — new Micron/Samsung/SK hynix fabs arriving just as floor/ceiling LTAs cap upside, compounded by HBM4 yield/allocation execution vs SK hynix and any hyperscaler capex pause hitting the most cyclical name in the batch.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 — evidence and scenarios

**1. MU:** The single most compelling point is cash, not commentary — $12.7B of customer deposits received in FY2026 plus >$100B of take-or-pay contracts through 2030/31 prove buyers fear shortage more than Micron fears a downturn, in a 3-player oligopoly no hyperscaler can in-source. Base +40-60% over 12-18 months on contracted duration and a modest re-rating from fwd P/E 4.1; bull +80-100% if 2027-28 tightness exceeds 2026 as guided.

**2. META:** There is no product shortage here, and that is the contrarian point — the underappreciated asset is an irreplaceable (8/10) ad monopoly still compounding: Q2 2026 revenue +28% YoY, ad impressions +14%, price per ad +12%, and Advantage+ above a $75B run-rate, valued at only fwd P/E 18.6 with +30.0% screen upside after a -11% 12-month return because the market is capitalising the $130-145B capex bill, not the pricing power funding it. Base +25-30% if ad growth holds and capex growth merely stops accelerating; bull +45-55% if AI targeting/glasses convert spend into visible incremental revenue — downside is a 2022-style de-rating if 2027 capex ($174-280B analyst range) rises with no monetisation proof.

**3. NEM:** Gold is the cleanest structural shortage outside tech — central banks buying ~850t in 2026 (900-1,000t/yr since 2022, ~25% of mine production) against mine supply up only ~2.4% and a 15.2-year discovery-to-production lag — yet the world's #1 producer (5.89M oz in 2025 vs ~3.3-4.5M for #2) trades at fwd P/E 11.3 with net cash ~$3.2B and a fresh Q3 beat (adj. EPS $2.20 vs $1.94). Base +15-20% (in line with +19.3% screen upside) if gold holds near $4,100-4,300/oz; bull +35-45% toward the Metals Focus ~$4,920/oz 2026 average forecast — explicitly a gold-price bet, since Q3 production actually fell YoY and NEM has no pricing power over its fungible ounce.

**Left out of top 10:** ANET, V, NUE, WST — ANET is the doctrine trap itself (irreplaceability 5/10, fwd P/E 32.8, NVIDIA bundling and Celestica white-box already taking AI back-end share); V has no shortage at all (2/10) at fwd P/E 27.6 for ~11% growth with DOJ/A2A/stablecoin disintermediation overhang; NUE's scarcity is tariff-rented (irreplaceability 4/10, fully substitutable steel, HRC at 3x China) so duration depends on policy, not geology; WST has real regulatory lock-in (8/10) but at fwd P/E 39.5 with consensus target essentially at the $394 screen price, the GLP-1/Annex 1 re-rating is already paid for.
