## (1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT — Agent C, Quality / Moat & Irreplaceability

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** ISRG
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron is the only name here where the customers' own in-sourcing actually increases its demand: Google, Amazon, Microsoft and Meta can design their own accelerators, but every TPU, Trainium, Maia and MTIA still needs merchant HBM stacked on it, and nobody can fab leading-edge DRAM/HBM themselves — it is a 3-player oligopoly with decade-long process learning. That irreplaceability is now paired with the best quality numbers on the screen: 80.7% operating margin, 80.7% gross margin, 88.3% ROE, 44.6% ROA, net cash (long-term debt cut from $14.0B to $4.7B), and a forward P/E of only 4.1 despite revenue growth of 256% TTM. Management says demand exceeds supply in 2027 and 2028, tighter than in 2026, with no line of sight to balance — so the current pricing power is contracted, not spot. If even part of that tightness holds, earnings durability at this multiple gives the explosive re-rating the other high-moat names, priced at 28-40x forward, cannot.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** CEO Mehrotra on the FYQ4 call (2026-09-30): Micron can satisfy only 50-66% of key customers' demand, its entire 2026 HBM output is committed under long-term contracts, and customers wired $12.747B in contract-liability deposits in FY2026 — cash paid years ahead to lock supply, alongside >$100B of take-or-pay contracts cited in earnings coverage and 75% of capacity booked through end-2027.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — hyperscalers design accelerators, not memory fabs; there is no realistic in-sourcing path, custom ASICs still require merchant HBM, and Micron is co-designing custom HBM4E with NVIDIA ("NVHBM"), the first major custom HBM product expected in the market. The real risk is share loss to SK hynix (~70% of NVIDIA's HBM4 orders), not disintermediation.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +40-60% if HBM/DRAM tightness into 2028 and the FYQ1 2027 guide of $61.5B ±$1.5B deliver; bull +80%+ toward the screen's analyst mean target ($1,561.91 vs $856.49 screen price, +82.4% upside) if 2027 HBM pricing increases hold and forward earnings stay anywhere near the current run-rate.
- **Top risk:** Cyclicality and share execution — new fabs arriving in 2028 can end the shortage just as floor/ceiling LTAs cap upside, and hybrid-bonding HBM4 yields / NVIDIA allocation versus SK hynix are binary quarterly signals; it is #3 in DRAM, HBM and NAND, #1 in nothing.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

**Top 3 — moat / shortage detail and scenarios:**

**1. **2. ISRG:** The deepest moat in the dossier at 8.5/10 irreplaceability — ~70-72% global share, 12,094 da Vinci systems installed (more than all rivals combined), >18,000 surgeons trained on da Vinci 5 alone, per-procedure FDA/CE clearances rivals must queue for category by category, and 84% recurring revenue at $1,860 instruments-and-accessories revenue per procedure; a hospital cannot in-source a surgical robot and a surgeon does not retrain lightly. The catch for explosiveness is price: forward P/E 35.7 and only +7.8% analyst upside, with management itself cutting per-procedure pricing ~3-5%. 12-18mo: base +10-20% on 14-15% procedure growth compounding the installed-base annuity, bull +30-40% if dV5, cardiac and SP adoption accelerates share of the still ~95%-unpenetrated surgery market faster than Hugo/Ottava erode it.

**3. LLY:** Clear #1 in incretins at 60.9% US prescription share, pulling away from Novo, protected two ways no customer can route around — tirzepatide US patent protection to ~2036 and the scarcest fill-finish/peptide capacity on earth after >$50B of manufacturing capex since 2020 — with 54.2% operating margin, 83.4% gross margin and 102% ROE on the screen. Mounjaro plus Zepbound did $27.7B in H1 2026 (+~88% YoY), and the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge from 1 July 2026 expands access to ~20M eligible beneficiaries, though the literal shortage has now been manufactured away and realised prices are falling low-to-mid teens. 12-18mo: base +10-15% on volume-led growth toward the raised $85-87B FY26 guide, bull +25-35% if Foundayo oral share and retatrutide (Phase 3, up to 20.8% weight loss, filing planned Q1 2027) extend the franchise — capped near-term by a 27.7x forward P/E and only +1.9% screen analyst upside.
