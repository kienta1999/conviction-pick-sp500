### Top 3 deep reads (contrarian lens)

**1. KLAC.** The single most compelling data point is the $12.57B backlog (+60% YoY) *paired with the company telling you it can't ship fast enough* — "customer lead times for our products are increasing due to supply constraints, limiting first-half growth potential" (shareholder letter), with H2 CY2026 expected ~20% above H1 "supported by improving supply availability." That's a sold-out franchise on sale: 37.6% off highs, 28x forward, consensus Buy with ~$230 average target (+22% already implied). Moat is the dossier's best — 62–74% process-control share and a 25-year defect-data moat. 12–18mo: base $230–260 (+22–38%) as backlog converts and 2027 sequential growth ("at least as strong") prices in; bull $300+ (+60%+) if the market decides a 62–74% share toll on every leading-edge wafer was never a cyclical.

**2. AMAT.** "Priced to disappoint" is the contrarian's favorite headline when it attaches to a record quarter: $9.12B (+24.8% YoY) followed by a guided first-ever $10B quarter (+51% YoY) — and the stock fell after hours, sitting ~38% below its $739.67 high. The fear (capacity-build margin friction, WFE cyclicality, China decline) has overshot a business with an eight-quarter customer forecast book, tools embedded in node roadmaps, and a BofA WFE ramp to $156B in 2026 and $210B in 2027. Irreplaceability 8/10: no fab can in-source the breadth of the toolbox. 12–18mo: base ~$600–658 (+24–36%, consensus target) as the $10B-quarter run-rate proves demand is structural, not a blip; bull $700–750 (+45–55%) if multiple re-rates toward LRCX's as the 2027 WFE super-cycle is priced.

**3. LRCX.** The cleanest "demand outruns supply" statement in the group, from the CFO's own mouth: "The industry is fundamentally under-supplying demand, and clean room is a constraint" (9/20/2026) — backed by a record $6.72B June quarter, gross margin at a 20-year high (52.0%), and a September-quarter guide of $8.10B ± $400M (+20% Q/Q) that crushed the $7.13B consensus. #1 in etch (45–50% share), and the record $2.47B customer-support revenue (+42.6% YoY) proves installed-base monetization, not just tool sales — qualification lock-in is real. 12–18mo: base $400–450 (+27–43%) on delivered guide and the low-$150B WFE view; bull $500+ (+59%) if etch intensity keeps rising with advanced packaging and the high-30% SAM-share goal starts pricing. Watch the real risks: China 26% of revenue, ~54x P/E, and classic memory-capex rollover exposure.
