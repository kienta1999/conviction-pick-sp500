# Lens A — Supply-Chain Analyst Ballot — 2026-09-28
Panel: S&P 500 momentum stock-pick. Lens mandate: weight shortage/backlog evidence and multi-year revenue visibility above all; rank names where supply is the binding constraint highest; discount names whose "shortage" is really a demand surge with no supply constraint, or whose backlog is soft/cancelable.

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** NVDA
- **Runner-up:** MU
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** NVIDIA is the one name where the shortage IS the product: Blackwell sold out through mid-2026 with a 3.6M-unit backlog from the largest cloud providers alone, management has $500B of Blackwell+Rubin revenue visibility through end-2026, and Huang expects combined purchase orders to reach $1T through 2027. Supply commitments nearly doubled to $95.2B, locking capacity through calendar 2027 — this is signed, pre-committed demand chasing capacity, not demand that must be created. "The clouds are sold out" is as close to a pure supply-binding constraint as exists in the S&P 500. At ~14.4x forward earnings with 45.6% analyst upside, it also carries the cheapest multiple in the AI complex despite the deepest moat and backlog.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** 3.6M-unit Blackwell backlog from the largest cloud providers alone, with $500B Blackwell+Rubin revenue visibility through end-2026 and ~$1T in purchase orders expected through 2027 — the deepest contracted demand book in the dossier.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** MEDIUM — no single firm can replicate the full stack (silicon + NVLink/networking + CUDA + annual roadmap cadence), and the hyperscalers building their own ASICs remain Nvidia's largest customers, so this is coexistence, not substitution. But the in-sourcing threat is real and maturing: J.P. Morgan forecasts custom AI chips surpassing GPUs in 2027 with 53% share, and inference — projected ~70% of AI compute by 2026 — is where TPUs are 1.4–2x more cost-efficient. Share of incremental inference will erode; absolute revenue can still grow because the pie is exploding.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35–45% (clean Rubin ramp, hyperscaler capex sustained, modest inference-share erosion offset by exploding pie). Bull ~+75–90% (Rubin executes cleanly AND China datacenter compute reopens — currently assumed zero in guidance, pure upside option).
- **Top risk:** Hyperscaler capex pause by one of the ~4 customers that drive the business, or ASIC in-sourcing accelerating past J.P. Morgan's 53%-by-2027 forecast — combined with elevated accruals (earnings running ahead of cash on the Rubin working-capital build).
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top-3 detail

**#1 NVDA (~$225, ~$5.4T mkt cap).** The single most compelling data point: Blackwell sold out through mid-2026 with a 3.6M-unit backlog from the largest cloud providers alone, backed by $500B of Blackwell+Rubin revenue visibility through end-2026 and ~$1T in purchase orders expected through 2027, with supply commitments doubled to $95.2B locking capacity through CY2027. This is multi-year, signed revenue visibility with supply as the binding constraint. Base ~+35–45% over 12–18 months (Rubin ramp executes, capex sustained); bull ~+75–90% (China datacenter compute reopens against zero current guidance assumption).

**#2 KLAC (~$188, ~$244B mkt cap).** The single most compelling data point: backlog of $12.57B as of 6/30/2026, up 60% YoY, with management's own shareholder letter stating "customer lead times for our products are increasing due to supply constraints, limiting first-half growth potential" — i.e., shipments, not orders, are the constraint. That is the textbook supply-binding signal this lens is built to find, on the hardest-to-displace franchise in semicap (irreplaceability 10/10 — yield models trained on ~25 years of process data; no fab risks yield to save capex). Base ~+30% (H2 growth ~20% over H1 converts the backlog); bull ~+60% (management's "2027 at least as strong" proves out and lead times keep extending).

**#3 MU (~$1,082, ~$1.22T mkt cap).** The single most compelling data point: 16 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements with minimum contracted revenue near $100B (~20% of DRAM volume, ~30% of NAND), and CEO Mehrotra stating Micron can meet only ~50% to two-thirds of demand from several key customers — a genuine manufacturing constraint with multi-year contracted floors. As the supply-chain lens, the 9/30 FQ4 print (2 days out, $50B guide midpoint already $6.55B above Street when issued, Burry short into it) is treated as a risk factor, not a reason to ignore the backlog — hence runner-up, not top pick. Base ~+25% (survives the print, LTAs intact, tightness through 2027 per Citi); bull ~+70% (clean beat-and-raise with no print-day dislocation, DRAM/NAND undersupplied through 2027). Binary downside if guidance breaks.

### Deliberately left out of top 10

- **ANET** — lost the #1 DC Ethernet seat to Nvidia's bundle (21.5% vs 20.7%); its $9.7B "commitments" are Arista's *supplier* obligations, not customer backlog — exactly the soft/non-customer visibility this lens discounts.
- **CF** — the sharpest *live* shortage in the dossier (98% utilization, volumes −15.3% for lack of supply) but a geopolitical binary with zero order-book visibility; it collapses from 9/10 to ~4/10 on a ceasefire. This lens pays for multi-year revenue visibility, not war duration.
- **LLY** — the FDA-declared acute shortage ended in Oct 2024 and the company confirmed it in 2025; growth is volume/pricing driven by access expansion, not rationed supply. This is precisely the "demand surge with no supply constraint" this lens is told to discount, however strong the momentum.
