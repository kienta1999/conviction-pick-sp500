# Momentum panel — ballot D (contrarian / risk skeptic) — 2026-11-17

## 1. Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** APH
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The market is pricing Micron as a peak-cycle trap — 3.7x forward earnings, −35.8% off its 52-week high, sold on a capacity headline (Fab 11E) and a class-action headline in the same month — while the business underneath has quietly stopped being a spot commodity cycle. More than 75% of FY2027 output is already committed, 26 Strategic Customer Agreements run through 2030, and SK Hynix does not expect supply-demand balance before 2030. The underappreciated fact is structural: HBM is now a contracted, multi-year backlog business, not a quarterly spot price. At 3.7x forward, even a 50% haircut to forward earnings leaves the stock at ~7.4x — the skeptic's margin of safety is the valuation itself.
- **Shortage / backlog evidence:** The single most compelling data point is the contractual lock-in: >75% of FY2027 shipments committed, "the vast majority" of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply sold at prices "significantly higher" than a year ago, and 26 customer agreements through 2030 (FQ4 FY26 earnings, Sep 30, 2026). TrendForce has conventional DRAM contract prices +90–95% QoQ in Q1 2026 and +58–63% in Q2 — this is a shortage being priced in quarters, not a one-off print.
- **Irreplaceability:** Low risk — no hyperscaler can in-source memory fabrication (multi-billion-dollar fabs, EUV lithography, years of JEDEC/Nvidia qualification), and only three firms on earth make DRAM at scale. The real risk is share, not disintermediation: Micron is #3 in HBM (18%) and its HBM4 share is estimated at just 5–20% vs SK Hynix's ~70% of Nvidia's initial HBM4 orders, and Samsung's HBM4 ramp is the live competitive threat.
- **Catalyst & timing:** The FQ1 FY27 print, tentatively Dec 23, 2026 (guide ~$61.5B revenue, ~$38.15 adjusted EPS) — the next hard test of whether contract pricing holds into the new fiscal year; HBM4 qualification wins would be the second leg.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~$1,100 (+41%) — contract pricing holds, FQ1 guide is met, the market re-rates from 3.7x toward even a still-skeptical ~6–7x on FY27 earnings. Bull ~$1,500 (+93%) — HBM4 share stabilizes, the 2030 balance call holds, and the market accepts memory as a contracted oligopoly, toward the $1,561.91 analyst mean target.
- **Top risk (disintermediation case):** The cycle turns — memory is the most cyclical input in the AI stack, and a capex pause (cf. the SemiAnalysis accelerator-capex cut, Nov 2026) hits memory first; the "E" in 3.7x can shrink faster than the multiple can expand. Secondary: HBM share loss to Samsung in HBM4, the Fab 11E labor dispute delaying 2028 capacity, and the DRAM class action (Nov 14 refusal to dismiss).
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
