# Ballot B1 — Growth/Momentum Investor — RUN_DATE 2026-11-15

## SINGLE-PICK ballot

- **Top pick:** APH
- **Runner-up:** ANET
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Amphenol is the fastest compounder in this set on the numbers that matter to this lens: revenue growth 49.7% TTM, Q3 sales +65% (+37% organic), adjusted EPS +74% with a beat, and guidance raised to FY2026 sales of $36.9–37.1bn and adjusted EPS of $2.82–2.84. The acceleration is demand-led, not financial engineering — IT datacom is 48% of sales with AI roughly three-quarters of that, and orders are growing even faster than sales. At 21.6x forward earnings it is also the cheapest of the three hyper-growers (ANET 32.7x, APP 19.6x but with no shortage), so the growth is not yet priced as expensively as its rate deserves. This is the one name where revenue acceleration, estimate beats, guidance raises and a genuine order surge all point the same way at once.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Record Q3 orders of $12.17bn, +99% year-on-year and +13% sequentially, with a book-to-bill of 1.19 — orders nearly doubling while sales grew 65% means demand is still outrunning Amphenol's ability to ship it.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — Amphenol is the world's largest connector maker by revenue and its moat is breadth, scale and qualification depth, not proprietary technology; TE Connectivity, Molex, Foxconn and Luxshare make equivalents, hyperscalers dual-source deliberately and set the specs, and Nvidia's 2028 Kyber shift is a real architectural risk. But no hyperscaler in-sources precision high-speed interconnect manufacturing at this volume, and qualification across thousands of SKUs is slow to replicate — so the risk is share/price pressure, not outright bypass. It is the weakest moat among the top growers, which is why conviction is capped below 9.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base +25–35% (toward the $106.97 target, +36.7%, as the raised FY2026 guide compounds and the order book converts); bull +50–60% if AI capex stays at this pace, orders stay near book-to-bill above 1.1 and the multiple re-rates from 21.6x toward the high-20s on sustained 40%+ growth.
- **Top risk:** The AI capex cycle rolls over or pauses — the backlog is cancellable, the business is standards-based and multi-sourced, and a hyperscaler capex cut would hit orders (which lead sales) first and fastest.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

### Top 3 in depth

**1. APH —** The single most compelling data point is the order print itself: $12.17bn of Q3 orders, +99% year-on-year against $10.23bn of sales, a 1.19 book-to-bill with IT datacom at 48% of sales and AI roughly three-quarters of that — demand is accelerating ahead of an already 65%-growing revenue line, and management raised the full-year guide and the dividend 20% alongside a new $4bn buyback. Base case 12–18 months is +25–35% toward the $106.97 target as the backlog converts; bull case is +50–60% if AI interconnect demand holds near this pace and the 21.6x forward multiple re-rates.

**2. ANET —** The most compelling point is the guide, not the quarter: Q3 revenue +58.5% year-on-year, the highest growth since IPO with a ~10.8% beat of its own guide, followed by a 2027 outlook of ~$18.5bn revenue (+38–40%) with AI fabrics doubling to ~$7.0bn, backed by RPO of $8.6bn (+41% in nine months). That is a second hyper-growth leg, but the moat is thinner than the growth — Arista has lost the #1 datacenter Ethernet revenue spot to Nvidia, depends on Broadcom switch silicon with no written supply guarantee, and its top customers run white-box SONiC/FBOSS. Base case 12–18 months is +20–30% toward the $249.65 target (+26.5%); bull case is +40–50% if AI fabrics double as guided and supply constraints ease.

**3. APP —** The most compelling point is pricing power from the model itself: net revenue per install +55% year-on-year while install volume rose only ~2%, with Q3 revenue +55%, adjusted EBITDA of $1.84bn at an 84.3% margin and FCF of $1.42bn, and Q4 guidance raised to $2.365–2.395bn (+43–44%). But this lens must dock it hard on doctrine: shortage is 2/10 — there is no physical scarcity, only a better ad-targeting model — and the edge is a model that a rival model or a platform policy change can route around. Base case 12–18 months is +15–25% as e-commerce spend compounds; bull case is +50% if Axon model gains keep landing and e-commerce keeps growing triple digits, with the warning that the stock fell ~65% in 2026 before the November pop and beta is 2.53.

### Left out of the top 10

AME, CF, MSI, VRTX — AME is a quality compounder but a portfolio-average 11.6% grower whose organic order growth is decelerating (25% → 22% → 17% across Q1–Q3) and whose Indicor leverage jumped to 2.1–2.3x EBITDA; CF is a war-driven cyclical spike with FY2027 EPS expected to shrink ~24% and no structural re-rating; MSI is a steady ~5% organic grower whose Q3 growth was ~7pp acquisition-bought; VRTX is a 9.1% grower whose CF monopoly serves a fixed ~90,000-patient population, so the upside is a binary November 30 PDUFA coin-flip, not compounding growth.
