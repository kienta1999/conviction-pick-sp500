- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — Hyperscalers (NVIDIA, Apple, Google) have large internal CAD teams but buy Cadence/Synopsys tools and add internal flows on top; nobody has replicated a full-flow production-grade EDA stack, and a 2nm chip cannot be taped out without a TSMC/Samsung-qualified flow that takes years to qualify, with 30+ years of accumulated process-design kits, foundry-certified flows and verification IP behind it. Switching costs are existential — ripping out a design flow mid-program risks a tape-out — which is why retention is near-100%. The live threat is AI designing chips autonomously (Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 episode, Jul 17, 2026, when CDNS fell 9–10%), but Cadence's answer is to own that shift via Maestro and 100+ AI Super Agent customer engagements, rather than being bypassed by it.

Bull ~+40–50% if Maestro/Super Agents accelerate renewals and expansions and the AI design-capacity bottleneck keeps backlog growing in what should be a heavier renewal period.
- **Top risk:** Valuation — ~39x FY2026 non-GAAP EPS guidance ($8.40) — so any AI-capex air pocket hits the multiple first; plus China export controls (management's outlook depends on regulations remaining substantially unchanged) and a repeat of the "AI replaces EDA" narrative.
