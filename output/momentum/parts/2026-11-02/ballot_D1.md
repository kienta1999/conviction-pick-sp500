# Panelist D — Contrarian / risk skeptic — stock-pick-momentum, run 2026-11-02

## (a) SINGLE-PICK ballot

- Top pick: CDNS
- Runner-up: APH
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Cadence is the shortage thesis the market is not paying a shortage price for. While attention chases sold-out HBM and GPUs, every AI accelerator must first be designed and verified in EDA, and AI-driven design complexity is expanding iterations faster than engineering headcount — a bottleneck that cannot be solved by ordering more hardware. Cadence has just delivered a third consecutive beat-and-raise, raised FY2026 guidance to $6.387–$6.417bn (~21% growth), and sits on a record backlog, yet its 12-month return in the screen is −3.97% and it has de-rated to ~32× FY27 EPS versus 40–45× historically. The explosive leg is not scarcity pricing but a pricing-model shift: agentic AI workflows increase parallel engine iterations rather than cannibalising seat licences, so consumption, not seats, could re-rate a toll road the whole industry must use.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Record $8.2bn backlog at 30 Sep 2026, of which $4.2bn of remaining performance obligations is expected as revenue in the next 12 months — about 2.5× Q4 2026 revenue guidance ($1.695–$1.725bn), so, as Morgan Stanley noted in the dossier, essentially all of Q4 revenue is already covered in backlog.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — hyperscalers designing their own chips are Cadence customers, not competitors: they buy Cadence/Synopsys tools to do it and do not build competing EDA suites, and ripping out a foundry-qualified flow mid-design risks tape-out failure. The long-tail threat is LLMs/agentic AI commoditising parts of design, but Cadence's answer is to sell the agents itself (Maestro, ChipStack, InnoStack), with >100 AI Super Agent customer engagements already.
- 12-18 month return scenario: Base — a move toward the analyst mean target of $411.76 (screen price $342.75), supported by ~21% FY2026 growth guidance and backlog conversion, with recurring revenue (+18% YoY in Q3 2026) cushioning any single-quarter wobble. Bull — above that target if agentic consumption pricing lands at scale and the market re-rates Cadence back toward its historical 40–45× multiple range on continued beat-and-raise execution.
- Top risk: The LLM-disruption narrative for EDA has not shown up in the numbers yet but remains the long-tail bear case, compounded nearer-term by ~15% of FY2026 guidance from China (export-control reversal risk) and Hexagon-related margin dilution (non-GAAP margin 44.1% versus 47.6% a year earlier).
- Conviction (1-10): 7
