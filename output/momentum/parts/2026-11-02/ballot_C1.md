# Panelist C — Quality / moat & irreplaceability — stock-pick-momentum, run 2026-11-02

## (a) SINGLE-PICK ballot

- Top pick: CDNS
- Runner-up: NVDA
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Cadence is the toll road on the AI build-out that hyperscalers cannot route around: every AI accelerator, GPU and chiplet must be designed and verified in EDA before tape-out, and rising design complexity is expanding iterations rather than shrinking them. It sits in a two-player duopoly with Synopsys (about 36% share versus Synopsys at about 38% in the dossier's summaries), with near-100% retention cited for the duopoly, recurring revenue up 18% year over year in Q3 2026, and a record backlog that already covers near-term revenue. Crucially, hyperscaler in-sourcing helps rather than hurts Cadence — Google, Microsoft, Meta and Amazon design their own chips but buy Cadence tools to do it. The explosive leg is agentic-AI consumption pricing on top of that locked-in base, plus record Palladium/Protium emulation hardware, rather than a cyclical price spike.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Record $8.2bn backlog at 30 September 2026, of which $4.2bn of remaining performance obligations is expected as revenue in the next 12 months — about 2.5x Q4 2026 revenue guidance ($1.695–$1.725bn), so essentially all of Q4 revenue was already covered in backlog at the print.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — hyperscalers design their own chips but do not build competing EDA suites, and in-house point tools have never displaced the duopoly at the leading edge; ripping out a foundry-qualified flow mid-design risks tape-out failure after 30+ years of PDK integration. The genuine substitute threat is long-tail only: LLMs/agentic AI commoditising parts of design, which Cadence is answering by selling the agents itself (Maestro, ChipStack, InnoStack, with over 100 AI Super Agent engagements).
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case: Base — backlog conversion plus roughly 21% FY2026 guided revenue growth carries the stock broadly toward the post-print analyst targets cited in the dossier ($400–$425, versus $342.75 in the screen), roughly +17–24%. Bull — agentic consumption pricing and another record emulation year accelerate recurring growth above guide, pushing returns materially above those targets, roughly +40% or more over 12–18 months.
- Top risk: The LLM-disruption narrative for EDA, even though it has not shown up in numbers yet; compounded by China exposure at about 15% of FY2026 guide (export-control reversal risk) and Hexagon D&E integration diluting non-GAAP margin to 44.1% from 47.6% a year earlier.
- Conviction (1-10): 8

### Top 3 detail

**1. CDNS:** The single most compelling moat data point is the record $8.2bn backlog with $4.2bn due in the next 12 months, built on multi-year recurring arrangements in a duopoly where switching risks tape-out failure — that is contracted, locked-in demand, not a spot-price bet. Rough 12–18 month scenario: base roughly +17–24% toward the $400–$425 post-print targets; bull roughly +40% or more if agentic-AI consumption pricing compounds on top of the backlog.

**2. NVDA:** The single most compelling data point is CFO visibility to $0.5 trillion in Blackwell and Rubin revenue from the start of 2026 through end of calendar 2026, backed by $279bn of supply/purchase commitments at 26 July 2026 and CEO language that cloud GPUs are sold out and capacity is the constraint. Rough 12–18 month scenario: base — Rubin shipments and the $108bn Q3 FY2027 guide keep growth compounding, with the screen's analyst mean target implying about +61% upside from $204.64, tempered by margin compression toward 71–72%; bull — capacity deals delivering over 300% more interposer capacity by end-2028 sustain share above 80% and push returns well above that target, while the bear path is the receivables/guarantee financing loop and inference share loss to custom silicon.

**3. LLY:** The single most compelling moat data point is prescription dominance — Mounjaro at 51% of US incretin-class prescriptions in Q1 2026 (up from 39% a year earlier) and Lilly at 60.9% of US incretin-analog prescriptions by June 2026, with combined Mounjaro + Zepbound revenue above $16bn in Q3 2026 alone and FY2026 guidance raised to $91–$92bn. Rough 12–18 month scenario: base — volume-led compounding in the mid-teens to mid-20s percent range as Foundayo (oral orforglipron) rolls out globally in 2027, despite realised prices already down 15% year over year in Q3; bull — roughly +35% or more if the oral leg and retatrutide (Phase 3 complete, US filing planned Q1 2027) extend the ladder faster than Novo's planned 2027 price cuts compress the umbrella.
