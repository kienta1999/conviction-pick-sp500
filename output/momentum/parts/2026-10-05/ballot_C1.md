## (a) Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** CDNS
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** CDNS is the one name where the moat is genuinely uncopyable: one half of a ~74%-share EDA duopoly with near-100% retention, foundry-mandated tool flows, and record USD 8.1B backlog at ~80% recurring revenue. You cannot tape out a modern AI chip without it — and nobody, hyperscaler included, can in-source leading-edge EDA at 3nm and below. Demand is structural (design costs up ~12x from 28nm to 3nm; Palladium/Emulation hardware at all-time-high demand), the margin durability is software-grade, and the stock sits ~25-30% below its USD 416.69 high — a momentum-recovery setup into Oct 26 earnings, not a chase at the top.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Record USD 8.1B backlog (Q2 2026) with USD 4.2B recognized within 12 months and ~80% recurring revenue; foundries (TSMC A14/N2P certification, Sep 2026) specify the tool flow, so the revenue is contractual, not cyclical hope.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low. Dossier scores it 10/10 — no customer in-sources EDA at 3nm and below; switching costs are existential (a failed tape-out); foundry lock-in. The only watch-item is agentic-AI interface disintermediation (Synopsys/OpenAI parallel, Kimi K3 scare) and 3-yr federal probation to ~Jul 2028 — real but not routing-around-the-product, and the thesis here is priced accordingly, ~30% off highs.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: ~+18-25% as the USD 8.1B backlog converts, two prior guide raises confirm the ~19% FY2026 growth path, and the stock re-rates back toward its USD 416.69 high. Bull: ~+35-50% if emulation-hardware mix and agentic-AI engine-consumption narrative re-expand the multiple toward duopoly peers.
- **Top risk:** Federal probation/export-control overhang (to ~Jul 2028) restricting China revenue; PEG ~3.52 and fwd P/E ~29x leave no room for a guide cut; Hexagon D&E acquisition dilutes 2026 EPS USD 0.28.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
