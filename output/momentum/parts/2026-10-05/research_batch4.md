## FCX — Freeport-McMoRan Inc.

| Question | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| Shortage thesis | **Strong structural shortage case.** Analysts project a global refined copper deficit of ~330 kmt in 2026 (J.P. Morgan; multiple ainvest/industry pieces, 2026), driven by supply disruptions + sluggish mine growth (2026 mine supply growth est. only ~+1.4%). Drivers: AI datacenter buildout (AI copper demand +8–10% YoY: transformers, cooling, cabling), EVs (~6x copper vs ICE), renewables, grid upgrades, plus copper's USGS "critical mineral" designation and US tariffs creating a domestic premium. Benchmark via Fastmarkets: Grasberg disruption alone could erase 600,000 tons of supply by end-2026, pushing the market into a 400,000-ton deficit. Copper hit record highs above `$6.00/lb`; J.P. Morgan sees `$12,500/mt` in Q2 2026, ~`$12,075/mt` avg for the year. Long-term: ~10 Mt supply gap projected by 2040. |
| Backlog / order book | N/A — miners don't carry an order backlog; the "book" is the copper price. Realized `$6.17/lb` in Q2 2026, +36% YoY; gold `$4,520/oz`, +37% YoY (Reuters, 7/23/2026). Q3 production expected "significantly higher than sales" as concentrate ships to the new Manyar smelter — inventory is being staged for refined sales in Q4 2026/2027. |
| Category position | World's largest publicly traded copper producer. Owns stakes in 10 copper mines incl. three of the world's largest: ~49% of Grasberg (world's #2 copper mine, #1 gold mine), ~55% of Cerro Verde (Peru), ~72% of Morenci (Arizona). More copper-pure-play than BHP/Rio (iron ore/coal-diluted); cited as the "marginal price setter" for US/Indonesian markets. |
| Irreplaceability | Moat = resource scarcity + capital intensity + permitting timescale (new mines take ~a decade; declining ore grades). Geopolitical moat: dominant US footprint preferred for secure domestic supply chains; Indonesia downstreaming satisfied via the completed Manyar Smelter (2025). In-sourcing is effectively impossible — no customer can build a copper mine on demand. Substitution (aluminum in some wiring) exists but copper's conductivity makes it irreplaceable in motors/transformers. **Irreplaceability score: 9/10** — geology + decade-long lead times + permitting walls. |
| Recent catalysts | 7/23/2026 Q2 beat: adjusted EPS `$0.74` vs `$0.59` est. (+25%), revenue `$7.03B` vs `$6.71B` est.; net income `$984M`. 2026 unit net cash costs lowered to `$1.90/lb` from `$1.95`. Morenci mining rates 30% above 5-yr avg; US ops generated 2.4x more operating income YoY (1H26). Grasberg Block Cave phased restart on track: production rates doubled Apr→Jun (34k→69k t/day); target ~65% capacity H2 2026, 80% by mid-2027, full by end-2027. Formally applied to extend Grasberg permit beyond 2041 (decision hoped by end-2026). Q1 2026: stock cratered 13% on ramp-up timeline cut (H2-2026 target reduced from 85% to ~65% of capacity) — overhang now largely known. |
| Risks | Execution risk at Grasberg (block cave mining is complex; any setback pushes the Q4 volume surge into 2027). Earnings currently benefit from high prices on depressed volumes — "earning more while selling less" reverses as volumes normalize, so forward growth optics get harder. Extreme cyclicality: FCX is a leveraged commodity bet; a demand shock (China slowdown, recession) would hit price AND volume. Q2 unit costs `$1.97/lb` vs `$1.13` a year earlier — fixed-cost deleverage while volumes are low. Indonesia government is majority owner of PTFI — sovereign/political risk around the 2041 extension. |
| Shortage score | **9/10** — present-tense global deficit, own flagship mine offline tightening the market further, structural demand (AI/EV/grid) against a decade-long supply response. |
| Verdict | The purest structural-shortage equity in the batch: a genuine 2026 copper deficit amplified by its own Grasberg outage — explosive upside if the ramp holds, but the thesis is the commodity, not the company, and execution risk is real. |

### FCX INVENTORY_BUILD flag (inv/rev divergence 0.2038; CFO/NI 2.00) — explained

**Verdict: benign, explained by disclosed mine-to-smelter timing.** CEO Kathleen Quirk told analysts on the Q2 2026 call (7/23/2026): "For the Q3, our production is expected to be significantly higher than sales... We'll start shipping in the Q3 to the new smelter, and it'll take some time to build up that inventory to be able to run consistently in the new smelter... We do expect to build some inventory in the Q3 and make up some of that in the Q4." The ~100M lbs copper / 50k oz gold held as smelter inventory in 2026 converts to refined sales in 2027 — a deferral that depresses 2026 sales slightly and adds to 2027 revenue. Concentrate must flow through PTFI's smelting before sale, so this is textbook copper-mining inventory dynamics (concentrate in transit + smelter pipeline fill), not earnings running ahead of cash. CFO/NI 2.00 actually shows cash flow RUNNING AHEAD of net income (`$2B` quarterly operating cash flow vs `$984M` net income), the opposite of an earnings-quality problem.

| Category position | Clear #1 in US nitrogen: 39% of US domestic ammonia production capacity vs Nutrien 16% (farmdoc daily, Univ. of Illinois, May 2026). Globally a top-tier pure-play nitrogen producer alongside Yara and Nutrien, but the margin leader in nitrogen (per 2026 industry deep-dives). `$200`–`$400/ton` cost advantage over Yara on European gas costs. |
