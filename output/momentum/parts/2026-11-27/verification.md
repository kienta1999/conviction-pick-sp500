## CBOE — Cboe Global Markets (\$351.80)

### 1. Volumes — CONFIRMED

Cboe September 2026 trading-volume release (5 Oct 2026, PR Newswire): "Quarterly SPX zero-days-to-expiry (0DTE) ADV record of 3.2 million contracts" and quarterly SPX options ADV of 4.9 million contracts. Cboe Q3 2026 earnings call (30 Oct 2026, transcript via Motley Fool / StockAnalysis): "Index options led the way, with third quarter average daily volume rising 24% year-over-year to 6.1 million contracts" and "0DTE activity made up a record 65% of overall SPX activity." Zacks (30 Oct 2026): "SPX 0DTE average daily volume reached a record 3.2 million contracts, up 34%."

Note: the +34% YoY figure for SPX 0DTE ADV comes from press coverage of the print (Zacks), not from the Cboe volume release itself, which states the 3.2M record without the YoY rate. All four numbers check out.

### 2. Exclusive supply — CONFIRMED

Cboe / S&P DJI joint press release (29 Sep 2026, PR Newswire / Cboe IR): "Cboe will continue to have the exclusive rights to offer trading in its flagship S&P 500® Index (SPX) options." Q3 2026 earnings call (30 Oct 2026): "our exclusivity continues across the S&P options products we offer today, including Mini-SPX, VIX, and S&P binary options."

Note: the exclusivity covers standardized, exchange-traded SPX options in the US. Close substitutes that trade elsewhere (SPY/QQQ ETF options, Nasdaq-100 options, OTC variance swaps, futures) exist and are already acknowledged in the dossier; they do not replicate listed SPX options.

### 3. Catalyst + date — CONFIRMED

Licence extension: Master License Agreement effective 28 Sep 2026, announced 29 Sep 2026 (Cboe press release 29 Sep 2026; Form 8-K filed 29 Sep 2026, accession 0001104659-26-111989). The dossier's "signed on 28–29 Sep 2026" is accurate — effective 28th, announced 29th. Q3 2026 results: Cboe press release / SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (30 Oct 2026): "Net Revenue for the Quarter of \$712.2 million, Up 18 percent" and "Adjusted Diluted EPS for the Quarter of \$3.63, Up 36 percent."

### 4. Moat / licence to 2051 — CONFIRMED

The 8-K filing records the Master License Agreement as "effective September 28, 2026 and running until December 31, 2051" (SEC Form 8-K, 29 Sep 2026; corroborated by FMI World Newsroom, 10 Oct 2026, citing the filing). The index is S&P DJI intellectual property and the licence is exclusive, so no customer can list its own standardized SPX options market — the dossier's reasoning holds, with the same scope caveat as claim 2 (US exchange-traded standardized options).

## EMR — Emerson Electric (\$157.23)

### 5. Backlog / funnel — CONFIRMED

Emerson Q4 FY2026 earnings release and call (4 Nov 2026): "Backlog ended the quarter at \$8.2 billion, up 11% year-over-year, and our book-to-bill was 1.01" (call transcript, MarketBeat / Backpack); Zacks (4 Nov 2026): "Backlog, excluding AspenTech, increased 11% to \$8.2 billion"; "The company's project funnel reached \$13 billion, increasing \$600 million sequentially" and +17% YoY (TradingKey / TipRanks summaries of the call).

### 6. Shortage-driver orders/sales — CONFIRMED

Emerson Q4 FY2026 call (4 Nov 2026): "Underlying orders grew 14% in the fourth quarter"; "Test and measurement orders growth of 14% ... with semiconductor up 59%"; "Growth was strongest in LNG, which was up 68% ... Growth was also robust in semiconductor, up 43%, and power, up 24%."

Note — metric mix, as worded in the claim: the +59% is semiconductor *orders within Test & Measurement* (the NI test business only), while semiconductor *sales* grew +43%; LNG +68% and power +24% are *sales* growth by end market in Q4, not order rates. The claim's numbers are all correct, but the dossier should not present the +59% order rate and the +68%/+24% sales rates as if they were the same kind of metric.

### 7. Catalyst + date — CONFIRMED

Emerson 8-K / press release (4 Nov 2026): adjusted EPS \$1.86, +15% YoY; revenue \$5.20B, +7% YoY, beating the \$5.12B consensus (Zacks / Nasdaq, 4 Nov 2026). FY2027 guidance from the same release: "net sales growth of ~5% and underlying sales growth of ~5% ... adjusted earnings per share of ~\$7.25" (+10% vs FY2026 adjusted EPS of \$6.59).

### 8. Moat / DCS share — CONFIRMED

Verified Market Research (Feb 2026, Q1 2026 Market Intelligence Report): "While their overall market share sits at 6.7%, Emerson owns nearly 29% of the Oil & Gas DCS niche," describing DeltaV as the platform behind that position.

Note — source quality: the ~29% figure comes from a single market-research vendor and is definition-sensitive; other vendors size Emerson's oil-DCS position differently (e.g. Dataintelo puts Emerson at ~12.5% of the oil DCS market). "DeltaV is the standard in process industries" and "switching costs measured in years" are qualitative characterizations, not quantified company disclosures — directionally supported by Emerson's installed base and the safety-certification/validation burden of replacing a DCS, but not precisely sourced.

## ISRG — Intuitive Surgical (\$427.18)

### 9. Placements / installed base — CONFIRMED

Intuitive Q3 2026 earnings release, SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (20 Oct 2026): "The Company placed 546 da Vinci surgical systems, compared with 427 in the third quarter of 2025" (+28% YoY); "included 315 da Vinci 5 systems"; "grew its da Vinci surgical system installed base to 12,094 systems as of September 30, 2026, an increase of 12%."

### 10. Shortage-driver procedures — CONFIRMED

Same release (20 Oct 2026): "Worldwide procedures (da Vinci and Ion combined) grew approximately 15% ... Da Vinci procedures grew approximately 14%, and Ion procedures grew approximately 31%"; CEO Dave Rosa: customers used the systems "to perform, on average, over 10,000 procedures per day throughout the period." No capacity-sold-out, lead-time or supply-constraint disclosure was found; the growth is demand-led, as the dossier states.

### 11. Catalyst + date — CONFIRMED

Q3 2026 reported 20 Oct 2026 (SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1): revenue \$3.05B, +22% YoY; non-GAAP EPS \$3.00 vs \$2.65 consensus (TradingView / GuruFocus, 20–21 Oct 2026; Zacks: adjusted EPS \$3.00, beat by 13.6%). Per-procedure pricing cut: CFO Jamie Samath on the Q3 call (20 Oct 2026, via StockRow transcript summary): starting in H1 2027 Intuitive will extend the useful lives of selected instruments and introduce volume-based pricing, which "together ... lower da Vinci instruments-and-accessories revenue per procedure by approximately 3% to 5% once fully implemented, holding mix constant."

Note: the 3–5% is the *fully implemented* effect and also includes a China policy-driven price cut beginning November 2026; the US extended-use/volume-pricing rollout is the H1 2027 piece. The dossier's "from H1 2027" is correct for the US programme.

### 12. Moat shares / surgeon training — CONFIRMED

~60%+ of surgical robotics: Precedence Research estimate, cited by DeepVal moat analysis and The Teardown (Jun 2026): "Intuitive holds more than 60% of robotic-assisted soft-tissue surgery." ~83% of US robotic urology: analyst estimate cited by The Teardown (Jun 2026): "about 190,000 of roughly 230,000 annual U.S. procedures (~83%)." 18,000+ surgeons on da Vinci 5: Intuitive Q3 2026 call (20 Oct 2026, via Nasdaq / TipRanks): "more than 2,000 da Vinci 5 systems installed worldwide, used by over 18,000 surgeons since launch."

Note — wording: the company says surgeons have *used* da Vinci 5, not that 18,000 were *trained* on it; the dossier's "trained" overstates the company's wording slightly. Also, the 60% and 83% figures are third-party/analyst estimates, not company disclosures, and definitions vary — treat them as estimates, not audited shares.
