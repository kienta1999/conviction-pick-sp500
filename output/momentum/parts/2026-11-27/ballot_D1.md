- **Top risk:** Orders are long-cycle and slow to convert — the share of backlog expected to convert within 12 months already slipped to 70% from about 75% — so a data-centre or LNG final-investment-decision slowdown would hit the funnel before it ever becomes revenue, and FY2027 guidance already concedes that risk.
