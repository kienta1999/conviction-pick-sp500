Voted off `research_dossier_2026-09-22.md` only. Lens: revenue acceleration, earnings revisions, price momentum — the fastest compounder wins, even if the moat is thinner.

## Ballot 1 — single pick

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** APH
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron is the steepest growth inflection in the dossier: Q3 FY2026 revenue $41.46B (+346% YoY) at 84.9% gross margin, with HBM sold out through CY2027 and no new fab output until FY2028. Demand is so strong customers are handing it cash upfront — ~$100B minimum contracted revenue plus $22B in customer prepayments — which is the strongest possible revisions signal: every downstream estimate has to ratchet toward that contracted floor. Price momentum confirms it (+250% YTD). This lens wants demand acceleration, and MU is compounding faster than any other name in the batch.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** $22B of customer prepayments — cash deposited before silicon ships. That is the single most compelling demand proof in the dossier: customers are financing Micron's fabs to secure allocation.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — hyperscalers cannot build HBM themselves (dossier rates in-sourcing threat LOW, irreplaceability 8), but the lead is explicitly temporal: Samsung is qualifying HBM4 and SK Hynix is neck-and-neck. The moat is time, not permanence.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base +40–60% as the contracted floor converts to recognized revenue through FY2028; bull +80–100%+ if HBM4 pricing holds and margin mix keeps expanding (consensus keeps chasing revisions).
- **Top risk:** Priced for perfection after +250% YTD. Memory is historically cyclical — any HBM4 share loss, demand air pocket, or capacity response collapses the premium multiple fast.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
