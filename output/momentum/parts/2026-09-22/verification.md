## NVDA (NVIDIA)

8. **CEO Jensen Huang: combined Blackwell + Rubin order backlog of ~$1T extending through 2027 (stated ~GTC 2026)** — **CONFIRMED** (with nuance on wording).
   - Devstyler (GTC 2026, San Jose, March 16, 2026): Huang said he now sees "at least $1 trillion" in orders for Blackwell and Vera Rubin through 2027, up from ~$500B a year earlier (https://devstyler.io/blog/2026/03/17/jensen-huang-nvidia-sees-1-trillion-in-ai-demand/).

9. **"Meeting only 70% of the supply needed" / demand much higher (Q2 FY2027 call, Aug 26, 2026)** — **CONFIRMED in substance, with a precision caveat.**
   - Motley Fool transcript of the Aug 26, 2026 call: management guided fiscal 2028 revenue growth of ~70% YoY, explicitly a "supply-constrained outlook." Huang: "Even though our demand is much greater than 70%, our supply allows us to confidently deliver 70%." Also: "at this moment, we have supply for 70%... Our demand is much higher than that." An analyst summarized the gap as ~100% unconstrained demand growth vs. ~70% fulfillable (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/31/nvidia-nvda-q2-2027-earnings-call-transcript/).
   - Caveat: the claim as phrased ("meeting only 70% of the supply needed to fulfill customer orders") is a loose paraphrase. The actual quote is about being able to deliver ~70% *revenue growth* in FY2028 while demand is "much greater." It is not literally a statement that 70% of orders get filled. The direction (demand > supply) is right; the framing is imprecise.
   - Call facts cross-checked: Q2 FY2027 revenue $96.2B (+106% YoY, ended July 26, 2026), Data Center $89.0B, both GAAP and non-GAAP gross margin 75.0% — matches NVIDIA newsroom/SEC 8-K PR (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000073/q2fy27pr.htm).
