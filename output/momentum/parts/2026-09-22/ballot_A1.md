## BALLOT 1 — SINGLE PICK

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** NVDA
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron is the single name in the dossier with the hardest, most contracted supply-constrained demand: a ~$100B minimum contracted revenue floor backed by $22B in customer prepayments, HBM sold out through CY2027, and no new fab output until FY2028. It is fulfilling only half to two-thirds of HBM demand — demand is so far ahead of supply that customers are paying years in advance to reserve capacity. The Q3 FY2026 print ($41.46B revenue, +346% YoY, 84.9% gross margin) shows the shortage is monetizing at record prices. With the AI memory bottleneck still binding for 12-18 months, that contracted revenue converts into earnings with near-zero demand risk.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** ~$100B minimum contracted revenue floor + $22B in customer cash prepayments; HBM sold out through CY2027 with no new fab output until FY2028 (June 24 call). Customers don't prepay $22B unless the shortage is real.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** LOW — hyperscalers cannot build their own HBM (dossier rates trap exposure LOW). The one caveat through this lens: the lead on HBM4 is temporal (Samsung will qualify; SK Hynix neck-and-neck), so this is a duration edge, not a permanent moat.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+20-30% as the contracted backlog converts into shipments at record pricing and HBM remains sold out through 2027. Bull ~+50-60% if AI capex holds through 2027 and memory pricing stays at record levels into the FY2028 capacity additions.
- **Top risk:** Priced for perfection — stock +250% YTD and the dossier flags cycle-top markers: pricing power can flip fast in memory, and Samsung HBM4 qualification plus new fab supply by FY2028 could normalize the shortage premium. This is a bet the supply stays tight for the full 12-18 months.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 detail (single most compelling shortage/backlog data point + 12-18mo scenarios)

**1. MU.** The $22B in customer prepayments is the dossier's single most credible shortage signal — customers committing cash years in advance to reserve HBM capacity, with Micron fulfilling only half to two-thirds of demand and no new fab output until FY2028. ~$100B minimum contracted revenue means 2026-27 revenue is largely pre-sold. 12-18mo: base ~+20-30% as the backlog converts at record pricing; bull ~+50-60% if HBM stays sold out and AI capex holds through 2027.

**2. NVDA.** ~$1T Blackwell+Rubin backlog through 2027 while the supply chain "runs flat out" and meets only ~70% of order demand (Aug 26 call) — the largest order-book deficit in the dossier, with 80–85% share and a CUDA moat. 12-18mo: base ~+15-25% (21x forward EPS looks "undemanding if durable"); bull ~+40-50% on clean Blackwell/Rubin conversion with no capex digestion pause. Through the supply-chain lens, the concrete risk is the trap axis the dossier flags: hyperscaler custom-ASIC in-sourcing of inference is in progress and share has eroded 92%→80–85%.

**3. MSFT.** The dossier's cleanest admission of a hard shortage: "customer demand continues to outpace available compute capacity" (CFO, multi-quarter), with $678B RPO (+84% YoY), ~$80B in unfulfilled Azure orders, and GPUs delivered but un-installable for lack of power. Shortage score 10/10 — the single highest shortage print in the batch. 12-18mo: base ~+12-18% as capacity additions monetize the queued RPO; bull ~+25-30% if the market reprices the duration of the capacity-constrained runway. Mega-cap math is the ceiling on "explosive" — this is durability, not a 50% runner, which is why it ranks behind MU and NVDA on this momentum panel.
