## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** AMAT

**Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron sits atop the tightest memory market since ~2017 — its entire 2026 HBM output is sold out under multi-year contracts booked into 2027, and it can supply only ~50-66% of key customers' medium-term needs. Yet the market values it at 6.5x forward earnings with +44% analyst upside remaining — the earnings repricing hasn't happened despite +280% YTD. The $100B in take-or-pay minimum-revenue floors plus ~$22B in customer deposits through 2030 is not a demand signal, it's a demand *contract*: customers are prepaying to guarantee supply. This converts the classic memory-cycle downside the skeptic fears into contracted floors.

**Key shortage/backlog evidence:** 16 strategic take-or-pay agreements, 14 carrying ~$100B minimum revenue floors, ~$22B customer financial commitments (~$18B cash deposits) through 2030 — customers funding their own supplier because allocation, not price, is the constraint.

**Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** MEDIUM (7/10). No hyperscaler builds leading-edge DRAM; only three HBM makers exist. Real threat is Chinese supply (CXMT 4%→~10% DRAM share in a year, LPDDR6 mass production Sep 2026) — medium-term, and commodity-DRAM-concentrated, not HBM.

**12-18 month return scenario:** Base: +44% to analyst mean ~$1,520 as contracted earnings get priced. Bull: HBM4 ramp (2x faster than HBM3E) extends the cycle and multiple re-rates off 6.5x — +70-80% toward ~$1,800-1,900.

**Top risk:** Earnings tomorrow (Sep 30) — the bar is high after +280% YTD and a $1T cap on memory earnings; a guidance wobble or 2027 price-softening confirmation (Citi trim rationale) could take it down 15-20% fast.

**Conviction (1-10):** 8
