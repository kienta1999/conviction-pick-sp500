### Top 3 shortage/backlog notes

**MU:** The single hardest contracted demand in the set: ~$100B in take-or-pay minimum-revenue floors (14 agreements) plus ~$22B in customer financial commitments through 2030, with 2026 HBM entirely sold out and booked into 2027 while Micron can cover only half-to-two-thirds of customer needs. Earnings tomorrow (Sep 30) is the near-term ignition. Base +30-50% / bull +80-100% on 12-18mo horizon.

**MSFT:** $678B commercial backlog is the second-largest contracted revenue pile here, and CFO Hood has said Azure is "short" through at least end of FY27 — after adding 31 DCs and ~1GW in a single quarter, still not enough, with Azure +43% cc guided to ~45%. Enterprises cannot in-source hyperscale GPU clusters. Base +15-25% / bull +40-60% if Azure growth persists and the $175B capex treadmill shows ROI.

**SNDK:** $93.9B in NBM minimum-revenue floors with $16.5B customer deposits averaging 4+ years of duration, and bits "on allocation well beyond 2027" — the purest NAND physical shortage, amplified by rivals reallocating fab capacity from NAND to HBM. Downgrade: NAND is commodity, YMTC at ~14% share and Samsung adding capacity are the disintermediation-by-competition trap. Base +20-35% / bull +60% if the shortage persists into 2028 without price growth stalling.

### Deliberately left out of top 10

- **LLY** — the injectable shortage is over (FDA delisted tirzepatide); formulary lock-in is sticky but not a contracted backlog, and it fails the structural-shortage doctrine.
- **WST** — picks-and-shovels but "not itself in shortage"; no backlog, and oral GLP-1s threaten format substitution.
- **AME** — excellent compounder, but no sold-out/lead-time language; second-order exposure fails the explosive-return geometry.
- **VRTX** — perfect monopoly (irreplaceability 10/10) but zero shortage; monopoly ≠ shortage under this doctrine.
- **CSCO** — supplies into the shortage but is itself the squeezed link: GM −210bps from eating memory costs, and in AI back-ends it is increasingly optional versus Nvidia Spectrum-X/white-box; contracted RPO ($46.7B) exists but the demand-side pricing power runs the other way.
