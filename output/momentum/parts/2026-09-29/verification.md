## MU (Micron Technology)

### Claim 1 — ~$100B take-or-pay minimum revenue, ~$22B customer financial commitments (~$18B cash deposits), through calendar 2030
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

- 16 Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs), structured as take-or-pay with binding multi-year volume commitments. Fourteen of the 16 carry cumulative minimum revenue of approximately $100 billion over the remaining term; ~$22B in cash deposits and related customer financial commitments, including ~$18B in cash deposits. Agreements cover ~20% of DRAM volume and ~30% of NAND volume through calendar 2030.
- Sources: Micron FY26 Q3 earnings call / 10-Q as reported by multiple outlets in Sep 2026 — e.g., mexc.com "Micron (MU) Earnings Preview" (Sep 2026): "fourteen of them carrying cumulative minimum revenue near $100 billion and approximately $22 billion in customer financial commitments, including about $18 billion of cash deposits"; dollarstockclub.com (Sep 28, 2026); stockmoguls.com (Sep 24, 2026).

### Claim 2 — Entire 2026 HBM output sold out under multi-year contracts, booked into 2027; supply meets only ~50–66% of key customers' medium-term requirements
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (with caveat below)

- Entire calendar-2026 HBM output committed under long-term/multi-year contracts; CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said Micron can satisfy only 50–66% of key customers' HBM demand. Multiple outlets also report HBM sold out through 2027.
- Sources: stockmoguls.com (May 2026): "entire 2026 high-bandwidth memory (HBM) output is already committed under long-term contracts... CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said Micron can currently satisfy only 50% to 66% of key customer HBM demand"; ad-hoc-news.de (Sep 2026): "entire HBM output for calendar 2026 is already sold out under multi-year contracts"; fool.com (Sep 23, 2026): "HBM was sold out through 2027 as well."
- Caveat: one outlet (stockmoguls.com, Sep 24, 2026) notes Micron has not formally disclosed "fully booked through 2027" as a company figure — the 2027 extension rests on press/investor-day reporting, not an SEC filing number.

### Claim 3 — Q4 FY26 earnings Sep 30, 2026; consensus ~$51B revenue, ~$31.50 adjusted EPS
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

- Micron reports fiscal Q4 2026 results Wednesday, September 30, 2026 (after close). Consensus ~$51B revenue, ~$31.50 adjusted EPS (vs $3.03 in the year-ago quarter).
- Sources: ad-hoc-news.de (Sep 2026): "consensus calls for adjusted earnings of roughly $31.50 per share, on revenue of about $51 billion" with results on Wednesday, September 30; dollarstockclub.com (Sep 28, 2026): "analysts project about $31.5 per share on roughly $51 billion in revenue when Micron reports fiscal Q4 results on September 30, after the close."

### Claim 7 — CFO Amy Hood said Microsoft remains "short in Azure," constrained through at least the end of FY27 (June 2027)
**Verdict: CONTRADICTED (as dated)**

- The "short in Azure" phrasing traces to the **FY25 Q4 call (July 2025)**, not July 2026: "demand remains higher than supply... we currently expect to remain capacity constrained through the first half of the fiscal year" (fool.com, Aug 4, 2025). Subsequent dated guidance: FY26 Q1 call (Oct 2025) — "capacity constrained through at least the end of our fiscal year" (i.e., **June 2026**, fool.com transcript); FY26 Q3 call (Apr 2026) — "we expect to remain constrained at least through 2026" (fool.com transcript). The July 29, 2026 Q4 call confirms "Customer demand continues to exceed available capacity" (alphastreet transcript) but **no "constrained through end of FY27 (June 2027)" guidance from Hood was found**.
- The writeup must NOT present this as current guidance; the latest dated statement on the record is "at least through 2026."
