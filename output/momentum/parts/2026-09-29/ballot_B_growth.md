## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick: MU**
- **Runner-up: SNDK**

### Thesis (3-5 sentences)
Micron is riding the tightest memory market in a decade with the most explosive momentum geometry in the set: revenue +346% YoY last quarter with +204 percentage points of revenue *acceleration*, 8 straight quarterly EPS beats with a rising surprise trend, and +195% over six months while still trading at just 6.5x forward earnings — earnings tomorrow (Sep 30) are a potential re-rating catalyst with the stock only 13% below its 52-week high after crossing $1,000. The growth is compounding *into* the shortage: 2026 HBM output fully sold out under multi-year contracts, and Micron can supply only ~50-66% of key customers' medium-term needs, which is the classic setup for pricing power to keep accelerating. HBM4 is already over $1B in shipped revenue and ramping 2x faster than HBM3E, so the fastest-growing, highest-margin segment is itself accelerating.

### Key shortage/backlog evidence
16 take-or-pay strategic agreements, 14 carrying ~$100B minimum revenue, plus ~$22B in customer financial commitments (~$18B cash deposits) through 2030 — customers are pre-paying for supply that doesn't exist yet.

### Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: MEDIUM
No hyperscaler builds leading-edge DRAM and there are only three HBM makers, so customer in-sourcing is not a credible threat. The real risk is Chinese supply (CXMT 4%→~10% DRAM share in one year, LPDDR6 mass production Sep 2026) — medium-term and commodity-DRAM-concentrated, not a near-term disintermediation story.

### 12-18 month return scenario
- Base: +60-80% (~$1,700-1,900). HBM shortage persists through 2027, DRAM contract prices stay elevated, EPS normalizes from peak-cycle panic; fwd PE re-rates from 6.5x toward 10x on confirmed take-or-pay durability.
- Bull: +150-200% (~$2,600-3,100). BMO's $1,300 PT is already in sight at +24%; if 2027 DRAM prices hold flat instead of softening as Citi fears, the "peak earnings" thesis breaks and the stock trades like a growth name on 2027 estimates.

### Top risk
Memory is cyclical and $1T+ of market cap now sits on memory earnings — DRAM/NAND price softening by 2027 (the Citi trim rationale) would unwind the multiple and the momentum at once.

### Conviction (1-10): 8.5
