### Top-3 expanded

**MU (#1):** The single most compelling data point is the pair of numbers together: ~$100B in binding multi-year AI memory contracts against an HBM market that is ~40% short through end-2027. That is contracted multi-year revenue visibility no other candidate approaches in absolute contract dollars — not the WFE backlogs ($12–15B), not NAND's RPO ($41.6–42B), not Cisco's RPO ($43.5B) — and it sits on the most supply-constrained component in the AI stack, made by ~3 firms. Base +35–45% on multiple re-rating as contract cash lands through 2027; bull +70–90% if the HBM deficit extends into 2028 and price escalation hits the non-bound volume.

**FCX (#2):** The strongest structural-shortage data point is the multi-year copper deficit — JPM ~330 kmt and UBS ~407 kt for 2026 against only +1.4% mine supply growth, with LME prices at records above $11,400/t and AI datacenters consuming >2x copper per MW. Unlike every semiconductor name here, the moat is geologic and regulatory: no one can in-source a mine, permit one quickly, or capex one cheaply — and FCX adds its own volume ramp (Grasberg to 80% normalized output by mid-2027) into record prices. Base +20–30% on sustained >$6.50/lb realized pricing plus volume growth; bull +50–70% if the deficit tightens further and copper enters a super-cycle repricing. The Grasberg single-asset concentration and Indonesian regulatory risk are real discounts, but they don't change the physics of the shortage.

**LRCX (#3):** The sharpest leading-edge tool monopoly in the panel: >80% share of advanced-node etch with customer lead times doubled to ~12 months and customers booking into 2027+, backed by $2.43B of deferred revenue on customer down payments (a true order proxy) and a record 52% GM quarter on $6.72B revenue (+30% YoY). Etch is the step every new fab must have and almost nobody else can supply at the leading edge. Base +20–30% as the raised CY2026 WFE outlook (low-$150B) flows through; bull +40–50% if leading-edge demand sustains into 2028. Caveats: cyclically exposed (WFE is capex's front line), +126% over 52 weeks with ~32x fwd P/E prices much of this in, and earnings on 2026-10-21 trigger the earnings-halving policy if picked.
