## MU (Micron Technology)

1. **~$100B in binding multi-year AI memory contracts (through end of decade)** — CONFIRMED
   - Micron's own Q4 FY2026 press release (investors.micron.com, Sept 30, 2026) references "16 multi-year strategic customer agreements"; multiple outlets report ~$100B of cumulative minimum-price revenue, take-or-pay, with contractual price floors through 2030 (ainvest.com, Sept 2026; barchart.com, ~Oct 1, 2026). Backed by ~$22B in customer deposits/commitments.
2. **HBM4 (12-Hi) sold out for all of 2026; only ~50–66% of key-customer HBM demand fillable into 2027 (~40% shortfall through end-2027)** — CONFIRMED
   - Sold-out-all-2026: r40.io (Feb 2026, Wolfe conference), The Tech Marketer via machineherald.io (May 2026), newscase.com (June 2026), ainvest.com (Sept 2026) — CEO confirmed 2026 HBM supply 100% sold out under long-term contracts; HBM4 in volume production, ramping ~2x HBM3E pace (barchart.com, ~Oct 1, 2026). 12-Hi confirmed via momoview.com (FQ3 2026 recap) and newscase (bandwidth >2.8 TB/s).
   - 50–66% fill: newscase.com (June 2026) "can meet only 50% to 66% of customer demand"; momoview.com "only 50-66% of demand fillable, tightness beyond 2027"; ainvest.com "the CEO still can only fill some half to two-thirds of what customers are asking for". The "~40% shortfall through end-2027" is the implied midpoint of the reported 50–66% fill range (34–50% shortfall) — a derived figure, not a directly quoted one, but consistent with reported ranges. Tom's Hardware via noah-news.com (June 2026): shortage persists through 2027, easing gradually in 2028.
3. **FQ4 FY2026 (reported Sept 30, 2026): record revenue ~$54.23B with ~87% gross margin** — CONFIRMED
   - Micron's official press release (investors.micron.com, Sept 30, 2026): revenue $54.23B; non-GAAP gross margin 87.0% (GAAP 86.8%). Adjusted EPS $33.42 vs $31.61 consensus.
4. **One of only ~3 firms on earth that can manufacture advanced HBM at scale** — CONFIRMED
   - Repeated across independent sources: SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron — "only three companies on the planet manufacture it at scale" (indmoney.com, ~July 2026; techtimes.com, Oct 9, 2026; bitcoininsider.org; europesays.com/cryptobriefing.com, May 2026). ## AMAT (Applied Materials)

5. **~$15B order backlog, described as booked ~8 quarters out** — CONTRADICTED (as phrased)
   - The $15B figure itself is correct: AMAT's FY2025 10-K (filed Dec 12, 2025) reports total backlog of **$15,002M** as of Oct 26, 2025 ($7,105M Semiconductor Systems + $7,141M AGS + $756M other) — via stocktitan.net.
   - The "booked ~8 quarters out" gloss is wrong. At the current run rate (~$9B/quarter), $15B of backlog covers **roughly two quarters of revenue** (ainvest.com, Aug 2026: "visibility into roughly two quarters of revenue"). The "eight quarters" figure refers to something different: CEO Gary Dickerson asking customers for an **unprecedented eight quarters of order-visibility notice** (finimize.com, ~June 2026), i.e., customers' rolling forecasts eight quarters out (trefis.com, Sept 16, 2026) — not backlog coverage.
6. **Q3 FY2026 (reported Aug 13, 2026): record revenue ~$9.12B (+25% YoY), record adjusted EPS ~$3.50 (+41%)** — CONFIRMED
   - Revenue $9.12B, +24.8% YoY; non-GAAP EPS $3.50 vs $2.48 in the prior-year quarter (+41.1%) (marketbeat.com, americanbankingnews.com, themarketsdaily.com — all reflecting the Aug 13, 2026 report; ainvest.com: "Revenue hit $9.12 billion, up 25% year over year").
