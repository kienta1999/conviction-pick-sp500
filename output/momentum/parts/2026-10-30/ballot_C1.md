# Panelist C — Quality / moat and irreplaceability ballot — 2026-10-30

## Single-pick ballot

- Top pick: ISRG
- Runner-up: WST
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Intuitive is the only name in this dossier whose moat is close to uncopyable: a 25-year FDA-clearance, surgeon-training, and installed-base ecosystem that no customer can build for itself. That moat is now compounding through a product cycle, not just defending an old base — da Vinci 5 accounted for 315 of 546 Q3 2026 placements, Ion procedures rose 31%, and 84% of revenue is recurring instruments, accessories, and service. The upside runway is underpenetration, not a one-quarter price spike: only about 5% of world surgeries and 22% of US surgeries use robotics, against a market growing about 13-15% per year and a US doctor shortfall heading toward 130,000 by 2030. If placements keep converting into procedure volume, the razor-and-blade installed base does the compounding even without a commodity price wave.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Q3 2026 placements of 546 da Vinci systems, up 28% year over year, lifting the da Vinci installed base to 12,094 systems (plus 1,152 Ion) — demand-side scarcity of surgical capacity meeting a fully supplied, dominant platform, rather than a booked backlog figure, which the dossier notes is not disclosed for ISRG.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — hospitals cannot realistically build a surgical robot themselves, switching requires retraining, workflow change, and new capital, and the named challengers are far behind (Medtronic Hugo was cleared in the US only in December 2025 for urology only, with 42 European installs at end-2025 versus Intuitive's 12,094 da Vinci systems; Johnson and Johnson Ottava is still pending). The live substitutes are laparoscopy or open surgery and, eventually, a rival robot — not customer in-sourcing.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base about +10-15%, with procedure volume broadly offsetting the planned per-procedure pricing dilution; bull about +25-35%, if da Vinci 5 and Ion adoption reaccelerate US procedure growth and the January 2027 guidance clears the pricing-dilution fear.
- Top risk: Self-inflicted pricing dilution — a deliberate 3-5% cut to da Vinci instrument/accessory revenue per procedure (extended-life EndoWrist instruments, volume-based pricing from H1 2027, and a China policy-driven price cut from November) — compounded by US da Vinci procedure growth slowing to 10%, the lowest since COVID on the dossier's Wells Fargo citation, and the 450M USD class-action settlement over third-party remanufacturing/servicing.
- Conviction (1-10): 7

Top 3 detail:

ISRG: The single most compelling moat data point is the installed base itself — 12,094 da Vinci systems plus 1,152 Ion systems, with 84% recurring revenue and Q3 2026 placements up 28% — because every new system locks in years of instruments, service, and trained surgeons. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base about +10-15% as volume offsets the 3-5% per-procedure pricing cut; bull about +25-35% if da Vinci 5 adoption and Ion growth reaccelerate procedures and 2027 guidance reassures on pricing.

WST: The single most compelling shortage/moat data point is the compliance lock-in meeting a multi-year wave — nearly 800 Annex 1-related projects in hand, more than 200 HVP upgrade projects added during 2026, GLP-1 HVP revenue growing well into the 30% range organically, and Q3 gross margin expanding 310 basis points to 39.7% on HVP mix and pricing. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base about +15-20% as the raised FY2026 guidance (adjusted diluted EPS 9.38-9.48 USD) plays out; bull about +30-40% if Annex 1 conversion accelerates well beyond the third-party estimate of only about 15% done at end-2025 — tempered by stretched valuation, with screen analyst upside of minus 0.1%.

NVDA: The single most compelling moat data point is the full stack at scale — about 75-86% data-center AI-accelerator revenue share, CUDA plus NVLink plus TSMC CoWoS priority allocation, with management admitting demand exceeds supply — but that is also why it ranks third for this lens rather than first. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base about +15-25% as Vera Rubin ramps; bull about +40-55% if supply unlocks and the 500B USD Blackwell-plus-Rubin visibility framing converts — with the caveat that the dossier stresses this visibility figure is not an audited backlog, and dual-sourcing plus hyperscaler custom silicon (Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Microsoft Maia, Meta MTIA) is the live disintermediation threat.

Deliberately left out of my top 10: ANET, CF, FANG — ANET because its own customers are already in-sourcing or white-boxing (Microsoft SONiC, Meta FBOSS, about 20-25% of data-center Ethernet revenue) and it buys 100% of its switching silicon from Broadcom while losing the data-center number-one spot to NVDA; CF and FANG because nitrogen and crude are fungible commodities with no patent, certification, or software lock-in, so neither has a company-specific moat to rank.
