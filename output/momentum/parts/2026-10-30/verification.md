## APH — Amphenol — verdict: proceed

- **APH claim 1 — CONFIRMED.** Q3 2026 orders 12.17B, up 99 percent year over year and up 13 percent sequentially, book-to-bill 1.19:1, positive in all but one end market. Source: Amphenol Q3 2026 earnings call transcript and results coverage of the company release dated 2026-10-28 (Motley Fool transcript, 2026-10-28; MarketBeat transcript, 2026-10-28). The implied sales base, 10.23B, matches the reported Q3 sales figure in the same release coverage. The dossier's attribution to the 8-K Exhibit 99.1 dated 2026-10-28 is consistent with these reports.
- **APH claim 2 — CONFIRMED.** IT Datacom sales grew 117 percent in USD and 77 percent organically year over year in Q3 2026, and AI applications represented approximately three-quarters of IT Datacom sales, up from roughly half in 2025. Source: Amphenol Q3 2026 earnings call, 2026-10-28 (Motley Fool and MarketBeat transcripts; Zacks call highlights, 2026-10-29).
- **APH claim 3 — CONFIRMED, as a documented channel report, not a company disclosure and not a bare anecdote.** Edgewater Research's September 2026 Interconnect, Passives and Electromechanical Components market insights state: "CCS/Amphenol fiber lead times extending to about 50 weeks as hyperscalers increasingly secure capacity years ahead through LTAs/POs," and that customers are asking Amphenol to source Corning-spec-equivalent fiber because they cannot get enough directly from Corning. Source: Edgewater Research September 2026 summary, published 2026-09-30, via passive-components.eu. Caveat for the panel: this is a channel-survey finding, approximate ("about 50 weeks"), and it is not an Amphenol company filing; it should not be quoted as a company-reported lead time.
- **APH claim 4 — CONFIRMED.** Bishop and Associates 2024 ranking: TE Connectivity number 1 at 12.88B, Amphenol number 2 at 11.51B (11,510.7M, up 18.7 percent year over year), Molex number 3. Source: Bishop and Associates Top 100 Connector Manufacturers 2025 report (2024 sales), summarised in TTI MarketEye / passive-components.eu, 2026. The dossier's rounded figures match the report table exactly.

## WST — West Pharmaceutical Services — verdict: proceed

- **WST claim 1 — CONFIRMED.** Q3 2026, reported 2026-10-29 (pre-open): net sales 874.5M, up 8.7 percent reported and 11.9 percent organic, versus consensus about 827.1M; adjusted diluted EPS 2.66, up 35.7 percent year over year, versus consensus 2.20. Source: West Q3 2026 results press release, 2026-10-29, via PR Newswire / TradingView; consensus cross-checked in StockStory (S and P consensus 827.1M and 2.20) and MarketBeat (827.12M and 2.20), both 2026-10-29.
- **WST claim 2 — CONFIRMED.** Guidance raised on 2026-10-29: FY2026 net sales 3.41B to 3.42B (from 3.345B to 3.38B) and adjusted diluted EPS 9.38 to 9.48 (from 8.85 to 9.05). Source: West Q3 2026 results press release, 2026-10-29, via PR Newswire / TradingView; also Zacks, 2026-10-29.
- **WST claim 3 — CONFIRMED.** On the Q3 2026 call, 2026-10-29, GLP-1 HVP revenue grew "well into the 30 percent range" organically and HVP for GLP-1 was "just under 12 percent" of total revenue; HVP outside GLP-1 grew in the high teens. Source: West Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 2026-10-29 (Motley Fool; MarketBeat transcript states HVP GLP-1s "represented just under 12 percent of total revenues in the quarter").
- **WST claim 4 — CONFIRMED, with a scope qualification.** The filing lock-in mechanism is real and documented, but it is a regulatory burden, not an absolute bar on switching. West states its elastomer formulation Drug Master Files support customers' regulatory applications in the US and Canada and that its submission support packages supply the container-closure information customers file in Common Technical Document format. Source: West Pharmaceutical regulatory-support pages (westpharma.com). Independently, FDA post-approval-change guidance treats changes to a container closure system — including stopper material, dimensions, and source or site of manufacture — as application changes requiring a supplement (prior-approval, changes-being-effected, or annual-report category depending on risk) with supporting comparability and stability data. Source: FDA container-closure-change guidance, summarised by RAPS, and the NDA supplement framework. Qualification for the panel: the dossier's shorthand "forces equivalence / no-new-risk data" is fair as a description of the burden, but the reporting category is risk-dependent, and an approved comparability protocol can lower it — so do not present every supplier switch as requiring prior FDA approval.

- **MU claim 4 — CONFIRMED.** Q2 2026 shares, within the ranges stated: DRAM revenue share Samsung 39.4 percent, SK hynix 24.9 percent, Micron 23.3 percent (TrendForce, 2026-09-07); HBM revenue share SK hynix about 50 percent, Samsung about 33 percent, Micron about 18 percent (Counterpoint Research, 2026-09-02).
