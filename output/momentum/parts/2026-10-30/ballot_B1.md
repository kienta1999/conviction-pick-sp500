# Panelist B — Growth / momentum ballot — 2026-10-30

## 1. SINGLE-PICK ballot

- Top pick: MU
- Runner-up: APH
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): MU is the fastest compounder in this dossier because it sits at the intersection of all three constrained memory markets at once — DRAM, NAND and HBM — rather than riding a single product cycle. Fiscal Q4 2026 revenue rose 379% year over year to 54.23B USD with a beat-and-raise Q1 FY27 guide of about 61.5B USD, and management said DRAM and NAND would remain supply-constrained in both 2027 and 2028 with no line of sight to balance. That combination of revenue acceleration, upward earnings revisions, and a sold-out forward book is exactly what this lens is built to buy. Unlike the AI-networking and accelerator names, its customers cannot realistically in-source the product, so the shortage accrues to the oligopoly rather than being designed around by it.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Over 75% of expected fiscal/calendar 2027 output is already committed, HBM is sold out for nearly all of calendar 2027 at significantly higher prices, and 26 long-term agreements represent 32B USD in customer financial commitments, up from 22B USD.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: medium — and why: No hyperscaler can realistically build a DRAM/HBM fab — there are only three DRAM makers on earth, and MU spent 27.37B USD in FY26 capex — so true in-sourcing is not the threat. The real discount is substitution within the oligopoly: SK hynix is Nvidia's primary HBM partner and Samsung gained HBM share rapidly in one quarter, so MU can lose share even while the category stays sold out.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case: Base: roughly +30% to +45% if 2027 contracted pricing holds and conventional DRAM/NAND prices merely stay elevated rather than rising further. Bull: roughly +65% or more, in line with the dossier's +67.2% analyst upside and the 1400 USD to 1540 USD targets from Mizuho and JPMorgan, if HBM pricing and tightness extend into 2028 as management guides.
- Top risk: Cyclical price reversal — under 10% of revenue is HBM, so most of the colossal growth is conventional DRAM/NAND pricing, which can reverse sharply, and a class-action suit alleging coordinated production shifts toward HBM is an overhang.
- Conviction (1-10): 8

### Top 3 detail

**MU:** The single most compelling data point is that over 75% of expected 2027 output is already committed and HBM is sold out for nearly all of calendar 2027 at significantly higher prices, with customer discussions already moving to 2028 — that is a forward revenue floor, not a hope. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base roughly +30% to +45% on contracted 2027 pricing holding; bull roughly +65% or more if tightness extends into 2028, consistent with the +67.2% analyst upside in the dossier.

**APH:** The single most compelling data point is record Q3 2026 orders of 12.17B USD, up 99% year over year, at a 1.19x book-to-bill on 10.23B USD of sales, with AI now about three-quarters of IT Datacom revenue and fiber lead times extending to about 50 weeks. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base roughly +20% to +30%, near the dossier's +27.9% analyst upside, as the order book converts; bull roughly +40% to +50% if hyperscaler interconnect content per system keeps rising, as with the TPU backplane content jump cited in the dossier.

**SNDK:** The single most compelling data point is the booked-through-2030 framing — about 50% of FY27 supply and about two-thirds of FY28 supply booked, with 10 long-term agreements with 8 customers carrying at least 93.9B USD in minimum expected revenue at floor pricing and about 91B USD in remaining performance obligations from the Q4 FY26 print. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base roughly +25% to +35% if NAND prices stay elevated through 2027; bull roughly +60%, near the dossier's +61.2% analyst upside, if Citi's supply-constrained-through-2028 call proves right and the pure-play premium holds.

Deliberately left out of my top 10: CF, FANG, AME — CF is a fungible nitrogen cycle with only +9.1% analyst upside and a visible supply-normalisation exit; FANG has no company-specific scarcity, only leveraged oil-price beta near its 52-week high; AME has no single shortage moat, its 4.11B USD backlog burns down in about 12 months by management's own framing, and its 2026-11-03 print is a binary event rather than a confirmed acceleration.
