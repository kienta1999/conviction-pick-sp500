**ISRG:** The single most compelling data point is the installed base that cannot be replicated by its customers: 12,094 da Vinci systems plus 1,152 Ion systems, with Q3 2026 placements up 28% to 546 da Vinci systems and recurring revenue at 84% of total, while robotic-assisted procedures still cover only about 5% of world surgeries. Rough 12-18 month scenario: base roughly +10% to +15%, held back by the deliberate 3-5% cut to instrument revenue per procedure and only +7.5% analyst upside; bull roughly +25% to +30% if da Vinci 5 adoption and procedure growth more than offset that pricing dilution.
