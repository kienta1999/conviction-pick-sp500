# Ballot D1 — Contrarian / risk skeptic lens

## (1) SINGLE-PICK ballot

- Top pick: KLAC
- Runner-up: MU
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): KLAC is the real shortage the market is still pricing as a generic WFE cycle peak: process-control intensity rises with every added layer, node and HBM stack, so its demand structurally outgrows WFE itself, yet the stock sits ~37% below its $307 high (screen) at 28.2x forward with a Morgan Stanley target cut (2026-10-05) hanging over it. Unlike the memory and GPU names, there is no peak spot price in its P&L to mean-revert — revenue is gated by its own 12–24-month lead times and parts supply, with H2 2026 guided ~+20% vs H1 purely as long-lead supply comes online. The explosiveness is backlog conversion plus advanced-packaging process control (~$1.1B in 2026, +>70% YoY) compounding into 2027, not a commodity price that can halve in a quarter. Downside is cushioned relative to peers by a hard, disclosed backlog and 62.4% gross margins rather than untested price floors.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: FY2026 10-K backlog of $12.57B, up 60% YoY from $7.86B explicitly on AI infrastructure demand, with the CFO saying in September 2026 it was "somewhat supply constrained… I could ship more if I had more parts even today" and average lead times ~12 months, some products 18–24 months.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — no fab can in-source inspection/metrology: you cannot yield-manage a 2nm/GAA or HBM line without KLA-class broadband-plasma/e-beam tools, KLA holds ~52–62% global process-control share (~85% in reticle inspection), and ~1,600–1,700 applications engineers are embedded in customer fabs with tools qualified into process flows and almost never ripped out mid-node. Hyperscalers are irrelevant as in-sourcers here (customers are TSMC/Samsung/SK hynix); the only leakage is edge share-nibbling from AMAT diagnostics and ASML bundling, not a bypass technology.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base ~+25% (toward the $232.77 mean target as the $12.57B backlog converts and 2027 growth lands at least as strong as 2026, per management) / bull ~+55–60% (re-rating back toward the prior ~$307 high if advanced-packaging control keeps growing >70% and the market stops pricing a WFE peak).
- Top risk: China was ~28% of 2025 revenue (mid-to-high-20s% expected 2026), so export-control escalation is the single biggest exogenous hit — compounded by ordinary WFE cyclicality and the 10-K's own warning that backlog can be pushed out as customers rebalance project timing.
- Conviction (1-10): 8

### Top 3 — detail

**1. KLAC.** The single most compelling data point is the hard $12.57B backlog (+60% YoY) paired with management stating it could ship more today if it had more parts — a supplier-side shortage at a ~60%-share category leader, not a demand forecast. Base ~+25% over 12–18 months toward the $232.77 target on backlog conversion; bull ~+55–60% if 2027 growth matches or beats 2026 and the stock re-rates toward its prior high.

**2. MU.** The underappreciated fact is not the shortage — everyone sees that — it is the contract structure: ~$150B of take-or-pay RPO across 26 Strategic Customer Agreements, $32B in customer financial commitments and >75% of 2027 output already committed, yet the screen still prices it at only 4.8x forward earnings, i.e. the market assumes the cycle kills it as always. That scepticism is the opportunity, but also the honest cap: HBM share already slipped 21%→18% in Q2 2026 as Samsung's HBM4 ramped, and new industry supply lands in 2028–29. Base ~+35–40% if SCA floors hold ASPs through the window; bull ~+60% toward/through the $1,555.13 target if 2027–28 stays tighter as management claims.

**3. GOOGL.** Alphabet is the disintermediator, not the victim: its own TPUs displace GPUs internally and are now sold externally (Wells Fargo modelled ~$9.2B/$11.5B of external TPU system sales in Q3/Q4 2026 alone), on top of a $514B Google Cloud RPO at Q2 2026 — at 23.2x forward that merchant-silicon option looks underpriced, provided you strip out the ~$98B non-cash equity mark that inflated Q2 headline net income. Base ~+22% toward the $429.47 mean target as backlog converts (>50% expected within 24 months); bull ~+40% if TPU system revenue scales into 2027 as guided and cloud keeps outgrowing AWS/Azure.

Left out of top 10: SNDK (weakest moat in the set at 4/10, only #5 in NAND at 11.4% share, NAND price gains already decelerating from +70–75% in Q2 to a forecast +10–20%, and ~2/3 of growth is price on peak 84.6% margins — the classic peak-cycle trap despite a 5.9x forward multiple), ANET (40.4x forward for a box it does not make the silicon for — Nvidia Spectrum-X already took the #1 data-center Ethernet share spot at 21.5% vs Arista's 20.7%, so the shortage benefits the category, not Arista), APH (interconnect itself cannot be bypassed but any given Amphenol socket can — TE/Molex/Luxshare substitution, copper-displacement fears, 43% of sales now in one hyperscaler-driven segment and leverage from the $10.5B CommScope deal), and CSCO (5/10 irreplaceability: hyperscalers demonstrably route around it with white-box/SONiC on merchant silicon and Nvidia bundles, gross margin already down 2.1pts YoY, and a near-doubling of inventory purchase commitments to $17.2B becomes a liability the moment AI orders pause).
