### Top 3 — shortage / moat detail and scenarios

**1. MU:** The single most compelling point is the contract structure around the shortage: ~$150B of take-or-pay RPO across 26 SCAs, $32B of customer commitments, HBM sold out for 2026 and >75% of 2027 output committed, with no meaningful new fab volume until late 2028. That converts a historically spot-priced cycle into pre-sold acceleration. 12-18mo scenario: base +45% to +60%, bull +85% to +110%.

**2. SNDK:** The shortage is the tightest on price — TrendForce NAND contract prices +55-60% in Q1 2026 and +70-75% in Q2, FQ4 revenue +372% YoY at 84.6% gross margin, backed by 10 New Business Model agreements totalling $93.9B minimum contracted revenue at floor pricing and $16.5B in customer deposits/guarantees, with ~2/3 of FY28 bits already booked. The moat warning is explicit: #5 in NAND at 11.4% share, no software lock-in, and easy dual-sourcing to Samsung/SK hynix/Micron. 12-18mo scenario: base +25% to +40% (toward the $2,173 target), bull +60% to +80% if pricing deceleration stops at +10-20% per quarter and datacentre mix keeps rising — with far fatter downside than MU if it does not.

**3. NVDA:** The compelling point is that guidance itself is supply-set, not demand-set: Q2 FY27 revenue $96.2B, +106% YoY, Q3 guided to $108B, and a first full-year FY28 outlook of ~+70% described as supply-constrained, with ~$500B of Blackwell + Rubin order visibility and cloud GPUs repeatedly called "sold out." CUDA (irreplaceability 8/10) is the deepest moat among the accelerators, but customer ASICs (TPU, Trainium, Maia, MTIA) already taking an estimated 10-15% of hyperscaler workloads, concentrated in inference, is the live disintermediation leak — share has already normalised ~92% to ~80%. 12-18mo scenario: base +35% to +45% (toward the $328.72 target), bull +60% to +75% if Rubin ships on cadence and ASIC share gains stall.

Deliberately left out of top 10: NEM, PH, EW, VRTX — all are low-teens or single-digit revenue growers with no AI-driven acceleration (PH revenue +9.8% YoY with -2.9% 6-month return, VRTX +12.5%, EW +16.7% gated by hospital capacity, NEM +15.1% with negative revenue acceleration and falling production), so they cannot compete on this lens for fastest compounding despite EW/VRTX having strong moats.
