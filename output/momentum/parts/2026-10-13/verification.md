## MU (Micron) — tentative #1

### 1. Backlog: ~$150B RPO, 26 take-or-pay SCAs, ~$32B customer commitments, >35% of estimated revenue through 2030
**CONFIRMED — with one scope nuance.**

- Micron's fiscal Q4 FY2026 earnings call (2026-09-30): CFO Mark Murphy put remaining performance obligations at approximately $150 billion, and customer financial commitments at $32 billion, mostly cash deposits. Multiple independent call summaries agree verbatim in substance.
- The count (26 SCAs, up from 16 the prior quarter), the take-or-pay volume commitments in all agreements, and the estimate that they account for more than 35% of revenue through 2030 (some now extending into 2031) are likewise reported from the same call.
- Nuance the panel should keep: the ~$150B RPO is specifically for agreements with a determined / defined pricing framework — roughly three-quarters of expected SCA revenue, priced at committed volumes and minimum pricing, which management called inherently conservative. It is not a simple sum over all 26 agreements at expected prices. The claim as worded does not contradict this, but the memo should not present $150B as total expected SCA revenue.
- Sources: Micron Q4 FY2026 earnings call, 2026-09-30, as reported by Carbon Finance "Micron Earnings Review (Q4 FY2026)"; MarketBeat, "Micron Technology Q4 Earnings Call Highlights," 2026-09-30; AI Stock Wire, "Micron Q4 FY2026 earnings: $54.2B revenue, $150B RPO," ~2026-10-01.

### 2. Shortage driver: HBM sold out for calendar 2026, most of 2027 contracted, 2027/2028 "even tighter"; FQ4 FY26 revenue $54.2B, FQ1 FY27 guide ~$61.5B
**CONFIRMED.**

- Revenue/guide: Micron reported fiscal Q4 FY2026 (14 weeks ended 2026-09-03) revenue of $54.23B on 2026-09-30, and guided fiscal Q1 FY2027 revenue to $61.5B ± $1.5B. Confirmed in Micron's 2026-09-30 release as reported by CNBC (via multiple summaries) and by STK Market / EquitySwarm summaries of the release.
- Sold out / contracted: Micron had previously disclosed its entire calendar-2026 HBM supply, including HBM4, was fully contracted / sold out under binding agreements. On the 2026-09-30 call, management said agreements had been completed for the vast majority / most of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply at prices well above 2026, and that more than 75% of total planned 2027 output was already committed.
- Tighter: CEO Sanjay Mehrotra on the 2026-09-30 call said demand exceeds supply in calendar 2027 and 2028, with greater tightness in 2027 and 2028 versus 2026, and no line of sight to when supply and demand return to balance. Prepared remarks carried the same tighter-in-2027/2028 framing.
- Sources: Micron Q4 FY2026 release and call, 2026-09-30 (Computerworld / CIO reporting, 2026-10-01; Investing.com call summary); AInvest reporting on Micron's fully contracted calendar-2026 HBM supply; Tech-Insider timeline of the 2026-09-30 call.

## KLAC (KLA) — tentative #2

### 5. Backlog: $12.57B at FY2026 year-end, up ~60% YoY from $7.86B, per FY26 10-K
**CONFIRMED.**

- KLA reported backlog of $12.57 billion as of June 30, 2026, up from $7.86 billion at June 30, 2025, in its FY2026 Form 10-K (filed August 2026). $12.57B / $7.86B − 1 = +59.9%, i.e. ~60% YoY — the arithmetic in the claim is correct.
- The 10-K attribution, as summarized, is strong demand from the ongoing AI infrastructure buildout, with orders subject to timing shifts, modifications, and cancellations.
- Sources: KLA FY2026 Form 10-K summary, StockTitan, August 2026; Zacks via TradingView, "KLAC's Backlog Surges on AI Demand," ~2026-09-29, reporting the same $12.57B vs $7.86B figures.

### 6. Shortage driver: "virtually sold out", lead times ~12 months average / 18–24 months on some products; CFO said it could ship more if it had more parts (Sept 2026)
**CONFIRMED in substance — but the three elements come from three different dates; do not attribute all of them to September 2026.**

- "Virtually sold out": CFO Bren Higgins characterized KLA as virtually sold out across most products in H1 2026, with lead times extending — attributed to the fiscal Q2 2026 earnings call in AInvest's earnings-call contradiction summary. It was not located as a September 2026 quote.
- Lead times ~12 months / 18–24 months: Higgins on the fiscal Q4 FY2026 earnings call (July 2026, MarketBeat transcript): lead times across the company are in about the 12-month range, and on certain products closer to 18 to 24 months, with customer conversations about deliveries in H2 2027.
- "Could ship more if it had more parts": Confirmed for September 2026. At the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference, 2026-09-11, CFO Bren Higgins said KLA was somewhat supply-constrained in H1, in words paraphrased as: he could ship more if he had more parts even today, with H2 revenue expected up ~20% vs H1 as supply improves. Source: Seeking Alpha / StockAnalysis transcript of that conference, 2026-09-11.
- Net: the shortage picture is real and management-sourced; only the panel's implied single-date (Sept 2026) attribution for all three elements is imprecise.

### 7. Moat: ~52–62% global process-control share, ~85% in reticle inspection; no fab can in-source this
**CONFIRMED in substance for overall share; UNVERIFIED for reticle at exactly 85% and for the absolute in-sourcing claim.**

- Overall process-control share: Independent research clusters at ~56–58% for CY2025 (KLA investor-day material citing ~58%; other research ~56%), with other reputable summaries giving 50–60%. The claimed 52–62% range brackets all of these estimates, so it is not contradicted — but no single primary source stating exactly "52–62%" was found; the best-supported point estimate is ~56–58%.
- Reticle inspection: Sources consistently say 80%+ (near-monopoly), not a pinned 85%. ~85% is within a plausible reading of "80%+" but is UNVERIFIED as a precise figure from any primary or reputable secondary source found.
- "No fab can in-source this": UNVERIFIED as stated. No source found proves this absolute. What sources do support is the weaker, defensible version: KLA is ~7× its nearest competitor (Applied Materials ~10% in metrology/inspection), process control is structurally distinct, and no fab is known to self-supply leading-edge patterned-wafer / reticle inspection at scale. The memo should soften the absolute wording or cite it as panel judgment, not fact.
- Sources: KLA research summaries citing KLA investor-day material and industry research, September–October 2026; GuruFocus via TradingView, "KLA: Semiconductor Inspection Market Leader"; KLA Q3 FY2026 call reporting (share +360 bps since 2021, ~7× nearest competitor), April 2026.

- Sources: Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings release and call, 2026-07-22, as reported by The Motley Fool, 2026-10-07 / 2026-10-08 / 2026-10-11; Zacks, "GOOGL Q2 Earnings Call Centers on AI Capacity," 2026-07-22.
