### Top 3 — moat / shortage detail and scenario

**1. KLAC:** The most compelling point is the $12.57B backlog (+60% YoY) alongside CFO comments that KLA could ship more if it had more parts, with 12–24-month lead times — a sold-out gatekeeper, not a demand chaser. Its moat compounds because yield data and qualification accumulate per node, so intensity per wafer rises as geometries shrink. 12–18mo scenario: base +20–25% to the $232.77 mean target; bull +40–50% on sustained low-$150B+ WFE and packaging-control growth.

**2. NVDA:** The moat point is CUDA friction itself — the dossier notes AWS's own internal documents reportedly identified CUDA migration as the main factor holding back its own chips, on top of ~80% AI accelerator revenue share, 74.7% gross margin and 117.2% ROE in the screen. That is real irreplaceability in training, even as Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Microsoft Maia and Meta MTIA route predictable inference (now ~2/3 of compute) around it — which is why it is runner-up, not top pick, on this lens. 12–18mo scenario: base +30–45% toward the $328.72 mean target from $226.32 on supply-constrained ~+70% FY28 growth guided on the Q2 FY27 call; bull +60%+ if Rubin ramps without further share normalisation from ~80%.

**3. VRTX:** The moat point is absence of competition: ~90% global share in CF modulators, eligible for ~90% of the CF population, >75,000 patients on therapy, key TRIKAFTA patents to Dec 2037, and would-be challengers (AbbVie's programme, a leading Sionna candidate in 2026) have failed — patients and payers simply cannot in-source a patented small molecule. Shortage here is patent scarcity (shortage score only 5/10), so explosiveness is binary, not backlog-driven. 12–18mo scenario: base +10–15% toward the $571.61 mean target from $510.08 on the raised $13.1–13.2B FY26 guide; bull +30–40% if povetacicept wins at the 2026-11-30 PDUFA and the renal franchise (cited $4.3B peak-sales estimate) is credited.

Deliberately left out of top 10: SNDK (irreplaceability 4/10, #5 in NAND at 11.4% share, most commoditised layer — hyperscalers can dual-source Samsung/SK hynix/Micron/YMTC with no lock-in, and ~2/3 of growth is price), ANET (5/10, makes no switch ASIC of its own — Broadcom Tomahawk — while Nvidia Spectrum-X took DC Ethernet share from <4% to 21.5% and routes around EOS), CSCO (5/10, hyperscalers demonstrably bypass its boxes with white-box/ODM switches on merchant silicon and SONiC, plus Nvidia bundling), and NEM (6/10 but a price-taker on a perfectly fungible commodity — buyers can switch to Barrick, Agnico, recycled gold or an ETF in a day, with zero lock-in and its own 2026 production guided down ~10%).
