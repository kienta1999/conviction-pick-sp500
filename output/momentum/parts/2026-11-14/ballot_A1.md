# Panelist A — Supply-Chain Analyst ballot — momentum 2026-11-14

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick: CF**
- **Runner-up: GE**

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** CF is the cleanest shortage in the entire screened set, and the only name where the supplier is genuinely sold out rather than merely growing fast. It ran its available ammonia capacity at 97% utilisation in the first nine months of 2026, shipped its largest quarterly UAN volume this decade, and has its fill-programme order book extended into Q1 2027 — there is nothing left to sell. The shortage is structural and multi-sourced (war, European curtailment, Trinidad shutdowns), management expects the balance to stay constructive into 2027 with pricing on a positive trajectory through at least H1 2027, and the next volume driver (Yazoo City restart, H1 2027) is already in hand. At 10.7x forward P/E — the cheapest multiple in the set — the market is pricing a commodity, not a sold-out #1 producer in a multi-year supply squeeze.

- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** ~9 million metric tons of urea supply removed by Middle East hostilities since late February 2026, with Middle East urea exports running ~800,000 t/month vs a normal 1.5–1.7 Mt/month — and management stating normalisation would take four to six months even if hostilities ended immediately (Q3 2026 call, Nov 5, 2026). Over 35% of European ammonia capacity curtailed, Trinidad permanently shutting 35% of its capacity, and global nitrogen inventories low across every major region.

- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: LOW.** Nitrogen is biologically irreplaceable — there is no substitute for fixed nitrogen in high-yield farming, and Haber-Bosch is the only industrial route at scale. Customers (farmers, distributors) cannot in-source: a world-scale ammonia plant costs billions, takes years to permit and build, needs cheap gas and a hazardous-chemical distribution network. CF is the world's largest ammonia producer and North America's largest pure-play nitrogen manufacturer, holding ~39% of US ammonia capacity with the lowest-cost gas on earth. The only slow-burn substitutes (biological nitrogen, precision ag) trim application rates over a decade, not a cycle.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +20–30% — the disruption persists into 2027 as management guides, pricing follows the positive trajectory through H1 2027, Yazoo City adds volume, and buybacks (3M shares for $367M in Q3 alone) plus the guided 15% nitrogen-per-share growth by 2030 compound the story. Bull case roughly +50–70% — the India tender returns, Middle East exports stay disrupted, and urea re-tightens toward its 12-month highs, with the market re-rating a sold-out producer off a 10.7x forward multiple.

- **Top risk:** Geopolitical normalisation — the shortage is partly war-driven, and management itself says supply could normalise in 4–6 months if hostilities end; the early-November urea softening (~$434.50/t on Nov 11 vs $442.50 on Nov 4, India tender delayed) is the live warning that the price tape can sag even while supply stays disrupted.

- **Conviction (1-10): 8**
