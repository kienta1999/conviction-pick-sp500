## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: CAT
- Runner-up: APH
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): CAT is the one name in this batch whose shortage is both the most tangible (a physical $72B backlog, +92% YoY, with gas-engine prime-power lead times stretching into 2028–2029) and the least loved — the stock has pulled back 26% from its highs while the backlog kept compounding, so the market is discounting a cycle peak it may not be getting. Hyperscalers cannot conjure prime power: every alternative (turbines, other gensets) faces the same supply-chain constraints, which gives CAT pricing power no spreadsheet is modeling. The $2.2B tariff bill is real but knowable and largely passable; at a 25.3x forward P/E with +19% analyst upside, it is a reasonable multiple for a multi-year booked pipeline. This is exactly the contrarian setup: fear priced in, delivery already sold.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: record $72B backlog (+92% YoY) with gas-engine prime-power lead times into 2028–2029 — a multi-year sold-out book in the one input (power) AI data centers cannot defer.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: MEDIUM — hyperscalers cannot in-source power generation, and competing engine/turbine makers face identical supply constraints, so substitution is a capacity story not a bypass story. The residual risk is technology shift (e.g., grid-scale batteries displacing gas engines over a decade), not near-term disintermediation.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base ~+20–25% as the backlog converts and tariff pass-through holds margins; bull ~+45% if prime-power pricing power surprises to the upside and the cycle-peak-margin fear unwinds.
- Top risk: the $2.2B tariff bill combined with cycle-peak margins — if AI capex decelerates, a $72B backlog can reprice fast, and CAT is still a cyclical at heart.
- Conviction (1-10): 7

### Top 3 deep dive (this lens)

**1. CAT** — Gas-engine prime-power lead times into 2028–2029 against a record $72B backlog (+92% YoY) is the single most physical shortage in the batch: you cannot virtualize megawatts. Meanwhile the stock sits 26% below its highs on cycle-peak-margin fears while the backlog — the actual forward demand — kept growing, a dislocation I read as underappreciation, not information. Base ~+20–25% (backlog converts, tariffs passed through); bull ~+45% (pricing power on prime power surprises, margin fear unwinds).

**2. APH** — Record $10.7B orders (+94% YoY) with a 1.23 book-to-bill means orders are running 23% ahead of shipments: hard, unshipped demand the market cannot yet see in revenue. AI datacom hit $3.8B in Q2 (+63%, 43% of sales), and the CommScope deal makes APH copper/optical-neutral across whatever interconnect standard wins. Base ~+15–20% as the book ships; bull ~+40% if the neutrality thesis extends AI share. Capped by 43% sales dependence on one hyperscaler capex wave and a ~38x trailing multiple.

**3. FCX** — A ~330 kmt 2026 copper deficit with mine output down 1.1% in 1H26 is supply destruction, not a cycle wobble, and copper at a record $6.83/lb confirms the market is feeling it. The Grasberg ramp is the one owned growth lever that does not depend on anyone else's capex. Base ~+10–15%; bull ~+35% if the deficit tightens further. Contrarian caution: analyst upside is only +1.1%, so the Street already sees the record price — the edge is deficit persistence, not surprise.
