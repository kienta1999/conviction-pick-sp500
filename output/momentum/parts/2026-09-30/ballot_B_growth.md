## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick: MU**
- **Runner-up: SNDK**

**Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is sitting in the worst memory crisis in 15 years (IDC, Feb 2026) with the steepest acceleration trajectory in the batch: FQ3 record $41.5B revenue (+346% YoY), 84.9% gross margin, and record $18.3B free cash flow. Customers are receiving only 55–60% of their DRAM/NAND asks while DRAM prices have risen +400% since 2024 (JPM) and HBM consumes ~3x fab capacity per bit of DDR5, so every AI build-out structurally tightens supply. Street sits at/above the guidance high end ($50B±1B rev / $31±1 EPS) ahead of today's print, and at a 6.6x forward P/E with +42.8% analyst upside (highest in the batch) the momentum is the least priced-in of the big memory names.

**Key shortage/backlog evidence:** 16 long-term agreements covering ~20% of DRAM bits / ~30% of NAND bits, backed by ~$22B in customer cash deposits (FQ3 call, June 2026) — customers are literally prepaying years of capacity because they only get 55–60% of what they ask for.

**Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: LOW.** Only three firms on earth make leading-edge DRAM, and no hyperscaler can in-source a DRAM fab — the capex, lithography know-how, and qualification cycles (>1 year) make customer self-build a non-starter. The disintermediation trap that threatens NVDA and ANET does not exist here.

**12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+35–50% (shortage persists through the HBM ramp, pricing stays elevated, multiple stays in the single digits on next-year earnings); bull ~+80–120% (today's print sparks a buyback, DRAM contract pricing keeps stair-stepping through 2027, and the market finally re-rates memory off its "peak cycle" discount).

**Top risk:** Memory's historic cyclicality — the bar going into today's print is already high, and any guide-down on capex discipline or a demand air-pocket would hit a name where analysts see +42.8% upside very hard.

**Conviction (1-10): 9**

### Top 3 deep notes

**MU (#1):** The single most compelling datapoint is customers receiving only 55–60% of their memory asks against +400% DRAM price rises since 2024 — demand is being rationed, not competed for. With HBM eating ~3x fab capacity per bit and ~$22B in prepaid LTAs, this is acceleration with contracted visibility. Base case +35–50% over 12–18 months as pricing stair-steps through the print cycle; bull +80–120% if today's earnings (guidance beat + possible buyback) finally kills the "peak cycle" discount.

**SNDK (#2):** The $42B minimum-revenue backlog from the New Business Model — 5 agreements covering >1/3 of FY27 bit supply with >$11B enforceable guarantees — is the strongest contracted-visibility datapoint outside MU, and Q4 FY26 revenue was +372% YoY. Structurally the same NAND shortage story with data centers now >50% of the NAND market and enterprise SSD demand +43% in 2026E. Base +25–40%; bull +60–90% if S&P 100 index flows and the $14B buyback compound, tempered by the 5,000%-since-spin run that means the explosion is partly priced in — which is exactly why it trails MU.

**AMAT (#3):** Irreplaceability 9 with zero in-source risk — physics-gated deposition/HBM packaging where the company itself is capacity-constrained in 2026 and negotiating 2030 contracts. The growth lens loves the acceleration: Q3 $9.12B (+25%) with Q4 guided to +51% revenue growth, plus a $15B backlog (~2 quarters of cover). Base +20–35%; bull +50–70% if WFE hits BofA's $210B 2027 view. The SEMI 7.6% 2027 equipment deceleration is the growth-lens demerit that keeps it at #3.
