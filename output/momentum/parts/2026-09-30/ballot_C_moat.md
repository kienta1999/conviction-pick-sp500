# Ballot C — Quality / Moat & Irreplaceability — 2026-09-30

Panelist lens: category dominance, margins, returns on capital, balance-sheet durability, and above all irreplaceability — customers (especially hyperscalers) must not be able to build or in-source the product, and no substitute technology should be able to route around it.

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** MU
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam Research is the purest un-copyable moat in the batch. It holds ~30% of etch and >80% of advanced-node etch, and the moat is physics-gated: atomic-level etch precision for GAA and HBM cannot be willed into existence by a hyperscaler's checkbook — building this capability requires decades of process IP and a qualification lock-in at every leading fab. Cleanroom capacity is now the pacing item for a WFE market raised to the low-$150Bs, with $210B seen in 2027 (BofA), and deferred revenue ($2.43B) shows demand pulling forward. MU was a close second — the memory triopoly is real pricing power and FQ3 printed 84.9% gross margins — but memory's historic cyclicality makes its 2026 supernormal margins mean-revertible, whereas LRCX's installed-base-driven revenue recognition (flags judged benign) and replacement-free position compound.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** 2026 WFE view raised to low-$150Bs with cleanroom capacity as the pacing item; BofA sees $210B in 2027 — Lam's ~30% etch share converts a raised industry capex envelope directly into Lam revenue.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** LOW. No hyperscaler can in-source fab tools — the physics-gated precision and multi-year fab qualification process make disintermediation effectively impossible. This is the polar opposite of the NVDA/ANET trap (hyperscaler custom silicon taking share).
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: raised WFE + etch pricing power sustains mid-teens earnings growth; analyst upside +15.4% with momentum re-rating as cleanroom expansions land — base ~+20-25%. Bull: 2027 $210B WFE materializes and advanced-node etch intensity (GAA/HBM) drives share gains — ~+40%.
- **Top risk:** Most cyclical name in the batch (dossier flags it); a memory capex pause or the SEMI 7.6% 2027 equipment-growth deceleration would hit LRCX first and hardest. 54.7x trailing P/E leaves no multiple cushion.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 deep-dives

**LRCX.** The single most compelling moat datapoint: >80% share of advanced-node etch — meaning the leading-edge transistor roadmap (GAA, then HBM3e stacking) literally cannot be built without Lam's tools, and every new fab must re-qualify competitors from scratch. 12-18mo: base ~+20-25% on raised low-$150B WFE and share stability; bull ~+40% if BofA's $210B 2027 WFE hits and etch intensity rises with HBM.

**MU.** Most compelling datapoint: 16 LTAs tied to ~$22B in customer cash deposits (FQ3 call, June 2026) — customers are prepaying billions to secure allocation because they receive only 55–60% of their asks (IDC). 12-18mo: base ~+25-30% on $50B±1B revenue guide and +42.8% analyst upside; bull ~+60% if tonight's earnings confirm the shortage extends and a buyback is announced. Risk that keeps it #2: memory's historic cyclicality; the bar into earnings today is already high.

**AMAT.** Most compelling datapoint: the company itself is capacity-constrained in 2026 and is already negotiating 2030 contracts — customers booking five years out is the signature of an irreplaceable supplier. 12-18mo: base ~+20% on $15B backlog (2 quarters cover) and Q4 +51% revenue guide; bull ~+35% if 10+ announced fabs convert on schedule. Same WFE-deceleration risk as LRCX.

### Left out of top 10 and why

- **ANET** — the disintermediation trap bites hardest: real white-box/custom-silicon threat (Spectrum-X), Nvidia briefly overtook it in DC Ethernet, ~42% revenue from two hyperscalers who could in-source. Disqualified on this lens.
- **CSCO** — lost DC switching share to Nvidia (17.8% vs 21.5%), backlog is order-pull-forward priced in at 33x P/E, and irreplaceability 5 is the batch's weakest.
- **AME** — a compounder with visibility, not a shortage story; irreplaceability 5 and backlog $4.11B are an order of magnitude below the names above.
