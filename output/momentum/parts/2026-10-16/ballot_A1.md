# Panelist A — Supply-chain analyst — Momentum panel, 2026-10-16

## Ballot 1 — Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** GOOGL
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is the hardest shortage in the 14: a three-firm DRAM/HBM oligopoly where management says it can satisfy only 50–66% of key customers' HBM demand, with calendar-2026 HBM capacity entirely sold out and the large majority of calendar-2027 HBM already under contract at higher prices. That scarcity is not a stage claim — it is contracted, take-or-pay, and prepaid, giving multi-year revenue visibility that covers more than 35% of revenue through 2030, with some agreements running into 2031. The screen still prices it as peak earnings at fwd P/E 4.51 despite 256.3% TTM revenue growth and Q1 FY2027 guidance of ~$61.5B, another record after Q4 FY2026 revenue of $54.23B. If tight supply holds through 2027 as management, UBS and KB Securities expect, contracted volume at rising prices converts directly into explosive earnings leverage.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** ~$150B remaining performance obligations under 26 strategic customer agreements, backed by $32B in customer financial commitments, the vast majority as cash deposits — $12.3B/$12.747B of which arrived in Q4 alone.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — only Micron, SK hynix and Samsung perform HBM stacking at scale and hyperscalers do not fab memory (they prepay it instead), but those two rivals are genuine substitutes, so Micron has oligopoly pricing, not monopoly pricing, and China's CXMT is rising in conventional DRAM.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base — toward the screen analyst upside of +67.6%, supported by the ~$61.5B Q1 FY2027 guide; bull — upside beyond that consensus if the already-contracted 2027 HBM volumes at higher prices and tight supply through 2027 force another guidance step-up like the Q4 record beat.
- **Top risk:** Cyclicality in disguise — the LTAs cover only ~35%+ of revenue through 2030, the rest is spot-exposed, and >$50B of guided FY2027 capex (call-reported) plus industry capacity landing in 2028 could turn the 4.51x forward multiple into a peak-earnings value trap.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 9
