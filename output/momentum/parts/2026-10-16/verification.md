# Phase 3.5 Verification — Momentum Panel — Run 2026-10-16

Verified 2026-10-16 via web search, primary sources first (company earnings releases / calls, SEC-filing excerpts) where available, reputable financial press / transcripts second. Verdicts are per sub-claim as worded in the brief.

## WINNER + BORDA #1 — MU (Micron)

### 1. Backlog: ~$150B RPO, 26 SCAs (take-or-pay), $32B commitments, >35% of revenue through 2030
**CONFIRMED**
- Micron FQ4 FY26 call, 2026-09-30: 26 multi-year strategic customer agreements, all with take-or-pay volume commitments; ~$150B remaining performance obligations for agreements with determined pricing frameworks; customer financial commitments up to $32B, mostly cash deposits; agreements estimated at >35% of revenue through 2030, ~three-quarters of that revenue under defined pricing frameworks. Actual SCA revenue is expected to exceed the $150B because it is counted at committed volumes / minimum pricing.
- Sources: MarketBeat transcript summary of Micron Q4 call, 2026-09-30; Carbon Finance / AI Stock Wire earnings reviews, early Oct 2026, both reporting the same call figures.

### 2. Shortage driver: HBM sold out CY2026, large majority of CY2027 contracted at higher prices; can satisfy only 50–66% of key customers' HBM demand; only ~3 firms make HBM at scale
**CONFIRMED**
- Sold out / contracted: Micron completed price-and-volume agreements for its entire calendar-2026 HBM supply (FQ2 FY26 call, Mar 2026), and on the 2026-09-30 call said the large majority of calendar-2027 HBM volumes were already under contract at higher prices than 2026. Source: TechTimes, 2026-10-16, summarising the Sept 30 call.
- 50–66%: CEO Sanjay Mehrotra has repeatedly said Micron can meet only ~50–66% (also phrased 50% to two-thirds) of demand from several key customers. Sources: AInvest summary of Micron Q1 FY26 call Q&A (Dec 2025); multiple 2026 press summaries of Mehrotra remarks.
- Only 3 at scale: Only Micron, SK hynix and Samsung perform HBM stacking/packaging at scale for AI accelerators. Source: TechTimes, 2026-10-16.

### 3. Catalyst + date: FQ4 FY26 revenue $54.23B, FQ1 FY27 guidance ~$61.5B; next earnings 2026-12-23 — confirmed or estimated?
**Revenue / guidance: CONFIRMED. Date 2026-12-23: ESTIMATED, not company-confirmed (so UNVERIFIED as a confirmed date)**
- Revenue / guidance: Micron release, 2026-09-30: FQ4 FY26 revenue $54.23B; FQ1 FY27 guidance $61.5B ± $1.5B, non-GAAP EPS $38.15 ± $1.00. Corroborated by CNBC via press summaries, 2026-09-30 / 2026-10-01.
- Date status: MarketBeat (checked Oct 2026) and SmartCalendars both label 2026-12-23 as *estimated, based on past reporting schedules*. TipRanks' page is internally inconsistent — a header says "Confirmed" while its own body text says the date is estimated and may be updated when actually announced. No Micron IR announcement confirming Dec 23 was found as of 2026-10-16. The screen's date matches the consensus estimate; it should not be presented as confirmed.

### 4. Moat: hyperscalers prepay Micron rather than in-source memory fabrication
**CONFIRMED in behaviour; UNVERIFIED as a stated motive / rejected alternative**
- Prepay behaviour is confirmed: the $32B in customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits ($12.7B received in FQ4 alone, booked as financing cash flow / customer contract liabilities), is hyperscalers/customers paying in advance to secure supply — see Claim 1 sources, 2026-09-30 call.
- "Rather than in-source" is an inference, not a disclosed customer decision: no primary source found in which a hyperscaler says it considered and rejected building DRAM/HBM fabs. It is consistent with the structure — no hyperscaler operates a memory fab and only the 3 firms in Claim 2 make HBM at scale — but the panel should label the in-sourcing contrast as inference, not as a sourced fact.

## BORDA #2 — GOOGL (Alphabet)

### 1. Backlog: Google Cloud backlog / RPO $514B in Q2 2026, >50% to convert within 24 months, up from ~$106B a year earlier
**CONFIRMED**
- Q2 2026: CEO Sundar Pichai on the Q2 call (results 2026-07-22) reported a $514B Google Cloud backlog; press coverage of the call / Alphabet IR summary states slightly more than half is expected to convert to revenue within 24 months, and sequential growth was >$50B. Sources: Motley Fool, Oct 2026; CryptoBriefing and TMC Insight summaries of Q2 2026 results, Jul–Sep 2026.
- Year-earlier comparator: Q2 2025 backlog was $106B, with >50% then also guided to convert within two years. Sources: CRN interview with Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian (Q2 2025); Motley Fool Q2 2025 call transcript takeaways — Cloud backlog $106B at quarter-end. Note the ~5x jump in one year is as reported; the $514B includes newer categories (e.g. TPU hardware agreements flagged on the Q1 2026 call), so like-for-like comparability is management-defined.

### 2. Shortage driver: Cloud revenue +82% to $24.8B in Q2 2026, capacity-constrained, including renting external GPU capacity
**CONFIRMED**
- Revenue: Alphabet Q2 2026 release, 2026-07-22 (Exhibit 99.1): Google Cloud revenues increased 82% to $24.8B, led by GCP enterprise AI infrastructure / solutions.
- Capacity-constrained + external renting: Pichai has said the company is compute-constrained in the near term and Cloud revenue would have been higher if demand could be met (Q1 2026 call). CFO Anat Ashkenazi on the Q2 2026 call said Alphabet plans to expand use of third-party capacity in Q3 as a bridging strategy while building internal capacity. The specific external deal widely reported is the June 2026 SpaceX compute lease (~$920M/month for ~110,000 Nvidia GPUs, described by Google as bridge capacity). Sources: MarketWatch live coverage of Q2 2026 earnings, Jul 2026; AInvest, Aug 2026, on the SpaceX leases.

### 3. Catalyst + date: next earnings 2026-10-28 — confirmed / scheduled? 2026 capex guide $195–205B
**CONFIRMED (both)**
- Earnings date: 2026-10-28 after close is listed as a confirmed date by Zacks earnings calendar and by Global X's calendar checked 2026-10-08; multiple Oct 2026 previews state Alphabet has confirmed / is scheduled to release Q3 2026 results on Oct 28. Sources: Zacks GOOG earnings calendar; WEEX / Motley Fool previews, 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-15.
- Capex: Alphabet raised full-year 2026 capex guidance to $195B–$205B on the Q2 2026 call (from $180–190B). Sources: Alphabet Q2 2026 coverage, Jul 2026; Motley Fool, 2026-08-25.

### 4. Moat / earnings-quality: Q2 headline EPS ~$9.11 flattered by ~$98B unrealised equity-securities gain; Search share ~89–90%
**CONFIRMED (both)**
- EPS quality: Alphabet Q2 2026 release, 2026-07-22: EPS $9.11 (+294%), net income +298%; other income reflected a net gain of $98.0B, primarily net unrealised gains on equity securities. Follow-on analysis puts the gain's contribution at ~$6.26 of diluted EPS, i.e. headline EPS / trailing P/E materially overstate operating earnings. Sources: Alphabet Exhibit 99.1, 2026-07-22, via Quartr; AInvest earnings-quality analysis, Sep 2026.
- Search share: StatCounter puts Google at 89.9% global search share in September 2026 (85.2% in the US), consistent with a 89–90% global band through 2026 (90.0–90.1% in Apr / Jan 2026). Sources: StatCounter via Colorlib search statistics (Sep 2026 data) and Bank of America / Sensor Tower summary via CityBiz, Oct 2026.

## BORDA #3 — NVDA (Nvidia)

### 1. Backlog: $500B Blackwell / Vera Rubin demand booked through 2026, superseded by at least $1T demand through 2027 — filing RPO or management demand figure?
**CONFIRMED as a management demand figure; CONTRADICTED if presented as a filing RPO / booked backlog**
- History: At GTC 2025 Huang cited ~$500B of Blackwell + Rubin demand through 2026. At GTC 2026 keynote, 2026-03-16, he raised it to at least $1T through 2027. Sources: TechCrunch, 2026-03-16; TipRanks GTC 2026 summary; CNBC via PulseMark, Mar 2026. Characterisation in coverage varies (orders / demand / revenue projection) — it is a keynote visibility claim, not an audited order book.
- Not a filing RPO: Nvidia's SEC filings show remaining performance obligations from contracts >1 year of only $2.3B as of 2026-01-25 (10-K) and ~$3.2B beyond one year in later 10-Q discussion — three orders of magnitude below $1T, because hardware ships/bills in weeks. Source: Nvidia FY26 10-K via SEC EDGAR; AInvest analysis, Aug 2026, explicitly contrasting the $1T keynote figure with the $3.2B filing RPO. Any panel text calling $500B / $1T "backlog" or "RPO" in the Micron/Alphabet sense is wrong; "management demand visibility" is the correct label.

### 2. Shortage driver: ~80% AI accelerator share; Q2 FY27 revenue $96.2B, Q3 guide ~$108B
**CONFIRMED**
- Share: Multiple 2026 trackers put Nvidia at ~80% (80–85% by revenue) of the data-center AI accelerator market, down from ~92% in 2023, with AMD at ~5–7%. Sources: Silicon Analysts, Apr 2026; Presenc AI chip market-share research, May 2026.
- Financials: Nvidia Q2 FY27 release, 2026-08-26: revenue $96.2B (+106% YoY, +18% QoQ), Data Center $89.0B, GAAP gross margin 75.0%; Q3 FY27 guidance $108.0B ± 2%, assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China. Sources: Nvidia release via StockTitan / Financial News, 2026-08-26.

### 3. Catalyst + date: next earnings 2026-11-17 — confirmed or estimated?
**CONFIRMED — company-scheduled, not merely estimated**
- At the close of the Q2 FY27 call (2026-08-26), Nvidia IR stated the Q3 FY27 earnings call is scheduled for November 17th. MarketWatch (Aug 2026) notes Nov 17, 2026 is a Tuesday and the first non-Wednesday report in three years, per the company's own announcement at the end of the call. Source: MarketBeat transcript of Nvidia Q2 2027 call; MarketWatch, Aug 2026.
- Caution for the panel: several third-party calendars still show conflicting estimates (TipRanks / TradingView: Nov 25, labelled estimated in their body text; Global X: Nov 18, estimated; Polymarket: Nov 18). Those are stale third-party projections and are superseded by the company's Nov 17 scheduling statement. The screen's 2026-11-17 is correct.

### 4. Moat / trap: hyperscaler customers are shipping / building their own ASICs (Google TPU, AWS Trainium, OpenAI–Broadcom) — live and evidenced?
**CONFIRMED — live, shipping, and scaled, not vapourware**
- Google TPU: v6 Trillium shipping, v7 Ironwood (Nov 2025) with large superpods; Google–Broadcom partnership through 2031; TPU capacity committed to external customers including Anthropic (1M+ chips). Sources: Hashrate Index hyperscaler ASIC report, mid-2026; 2026 ASIC coverage.
- AWS Trainium: Trainium2 deployed at 500,000+ scale, Trainium3 shipping early 2026 on 3nm with claimed 40–65% lower cost vs comparable Blackwell setups; Anthropic committed >$100B over 10 years on AWS centred on Trainium 2/3/4 (Apr 2026 agreement). Sources: Hashrate Index, mid-2026; TechCrunch via WebProNews summary, Oct 2026.
- OpenAI–Broadcom: agreement to deploy 10 GW of custom AI accelerators, deployments targeted H2 2026 through end-2029; Broadcom reported custom-silicon (AI semiconductor) revenue surging (e.g. $16.7B in the quarter ended 2026-08-02, +221% YoY, in AInvest's Oct 2026 summary) across six custom customers including Google, Anthropic and OpenAI. Sources: DC Market Insights via NYNewsly, Oct 2026; AInvest, Oct 2026.
- Assessment: the in-sourcing threat is evidenced by shipping silicon and gigawatt-scale commitments, concentrated in inference (about two-thirds of AI compute spend). It does not yet contradict Nvidia's ~80% share or Q2/Q3 numbers above — both can be true: ASICs are taking a growing slice, especially captive inference, while Nvidia still dominates merchant / training demand.
