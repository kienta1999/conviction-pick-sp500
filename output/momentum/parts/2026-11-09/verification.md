## CBOE (tentative winner)

### Claim 1 — SPX 0DTE ADV a record 3.2m contracts in Q3 2026, +34% YoY; 0DTE a record 65% of all SPX activity
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

- Cboe Q3 2026 earnings call, 30 Oct 2026 (transcript via AlphaStreet / StockAnalysis / Cboe's own earnings-call slides, s202.q4cdn.com): "0DTE SPX options, where 0DTE activity made up a record 65% of overall SPX activity" and, in Q&A, 0DTE "has averaged almost 3.2 million contracts in the third quarter".
- Zacks, 30 Oct 2026 ("CBOE Q3 Earnings Beat Estimates on Strong Options Volumes"): "SPX 0DTE average daily volume reached a record 3.2 million contracts, up 34%."
- Cboe September 2026 trading-volume release, 5 Oct 2026 (PR Newswire via Morningstar): "Quarterly SPX zero-days-to-expiry (0DTE) ADV record of 3.2 million contracts."
- Note: the call wording is "almost 3.2 million" — the 3.2m figure is a rounded record, not an overstatement.

### Claim 2 — Exclusive S&P Dow Jones Indices licence extended 25 years, through 2051, covering SPX and VIX options/futures (announced 30 Oct 2026)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED in substance — DATE CORRECTION (not material)**

- The 25-year extension through 2051 and the exclusivity are confirmed. Cboe Q3 call, 30 Oct 2026: "At the end of September, we signed a 25-year extension of our exclusive licensing agreement with S&P Dow Jones Indices. It is our longest agreement with S&P to date and extends our collaboration through 2051" and "we will continue to have the exclusive rights to offer trading in SPX options, and our exclusivity continues across the S&P options products we offer today, including Mini SPX, VIX, and S&P binary options."
- **Correction:** the extension was *not* announced on 30 Oct 2026. It was announced 29–30 Sep 2026 — Markets Media, 30 Sep 2026 ("Cboe, S&P DJI Extend Exclusive Licensing Agreement to 2051"), and The Coin Republic / CCN, 30 Sep 2026, both reporting the joint announcement of 29 Sep. The 30 Oct earnings call only reiterated it. The dossier's "announced 30 Oct 2026" dating is wrong by a month; the substance (25 years, through 2051, SPX/VIX exclusivity) is correct.
- Minor scope note: the joint announcement's headline wording is exclusive rights to SPX options; the call transcript extends the description to Mini-SPX, VIX and S&P binary options. "VIX futures" is not separately singled out in the sources checked — the claim's "options/futures" phrasing is a slight compression, not a contradiction.

### Claim 3 — Q3 2026: net revenue $712.2m, +18% YoY; adjusted diluted EPS $3.63, +36% YoY, beating $3.43 consensus
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

- Cboe Q3 2026 earnings release, 30 Oct 2026 (PR Newswire, reproduced by UK Newshour / Earnings Whispers): "Net Revenue for the Quarter of $712.2 million, Up 18 percent"; "Adjusted Diluted EPS for the Quarter of $3.63, Up 36 percent"; GAAP diluted EPS $4.08, +43%, a record.

The $3.43 EPS consensus is consistent across Zacks, MarketBeat and StockRow.

### Claim 4 — Cboe Predicts / KPI event contracts: SEC approved listing (13 Oct 2026); launch expected November 2026 with Robinhood and Interactive Brokers as launch partners
**Verdict: CONFIRMED (as an expected launch, not a firm date)**

- SEC approval: SEC Release No. 34-106659, File No. SR-CBOE-2026-061, dated 13 Oct 2026 (sec.gov) — order granting accelerated approval to list binary options on KPIs reported by certain issuers. Corroborated by Intelligize, 21 Oct 2026, and iGaming Times, 15 Oct 2026.
- November launch expectation: Cboe Q3 call, 30 Oct 2026 — "we have received SEC approval to expand Cboe Predicts with our offering of company KPI event contracts. Additionally, the SEC has approved our filing to temporarily clear these innovative contracts through Cboe Clear U.S. … and we anticipate a November product launch." Q&A: "we are targeting sometime in November, pending really clearing member broker and market maker readiness."
- Partners: same call — "In September, we announced Robinhood as our launch partner" and "Robinhood and Interactive Brokers will be there to support client access on day one" (Schwab also named as showing interest/support, not as a day-one launch partner).
- Caveat to carry, not a contradiction: mid-October reporting (CoinDesk via The Agent Bets, 15–16 Oct 2026) correctly noted trading could not begin until Cboe Clear U.S. registration was resolved; by the 30 Oct call Cboe said the SEC had approved temporary clearing through Cboe Clear U.S., which is what made the November target credible. The launch remains an *expectation* contingent on broker/market-maker readiness — no launch date had been fixed as of 30 Oct.

## Material corrections

1. **Claim 2 date — correct the announcement date.** The S&P licence extension was announced 29–30 Sep 2026, not 30 Oct 2026. The 30 Oct date is when it was reiterated on the Q3 earnings call. Substance (25 years, through 2051, SPX/VIX exclusivity) is confirmed.
2. **Claim 9 rounding — EPS growth is +15.5%, rounded to +16%.** Immaterial.
3. **Claim 4 status — November launch is a target, not a done deal.** As of the 30 Oct call it was "anticipated"/"targeting sometime in November, pending clearing member broker and market maker readiness." The earlier Cboe Clear U.S. blocker had been cleared by temporary SEC approval by 30 Oct, but no fixed launch date had been set.
4. **Claim 8 status — the ~30% Silvus 2027 growth is preliminary commentary**, not formal guidance (formal 2027 guidance due February 2027). Same applies to the ~$14.25B preliminary 2027 revenue figure cited in the dossier.

No figure was found to be wrong in a way that changes any thesis: no contradiction on any of the nine claims.
