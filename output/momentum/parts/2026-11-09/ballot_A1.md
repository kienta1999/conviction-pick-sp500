**2. Base +10–15% over 12–18 months as the backlog converts and preliminary 2027 revenue of ~$14.25B is delivered; bull +20–25% if Silvus/defence orders accelerate beyond that.

**3. CBOE:** The single most compelling data point is Q3 2026 SPX 0DTE average daily volume of a record 3.2 million contracts (+34% YoY), a record 65% of all SPX activity, with retail 0DTE volume +46% YoY — demand for a product only Cboe can legally supply, now locked in by the 25-year exclusive S&P Dow Jones Indices licence extension through 2051. Base +10–15% over 12–18 months if 0DTE volumes stay hot and event contracts (launch expected November 2026, with Robinhood and Interactive Brokers) add a new leg; bull +25–30% if retail volumes compound at the current pace.
