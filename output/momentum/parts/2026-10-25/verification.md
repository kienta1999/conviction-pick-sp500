## MU — Micron Technology

### 1. Backlog: ~$150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs, backed by ~$32B customer financial commitments, disclosed ~30 Sept 2026
**CONFIRMED.**
- Primary: Micron fiscal Q4 2026 earnings call, **Wednesday 2026-09-30** (quarter ended 2026-09-03). CFO Mark Murphy, verbatim in the call transcript (Longbridge / AlphaSpread / MarketBeat transcript reproductions): "To date, we have signed 26 SCAs in total, and our remaining performance obligations, or RPO, is approximately $150 billion. All SCAs have take-or-pay contracted volumes, and RPO reflects the contract value for only SCAs that have a determined pricing framework, which can be either a fixed price or subject to a pricing floor and ceiling. RPO is based on committed volumes and minimum pricing and is inherently conservative."
- CEO Sanjay Mehrotra on the same call: "financial commitments from customers have increased to $32 billion, the vast majority of which are cash deposits" for "the 26 signed SCAs"; SCAs are estimated at >35% of revenue through 2030, some extending into 2031.
- Corroboration: AIStockWire and SignalStack summaries of the Q4 FY26 8-K/call (2026-09-30 / 2026-10-02) report the same $150B RPO / 26 SCAs / $32B commitments, with $12.7B of customer deposits on the balance sheet at period end.
- Note for the panel: the $150B is a *minimum-price, pricing-framework-only* subset — management said actual revenue is expected to "well exceed" the RPO. The claim as written does not overstate it.

### 2. Shortage: HBM sold out for 2026 and 2027 with price/volume agreed (incl. HBM4); ~75% of FY27 shipments committed
**CONFIRMED — with one precision caveat on the 2027 leg (see below).**
- 2026 leg, verbatim from Micron's prepared remarks (fiscal Q1 2026 call, 2025-12-17, reproduced by TS2/Reuters coverage): Micron "has completed agreements on price and volume for its entire calendar 2026 HBM supply, including HBM4."
- 2027 leg, primary wording on the 2026-09-30 call (Mehrotra, transcript): "We have completed agreements for the vast majority of our calendar 2027 HBM bit supply with significant price increases year over year." Secondary coverage (Startup Fortune, 2026-09-30 report summary) phrases this as "HBM3E and HBM4 chips are sold out through calendar 2027."
- ~75% leg: MomoView's Q4 FY26 call summary (2026-09-30): "More than 75% of fiscal 2027 output is already committed"; MindCron's call write-up (2026-10-03): "More than 75 percent of Micron's total 2027 output is already committed, and … most talks with customers have moved to 2028."
- **Caveat (not a contradiction):** the company's own Sept-30 wording for 2027 HBM is "vast majority," not a literal 100% "sold out." "Sold out for 2027" is fair shorthand used by the press, but the panel should not model 2027 HBM as 100.0% contracted on this source.
