## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** FCX
- **Runner-up:** MU
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** FCX at $65.18 is the one shortage in this screen the market still prices as ordinary commodity beta rather than as a structural deficit — only +21.5% to the $79.20 mean target and ~15x forward earnings for the largest publicly traded copper producer, with ~25 years of reserves. The explosive leg is operating, not speculative: Grasberg ran at only 67% of normalised throughput in Q3 2026 after the Sep 2025 mudslide, with 80% targeted by mid-2027 and near-full by end-2027, so guided volumes rise >20% in H2 2026 and >20% again in 2027 into realised prices already guided above $6.50/lb. Every $0.10/lb of copper is ~$390M of EBITDA, so volume recovery and price multiply each other in a way the post-incident, volume-depressed earnings base hides. Crucially, this is the anti-trap name: a utility or data-centre builder cannot in-source an ore body, and copper is <0.5% of a data-centre project's cost, so demand does not ration away at high prices the way a supplier's customers ration away from it.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** IEA-flagged structural deficit — ~330kt refined-copper deficit projected for 2026 widening toward ~1.3Mt in 2027 and a ~30% supply shortfall by 2035, against 16–17-year discovery-to-production lead times; spot confirmation is FCX's realised $6.17/lb in Q2 2026 (+36% YoY) guided to exceed $6.50/lb in Q3, with Shanghai inventories at their lowest since 2023.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — irreplaceability 9/10 in the dossier; tier-one ore bodies (Grasberg, Morenci, Cerro Verde) are geologically scarce and un-permittable inside the horizon, buyers have no in-sourcing route, and the only bypasses are slow aluminium substitution and recycling. The INVENTORY_BUILD flag is benign: Q3 production (830M lb) exceeded sales (750M lb) on smelter-restart timing and an explicit Q3→Q4 gold deferral — earnings lag production, they do not run ahead of cash.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base +25–35% (~$82–88) on the Grasberg ramp at ~$6/lb copper; bull +60–80% (~$105–117) if copper holds near $7/lb, where management's sensitivity implies ~$20B EBITDA vs ~$13B at $5.
- **Top risk:** Commodity cyclicality with single-asset concentration — a China/global growth scare reverses the copper price leg fast ($0.10/lb ≈ $390M EBITDA cuts both ways), and any further Grasberg slip or Indonesian policy change hits the volume leg at the same time.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

### Top 3 — shortage/moat evidence and scenarios

**1. FCX ($65.18).** The single most compelling data point is the collision of a ~330kt 2026 refined deficit (widening for years, per IEA-derived estimates) with Grasberg at only 67% of normalised throughput — FCX's own supply is the shortage in miniature, and its recovery is scheduled, not hoped for. Add the leach pipeline (200M lb/yr now toward 800M lb/yr by 2030 at <$1/lb incremental cash cost) and the market is underpaying for volume it can already see. Base +25–35% (~$82–88); bull +60–80% (~$105–117) at ~$7/lb copper.

**2. MU ($877.55).** The data point that changes the old cyclical story is ~$150B of remaining performance obligations across 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements, backed by ~$32B of customer financial commitments — customers are pre-financing the fabs of a supplier they supposedly could squeeze. Yet after +343% in 12 months the stock still trades at only ~4.2x forward earnings: the market is explicitly refusing to capitalise these earnings, which is exactly the skepticism a contrarian wants on his side. I dock it below FCX for HBM share slippage (21% → 18% in 2Q26) and CXMT's advance in conventional DRAM. Base +30–45% (~$1,140–1,270); bull +75–90% (~$1,535–1,665, around the $1561.91 mean target) if shortage pricing holds into 2028.

**3. VRTX ($523.93).** No shortage (score 4/10) — the appeal is the opposite: a monopoly floor. Vertex is the only approved disease-modifying therapy for ~90% of the CF population, CF is ~96% of revenue, and that annuity funds optionality the $571.46 mean target barely prices: povetacicept's FDA PDUFA on 30 Nov 2026, in a franchise management says could one day rival CF, plus a Casgevy/Journavx ramp that must step up +53% half-on-half to hit ≥$500M in 2026. Downside is a cash-cow multiple, upside is a second franchise — the asymmetry the priced-to-perfection names lack. Base +15–25% (~$600–655); bull +40–55% (~$735–810) on a povetacicept approval and a credible non-CF ramp.
