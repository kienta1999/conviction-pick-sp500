## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** SNDK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron at $877.55 is the fastest large-cap compounder in this screen by every growth metric that matters: TTM revenue growth of 157%, latest-quarter revenue +379% YoY with revenue acceleration of +2.22, fiscal Q4 2026 revenue of $54.23B vs. $11.32B a year earlier, and fiscal Q1 2027 guided to ~$61.5B with sequential growth guided for every quarter of FY2027. No other name combines triple-digit growth, upward revisions of this magnitude and a single-digit forward multiple.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Remaining performance obligations of ~$150 billion under 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements (up from ~$100B / 16 SCAs one quarter earlier), backed by ~$32 billion of customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits — customers are pre-financing Micron's fabs because HBM, conventional DRAM and NAND are simultaneously short, with HBM demand estimated 50–67% above available supply.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — Micron is one of only three leading-edge HBM/DRAM suppliers on earth and no hyperscaler operates a memory fab; crucially, the in-sourcing trap does not bite here, because every custom accelerator the hyperscalers build for themselves (TPU, Trainium, Maia, MTIA) still needs someone else's HBM — usually Micron's, SK hynix's or Samsung's — and Micron is co-designing a custom HBM4E with Nvidia, which is design-in lock-in, not spot commodity selling. The real risks are share shifts among the three suppliers (HBM share slipped 21% → 18% in 2Q26) and CXMT in conventional DRAM, not customer in-sourcing.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +55–70% (to roughly $1,360–$1,490, toward the $1,561.91 mean target) as FY2027 delivers the guided record year on contracted volumes; bull +110–130% (to roughly $1,840–$2,020) if the shortage extends toward 2028 as SK hynix's CEO has warned, DRAM ASP momentum holds, and the market begins capitalising even part of the $150B RPO beyond peak-cycle earnings.
- **Top risk:** Cyclicality priced as if it ended — if DRAM ASP momentum (already moderating from +58–63% QoQ in 2Q26) rolls over, or CXMT breaks conventional-DRAM pricing where Micron's recent margin gains came from, earnings fall faster than the 4.24× forward multiple can expand.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 9
