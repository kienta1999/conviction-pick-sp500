## MU — Claim 1

**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

Claim: Remaining performance obligations ~$150B across 26 signed multi-year strategic customer agreements, with $32B in customer financial commitments (mostly cash deposits), covering ~35% of revenue through 2030 and >75% of fiscal 2027 output committed, attributed to Micron's fiscal Q4 FY26 release, 30 Sep 2026.

Evidence: Micron's fiscal Q4 FY2026 earnings-call transcript (CFO Mark Murphy, prepared remarks, 30 Sep 2026, via MarketBeat/Longbridge transcript): "To date, we have signed 26 SCAs in total, and our remaining performance obligations, or RPO, is approximately $150 billion." The same transcript and contemporaneous coverage (AIStockWire, 30 Sep 2026; Zacks, 30 Sep 2026) report $32B in customer financial commitments, "the vast majority" in cash deposits, SCAs covering more than 35% of revenue through 2030, and more than 75% of fiscal 2027 output already committed (TechRepublic/MomoView/Particle summaries of the 30 Sep 2026 call use the "fiscal 2027" wording).

Caveat (scope, not a contradiction): RPO counts only SCAs with a determined pricing framework (fixed price or floor/ceiling), is based on committed volumes and minimum pricing, and management called it "inherently conservative" — it is a floor for those agreements, not total SCA value or recognised revenue. Also note a wording split in secondary coverage: some summaries say "calendar 2027 output" rather than "fiscal 2027 output" for the >75% figure; the substance (>75% of 2027 output committed) is the same, but the exact fiscal/calendar label should be quoted with care.

Sources and dates: Micron fiscal Q4 FY2026 earnings call, 30 Sep 2026; AIStockWire, 30 Sep 2026; Zacks, 30 Sep 2026; TechRepublic, Sep/Oct 2026.

## NVDA — Claim 4

**Verdict: CONFIRMED for the $500bn figure; the ~$307bn derivation is flagged (see caveat)**

Claim: $500B of Blackwell + Rubin revenue visibility spanning calendar 2025–2026 (GTC Washington, 28 Oct 2026), with a cleaner forward figure near ~$307bn after excluding already-shipped chips (~30% of the $500bn).

Evidence for $500bn: At GTC Washington on 28 Oct 2026, Jensen Huang cited $500bn of Blackwell + Rubin revenue through calendar 2026 (Webull/Fool coverage of GTC, 28 Oct 2026; ts2.tech, Oct 2026, citing Reuters: Huang "disclosed Nvidia's jaw-dropping $500 billion backlog of AI chip orders" at the 28 Oct event). The same figure was also stated earlier: Data Center Dynamics, reporting the Q2 FY2027 earnings period, quotes CFO Colette Kress: "We currently have visibility to $0.5 trillion in Blackwell and Rubin revenue from the start of this year through the end of calendar year 2026," explicitly excluding the then-newest Anthropic and expanded HUMAIN deals. A later Webull earnings-call report confirms the $500bn was "previously announced" at GTC and that ~$150bn had already been shipped at that point.

Evidence for ~$307bn: Secondary coverage does report a trued-up figure near $307bn — "Reports suggest that the trued-up backlog figure is closer to $307 billion, which should be recognized over the next year or so" (Webull/Fool, Oct 2026); a separate AInvest summary likewise says "Even adjusted to $307 billion" — so the figure itself is attested, not invented by the dossier.

Caveats — material to how the claim should be quoted:
1. Attribution: the $500bn figure did not originate at GTC on 28 Oct 2026; it was given on the Q2 FY2027 earnings call (26 Aug 2026) and restated/reaffirmed at GTC. Quoting GTC as the sole origin is imprecise.
2. The $500bn is a visibility/cumulative-revenue claim, not formal guidance and not a forward-only backlog: it spans 2025 to date plus Rubin, and includes product already shipped.
3. The ~$307bn derivation as briefed does not arithmetically reconcile: $500bn minus ~30% (~$150bn) already shipped is ~$350bn, not ~$307bn. The published breakdowns differ from each other (one cites ~$150bn already shipped; another cites $100bn already shipped plus $193bn of ancillary networking inside the $500bn). The ~$307bn should therefore be quoted only as a secondary estimate ("reported trued-up figure"), not as a clean $500bn-minus-shipped calculation, and its exact components were not independently confirmed from a primary Nvidia source in this pass.

Sources and dates: Data Center Dynamics, Q2 FY2027 earnings period (Aug 2026); Webull/Fool GTC coverage, 28 Oct 2026; Webull earnings-call report (post-GTC, Oct/Nov 2026); AInvest summary, 2026.

## Material framing flags (beyond the nine verdicts)

1. NVDA $500bn: as in Claim 4, do not present it as a forward-only order backlog or as formal guidance, and do not attribute its origin solely to GTC on 28 Oct 2026 — it was first given on the 26 Aug 2026 earnings call and restated at GTC. The ~$307bn "cleaned" figure is a secondary estimate with inconsistent published breakdowns; quoting it as $500bn minus 30% shipped is arithmetically wrong ($500bn − $150bn = $350bn).

Presenting $150bn as the full backlog value understates the agreements on management's own terms, while presenting it as recognised revenue would overstate it.
3. MU HBM shares: ranking is stable, but the Samsung share is ~33% on Q2 2026 all-HBM measures and ~28% on HBM4-only forecasts — cite the measure with the number.
