# Panelist D (Contrarian / risk skeptic) — Momentum ballot, 2026-10-29

## 1. Single-pick ballot

- Top pick: MU
- Runner-up: FCX
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Micron is the case the market is still pricing as a cycle that must collapse, despite a contracted order book that reaches through 2030. At a 4.75x forward PE on the screen and 58.9% analyst upside to a $1,561.91 mean target from the $983.23 screen price, the market is discounting earnings that customers have already put cash deposits behind. Unlike a pure spot-price bet, Micron's shortage is locked in by multi-year agreements, and it sits in a three-maker oligopoly that hyperscalers cannot replicate by building their own fabs. That combination — disbelieved earnings, contracted demand, and an in-sourcing-proof supply chain — is exactly where explosive upside hides for a skeptic.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Remaining performance obligations of ~$150B across 26 signed multi-year strategic customer agreements, with $32B in customer financial commitments (mostly cash deposits), covering ~35% of revenue through 2030 and >75% of fiscal 2027 output committed.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — only three vertically integrated memory makers exist at scale (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron), with fab capital intensity ($50B+ FY27 capex guided), process leadership (1-gamma DRAM, HBM3E/HBM4 roadmap) and multi-year qualification lock-in; the realistic threat is substitution toward SK Hynix, the HBM leader, not hyperscalers in-sourcing DRAM/HBM fabs themselves.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case: Base — backlog converts roughly as guided, with sequential revenue growth expected every quarter of FY27, moving the stock toward the analyst mean target of $1,561.91 (+58.9% from the $983.23 screen price). Bull — calendar-2027 HBM supply, already mostly under agreement at significantly higher prices, reprices the earnings base upward and the market stops applying a cycle-collapse multiple, pushing the stock above that mean target.
- Top risk: Memory cyclicality — industry-wide fab additions landing in 2028–29, plus CXMT's progress, could flip shortage to glut, and Micron is only #3 in HBM behind SK Hynix.
- Conviction (1-10): 8

### Top 3 detail

**1. MU:** The single most compelling data point is the ~$150B of remaining performance obligations with $32B in customer cash commitments, alongside entire calendar-2026 HBM supply fully allocated and most calendar-2027 HBM supply already under agreement at significantly higher prices. That is not a demand forecast, it is contracted, deposit-backed volume. Base case over 12–18 months is movement toward the $1,561.91 analyst mean target (+58.9% from the $983.23 screen price) as FY27 guidance for sequential quarterly revenue growth is delivered; the bull case is a re-rating above that target if the higher-priced 2027 HBM agreements hold and the market concedes the backlog de-risks the cycle.

**2. FCX:** The most compelling point is the volume snap-back embedded in guidance — copper sales of ~3.0bn lbs in 2026 rising to ~3.7bn lbs in 2027 (>20% growth) and >4.0bn lbs in 2028, with Q4 2026 gold sales guided at ~300k oz versus just 105k oz in Q3 — against a documented 2026 refined-copper deficit (ICSG ~150,000 tonnes; J.P. Morgan ~330,000 tonnes). The market is still pricing Grasberg's disruption, not its recovery, which needs no new capital for the 2026→2028 step. Base case is movement toward the $78.75 screen mean target (+18.2% from $66.60), with the latest cited Buy target of $84 as the bull-side marker if Grasberg reaches 80% of capacity by mid-2027 and copper prices stay near record levels.

**3. SNDK:** The most compelling point is the price action itself — TrendForce projected enterprise SSD prices +~235% in total during 2026, with NAND contract prices +15–20% q/q in 4Q26, and Gartner cited as expecting NAND prices +30% in 2027 after a +293% jump in 2026 — flowing to an 84.6% gross margin in fiscal Q4 FY26. That is maximum octane, but it is also maximum binary risk: consensus (~$10.6B revenue / ~$46 EPS) sits at or above the top of fiscal Q1 FY27 guidance ($10.3–10.8B / $44–46) for the print due after the close today, 2026-10-29. Base case is a move toward the ~$2,173 consensus average target if the print clears that bar; the bull case is toward the cited high targets ($2,745 Susquehanna, $3,000 Bernstein) if Citi's view that NAND stays undersupplied in 2027 and 2028 proves out.

Deliberately left out of my top 10: NEM, CF, FANG — all fungible commodity price-takers with no order backlog, NEM with falling production (5.89 to 5.26 Moz guided), CF with a war-driven squeeze that already retraced and a Q2 earnings miss, and FANG with the lowest irreplaceability in the dossier (2/10) and a war premium the IEA's 2027 supply-recovery forecast threatens.
