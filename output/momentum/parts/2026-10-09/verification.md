- **2026 WFE outlook low-$150B** — CONFIRMED.
  Sources: Zacks via TradingView: KLA "raised its calendar 2026 wafer fabrication equipment market expectation, including advanced packaging, to the low-$150-billion range, up from its earlier view of more than $140 billion"; equityswarm.com: "raised its full-year 2026 wafer equipment market outlook to the low $150 billion range, up from earlier expectations slightly above $140 billion."

So ">$100B" understates the current guidance rather than contradicting it.
- **~70% custom-AI-ASIC market share** — CONFIRMED (secondary sources only; not a company-stated metric).

Widely repeated analyst/press figure; no primary Broadcom disclosure located.
