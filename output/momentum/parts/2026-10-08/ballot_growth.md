## Ballot 1 — Single-pick nomination

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** AVGO

**Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron prints the most violent revenue acceleration in the 15-name field — fiscal Q4 2026 revenue \$54.2B, **+379% YoY**, with an 86% gross margin and adjusted EPS \$33.42, then guided *higher* (Q1 FY2027 \~\$61.5B revenue, \~\$38.15 EPS), i.e., the revisions vector is still up, not rolling over. It trades at \~5x forward earnings while growing revenue ~3.8x and earnings 10.6x — the cheapest forward multiple in the field attached to the fastest acceleration, which is exactly what a momentum lens wants: maximum compounding speed per multiple paid. Morgan Stanley called it the best revenue/earnings beat in US semiconductor history outside Nvidia, and the conversation has already shifted from "peak performance" to "sustainability." Two consecutive quarters of raised guidance, strategic customer agreements doubling 16 → 26 in three months, and a \$150B contracted order book say this is a demand repricing, not a one-quarter pop.

**Key shortage/backlog evidence:** \~\$150B in remaining performance obligations disclosed at the Sept 30, 2026 earnings print — every agreement take-or-pay, and the figure counts *only* agreements with set pricing frameworks, which CFO Mark Murphy called "inherently conservative." Customers put up \$32B in financial commitments, "the vast majority of which are cash deposits." The 26 strategic customer agreements cover >35% of revenue through 2030 (some into 2031). This is the single most contracted revenue book in the field — nearly three quarters of annualized revenue already committed.

**Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low-to-medium. Only three companies on earth produce HBM at AI-grade scale (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron); hyperscalers build their own AI chips but sign multi-year *memory supply agreements* with Micron rather than in-source memory fabs — none can. The real threat is oligopoly overbuild when 2028 cleanroom capacity lands, not customer disintermediation. Dossier score: 8/10 irreplaceability.

**12-18 month return scenario:** base — Q1 FY2027 guide (\$61.5B / \$38.15 EPS) is met or slightly beaten, HBM 2027 price surcharges flow through, the stock rerates from \~5x forward toward 8-10x forward on contracted visibility → +40-70% from current levels. Bull — memory shortage persists past 2028, take-or-pay repricing converts 2027 into a second 379%-style step-up, multiple reaches even 12-15x → +100%+ in 12-18 months. Downside is a 2028 overbuild breaking price discipline, so sizing respects that this is peak-cycle.

**Top risk:** the classic memory cycle — 2028 cleanroom capacity gets built by *all three* players (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron), and only ~a third of revenue is covered by the long-term agreements, so a supply-discipline break would hit peak earnings at a peak-cycle multiple.

**Conviction (1-10):** 9

**AVGO.** The single most compelling point is the AI backlog's contracted certainty: \~\$73B "expected to ship over the next 18 months" with six hyperscalers on multi-year agreements through 2028, Q3 AI semiconductor revenue \$16.7B (+221% YoY) accelerating to a Q4 guide of \$21.7B (+236% YoY), and Hock Tan's "line of sight to over \$100 billion" in FY2027 AI chip revenue — roughly doubling the current run rate. 12-18mo: base +30-50% as the \$73B converts to revenue and FY2027 AI revenue lands \~\$100B+; bull +80%+ if the OpenAI >\$50B financing closes before year-end and the >\$100B line of sight becomes a firm backlog. **SNDK.** The single most compelling point is the allocation mechanism: "memory bits on allocation well beyond calendar 2027," manufacturing capacity "completely sold out," two-thirds of fiscal Q4's sequential revenue growth from *pricing* not volume, and NAND contract prices up ~60% in Q1 2026 with another 70-75% forecast for Q2 — the shortage is flowing through as price, the fastest route to margin explosion. 12-18mo: base +30-50% if NAND pricing holds into 2027 against a forward PE of ~6.6; bull +100%+ if the structural crunch (Samsung/SK Hynix shrinking NAND for HBM, capacity down >40%) persists to 2028. Offset: #5 NAND position, already +~500% YTD with two 30%+ drawdowns this year — this is the most violent ride of the three.
