## Ballot 1 — single-pick nomination

- **Top pick: MU**
- **Runner-up: GOOGL**

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The market still prices Micron as a spot-commodity memory cycler — forward P/E ~5.0, trailing ~14.3 — while the business model has structurally converted to contracted offtake: ~\$150B of take-or-pay remaining performance obligations that count *only* agreements with set pricing frameworks (CFO called the figure "inherently conservative"), 26 strategic customer agreements covering >35% of revenue through 2030 (some to 2031), and \$32B in customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits. HBM demand runs an estimated 50–67% above available supply, 2026 HBM is fully sold out under binding agreements, and the shortage consensus runs to at least 2028 with no fresh cleanroom capacity online until late 2028. This is the first memory cycle where the downturn has a contracted floor — the bear case is cushioned by take-or-pay volumes, yet the multiple prices a normal cycle collapse. The mismatch between contracted-cash reality and commodity-multiple pricing is the underappreciated gap this lens is built to find.

- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** \$150B in take-or-pay RPO with \$32B in customer cash deposits — "more than sold out," per the CEO, with customers shifting to 3–5 year supply agreements instead of quarterly spot negotiation.

- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Medium. Dossier irreplaceability 8/10: only three companies on earth produce AI-grade HBM at scale, and hyperscalers sign multi-year *memory supply agreements* with Micron rather than building fabs — the in-sourcing threat is effectively nil (their in-sourcing is in AI chips, not memory). But it stops short of low risk because the credible threat is oligopoly overbuild: the 2028 cleanrooms get built by *all three* players, not just Micron, and only about a third of revenue is covered by the long-term agreements.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+25–40% — the \$150B book converts to cash on take-or-pay schedules, earnings deliver on the record guide (~\$61.5B fiscal Q1 2027 revenue, ~\$38.15 EPS), and the stock closes part of the gap to the \$1,535.57 analyst mean target (+44–47% implied from ~\$1,046–1,064) without a full multiple rerate. Bull ~+60–80%+ — the market starts pricing contracted earnings quality, rerating the forward multiple from ~5x toward 8–10x on growing contracted coverage, amplified by 2027 HBM price surcharges management is already pushing because fixed contracts locked it out of this year's surge.

- **Top risk:** The classic memory-cycle overbuild — 2028 cleanroom capacity arrives built by Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron together, supply discipline breaks, and NAND/DRAM prices roll over violently. Only ~1/3 of revenue is under the long-term agreements, industry-wide expansion pace isn't bound by them, and the stock is already up ~200% in 12 months, so entry risk compounds the cycle risk.

- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
