## BALLOT 1 — SINGLE PICK

- **Top pick:** AVGO
- **Runner-up:** APH

**Thesis (3-5 sentences):**
Broadcom is the only name in this field guiding a **+32% sequential** revenue step (~$29.4B in Q3 FY26 vs $22.2B delivered in Q2, +84% y/y) — the steepest forward acceleration in the entire 14-name field, and my lens pays for exactly that. The acceleration is not a one-quarter artifact: AI semiconductor revenue was $10.8B in Q2, **+143% y/y**, FY26 AI revenue is guided to ~$56B (**+~180%**), and management has put a **$100B+ FY27 AI semiconductor target** on the table underpinned by multi-year contracted programs. Unlike the trailing-backlog names, Broadcom's forward book was *disclosed and quantified this quarter*: **$30B of AI bookings against $10.8B shipped, a 2.8x book-to-bill**, with an implied **$200B+ 18-month backlog** through FY27. The revision cycle is still moving up — Q3 guidance was set above the delivered quarter by a third, and the Apollo/Blackstone **$35B AI XPV vehicle for 20GW of capacity through 2028** removes the financing constraint on that demand rather than leaving it as a hope. Critically for a momentum lens, the price has *not* front-run the numbers: AVGO sits **-13% from its 52-week high** with a **$528 mean target (+26.2%)** still ahead of spot, so unlike ANET, GE and HWM I am not buying a name that has already run past the sell side.

**Key shortage/backlog evidence:**
**$30B of AI semiconductor bookings in Q2 FY26 against $10.8B shipped — a ~2.8x book-to-bill on the AI line alone**, with management confirming an implied $200B+ 18-month backlog covering 2H FY26 through FY2027. Hock Tan's stated cause is customers queueing for chip availability, power and memory — i.e. supply is the binding constraint.

**Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: MEDIUM (7/10 per dossier).**
The classic trap is inverted here — Broadcom's business *is* the in-sourcing. Its customers hire it precisely to route around Nvidia, so the "hyperscaler builds it themselves" risk is a step removed: to displace Broadcom a customer must build a full custom-silicon organisation (SerDes, packaging, IP libraries, TSMC relationship). Programs are 2-3 year co-developments with contracted multi-year revenue (Anthropic 1GW in 2026 → 3GW in 2027; OpenAI first-gen XPU shipping 2027 at 1GW+), and Broadcom holds **70%+ of custom AI accelerator design services** with Marvell the only real #2. The genuine risk that keeps this medium rather than low: **Google has progressively internalised TPU design over successive generations** — the precedent that the anchor customer can erode content exists — and there is no CUDA-equivalent software lock-in. Losses in this business are binary and abrupt.

**12-18 month return scenario:**
- **Base: +20% to +30%** (~$500-545). Tracks the $528 mean target as Q3's guided $29.4B lands and FY27 consensus EPS of $17.54 gets underwritten; ~30x FY27 is roughly today's multiple applied to delivered numbers.
- **Bull: +50% to +60%** (~$630-670). Requires FY27 tracking toward the **$100B+ AI semiconductor target** (vs ~$56B in FY26), which forces FY27 EPS estimates above the current $17.54 and lets the market keep capitalising the acceleration at the ~36x it currently pays on FY26 earnings.

**Top risk:**
Program concentration. A handful of XPU programs drive roughly half of revenue and the losses, when they come, are total and abrupt — plus the **$100B FY2027 AI target is now embedded in the price**, so delivering "only" $80B would be a severe de-rating. Secondary: the circularity of Broadcom part-financing (via the $35B Apollo/Blackstone vehicle) the customers who buy its chips, which means demand and financing could fail together if AI credit tightens.

**Conviction: 8 / 10**

**2. APH — Amphenol.**
Single most compelling datapoint: **record $10.7B of orders with a 1.23:1 book-to-bill**, Q2 revenue +55% y/y (+30% organic) with IT datacom +89% USD / +63% organic at 43% of sales, and a **second 2026 price increase of +5-15% effective 2026-07-01** — pricing power confirmed in a market where upstream inputs (CCL +70%, MLCC +200-300%) are exploding. Base +15-20% toward the $198 mean target; bull +35-40% if Q3 lands at the top of the guide and the CommScope raise repeats, which would push the multiple onto materially higher numbers.

**3. LRCX — Lam Research.**
Single most compelling datapoint: **September-quarter guidance of $8.1B ±$400M against $6.72B just delivered — over +20% sequential off a fourth consecutive record quarter**, with the company having raised its CY2026 WFE forecast twice in two months (~$140B on 2026-06-02, then the "low $150B range" on 2026-07-29). Gross margin of 52% is a 20-year high and inventory turns of 3.0x say the ramp is clean. The only reason it is not higher: it **fell ~27% from its 2026-06-30 high in five weeks while printing records**, which is the market pricing cycle durability, not the quarter. Base +15-20% to the $368 mean target; bull +45-55% toward the Susquehanna/Cantor $475-500 targets if the memory cycle runs through 2027 as management's "extraordinary" framing implies.
