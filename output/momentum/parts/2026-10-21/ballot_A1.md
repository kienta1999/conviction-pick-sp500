## 1) SINGLE-PICK ballot

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** NVDA
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is the only name where the shortage is contractual, prepaid, and sold out — not inferred from price or orders commentary. HBM is sold out for calendar 2026 and, per later reporting in the dossier, through 2027, with Micron able to meet only about one-half to two-thirds of its largest AI customers' HBM demand. That scarcity is locked into ~$150B of remaining performance obligations under 26 multi-year take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements covering over 35% of revenue through 2030, with more than 75% of fiscal 2027 output already committed. No other name in the 14 combines this duration, this prepayment, and a 4.4x forward P/E. On a supply-chain lens, visibility *is* the thesis.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** ~$150B RPO (up from ~$100B in June, at SCA minimum prices); 26 SCAs (up from 16); ~$32B in customer financial commitments with $12.7–12.9B of customer cash deposits actually on the balance sheet; DRAM contract prices topped +170% YoY in Q3 2026, DDR5 spot +15% since the last guidance update alone, and TrendForce expects average HBM prices to rise ~121% in 2027; new fabs deliver no significant volume until late 2028, and Micron is racing to roughly double HBM output to ~100,000 wafers/month by end-2026.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — hyperscalers cannot in-source HBM (only three firms make it at scale, qualification cycles are long, and Micron is co-developing Nvidia's first custom HBM design), so there is no bypass around the category; the real risk is substitution *within* it — Micron is #3 at 18% HBM revenue share vs SK hynix 50% and Samsung 33% (Counterpoint, Q2 2026), Samsung is re-entering with HBM4, and most SCA revenue carries floor *and ceiling* pricing that caps upside if supply loosens in 2028.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** rough base ~+50-70% toward the screen analyst mean (~$1,560 vs $911.63 close, +71.2% upside) if SCA floors hold and HBM4 pricing steps up as guided; bull ~+90%+ if the shortage extends through 2027 at rising HBM prices and fiscal 2027's >75%-committed output reprices above SCA minimums — downside is a memory-cycle turn, not in-sourcing.
- **Top risk:** Cyclicality at peak pricing — ~86% gross margin and +170% DRAM contracts are peak markers, ~65% of revenue is *not* under SCA, industry wafer capacity is forecast to grow ~20% in 2027 and new fabs land late 2028; plus the Netlist ITC complaint and a $500M patent license charge already in FQ4.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 9
