# Panelist B — Growth / Momentum Lens — Momentum Panel 2026-10-21

Lens: revenue acceleration, earnings revisions, and price momentum first. I want the fastest compounder whose numbers are still inflecting up, and I penalise hard where the doctrine trap — disintermediation / in-sourcing — is already taking share.

## 1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** NVDA
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is the fastest compounder in the field by an order of magnitude: screen revenue growth TTM +256%, latest-quarter revenue +346% YoY with revenue acceleration of +89pp, EPS +1,003% YoY, and 8/8 EPS beats over 8 quarters — and the price confirms it, +87% in 6 months and +341% in 12 months, the strongest momentum tape of all 14 names. Crucially this is not just a spot-price spike: FQ4 revenue was $54.23B (+379% YoY) with FQ1 FY27 guided to $61.5B at ~86% gross margin, so the revision direction is still up at a forward P/E of only 4.4 with +71.2% analyst upside. NVDA is the runner-up on the same logic at larger scale — revenue +106% YoY, +48pp acceleration, ~70% FY28 growth pre-guided — but its 6-month price momentum (+9%) has stalled versus MU's, so it compounds second, not first.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** HBM is sold out for calendar 2026 and, per later reporting, through 2027, with Micron able to meet only about one-half to two-thirds of its largest AI customers' HBM demand; ~$150B of remaining performance obligations across 26 multi-year take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements (up from ~$100B / 16 SCAs in June), >75% of fiscal 2027 output already committed, and ~$32B of customer financial commitments including $12.7–12.9B of cash deposits actually on the balance sheet. Pricing corroborates it: DRAM contract prices topped +170% YoY in Q3 2026 and TrendForce expects average HBM prices to rise ~121% in 2027 as HBM4 takes over.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Low-medium. Disintermediation in the doctrine sense is essentially impossible here — no hyperscaler can fab leading-edge DRAM/HBM, only three firms on earth make HBM at scale, and qualification cycles plus a first custom HBM design with Nvidia lock Micron in; the real risk is substitution *between* the three suppliers (Micron is #3 at 18% HBM revenue share vs SK hynix 50% and Samsung 33%, and its share fell from 21%), plus SCA floor-and-ceiling pricing capping upside once 2028 capacity lands — share-shift risk, not in-sourcing risk.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~$1,400 (+~55% from $911.63) if the shortage holds through 2027 and SCA floors convert the guided $61.5B quarter into sustained prints; bull ~$1,750 (+~90%) if HBM4 pricing (+~121% expected in 2027) and the doubling of HBM output to ~100k wafers/month drive another leg of estimate raises toward the upper analyst targets (screen mean $1,560; targets cited up to $1,600–$3,000).
- **Top risk:** This is peak memory pricing — ~86% gross margin and +170% contract prices invite the cycle turn when industry wafer capacity grows ~20% in 2027 and new fabs land late 2028; with the stock already +341% in 12 months, any HBM content/spec downgrade by AI customers or faster Samsung share gains compresses both earnings and the multiple at once.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 9
