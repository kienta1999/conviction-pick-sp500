## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: LLY
- Runner-up: MSI
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): LLY is the only name in the field where demand has exceeded supply since late 2023 and the constraint is literally how much it can manufacture, not how much it can sell — Q3 2026 revenue was $24.4bn, +39% YoY, with volume +53% YoY while realised prices fell 15%. At $1,347.11, -6.7% off its 52-week high, +20.8% above its 200-day SMA, with TTM revenue growth of 37.2% and forward PE 26.48, the momentum and the shortage point the same way.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Q3 2026 incretins — Mounjaro $10.72bn (+64.6% YoY) and Zepbound $5.35bn (+49.2%) — were 66.6% of the $24.4bn quarter, and the dossier's own summary is that "Lilly's revenue trajectory through 2027 is largely a function of capacity, not demand"; the 2026 capacity ramp targets $45–50bn of supportable incretin revenue and 2027 above $60bn, versus ~$30bn in 2025.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — no customer can in-source this. Patients and payers do not manufacture drugs; the moat is patents on tirzepatide/retatrutide/orforglipron plus sterile fill-finish and synthetic-peptide API capacity that takes 3–4 years and billions to build, FDA-qualified site by site. The real threat is competitor supply (Novo, compounding pharmacies), not customer in-sourcing — dossier irreplaceability 8/10.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case — Base: ~+15–25% (continued capacity-led volume growth roughly tracking the analyst mean target of $1,470.06, +9.1%, plus further guidance raises as Lebanon capacity scales). Bull: ~+35–50% (retatrutide BLA in Q1 2027 and oral Foundayo ramp land cleanly, capacity comes on ahead of schedule, and the market pays up for a second leg of 40%+ revenue growth).
- Top risk: Price, not volume — realised prices already fell 15% in Q3 on US payer pressure, incretins are two-thirds of revenue, and Novo's oral Wegovy pill (265k+ weekly prescriptions by Jul 2026) plus compounding are the live competitive threats; valuation is ~46x TTM EPS, so flawless execution is already priced in.
- Conviction (1-10): 8
