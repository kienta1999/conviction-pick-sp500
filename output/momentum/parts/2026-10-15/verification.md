## MU — Micron Technology

### MU-1. Backlog: ~$150B RPO, 26 take-or-pay SCAs, $32B customer commitments, disclosed 30 Sep 2026
**CONFIRMED, with nuance.**
- Multiple independent reports of the 30 Sep 2026 FQ4 FY26 call/release agree: SCAs rose from 16 to 26, remaining performance obligations of about $150B, customer financial commitments of $32B (mostly cash deposits, $12.3B received in FQ4 alone), SCAs covering >35% of revenue through 2030, some extending into 2031. Sources: iFAST/Minichart broker summary of the call, 6 Oct 2026; AI Stock Wire report of the 30 Sep 2026 earnings, ~1 Oct 2026; Carbon Finance earnings review, ~9 Oct 2026.
- Nuance the memo must carry: take-or-pay applies to the volume commitments across the agreements, but the $150B figure is computed at committed volumes and minimum pricing only for agreements with a defined pricing framework (about three-quarters of projected SCA revenue). The remainder is repriced periodically / market-exposed. Management characterised the $150B as inherently conservative for that reason. Reporting it as "$150B priced backlog across all 26 SCAs" would overstate it.

### MU-2. Shortage driver: HBM sold out CY2026; vast majority of CY2027 contracted at higher prices; 2027/2028 tighter than 2026; FQ4 FY26 revenue $54.23B; FQ1 FY27 guide $61.5B ±$1.5B
**CONFIRMED (composite — components date from different disclosures).**
- FQ4 FY26 revenue $54.23B and FQ1 FY27 guide $61.5B ±$1.5B: company release / 8-K filed 30 Sep 2026, corroborated by CNBC-reported consensus comparisons the same day. Note FQ4 was a 14-week quarter (fiscal 2026 is 53 weeks), which flatters sequential growth — not a contradiction, a comparability caveat.
- Vast majority of CY2027 HBM bit supply already agreed, at significantly / much higher prices year over year: CEO prepared remarks on the 30 Sep 2026 call, reported by The Next Platform, 2 Oct 2026, and BigGo/earnings-call coverage, 30 Sep 2026.
- Greater tightness in 2027 and 2028 than in 2026, demand exceeding supply in both years, no line of sight to balance: CEO on the same 30 Sep 2026 call, reported by The Register, 1 Oct 2026.
- HBM sold out for CY2026: this specific wording comes from an earlier disclosure — Micron's Dec 2025 earnings materials stating price/volume agreements completed for its entire CY2026 HBM supply including HBM4, reported 18 Dec 2025. It was not re-stated in that form on 30 Sep 2026 (by then the forward claim had moved to CY2027). The claim is accurate, but the memo should not date the "sold out 2026" line to the September call.

### MU-3. Catalyst/date: next earnings 2026-12-23 — confirmed or estimated?
**UNVERIFIED as confirmed — it is ESTIMATED.**
- MarketBeat lists 23 Dec 2026 as "After Market Closes — Estimated" and states the date is estimated for Wednesday 23 Dec 2026. TipRanks shows the same date and its own text states the upcoming date is estimated from past reporting schedules, despite a "Confirmed" tag in one table header — internal inconsistency in the aggregator, resolved in favour of its explicit estimated wording.
- No Micron company announcement of the FQ1 FY27 date was found as of 15 Oct 2026. Treat 2026-12-23 as a well-grounded estimate (matches the prior-year Wednesday pattern), not a confirmed catalyst date.

### MU-4. Moat: only ~3 firms make HBM at scale; Micron ~18–21% HBM share
**CONFIRMED.**
- Only three major HBM suppliers (SK hynix, Samsung, Micron): Reuters characterisation repeated in Dec 2025 coverage and consistent across all share trackers found; no fourth scaled supplier appears in any source.
- Share range is exactly the last two reported quarters: Counterpoint Research via Korea Herald / The Investor, 3–4 Sep 2026 — Micron 21% of HBM revenue in Q1 2026, slipping to 18% in Q2 2026 (SK hynix 50%, Samsung 33% in Q2). The 18–21% range brackets reality; note the direction is down, and Samsung took the share — the memo should not present 21% as current.

## KLAC — KLA Corporation

### KLAC-1. Backlog: $12.57B at 30 Jun 2026, up ~60% from $7.86B a year earlier
**CONFIRMED.**
- KLA FY2026 Form 10-K backlog section (text reproduced by StockTitan SEC filing coverage): backlog increased from $7.86B as of 30 Jun 2025 to $12.57B as of 30 Jun 2026, attributed to strong demand from the AI infrastructure buildout. Arithmetic: $12.57B / $7.86B − 1 = +59.9%, so "~60%" is correct. Zacks/TradingView, ~29 Sep 2026, reports the same two figures.

### KLAC-2. Shortage driver: CY2026 WFE outlook raised to low-$150B range; advanced-packaging process control ~$1.1B in CY26, >+70% YoY
**CONFIRMED.**
- Both figures from KLA's FQ4 FY26 call (late Jul 2026): WFE outlook for calendar 2026 raised to the low-$150B range from slightly above $140B previously (vs ~$120B in CY2025); advanced-packaging process-control revenue expected to approach / reach ~$1.1B in CY2026, up more than 70% year over year. Sources: Zacks via TradingView and EquitySwarm summaries of the call, Sep–Oct 2026; Fortune call transcript reporting the same $1.1B / >70% figures, cited Oct 2026.
- Minor wording variance: some secondary pieces round the packaging figure to "about $1B" — the call figure is $1.1B, use that.

### KLAC-3. Catalyst/date: Q1 FY27 revenue guide $4.0B ±$200M; next earnings 2026-10-28 — confirmed or estimated?
**CONFIRMED — both parts, and the date is company-confirmed.**
- Guide: KLA's own FQ4 FY26 earnings release — total revenues for Q1 FY27 (quarter ending Sep 2026) expected at $4.0B ± $200M, GAAP EPS $1.14 ± $0.10, non-GAAP EPS $1.16 ± $0.10. Primary press release, 28 Jul 2026.
- Date: KLA announced on 1 Oct 2026 that it will publish Q1 FY27 results after market close on 28 Oct 2026, with an earnings review/webcast at 2:00 p.m. PT — reported in multiple 1–14 Oct 2026 items citing KLA's investor-relations release. This is a CONFIRMED date, unlike MU's.

### KLAC-4. Moat: ~56–58% semiconductor process-control share, ~7x nearest rival
**CONFIRMED (company-cited industry research, not an audited filing figure).**
- Share estimates cluster at 56% in independent/analyst write-ups and ~58% in KLA's own investor-day material; KLA has stated its share expanded 360 bps since 2021 and is now roughly seven times its nearest competitor — reported by Zacks, 3 Jun 2026, from KLA's own commentary, and repeated in Oct 2026 analyst summaries. The 56–58% range fairly represents the source spread; nearest rival is generally put at ~10%, consistent with the ~7x claim.

## GOOGL — Alphabet

### GOOGL-1. Backlog: Google Cloud RPO/backlog $514B at Q2 2026, >50% expected to convert within 24 months
**CONFIRMED.**
- Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings call, 22 Jul 2026: CFO stated Cloud backlog reached $514B, up more than $50B sequentially, with just over 50% expected to convert to revenue within 24 months. Corroborated by Zacks call coverage, 23 Jul 2026, Motley Fool, 11 Oct 2026, and CryptoBriefing's report of the release. Trajectory in the same sources: $240B (Q4 2025) → ~$462B (Q1 2026) → $514B (Q2 2026), so the figure is RPO/backlog as management defines it on the call — the earnings release itself does not print the $514B; cite it as a call disclosure.

### GOOGL-2. Shortage driver: Cloud revenue +82% to $24.8B in Q2 2026; capacity-constrained; 2026 capex guide raised to $195–205B
**CONFIRMED — all three parts.**
- Cloud revenue +82% to $24.8B (and Cloud operating income $8.8B, 35.6% margin): Alphabet's Q2 2026 earnings release, 22 Jul 2026 (primary release text reproduced via Quartr filing copy).
- Capacity-constrained: CEO stated on the Q2 call that Alphabet continues to be supply constrained; CFO stated demand still outpaces capacity investment despite significant capacity increases over three years. Reported by Zacks, 23 Jul 2026, and Fool, 11 Oct 2026.
- Capex guide: raised on the Q2 call to $195–205B for 2026, up from $180–190B at Q1 — same 22 Jul 2026 call/release coverage, all sources agree. Context the memo should keep: Q2 free cash flow was negative $5.9B because of this spend.

### GOOGL-3. Catalyst/date: next earnings 2026-10-28 — confirmed or estimated?
**CONFIRMED as scheduled, with an aggregator discrepancy flagged.**
- Motley Fool, 14 Oct 2026, and a second Fool piece the same day state Alphabet is scheduled to report Q3 results on 28 Oct 2026; Zacks' earnings calendar states Alphabet has announced it will report on 28 Oct 2026; Oct 14 2026 reporting citing Alphabet Investor Relations gives a scheduled Q3 call on 28 Oct at 4:30 p.m. ET / 1:30 p.m. PT.
- Caveat: MarketBeat still labels 28 Oct 2026 "estimated from past reporting schedules." No full Alphabet IR announcement text was fetched directly in this run, so confirmation rests on multiple secondary reports citing the IR schedule rather than the IR page itself. Verdict: treat the date as scheduled/confirmed for catalyst purposes, but it carries slightly weaker primary-source footing than KLAC's 28 Oct date, which traces to a dated company announcement (1 Oct).

### GOOGL-4. Moat: Search ~89.9% global share; $98.0B unrealised equity-securities gain in Q2 other income flattering headline EPS
**CONFIRMED — both parts.**
- Search share: StatCounter September 2026 — Google 89.9% global (85.2% US), next competitor Bing 5.3%; reported by Colorlib's StatCounter-based roundup, Oct 2026, and by citybiz coverage of Bank of America / StatCounter data, ~7 Oct 2026, which also notes the share was down about a point month-over-month — dominant but not rising.
- $98.0B gain: Alphabet's Q2 2026 earnings release, 22 Jul 2026, states other income reflected a net gain of $98.0B, primarily net unrealised gains on equity securities. Consequence, also from release-based coverage: net income rose 298% to $112.1B and diluted EPS to $9.11, with the gain contributing roughly $6.26 of EPS on an after-tax basis per release-day analysis (FX Leaders, 22 Jul 2026). The panel's warning is correct — headline Q2 EPS is not operating EPS, and any GOOGL valuation line in the memo must use ex-gain / operating figures.
