### 1. Shortage thesis
Structural demand surge, not a classic parts shortage: incretin / GLP-1 demand for diabetes and obesity still outruns what the industry built capacity for over the prior three years, and Lilly is converting previously constrained demand as new capacity comes online.
- Driver: tirzepatide (Mounjaro / Zepbound). - Product split, Q2 2026: Mounjaro $9.94B (+91% YoY), Zepbound $4.93B (+46% YoY) — together ~$14.9B, roughly two-thirds of quarterly revenue.
- Supply response as evidence demand was the ceiling, not demand softness: Lilly committed an additional $4.5B in Q2 2026 to expand Indiana incretin manufacturing sites, on top of its multi-year capacity build-out.
- Verdict on shortage character: this is now a *capacity-ramp* shortage easing into volume growth, not an unmet-order backlog — the scarcity rents are shifting from availability to share, oral convenience, and pipeline succession.

### 2. Backlog / order book
**Not found** — Lilly does not disclose a drug order book / backlog, and none was found in the Q2 2026 release. The visibility substitute is guidance: Lilly raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance on 2026-08-05 to $85.0–$87.0B (from $82–$85B, itself raised in April), and raised its underlying non-GAAP EPS outlook by $2.78 at the midpoint before a $3.03/share acquired-IPR&D charge brought the stated range to $35.50–$36.50.

### 3. Category position
Clear #1 in incretins.
- Lilly's Q2 2026 earnings presentation put its share of the US obesity-and-diabetes incretin market at 60.9% vs Novo Nordisk's 38.8% (cited by BioNixus, Oct 2026, and Fast Company, Sep 2026).
- Tirzepatide held ~57% of the total GLP-1 market in Sep 2026 script data (IQVIA via Citi, Fierce Pharma tracker updated 2026-09-11), up from ~49.5% a year earlier, vs semaglutide ~37%.
- Caveat — oral segment: Novo's Wegovy pill still led orals in Q2 2026 (~$497M vs Foundayo's ~$98M); Lilly said Foundayo had taken >30% of *new* US patients by Sep 2026, so the oral race is open.

### 4. Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk
Moat: patents + regulatory exclusivity on tirzepatide, best-in-class efficacy, manufacturing scale for complex peptides / KwikPen devices, formulary position (Zepbound and Foundayo on all three largest US PBM formularies in 2026, including CVS restoring Zepbound as preferred from 2026-10-01), and a succession pipeline no rival currently matches (oral small-molecule Foundayo/orforglipron, FDA-approved 2026-04-01; triple-agonist retatrutide with its Phase 3 obesity package complete and a US BLA planned Q1 2027).
- Customers cannot "in-source" a patented drug — the disintermediation analogue is substitution: Novo, plus Pfizer / Amgen / Roche entrants, compounded/counterfeit copies, and generics ex-US for rival molecules.
- Threat is real but dated: the bypass arrives at patent expiry / next-generation readouts, not from a buyer building the product tomorrow.
**Irreplaceability score: 8/10.**

### 5. Recent catalysts (last ~3 months)
- 2026-08-05 — Q2 beat and second guidance raise of the year (above); retatrutide completed three more positive Phase 3 trials; multiple Q2 acquisitions closed, with more infectious-disease deals after quarter-end.
- Sep 2026 — Foundayo share of new US oral patients >30% and rising script counts (IQVIA via Citi/Jefferies, Sep 2026); Medicare GLP-1 Bridge (started 2026-07-01, $50 Part D copay) widened the insured pool.

- Early Oct 2026 — mid-stage Zepbound + eloralintide combination showed 23.3% weight loss among completers at the highest dose, with tolerability/dropout questions flagged by STAT (2026-10-01).

### 6. Risks
- Concentration: Mounjaro + Zepbound ~65% of Q2 revenue — any tirzepatide safety, supply, or share shock is a company shock.
- Price-for-volume trade: US pricing deals (Medicare/Medicaid ~$245/month framework, TrumpRx cash prices, Medicare Bridge through 2027-12-31) cut realised price ~13% in Q2; volume must keep outrunning price.
- Competition: Novo oral lead, CagriSema and other next-gen entrants, compounding/telehealth copies; oral shift lowers revenue per patient vs injectables.
- Valuation: forward P/E 24.5 and price near highs leave little room for a guidance miss; acquired-IPR&D charges already dented stated EPS guidance.

### 7. Shortage score and verdict
**Shortage score: 7/10** — demand still exceeds the industry's installed base and Lilly is the share-taker, but its own capacity ramp is deliberately dissolving the shortage, so scarcity itself is no longer the earnings engine; volume and succession are.
**Verdict:** The cleanest large-cap demand-surge name in the batch — #1 with 60.9% US incretin share and a credible oral + triple-agonist succession, priced as if much of it must go right.

## NEM — Newmont Corporation (Gold)

Screen: rank 7, price $115.59, +3.6% vs 200d SMA, ret_6m −2.7%, ret_12m +37.0%, revenue +15.1% YoY latest quarter, TTM revenue growth +18.1%, forward P/E 11.6, trailing P/E 14.6, net cash position (net debt/EBITDA −0.20). Earnings trend: 4/4 EPS beats, avg surprise +20.6%. Next earnings 2026-10-22.

### 1. Shortage thesis
A commodity shortage thesis, not a company order thesis: gold mine supply is essentially frozen while official-sector and investment demand structurally stepped up.
- World Gold Council data as reported through 2026: total 2025 demand topped 5,000 tonnes for the first time in the series; investment demand rose ~84% to 2,175 tonnes; central banks added 863 tonnes in 2025 and were forecast to buy roughly 850 tonnes in 2026 (MINING.com / WGC, Mar 2026) — about a quarter of annual mine output, bought on reserve policy rather than price.
- Supply side: 2025 mine production was 3,672 tonnes, up only ~1% and barely above the 2018 record of 3,663 tonnes (WGC via GoldSilver, mid-2026) — seven years to add nine tonnes of annual output, with declining grades and a discovery pipeline lagging price by years.
- Price evidence: Newmont realised an average $4,414/oz in Q2 2026; gold had set a record close of $5,318 on 2026-01-29 (secondary market coverage, Oct 2026) and remained in historically unprecedented territory through the autumn.
- Honest limit: Newmont does not create or control this shortage, and it sells at spot — the "shortage" accrues to it only as price leverage on its reserve base.

### 2. Backlog / order book
**Not found** — miners do not report an order book, and Newmont discloses none. Visibility substitutes:
- Production guidance (reaffirmed at Q2, 2026-07-23): ~5.26–5.3M attributable gold ounces for 2026 (a planned trough/sequencing year, down from 5.89M oz in 2025), gold AISC guidance ~$1,680/oz, plus ~102kt copper and ~32M oz silver.
- Reserve scale: secondary Feb 2026 analyses cite the world's largest gold reserve base at over 125M ounces — treat as an analyst-cited figure, not a number verified here from a primary filing.

### 3. Category position
Clear #1 gold miner.
- World's largest gold producer by output and market value, operating across North America, South America, Africa, Australia and Papua New Guinea (company-profile summaries, 2026); ~15% share of global production among top-tier miners, the highest in the industry (Feb 2026 sector analysis — secondary source).
- Nearest rivals: Barrick (now "Barrick Mining") and Agnico Eagle, both materially smaller on 2026 guided output (~5.3M oz for Newmont vs Barrick's smaller attributable base); the screen likewise ranks NEM #1 in the Gold sub-industry.

### 4. Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk
The in-sourcing question inverts for a miner: no customer can in-source a gold deposit, and there is no substitute technology that routes around gold's monetary role — central banks are buying the asset itself. The moat is geology and jurisdiction: tier-one, long-life reserves in mostly safe jurisdictions, permitting timelines measured in decades, and scale no entrant can replicate at any plausible cost.
- The genuine bypass risks are financial, not technological: investors can buy bullion/ETFs or royalty/streaming companies instead of an operator, capturing the gold price without AISC inflation, seismic events, or host-government royalty grabs — i.e., NEM competes with its own commodity for the investor's dollar.
- Operational substitutability is nil; equity-level substitutability is high.
**Irreplaceability score: 7/10** (asset irreplaceability 9–10, discounted because the equity is an optional, cost-levered wrapper on a fungible commodity).

### 5. Recent catalysts (last ~3 months)
- 2026-07-23 — Q2 2026 results: ~1.3M attributable oz, adjusted EPS $2.10 (beat ~$2.05 consensus), revenue $6.12B (+15.1% YoY, below ~$6.35B consensus), record Q2 free cash flow of $2.2B on $2.9B operating cash flow, AISC $1,621/oz (below full-year guide), $1.9B returned to shareholders since the prior call, $0.26/share dividend declared, full-year guidance reaffirmed.

Analysts cited in Sep 2026 coverage estimated the implied value of Newmont's Fourmile interest well above the payment.
- Sep 2026 — analyst target raises followed Q2 (e.g. UBS and RBC to $155 in secondary roundups); stock set a Sep high near $124 before easing.
- Operational negative inside Q2: copper output fell 43% after seismic events at Cadia (Australia), and attributable gold production fell ~13% YoY — the cost/execution watch item into the 2026-10-22 Q3 print.

### 6. Risks
- Gold price reversal is the whole thesis in reverse: at a $5,318 Jan 2026 record and ~$4,414 realised in Q2, a central-bank bid pause or real-rate shock compresses the $2,700+/oz margin fast; there is no backlog to cushion it.
- Cost creep: 2026 AISC guide $1,680/oz vs $1,358 the prior year — labour, energy and host-government royalty/tax pressure (Ghana, Papua New Guinea) at record prices.
- Execution/geology: Cadia seismic disruption, grade decline and mine sequencing make 2026 a trough production year; the $1.95B Nevada top-up is real cash out for long-dated ounces.
- Momentum is the weakest in the batch: only +3.6% above the 200d SMA and −2.7% over 6 months — the screen's momentum gate is barely met.

### 7. Shortage score and verdict
**Shortage score: 6/10** — the underlying commodity shortage is as structural as any in this run (flat mine supply vs ~850t/yr of price-insensitive official buying), but Newmont holds no backlog, no pricing power and no volume growth in 2026, so the shortage reaches shareholders only through a spot price already near records.
**Verdict:** A high-quality, cash-gushing price-taker on a genuinely supply-frozen commodity — a gold-beta vehicle with a #1 reserve moat, not a shortage-compounder, and the batch's thinnest momentum.
**Earnings-quality flag: none.** Screen: accrual ratio −0.071, CFO/NI 1.47 — earnings are strongly cash-backed; no flag to explain.
