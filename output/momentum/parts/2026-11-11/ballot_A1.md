# Ballot A1 — Supply-Chain Analyst lens (stock-pick-momentum, 2026-11-11)

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** CDNS
- **Runner-up:** MSFT
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Cadence is the cleanest demand-visibility story in the field: a record backlog that locks in more than a year of revenue at a time when every hyperscaler and system company is designing its own silicon, which only increases EDA demand. It is a duopoly (co-#1, ~31–36% share) with near-100% retention and 80–85% recurring revenue, so the backlog is not a one-off order spike but a contracted, sticky revenue stream. Q3 was the third consecutive beat-and-raise of 2026, with double-digit growth in every product group, and the stock sits ~20% below its June high despite that — the backlog de-risks the growth while the price has already corrected. The explosive upside comes from AI chip-design demand compounding on top of a backlog that is already growing into the outer years.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Record backlog of USD 8.2bn at 30 Sep 2026, verified still standing (up from USD 8.1bn in Q2 and USD 7.8bn at YE2025), including USD 4.2bn RPO in the next 12 months — covering 1.34x TTM revenue. Only 2–3 firms on earth supply production-grade EDA.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Low. Irreplaceability 9/10: duopoly plus foundry-PDK qualification lock-in and near-100% retention. In-sourcing cuts the other way — hyperscalers designing their own chips (45% of revenue now from system companies) increases EDA demand rather than bypassing it. The live threats are agentic-AI seat cannibalisation and, over a generational horizon, Chinese domestic EDA; neither unwinds the current backlog.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: +25–35% as the backlog converts and the stock re-rates toward analyst PTs of USD 400–440 (vs ~USD 332 close). Bull: +50%+ if AI Super Agent / Maestro adoption accelerates backlog growth and the multiple expands back toward the June highs.
- **Top risk:** Valuation (~34x forward, ~2% FCF yield) and China export controls; also flat 12-month RPO means the growth is in outer years, so a slowdown in new bookings would show up before revenue does.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
