## CDNS (Cadence Design Systems)

### 1. Backlog USD 8.2bn; USD 4.2bn RPO in next 12 months — CONFIRMED

- Cadence Q3 2026 results release (investor.cadence.com, 26 Oct 2026): "Quarter-end backlog was $8.2 billion and revenue expected to be recognized in the next 12 months from remaining performance obligations was $4.2 billion." Headline of the same release: "Q3 Recurring Revenue Up 18% YoY, Record Backlog of $8.2 Billion."
- Corroborated by the Motley Fool Q3 2026 earnings-call transcript (26 Oct 2026) and Converge Digest (26 Oct 2026), both reporting the same two figures.

### 2. Q3 revenue +22% YoY, double-digit growth in every product group; FY2026 guidance raised a third time in 2026 — CONFIRMED

- Revenue: Cadence Q3 2026 release (26 Oct 2026): revenue USD 1.633bn vs USD 1.339bn in Q3 2025 = +22%. Non-GAAP EPS USD 2.11.
- Product-group growth: Motley Fool call transcript (26 Oct 2026): Core EDA +20%, Semiconductor IP +24%, System Design and Analysis +30% — double-digit in every group, as claimed.
- Guidance: same release / Fool transcript: FY2026 revenue guidance raised to USD 6.387–6.417bn; full-year non-GAAP EPS guidance USD 8.37–8.43, midpoint USD 8.40 — matching the claim's "USD 8.40" (it is the midpoint, not the range).
- "Third time in 2026": EffectStory (26 Oct 2026), citing the SEC filing: "Cadence said it was the third increase this year." The Technosight note on the same print likewise calls it "its third beat-and-raise on 26 Oct."

### 3. Maestro AI launch and >100 AI Super Agent engagements, 26 Oct 2026 — CONFIRMED

- Zacks (27 Oct 2026, reporting the 26 Oct earnings call): CEO Anirudh Devgan introduced Maestro, "a portfolio of domain-specialized AI models designed for chip and system development," and "reported more than 100 active customer engagements involving Cadence's AI Super Agents."
- Reuters-derived coverage (27 Oct 2026): "Cadence launched Maestro on October 26" and "Its AI Super Agents have more than 100 active customer engagements."
- Precision note: the figure is "more than 100 active customer engagements," not paid production deployments; first Maestro models go to select early-access customers in Q4 2026. The claim as worded ("engagements") is accurate.

### 4. Only 2–3 firms supply production-grade EDA at scale (Cadence + Synopsys duopoly, ~70–80% combined share; Siemens EDA distant #3) — CONFIRMED in substance; SHARE FIGURE NEEDS CORRECTION

- The "2–3 firms" structure is confirmed. SemiAnalysis ("EDA Market Primer," 22 May 2026), as reported by shashi.co (22 Jul 2026): "Synopsys holds close to a third of the global EDA market… Cadence holds a similar share… Siemens EDA… holds an estimated ten to fifteen percent… Together the three control seventy to eighty percent of global EDA revenue."
- TalkMarkets (updated 9 Nov 2026): "Cadence Design Systems (CDNS) and Synopsys (SNPS)… each with about 30% market share. The only other firm even close is Siemens EDA… at about 13% share."
- **Correction:** the "~70–80% combined share" figure belongs to the top **three** firms together (per SemiAnalysis), not to the Cadence + Synopsys duopoly alone. Estimates for the duopoly alone are lower: Verified Market Research (Mar 2026) puts Synopsys at 35.8% and Cadence at 31.4% (= 67.2% combined); Mordor Intelligence (Jan 2026 data) puts Synopsys + Cadence at a combined 62% (2025, AI/HPC EDA segment). The dossier's own range (~31–36% Cadence, ~36–38% Synopsys) sums to ~67–74%, i.e. the top end of the claimed 70–80% band is only just reached. The "Siemens a distant #3" element is confirmed by all sources cited (~10–19% vs ~30%+ each for the leaders).
- The moat's central factual claim therefore stands; the panel should cite the duopoly share as ~62–67% (or the top-three share as ~70–80%), not ~70–80% for the duopoly alone.

## MSFT (Microsoft)

### 9. Commercial RPO USD 677bn at 30 Sep 2026, +73% y/y, essentially flat vs USD 678bn at 30 Jun 2026 — CONFIRMED

- Microsoft Q1 FY2027 results release (28 Oct 2026), as summarised by SEC-API.io filing analysis: "commercial RPO $677bn at 30 Sep 2026 against $392bn a year earlier" (+73%). SOTKN (29 Oct 2026): "Commercial remaining performance obligation rose 73% to $677 billion."
- The flat q/q element: The Edge Research (28 Oct 2026): "The commercial backlog paused, though: commercial remaining performance obligation ended September at $677bn, $1bn below June" — i.e. USD 678bn at 30 Jun 2026. Confirmed exactly as claimed.

### 10. Azure USD 33.2bn, +48% y/y in Q1 FY27 (28 Oct 2026); management stating demand "remains significantly ahead of the capacity we have available" — CONFIRMED in substance; QUOTED WORDING NEEDS CORRECTION

- Azure figures: Microsoft Q1 FY2027 release (28 Oct 2026): Azure USD 33.165bn, +48.2% YoY — the first time Microsoft disclosed Azure revenue in dollars. Confirmed by SEC-API.io, SOTKN, GeekWire-derived coverage (29 Oct 2026).
- Demand-exceeds-capacity substance: confirmed on the 28 Oct 2026 call. CFO Amy Hood, as quoted by CNBC and reported 29 Oct 2026: "We still have demand that exceeds supply" (Binance/CNBC, 29 Oct 2026); a machine transcript of the call has Hood saying "Customer demand continues to exceed available supply" (FourWeekMBA, 28 Oct 2026). AI Early Edition (29 Oct 2026): Hood "told analysts on the earnings call that 'customer demand continues to exceed available supply.'"
- **Correction:** the exact sentence "demand remains significantly ahead of the capacity we have available" could not be found verbatim in any Q1 FY27 call reporting checked today. The closest verified wording is Hood's "Customer demand continues to exceed available supply" / "We still have demand that exceeds supply." A similar phrase — CEO Satya Nadella, "Customer demand continues to exceed available capacity" — was said on the **FY2026 Q4** call (29 Jul 2026), not the Q1 FY27 call. The claim's substance (management said demand exceeds available capacity on the 28 Oct call) is confirmed; the quotation should be replaced with one of the verified wordings above.
