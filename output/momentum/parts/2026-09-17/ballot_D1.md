# Panelist D Ballot (Contrarian / risk skeptic) — run 2026-09-17

Lens: hunt for a thesis that is REAL but still UNDERAPPRECIATED by the market; avoid anything already priced for perfection. Weigh valuation, downside, and disintermediation risk. Great business at a bull-case price = pass. Mispriced shortage, not crowded momentum.

## BALLOT 1 — single-pick nomination

- **Top pick:** CAT
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** CAT is the only name in the dossier where the shortage is confirmed by a hard backlog number AND the tape hasn't priced it in: $72B backlog, +92% YoY, while the stock sits only +2% above its 200-day at a 24.7x forward P/E. The market still frames CAT as cyclical construction equipment, but the mispriced piece is structural: AI datacenters are buying large gensets and turbines as *primary* power to bypass grid constraints — CAT signed its sixth 1-gigawatt prime-power deal and raised its 2030 engine capacity target to ~3x 2024 levels, i.e. demand is forcing capacity investment, the textbook shortage signature. CEO Joe Creed's Aug 4, 2026 line — "if we can get more units out, they're asking us to give them more units" — is the cleanest demand > supply statement in the batch. Power & Energy operating margin hit a record 29.8% in Q2, so the shortage is already converting to price/mix, not just volume.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** $72B backlog at end of Q2 2026, up 92% YoY (Q2 earnings presentation, Aug 4, 2026); buyers asking for more large gensets than CAT can ship; 2 GW Monarch Compute Campus order (deliveries Sep 2026–Aug 2027, scaling toward 8 GW).
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — Cummins, Generac, Rolls-Royce, Wärtsilä all compete in power generation, and datacenter buyers *could* switch vendors; there is no technology monopoly. What they cannot do is in-source or bypass the category: no hyperscaler builds 10 MW reciprocating engines or 1–39 MW turbines, and CAT's independent dealer network + fleet-standardization switching costs + 827 autonomous trucks in operation give it the stickiest installed base. Low on customer in-sourcing, medium on vendor substitution.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base +25% — mid-to-high-teens revenue growth holds, backlog converts, Power & Energy margins sustain ~28-30%, multiple holds near 25x. Bull +45% — genset lead times keep extending, backlog passes $90B, 2030 capacity-build narrative gets a multi-year growth premium, plus analyst upgrades off the +22% current upside.
- **Top risk:** The non-power businesses (construction + resources, most of CAT) are cyclical — if infrastructure/mining spend rolls over or AI capex pauses, the datacenter genset wave reverses fast and the backlog deflates; the stock is already +83% over 12 months on this narrative, so any backlog deceleration gets repriced hard.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

**Top 3 detail:**

- **CAT:** The $72B backlog (+92% YoY) is the single hardest shortage number in the dossier — no other name publishes a backlog this explosive. CAT is building toward ~3x 2024 engine capacity by 2030 *because* current capacity is insufficient, and customers are deploying gensets as primary (not backup) datacenter power to bypass grid constraints. Base 12–18mo: +25% (backlog converts at record 29.8% Power & Energy margins, mid-to-high-teens revenue growth holds). Bull: +45% (backlog extends toward $90B+, capacity-build gets a structural growth premium).

- **LRCX:** CFO Doug Bettinger's Sept 10, 2026 statement that the chip-equipment industry is "fundamentally sold out" — with clean-room availability, not customer appetite, as the binding constraint — is the bluntest supply-constraint admission in the batch; GAA adds ~$1B SAM per 100k wafer starts/month and WFE outlook was raised three times (to low-$150B). Only +1% above the 200-day with 37% op margins, so this isn't crowded momentum. Irreplaceability 9/10: no fab can in-source etch. Base 12–18mo: +30% (WFE holds low-$150B, EPS compounds 20-30%). Bull: +55% (continued WFE upgrades + pricing power sustain record quarters).

- **CF:** The most honestly quantified supply deficit in the dossier: ~4–4.5Mt of traded urea and ~1Mt of ammonia removed by the Iran conflict, China export quotas, European producers structurally disadvantaged — CF ran at >99% ammonia capacity in Q1 and guides tightness into 2027. Analyst upside is *negative* (-6%), i.e. the market expects this to fade — the contrarian setup. Base 12–18mo: +30% (tightness persists into 2027, earnings stay elevated). Bull: +60% (any escalation/disruption re-prices the scarcity premium).

**Deliberately left out of top 10:** ANET — white-box and NVIDIA Spectrum-X substitution threat is the most concrete disintermediation risk in the dossier, at a 38.7x forward P/E with NVIDIA already #1 in DC Ethernet; LLY — shortage is morphing into a share-and-price war (net realized prices -13%), and the stock is priced on perfection; NEM — leveraged gold-price proxy, not a structural shortage; gold never "runs out," so this is a commodity price wave outside this lens's mandate.

**Explicitly fully priced (pass on valuation, per lens):** SNDK (+1663% in 12 months; the dossier's own verdict says margin expansion is "largely priced in"); NVDA (tape prices perfection while hyperscaler ASICs build an inference bypass at 40–65% TCO advantage — margin expansion upside is gone); LLY (priced on perfection, 65% of revenue in one category, pricing already eroding); ANET (~62x trailing P/E per Zacks, momentum priced in, NVIDIA growing +181% YoY against it).
