# BALLOT 1 — Single-Pick Nomination

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** SNDK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is the purest acceleration-plus-revision setup in the dossier: TTM revenue +142%, Q3 FY26 revenue +346% YoY at record 84.9% gross margin, and the Q4 guide of ~$50B lands ~14% above the ~$44B consensus — estimates are still chasing, not leading. The entire 2026 HBM book is sold out, CEO Mehrotra can fill only half to two-thirds of demand, and Micron is collecting $22B in customer prepayments — this is contracted, paid-for demand, not hope. At 6.3x forward P/E with +55% analyst upside on screen, a +516% twelve-month run has not priced the revision cycle in.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** DRAM contract prices +90–95% (Q1), +58–63% (Q2), +13–18% (Q3) — a chip costing $1 in Jan 2026 costs ~$3.50 in Sept 2026 (TrendForce via Zacks, 2026-09-17) — while spot still sits above contract, meaning the memory turn has not started.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium. No hyperscaler fabricates DRAM/HBM, so customers cannot in-source; but memory is ultimately a commodity and qualification lock-in is the only moat. Live bypass threats: Sandisk's high-bandwidth flash (HBF) as an HBM-adjacent substitute, and CXMT/YMTC share gains. Nobody can bypass Micron today; they can shop around it tomorrow.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: the Sept 30, 2026 Q4 print confirms the $50B guide and forces Street estimates up toward the flagged +55% upside → roughly +45–60%. Bull: shortage visibly extends past CY2027, contract pricing keeps grinding up, and EPS explodes off a 6x forward multiple → double or better.
- **Top risk:** The memory cycle turns — spot prices cracking below contract, or the Big 4 over-investing and flooding the market in 2027–28. Extreme operating leverage cuts both ways.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

*Pricing check:* Is MU's momentum fully priced? The +516% one-year run looks scary, but a 6.3x forward P/E with analysts flagging +55% upside and guidance running 14% ahead of consensus says revisions are still behind the curve, not ahead of it. What would change my mind: Q4 print failing to clear the $50B guide, or spot DRAM breaking below contract prices.

## Top 3 detail — shortage/backlog data point + 12-18mo scenario

**1. MU.** Entire 2026 HBM supply sold out under multi-year contracts; Mehrotra can fulfill only "half to two-thirds" of demand, and Micron is sitting on $22B in customer prepayments while contract DRAM prices ran +90–95%/+58–63%/+13–18% over the last three quarters — a $1 chip in January costs ~$3.50 in September. Base: the Sept 30 Q4 beat drives Street toward the +55% flagged upside → +45–60%. Bull: deficit persists past 2027 and the 6x forward multiple re-rates hard → double.

**2. SNDK.** The single most compelling contractual backlog in the dossier: $59.8B remaining performance obligation at quarter end, $91.1B including two NBMs signed after the close, with contracts expected to cover over half of FY27 bits and ~two-thirds of FY28 bits — at floor pricing, gross margins >80%, backstopped by $16.5B in cash deposits/financial guarantees. The purest "every bit sells" vehicle in the panel. Base: pricing power holds through CY2026 into 2027 → +30–50%. Bull: HBF cracks the DRAM-adjacent market and the NAND shortage runs through 2027 → double again (with the +1,663% twelve-month run as the reminder that momentum here can overshoot and reverse).

**3. NVDA.** Supply constrained through the end of FY28 (~Jan 2028) per the Aug 27 earnings call, with $119B in supply-related purchase commitments — Nvidia is pre-buying capacity at unprecedented scale — and Rubin production began with purchase orders already in hand from every major hyperscaler, expected to be the fastest product ramp in company history; management's preliminary FY28 revenue growth guide is ~70% vs the Street's ~42%. Base: Rubin ramp + continued beats grind the stock up → +40–60%. Bull: the FY28 guide proves conservative, the 14x forward P/E re-rates toward growth-stock multiples → +100%.
