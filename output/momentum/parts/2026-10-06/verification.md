## MU — Micron Technology

### Claim 1 — ~$32B in customer cash deposits / SCA commitments (up from ~$22B a quarter earlier), vast majority on the balance sheet as cash deposits.
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED**
- **Source:** Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra, FQ4 FY2026 earnings call (Sep 30, 2026), reported by multiple outlets Oct 2026 (aistockwire.com; finance.biggo.com; mexc.com "Crypto Pulse", Oct 2026; TradingView/Zacks news roundup, Oct 2026).
- **Note:** Customer financial commitments under the 26 Strategic Customer Agreements reached **$32B, up from $22B a quarter earlier**, "the vast majority of which are cash deposits" (Mehrotra's wording); $12.3B of deposits arrived in Q4 alone. Associated disclosed figure: ~$150B remaining performance obligations under SCAs (>35% of revenue through 2030).

### Claim 2 — HBM sold out / supply guided tighter through 2027–2028.
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED**
- **Source:** Micron FQ4 FY2026 earnings call (Sep 30, 2026), reported by fool.com (Oct 1, 2026) and TradingView (Oct 2026); HBM sold-out status via startupfortune.com (Oct 2026).
- **Note:** Management guided "memory and storage supply-demand conditions to be much tighter in fiscal 2027 and 2028 than they were in 2026" — first time 2028 was called out (prior guide only reached calendar 2027). HBM3E/HBM4 sold out through calendar 2027, >75% of total 2027 output already committed, customers already locking in 2028 HBM supply at prices "much higher" than 2026; Mehrotra: "We do not have line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance."

### Claim 3 — Only US-based HBM maker / one of ~3 firms on earth that can make HBM.
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED** (with a nuance worth knowing)
- **Source:** coinw.com (Oct 2026); cryptobriefing.com; fool.com (Jul 2026, updated Oct 5, 2026); finance.biggo.com; Counterpoint Research Q2 2026 HBM share data via aistockwire.com (Sep 2026).
- **Note:** Exactly three firms manufacture HBM at scale — Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron (Q2 2026 shares ~33/50/18 per Counterpoint); Micron is the only one based/headquartered in the US (~$6.1B CHIPS Act support). Nuance from techtimes.com (Aug 2026, ForcedAlpha supply-chain analysis): Micron currently fabricates its HBM wafers in Japan and packages them in Taiwan — no HBM is manufactured or packaged *inside* the US today; US-based HBM-capable production follows Micron's Idaho fab (H2 2027) by years. The dossier's phrasing ("US-based HBM maker") is accurate; "only US-made HBM" would not be.
