### 1. Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** MU

- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** LOW. Only ~3 firms on Earth can make DRAM/HBM at scale — a fab costs tens of billions, HBM needs advanced packaging plus years of qualification. No hyperscaler builds DRAM; Nvidia is co-designing custom HBM *with* Micron ("industry's first custom HBM implementation") rather than replacing it. Moat layers: 1γ/1δ process nodes, HBM4 shipping in high volume to a lead customer ahead of schedule, and $250B committed for two new US campuses.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: +25–40% — guided tightening into 2028, UBS FY2027/28 EPS trajectory ($184.19/$274.97), Bernstein $1,300 target (~+22% from ~$1,064), forward P/E under 7x on peak-cycle earnings that management says keep getting tighter. Bull: +60–80% if the HBM4/HBM4E pricing (+76% YoY per UBS) flows through and the supercycle runs past 2028 without a cycle peak.
- **Top risk:** Memory cyclicality — the EPS trajectory embeds a cycle peak; a DRAM price reversal or demand shock turns the oligopoly from pricing power into a value trap. Secondary: the June 2026 California class-action alleging the Big Three coordinated the DRAM→HBM shift — cartel litigation overhang on a $1.2T market cap.
