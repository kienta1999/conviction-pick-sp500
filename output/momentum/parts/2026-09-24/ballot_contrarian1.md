## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** MSFT
- **Runner-up:** AMAT
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The crowd is bidding up memory and GPUs while the actual binding constraint moved up the stack to power and deployable buildings — the one shortage you cannot solve by throwing capex at it this quarter. Microsoft's CFO admitted Azure growth would have printed over 40% if the capacity simply existed, and the stock is flat over 12 months (ret12m -0.02) because the market keeps selling the beats on capex fears. The OpenAI restructuring removed the partnership overhang while locking IP through 2032 plus a $250B Azure purchase commitment. This is the most under-owned quality name on the list — the shortage is real, the backlog is contracted, and the move hasn't happened.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Commercial backlog $678B (FQ4 FY26) with RPO $392B (+51% YoY), against CFO Amy Hood's admission that FQ2 Azure growth "would have been over 40%" had newly-online GPUs all been allocated to it — demand verifiably exceeds deliverable supply, gated by electricity and ready buildings, not chip supply.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — enterprises cannot in-source hyperscale cloud economics and Azure AI Foundry pull-through deepens the moat; but the risk is real and named: OpenAI diversified with a $300B Oracle deal starting 2027 and landed on Amazon Bedrock, partially mitigated by Microsoft's $135B-valued 27% stake, IP rights through 2032, and the $250B Azure purchase commitment.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: Azure holds 40–45% growth, backlog converts as capacity (Fairwater-style early completions, 20% faster dock-to-live) comes online — rerating toward $620–660 (+25–32%). Bull: capacity delivery inflects, Copilot attach drives platform pull-through, capex narrative flips from fear to proof — +50–60% with multiple expansion back toward hypergrowth-era levels.
- **Top risk:** The capex-disappointment cycle — the market has repeatedly sold the beat on capex intensity (Jan 2026: -$360B single-day drawdown), gross margin is compressed to ~68%, and any Azure print below ~40% gets punished; OpenAI's Oracle/AWS path is a genuine long-run demand leak.
