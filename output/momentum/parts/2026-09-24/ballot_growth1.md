# Growth/Momentum Panelist Ballot — 2026-09-24

Lens: revenue acceleration, earnings beats/revisions, price momentum, composite score. I want the fastest compounder, not the safest holder.

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** SNDK
- **Runner-up:** MU
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** SNDK is the purest velocity instrument in this shortlist: quarterly revenue +372% YoY with revenue acceleration of +2.77 (the highest in the 15-keeper set), 6/6 EPS beats with an average surprise of +46%, and a demand shock that is structurally supply-side — Samsung and SK Hynix are *withdrawing* NAND wafers to fund HBM, while AI data centers are pulling enterprise SSDs faster than the Big Four can supply. The stock is down ~22% from its 52-week high on a guidance "miss" that was really just a smaller beat, so momentum money gets in at a discount to the run. Forward P/E of ~6.9 on a company guiding ~80% gross margins for FY28-30 is a re-rating waiting for a catalyst.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** The single most compelling data point is the take-or-pay stack disclosed on the Aug 5, 2026 call: 8 major customers with >50% of FY2027 bit supply committed, backed by $16.5B in financial guarantees and ~$93.9B in minimum contracted revenue at price floors — that's four-plus years of demand visibility that converts a cyclical commodity into quasi-contracted cash flow.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Medium. Hyperscalers cannot replicate NAND fabrication ($10B+ fab capex, decade-long 3D-stacking know-how, Kioxia JV fab lock-in), but NAND is a fungible commodity — SanDisk has no pricing moat, only supply-discipline leverage. Nobody needs to in-source it; anyone can wait out the cycle. The barrier is real, the product is interchangeable — that nets to medium.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: NAND pricing stays elevated through 2027 on the Big Four's capacity discipline, SNDK retests and clears the late-June high — roughly +35–45% from current levels as the 6.9x forward multiple normalizes toward a cycle-peak ~12x. Bull: the shortage extends into 2028 (major expansions delayed until 2028+), BiCS10 density gains land, and the Investor Day targets (80% GM, 100% of excess cash returned, $15.5B buyback) turn it into a compounding machine — +80–100% as it becomes the defining AI-memory trade of the cycle.
- **Top risk:** This is a commodity-cycle peak play — Seeking Alpha notes new supply entering the market in early 2027, and TrendForce's own QoQ price-rise forecast is already decelerating from 70%+ to 10–15%. If the Big Four break discipline or AI capex pauses, NAND reprices down as violently as it rose, and a stock up ~490–600% YTD is priced for perfection.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
