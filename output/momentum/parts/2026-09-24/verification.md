## AMAT — Applied Materials (Borda #1, tentative single pick)

A follow-up TrendForce piece (2026-08-19) confirms the bottleneck has shifted from "not enough chips" to "not enough equipment to make them," with front-end/memory tools at 12–24 months. (https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/09/18/news-chip-equipment-component-lead-times-reportedly-double-stretching-to-40-months-as-price-pressures-mount/)
- **VERIFIED** — Q3 FY2026 (reported ~2026-08-13): record revenue **$9.12B, +25% YoY**; non-GAAP EPS **$3.50 vs $3.39 consensus**; gross margin ~50.3–50.4%; Q4 FY2026 revenue guide **$10.25B ± $500M**; calendar-2026 Semiconductor Systems outlook raised to **>30% equipment-market growth**. (ainvest 2026-08, stocktitan, marketbeat 2026-08-13)
- **VERIFIED** — $500M Singapore campus: opened and already operating, more than doubling advanced cleanroom capacity there (finimize, ~2026-06). The dossier's "~1,000 new hires" figure was not independently confirmed (minor detail).
- **Substance VERIFIED, verbatim quote UNCONFIRMABLE** — Dickerson's "investing through the end of the decade" line (dossier cites Q3 FY2026 release) was not found verbatim, but the substance (multi-year capacity investment, 8 quarters of customer notice requested, $5B EPIC Center build) is confirmed by finimize and marketbeat.
- Note: AMAT discloses no single backlog dollar figure (dossier correctly states "no figure found").

Samsung/SK hynix reportedly exploring earlier equipment orders to secure supply. No 2026 evidence found against the AI-memory→equipment-bottleneck causal chain.
- The dossier's specific "≈80% of 2026–27 WFE growth runs through AI-linked segments" share is dossier-attributed; the exact 80% was **not independently confirmed** (direction correct, number unverified).

- **VERIFIED (no counter-evidence)** — No in-sourcing or disintermediation event since 2026-06 found. What was found runs the other way: on 2026-09-08, TSMC and Samsung both committed *deeper* to ASML's High-NA EUV roadmap (TSMC HVM from 2030, Samsung for DRAM from 2028; 12-inch mask pilot line 2031) — i.e., the foundries are deepening dependence on the equipment oligopoly, not disintermediating it (hothardware, technode.global, startupfortune). ### 5. Trap red flags
- **VERIFIED (real, disclosed)** — China exposure: FY2025 10-K shows China **$8.529B (30%)** of revenue (ts2.tech/Reuters); Reuters (Nov 2025) confirms AMAT flagged a **$600M FY2026 revenue hit** from expanded export restrictions. The two likely use different FCF definitions (GAAP vs non-GAAP); the cash-quality question exists but is not one-sided.
- **VERIFIED (genuine cycle-peak evidence)** — Citi (analyst Atif Malik, note 2026-08-07, Buy, PT since raised to $1,300): DRAM and NAND price increases **decelerate over the next four quarters, peaking in 2Q 2027** (finance.biggo.com; tradingview; coincentral). This is the one material counter-signal: it caps the memory-capex demand leg that feeds AMAT's equipment orders, even though Citi simultaneously says markets stay "undersupplied" on AI demand for the foreseeable future.

### AMAT verdict
One-line: **Verified evidence supports keeping AMAT at #1 and as the tentative single pick** — the equipment-bottleneck thesis is independently confirmed by TrendForce's 2026-09-18 reporting, the record quarter + raised outlook + live analyst PT wave check out, no disintermediation event has occurred; the real traps (30% China exposure, Citi's 2Q2027 memory price-peak call) are genuine and already disclosed in the dossier, and they temper — not overturn — the thesis.
