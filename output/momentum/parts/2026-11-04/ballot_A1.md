# Panelist A (supply-chain analyst) — RUN_DATE 2026-11-04

## 1. Single-pick ballot

- Top pick: MU
- Runner-up: NVDA
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Micron is the purest supply-constrained play in the dossier: AI demand is pulling HBM, server DDR5 and enterprise SSDs faster than wafer supply can grow, and every HBM wafer displaces roughly 3x the conventional DRAM wafer capacity. With next year's output largely pre-sold and management saying 2027 and 2028 could be tighter than 2026, pricing power — not just volume — should persist well beyond a single quarter. New fab relief does not arrive at scale until Idaho ID1 in mid-2027 at the earliest, with further capacity in late 2028 and beyond, so the tightness has a multi-year runway. That combination of sold-out supply, contracted visibility and a long capacity lead time gives MU the most explosive 12–18 month setup on this lens, even though it is a cyclical oligopolist rather than a monopolist.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: More than 75% of fiscal 2027 output already committed under formal customer commitments, after Micron completed price-and-volume agreements for its entire calendar 2026 HBM supply.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: medium — only three firms make leading-edge DRAM/HBM at scale, and no hyperscaler fabs DRAM itself, so the trio is irreplaceable; but Micron individually is substitutable within the trio, as Samsung's HBM share jump from 21% to 33% in one quarter shows.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case: Base: pricing holds through the pre-committed 2027 book and tightness extends into 2028, delivering a strong double-digit gain well above the broad market. Bull: 2027–2028 proves tighter than 2026 as guided, contract prices stay elevated, and the stock moves toward the screen's analyst mean-target upside of about 83%.
- Top risk: Classic memory cyclicality — a peak-cycle 87% non-GAAP gross margin in FQ4 2026, customer double-ordering, and more than $50bn of guided FY2027 capex could reverse quickly if new capacity arrives and pricing rolls.
- Conviction (1-10): 8
