# Ballot 1 — single pick (Panelist C: Quality/moat & irreplaceability)

- Top pick: AVGO
- Runner-up: KLAC
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): AVGO owns the deepest defensible moat in this AI supply chain: a decade-plus co-design lock-in with Google on TPUs (~70% custom-AI-accelerator share), a $110B consolidated backlog with a ~3x book-to-bill, and >$100B of FY27 AI visibility on 9–10 GW of committed capacity. Unlike NVDA, Broadcom is not the thing hyperscalers want to disintermediate — it is the enabler of their in-sourcing (TPU co-design), so the "in-source the product" threat works in its favor as a vendor, not against it. The 93%-GM VMware cash flows fund the debt load while semis compound. At 19.4x forward P/E and 23% off its $481.57 peak, it is the only irreplaceability-9 name in the set trading at a discount to its own trajectory.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: Q2 bookings of $30B at a ~3x book-to-bill against a $110B consolidated backlog, plus the 5-year Google TPU deal through 2031 and the Anthropic $125.2B five-year TPU lease.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: medium — Google is multi-vendor-ing (Marvell, MediaTek, in-house team) and share may drift 70%→~60%, but the dependency is structural (TPU co-design is a decade-long relationship, not a part you swap), and Broadcom also owns the AI networking fabric (Tomahawk 6 / Jericho 4) that every cluster needs. Hyperscalers can buy ASIC design from Broadcom instead of Nvidia — they cannot route around co-design expertise.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base ~+40% (analyst upside +41.4%, backlog converts, FY27 >$100B AI revenue target lands); bull ~+80% if the OpenAI 10 GW partnership and Anthropic XPU ramp convert to committed revenue and the VMware deleveraging story wins a multiple re-rate.
- Top risk: credit concentration — the $42B bank syndication making Broadcom Anthropic's creditor means a single customer's solvency/funding shock hits revenue AND balance sheet simultaneously; second-order, any slip in gigawatt deployment timelines strands pre-stocked inventory.

**Top 3 deep dives:**

**AVGO:** The $110B consolidated backlog (majority AI, semis ≥50%) with a ~3x book-to-bill is the strongest contracted demand signal in the set, and the Google 5-year TPU deal through 2031 plus the Anthropic $125.2B lease push visibility 4–5+ years out. No hyperscaler can realistically replace a decade of TPU co-design or the Jericho/Tomahawk networking stack in-house. Rough 12–18mo: base ~+40% (41.4% analyst upside, backlog converts); bull ~+80% (OpenAI 10 GW converts, multiple re-rates on deleveraging).

**CDNS:** Irreplaceability 10 — the tightest chokepoint in the batch: every leading-edge AI chip is designed in Cadence or Synopsys, chip flows take years to re-validate, and even hyperscalers designing their own silicon still pay the EDA toll. Record $8.1B backlog with $4.2B in the next 12 months (~2/3 of guided revenue), fourth straight beat-and-raise. Rough 12–18mo: base ~+15–20% (compounder at 70x P/E, flawless-compounding pricing); bull ~+40% if agentic-AI engine consumption re-accelerates backlog growth.

**KLAC:** A near-monopoly (73.8% metrology/inspection, 60% brightfield / >50% darkfield, 80%+ reticle inspection) whose algorithmic moat is decades of defect-classification data that no entrant can replicate — tool-referenced fab infrastructure locks it in at the process level. $12.57B backlog, +60% YoY, with process-control intensity rising per wafer at 3nm/2nm and advanced packaging. Rough 12–18mo: base ~+25–35% (12.3% analyst upside plus growth into 2027); bull ~+60% if control-intensity pricing power flows through to margin expansion.

**Left out of my top 10 and why:** NVDA — live in-sourcing threat is real (TPU v7, Trainium 3, MTIA v3, Maia 200 all in production; Meta targeting 100% internal inference by 2027); through this lens the trap is disqualifying-grade despite the $1T order book. ANET — irrepl only 6: merchant silicon from Broadcom means no hardware chokepoint, Nvidia Spectrum-X is now #1 in DC Ethernet per IDC, and 42% top-2 customer concentration compounds the fragility.
