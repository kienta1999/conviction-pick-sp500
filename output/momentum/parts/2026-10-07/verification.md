## AVGO (Broadcom)

**1. Consolidated backlog $110B at end of Q3 FY2026 (~Sep 3, 2026); semiconductors ≥50% and majority AI; book-to-bill ~3x; AI-specific backlog $73B+.**
→ **CONTRADICTED** (number/date misattributed). The $110B consolidated-backlog figure — with "perhaps fully 50 percent of it at least is semiconductors" from CEO Hock Tan — was disclosed on the Q3 **FY2025** call (reported ~Sept 4, 2025), a full year earlier (MarketBeat earnings transcript, Sept 2025; Futurum Group analysis, Sept 2025; CRN, Sept 2025). At end of Q3 FY2026 (Aug 2, 2026), reported RPO was ~$179.2B, up from ~$164.6B at end of Q2 FY2026 (CryptoBriefing, Sept 2, 2026; TheStreet, Sept 14, 2026). Book-to-bill ~3x is separately supported for AI semis: Q2 FY2026 AI bookings >$30B vs $10.8B shipped (~2.8x) (Webull/transcript recap; AInvest, Sept 2, 2026 noted backlog compounding "at three times the shipping rate"). The "$73B+ AI-specific backlog" figure was not found in any source searched.

**2. Management line of sight to >$100B FY2027 AI revenue on 9–10 GW of committed capacity.**
→ **CONFIRMED**. On the June 2026 call Tan said FY2027 AI chip revenue would clear $100B; on the Sept 2, 2026 Q3 call he raised it to ~$115B FY2027 (and $230B FY2028), saying the figure is backed by supply already locked in (Motley Fool, Sept 22 & 27, 2026; BigGo Finance transcript recap, Sept 2, 2026; BigGo Finance, Sept 14, 2026 — Tan on CNBC Mad Money stood by $115B). Per-customer 2027 deployments disclosed on the Sept 2 call: Anthropic 5 GW of TPU v8i, OpenAI 1.3 GW of Jalapeño, Meta ~3 GW of MTIA through 2028 — ~9 GW for 2027 — plus Google's long-term TPU agreement at "multi-tens of billions annually" (BigGo Finance, Sept 2, 2026; TechTimes, Sept 3, 2026; TechFlowPost, Sept 16, 2026).

**3. Google 5-year TPU agreement through 2031 (four generations, v8–v11).**
→ **CONFIRMED**. SEC filing disclosed after market close April 6, 2026: new five-year agreement to develop/build future TPU versions, plus networking/rack components through 2031 (Motley Fool, April 7, 2026). JPMorgan note (June 17, 2026) and TradingView/TechTimes (Oct 6, 2026) confirm the pact locks in the next four TPU generations (v8, v9, v10, v11) with commitments to increase TPU revenue annually through 2031; JPMorgan describes Broadcom as "anchored as the primary supplier across the 5-year term."

**4. $42B senior financing syndication for Anthropic's Broadcom-TPU deployments (~Oct 2, 2026), and Anthropic's $125.2B five-year TPU lease commitment.**
→ **CONFIRMED**. Bloomberg (reported Oct 2, 2026; recapped by Temperature2, Oct 2, 2026): Broadcom's banking syndicate raising a $60B debt package for Anthropic's chip leases — a $42B Class A senior-secured tranche syndicated to banks plus an $18B Class B junior tranche led by Blackstone. Reuters (Oct 1, 2026): Anthropic's IPO prospectus discloses Broadcom agreed to lend up to $42B via convertible notes; the prospectus also states Anthropic's $125.2B five-year commitment to lease TPU compute capacity (Motley Fool, Oct 6, 2026; Techstrong Semi, Oct 5, 2026; GPUTrade, Oct 2026).
