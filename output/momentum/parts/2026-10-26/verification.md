## MU — Micron

### 5. ~$150B RPO under 26 take-or-pay SCAs, ~$32B customer financial commitments, 75% of FY2027 shipments committed; HBM sold out through 2027 (fiscal Q4 2026, ~30 Sep 2026) — **CONFIRMED in substance, with two definition/wording caveats**

Fiscal Q4 2026 was reported **2026-09-30** (quarter ended 2026-09-03). Each element:

- **26 SCAs, all take-or-pay:** Earnings-call transcript (Longbridge, 2026-09-30), CFO Mark Murphy: "To date we have signed 26 SCAs in total… All SCAs have take-or-pay contracted volumes." Up from 16 in June. Management estimates SCAs cover >35% of revenue through 2030; some now extend into 2031.
- **~$150B RPO — definition matters, dossier must not call this generic backlog:** Murphy, same transcript: "our remaining performance obligations, or RPO, is approximately $150 billion. **RPO reflects the contract value for only SCAs that have a determined pricing framework**, which can be either a fixed price or subject to a pricing floor and ceiling. **RPO is based on committed volumes and minimum pricing and is inherently conservative.** We expect revenue to well exceed the associated RPO over the terms of the agreements." So $150B ≠ total value of all 26 SCAs, and ≠ backlog in the ordinary sense — it is the priced-framework subset at minimum pricing. The June figure (~$100B for 14 of 16 SCAs) was measured differently and is not like-for-like (AI Stock Wire, Oct 2026).
- **~$32B customer financial commitments:** CEO Sanjay Mehrotra, same call, via multiple reports: customers have put up $32B in financial commitments, "the vast majority of which are cash deposits"; $12.3B of deposits were received in fiscal Q4 alone and are booked as **financing cash flow, not revenue** (AI Stock Wire / Carbon Finance earnings reviews, Oct 2026).
- **75% committed:** Mehrotra on the call (MarketBeat transcript, 2026-09-30): "more than 75% of our output is already committed" for 2027, clarified as **across SCA *and* non-SCA customers** (including annual-basis large customers). Secondary press paraphrases this as "more than 75% of fiscal 2027 output / shipments already committed" (Stocktwits news, Aju Press 2026-10-01, SiliconAI News). The dossier's "75% of FY2027 shipments committed" is a fair paraphrase — but the primary word is **output**, not shipments, and the commitment pool is broader than the SCAs alone. Do not attribute the 75% to the SCAs only.
- **HBM through 2027 — dossier wording is stronger than the primary source:** Primary/call-derived wording is that Micron "has completed agreements for the **vast majority of its calendar 2027 HBM bit supply**, at prices significantly [well] above 2026," and Mehrotra: "a large part of the volume is already sold out for 2027 for HBM" (Aju Press, 2026-10-01; FXCM post-earnings note; Mindcron call summary). Some secondary press does use the dossier's exact shorthand — "HBM3E and HBM4 chips are sold out through calendar 2027" (Startup Fortune, 2026-09-30/10-01). Verdict: directionally correct and widely reported, but against the primary disclosure the accurate phrase is "vast majority of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply contracted," not an unqualified "sold out through 2027."

## VRTX — Vertex Pharmaceuticals

### 9. Sole supplier of disease-modifying CFTR modulators eligible for ~95% of US CF patients after Apr 2026 label expansion; core patents extend past 2037 — **CONFIRMED, with a patent-date split the dossier should state correctly**

- **~95% / April 2026 expansion:** Vertex press release via Business Wire, **2026-04-01**: FDA approved expanded use of ALYFTREK (ages 6+) and TRIKAFTA (ages 2+); with the expansion "any variant that results in production of CFTR protein is now included," supported by data on 564 (ALYFTREK) / 521 (TRIKAFTA) variants; "approximately 800 more people with CF in the U.S. are now eligible… approximately **95% of people with CF in the U.S.** are now eligible for treatment with a CFTR modulator" (also HCPLive, 2026-04-01).
- **Sole commercial supplier:** No approved competing CFTR modulator was found. Motley Fool (2026-08-14 and 2026-10-20): Vertex has "no meaningful competition" in CF and provides "the only therapies that treat the underlying cause of cystic fibrosis." Challengers are pipeline-only — Sionna Therapeutics (a lead candidate recently failed a mid-stage study) and Krystal Biotech; AbbVie previously abandoned its CF programs. "Sole supplier" is therefore correct for *commercial / disease-modifying* CFTR modulators as of Oct 2026, not a claim that no one is developing competitors.
- **Patents — split verdict inside this claim:** TRIKAFTA's core patent protection runs **through 2037** (Vertex annual report / SEC, via Reuters and TheStreet; GlobalData: "the year Trikafta loses patent protection" is 2037). What extends **past** 2037 is the successor product: "Alyftrek extends Vertex's CFTR modulator composition-of-matter patent protection **from 2037 for Trikafta to 2039 for Alyftrek**" (William Blair analyst Miles Minter, via Pharmaceutical Technology / Patsnap, 2025). So "core patents extend past 2037" is CONFIRMED only at the franchise/portfolio level (via Alyftrek, 2039); if the dossier attributes a post-2037 date to Trikafta's own core patents, that specific attribution is imprecise — Trikafta = 2037.

### 10. Povetacicept Phase 3 RAINIER showed ~52% proteinuria reduction, FDA target action date 2026-11-30 — **CONFIRMED**

- Vertex press release via Business Wire, **2026-10-23** (NEJM publication + ASN Kidney Week 2026 presentation): pre-specified Week 36 interim analysis of Phase 3 RAINIER in IgA nephropathy — "povetacicept achieving a **52.0% reduction from baseline in proteinuria at Week 36** and a statistically significant and clinically meaningful **49.8% reduction versus placebo (P<0.0001)**," both secondary endpoints met; 42.2% reached 24-h UPCR <0.5 g/g vs 6.2% on placebo. (Topline 52% was first announced March 2026; the Oct 23 release is the full NEJM/ASN disclosure.)
- Precision note: 52.0% is the *from-baseline* reduction; the placebo-adjusted reduction is 49.8%. The dossier's "~52% proteinuria reduction" is correct if read as from-baseline.
- **PDUFA / target action date:** Vertex Q2 2026 release, 2026-08-03: "The U.S. FDA accepted the BLA submission for accelerated approval of povetacicept for adults with IgAN and assigned a **PDUFA target action date of November 30, 2026**." The Oct 23 RAINIER release repeats "FDA Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) date of November 30." This is for *accelerated* approval in IgAN — the dossier should keep that qualifier.

### 11. Q2 2026 revenue ~$3.33B and FY guidance raised to ~$13.1–13.2B — **CONFIRMED (exact, from the primary release)**

- Vertex press release, "Vertex Reports Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results," **2026-08-03** (Business Wire; full text read): "Total revenue increased 12% to **$3.33 billion** compared to the second quarter of 2025" and "Vertex today **raised its full year 2026 total revenue guidance to $13.1 billion to $13.2 billion from $12.95 billion to $13.1 billion previously**," embedding ≥$500M of non-CF revenue (CASGEVY + JOURNAVX).
- Caveat on currency of the guidance: the Q2-release guidance **explicitly excluded** the then-pending Crinetics acquisition, and Vertex said it would provide updated guidance after close. Secondary sources report Crinetics closed 2026-09-01 (~$10B equity value). The $13.1–13.2B range is exactly right *as the Q2 raise the dossier describes*; if the dossier presents it as Vertex's current FY2026 guidance in late October, the orchestrator should check for a post-close guidance update.
