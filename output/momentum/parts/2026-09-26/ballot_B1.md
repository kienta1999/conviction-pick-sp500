# Ballot B1 — GROWTH/MOMENTUM INVESTOR — 2026-09-26

Lens: revenue acceleration, earnings-revision freshness, and price momentum. Explosive YoY growth + accelerating momentum wins; multiple expansion ahead of numbers loses.

---

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** SNDK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is the steepest real compounder in the panel: Q3 FY26 revenue +345.8% YoY with a $5.6B beat, the 5th consecutive quarterly revenue record, and an EPS beat streak of 8/8 (avg surprise +20.3%). Unlike SNDK, it printed no guide miss — management raised capex to >$25B and pulled Boise forward to mid-2027 against "unprecedented," sold-out HBM demand. Consensus has already moved sharply above the $50B FQ4 guide ahead of Sept 30 earnings, i.e., the upward-revision cycle is live, not spent. At $1.2T it's big, but 6.8x forward P/E is a peak-earnings discount multiple on numbers still accelerating.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** "We're sold out for 2026" (CBO Sumit Sadana, CES 2026) plus **$22B in customer prepayments** (Q3 FY26) — customers are paying cash upfront because Micron can meet only 50–66% of medium-term bit demand.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** LOW. One of only 3 HBM makers on earth; hyperscalers cannot build DRAM fabs (zero realistic in-sourcing threat); dossier irreplaceability 8/10, limited only by CXMT/Chinese share creep in conventional DRAM.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+25% as HBM4 revenue (> $1B, ramping 2x HBM3E) compounds and the $50B-guide quarter lands; bull ~+60% if Sept 30 triggers a re-rating off the peak-earnings multiple and analysts re-model HBM pricing through 2027.
- **Top risk:** Memory boom-bust — if AI demand pauses, >$25B capex converts to oversupply at a $1.2T market cap; the Sept 30 print is a binary on the peak-earnings question.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 deep notes

**MU (#1):** The single most compelling data point is the combination of "sold out for 2026" with $22B of cash-in-hand prepayments — customers fund the capex because supply is physically capped until Boise (mid-2027) and Clay NY (~2030). Base ~+25% over 12–18 months on compounding HBM4 ramp; bull ~+60% if the Sept 30 beat breaks the peak-earnings discount and the $100B-2028 HBM market ($35B in 2025) gets re-underwritten.

**SNDK (#2):** Eight NBM framework agreements covering >50% of FY27 bits and ~two-thirds of FY28 bits, signed at ≥$93.9B expected revenue + $16.5B financial guarantees — contracted, allocated demand beyond calendar 2027. But the FQ1 guide miss vs $11.16B consensus and TrendForce's price-hike deceleration (70–75% QoQ → 10–15%) keep it #2 on this lens: acceleration is intact on volume, stalling on price. Base ~+20%; bull ~+50% if contract pricing holds through 2027.

**APH (#3):** Record $10.7B orders in Q2 2026 at a 1.23:1 book-to-bill — demand provably outrunning shipments two quarters running — with IT datacom +89% YoY and +63% organic, "virtually all" of the +22% sequential growth AI-related. Reference-architecture embed in NVIDIA H100 / AMD Instinct keeps share defensible through the buildout. Base ~+30% as backlog converts; bull ~+60% on mid-teens sequential datacom growth compounding.

### Deliberately left out of the top 10

**GOOGL (#11):** momentum is the problem — price momentum is modest (ret_12m +36.4% vs peers at 40%+ to 16x), the Q2 print shocked on capex (−$5.9B FCF), and headline EPS is inflated by ~$98–99B of unrealized SpaceX/Anthropic paper marks (operational EPS ~$2.85–2.87); the $514B backlog is real but not accelerating fast enough to win this lens.
**CSCO (#12):** $9.3B AI orders are real, but growth is order-flow, not revenue-acceleration (adj product GM 64.8% vs 67.5% a year ago — margin dilution), and NVIDIA already leads DC Ethernet; momentum without pricing power loses.
**CF (#13):** earnings are price-only — EPS +98% YoY but Q2 volume fell 15.3%, consensus is Hold with ~5.7% upside, and the "growth" is an Iran-spike commodity event, not a compounder; slowest growth trajectory in the 13.
