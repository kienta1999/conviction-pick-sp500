Substitute threat is economic: at $650+ urea farmers pivot acres to soybeans ("soybean pivot," USDA planting data, spring 2026) — demand destruction, not disintermediation. **Irreplaceability 6/10** (commodity; moat is cost position and scale, not product lock-in).

- Commodity cyclicality: China expects 4–6M tons of urea exports in 2026; India demand can be deferred; demand destruction at high prices (soy pivot).
