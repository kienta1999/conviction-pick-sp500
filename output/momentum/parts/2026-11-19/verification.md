## CF — CF Industries

### CF-1. Urea supply loss of ~nine million metric tons — CONFIRMED

The Q3 2026 earnings call transcript (Nov 5, 2026) quotes EVP and Chief Commercial Officer Bert Frost verbatim: "Since hostilities began in the Middle East in late February, we estimate that the global market has lost approximately nine million metric tons of urea supply." Multiple transcript sources (Nasdaq / Motley Fool, StockAnalysis, Roic AI) carry the identical wording, and American Market News (Nov 7, 2026) summarises the same figure. Source: CF Industries Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, Nov 5, 2026.

### CF-2. European curtailment and Trinidad shutdown — CONFIRMED

CF's own Q3 2026 results release (Business Wire, Nov 4, 2026) states: "An estimated 37% of Europe's nameplate ammonia capacity was reportedly offline or operating at reduced rates in October 2026. In addition, approximately 35% of ammonia capacity in Trinidad is being permanently shut down." That supports both halves: 37% is over the claimed 35% threshold, and the Trinidad figure matches exactly. The call transcript also has Frost saying "We believe more than 35% of ammonia capacity in the region is curtailed." Note the release's driver wording is "sharply higher natural gas prices" and gas availability in Trinidad, matching the claim's "high natural gas costs". Source: CF Industries Q3 2026 results release via Business Wire, Nov 4, 2026; Q3 2026 call transcript, Nov 5, 2026.

### CF-3. Q3 2026 catalyst and mid-cycle EBITDA — CONFIRMED (timing note)

Results were released after the bell on Nov 4, 2026. The release and Zacks (Nov 4, 2026) confirm net sales of USD 1.86B vs USD 1.66B a year earlier (+12.1%) and diluted EPS of USD 2.87 vs USD 2.19 (+31.1%); net earnings USD 436M and adjusted EBITDA USD 833M. The USD 2.9B mid-cycle EBITDA figure is confirmed as management's current estimate on the Nov 5 call ("Our low-cost position on the global nitrogen cost curve supports our current USD 2.9 billion mid-cycle EBITDA estimate"), with growth projects expected to lift it to USD 3.3B by 2030. Timing note: the raise to USD 2.9B appears to have happened earlier — a thesis timeline (Finn, reviewed Nov 8, 2026) dates the formal raise to August 2026 ("Management formally raised mid cycle EBITDA expectations to USD 2.9 billion based on structural capital costs") — so the Q3 call restated rather than newly raised the figure. The figure itself is correct. Context not in the claim: EPS missed the Zacks consensus of USD 2.93 by about 2%. Sources: CF Q3 2026 release, Business Wire, Nov 4, 2026; Zacks, Nov 4-5, 2026; Motley Fool Q3 2026 call transcript, Nov 5, 2026.

### CF-4. Moat: capacity shares, Donaldsonville, utilisation — CONTRADICTED on the Nutrien figure only

- CF's 39% of US domestic ammonia capacity: CONFIRMED. Farmdoc daily (Monaco, Paulson, Schnitkey and Zulauf, May 26, 2026, summarised by AgManager, Oct 1, 2026): "CF Industries held 39 percent, Nutrien 16 percent, Koch Fertilizer 10 percent, and LSB Industries 5 percent" of domestic ammonia capacity.
- CF's ~40% of North American ammonia capacity: CONFIRMED in substance. Company-aligned coverage puts CF at "approximately 40% of North American ammonia capacity" (also 41% of granular urea, 44% of UAN).
- Donaldsonville as the world's largest ammonia production complex: CONFIRMED. Multiple sources describe the Donaldsonville, Louisiana complex as the world's largest (and most flexible) ammonia production complex; CF is also described as the world's largest producer of ammonia.
- 97% ammonia capacity utilisation for the first nine months of 2026: CONFIRMED. The Nov 4, 2026 release states year-to-date operation "at a 97% utilization rate of available ammonia capacity" (available capacity excludes the idled Yazoo City complex); gross ammonia production ~7.2M tons for the nine months.
- Nutrien ~23%: CONTRADICTED. The same farmdoc source that confirms CF's 39% puts Nutrien at 16% of US domestic ammonia capacity — not ~23%. No source found supports a 23% Nutrien share on any capacity basis checked; Nutrien's own 2025 Fact Book shows total gross ammonia nameplate capacity of 7.3M tonnes including 2.2M tonnes in Trinidad. Correct figure to use: Nutrien ~16% of US domestic ammonia capacity. This error flatters Nutrien; correcting it widens, not narrows, CF's lead. Sources: farmdoc daily via AgManager, Oct 1, 2026; CF Q3 2026 release, Nov 4, 2026; Nutrien 2025 Fact Book.

### MSFT-2. Azure +48% / USD 33.2B and capacity constraints — CONFIRMED (sourcing note)

Azure revenue of USD 33.165B, +48% YoY, is confirmed by the earnings release and call ("Azure revenue was ahead of expectations and increased 48%"), and multiple outlets note this was the first time Microsoft disclosed Azure revenue in dollars. On capacity, CFO Amy Hood said on the Oct 28 call that "customer demand continues to exceed available supply" and that some third-party capacity deliveries expected in Q1 were delayed, with some GPU-as-a-service contracts slipping to the second half of FY2027. Sourcing note: the specific "at least through the end of 2026" phrasing traces to Hood's April 2026 call ("In April, she said Microsoft expected to stay constrained 'at least through 2026'"), as reported in the Oct 28 earnings preview; the Oct 28 call itself did not repeat that exact end-date formulation, but it confirmed the constraint was still binding and extended the delivery slippage into H2 FY2027 (Jan-Jun 2027), which is consistent with, and if anything longer than, the claimed horizon. Sources: Microsoft Q1 FY2027 earnings call transcript, Oct 28, 2026; Microsoft earnings release, Oct 28, 2026.

## ANET — Arista Networks

### ANET-1. Purchase commitments USD 11.4B; beyond-12-months split — CONFIRMED on totals; USD 2.1B split UNVERIFIED from the call

Headline totals confirmed: CFO Chantelle Breithaupt on the Q3 call (Nov 3, 2026) said "Our purchase commitments at the end of the quarter were USD 11.4 billion, up from USD 9.7 billion at the end of Q2." The USD 6.8B year-end 2025 figure is confirmed by the Q2 2026 10-Q trail (SEC-API analysis: USD 9.7B at Jun 30, 2026 "against USD 6.8bn at 31 Dec 2025"). The June beyond-12-months figure is confirmed: at Jun 30, 2026, USD 9.4B of the USD 9.7B had expected receipt dates within twelve months, leaving USD 0.3B beyond (Vestick/10-Q). The September beyond-12-months figure of USD 2.1B was NOT stated on the call — Breithaupt said the new multi-year memory agreement is "a multiyear agreement that you'll see as the Q comes out," i.e. the split lives in the Q3 10-Q, which was still pending at the time of the call. Corroboration in magnitude: Zacks (Nov 2026) reports "Approximately 80% of these commitments are expected to be utilized within 12 months," which implies roughly USD 2.3B beyond 12 months — close to, but not exactly, the claimed USD 2.1B. Verdict: totals CONFIRMED; the USD 2.1B split is UNVERIFIED from the sources checked, though consistent with the ~80% figure. The dossier should cite the Q3 10-Q for the split once filed rather than the call. Sources: Arista Q3 2026 earnings call transcript (Nasdaq / Motley Fool), Nov 3, 2026; Q2 2026 10-Q via SEC-API analysis; Zacks, Nov 2026.

### ANET-2. Ullal "2-year problem... until 2028" — CONFIRMED

Network World (Aug 5, 2026) quotes the same lines in context (memory and other silicon availability). Note the context was industry-wide supply-chain tightness, and Ullal in the same answer said Arista's own steps should show results in the back half of 2026 — the quote is about the industry, not Arista specifically. Source: Arista Q2 FY2026 earnings call transcript, Aug 4, 2026.

Arista reported after the close on Nov 3, 2026: revenue USD 3.66B (USD 3,657.6M), up 58.5% YoY and 20.5% QoQ, the fastest growth since IPO, beating the ~USD 3.33B consensus and ~USD 3.3B company guide; non-GAAP EPS USD 1.20 (+55.8%). The ~USD 16.4B pre-report consensus is confirmed: TIKR's Nov 2, 2026 preview ("Analysts expect revenue to climb... to USD 16.4 billion in 2027") and Simply Wall St (Nov 7, 2026: "prior to the latest earnings, the analysts had been anticipated revenues of US USD 16.4b... in 2027"). The guide is therefore roughly USD 2.1B above consensus. Sources: Arista Q3 2026 earnings release and call, Nov 3, 2026; TIKR, Nov 2 and Nov 4, 2026; Simply Wall St, Nov 7, 2026.

### ANET-4. IDC Q2 2026 datacenter Ethernet switch shares — CONFIRMED

IDC's Quarterly Ethernet Switch Tracker release for 2Q26 states: Nvidia held 20.4% of datacenter segment revenues (USD 2.5B, +181.1% YoY) and "maintained its position as the #1 branded vendor in datacenter Ethernet switching"; Arista held 18.7% in the datacenter segment (revenues USD 2.5B, +37.6% YoY); Cisco generated USD 2.2B in the datacenter segment (18.2% share). The datacenter segment totalled USD 12.3B, +64.5% YoY. The Spectrum-X threat characterisation is supported by Nvidia's +181.1% YoY datacenter Ethernet growth and IDC calling Spectrum-X "the preferred network interconnect for large-scale AI training" in its Q1 2026 release. Source: IDC, "Ethernet Switch Market Surges 43.4% to USD 18.9B in 2Q26", 2Q26 tracker release.

## Overall verdict

No contradiction found changes the top-3 adjudication. The single hard contradiction is in CF-4: the dossier's "Nutrien ~23%" is wrong — the authoritative farmdoc capacity-share source puts Nutrien at 16% of US domestic ammonia capacity against CF's confirmed 39% — but correcting it widens CF's moat lead rather than narrowing it, so it strengthens, not weakens, the CF case; the dossier should be corrected to 16% before the memo is finalised. The only other gaps are sourcing caveats, not contradictions: ANET's USD 2.1B beyond-12-months commitments split was not stated on the earnings call and should be cited to the Q3 10-Q once filed (secondary reporting implies ~USD 2.3B, consistent in magnitude), MSFT's "constraints at least through end of 2026" wording traces to the April 2026 call rather than the October 28 call (which confirmed demand still exceeds supply and pushed some third-party deliveries into H2 FY2027), and CF's mid-cycle EBITDA raise to USD 2.9B dates to August 2026 rather than the Q3 call, which merely restated it. All other figures — CF's nine-million-ton urea loss, the 37%/35% Europe and Trinidad curtailments, Q3 sales and EPS, MSFT's RPO, Azure and OpenAI figures, and ANET's revenue, 2027 guide, consensus beat and IDC shares — verified against primary or near-primary sources as stated.
