### Top 3 — shortage/backlog detail and scenarios

**1. ANET.** The single most compelling point is the lengthening forward book: purchase commitments of \$11.4B with the over-12-month slice jumping to \$2.1B from \$0.3B, alongside Ullal's two-year industry shortage to 2028 and about 52-week lead times. Base 12-18 months about +25-35% on delivery of the about \$18.5B 2027 guide; bull about +50-70% if the shortage persists and Spectrum-X stalls.

**2. APH.** Orders of \$12.17B in Q3, up 99% year over year, a 1.19 book-to-bill, and orders exceeding sales for three straight quarters, adding \$5.7B to backlog year to date — the broadest verified order surge in the dossier, with IT datacom up 117% year over year. The caveat for this lens is that the book is short-dated: the dossier notes a substantial portion of RPOs is fulfilled within three months, and no aggregate backlog or lead-time figure was found. Base 12-18 months about +20-30% if AI interconnect demand holds; bull about +40-55% if datacom re-accelerates after the guided mid-single-digit sequential Q4 pause.

**3. CF.** The only verified physical shortage in the dossier: about nine million metric tons of urea supply lost since hostilities began in late February, Middle East exports cut about half, over 35% of European ammonia capacity curtailed in October 2026, and CF itself at 97% ammonia utilisation, with new plants taking 3-4 years to build. That is a genuine supply hole with a multi-year rebuild lag, and CF is the clear North American leader at about 40% of capacity. Base 12-18 months about +10-20% if tight markets persist into 2027; bull about +35-50% if the disruption extends and urea pricing holds, with the bear case being a ceasefire or restored output reversing the war premium quickly.
