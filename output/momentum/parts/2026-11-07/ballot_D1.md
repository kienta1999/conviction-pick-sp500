The market is chasing the flashy AI and GLP-1 drug names while this bottleneck supplier to the railroads compounds quietly underneath them. That combination — real scarcity, reasonable valuation, and a boring story — is exactly what this lens is hunting for.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Total multi-year backlog of \$31.44 billion at 30 September 2026, up 22.9% year on year, equal to roughly 2.5x guided FY2026 revenue of \$12.30–\$12.60 billion, with management stating order coverage is at its strongest level to date, particularly for long-lead-time new locomotives at 24–36 months of coverage.

Switching is prohibitively costly because railroads standardise fleets around one builder's platforms, and the battery/hydrogen transition is one Wabtec itself leads rather than a bypass.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base roughly +19% to the analyst target of \$332.08 as the backlog converts at the guided FY2026 adjusted EPS range of \$10.85–\$11.05; bull roughly +35–45% if international locomotive orders sustain and Freight margin expansion continues, with the backlog providing downside cover that a spot-price thesis cannot.
- **Top risk:** Freight cyclicality — a downturn lets railroads defer fleet renewal, turning a 24–36-month backlog into delayed deliveries, and customer concentration means two Class I customers accounted for over \$600 million of Q3 North American orders.
