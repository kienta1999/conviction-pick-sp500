### Note on the LLY shortage disagreement

Yesterday's panel scored LLY shortage 4/10 ("shortage over"); today's research scores 8/10. I side with today's evidence on direction but not on magnitude: the FDA did declare the tirzepatide shortage resolved in December 2024, and the 2026 gaps are narrower — device-constrained, high-dose pens only, regional, 1–4 weeks — not a broad drug shortage. An honest score is closer to 6–7/10 than a full 8/10, and the $50bn capacity build is forward visibility, not current scarcity pricing (realised prices fell 15%). Even tempered, that is still the strongest shortage evidence in this batch, which is why LLY keeps my top pick — but I am not inflating the 8/10 into a claim that patients broadly cannot get the drug.
