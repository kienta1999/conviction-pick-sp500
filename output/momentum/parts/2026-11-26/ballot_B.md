The stock is also priced for perfection at fwd PE 27.4 with only +5.6% analyst upside.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

**Adjudicating the LLY shortage disagreement:** yesterday's panel scored LLY shortage 4/10 ("shortage over"); today's research scores it 8/10. The FDA did declare the tirzepatide shortage resolved in Dec 2024, and the dossier's own verdict is that the constraint is now "can't charge enough, not can't make enough." For this lens that matters less than the growth: the volume engine is still accelerating, which is what a momentum pick needs.
