## ANET — Arista Networks (tentative single-pick winner)

### 1. Backlog: deferred revenue ~$6.9B at end-Q2 2026 (up from $6.2B Q1); purchase commitments $9.7B (up from $3.6B YoY)
**CONFIRMED**
- Q2 2026 earnings call transcript (call 2026-08-04; transcript published 2026-08-11): CFO Chantelle Breithaupt — "Our purchase commitments at the end of the quarter were $9.7 billion, up from $8.9 billion at the end of Q1… Our total deferred revenue balance was approximately $6.9 billion, up from $6.2 billion in the prior quarter." Sources: Motley Fool transcript (fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/11/arista-anet-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) and MarketBeat transcript mirror of the same call.
- YoY base for commitments: Arista Q2 2025 10-Q states purchase obligations of $3.6B as of June 30, 2025; multiple Q2 2026 write-ups (TipRanks, Zacks, Fool takeaways) state $9.7B "tripling from $3.6 billion one year ago." So the $3.6B YoY comparator checks out, even though the Q2 2026 call itself quoted the sequential $8.9B Q1 figure.

### 2. Shortage driver: Dell'Oro — AI back-end networks supply-constrained (not demand) for at least next 1–2 years; 52-week lead times (COO Todd Nightingale, Q1 2026 call)
**CONFIRMED**
- Dell'Oro Group primary release, 2026-09-03, "AI Back-End Networks Switch Sales Surpass Front-End Networks for the First Time in 2Q 2026" (delloro.com; also PR Newswire 2026-09-03): VP Sameh Boujelbene — "We expect the market to remain supply-constrained, rather than demand-constrained, for at least the next one-to-two years." Verbatim match.
- Q1 2026 call transcript (call 2026-05-05, MarketBeat transcript): Todd Nightingale, President and COO — "We are experiencing 52-week lead times pretty reliably with reservation needs beyond that, and our customers certainly do not want to wait that long." Attribution to Nightingale is correct in the primary transcript; note one secondary article mis-attributed the line to the CTO — do not cite that version.

### 3. Catalyst + date: FY2026 revenue guidance raised to ~$12.6B (+40% YoY); next earnings 2026-11-03
**CONFIRMED**
- Guidance: Q2 2026 call (2026-08-04) — CFO: "we are raising our 2026 fiscal year outlook to 40% revenue growth, equating to approximately $12.6 billion"; CEO Jayshree Ullal confirmed the third raise of the year, from $11.5B in May. Corroborated by Morningstar's earnings note (guide raised from $11.5B to $12.6B, +40%).
- Date: Company press release dated 2026-10-06 (Business Wire, hosted as PDF on Arista IR CDN, s21.q4cdn.com) — "Arista Networks to Announce Q3 2026 Financial Results on Tuesday, November 3, 2026," after market close, call 1:30 PM PT. Note: at least one aggregator piece said "Nov. 2" — that is contradicted by the company's own release; the dossier's 2026-11-03 is correct.

### 4. Moat: EOS/design-in makes rip-out costly, but Celestica white-box is #1 in AI back-end Ethernet and NVIDIA Spectrum-X is a live bypass
**CONFIRMED** (with scope nuance)
- EOS switching costs: Morningstar (wide-moat rating for ANET) — moat "based on intangible assets and switching costs"; EOS is differentiated, proprietary software/hardware configurations require training/certification, "networking setups take months to implement and years to optimize." Multiple secondary analyses describe EOS + CloudVision integration making rip-and-replace "expensive, disruptive, and risky." The design-in/switching-cost direction is well supported; the precise cost of rip-out is not quantified in any primary source found.
- Celestica #1: Dell'Oro primary release, 2026-09-03 — "Celestica retained the leading position in Ethernet AI back-end switch sales, followed closely by NVIDIA. Arista ranked third, but would have been more closely tied if deferred AI revenue were included." Dell'Oro's 2025 full-year release (2026-03-10) likewise had Celestica and NVIDIA leading with ~50% combined share, Arista third. Scope nuance: the #1 ranking is specifically *Ethernet* AI back-end switch sales, as the claim states.
- Spectrum-X as live bypass: Dell'Oro's ranking itself places NVIDIA #2 in Ethernet AI back-end — i.e., a shipping, share-holding alternative that does not use Arista/EOS. Secondary reporting (TechTime, citing Jensen Huang) names Meta, Microsoft, Oracle and xAI building gigawatt-scale AI factories on Spectrum-X. That supports "live bypass" in substance; "bypass" is the panel's framing, not a term used by Dell'Oro or NVIDIA in the sources checked.

## AME — AMETEK (Borda #1)

**CONFIRMED**

Verbatim match on all four figures. Sales were a record $2.04B, up 15%, in the same release.

### 6. Shortage driver: CEO called it "beginning stages of a multiyear infrastructure build-out" (AI semi metrology, power/data-center, defence)
**CONFIRMED**
- Exact quote verified in Q2 2026 call coverage: TIKR's call write-up (2026-08) quotes Zapico — "I believe we're in the beginning stages of a multiyear infrastructure build-out, and we're incredibly well positioned" — and ties it to AI-driven semiconductor capacity, power grid buildouts, and defense modernization. Mitrade's transcript summary carries the same quote. The end-market linkage matches the claim (semiconductor metrology demand at Zygo was named as an orders driver in the same quarter's coverage).

### 7. Catalyst + date: $5.0B Indicor deal closed Aug 2026; next earnings date — dossier conflict 2026-10-29 vs 2026-11-03, resolved
**CONFIRMED — conflict resolved in favour of 2026-11-03**
- Indicor: Company PR via PR Newswire, BERWYN, Pa., Aug. 26, 2026 — "AMETEK Completes Acquisition of Indicor Instrumentation… in an all-cash transaction valued at $5.0 billion," expected to contribute ~$350M to 2026 sales, modestly accretive, joining EIG and EMG. Primary source, exact match.
- Next earnings: Company PR via PR Newswire dated 2026-10-15 — "AMETEK Announces Third Quarter 2026 Earnings Call… Earnings to be released before market opens on Tuesday, November 3, 2026," call 8:30 AM ET (prnewswire.com/news-releases/ametek-announces-third-quarter-2026-earnings-call-and-webcasted-investor-conference-call-information-302908794.html). The 2026-10-29 date came from MarketBeat's earlier earnings-calendar estimate (repeated in analyst-note roundups on 2026-10-09/13) and is superseded by the company's own 2026-10-15 announcement. **Use 2026-11-03; any dossier page carrying 2026-10-29 should be corrected.**

### 8. Moat: design-in/validation lock-in, irreplaceability 8/10
**UNVERIFIED**
- The qualitative basis is directionally supported, but only by secondary sources: AMETEK is repeatedly described as supplying highly engineered, application-specific, mission-critical products with "limited direct substitutes" and "strong customer switching costs" (FinanceCharts company profile), products "embedded deeply enough that switching costs are significant" and co-designed/customised with OEMs against their certification requirements (secondary industry write-ups). The Q2/Indicor company PRs support "mission-critical" positioning and recurring consumables/service/aftermarket revenue.
- No primary source (10-K, company PR, call transcript) found in this check quantifies design-in/validation lock-in, and the "8/10 irreplaceability" figure is the panel's own scoring rubric, not an externally sourced fact — it cannot be confirmed or contradicted externally. Verdict is UNVERIFIED for the claim as written (score + specific validation-lock-in mechanism), not a finding that the moat is absent.

### 10. Shortage driver / catalyst: Q3 2026 adjusted EPS $3.02 beat, reported 2026-10-21/22; margin savings >$100B/day in Q3

The claim's ">$100B/day" matches the call wording.

### 11. Moat: clearing/liquidity network cannot be replicated within ~3 years; exclusive S&P/Nasdaq licences to 2039 / Russell to 2037
**UNVERIFIED** (two of three licence dates confirmed; S&P date and the ~3-year replication horizon not established)
- Nasdaq to 2039 — CONFIRMED sub-part: CME/Nasdaq joint announcement, 2025-07-22/23 — "a ten-year extension of CME Group's exclusive license to offer futures and options on futures based on the Nasdaq-100 and other Nasdaq indexes, through 2039" (MondoVisione / Markets Media carrying the joint release).
- Russell to 2037 — CONFIRMED sub-part: CME/FTSE Russell joint announcement (LSEG release, 2025) — "a 12-year extension of CME Group's exclusive licence to offer futures, options on futures and OTC-cleared products based on FTSE Russell indexes," through 2037.
- S&P to 2039 — NOT FOUND: CME's exclusive S&P futures licence itself is documented (filed S&P licence agreement, Law Insider/Justia contract copies: exclusivity for futures/options on futures, with S&P 500 exclusivity extending to one year before the end of the agreement's Term — the Term end date is redacted/not stated in the public copies), and CME holds a minority stake in S&P Dow Jones Indices. No primary or reputable secondary source found states a 2039 end date for the *S&P* licence. The 2039 figure appears to be a conflation with the Nasdaq-100 extension. Caution: do not confuse with Cboe's separate S&P 500 *options* (SPX) licence, extended 2026-09-29 through 2051 — a different product and venue. Because no source shows a different S&P end date either, this is UNVERIFIED rather than CONTRADICTED.
- "~3 years to replicate" — NOT ESTABLISHED as a fact: it is an analytical horizon, not a sourced figure. Directional support exists: Duffy on the Q3 2026 call called the $100B-plus efficiencies "really difficult to replicate" and stressed the institutional liquidity ecosystem; secondary moat analyses note FMX Futures Exchange (launched Sep 2024, bank-backed) held negligible share a year later and that systemically-important clearing status takes years of regulatory approval. None of these sources states a ~3-year replication timeframe.
