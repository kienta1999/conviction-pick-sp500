## MSFT (Microsoft)

### 1. Commercial RPO \$677B at 30 Sep 2026, +73% YoY — CONFIRMED

Verified figure matches exactly. Microsoft's Q1 FY27 earnings release (28 Oct 2026) states: "Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 73% to \$677 billion." The release's constant-currency table shows the same 73% (0% currency impact). The Q1 FY27 earnings-call transcript (Microsoft IR, 28 Oct 2026) repeats: "Commercial remaining performance obligation grew 73% to \$677 billion, with a smaller sequential decline relative to historical seasonality due to the large Azure commitment this quarter."

Source: Microsoft, "Microsoft announces first quarter results," 28 Oct 2026 (news.microsoft.com / microsoft.com Investor Relations, FY27 Q1 press release).

### 2. Azure revenue +48% YoY in Q1 FY27 — CONFIRMED

Verified figure matches exactly. CFO Amy Hood on the Q1 FY27 call (28 Oct 2026): "Azure revenue was ahead of expectations and increased 48%, even with the 3P capacity delays noted earlier, driven by Foundry and improved CPU monetization." Azure revenue was \$33.2B in the quarter (first quarter Microsoft disclosed Azure revenue in dollars); Microsoft Cloud revenue was \$63.9B, +30%.

Source: Microsoft FY27 Q1 earnings conference call transcript, 28 Oct 2026 (microsoft.com Investor Relations); corroborated by Zacks/GeekWire coverage of the same release, 28–29 Oct 2026.

### 3. Hood said customer demand continues to exceed available supply / capacity is the binding constraint — CONFIRMED

Verified wording is essentially verbatim. In the outlook section of the Q1 FY27 call (28 Oct 2026), Hood said: "Customer demand continues to exceed available supply." Multiple independent summaries of the call (Zacks, 4 Nov 2026; TradingView/MarketBeat call summary) quote the same sentence and note delayed third-party GPU capacity deliveries shifting into the second half of FY27 as the operational constraint. Hood also guided Q2 FY27 capex above \$50B and calendar-year capex of ~\$170B, consistent with capacity — not demand — being the binding constraint.

Source: Microsoft FY27 Q1 earnings call transcript, 28 Oct 2026 (via Motley Fool transcript and Microsoft IR); Zacks, "MSFT Q1 Earnings Call Highlights Azure Growth and AI Spending," 4 Nov 2026.

### 4. ~45% of Microsoft's commercial RPO at Dec 2025 was OpenAI (CFO disclosure, Jan 2026) — CONFIRMED

Verified against the primary transcript. On the FY26 Q2 earnings call (28 Jan 2026), CFO Amy Hood said: "Commercial remaining performance obligation ... increased to \$625 billion and was up 110% year-over-year ... Approximately 45% of our commercial RPO balance is from OpenAI. The significant remaining balance grew 28%." The quarter in question ended 31 Dec 2025, so "at Dec 2025" is correct. Note: Microsoft has not updated this share since — the Q1 FY27 release and call give no OpenAI share of the \$677B, so the 45% figure must not be applied to the current RPO balance.

Source: Microsoft FY26 Q2 earnings conference call transcript, 28 Jan 2026 (microsoft.com Investor Relations).

## MSI (Motorola Solutions)

### 5. Record ending backlog \$16.6B at Q3 2026, +14% YoY and +\$1B sequentially — CONFIRMED

Verified figure matches exactly. Motorola's Q3 2026 press release (4 Nov 2026) headlines: "Record ending backlog of \$16.6 billion, up 14% versus a year ago, driven by record Q3 orders." The release body adds: "The company ended the quarter with record backlog of \$16.6 billion, up 14% or \$2.1 billion from the year-ago quarter." The earnings-call transcript (4 Nov 2026) confirms the sequential leg: "Q3 ending backlog was an all-time record of \$16.6 billion, up \$2.1 billion, or 14% versus last year, and up \$1 billion sequentially, driven by record Q3 orders in all three technologies."

Source: Motorola Solutions, "Motorola Solutions Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results," 4 Nov 2026 (motorolasolutions.com / SEC 8-K exhibit); Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 4 Nov 2026.

### 6. Q3 2026 orders rose more than 20% YoY across all three technologies — CONFIRMED (wording nuance)

The >20% figure is confirmed for total orders; the "across all three technologies" phrasing slightly overstates what management said. On the Q3 2026 call (4 Nov 2026), CEO Greg Brown said: "Orders grew over 20% versus last year with broad-based growth in both segments." CFO Jason Winkler later specified total orders growth of 25%. Separately, management said the backlog increase was "driven by record Q3 orders in all three technologies" — i.e. record orders in each technology, not a >20% growth rate stated for each technology individually. No per-technology order growth rate was disclosed.

Note: the substance (orders up sharply, broad-based, record in all three technologies) is confirmed; the claim should be read as "total orders +>20% YoY, with record orders in all three technologies" rather than ">20% in each technology."

Source: Motorola Solutions Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 4 Nov 2026 (motorolasolutions.com; corroborated by StockAnalysis and TradingView/MarketBeat call summaries).

### 7. Motorola controls more than 80% of the US land-mobile-radio market for public safety — CONFIRMED (analyst estimate, not a company disclosure)

Morningstar's analyst research on Motorola Solutions states verbatim: "Motorola Solutions is the major supplier of land mobile radio, or LMR, technology for the public safety space, controlling more than 80% of the U.S. market." That is the source of the figure, and it matches the claim as stated.

Note: this is an analyst estimate, not a figure Motorola discloses in its filings (its 10-K describes itself only as "a global leader in the two-way radio category"). The copy retrieved carries no publication date (Morningstar AUM cited as of 30 Jun 2016 suggests an older note), and other secondary estimates are lower — e.g. one 2026 overview puts Motorola at "60%+ of the North American LMR market," and a 2017 Citron report claimed ~85% of US first-responder radio. Direction (dominant, entrenched share) is corroborated by multiple sources, but the precise ">80%" should be treated as Morningstar's estimate, not an audited current fact.

Source: Morningstar, "Motorola Solutions Remains the Public Safety Leader" (via ResearchPool); corroborating direction from company 10-K (FY2024, filed 2025) and third-party overviews.

## WST (West Pharmaceutical Services)

### 8. HVP Components grew 20.3% organically to \$466.7m in Q3 2026, 53% of total sales — CONFIRMED

Verified figure matches exactly. West's Q3 2026 press release (29 Oct 2026): "High-Value Product ('HVP') Components net sales of \$466.7 million increased 19.7% and rose 20.3% on an organic basis. HVP Components accounted for 53% of total company net sales in the quarter." Total net sales were \$874.5m (+8.7% reported, +11.9% organic).

Source: West Pharmaceutical Services, "West Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results," 29 Oct 2026 (PR Newswire / Nasdaq; also filed as Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1, 29 Oct 2026).

### 9. GLP-1-related HVP revenue grew "well into the 30% range" organically in Q3 2026 — CONFIRMED

Verified wording is essentially verbatim. On the Q3 2026 earnings call (29 Oct 2026), management said: "Our GLP-1 HVP business also performed very well, accelerating nicely in the quarter, growing well into the 30s organically. HVP GLP-1s represented just under 12% of total revenues in the quarter." (Transcripts render "30s" as "[30s]"; secondary summaries phrase it as "well into the 30% range.")

Source: West Pharmaceutical Services Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 29 Oct 2026 (Motley Fool / AlphaStreet / StockAnalysis).

### 10. New HVP finishing capacity takes ~18–24 months to contribute meaningfully, with finishing capacity planned for late 2028 — CONFIRMED

Both halves confirmed, from two separate management remarks. On the Q3 2026 earnings call (29 Oct 2026), CEO Michel Lagarde said of the new HVP finishing-capacity investments in Europe and the U.S.: "If we start on these projects now, the right assumption is probably 18 to 24 months for those to come online and deliver a meaningful contribution to the growth of the company." Separately, at the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference in London (18 Nov 2026), management said molding capacity is sufficient through 2030 while new finishing capacity is expected to come online in late 2028.

Note: the two halves come from different events (Q3 call vs. Jefferies conference three weeks later) — the claim correctly combines them, but the late-2028 date was not stated on the Q3 call itself.

Source: West Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 29 Oct 2026 (FolioObs / MarketBeat call summary); Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference presentation summary, 18 Nov 2026 (Newsfilter / AlphaI transcript summary).
