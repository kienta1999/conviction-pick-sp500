### Top 3 in detail

**1. LLY:** The single most compelling data point is the demand depth behind the volume: only about 1 in 10 eligible US patients and 2 in 100 internationally are on an incretin, yet Lilly already did \$24.4 billion in a quarter with Mounjaro and Zepbound at over \$16 billion combined. That is a multi-year volume runway with a 62.8% US branded GLP-1 prescription share and a retatrutide pipeline that could reset efficacy expectations. Rough 12–18 month scenario: base +15% to +25%, bull +35% to +50%.

**2. MSFT:** The single most compelling data point is the \$677 billion commercial RPO, up 73% year over year, behind Azure growth accelerating to 48% year over year with management guiding 49–50% constant-currency growth next quarter — demand literally exceeds the capacity Microsoft can build, at about 1 GW added per quarter. Copilot passing 30 million paid seats from about 20 million three months earlier adds a second accelerating leg. Rough 12–18 month scenario: base +20% to +30%, in line with the +31.3% analyst upside, bull +40% to +50% if Azure sustains 48–50% growth and the RPO converts without an OpenAI stumble.

**3. WST:** The single most compelling data point is the capacity lag: HVP Components grew 20.3% organically, GLP-1-related HVP revenue grew well into the 30% range organically, and 200+ HVP upgrade projects were added in 2026, while new HVP finishing capacity takes about 18–24 months to contribute meaningfully. That is a picks-and-shovels toll on every injectable biologic, with regulatory qualification lock-in that makes switching suppliers rare. Rough 12–18 month scenario: base +10% to +20%, bull +25% to +40% if HVP growth stays above 20% and the 12-month run does not simply consolidate.

Left out of top 10: VRTX, PM, EMR — VRTX revenue grew only 12% in a capped CF population and Q3 EPS missed consensus despite the revenue beat; PM had the slowest revenue growth in the field at 6.9% TTM and ZYN US shipment growth decelerated to 4.2%; EMR had the slowest TTM revenue growth at 4.5% with only about 5% FY27 sales growth guided despite the order surge.
