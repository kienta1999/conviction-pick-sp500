# Panelist D — Contrarian / Risk Skeptic Ballot — Momentum, 2026-10-19

Lens note: I rank on what is real but still mispriced, not on shortage score alone. Peak-cycle earnings, crowded multiples, circular AI financing, and names a customer or a rival platform can route around get penalised even when their shortage is genuine.

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** SNDK
- **Runner-up:** FCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The market is still pricing SanDisk as an old, unprotected NAND commodity cycle — it fell -17.86% in the week to 2026-10-16, to $1299.30, more than 35% below its $2354.39 52-week high, partly on a CXMT-driven memory sell-off that the dossier notes does not even touch NAND (CXMT makes DRAM, not NAND). That pricing ignores the New Business Model: roughly $93.9bn of minimum future revenue at floor pricing, which converts a spot-price windfall (Q4 FY2026 non-GAAP gross margin 84.6%) into contracted, downside-floored revenue covering >50% of FY2027 bits and ~2/3 of FY2028 bits. At a 4.9x forward P/E against a $2173 analyst target (+67.2%), the explosive case is simply that scarcity lasts into 2027 while the floor holds if it does not. The 2026-10-29 print, against consensus of ~$46-plus EPS on ~$10.7bn revenue, is the near-term proof point.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Management says NAND bits will "remain on allocation beyond calendar year 2027," all of SanDisk's 2026 manufacturing capacity is pre-sold, and TrendForce still projects Q4 2026 NAND contract prices +15% to +20% q/q — scarcity is intensifying, not rolling over, at the moment the stock de-rated.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — nobody can in-source NAND (a modern fab takes 3–5 years and >$20bn, and hyperscalers are not building memory), but NAND is fungible: Samsung, SK hynix/Solidigm, Micron and Kioxia can all take share on price when supply loosens, and China's YMTC (~13% bit share in Q1 2026, third Wuhan fab due end-2026) is the real long-term bypass. Irreplaceability 6/10 per the dossier; the NBM contracts, not the product, are the protection.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +35% to +45% (pricing holds through 2027, NBM floors validate, partial re-rating toward consensus); bull +65% to +75% (undersupply persists past H2 2027 and the $2173 target zone is reached). Downside is real if NAND prices break early — the floor-pricing structure is what keeps this from being a pure peak-cycle gamble.
- **Top risk:** This is still peak pricing in a commodity — TrendForce sees supply balance restoring from H2 2027, rivals can reallocate wafers back to NAND, and a 4.9x forward P/E is only cheap if peak earnings hold; an in-line-to-soft 2026-10-29 print after a $46-plus consensus could de-rate it further, fast.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

### Top 3 — detail

**1. SNDK.** The single most compelling point is the combination of allocation beyond calendar 2027 with ~$93.9bn of NBM minimum revenue at floor pricing, backed by $16.5bn of cash deposits and financial instruments — prior NAND cycles had no such contracted downside protection, and the market is valuing this one as if it does not exist. Base scenario +35% to +45% over 12–18 months on pricing persistence and re-rating; bull +65% to +75% if the 4–5% industry undersupply extends past H2 2027.

**2. FCX.** Copper is the textbook structural shortage: the IEA expects supply to fall ~30% short of 2035 requirements, FCX realised >$6.50/lb expected for Q3 2026 against ~$1.90/lb guided unit cash costs, and its own recovery (Grasberg at ~67% of normalised mill rates, H2 2026 sales guided >20% above H1 with a further >20% rise in 2027) plus the sub-$1/lb leach leg (200M lb/yr run-rate toward a 300M lb/yr target) give it volume growth that does not depend on a higher copper price. Base scenario +20% to +25% (price holds near records, Grasberg ramps on schedule, newer $85–$92 targets supplant the $79.06 consensus); bull +40% or more if copper stays above $6/lb into the 2027 volume step-up — with the explicit caveat that a copper price break cuts EBITDA roughly 35% between management's $7 and $5 frames.

**3. CSCO.** The underappreciated data point is orders running ahead of shipments: $9.3bn of FY2026 AI orders (~4.5x FY2025) with only ~$4.0bn converted to revenue, RPO of $46.7bn, and FY2027 AI revenue guided to $7.5bn (+87.5%) — yet the stock closed at $107.39, ~17.6% below its all-time high after five straight red days, at 19.1x forward with a $0.42 quarterly dividend payable 2026-10-21 supporting the downside. Base scenario +20% to +25% (AI guide converts, campus refresh sustains, re-rating toward the +26.8% analyst upside); bull +40% if hyperscaler Silicon One design wins make the "prudent" AI guide look sandbagged. The INVENTORY_BUILD flag (+80% inventory, +126% commitments) is the tripwire, not yet the thesis break — it is benign only while orders keep outrunning shipments.

Left out of my top 10: LLY, ANET, WST — deliberately excluded because each is priced for the good outcome already: LLY at 26.6x with only +5.7% analyst upside and a shortage the FDA declared resolved in December 2024; ANET at 33.9x with the panel's weakest irreplaceability (5/10) against Nvidia bundling the network into the GPU sale; WST at 38.4x with only +7.5% upside and an oral-GLP-1 format shift that routes around its filing-locked moat entirely.
