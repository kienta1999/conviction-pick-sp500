- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** GOOGL
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU is the fastest-accelerating name in the field: record quarterly revenue up 21% sequentially to $13.64B, gross margin ~57%, and guided non-GAAP EPS +440% YoY — a revision cycle, not a single beat. This is what the fastest horse that can keep running looks like.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** HBM3E/HBM4 supply for 2026 fully sold out and booked into 2027, meeting only ~50–66% of core-customer HBM demand (TrendForce via Needham, Aug 2026).

The commodity tail remains cyclical, but the HBM franchise is a real moat. 7/10.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+30–35% (HBM pricing holds, 2027 capacity absorbed, multiple holds on delivered earnings); bull ~+60–75% (DRAM undersupply deepens per Goldman's 5.9% 2027 projection, HBM pricing power extends into 2028).

- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
