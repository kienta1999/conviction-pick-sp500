# Ballot C — quality/moat & irreplaceability investor (momentum panel, 2026-09-23)

Lens: category dominance, margins, returns on capital, balance-sheet durability, and above all irreplaceability. Rejected any name whose customers (especially hyperscalers) could realistically build or in-source the product, or that a substitute technology could route around. Judged only on the dossier; no new research.

## Ballot 1 — single pick

- **Top pick:** NVDA
- **Runner-up:** MSFT
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The shortage is the most structural in the field — BofA's GPU supply-demand gap does not close until 2029, TSMC CoWoS is fully subscribed through mid-2027, and HBM is sold out through 2026 — and it sits behind the moat hyperscalers cannot copy: CUDA plus the full-stack networking and software platform, with ~90%+ of the AI accelerator market. The in-sourcing threat is real (Google TPU, Meta MTIA, Amazon Trainium) but remains narrow and specialized, not general-purpose; no customer can replicate the CUDA stack. $119B in manufacturing, supply, and capacity commitments ($95B due within FY2027) turns the shortage into contracted visibility. The explosiveness comes from years of runway plus pricing power — B200 resale value at 158% of launch.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** $119B in manufacturing/supply/capacity commitments, $95B due within FY2027 (fiscal Q1 2027 filing) — contracted, not forecasted.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — hyperscaler custom ASICs are the genuine in-sourcing threat, but they remain narrow and specialized while CUDA + full-stack networking + software is a general-purpose moat with ~90%+ share; low risk of near-term displacement.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** rough base ~+30–35% (shortage persists through 2027, multiple holds near current levels on delivered earnings); bull ~+60%+ (Vera Rubin cycle lands, pricing power extends, supply-demand gap confirmed out to 2029).
- **Top risk:** the AI-bubble question — a hyperscaler capex pause breaks everything at once, and NVDA is the most consensus long in the market, so the crowdedness is the drawdown accelerant.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

**Top 3 deep dives:**

**NVDA** — the single most compelling data point is the $119B in manufacturing/supply/capacity commitments ($95B due within FY2027): the shortage is contracted revenue visibility, not a demand forecast. The moat is CUDA plus full-stack networking and software at ~90%+ AI accelerator share — no hyperscaler can in-source a general-purpose CUDA stack. Rough 12–18mo: base ~+30–35% on the shortage persisting through 2027; bull ~+60%+ if the Vera Rubin cycle lands and the supply-demand gap is confirmed out to 2029.

**MSFT** — the single most compelling data point is the $678B commercial RPO (+84% yoy), with ~30% recognized in the next 12 months: a floor, not a forecast. Azure +43% and accelerating, and the enterprise entrenchment (M365/Windows/Azure as the corporate standard) means the threat is workload migration to a rival cloud, never in-sourcing — the lowest in-sourcing risk in the field. Rough 12–18mo: base ~+25–30% on the RPO converting; bull ~+50% if Azure holds ~45% growth and Copilot monetization inflects.

**GOOGL** — the single most compelling data point is the $514B Google Cloud backlog (up $50B+ sequentially) alongside Cloud revenue accelerating to +82% yoy, with management's own words: "demand is not the problem. Capacity is." The moat is the full AI stack — proprietary TPUs, Gemini models, private fiber, distribution through Search/Android/Workspace — and the Q2 print showed AI expanding Search queries, not cannibalizing them. Rough 12–18mo: base ~+25–30% (operational EPS ~$2.87 ex the SpaceX/Anthropic marks, Cloud margins compounding); bull ~+50%+ if the capex build converts the backlog without margin damage.

**Deliberately left out of the top 10 and why:** ANET (hyperscaler in-sourcing risk — ~48% of revenue from cloud/AI titans who can buy the Nvidia bundle or roll their own; the Spectrum-X shadow); SNDK (NAND is a commodity hyperscalers multi-source — a pure shortage with no process moat); HOOD (no switching costs; Schwab Crypto is a direct competitive response); EXPE (Google AI travel features could route around OTAs); FANG (commodity barrel; OPEC is the bypass); NEM (gold is fungible — cost position, not a moat); FCX (the "irreplaceable" Grasberg asset sits under a pending Indonesian permit extension with the government as majority owner — political risk, not a moat); UNP (moat on the rails, but GDP-beta freight with a 4/10 shortage thesis — not a momentum name); SCHW (thinner moat than MS/JPM, engagement-cycle leverage); AME (diversified qualification lock-in, but no single unassailable moat).
