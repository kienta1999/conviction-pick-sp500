## MU (Micron Technology)

**Claim 1: HBM3E/HBM4 supply for 2026 fully sold out, booked into 2027, meeting only ~50–66% of core-customer HBM demand into 2027.**
**Verdict: CONFIRMED.** CEO Sanjay Mehrotra on the Q3 fiscal 2026 call (~Aug 2026) said HBM3E and HBM4 are "fully booked through calendar 2027, with demand extending into 2028" (ainvest, 2026-08-17); the February 2026 100%-sold-out confirmation for 2026 capacity stands (r40.io, 2026-02-11); "entire HBM production capacity for calendar 2026 is locked in through binding contracts, with the order book reportedly extending into 2027" (newscase.com); the ~50–66% core-customer demand coverage into 2027 is a TrendForce figure as cited in secondary sources.

**Claim 2: Record quarter revenue $13.64B (+21% QoQ) with gross margin ~57%, guided non-GAAP EPS up ~440% YoY — attributed to "Sept 2026 earnings".**
**Verdict: CONTRADICTED on attribution.** Every figure matches the **FQ1-26 print reported 2026-12-17** exactly: revenue $13.643B (vs $11.315B prior quarter = +20.6% QoQ; Micron's release called it record revenue), gross margin 56.0% GAAP / 56.8% non-GAAP, and guided FQ2-26 non-GAAP EPS $8.42 vs FQ2-25's $1.56 = +440% (Micron official press release via micron.gcs-web.com, 2026-12-17; corroborated by Quartr exhibit and SiliconANGLE). The September 2026 earnings would be FQ4-26 — a much larger quarter (FQ2-26 was $23.86B; FQ3-26 guided $33.5B). The dossier judged MU on a 9-month-old quarter mislabeled as the latest print. Note this understates the actual latest financials but does not weaken the shortage thesis.
