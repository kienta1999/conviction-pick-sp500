## MU — Micron Technology (tentative winner)

**1. ~$100B minimum contracted revenue across 16 SCAs, ~$22B upfront deposits — CONFIRMED.**
Micron's FQ3 2026 results (reported June 24, 2026): 16 Strategic Customer Agreements, mostly 5-year take-or-pay terms 2026–2030. Fourteen of the 16 carry cumulative minimum revenue commitments of ~$100B (not all 16 — the other two do not). Micron expects ~$22B in customer deposits and financial commitments (~$18B cash + ~$4B letters of credit).
Sources: ainvest.com "Can Micron's Take-or-Pay Deals Improve Revenue Growth Visibility?" (Aug 2026); timesng.com (Sept 23, 2026).

**2. All 2026 HBM sold out; only half to two-thirds of demand fillable; no meaningful new fab output until FY2028 — CONFIRMED.**
CEO Sanjay Mehrotra on the FQ3 2026 earnings call (June 24, 2026): Micron can fulfill "only between half and two-thirds" of customer HBM demand, with entire 2026 HBM supply sold out under multi-year contracts; he told analysts "we currently do not have line of sight as to when memory supply will be able to catch up with increasing demand."
Sources: aiweekly.co (Micron Q3 2026 alert); timesng.com (Sept 23, 2026).
Partial nuance: the "no meaningful new fab output until FY2028" timing is corroborated by industry coverage — Boise Idaho 1 produces meaningful volume H2/mid-2027, the Taiwan (Tongluo) fab adds no wafer output until H2 2027, Singapore NAND not until H2 2028 — i.e. essentially no new Micron output in calendar 2026; first meaningful DRAM relief is mid-2027, not FY2028.
Source: tradingnews.com Micron deep-dive (Feb 2026).

**3. FQ4 FY2026 earnings Sept 30, 2026, guided ~$50B revenue, ~86% gross margin — CONFIRMED.**
Preview coverage on Sept 24, 2026 ("Micron Reports Wednesday") confirms earnings due Wednesday Sept 30, 2026, with consensus at $31.35 EPS on ~$50.97B revenue — the top end of the company's guide of $50B revenue, ~86% gross margin, EPS $30–32.
Sources: stockmoguls.com (Sept 24, 2026); newscase.com (2026-07).

**4. Only ~3 firms make leading-edge DRAM/HBM at scale — CONFIRMED.**
Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron are the only scaled leading-edge DRAM/HBM producers (the dossier's own phrasing is the industry consensus; multiple sources describe "three companies control global HBM supply" — e.g., tradingnews.com Feb 2026; aiweekly.co notes Micron ranks third in HBM behind SK Hynix and Samsung).

**Verdict: all four MU claims confirmed; no contradictions. Two phrasing nuances only (14-of-16 agreements carry the $100B; meaningful new fab relief is mid-2027/H2-2028).**

## GOOGL — Alphabet (tentative #2)

**1. Cloud backlog $514B, +$50B in one quarter (~4x Cloud run-rate), ~50% converting within 24 months, "compute constrained" — CONFIRMED (one attribution nuance).**
CFO Anat Ashkenazi on the Q2 2026 earnings call (July 22, 2026): Cloud backlog rose by more than $50B sequentially to $514B (from $460B in Q1), with just over 50% expected to convert to revenue over the next 24 months. Cloud revenue $24.8B (+82% YoY). On run-rate: $24.8B/quarter ≈ $99B annualized, so $514B is actually ~5.2x, not ~4x — the dossier understates the multiple slightly.
Nuance: the exact Q2 2026 quote from Pichai was "We continue to be **supply constrained**, a sign of momentum and rapid adoption" — "compute constrained in the near-term" was his Q1 2026 phrasing (per TechCrunch, April 29, 2026). Substance identical; the call attribution is one quarter off.
Sources: zacks.com (July 23, 2026); marketbeat.com earnings-call highlights; inferentialinvestor.com transcript notes.

**2. 2026 capex guidance $195–205B — CONFIRMED.**
Raised from $180–190B on the Q2 2026 call (July 22, 2026); quarterly capex hit a record $44.9B in Q2 and FCF went negative (-$5.9B) for the first time in ~20 years.
Sources: zacks.com (July 23, 2026); morningstar.com / MarketWatch (July 23, 2026); stocktwits.com (July 23, 2026).

**3. Q2 2026 GAAP EPS $9.11 included ~$99B non-cash gains on Anthropic/SpaceX stakes; core operating EPS ~$2.85 — CONFIRMED.**
StockTwits (July 23, 2026): "surprise $99 billion in equity securities gains" drove EPS to $9.11 (consensus $2.91). Motley Fool (Sept 24, 2026): other income showed a $98B net gain, "mainly unrealized gains on equity securities" — SpaceX and Anthropic marks. Morningstar (July 23, 2026): gains were "primarily the result of net unrealized gains on our equity securities." r40.io analysis: ~$6.26 of the $9.11 was non-cash mark-to-market (mostly the SpaceX-linked stake), putting core operating EPS at **$2.85–2.90**, inside the $2.86–2.89 consensus — i.e. the operating business merely met expectations while the headline quadrupled.
Source: r40.io (July 2026).

**Verdict: all three GOOGL claims confirmed. Two nuances: backlog is ~5x (not ~4x) the Cloud run-rate; "compute constrained" was Q1 wording, Q2 said "supply constrained."**

## NVDA — NVIDIA (tentative #3)

**1. CFO Colette Kress confirmed ~$500B Blackwell + Rubin visibility through FY2027 — CONFIRMED (framing has since moved higher).**
Timeline: Q3 FY2026 call (Nov 2025) — ~$500B visibility from start of 2025 through end of 2026. Feb 2026 (Q4 FY2026) call — Kress said sales would **exceed** the $500B forecast, with shipments into 2027 (Yonhap Infomax, Feb 26, 2026). Q1 FY2027 call — Kress expressed "full confidence in the **$1 trillion** in Blackwell and Rubin revenue we foresee from 2025 through calendar 2027." So the dossier's "$500B through FY2027" is consistent with Kress's stated figures but conservative — the latest company framing is $1T over 2025–2027.
Sources: alphastreet.com NVDA Q4 2025 transcript; usetranscribe.io Q1 FY2027 call transcript; ainvest.com Q3 2026 call summary.

**2. FQ2 FY2027 revenue $96.2B (+106% YoY); "cloud GPUs completely sold out"; FQ3 FY2027 guide ~$108B excluding China — CONFIRMED with one misattribution.**
- Revenue: NVIDIA press release, Aug 26, 2026 — Q2 FY2027 (ended July 26, 2026): $96.221B, +106% YoY; data center $89.0B (+117%); GAAP/non-GAAP EPS $2.46/$2.22.
- "Cloud GPUs sold out": the exact Huang quote — "Blackwell sales are off the charts, and cloud GPUs are sold out" — is from the **Q3 FY2026** earnings call (Nov 19, 2025), not the FQ2 FY2027 report; the dossier's "completely … for the foreseeable future" wording was not found verbatim. Substance still confirmed for the current period: Kress on the Q2 FY2027 call said the supply chain is "broadly constrained" and supply supports ~70% growth while demand exceeds it.
- FQ3 FY2027 outlook: $108.0B ±2%, "not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China" — confirmed verbatim in the company outlook.
Sources: nvidianews.nvidia.com press release (Aug 26, 2026); quartr.com earnings docs; barchart.com (Nov 2025); zolmax.com Q2 call highlights (Aug 2026).

**3. Vera Rubin in full production — CONFIRMED.**
Huang in the Aug 26, 2026 press release: "Vera Rubin, now in full production, was built to power exactly this moment." (The dossier's "entered full production March 2026" was not independently verified; "full production" is confirmed as of the August report.)
Source: nvidianews.nvidia.com (Aug 26, 2026).

**4. Huang's Sept 2026 Scotland comment that "chip sales could double next year" — CONFIRMED.**
On September 17, 2026, at the AI summit hosted by King Charles III at Dumfries House, Scotland, Huang told reporters (per CNBC): "I expect Nvidia to sell twice as many chips next year as this year." Important nuance: this is a **unit-volume** statement (chips sold), not revenue — the formal guidance is 70% revenue growth in FY2028 (the year ending January 2028, ~$673B on market estimates).
Sources: thestreet.com (Sept 22, 2026); en.sedaily.com (Sept 18, 2026); fool.com (Sept 20, 2026).

**Verdict: all four NVDA claims confirmed in substance. Nuances: (a) the $500B figure has since been superseded by Kress's $1T 2025–2027 framing; (b) "cloud GPUs are sold out" is Huang's Nov-2025 quote, not FQ2 FY2027; (c) the doubling pledge is unit volume, not revenue.**
