The scenario breaks if new supply arrives early-2027 — so this is a 12-18mo window, not a decade hold.
- **Top risk:** Extreme memory cyclicality — the stock looks cheapest at the peak (Seeking Alpha: 7.5x FY2028 peak-cycle EPS $232.88), +700% YTD with 3.45 beta, and a new NAND supply wave is expected from early 2027; if pricing decelerates faster than contracts cover, the de-rating is violent.

### Top 3 — moat/backlog detail + return scenarios

**SNDK (rank 1).** The moat data point is the NBM contract stack: 8 contracts potentially covering ~65% of FY2028 NAND output with a framework of mid-to-high-teens revenue growth at ~80% non-GAAP gross margin and ~75% operating margin. In a commodity industry, pre-selling two-thirds of output two years forward at software margins is structural — it converts the cycle from spot speculation into contracted compounding. Base 12–18mo: ~$2,400–2,800 (+30–50%) as super-cycle earnings land; bull ~$3,000 (~+60%) on clean contract execution (Bernstein high).

**MU (rank 2).** The moat data point: ~$100B of minimum contracted revenue across 16 strategic customer agreements with ~$22B already collected in upfront customer deposits — plus an oligopoly of exactly 3 scaled DRAM producers, so no customer can in-source and no fab can appear in under 2 years. HBM4 is ramping ~2x as fast as HBM3E, and the FQ4 print on Sept 30, 2026 is the immediate revision catalyst. Base 12–18mo: ~$1,400–1,600 (+30–50%, in line with UBS/Cantor PTs); bull ~$1,800+ (~+65%) if HBM shortage extends to 2028 and DRAM pricing holds.

**NVDA (rank 3).** The moat data point: ~$500B of confirmed Blackwell + Rubin revenue visibility (CFO Colette Kress), ~90% AI accelerator share, and the Vera Rubin cycle doubling TAM-per-gigawatt to ~$40B — Jensen's Sept 2026 comment that chip sales could double next year is the revision engine. The momentum-trap honest note: this is the one name whose largest customers (61% of revenue from the top 4) are genuinely building alternatives — Google TPU v7, AWS Trainium 3, Meta MTIA, Microsoft Maia — so the lens's priority (acceleration: revenue +106% YoY, data-center +117%, consensus ~91% FY27 growth) is what keeps it #3 despite the medium disintermediation risk. Base 12–18mo: ~$300–350 (+35–55%, Bernstein/Rosenblatt zone); bull ~$450–515 (~+100%, Raymond James $515) on a Rubin upside surprise.
