## MU (Micron) — tentative winner

### 1. ~$150B RPO under 26 take-or-pay SCAs; ~$32B customer financial commitments (mostly cash deposits) — FQ4 2026, 2026-09-30
**Verdict: CONFIRMED-WITH-CORRECTION**

Numbers are as disclosed on the 2026-09-30 FQ4 2026 call / prepared remarks:
26 multi-year take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements (up from 16 in June),
estimated >35% of revenue through 2030; customer financial commitments rose
to $32B (from $22B in June), "mostly / the vast majority" in cash deposits;
remaining performance obligations ~$150B.

Correction (definition matters): the ~$150B is **not** the RPO of all 26 SCAs.
It covers only the subset of agreements with a **determined / defined pricing
framework** — about three-quarters of expected SCA revenue, mostly
floor-and-ceiling pricing (the rest is repriced periodically at market) — and
is calculated at **committed volumes and minimum prices**, which CFO Mark
Murphy called "inherently conservative." Also distinguish commitments from
cash in hand: deposits actually received / held at period-end were ~$12.7B
(noncurrent customer contract liabilities $12.895B at 2026-09-03 on Micron's
own balance sheet), not $32B; the $32B is the committed total.

### 3. >75% of calendar-2027 output (or HBM output) committed; only Samsung, SK hynix and Micron make leading-edge HBM at scale
**Verdict: CONFIRMED-WITH-CORRECTION**

Correction (two different figures are conflated in the claim):
(a) ">75%" refers to Micron's **total 2027 output** — "more than 75% of the
company's 2027 output was already committed across SCA and non-SCA
customers" (Mehrotra, Q&A, 2026-09-30) — not to HBM specifically, and

said it has "completed agreements covering the **vast majority** of its
calendar 2027 HBM bit supply" at prices significantly higher than 2026.
Do not cite ">75% of HBM" — no source states that number for HBM.
The three-supplier part is confirmed in substance: multiple 2026 industry
sources state only three companies make HBM at scale — SK hynix, Samsung
and Micron.

### 6. "$500B Blackwell+Rubin" = cumulative 2025–26 revenue visibility from GTC Oct 2025, NOT a current backlog; formal RPO only ~$3.2B (Q2 FY27 10-Q)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED-WITH-CORRECTION**

First half confirmed: at GTC Washington, 2025-10-28, Jensen Huang cited
>$500B of cumulative Blackwell + Rubin revenue visibility through CY26.
NVIDIA's finance team clarified the next day that Huang had misspoken if
read as "next five quarters" alone: $500B is **cumulative Blackwell + Rubin
shipments over 2025–2026 (through end-CY26 / ~FY27), including related
networking (InfiniBand / NVLink)**, with ~30% already shipped (~$100B+ of
Blackwell revenue by then); the implied next-five-quarters remainder was
~$307B. It is a cumulative revenue-visibility figure, not an order backlog
or RPO, and it is a Oct-2025 disclosure, not a current (Oct-2026) book.

Correction on the second half (scope): the 10-Q figure is exact but narrow.
As of 2026-07-26: "revenue related to remaining performance obligations
**from contracts greater than one year in length** was $3.2 billion, which
included $3.0 billion from deferred revenue and $244 million that has not
yet been billed or recognized as revenue" (~39% to be recognised in the
next 12 months). So $3.2B is the formal RPO **for >1-year contracts only** —
it is correct that it is nowhere near $500B, but it should not be presented
as NVIDIA's total backlog / total demand book either; the two numbers are
different perimeters, which is precisely why neither can be substituted
for the other.
