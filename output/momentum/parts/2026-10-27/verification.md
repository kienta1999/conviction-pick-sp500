## MU — Micron Technology

### 1. ~$150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs, ~$32B customer commitments — CONFIRMED, with the definition caveat exactly as suspected

- Micron fiscal Q4 2026 disclosure, 2026-09-30: 26 Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs), up from 16 in Q3, all with take-or-pay volume commitments, estimated at >35% of revenue through 2030, some extending into 2031.
- Customer financial commitments tied to the SCAs rose to **$32B**, "the vast majority" in cash deposits ($12.3B received in FQ4 alone, booked as financing cash flow, not revenue).
- Remaining performance obligations **~$150B**.
- **Caveat — CONFIRMED:** The $150B is *not* all 26 SCAs at expected value. It is only the subset with a **defined / determined pricing framework** (roughly three-quarters of expected SCA revenue; fixed price or floor-and-ceiling bands), calculated on **committed volumes × minimum pricing**. CFO Mark Murphy called it "inherently conservative"; management expects actual revenue to exceed it. The remaining ~quarter of SCA revenue is periodically repriced at market. Do not present $150B as total SCA backlog or as expected revenue.
- Sources: Micron FQ4 2026 earnings call prepared remarks / highlights, 2026-09-30 (MarketBeat transcript summary; Carbon Finance earnings review, Oct 2026); AI Stock Wire, 2026-09-30/10-01, quoting Murphy/Mehrotra directly on the pricing-framework / minimum-pricing definition.

### 2. Vast majority of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply contracted; 2026 HBM sold out; only ~3 firms make leading-edge HBM at scale — CONFIRMED

- FQ4 2026 call (2026-09-30): Micron said it has "completed agreements for the vast majority of its calendar 2027 HBM bit supply" at prices well above / substantially higher than 2026. Separately, >75% of total fiscal/calendar 2027 output is already committed across SCA + non-SCA customers — do not conflate the two denominators.
- 2026 HBM: entire 2026 HBM supply/capacity sold out under contracts (repeated management statements through 2026; FQ4 reporting: "HBM capacity is sold out through 2026", HBM3E/HBM4 sold out through calendar 2026, with 2027 largely locked and talks moving to 2028).
- Only three at scale: SK hynix, Samsung, and Micron are the only companies performing leading-edge HBM manufacture/packaging at commercial scale for AI accelerators (TechTimes, 2026-10-16, on Micron's Taichung AATT2 opening; Counterpoint Research share cited Oct 2026: SK hynix ~50%, Samsung ~33%, Micron ~18% in Q2 2026). No qualified fourth volume supplier was found; CXMT has no qualified HBM product in volume.
- Sources: Micron FQ4 2026 prepared remarks as reported 2026-09-30 (Mindcron / TechTimes summaries of the call); TechTimes, 2026-10-16; Counterpoint via AI Stock Wire, Oct 2026.

## NVDA — Nvidia

### 7. Q2 FY27 revenue $96.2B (+106% YoY), Q3 guided ~$108B excl. China; $500B Blackwell+Rubin framing; formal RPO ~$3.2B — CONFIRMED-WITH-MATERIAL-CORRECTION

- Revenue / guidance — **CONFIRMED (primary source):** Nvidia official release for Q2 FY27 (quarter ended 2026-07-26): revenue **$96,221M, up 18% Q/Q and up 106% Y/Y**; Data Center $89.0B (+117% Y/Y); GAAP/non-GAAP gross margin 75.0%. Outlook: Q3 FY27 revenue **$108.0B ± 2%**, with the explicit statement "NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook."
- $500B framing — **CORRECTION: it is visibility / cumulative business, NOT backlog and NOT RPO.** Jensen Huang at GTC Washington, late Oct 2025, described ~$500B of Blackwell + Rubin business *for 2025–2026* (cumulative, over ~5–6 quarters). Nvidia's finance team subsequently clarified: ~30% related to Blackwell already shipped/recognized, and the figure also includes ancillary networking (NVLink/InfiniBand etc.); press/analyst truing put the *unshipped* backlog-like remainder nearer ~$307B at that time. It was never formal guidance, a purchase-order backlog, or an ASC 606 RPO figure. It has since been superseded: at GTC in March 2026 Huang raised the framing to **at least $1 trillion of visibility through 2027** for Blackwell + Rubin.
- **Do-not-conflate warning:** Nvidia's Q2 FY27 release contains a *different* $500B — strategic partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR "to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital" for AI infrastructure, subject to definitive agreements. That is third-party financing capacity, not Nvidia revenue, orders, or backlog.
- Formal RPO — **CONFIRMED at ~$3.2B:** Nvidia Form 10-Q for the quarter ended 2026-07-26, revenue note — remaining performance obligations on contracts **longer than one year** were **$3.2B** ($3.0B already in deferred revenue + $244M not yet billed/recognized). This narrow perimeter (long-duration license/development/PCS-type contracts only; it excludes ordinary short-cycle product orders) is why RPO is three orders of magnitude below the $500B visibility number. Comparing them directly without the perimeter note is a category error. Prior-quarter comparator: $2.6B at 2026-04-26.
- Sources: Nvidia Newsroom, "NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Second Quarter Fiscal 2027," Aug 2026 (release dated to Q2 ended 2026-07-26) — read directly; GTC Oct 2025 $500B reporting and CFO-team clarification (Motley Fool / Webull summary of GTC and follow-up, Oct–Nov 2025); TechRepublic GTC 2026 coverage ($1T through 2027); BTW Media analysis of Nvidia Form 10-Q (quarter ended 2026-07-26) revenue/deferred-revenue note, Aug/Sep 2026.

### 8. Receivables: AR ~$40.7B → ~$63.1B, DSO ~45 → ~60 days, extended payment terms — CONFIRMED

- AR figures — **CONFIRMED:** $40,710M was accounts receivable, net, at the end of Q1 FY27 (2026-04-26, per Form 10-Q). Q2 FY27 balance sheet (official release): accounts receivable, net **$63,059M at 2026-07-26** (rounded/reported as $63.1B), vs $38,466M at fiscal year-end 2026-01-25. Sequential jump is therefore +$22.3–22.4B in one quarter (+~55%) while revenue grew +18% Q/Q — receivables materially outran revenue in the quarter, so the HIGH_ACCRUALS / RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flags are arithmetically supported. Cash-flow statement confirms a $(22,346)M accounts-receivable drag in Q2; operating cash flow fell sequentially to $24.1B from $50.3B.
- DSO — **CONFIRMED:** CFO commentary for Q2 FY27 states verbatim: "Accounts receivable was $63.1 billion with **60 days sales outstanding (DSO), up from 45 days sequentially, due to extended payment terms on large, multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade customers**." (CFO commentary / Form 8-K excerpt, Aug 2026.)
- Extended terms — **CONFIRMED and slightly stronger than the headline:** Secondary analysis of the 10-Q reports Nvidia offering investment-grade customers extended terms of **90 days to one year** on large data-center purchases, and concentration: five direct customers accounted for ~70% of the $63.1B receivables balance at quarter-end. The 90-day-to-1-year range was found in reputable secondary reporting (CryptoBriefing, Oct 2026, summarizing the filing), not in the CFO commentary sentence itself — cite it as filing-derived secondary reporting, not as a direct company quote.
- Sources: Nvidia Q2 FY27 official release balance sheet & cash-flow statement — read directly (AR $63,059M; Jan 25, 2026 comparator $38,466M); Nvidia Form 10-Q Q1 FY27 (AR $40,710M at 2026-04-26, via Cape Fear Advisors note citing the filing, 2026-06-23); Nvidia CFO commentary via Form 8-K excerpt (ADVFN reproduction, Aug 2026); Beancount Q2 FY27 analysis, 2026-08-26 (AR $40.7B → $63.1B, DSO 45 → 60, quoting management's extended-terms explanation).
