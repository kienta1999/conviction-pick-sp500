# Agent A — Supply-chain analyst ballot (momentum, 2026-10-04)

Lens: weight physical shortage/backlog evidence and multi-year revenue visibility above all else. Vote for the name where a booked-demand or supply-constraint setup gives the clearest explosive-return configuration.

## 1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** KLAC
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Micron is the purest physical shortage in the S&P 500: HBM is sold out through 2027 with a triopoly chokepoint (only three firms make it at scale), and the DRAM capacity cannibalization is permanent — HBM eats ~3x the wafer area per bit while consuming ~22% of DRAM wafer input for only ~9% of bit supply. The demand side is booked, not hoped: 16 strategic customer agreements worth ~$100B minimum contracted revenue at floor pricing, $22B already collected in deposits, with >75% of 2027 production covered. Valuation is still cyclical-macro cheap (5.3x forward P/E) after a post-earnings fade, so the operating leverage of 86%-plus gross margins flows straight to EPS if the shortage persists into 2028 as Gartner/TrendForce/SK Hynix converge on. Cyclicality is the bear case, but a shortage analyst gets paid for the tight phase, and the tight phase is contractually front-loaded.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Entire 2026 HBM supply sold out; Micron can fill only 50–67% of key-customer demand, ~40% industry shortfall persisting through end-2027, ~$100B minimum contracted revenue over ~5 years with $22B in customer deposits already banked.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Low risk. HBM is a triopoly chokepoint with no practical substitute for AI accelerators; hyperscalers cannot realistically build leading-edge DRAM/HBM (capital intensity, process IP, multi-year qualification). Only genuine long-term threat is Samsung/SK Hynix peer scale and Chinese CXMT in mainstream DRAM — not disintermediation of the HBM layer itself.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35–45% (shortage persists through 2027, memory price discipline holds, HBM4 ramp ~2x faster than HBM3E); bull ~+80–100% (shortage extends into 2028, annual contract repricing catches up, stock re-rates off the 5.3x forward multiple as peak-cycle earnings skeptics capitulate).
- **Top risk:** Commodity cycle turn — 86–87% gross margins are cycle-top levels; if new fab capacity (not before 2028, 3–5yr builds) or demand collapse arrives early, spot prices crack and the $100B floors were negotiated at the top. Secondary: June 2026 California DRAM price-fixing class action (unproven, but pricing-dispute overhang).
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 — the single most compelling shortage/backlog data point + scenario

**1. MU (Micron).** The hardest physical bottleneck in the dossier: entire 2026 HBM supply sold out, only 50–67% of key-customer demand fillable, and Samsung+SK Hynix combined memory inventory below 10 days' supply (healthy = 8–12 weeks) — backed by ~$100B of floor-priced contracts with $22B in deposits. In a supply-constrained triopoly, booked floors plus deposited cash is the closest thing to multi-year revenue visibility on the list. Base ~+35–45% / bull ~+80–100% (see single-pick ballot).

**2. KLAC (KLA).** Near-monopoly in semiconductor process control — no leading-edge fab runs without its inspection/metrology — with backlog exploding +60% YoY to $12.57B (~one full year of FY2026 revenue already booked) and management explicitly stating 2H 2026 revenue will be ~20% above 1H "as additional supply becomes available," i.e., demand is not the constraint, its own supply is. That's the tightest demand-vs-supply statement from any management team in the dossier. Base ~+25–35% as supply unlocks against the backlog / bull ~+50–60% if 2027 growth proves "at least as strong" as guided and the stock re-rates off its ~24%-off-high entry.

**3. FCX (Freeport-McMoRan).** The clearest *structural* (not cyclical) deficit: copper at an all-time high of $14,745/ton with Shanghai inventories at their lowest since 2023, and a 15–20-year discovery-to-production lead time for new mines — meaning no supply wave can arrive this decade, and FCX's Tier-1 geology (Grasberg, Cerro Verde, Morenci) is unreplicable. The volume ramp (3.1B → 4.1B lbs by 2028) layers operating leverage on the price: management estimates ~$390M annual EBITDA per $0.10/lb copper move, ~$13B at $5 vs ~$20B at $7. Base ~+20–30% on price + ramp / bull ~+50–70% if copper holds $6.50+ and Grasberg hits its mid-2027 throughput targets.

### Deliberately left out of the top 10 and why

- **LLY** — shortage thesis is dead (FDA off-listed tirzepatide; supply caught up); it's a demand-share story, wrong slot for this lens.
- **NEM** — no backlog, no capacity-booking race, declining production and rising AISC; investors can buy gold directly — pure price beta, not a supply constraint.
- **EOG** — best-in-class shale operator but a cyclical commodity price-taker with no sold-out capacity; Hold consensus and declining 2027 EPS estimates tell the story.
- **COP** — oil is fungible; the only real shortage leg (LNG tightness through 2030) is a gas kicker on a barrel-priced compounder, not an explosive bottleneck.
