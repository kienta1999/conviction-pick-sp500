**MU:** The single most compelling data point is the $100B contracted-revenue book at floor pricing — roughly a full year-plus of revenue locked in at margins designed to hold above prior-cycle peaks even if spot prices crack, plus ~$22B of cash already collected from customers. That is hypergrowth with a floor under it. 12–18mo: base ~+60–80% as quarterly revenue marches $54B→$61.5B and the 5.3x fwd P/E re-rates; bull ~+150–200% if DRAM pricing holds through 2027 and HBM4 contract repricing captures the 55–70% premium over HBM3E.

**SNDK:** The single most compelling data point is the New Business Model: 10 long-term agreements with 8 customers for ~$93.9B minimum revenue at floor pricing (weighted avg duration >4 years, backed by $16.5B in customer guarantees), covering >50% of FY27 and ~two-thirds of FY28 bit shipments. That converts a commodity business into a contracted compounder. 12–18mo: base ~+40–60% as NAND contract pricing holds and FQ1 FY27's $10.3–10.8B revenue guide lands; bull ~+100%+ if the multi-year NAND gap (capacity ~40% below the 2022 peak) keeps pricing power into 2028 and the JPMorgan $2,250 / Rosenblatt $2,400 targets play out.

**LRCX:** The single most compelling data point is a September-quarter guide of $8.10B revenue (+52% YoY) with EPS +71%, after twice raising the 2026 WFE outlook to the low-$150B range — the CEO is openly planning not to be the production bottleneck (accelerating a second Malaysia facility) while deferred revenue rose to $2.43B. That is accelerating revenue with customers paying ahead. 12–18mo: base ~+35–50% as the WFE cycle runs and the +52% growth guide compounds; bull ~+90–120% if "Physical AI" extends the cycle into 2027–28 and the 58x trailing P/E compresses against 30%+ earnings growth.
