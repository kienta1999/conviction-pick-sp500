## KLA (KLAC)

### Claim 1 — Backlog +60% YoY to $12.57B (Q4 FY2026 / FY26 10-K)
**CONFIRMED.** KLA's FY26 10-K (filed ~2026-07-29) states: "Our backlog ... increased from $7.86 billion as of June 30, 2025, to $12.57 billion as of June 30, 2026, due to strong demand driven by the AI infrastructure buildout." $12.57B/$7.86B = 1.599 → **+60% YoY**, exactly as claimed.
Sources: stocktitan.net KLA 10-K page (crawled ~2026-09-09); also reported by TradingView/Zacks piece (updated ~5 days before 2026-10-04).

### Claim 2 — Supply-constrained into 2027; H2 2026 revenue ~20% above H1 "as additional supply becomes available"
**CONFIRMED.** Q4 FY2026 earnings-call coverage quotes CFO Bren Higgins: "KLA expects second-half calendar 2026 growth over the first half to be approximately 20%, supported by improving supply availability," and the company is "positioned for continued sequential growth into calendar 2027." Wording "expects second-half calendar 2026 revenues to increase roughly 20% from the first half as additional supply becomes available" appears verbatim in TradingView/Zacks coverage.
Sources: zacks.com Q4 earnings-call highlights (~67 days before 2026-10-04, i.e. late July 2026); tradingview.com/news/zacks piece.

### Claim 3 — Record Q4 FY2026 revenue ~$3.66B; September-quarter (Q1 FY27) guide ~$4.0B
**CONFIRMED.** KLA's July 28, 2026 press release: "total revenues were $3.66 billion" (Q4 FY2026; record); GAAP diluted EPS $1.04, non-GAAP $1.05. Q1 FY2027 guidance: "Total revenues ... $4.0 billion +/- $200 million"; non-GAAP EPS $1.16 +/- $0.10.
Sources: KLA press release via iconnect007.com (published 2026-07-28); stocktitan.net summary.

### Claim 4 — Near-monopoly in process control (~73–74% metrology share; no fab can in-source it)
**CONFIRMED (with source-quality caveat).** ainvest.com analysis: "KLA commands 73.8 percent of the global metrology and inspection market, up from roughly 50 percent in 2010... a near-monopoly." Supporting detail from an investor thesis (GitHub, jameswong2011/investmentvault): "75–80% patterned-wafer share, 80%+ reticle inspection, 95%+ in some end nodes." Caveat: no company-stated 73.8% figure was found; ainvest is third-party financial press, not a primary source. The "cannot in-source" qualitative half is analyst characterization (installed-base/switching-cost argument), not a company statement.
Sources: ainvest.com (2026-05); github.com/jameswong2011/investmentvault; guruFocus/TradingView (50–60% wafer inspection, 40–50% metrology — older, 2025 view).

Sources: aistockwire.com (2026-10-01: "$150B RPO", LSEG consensus $51.07B); callsheetdaily.substack.com MU Q4'26 call notes (quoting Mehrotra/Murphy on both June and Sep figures).

Sources: aistockwire.com (2026-10-01); startupfortune.com (2026-10-01); tech-insider.org (2026-10-03).
