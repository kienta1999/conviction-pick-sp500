# Panelist A — Supply-chain analyst — Momentum 2026-10-14

## 1) Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** MU
- **Runner-up:** GOOGL
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** MU has the best-evidenced multi-year shortage in the dossier: calendar-2026 HBM is sold out, management can fill only 50-66% of key customer demand, and it sees demand exceeding supply in 2027 and 2028 with no line of sight to balance. That scarcity is contracted, not spot — ~$150 billion of RPO under 26 strategic customer agreements, covering >35% of revenue through 2030, backed by $32 billion of customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits. At a screen forward P/E of 4.8 the market is pricing peak earnings, but the take-or-pay floors and prepayments give FY27-28 visibility no other shortage name matches in duration. This lens ranks contracted backlog and cash-backed visibility above pure moat or spot price leverage.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** ~$150B RPO (2026-09-30), 26 SCAs up from 16 in June, $32B customer commitments ($12.3-12.75B received in Q4 alone), entire calendar-2026 HBM supply sold out, most 2027 HBM already contracted at significantly higher prices, DRAM lead times reported past 40 weeks, and no meaningful new fab output until late fiscal 2028.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** medium — only three firms hold HBM/DRAM process technology and hyperscalers buy HBM even for their own TPUs/Trainium/Maia, so in-sourcing is not credible; risk is intra-oligopoly share shift to SK hynix (50% HBM) / Samsung and China's CXMT in conventional DRAM, not bypass.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** rough base +25-40% if SCA floors and Q1 FY27 guide ($60-63B revenue) convert as guided; bull +70-100% if 2027 HBM pricing re-contracts higher and the buyback unlock after 2026-12-09 supports re-rating off the 4.8x screen multiple.
- **Top risk:** Peak-cycle pricing — bit growth was only mid-single digits in Q4 while ASPs did the work, so an AI capex pause, CXMT supply, or late-FY28 fab wave could reverse ASPs as fast as they rose; SCA floors also cap upside on ~1/3 of revenue.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 9

**2. **3. SNDK:** At least $93.9B of minimum expected revenue under NBM agreements covering more than 50% of fiscal 2027 bits and ~two-thirds of fiscal 2028 bits, weighted-average duration >4 years, including $16.5B of financial guarantees — converting a historically spot commodity into floored volume.
