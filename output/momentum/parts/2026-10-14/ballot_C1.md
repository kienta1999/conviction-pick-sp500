## 1) Single pick

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** NVDA
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLAC is the purest quality/moat name in the set: clear #1 in process control at ~56-58% share, roughly 7x the nearest competitor, with ~80%+ corners in reticle inspection and ~75-80% in patterned-wafer inspection. That dominance converts to economics — 62.4% non-GAAP gross margin and 43.7% operating margin in Q4 FY26, plus a ~23%-of-revenue services annuity and a 2030 model targeting >90% of FCF returned. No fab operator builds, or has attempted to build, its own production inspection/metrology tools, so unlike the hyperscaler-exposed names there is no credible customer in-sourcing path. Process-control intensity rises with every node, die-size and advanced-packaging step, so KLAC grows faster than wafer volume without owning the cyclical end product.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Backlog/RPO $12.57B at 2026-06-30, up 60% YoY from $7.86B, ~0.93x FY26 revenue of $13.58B; advanced-packaging process-control revenue expected to approach $1.1B in 2026, up >70% YoY; calendar-2026 WFE outlook raised to low-$150B range; Q1 FY27 guided to ~$4.0B revenue vs $3.66B in Q4 FY26; management cited 12-24 month lead times to add capacity for certain components.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** low — dossier scores it 9/10; chipmakers cannot in-source inspection/metrology, the moat is decades of process-integration IP, optics/e-beam physics and defect data from a huge installed base, and requalifying a critical inspection step mid-node risks yield. Substitution is only step-level within the vendor set (AMAT, Onto), not a bypass of the category.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base +15-20% (toward the ~$227-230 Morgan Stanley / consensus targets cited in the dossier, on backlog conversion and the guided H2 acceleration) / bull +35-40% if the 2027 WFE setup and >70% advanced-packaging growth land at sustained ~62% gross margins
- **Top risk:** WFE cyclicality at a rich multiple — 28.2x forward for FY26 revenue growth of only 11.7%; orders/backlog are cancellable/reschedulable, China is roughly a third of revenue with ~87% international sales, and receivables outran revenue (+27.6% vs +11.7%, DSO ~68 to ~78 days) in FY26.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

**3.
