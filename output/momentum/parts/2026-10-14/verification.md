**CONFIRMED.**

- Google Cloud backlog reached $514B at end-Q2 2026, per Alphabet's Q2 2026 earnings call (2026-07-22), more than $50B above Q1 2026. CFO Anat Ashkenazi said just over 50% is expected to become revenue within 24 months.

- Sources: Alphabet Q2 2026 earnings call, 2026-07-22, backlog table and call reporting via Quantumrun Consulting summary (accessed 2026-10-14); The Motley Fool, 2026-10-07 / 2026-10-11.

- Sources: GTC DC disclosure, 2025-10-28, as reported by Yahoo Finance/CRN and case-study summaries; NVIDIA Q1 FY27 CFO commentary via SEC filing references; NVIDIA Q2 FY27 Form 10-Q summary, StockTitan, August 2026.

**CONFIRMED — with the same scope nuance as the prior run.**

- Micron's fiscal Q4 FY2026 call (2026-09-30): CFO Mark Murphy put remaining performance obligations at ~$150B; signed strategic customer agreements (SCAs) rose to 26 from 16 in June; customer financial commitments reached $32B, the vast majority cash deposits ($12.3B received in Q4 alone, booked as financing cash flow).

The SCAs are estimated at >35% of revenue through 2030, some extending into 2031.
- Sources: Micron Q4 FY2026 earnings call and prepared remarks, 2026-09-30, as reported by AI Stock Wire, 2026-10-01; BigGo Finance call summary, 2026-09-30; MarketBeat call highlights, 2026-09-30; Reuters via InvestingCube, 2026-10-01.
