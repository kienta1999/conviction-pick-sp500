## Single-pick ballot

MY SINGLE PICK: KLAC

KLA is the most durable, irreplaceable compounder in this field because its moat is not a product cycle but an accumulated data-and-physics fortress: 56–58% share of process control at ~7x the nearest competitor, near-monopolies in reticle and patterned-wafer inspection, and decades of defect-classification data that no rival can replicate quickly. Fabs are locked at the process level — replacing KLA means requalifying entire manufacturing flows against an installed reference platform — and the ~24%-of-revenue services annuity keeps compounding even when tool orders pause.

TRAP VETO: none — the pick passes the disintermediation test because no foundry, hyperscaler, or memory maker has ever in-sourced wafer-inspection equipment; the combination of multi-billion-dollar capital intensity, tool-chamber optical/e-beam physics, and the decades of defect data behind the algorithms makes DIY a non-starter, and competitors (Onto, Nova, Hitachi High-Tech, Lasertec) only nibble at niche applications rather than threatening the core platform.
