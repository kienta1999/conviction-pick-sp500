MY SINGLE PICK: MU

Micron is the cleanest explosive-growth case in the field: Q3 FY26 revenue quadrupled to ~$42B with >81% gross margins, and the Sep 30 Q4 print is guided to ~$50B vs ~$44B consensus — growth accelerating off a massive base, with 2026 HBM supply fully contracted at floor prices ($22B customer prepayments, $22B agreements through 2030) while the company can still only meet 50–66% of bit demand. HBM4 revenue already >$1B and ramping 2x faster than HBM3E into a TAM growing $35B to ~$100B by 2028 — yet the stock trades at a 6–12x forward P/E that still prices memory as a boom-bust cyclical, so the re-rating (contracted, price-floored business) is ahead, not behind. Momentum is intact (+58.7% above 200d) with a near-term catalyst (the Sep 30 earnings) rather than one priced to perfection like SNDK.

TRAP VETO: none — the pick passes the disintermediation test because only three companies on earth (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) can manufacture HBM/DRAM at scale, with >$25B/year capex and Nvidia qualification lock-in making hyperscaler in-sourcing a non-starter; the substitute threat is only a technology-mix question (ASICs using different memory), not DIY DRAM. Research notes the same evidence: "No credible in-sourcing threat from hyperscalers" (batch 1). The real risk is ordinary memory cyclicality, which is a momentum-lens timing risk, not the fatal disintermediation trap.
