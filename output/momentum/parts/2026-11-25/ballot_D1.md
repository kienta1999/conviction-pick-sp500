## 1. Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** APH
- **Runner-up:** CBOE
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Amphenol is the AI-shortage name the market still prices as a cyclical connector stock, not as the bottleneck. Its interconnects sit in every accelerator build — AI datacom grew 117% in Q3 — yet it trades at 21.4x forward earnings, the cheapest of the AI-shortage names (ANET 29.5x), with a mean analyst target of $106.97, +45.6% above the verified Nov 24 close of $73.47. Unlike Arista, it is not a single-architecture bet: IT Datacom is 48% of sales, with defence (#1 in military connectors, 36% share), auto, industrial and sensors diversifying the cycle. The Nov 24 selloff (−5.1% in a day) widened the gap between a record order book and a mid-cycle multiple — exactly the dislocation this lens is meant to buy.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Record Q3 2026 orders of $12.17B, +99% YoY, at 1.19x quarterly sales of $10.23B (book-to-bill ~1.19), with IT Datacom backlog "stretching further out than usual" and cable lead times stretching toward a year, at least one major manufacturer sold out through 2026.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Low — hyperscalers cannot realistically in-source precision connectors, cables and fibre interconnects: the moat is breadth across every accelerator vendor, multi-year qualification cycles, and co-design with each customer. The real substitution risk is architectural — optics replacing copper — and the dossier puts full optical scale-up no earlier than 2029. That is a 2029 problem for a 12–18 month bet, not a 2027 one. (Contrast ANET: Nvidia bundles Spectrum-X into the GPU sale, a live, present-tense disintermediation threat.)
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +35–45% to roughly $100–$107 (the $106.97 analyst mean target) as the $12.17B order book converts and the multiple holds near 21–22x on much higher earnings. Bull +70–80% to roughly $125–$130 if AI-datacom growth sustains above 50%, CommScope synergies land, and the market re-rates a 40%+ grower off a cyclical multiple toward ~25x.
- **Top risk:** Cyclicality plus leverage — net debt of $12.6B after the $10.5B CommScope deal, and Q4 IT Datacom guided at only mid-single-digit sequential growth. If hyperscaler capex pauses, orders (not backlog alone) are the first thing to roll, and a connector multiple compresses fast.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7

## Why not the consensus favourite (ANET)

ANET has the highest shortage score in the dossier (9/10) and the fastest print (Q3 revenue +58.5% YoY, FY2027 pre-guided +38–40%), which is precisely why the skeptic demotes it: the explosive case is already the consensus case, the stock is +49.1% over 12 months, and the disintermediation threat is not theoretical — IDC Q1 2026 already has Nvidia leading data-centre Ethernet (21.5% vs Arista 20.7%), Arista has no scale-up answer until trials in 2027/production in 2028, Microsoft and Meta each exceed 10% of revenue, and inventory is $3.21B with turns down to 1.6. A great company, a real shortage, and a priced-in, single-point-of-failure thesis. APH gives a comparable shortage at a lower multiple, with lower disintermediation risk and far broader end-markets.
