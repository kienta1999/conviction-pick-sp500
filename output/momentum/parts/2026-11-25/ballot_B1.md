## 1. Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** APH
- **Runner-up:** ANET
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Amphenol has the fastest revenue acceleration in the dossier — Q3 2026 sales $10.23B, +65% YoY (+37% organic), with IT Datacom +100% and AI datacom +117% — and it is diversified across defence, auto, industrial and telecom, not a single AI fabric bet. Its order book is growing faster than sales, which is the growth investor's cleanest signal that the acceleration is demand-led, not channel fill. At 21.4x forward earnings it is also cheaper than ANET (29.5x) for faster top-line growth, leaving more room for multiple expansion if the AI build-out persists. This is the fastest compounder in the batch with the broadest shortage exposure.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Record Q3 2026 orders of $12.17B, +99% YoY — 1.2x quarterly sales of $10.23B, a book-to-bill of ~1.19 — with IT Datacom backlog "stretching further out than usual" and cable lead times stretching toward a year.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Low — hyperscalers cannot realistically in-source precision connectors, cables and fibre interconnects; Amphenol co-designs with every accelerator vendor and qualification cycles are long. The real substitution risk is architectural (optics replacing copper), and the dossier puts full optical scale-up no earlier than 2029.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35–45% — roughly to the analyst mean target of $106.97 (about +46% from the Nov 24 close of $73.47) if AI datacom growth stays strong and the multiple holds near current levels. Bull ~+70–80% — to roughly $125–130 — if IT Datacom keeps compounding near triple digits, orders stay above 1.1x sales, and the market re-rates a 60%+ grower toward a higher multiple.
- **Top risk:** AI-datacom cyclicality — Q4 IT Datacom was guided to only mid-single-digit sequential growth, which could be the first sign of digestion after the surge; plus $12.6B net debt after the $10.5B CommScope deal.
