# Ballot A1 — Supply-Chain Analyst (Momentum, 2026-11-20)

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** APH
- **Runner-up:** WAB
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Amphenol is where the AI shortage has actually moved: from compute to the physical interconnect, where 224 Gb/s lanes force traffic off PCB traces into precisely engineered cable assemblies that only a short list of suppliers can build at scale. Orders have run ahead of shipments for three straight quarters, with Q3 orders up 99% year over year, so the demand signal is a booked order book, not a forecast. The CommScope CCS acquisition closed 9 Jan 2026 filled the one capability gap — fibre — just as industry fibre/preform capacity fell behind demand with up to two years needed to build new preform plants. That combination of a verified bottleneck, multi-quarter order cover, and a newly completed fibre position gives APH the most explosive 12–18 month upside in this field.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Q3 2026 orders of USD 12.17B, up 99% year over year and up 13% sequentially — a record — with a 1.19:1 book-to-bill in Q3, following 1.23:1 in Q2, 1.24:1 in Q1 and 1.31:1 in Q4 2025; customers are reserving capacity well into next year.
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Medium — high-speed interconnect is designed into rack architectures with the chip vendor and hyperscaler and then qualified there, and a failed link can take down an entire training run, which is real lock-in; but hyperscalers qualify multiple suppliers (TE Connectivity is the live second source), Amphenol has no software lock-in, and co-packaged optics / silicon photonics could over time reduce discrete connector content per rack. Customers cannot realistically in-source signal-integrity manufacturing at 224G, but they can and do dual-source it, so APH is breadth-plus-qualification, not a monopoly.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base — order growth moderates from the 99% print but the book-to-bill stays above 1.0, IT Datacom keeps growing well ahead of the group, and the stock compounds on earnings growth roughly in line with the FY2026 sales guide of USD 36.9–37.1B; roughly a mid-teens to low-20s percent gain. Bull — the fibre constraint and 224G ramp keep orders ahead of shipments, AI datacom revenue keeps compounding near its Q3 pace (up 117% year over year), and capacity reservations convert into a further guidance raise; roughly a 35–50% gain.
- **Top risk:** AI concentration — IT Datacom is 48% of sales and about 75% of that is AI, so a hyperscaler capex pause hits APH immediately, and some longer-dated orders may come from less-capitalised neocloud buyers.
