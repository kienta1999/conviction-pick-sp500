# Panelist B — Growth / Momentum Investor — Ballot, 2026-11-20

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** APH
- **Runner-up:** ANET
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Amphenol is the fastest compounder in the field: Q3 2026 sales grew 65% YoY (37% organically), adjusted EPS grew 74% YoY, and IT Datacom sales doubled YoY with AI-related datacom revenue up 117%. Demand is running ahead of supply for three straight quarters, with customers reserving capacity well into next year, so the growth has a visible runway rather than being a one-quarter spike. Guidance was raised across the board (FY2026 sales USD 36.9–37.1B, adjusted EPS USD 2.82–2.84 vs consensus USD 35.66B / USD 2.69), and the CommScope CCS fibre acquisition plugs the one capability gap in the interconnect stack. At 224 Gb/s per lane the bottleneck has moved from compute to the physical interconnect, and Amphenol is the largest player in that bottleneck.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** Q3 2026 orders of USD 12.17B, up 99% YoY and 13% sequentially — a record — with a book-to-bill of 1.19:1, the fourth consecutive quarter above 1.19 (1.31 in Q4 2025, 1.24 in Q1, 1.23 in Q2).
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** Medium. The moat is qualification lock-in: high-speed interconnect is co-designed into rack architectures with the chip vendor and hyperscaler, and a failed link can take down an entire training run, so switching is slow and costly. But hyperscalers qualify multiple suppliers (TE Connectivity is the live second source), Amphenol has no software lock-in, and co-packaged optics / silicon photonics could over time reduce discrete connector content per rack. It is breadth and process knowledge, not a monopoly.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +20–30% (orders convert into the guided FY2026 numbers and IT Datacom keeps growing at a high-double-digit rate, with the multiple roughly holding). Bull case roughly +50% or more if hyperscaler capex stays at the current pace, the 1.19 book-to-bill persists into 2027, and the market re-rates the interconnect layer toward the AI names.
- **Top risk:** AI concentration — IT Datacom is 48% of sales and ~75% of that is AI, so a hyperscaler capex pause hits APH immediately; the beat was already partly priced, with shares down ~5.7% in the week after the print.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
