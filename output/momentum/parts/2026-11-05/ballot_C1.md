## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: ISRG
- Runner-up: WST
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Intuitive Surgical owns the robotic-surgery category outright — ~86% US share and 70–80% globally, with an installed base of 12,094 da Vinci systems (+12% YoY) and over 60,000 trained surgeons, a training-and-evidence lock-in no rival can copy quickly. Only ~5% of world surgeries are robot-assisted, so the runway is structural, not a one-quarter shortage, and the da Vinci 5 upgrade cycle is already visible: 315 da Vinci 5 placements in Q3 2026 (58% of the 546 systems placed, +28% YoY) and da Vinci 5 now over 25% of US procedures. The razor/blade model compounds the moat — instruments & accessories were $1.80B in Q3, more than 2x systems revenue of $769m, at ~$1,860 per procedure. Q3 2026 revenue was $3.05B (+22% YoY) with non-GAAP EPS $3.00 (+25%), both beating consensus, so the momentum is an upgrade cycle on top of a monopoly installed base, not a price spike.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: 546 da Vinci systems placed in Q3 2026 (+28% YoY), including 315 da Vinci 5, taking the installed base to 12,094 (+12% YoY) — demand met by financing (291 operating leases, 160 usage-based), i.e. hospitals buying into the ecosystem faster, not a queue Intuitive cannot fill.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: low — hospitals cannot build their own surgical robot, and the moat is the installed base plus surgeon training plus clinical evidence plus the recurring instruments stream. The only real deduct is that substitutes are finally real: Medtronic Hugo holds ~5–7% and J&J Ottava is pending US approval, plus a $450m Q3 charge to settle a class action over third-party remanufacturing/servicing of its closed ecosystem.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base ~$560–$600 from the verified Nov 4 close of $482.10 (roughly +16–25%), on mid-teens procedure growth (Q3 +14% YoY, ex-US +20%) and continued da Vinci 5 conversion; bull ~$650–$700 (roughly +35–45%) if ex-US procedure growth stays near +20% and da Vinci 5 clearances/adoption accelerate in Europe and Asia.
- Top risk: Self-inflicted pricing — the extended-use programme and volume-based pricing from H1 2027 plus a China charge-code cut from Nov 2026 are expected to cut instruments & accessories revenue per procedure ~3–5%, capping the recurring-revenue engine, and US procedure growth is already decelerating (+10% vs a +16% year-ago comp) at ~51x earnings.
- Conviction (1-10): 7
