# Panelist A — Supply-Chain Analyst Ballot — 2026-11-05

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: MU
- Runner-up: ANET
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Micron is the only name in the field where the shortage is both physical and already paid for in cash: HBM is sold out through 2028, more than 75% of FY27 output is committed, and customers have put down $32B in cash deposits under 26 strategic agreements. Datacenters are set to absorb ~70% of all memory produced in 2026 (IDC, vs 20–30% in 2022), while management sees supply-demand "much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028 than in 2026" with no line of sight to balance returning. The Nov 4 selloff to a verified close of $795.23 (−7.0%, −26% in a month, −34–37% from the $1,255 52-week high) reprices the name after a sentiment reset, not after any order cancellation or capex cut — no hyperscaler cut capex guidance and no order cancellation was reported for any dossier name. That combination — contracted, cash-backed, multi-year demand against supply that cannot respond until 2028 — is the explosive setup this lens is built to find.
- Key shortage/backlog evidence: RPO of ~$150B at FQ4 2026 (from ~$100B the prior quarter), backed by $32B in customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits — customers are prepaying for memory that does not yet exist.
- Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk: medium — only three firms on earth make HBM at scale, fabs cost tens of billions and take years, and HBM must pass NVIDIA/AMD/Google qualification, so no customer can in-source it; but buyers dual/triple-source and Samsung's HBM share surged from 15% to 33% in four quarters, so Micron has no lock-in per customer and share, not volume, is the contested ground.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case — Base: the contracted FY27–28 book converts at still-elevated pricing and the stock recovers part of the recent drawdown, roughly +30–40% from the $795.23 close toward ~$1,030–1,110 (analyst upside cited in the dossier is +83%, with Needham's $1,650 target a street outlier, ~2x the close). Bull: HBM pricing holds through 2027, FQ1 FY27 guidance (~$61.5B revenue, ~$38.15 adj EPS) is beaten and raised again, and the stock re-rates toward +70–100% from $795.23.
- Top risk: The cycle turns before the capacity does — Kioxia's Oct 30 warning (NAND ASP growth decelerating from +70% QoQ to mid-20s) is the first fundamental crack in the price rate-of-change, Micron is spending ≥$50B capex in FY27 on new capacity (a 2028 supply response), and an 87% non-GAAP gross margin is a peak-cycle print by any historical standard.
- Conviction (1-10): 7
