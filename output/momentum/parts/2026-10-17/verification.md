## MU — Micron Technology

### 1. ~$150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs, ~$32B customer financial commitments / deposits, covering >35% of revenue through 2030
**Verdict: CONFIRMED — with a deposits clarification.**

- 30 Sep 2026, Q4 FY2026 call / prepared remarks (CFO Mark Murphy, CEO Sanjay Mehrotra), transcript reproduced at TradingKey: "To date, we have signed 26 SCAs in total, and our remaining performance obligations, or RPO, is approximately $150 billion. All SCAs have take-or-pay contracted volumes, and RPO reflects the contract value for only SCAs that have a determined pricing framework, which can be either a fixed price or subject to a pricing floor and ceiling. RPO is based on committed volumes and minimum pricing and is inherently conservative… We expect revenue to well exceed the associated RPO."

- Clarification the panel must keep: $32B is *financial commitments*, "the vast majority of which are cash deposits" — it is not $32B already received. Deposits actually received were $12.3B in Q4 alone, $12.7B / $12.75B at period end, booked as financing cash flow, with non-current contract liabilities of $12.90B at 3 Sep 2026 (FY2026 8-K / 10-K analysis). The dossier's wording is accurate if read that way; do not quote "$32B of deposits received."
- Sources: Micron Q4 FY2026 earnings call, 2026-09-30 — https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262195079-tradingkey ; https://aistockwire.com/blog/micron-mu-q4-fy2026-earnings-150-billion-rpo-guidance-september-2026 ; filing detail — https://www.signalstack.news/blog/micron-fiscal-q4-2026-results/

### 10. The $500B (to $1T) figure through 2027: filing RPO / backlog, or management demand visibility? What is the actual filing RPO?
**Verdict: CONFIRMED as management demand visibility — CONTRADICTED if presented as a filing RPO / backlog. Actual filing RPO is $3.2B.**

- $500B origin: Q3 FY26 call, 19 Nov 2025, CFO Colette Kress: "We currently have visibility to $0.5 trillion in Blackwell and Rubin revenue from the start of this year through the end of calendar year 2026." Coverage notes it explicitly excluded some later deals (Anthropic, expanded Humain). It was a *visibility* statement on a call, not a balance-sheet / RPO disclosure. Source: https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidia-reports-blowout-earnings-ai-data-center-boom-continues-despite-bubble-fears/
- $1T origin: GTC keynote, 16 Mar 2026, CEO Jensen Huang (TechCrunch): last year Nvidia saw ~$500B in demand through 2026; "right now where I stand… I see through 2027, at least $1 trillion." A keynote projection / demand view, not a filing. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/16/jensen-just-put-nvidias-blackwell-and-vera-rubin-sales-projections-into-the-1-trillion-stratosphere/
- Actual filing RPO: Form 10-Q for the quarter ended 26 Jul 2026 (filed Aug 2026): "As of July 26, 2026, revenue related to remaining performance obligations from contracts greater than one year in length was $3.2 billion, which included $3.0 billion from deferred revenue and $244 million that has not yet been billed or recognized as revenue. Approximately 39%… will be recognized over the next twelve months." Total deferred revenue balance at period end was $6,412M; H1 FY27 customer advances were $15.6B ($13.0B recognised in the period). Source (10-Q text): https://cdn.prod.nntech.io/company-events/reports/7fc9b874-c38b-3135-b3fd-260fda5cfdcd/report.pdf
- Why the gap is not itself a red flag: Nvidia's RPO disclosure by definition covers only contracts >1 year; most GPU / system sales are shorter-cycle purchase orders that never enter that line. But the two numbers must never be conflated.
- Also do not conflate: Nvidia's own *supply and capacity commitments* of $279B at Jul 2026 (up from $119B) are Nvidia's purchase obligations to its supply chain — the opposite direction from customer backlog.
