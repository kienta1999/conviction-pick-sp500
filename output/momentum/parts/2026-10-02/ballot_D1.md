## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** CAT
- **Runner-up:** FCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** CAT owns the scarcest bottleneck in the AI power chain — large gas-engine prime power that hyperscalers need on day one because grid interconnect takes 3–7 years — while the stock sits ~26% off its 52-week high ($1,073.46) on state-level data-center regulatory headlines (Texas, New York), not on any demand slowdown: CEO Joe Creed said on the Q2 2026 call (Aug 4, 2026) he had seen no demand slowing in the data-center market, and the quarter itself was the first-ever $20B+ sales print (+24% YoY) with FY2026 guidance raised to mid-to-high-teens growth. A customer cannot wait out a lead time that runs into 2029, and a hyperscaler cannot build or in-source a large reciprocating engine (EPA Tier 4 certification + extreme capital intensity + dealer/service ecosystem). This is the one genuinely discounted real shortage in the set — verifiable sold-out capacity trading at a fear discount while the memory names trade at hope premiums.
- **Key shortage/backlog evidence:** CEO Joe Creed, Q2 2026 call (Aug 4, 2026): *"if we can get more units out, they're asking us to give them more units"* — with gas-engine prime-power lead times running into the back half of 2028 and into 2029, a record **$72B backlog** at end of Q2 2026 (+92% YoY, ~1.0x TTM revenue of $74.7B), and some Power & Energy customers placing orders as far out as 2030 (Trefis, Sep 2026).
- **Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk:** **LOW.** Hyperscalers buy power equipment; they do not manufacture large reciprocating engines or gas turbines. Moat per dossier: extreme capital intensity, EPA Tier 4 / Stage V emissions certification, and CAT's dealer + service + financing ecosystem (~25% of revenue from services/aftermarket), plus Solar Turbines' 1–22 MW behind-the-meter portfolio. Substitute threat (fuel cells, batteries, SMRs) is medium-to-long term and nowhere near multi-MW continuous prime power today. Dossier irreplaceability: 8/10.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** **Base +20–30%** — the $72B backlog converts at current margins (Q2 adj operating margin 21.9%, +430 bps), FY2026 mid-to-high-teens sales growth materializes, and the regulatory headline discount partially re-rates. **Bull +50–70%** — data-center prime-power demand accelerates (the ~3x-2024 large-engine output target by 2030 gets pulled forward; Lafayette expansion already largely sold), pricing power sustains margins, and multiple compression reverses.
- **Top risk:** It's still a cyclical trading at ~34.9x TTM earnings vs ~22.4x for the S&P 500 (dossier) — a hyperscaler capex pause would collapse the premium fast, and the state-level data-center regulation depressing the stock could slow the very buildout driving orders. Plus a $1.3–1.5B tariff headwind flagged by the CFO.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7 — the most verifiable shortage at the clearest discount in the set, but cyclical timing risk caps it.
