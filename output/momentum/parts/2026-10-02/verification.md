## Claim 2 — HBM shortage driver (demand exceeds supply into 2027-2028; Micron can only fill ~50–66% of HBM orders)

**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (with attribution nuance noted below)

- The "half to two-thirds" fill-rate statement: CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said on the fiscal Q3 2026 call (June 24, 2026) that Micron can fulfill "only between half and two-thirds" of customer HBM demand, with 2026 HBM supply fully sold out under multi-year contracts.
  - Sources: TheStreet (https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/microns-memory-boom-faces-a-new-test-artificial-intelligence, "Mehrotra backed that up on the Q3 call. Micron can only fill 50% to two-thirds of demand from its biggest customers right now"); AI Weekly via The Next Web (https://aiweekly.co/alerts/micron-q3-2026-revenue-quadruples-to-42b-hbm-supply-sold-out); UBS analyst note covered by PreMarketDaily ("can only fill 50% to 65% of its key customers' medium-term requirements").
  - Nuance: the 50–66% quote originates from the **Q3** call (June 2026), not the Q4 call. It remains the operative management statement and was repeated in press coverage surrounding the Q4 results.
- The Q4 FY2026 call (Sep 30, 2026) independently confirms the sold-out state: Mehrotra — "a large part of the volume is already sold out for 2027 for HBM, and the prices are much higher than 2026 prices"; completed agreements for the vast majority of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply with significant YoY price increases; HBM demand expected to outgrow industry DRAM growth through 2028; "We do not have line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance"; expects memory supply-demand "much tighter" in fiscal 2027 and 2028 than in 2026; >75% of fiscal 2027 shipments already committed; most customer allocation discussions now focused on 2028.
  - Sources: AlphaStreet Q4 2026 call transcript (https://news.alphastreet.com/micron-technology-inc-mu-q4-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); BigGo call transcript (https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_MU_2026-09-30); The Cerbat Gem call highlights (https://www.thecerbatgem.com/2026/09/30/micron-technology-q4-earnings-call-highlights.html).
- Corroboration: TrendForce (per AJU Press, Sep 29 report) raised its 2027 HBM blended-ASP forecast to +121% YoY, echoing supplier supply constraints.

- MemoView HBM landscape piece (citing TrendForce 1Q26 HBM Bulletin, Astute Group): bit-share estimates SK Hynix ~50%, Samsung ~28%, Micron ~22% — again three firms covering the market.
