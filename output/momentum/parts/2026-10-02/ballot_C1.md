- **Top risk:** This cycle ends like all the others — commodity DRAM/NAND still carry most of the volume, expectations are extreme (the stock faded in thin trading after the FQ4 beat on an FQ1 gross-margin guide miss, 86.25% vs 86.7% expected), and the $200B capex plan becomes overbuild risk the moment AI capex pauses.
