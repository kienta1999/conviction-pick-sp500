Sources: web search 2026-11-16; primary sources preferred, secondary aggregators where noted. Figures are as reported; conflicts flagged.

## MU — Micron Technology (Semiconductors; mcap ~$880.6B; rev growth +131% TTM; op margin 74.6%; ret 12m +218%; fwd PE 3.7; analyst upside +100.7%; next earnings 2026-12-23)

1. **Shortage thesis:** HBM/memory in deep structural shortage. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra (FQ4 call, 2026-09-30): HBM capacity sold out through 2026; vast majority of calendar-2027 HBM supply already sold at "significantly higher" prices; >75% of FY2027 output committed. AI servers/HBM now consume ~70% of total DRAM output. Shortages expected to persist well beyond 2028; meaningful new global supply not before 2028 at earliest. Micron can satisfy only ~50–66% of key customer HBM demand (per stockmoguls, May 2026). SK Hynix sold out HBM/DRAM through 2029.
2. **Backlog / order book:** HBM 2026 fully contracted; 2027 HBM mostly sold; >75% of FY2027 shipments locked in. FQ1 2027 guide: revenue ~$61.5B, adj EPS ~$38.15 (above Street). FQ4 2026: revenue $54.23B (+379% YoY per aggregator), adj EPS $33.42 vs $31.16 est. **Data conflict flagged:** one source reports FY2026 revenue $133.19B (+256%) and GAAP profit $85B; that is inconsistent with a $54.23B Q4 alone (four quarters at that run-rate would be far higher). Treat FY totals as unverified; quarterly figures corroborated across multiple sources.
3. **Category position:** One of only 3 firms on earth that can make HBM at scale (with Samsung, SK Hynix). HBM4 in high-volume shipment to lead customer; HBM4E production scheduled CY2027.
4. **Irreplaceability:** Very high. HBM requires advanced packaging + DRAM process tech only 3 firms hold; hyperscalers cannot in-source memory fabs (capital intensity, $200B domestic fab expansion underway). Threat: customers could shift accelerator roadmaps; single lead-customer concentration. **Irreplaceability 9/10.**
5. **Recent catalysts (last ~3 months):** FQ4 beat + guide raise (2026-09-30); shares +220% YTD; Nov 11 jump on CoreWeave earnings read-through; Nov 5 −17.5% to $656.39 after Bloomberg reported construction at $4.5B Taiwan Fab 11E "all but halted" over a labor dispute (fab not due to produce until 2028 — a 2028 capacity story, not 2026 demand); Sen. Tom Cotton letter 2026-11-12 urging ban on Chinese rival CXMT. Six new plants planned over two years (3 by mid-2027, 3 in 2028). $1B invested in Anthropic private placement.
6. **Risks:** Memory cyclicality — the classic trap; capex $27.4B FY2026 could oversupply if AI spend softens; lead-customer concentration; Taiwan fab delay; stock already +218% in 12m and trading ~35.8% below 52w high after the Nov 5 drawdown (volatile). Forward PE 3.7 looks absurdly cheap vs history — either a historic mispricing or the market pricing a cycle peak.
7. **Shortage score: 10/10. Verdict:** The purest shortage thesis in the field; the question is cycle timing, not demand.

## ANET — Arista Networks (Communications Equipment; mcap ~$249.3B; rev growth +35.8%; op margin 43.8%; ret 12m +46.2%; fwd PE 32.7; analyst upside +26.5%; next earnings 2027-02-09)

1. **Shortage thesis:** AI datacenter networking demand surge, not a hard supply shortage — Arista sells into the buildout. AI fabrics revenue guided ≥$3.5–3.6B in 2026; Etherlink in use at 100+ customers. CEO: networks to support >10 GW of AI deployments by end-2027.
2. **Backlog / order book:** Q3 2026 (reported 2026-11-03): revenue $3.66B, +58.5% YoY (fastest since IPO), beat ~$3.33B consensus by ~10%; non-GAAP EPS $1.20 vs $1.08. Purchase commitments $11.4B (up from $9.7B in June); deferred revenue $6.84B — but management cautions deferred is not a demand gauge, and it fell sequentially for the first time. Q4 guide $3.9–4.0B revenue, 48–49% non-GAAP op margin. FY2026 raised (4th time) to ~$13.3B (+48%); first 2027 look ~$18.5B (+38–40%), AI fabrics doubling to ~$7B, campus to ~$2B.
3. **Category position:** #1 in cloud networking / high-speed Ethernet for AI clusters; ~49% non-GAAP op margin; no debt.
4. **Irreplaceability:** Medium-high. EOS software + switching silicon ecosystem is sticky, but the moat is software/integration, not fabs. Threat: Nvidia's InfiniBand/NVLink inside the rack; scale-up Ethernet revenue "no material number in 2027" (CEO Ullal, Q3 call) — production 2028. Hyperscalers (notably Meta, Google) design some networking in-house. **Irreplaceability 6/10.**
5. **Recent catalysts:** Q3 beat + FY raise + 2027 guide well above Street (~$18.5B vs ~$16.4–16.75B carried). Shares +6.3% on CoreWeave's $132.5B backlog read-through (2026-11-11).
6. **Risks:** Valuation ~30–35x forward earnings; deferred revenue sequential decline flagged by one analyst (downgrade to Hold); scale-up Ethernet delayed to 2028 — Nvidia keeps the rack-scale interconnect; hyperscaler in-sourcing; inventory build to $3.2B.
7. **Shortage score: 7/10. Verdict:** Fastest compounder in the field, but the shortage is demand-pull for Arista, not supply-constrained product; moat is good, not irreplaceable.

## LLY — Eli Lilly (Pharmaceuticals; mcap ~$1,285.8B; rev growth +37.9%; op margin 51.3%; ret 12m +42.9%; fwd PE 28.5; analyst upside +1.4%; next earnings 2027-02-03)

1. **Shortage thesis:** GLP-1 demand still compounding, but the narrative has shifted: capacity race moving from shortage to cost/price. Lilly Q3 2026 (2026-10-29): revenue $24.4B, +39% YoY; volume +53%, realized price −15% (US −7%, ex-US −29%). Mounjaro $10.7B (+65%), Zepbound $5.35B (+49%); together ~66% of revenue. ~6 in 10 US obesity incretin prescriptions are Lilly's; >7 in 10 injectable. Penetration still <5% of eligible US patients.
2. **Backlog / order book:** Not a backlog business; demand visibility via prescriptions and capacity. 2026 guidance raised to $91–92B revenue (was $85–87B), non-GAAP EPS $35–36. Capacity: $18B invested since 2023; broke ground on $6.5B Houston and $5B Richmond sites; 10 new US sites since 2020. Oral Foundayo $184M in Q3 (from $98M Q2).
3. **Category position:** Clear #1 in GLP-1 by revenue (~1.7x Novo's GLP-1 sales); tirzepatide efficacy edge (22.5% avg weight loss vs semaglutide). Lilly + Novo ~90% of market.
4. **Irreplaceability:** High for the molecule (patents, manufacturing scale), but the moat is not customer in-sourcing — it's competition and price. Threats: oral competitors, Novo's Wegovy pill, compounding (HHS policy), price erosion (−15% realized price), Medicare Bridge pilot uncertainty, employer coverage opt-outs. **Irreplaceability 7/10.**
5. **Recent catalysts:** Q3 beat (revenue $24.4B vs $22.58B est; non-GAAP EPS $7.48 vs $5.71); guidance raised ~$5.5B at midpoint; Nov 6, 2026 agreement with Hims & Hers to sell Zepbound/Foundayo at self-pay prices; retatrutide beat semaglutide in T2D (Oct 28), Phase 3 starts Q4 2026.
6. **Risks:** Price erosion is the story now — volume +53% vs price −15%; ~66% revenue concentration in two drugs; policy risk (Medicare Bridge, pricing pressure); valuation full at ~28.5x forward with analyst upside only +1.4% — market has priced the thesis.
7. **Shortage score: 6/10. Verdict:** Dominant franchise, but the shortage phase is ending and price is the new battleground; upside looks mostly priced in.

## APH — Amphenol (Electronic Components; mcap ~$193.2B; rev growth +49.4%; op margin 28.7%; ret 12m +11.0%; fwd PE 21.6; analyst upside +36.7%; next earnings 2027-01-27)

1. **Shortage thesis:** AI datacenter interconnect demand surge — every accelerator/rack needs more connectors, cables, fiber. IT datacom now ~48% of sales, +117% YoY, ~75% of it AI-related (~36% of the company). Not supply-constrained itself; it sells the picks-and-shovels of the buildout.
2. **Backlog / order book:** Q3 2026 (2026-10-28): sales $10.23B, +65% YoY (+37% organic), first $10B quarter; orders record $12.17B (+99% YoY), book-to-bill 1.19:1; backlog added $1.94B in Q3, $5.7B YTD. Adj EPS $0.80 (+74%) vs $0.73 est. Q4 guide $10.3–10.5B sales, adj EPS $0.81–0.83; FY2026 guide $36.9–37.1B sales, adj EPS $2.82–2.84 (+70%). CommScope CCS acquisition raised to $5.1B sales / $0.18 accretion. Dividend +20%, new $4B buyback. 2-for-1 split effective 2026-09-03.
3. **Category position:** One of the world's largest interconnect makers; #1 or #2 across connectors/fiber; diversified across IT datacom, defense, auto, industrial, aerospace.
4. **Irreplaceability:** Medium-high. Interconnect is engineered, qualified, and broad — but connectors are ultimately a component, not a fab. Moat: scale, breadth, qualification across thousands of SKUs, and being architecture-agnostic (wins whichever accelerator wins). Hyperscalers can't easily in-source thousands of custom interconnects, but they can dual-source and squeeze price. **Irreplaceability 6/10.**
5. **Recent catalysts:** Q3 beat on both lines, record orders, guidance raised, Zacks Rank #1 (Strong Buy, 2026-11-06); acquisitions (Empire Electronics ~$320M sales; AdvancedPCB, Dexgreen, Humanetics sensor business signed).
6. **Risks:** Orders ≠ cash — conversion and acquired-business returns unproven; Q4 datacom guided only mid-single-digit sequential growth (cadence, not the Q3 spike, is what the stock trades on); AI capex cyclicality; integration of CommScope.
7. **Shortage score: 8/10. Verdict:** Strongest order-book momentum in the field after MU; architecture-agnostic AI exposure with a real backlog.
