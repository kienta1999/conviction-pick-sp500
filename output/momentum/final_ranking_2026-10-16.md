## Per-name theses (top 5)

**MU (1).** The shortage is contracted, not spot: HBM is sold out for CY2026 with the large majority of CY2027 already agreed at significantly higher prices, Micron can satisfy only 50–66% of key customers' HBM demand, and a 150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay strategic customer agreements with 32B of customer commitments floors revenue, covering over 35% of revenue through 2030. Micron is one of only three firms making HBM at scale. FQ4 FY26 revenue was 54.23B with FQ1 FY27 guided to 61.5B plus or minus 1.5B.

**GOOGL (2).** Google Cloud backlog of 514B (from 106B a year ago), over 50% expected to convert within 24 months, on Cloud revenue +82% to 24.8B in Q2 2026, with the business capacity-constrained enough to rent third-party GPU bridge capacity and 2026 capex raised to 195–205B. Search holds about 89.9% global share. Mandatory haircut: Q2 headline EPS of 9.11 was materially flattered by a 98.0B unrealised equity-securities gain — judge GOOGL on operating income and backlog conversion, not reported EPS.

**NVDA (3).** Nvidia holds about 80% of AI accelerators, with Q2 FY27 revenue of 96.2B and a Q3 guide of about 108B. Its 500B to at-least-1T figure through 2027 is management demand visibility from GTC, NOT a filing RPO — the filing RPO is only about 2.3–3.2B because hardware ships in weeks (verified, Phase 3.5). The trap is live and shipping: Google TPU, AWS Trainium and the OpenAI–Broadcom programme are real in-sourcing, concentrated in inference, and both earnings-quality flags (HIGH_ACCRUALS partly real, RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN mixed/watch, DSO 45 to 60) are live.

**APH (4).** Record orders of 10.7B at 1.23 book-to-bill, IT datacom at 43% of sales and +89% YoY, in the one component every AI rack needs and nobody can skip, at 24.4x forward earnings for 35.2% growth with cash-backed earnings and no quality flag. It is Panelist D's single pick. The caveat is hyperscaler dual-sourcing and the long-run co-packaged-optics / Nvidia bundling bypass.

**PH (5).** Record backlog of 12.8B total / 8.5B aerospace at 30 Jun 2026 against a 17,000+ aircraft industry queue, with the highest irreplaceability in the field (9/10 — certified, designed-in flight-critical content). The risk is not demand: it is the roughly 11.8B of deal debt from the 9.25B Filtration Group close and the pending 2.55B CIRCOR deal at a full 24.2x multiple.
