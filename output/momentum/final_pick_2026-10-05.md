## THE PICK

**MU — Micron Technology, Inc.** — Semiconductors (DRAM incl. HBM, NAND). Current price **$1,074.90** (screen, 2026-10-05). Market cap ~$1.24T.

## One-paragraph thesis

Micron sits on the deepest structural memory shortage in industry history: HBM supply is fully contracted through calendar 2026, only ~50-66% of core-customer HBM demand can be met into 2027, and customers have signed 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements representing ~$150B of remaining performance obligations at minimum contracted prices — backed by $32B in customer cash commitments, $12.3B of it received in a single quarter. That contracted, deposit-backed revenue de-cyclicalizes the business exactly when the spot price is doing the work: FQ4 FY26 revenue was $54.23B (+379% YoY) at an 87.0% non-GAAP gross margin, and the FQ1 FY27 guide ($61.5B ± $1.5B revenue, $38.15 ± $1.00 EPS) was raised well above consensus. Only three firms on earth make DRAM at scale, and hyperscalers cannot in-source it — the disintermediation trap that kills most explosive bets cannot touch this one.

## Why it's the category leader

DRAM: #3 and closing (Samsung ~39%, SK hynix ~25%, Micron ~24% — TrendForce/Counterpoint Q2 2026), share up ~3pp YoY while SK hynix lost ~13pp. HBM: #3 at ~18% (Counterpoint Q2 2026), qualified on Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform with HBM4 shipping (HBM4E on track for 2027's post-Vera-Rubin platform). NAND: #3-4. It is gaining share at the leading edge — 1-gamma DRAM, HBM4 >11 Gbps.

## Why it's irreplaceable

Only three firms make DRAM at scale (~88% of the market). HBM is qualified, leading-edge technology no hyperscaler can build: custom AI ASICs still need HBM attached, and no substitute routes around memory. The SCA deposit structure adds qualification lock-in — customers prepay billions for capacity priority. Researcher irreplaceability score: 8/10. The concrete threat is CXMT's rising DRAM share (~7-10% in Q2 2026) — the structural supply risk is China, not in-sourcing. **Trap verdict: the disintermediation/in-sourcing trap does not fire — no veto.**
