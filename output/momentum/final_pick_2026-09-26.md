## Why it's the category leader

## Why it's irreplaceable

No fab builds its own process control (fabs literally cannot run without inspection), no hyperscaler competes with KLA, and the ~25 years of accumulated defect data compound into an algorithmic substrate that optics-copying cannot touch.
