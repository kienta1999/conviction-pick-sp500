### One-paragraph thesis

Micron is fulfilling only half to two-thirds of customer HBM demand, has ~$100B of minimum contracted revenue with ~$22B already collected in cash prepayments, and has no meaningful new fab output until fiscal 2028 — the purest "demand the world cannot supply fast enough" print in this panel's dossier. The HBM4 generation lead (shipping >$1B already, ramping ~2x faster than HBM3E) gives pricing power through 2027 even as Samsung and SK Hynix chase it. The explosive case is that the market still prices Micron as a commodity memory cyclical while its revenue has structurally converted to contracted, pre-sold backlog.

### The shortage + backlog evidence

- CEO Sanjay Mehrotra (Q3 FY2026 call, June 24, 2026): entire 2026 HBM supply sold out; Micron "can fill only half to two-thirds of customer demand"; HBM3E and HBM4 fully booked through CY2027, negotiating 2028.
- 16 strategic customer agreements = ~$100B minimum contracted revenue; ~$22B collected as upfront deposits (Q3 FY2026 results).
- Conventional DRAM: "most severe shortage in 15 years" — ~3x wafer intensity of HBM vs DDR5 starving conventional supply; prices +176% YoY expected in 2026 (Goldman).
- Q3 FY2026: record revenue $41.46B (+346% YoY, +74% sequentially), non-GAAP gross margin 84.9%; Q4 guide ~$50B at ~86% GM.
- All nine load-bearing claims CONFIRMED by the independent verifier (verification.md), one precision caveat: NVDA's "70%" is FY2028 growth vs demand, not a fill rate.

### Why it's the category leader

One of the big-3 DRAM/HBM oligopolists (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron); sole US-based producer delivering HBM4 at commercial scale; claims "clear technology leadership" in DRAM; ~65,000 patents.

### Why it's irreplaceable

HBM4 is the most sophisticated memory product to manufacture; 12–18 month qualification and design-in switching costs through product life; ~3x wafer intensity plus advanced-packaging capital intensity beyond any customer's reach. Hyperscalers can in-source a chip design; they cannot build a memory fab. Trap assessment: **low**.
