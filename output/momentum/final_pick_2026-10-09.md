## THE PICK
**KLAC — KLA Corporation.** Information Technology / Semiconductor Capital Equipment (process control). Market cap ~$256.9B (screen, 2026-10-09). Price at pick: $196.83.

## One-paragraph thesis
KLA is the non-displaceable tollbooth of the AI buildout: every new fab, HBM stack, and advanced package must be qualified through its inspection and metrology tools, and its installed base plus recipe libraries make displacement practically impossible. Backlog compounded 60% year-over-year to $12.57B with lead times stretching into 2027-28 — customers are queued because tools cannot be built fast enough, not because demand is pausing. The stock pulled back ~26% over the prior 90 days while the backlog kept compounding, so the entry sits below where it traded three months ago against materially better visibility. Two of four panel lenses nominated it independently (supply-chain and moat), and the trap filter clears it: unlike the hyperscaler-exposed names, no customer can in-source metrology.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- Advanced-packaging process-control revenue approaching $1.1B in 2026, up 70%+ y/y (VERIFIED).

- Record FQ4 revenue $3.66B (+15% y/y), September quarter guided to ~$4.0B.

## Why it's the category leader
Dominant #1 in process control (inspection + metrology) by a wide margin — the industry's yield police. Competitors (AMAT, Onto Innovation, ASML's HMI) compete in niches; none matches the integrated portfolio and installed base. TSMC has been >10% of total revenue three years running.

## Why it's irreplaceable
No hyperscaler or foundry can in-source process control: it requires decades of accumulated optics, algorithms, and fab-by-fab process knowledge. No substitute technology routes around yield management — advanced nodes, HBM, and hybrid bonding all *increase* process-control intensity. Disintermediation risk: LOW. (Irreplaceability 10/10 in the dossier.)
