# Momentum final pick — 2026-10-07

## THE PICK: AVGO — Broadcom Inc.
- **Sector:** Semiconductors (custom AI accelerators/XPUs, AI networking, infrastructure software)

## One-paragraph thesis
Broadcom is the toll collector on hyperscaler AI in-sourcing itself: with ~70% of the custom AI-accelerator co-design market, a ~$179.2B RPO (Q3 FY2026, corrected upward in Phase 3.5 verification), and ~3x book-to-bill on AI semis, it monetizes the very trend the market fears — customers building their own silicon still have to co-design it with Broadcom. The stock sits 23% off its peak on AI-bubble discount at a non-demanding 19.4x forward P/E while AI revenue runs +143% YoY with line of sight past $100B in FY27 — a genuine shortage (multi-gigawatt commitments through 2031) the market is underpricing.

## The shortage + backlog evidence
- **RPO ~$179.2B** at end of Q3 FY2026 (Aug 2, 2026), up from ~$164.6B a quarter earlier (Phase 3.5-verified; the widely cited $110B figure belongs to Q3 FY2025).
- **Book-to-bill ~3x** on AI semiconductors: Q2 FY2026 AI bookings >$30B vs $10.8B shipped.
- **$110B+ consolidated backlog visibility with 4–5+ year horizon:** Google 5-year TPU agreement through 2031 (v8–v11); Anthropic $125.2B five-year TPU lease; OpenAI 10 GW custom-chip partnership targeting 2027 productization.
- **Demand signal:** Q2 FY2026 AI semiconductor revenue +143% YoY to $10.8B; management guides FY2026 AI revenue to ~$56–58B (+180% YoY) with line of sight to >$100B in FY27 on 9–10 GW of committed capacity.
- **Credit-backed demand:** banks syndicating the $42B senior piece of up to $60B total financing for Anthropic's Broadcom-TPU deployments (Bloomberg, Oct 2, 2026) — Anthropic becomes Broadcom's largest XPU customer in 2027.

## Why it's the category leader
~70% of the custom AI-accelerator design-services market (Bloomberg Intelligence; #2 Marvell at 20–25%; together ~95% of co-design). #1 AI networking (Tomahawk 6 at 102.4 Tbps, Jericho 4). Only ~2 firms on earth can co-design at this scale.

## Why it's irreplaceable
Hyperscalers run multi-vendor strategies (Google added Marvell and MediaTek) but none has shown it can go alone — they co-design WITH Broadcom because of fabrication-scale risk. Moats: decade-plus Google TPU relationship (since 2014), vertical bundle (design services + networking makes defection punishing), 70%-share scale economics, 93%-gross-margin VMware cash flows. Disintermediation risk: **medium** — share drifts 70%→~60% (Counterpoint 2027) but the co-design dependency is structural, confirmed by the through-2031 agreement. The panel's moat specialist (Lens C) still ranked it #1.

Bear gets 0.25 on customer/credit concentration and cyclicality — not a default split; if the Anthropic financing cracked, bear would be the most probable scenario and this pick would not exist.
