## Per-name theses

**1. MU — the contracted shortage.** More than 75% of fiscal 2027 output pre-committed and the entire calendar 2026 HBM supply agreed on price and volume, with management guiding tighter conditions in 2027 and 2028 than in 2026 (all verified, 30 Sep 2026 call). Category position: one of only three firms making leading-edge DRAM/HBM at scale — leadership as trio membership, not sole dominance. Moat caveat, stated honestly: hyperscalers cannot fab DRAM, so the product cannot be in-sourced, but Micron is individually substitutable within the trio (Samsung's HBM share jumped 21% → 33% in one quarter). See final_pick_2026-11-04.md for the full thesis and scenarios.

**2. NVDA — the deepest moat with the live trap.** Fiscal 2028 growth of ~70% guided explicitly as a supply-constrained outlook, with demand "much greater" (26 Aug 2026 call, verified); Vera Rubin purchase orders from major hyperscalers, AI clouds and system OEMs (category-level management statement, verified). Category position: ~75–90% AI accelerator share by analyst estimates (not a company figure) with the CUDA ecosystem as the structural moat. Trap caveat, flagged not vetoed in ranked mode: custom ASICs are eroding share, especially in inference, and the dossier carries HIGH_ACCRUALS + RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flags (verdict: watch — Q3 FY2027 print due 17 Nov 2026 is the collection test).

**3. MSFT — the capacity-constrained seller.** Commercial remaining performance obligations of \$677bn at end-September 2026, +73% year over year (company release, 28 Oct 2026, verified), while management states customer demand for Azure continues to exceed available supply (verified). Category position: the enterprise stack + Azure entrenchment no rival replicates end-to-end. Caveat: Microsoft is the in-sourcer in this doctrine's trap framing, not the supplier — its explosive upside is capped by how fast it can build capacity, not by pricing power.

**4. ISRG —** da Vinci placements surging (546 in a quarter) on a 12,094-system installed base with FDA clearance lock-in per indication; irreplaceability 9/10, tempered by third-party instrument remanufacturing and Medtronic Hugo / J&J Ottava approaching scale.

**5. APH —** record orders at a 1.19 book-to-bill as AI interconnect demand outruns shipments; #1 connectors, but multi-sourced (#2 dynamics) and irreplaceability only 5/10.

**6. NUE —** backlogs up 25% to more than doubled, booked into early 2028 with 9–12-week lead times; largest US steelmaker, but a replaceable commodity whose tightness leans heavily on tariffs.

**7. CF —** 98% utilisation, below-average channel inventories, a 1.3 Mt/year plant offline into 2027; a genuine commodity squeeze, not a locked-in annuity. Note: next earnings today, 2026-11-04.

**8. WST —** HVP capacity pulled by GLP-1/biologics volumes with 18–36-month qualification lock-in; no reported backlog, and a 41.3x forward PE plus a RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flag to watch.

**9. ANET —** freshest catalyst in the field (Q3 2026 beat 3 Nov 2026, FY2026 guide raised to ~\$13.3bn, first 2027 guide ~\$18.5bn); share-and-execution story, with Nvidia Spectrum-X bundling as the #1 competitive threat.

**10. LLY —** incretin volume compounder with peptide-scale moat (irreplaceability 7/10); the acute shortage phase has passed (FDA shortage resolution), volume growth comes at falling prices, and the stock sits at its 52-week high with only +2.3% analyst upside.
