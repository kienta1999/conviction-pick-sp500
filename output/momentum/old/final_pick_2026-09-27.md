# Momentum conviction pick — 2026-09-27 (RUN_DATE)

**THE PICK:** KLAC — KLA Corporation. Technology / semiconductor equipment. ~$244B market cap (screen, 2026-09-26).

**One-paragraph thesis.** KLA owns the most monopolistic franchise in semiconductor equipment — ~74% of the global metrology/inspection market per industry press (KLA's own Investor Day frames ~58% of process control; definition-dependent) — with a backlog "just short of $13 billion" (verified: $12.57B in the early-August 2026 10-K, CEO Wallace confirmed he expects it to keep growing at the Sept 10, 2026 Citi TMT conference). WFE demand currently exceeds what the industry can physically ship ("just as fast as we can go as an industry" — CEO Wallace, April 29, 2026 earnings call), so 2H CY2026 revenue is rising ~20% vs 1H purely on improving supply availability — demand was waiting on supply. Shares corrected ~40% from the July peak ($307.37 52-week high, screen baseline $187.11), reopening the entry on a monopoly with 52 consecutive quarters of Y/Y services growth and a 24%-of-revenue recurring floor.

## The shortage + backlog evidence
- Backlog "just short of $13B" (10-K, early Aug 2026; verified $12.57B), management: "I expect that backlog to continue to grow" (Citi TMT, 9/10/2026).
- Advanced-packaging process-control revenue ~$1.1B, +70% Y/Y — HBM and hybrid-bonding capacity builds demand inspection that cannot be deferred (verified).
- 2H CY2026 revenue guided ~20% above 1H "supported by improving supply availability" — i.e., demand was waiting on supply (CFO Higgins, verified).
- CY2027 WFE management flagged at "low-20%, maybe higher" — a $200–225B cycle the panel called "the strongest equipment cycle ever contemplated."

## Why it's the category leader
~74% metrology/inspection share (~80% in patterned-wafer and reticle inspection), up from ~50% in 2010; algorithmic moat from ~25 years of cross-customer defect-classification data; fabs tune recipes layer-by-layer on KLA baselines — switching vendors requires re-baselining entire processes.

## Why it's irreplaceable
Irreplaceability 10/10 (dossier's highest). KLA's customers are foundries, not hyperscalers — hyperscalers have zero path to in-source process control. Competitors (Onto, Camtek, AMAT inspection) compete only in narrow niches. Fabs literally "cannot run without" this equipment category.
