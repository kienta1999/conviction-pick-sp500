**5. FCX** — The purest structural-shortage equity: a genuine 2026 copper deficit amplified by its own Grasberg outage, geology + permitting as the moat (9/10 irreplaceable). Thesis is the commodity, not the company; execution risk at Grasberg is the swing factor.
