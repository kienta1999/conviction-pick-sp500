## Per-name thesis (top 5)

**1. APH ($76.03).** The cleanest non-memory shortage evidence in the field: record Q2 2026 orders of $10.7B (+94% YoY) at 1.23x book-to-bill for a second straight quarter above 1.20x, with IT datacom +89% YoY at ~43% of sales, virtually all AI-driven — all verified from Amphenol's 2026-07-29 release/call. Category position is #2 globally in connectors behind TE Connectivity (Bishop's 2024 ranking, date-stamped per verification). The moat is co-design/qualification lock-in and architecture neutrality across copper vs optical; the caveat is concentration — the order book is a leveraged bet on hyperscaler capex continuing.

**2. MU ($912.27).** The shortage is contracted: ~$150B RPO under 26 take-or-pay SCAs (priced-framework subset at minimum pricing, per verification), ~$32B customer commitments, >75% of 2027 output committed, vast majority of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply contracted, from one of only three HBM makers. Also today's single pick. Caveat: 87% peak gross margin and #3 HBM share — cycle and share risk, not in-sourcing risk.

**3. VRTX ($513.31).** A legally enforced monopoly: sole commercial supplier of disease-modifying CFTR modulators, ~95% of US CF patients eligible after the 2026-04-01 label expansion (verified); Trikafta patents through 2037, Alyftrek to 2039 (corrected per verification). The dated catalyst is povetacicept: 52.0% proteinuria reduction from baseline (49.8% vs placebo) in RAINIER, PDUFA 2026-11-30 — both verified. Caveat: 96% of sales from one disease, and its EV is below the guardrail (flagged below).

**4. NVDA ($216.32).** Demonstrated shortage — cloud GPUs sold out, Q2 FY27 revenue $96.2B, Q3 guided $108B excl. China — on CUDA/NVLink lock-in for frontier training. The honest caveats from research: formal RPO is only ~$3.2B (the $500B/$1T figures are visibility framing, not backlog), and the earnings-quality flags were explained as partly benign composition (non-cash investment gain, Rubin inventory) but with genuinely deteriorated cash conversion and deliberate vendor financing (AR $40.7B → $63.1B, DSO 45 → 60) — a concentration/financing risk, not fictitious revenue.

**5. LLY ($1275.80).** Clear #1 in incretins (US 60.9% vs Novo 38.8%), revenue still capacity-gated (1.6x H1 doses, >$50B manufacturing committed), Q2 revenue +48% on volume +60% / price -13%, FY26 guide raised to $85–87B. Caveat is price: only +4.4% to the mean target into the 2026-10-29 print, with tirzepatide ~65% of revenue.

One line each for the rest: SNDK — purest NAND torque, #5 share caps it; META — shortage score 4 by construction, it is the demand side; CSCO — cheapest AI beneficiary at 19.5x with a real RPO, #3 where the growth is; WST — genuine Annex 1 shortage, fully priced at 39.4x; ISRG — deepest medtech lock-in, no margin of safety at 36.5x.
