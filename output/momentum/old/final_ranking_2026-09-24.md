## Per-name thesis

**1. AMAT (Applied Materials, $474.38).** The AI buildout's binding constraint migrated from foundry capacity to equipment supply: TrendForce (2026-09-18, independently verified) reports major-equipment lead times stretched 1.5–2x, deposition-tool parts 4→10 months, some components to 40 months. That converts into pricing power through fab qualification lock-in — no fab swaps a tool at the risk of yield on a $20B facility — while the GAA transition multiplies AMAT's process steps per wafer. Q3 FY2026 was a record ($9.12B, +25% YoY) with the CY2026 WFE outlook raised to >30% growth; the stock trades 34% below its high while a September analyst PT wave (UBS $695, TD Cowen $700, Susquehanna $900) and a fresh $5B India plan supply re-rating fuel. Hyperscalers cannot in-source WFE — the disintermediation trap can't touch it.

**2. GOOGL (Alphabet, $337.83).** Google Cloud RPO of $514B (+$52B sequentially, verified; the dossier's ~$490B prior-quarter baseline was wrong — actual Q1 was $462B, non-thesis-breaking) with Cloud revenue +82% YoY to $24.8B. Alphabet is literally leasing third-party capacity while it builds its own — demand exceeds deliverable supply. The ad-tech antitrust ruling avoided a forced breakup (2026-09-02), and a September PT wave (Evercore $450, Tigress $485) followed. Caveats: negative Q2 FCF (−$5.9B, first since listing), ~45% capex intensity, and the dossier's Mizuho $61B TPU-sales figure is unconfirmed.

**3. MSFT (Microsoft, $500.59).** Commercial RPO $678B, +84% YoY (verified; +25% ex-OpenAI), with Azure at 43% growth and the CFO on record that it would have been "over 40%" in FQ2 *if GPUs had existed*. The binding constraint is now power, not silicon — Nadella: "you may actually have a bunch of chips sitting in inventory that I can't plug in" — and Microsoft is locking 2.67 GW of behind-the-meter gas power for a West Texas AI center. The definitive OpenAI agreement (~2026-09-22, ~$250B of Azure services) removed the partnership overhang. Ret12m is −0.02: flat stock against the biggest verified backlog in the slate.

**4. TRAP FLAG: the cycle peak — Citi expects memory price increases to decelerate and peak in 2Q 2027; stock up ~700% in 12 months, so the shortage is substantially priced.

**5. APH (Amphenol, $82.19).** One-line: record orders at a 1.23 book-to-bill on AI-interconnect demand; picks-and-shovels on every rack build without GPU-hype multiple. Earnings 2026-10-28.
