## Per-name thesis

**1. MU — Micron.** The panel's conviction name (3 of 4 top picks). $100B of take-or-pay floors plus $22B of customer deposits through 2030 is the hardest contracted demand in the set, against a physical HBM sellout into 2027 — and the stock still trades at 6.5x forward earnings because the market prices a memory cyclical, not a contracted-backlog compounder. The Sep 30 print is the near-term binary. See `final_pick_2026-09-29.md` for the full writeup.

**2. LRCX — Lam Research.** The moat lens's top pick and the panel's conscience: "fundamentally sold out" with customers prepaying ($2.43B deferred revenue) for machines that don't exist yet, and structurally zero in-sourcing risk — the only name where both the shortage and the moat score 9/10. The debate is cycle timing, not competition: record 52% gross margins and a $150B+ WFE year are the classic peak shape, and 2027's "extraordinary setup" has to deliver.

**3. MSFT — Microsoft.** The purest demand-greater-than-supply in the index: $678B commercial backlog, Azure +43-45% while still capacity-short (constrained at least through 2026, corrected per verification — not end-FY27 as the dossier first stated), and $175B of capex that still isn't enough. The risk is the mirror image: if AI monetization disappoints, the shortage narrative inverts to overbuild, and the stock's range-bound action shows the market is already asking the ROI question.

**4. AMAT — Applied Materials.** The contrarian's runner-up: sold off ~32% from its high on 2027-deceleration fears while selling irreplaceable shovels (deposition/epitaxy/CMP/implant leadership) into the strongest WFE cycle ever, with zero in-sourcing risk. The 2026 +30% comp makes 2027 the question — SEMI sees 7.6% total market growth.

**5. SNDK — SanDisk.** The purest NAND leverage: $93.9B of NBM floors with $16.5B of deposits, bits on allocation past 2027, a shortage the diversified giants created by reallocating to HBM. But it is a commodity #5 vendor (~11% share), -35% from its June peak with the NAND price curve decelerating — the moat lens rejected it outright as first-to-break on any turn.

**6. APH — Amphenol.** The cleanest structural-shortage compounder outside memory: record $10.7B orders, 1.23 book-to-bill, a $10.5-11B/yr AI interconnect run-rate with rising content per rack, and genuine qualification lock-in (8/10 irreplaceability). The only debate is the ~40x multiple with 43% of sales tied to AI datacom.

**7. NVDA — Nvidia.** Real shortage ("cloud GPUs sold out," supply-capped ~70% FY28 guide) with the clearest disintermediation threat in the set: hyperscaler ASICs already run most frontier inference, and Nvidia is financing its own demand (extended terms, lease guarantees — the earnings-quality yellow flag). Training is locked; inference is leaking.

**8. GOOGL — Alphabet.** The demand-side anchor of the whole thesis: Cloud +82% YoY, $514B RPO converting over 24 months, TPUs as the only credible in-house Nvidia alternative. The bet is execution and ROI on $200B/yr capex — Q2 FCF went negative for the first time since 2004.

**9. ANET — Arista.** A genuine networking bottleneck ($3.5B AI fabrics target, $6.2B deferred revenue) with a capped moat: Nvidia has overtaken it in datacenter Ethernet (21.5% vs 20.7%), hyperscalers buy white-box/SONiC, and there is no scale-up revenue until ~2028.

**10. FCX — Freeport.** Maximum leverage to the hardest physical shortage (UBS 2026 copper deficit ~407kt, LME at records) — and a pure commodity equity, so the shortage cuts both ways. +1% analyst upside is the definition of fully priced; the contrarian ranked it 10th reluctantly.

## Risk lens & leverage-safety note

Sector concentration is extreme: 6 of 10 names are AI-compute/memory/semiconductor-equipment — a single AI-capex pause hits the whole list at once. The steadier compounders (MSFT, GOOGL, APH) and the commodity hedge (FCX) are the ballast; the memory pair (MU, SNDK) is the high-beta torque. Memory can draw down 50%+ fast when the cycle turns — brutal with margin. Education, not advice: never a specific leverage multiple; any leveraged exposure here must survive a move to the bear targets above.
