## Ticker dossiers

### SNDK — shortage 8, irreplaceability 8
NAND in structural AI-driven shortage: data centers >50% of NAND market (CEO, Goldman Sachs Sept 9, 2026); enterprise SSD demand +43% in 2026E (MS/TrendForce). "New Business Model": 5 agreements covering >1/3 of FY27 bit supply with >$11B enforceable guarantees; 3 Q3 deals alone = $42B minimum revenue backlog. #3 global NAND. Only ~5-6 firms make leading-edge NAND; hyperscaler qualification >1yr = lock-in. Risks: stock ran ~5,000% since spin — the explosion is priced in; NAND's historic cyclicality; Edge segment unit declines. Catalysts: S&P 100 inclusion Sept 21; $14B buyback; Q4 FY26 +372% YoY rev.

### MU — shortage 9, irreplaceability 9
Worst memory crisis in 15 years (IDC, Feb 2026): customers get 55–60% of asks; DRAM prices +400% since 2024 (JPM); HBM uses ~3x fab capacity per bit of DDR5. 16 LTAs covering ~20% DRAM bits / ~30% NAND bits tied to ~$22B customer cash deposits (FQ3 call, June 2026). FQ3: record $41.5B rev (+346%), 84.9% gross margin, record FCF $18.3B. Only 3 firms make leading-edge DRAM. Earnings TODAY: Street at/above guidance high end ($50B±1B / $31±1 EPS) — sentiment event on guidance + possible buyback. RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flag judged benign (price-spike timing artifact; OCF $25.4B kept pace). Risk: the bar is already high; memory's historic cyclicality.

### LLY — shortage 6, irreplaceability 8
GLP-1 demand outruns supply; $55B manufacturing buildout since 2020; Lebanon API site doubled to $9B, production late 2026. Q2: $22.97B +48%, EPS $8.38 +33%; raised FY26 guide to $85–87B. Medicare GLP-1 Bridge (July 1, 2026) + orals expand access. BUT: realized prices -13% in Q2 — it's a volume-vs-price race, not a true supply squeeze. Two products ~65% of revenue.

### ANET — shortage 4, irreplaceability 6
No structural shortage in switches — share fight in a booming market. #1 branded DC Ethernet (IDC Q1 2026: NVDA 21.5% vs ANET 20.7% — Nvidia briefly ahead via Spectrum-X GPU-bundle attach). Deferred revenue +130% to $5.4B; AI revenue guide ≥$3.6B for 2026; FY26 total guide raised to $12.6B. Moat = EOS software stack, but in-sourcing threat is REAL (white-box, Spectrum-X, custom). ~42% revenue from two hyperscalers.

### NVDA — shortage 8, irreplaceability 7
Blackwell sold out through mid-2026 (~3.6M-unit backlog, est.); Q3 FY27 guide $108B±2%; growth production-gated (HBM3e + TSMC CoWoS, 36–52wk lead times). Moat = CUDA + NVLink + annual cadence (Rubin shipping). THE TRAP IS LIVE: hyperscaler custom ASICs +44.6% YoY vs +16.1% merchant GPUs; custom silicon could reach 45% of AI chip market by 2028; >50% of hyperscaler internal inference already on custom ASICs. Flags (HIGH_ACCRUALS, RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN) judged benign business-model artifacts. Risk: customer concentration (top 2 = 36% rev); $5.4T market cap leaves no room for capex digestion.

### LRCX — shortage 8, irreplaceability 9
#1 etch (~30% share, >80% advanced-node); 2026 WFE view raised to low-$150Bs; cleanroom capacity is the pacing item; BofA sees $210B in 2027. Physics-gated moat (atomic etch precision for GAA/HBM); qualification lock-in; no hyperscaler can in-source fab tools. Flags judged benign (acceptance-based revenue recognition). Risk: most cyclical of the batch; 54x P/E; SEMI sees equipment growth decelerating to 7.6% in 2027.
