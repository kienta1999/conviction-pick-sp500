# Momentum final pick — 2026-10-26

## THE PICK: MU — Micron Technology

Technology / Semiconductors. Frozen screen price $912.27. Market cap $992.9B. Forward PE 4.41, analyst mean target $1561.91 (+71.2%).

## Thesis

Micron remains the doctrine's founding pattern: a structural HBM/DRAM/NAND shortage that customers have converted from spot exposure into contracted commitments. Roughly $150B of remaining performance obligations sit under 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements — verified in substance, with the definition caveat that the $150B is the priced-framework subset at minimum pricing, not total SCA value — backed by ~$32B of customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits. The vast majority of calendar-2027 HBM bit supply is contracted (the primary wording; secondary shorthand says "sold out"). Every custom ASIC a hyperscaler builds to in-source compute still needs HBM from one of only three makers on earth — the disintermediation trap points elsewhere.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- ~$150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs, ~$32B customer commitments, >75% of 2027 output committed across SCA and non-SCA customers — fiscal Q4 2026 disclosure, 30 Sep 2026, verified in Phase 3.5 with the definition caveats above.
- HBM shortfalls estimated by JPMorgan (in dossier) at ~20% (2026), 19% (2027), 16% (2028); Q1 FY2027 guided to $61.5B ± $1.5B revenue — verified.
- Screen: revenue growth +156% TTM, operating margin 81%, forward PE 4.41 at $912.27.

## Why it's the category leader

One of three leading-edge memory makers on earth (with SK hynix and Samsung); #3 in DRAM/HBM with a custom HBM4E co-design with Nvidia — design-in lock-in on top of process-tech scarcity.

## Why it's irreplaceable

No hyperscaler operates a memory fab and none can build one inside the horizon ($20B+, 3–5 years, deep process IP). The honest weakness is share, not in-sourcing: Micron is #3 in HBM (~18–21%) and customers can shift share to the other two makers at each qualification; CXMT presses conventional DRAM. Panel rated the trap medium (A, B) — no lens rated it high.
