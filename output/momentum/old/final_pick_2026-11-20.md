# Momentum pick — Amphenol (APH) — 2026-11-20

## THE PICK

**Amphenol (APH)** — Amphenol Corporation, Information Technology / Electronic Components. Screen price $75.61 (2026-11-19 close). Market cap ~$188.6B. Forward P/E 20.9. Analyst mean target $106.97 (+41.5%). Next earnings 2027-01-27.

## One-paragraph thesis

Amphenol is the cleanest shortage signal in today's field: its customers are ordering interconnect faster than it can ship it — record Q3 orders of $12.17B, +99% year over year, a 1.19:1 book-to-bill for the fourth straight quarter above 1.19 — because at 224 Gb/s per lane the AI datacentre buildout has moved the bottleneck from compute to the physical cable. IT Datacom is now 48% of sales and ~75% of that is AI, so this is a concentrated AI-cycle bet wearing a compounder's clothes, but the moat is real: high-speed interconnect is co-designed and qualified into rack architectures with the chip vendor and the hyperscaler, and only a short list of firms can do it at hyperscale volume. Three of four lenses picked it outright; the fourth ranked it 6th only on moat purity, not on the shortage.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- **Orders:** Q3 2026 orders $12.17B, +99% YoY and +13% sequentially — a record (company release, 28 Oct 2026). Book-to-bill 1.19:1 in Q3 (1.23:1 in Q2, 1.24:1 in Q1, 1.31:1 in Q4 2025) — orders exceeded sales by ~$2.0B in the quarter.
- **Sales:** Q3 2026 sales $10.23B, +65% YoY (+37% organically); IT Datacom sales +117% YoY (+77% organic) — the +117% is total IT Datacom growth, not AI-only revenue (corrected per Phase 3.5 verification; AI is ~75% of IT Datacom sales).
- **Capacity signal:** orders reflect customers "reserving capacity well into next year"; fibre preform/fibre production capacity "has not kept pace with demand, contributing to higher prices and longer lead times since mid-2025", with new preform plants taking up to two years to build (Nov 2026 market report).
- **No aggregate backlog disclosed:** Amphenol's 10-Q declines to disclose a backlog total (a substantial portion of remaining performance obligations is expected within three months). The order book is the evidence, not a backlog number.
- **Guidance:** Q4 sales $10.3–10.5B, adjusted EPS $0.81–0.83; FY2026 sales $36.9–37.1B and adjusted EPS $2.82–2.84 vs consensus $35.66B / $2.69 — a ~60% jump on 2025's $23.1B.

## Why it's the category leader

#1 in AI datacom interconnect by the estimates available: ~33% market share in the AI datacom segment (AInvest, 2025 analysis, repeated Jan 2026) — a single secondary estimate with no primary-source corroboration (Phase 3.5: UNVERIFIED; treat as an estimate, not established fact). In the broader fibre-optic connector market Amphenol led with over 12.5% share in 2025 (Global Market Insights), ahead of Corning, TE Connectivity, Molex and 3M. In datacentre fibre cabling/connectivity, Corning ranked first in 2025, followed by Fujikura, then Amphenol — so Amphenol is #1 in AI datacom connectors specifically, not in all fibre. Main rivals: TE Connectivity (strongest in 224G/lane copper) and Molex (Koch-owned, deep co-design with Microsoft and Meta).

## Why it's irreplaceable

The moat is co-development/qualification lock-in. High-speed interconnect is designed into rack/shelf architectures with the chip vendor and hyperscaler, then qualified there; a failed link can take down an entire training run, so "nobody chases the cheapest cable". Only a short list of firms — Amphenol, TE Connectivity, Molex — have decades of signal-integrity process knowledge at 224G. Amphenol is also architecture-neutral: it sells high-speed copper, power, active copper cable, passive fibre and active optics, so it wins whether the rack goes copper or optical; the CommScope CCS acquisition (closed 9 Jan 2026) added the fibre capability it previously lacked. Honest caveat: hyperscalers qualify multiple suppliers for every next-gen architecture (TE is the live second source), and co-packaged optics / silicon photonics could over time reduce discrete connector content per rack. There is no software lock-in comparable to EOS or M365. Irreplaceability 7/10; disintermediation risk **medium** — a determined hyperscaler can dual-source around it, but no customer can in-source 224G qualification itself, and no substitute bypasses the need for the interconnect.
