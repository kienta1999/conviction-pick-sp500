## Per-name thesis

**1. MSFT — Microsoft.** The biggest backlog in the set and a management-admitted shortage: commercial RPO \$677bn (+73% YoY, 2.3-year duration, ~\$203bn recognised in the next 12 months), Azure \$33.165bn (+48% YoY, the first dollar print), and CFO Amy Hood stating on the Oct 28 call that "customer demand continues to exceed available supply" — with some third-party GPU-as-a-service contracts slipping to Jan–Jun 2027 on delayed capacity deliveries. The moat is the enterprise stack (M365/Entra/Windows, >430m Microsoft 365 users) plus capital intensity only three firms can match (capex \$45.2bn in Q1 FY27 alone). The honest caveat: RPO was flat sequentially (\$678bn → \$677bn), Google Cloud is gaining share fastest (16%, +3pp YoY), the Nov 12–13 global M365 outage (20+ hours, ~7m Downdetector reports) damaged the reliability narrative days before Ignite, and the OpenAI concentration inside RPO is undisclosed. **EV guardrail flag: MSFT's EV upside is +11.8%, below the +15% guardrail** — it stays #1 in the ranking, but it is not actionable as a single pick on these numbers.

**2. GE — GE Aerospace.** The panel's single pick (see `final_pick_2026-11-15.md`): the only 9/10 shortage + 9/10 irreplaceability name in the set. RPO \$180.4bn, Q3 orders \$19.0bn (+49%), LEAP-1B sole-source on every 737 MAX, the P&W GTF crisis grounding ~650 rival aircraft — and GE is buying the casting bottleneck itself with the \$11.75bn acquisition of Consolidated Precision Products (close H2 2027). The honest caveat: ~35.6x forward earnings already price years of aftermarket growth, and the stock fell ~3.7% on Oct 20 despite the beat. **EV upside +15.4% — barely clears the guardrail.**

**3. APH — Amphenol.** The cleanest shortage print in the set: record Q3 orders \$12.17bn (+99% YoY), book-to-bill 1.19, sales \$10.23bn (+65%, +37% organic), IT datacom 48% of sales with AI roughly three-quarters of it — at 21.6x forward earnings with the widest analyst upside gap in the set (+36.7%). The honest caveat, and the reason it is #3 not #1: irreplaceability is only 5/10 — a standards-based, multi-source business where TE, Molex/Koch, Foxconn and Luxshare make equivalents, hyperscalers dual-source deliberately, total backlog \$ and booked-out horizon were not disclosed, and the backlog is cancellable. **EV upside +28.5% — clears the guardrail comfortably.** Note: the "40+ week lead times" figure cited in yesterday's run could NOT be verified in web search on 2026-11-15 and is not cited here; CEO Adam Norwitt sold 799,576 shares on Nov 2, 2026 at ~\$82.48 (~\$65.9m), reported Nov 13–14.

**4. ## Thesis-break / trap triggers to watch

- **Backlogs shrinking:** any top-10 name whose orders or RPO decline year-on-year — the backlog is the thesis.
- **Pricing rolling over:** CF urea/ammonia prices falling (already softening), MSFT cloud gross margin falling on Azure mix, ISRG instrument prices being cut.
- **Margins peaking QoQ:** APH/ANET gross margin compression on memory/silicon cost inflation; GE services margin on the casting bottleneck.
- **A customer announcing in-sourcing:** Nvidia shipping more Spectrum-X (ANET), hyperscalers shifting to Kyber 2028 (APH), Google TPUs displacing Azure share (MSFT), or a major airline ordering GTF-powered aircraft again (GE).
- **The trap firing:** any sign the moat is breached — Arista losing another share point in datacenter Ethernet, Amphenol losing a hyperscaler qualification, or a credible LEAP alternative emerging.
