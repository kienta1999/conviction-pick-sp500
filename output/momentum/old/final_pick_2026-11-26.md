## Why it's irreplaceable — and the honest disintermediation caveat

The moat is the patented dual GIP/GLP-1 molecule (SURMOUNT-5: −20.2% at 72 wks), the \$50bn+ purpose-built capacity, and label depth (diabetes, obesity, OSA; Mounjaro CV-risk approval Oct 2026). No customer can in-source a patented molecule, so the classic disintermediation trap does **not** fire here — the trap veto is not what blocks this pick. The honest caveat is substitution, not in-sourcing: oral semaglutide, CagriSema (FDA decision Q4 2026), Amgen's MariTide, and Chinese incretins can all contest the category, and the panel rates LLY's disintermediation risk **medium**. The trap filter therefore does not veto LLY; the EV guardrail is what blocks it.
