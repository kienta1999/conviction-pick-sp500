## Per-name thesis (top 5)

**MU — Micron.** The textbook explosive-return bet: ~$150B of contracted revenue across 26 take-or-pay agreements, $32B in customer cash deposits, HBM sold out through 2027 at higher prices, and only three firms on earth can make HBM. The moat is capital intensity + qualification lock-in no customer can replicate. The disintermediation risk reads LOW — customers pay Micron to hold their place in line. See final_pick_2026-10-02.md for the full scenario build (base $1,550 / bull $2,640 / bear-stop $640; EV +59% vs price).

**FCX — Freeport-McMoRan.** The purest equity lever on a genuine structural copper deficit (407 kt UBS / ~330 kt JPM for 2026, mine supply +1.4%, electrification demand compounding). Irreplaceability 9: you cannot in-source a copper mine; Grasberg is the world's 2nd-largest copper mine, and the restart + Indonesian permit extension is the double catalyst. Caveat flagged: EV upside is only +11.6% (base $72, bull $126, bear $50; bear 25% / base 55% / bull 20%) — below the +15% single-pick guardrail, so it ranks but would not qualify as an actionable single pick. Price $69.28 sits at base, so the market already prices the deficit — the upside needs the permit/restart news, not just the deficit thesis.

**SNDK — SanDisk.** The purest listed NAND play on the AI storage shortage: $93.9B minimum expected revenue at floor pricing across 8–10 multi-year contracts (>4-year average duration), backed by $16.5B in customer financial guarantees; RPO $59.8B + $31.3B in post-quarter signings. Datacenter bit share went from 12% to 38% of portfolio in FY26; NAND bits on allocation well beyond 2027. Scenarios: bear $1,100 (pricing rolls) / base $2,400 (contracts convert at ~80% gross margin) / bull $3,800 (shortage extends to 2028); bear 25% / base 50% / bull 25% → EV $2,425, +35.6% vs $1,787.69, clears the guardrail. Market price sits between bear and base — partially priced. Disintermediation: LOW — NAND fabs are as un-replicable as DRAM fabs.

**CAT — Caterpillar.** The skeptic's pick: $72B backlog, prime-power generation (gas turbines/gensets via Solar Turbines) sold out into 2029 because hyperscalers cannot wait for the grid. Irreplaceability 8: no hyperscaler builds reciprocating engines or turbines; EPA Tier 4 certification + dealer/service network lock in the aftermarket (~25% of revenue). This is the name the market fears as cyclical — the contrarian entry. Scenario note: the price $826.35 with fwd P/E 25.5 is a cyclical-premium multiple, the one honest weakness.

**KEYS — Keysight.** Not a capacity shortage — a validation-bandwidth shortage: orders structurally lead revenue three quarters running, record backlog, all-time-high pipeline. The DOJ-quantified 85% share of the electronic-design automation-adjacent test market gives it duopoly pricing power. Steadier compounder than an explosive bet.

One tight line for the rest: **CDNS** — EDA duopoly toll booth on every AI chip, $8.1B backlog, irreplaceability 9 (AI chip design *raises* EDA spend); **APH** — AI interconnect design-win leader at 1.23 book-to-bill, record orders; **ANET** — real two-year supply shortfall, but Nvidia Spectrum-X's jump to 11.6% DC share is the visible disintermediation FLAG; **LRCX** — #1 etch into the memory capex supercycle, consensus mainstay (5/5 runs); **EMR** — behind-the-meter power equipment for datacenters, Ovation orders surging, 65% sticky MRO.
