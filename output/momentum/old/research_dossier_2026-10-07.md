## Per-name detail

### SNDK — Sandisk (NAND flash pure-play, spun off WDC Feb 2025)
- **Shortage:** AI-datacenter NAND shortage — DC bit mix >50% (from 12% a year ago); FQ4 FY26 revenue $8.97B, ~2/3 from pricing. Gartner: global NAND revenue +372% in 2026. TrendForce 9/30/26: NAND contract prices +15–20% Q4, pace decelerating.
- **Backlog:** 8 NBM supply agreements (3 US hyperscalers), $93.9B min contract value ($91.1B to deliver), $16.5B financial guarantees, ~$400M prepayments; covers ~67% of FY2028 bits; "4+ years of demand visibility" (CEO Goeckeler).
- **Category:** ~11% NAND share (behind Samsung, SK Hynix, MU, ~Kioxia) — not #1; only pure-play NAND listed co; UltraQLC 256TB shipping.
- **Irreplaceability 7:** handful of firms make 3D NAND at scale; hyperscalers can't in-source fabs; supply certainty is the moat. Threats: YMTC capacity, HDD for cold storage.
- **Catalysts:** 7th straight beat (EPS ~$39.25 vs ~$33.3); FQ1 FY27 guide $10.3–10.8B; $14B buyback; S&P 100 inclusion 9/21/26; analyst targets to $2,900 (Cantor).
- **Risks:** peak-cycle margins (84.6% non-GAAP GM unsustainable historically); +620–820% YTD; edge segment unit declines; uncommitted 1/3 of FY28 bits.
- **Flags:** none.

### MU — Micron Technology (DRAM + NAND + HBM)

- **Category:** #3 DRAM globally; ONLY major US-headquartered HBM supplier.
- **Irreplaceability 8:** 3 firms make leading-edge DRAM/HBM; hyperscalers prepay ($32B) — opposite of disintermediation. Threats: CXMT/YMTC 2027+; Korean duopoly.
- **Catalysts:** FQ4 beat (rev $54.23B vs ~$51B; EPS $33.42 vs ~$31.5); FQ1 FY27 guide well above consensus; $150B RPO disclosure; >$50B FY27 capex; HBM4 ramp; custom HBM4E for NVDA.

- **Flags:** RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN — benign (late-quarter shipments + $12.3B deposits outside receivables; FQ4 OCF ~$44B).
