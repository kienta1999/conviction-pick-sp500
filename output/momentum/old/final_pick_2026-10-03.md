# Momentum conviction pick — 2026-10-03

**THE PICK: MU — Micron Technology** (Semiconductors, US; market cap ~\$1.24T as of 2026-10-02 close; entry price \$1,097.39).

## One-paragraph thesis

Micron is one of only three companies on earth that can make DRAM and HBM, and it is riding the broadest memory shortage on record — one its own CEO says will be "much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028 than in 2026." Demand is not forecasted, it is contracted: 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements cover >35% of revenue through 2030 with ~\$150B of remaining performance obligations (exceeding an entire year of record FY2026 revenue) and \$12.7B of customer cash already deposited. Customers are prepaying to reserve supply into 2031 because hyperscalers cannot build what Micron sells. Three of four panelists nominated it (conviction 8 ×3), and the independent verification confirmed all 11 load-bearing claims.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- ~\$150B take-or-pay RPO from 26 Strategic Customer Agreements covering >35% of revenue through 2030 — exceeding FY2026's entire \$133.2B revenue (confirmed vs company press release / call).
- \$12.7B of customer cash deposits on the balance sheet in Q4 FY2026 alone (sources vary \$12.3–12.7B — not a contradiction, noted).
- >75% of FY2027 planned output already committed; FQ1 FY27 guide: revenue \$61.5B ± \$1.5B vs \$56.6B consensus, EPS \$38.15 ± \$1.00 vs \$35.07 consensus (beat-and-raise, 2026-09-30).
- Goldman: DRAM 5.9% undersupplied in 2027, worst since 2017. Mehrotra: "no line of sight to when supply and demand will return to balance."
- Conventional DRAM contracts roughly tripled during 2026; gross margin went 39.0% → 84.9% in four quarters.

## Why it's the category leader

One of three DRAM makers on earth (Samsung, SK hynix, Micron — ~90% DRAM share per independent sources); the only US-headquartered memory supermajor; custom NV-HBM4E deal with NVIDIA inside the hardest-to-make product; HBM sold out for 2026 and 2027 contracted at much higher prices; SK hynix says demand exceeds supply past 2030, Samsung is short through 2028.

## Why it's irreplaceable

Disintermediation risk: **LOW**. No hyperscaler can in-source DRAM/HBM — ~\$50B+ in 300mm memory-fab equipment capex in 2026 alone, years of yield learning, multi-year hyperscaler qualification cycles, and HBM stacking's 19 extra materials-engineering steps. Even NVIDIA — the industry's canonical in-sourcer — buys custom HBM4E from Micron rather than making it. Take-or-pay contracts with cash deposits bind customers to the counterparty. Medium-term dilution only: CXMT's DRAM share doubled to ~10%, pricing-disciplined rather than predatory so far. Research irreplaceability score: 8/10.
