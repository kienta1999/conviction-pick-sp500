## Per-name thesis

**1. WST — West Pharmaceutical (\$392.48).** The purest shortage thesis in the field: high-value injectable components grew 20% organically in Q3 2026, GLP-1 HVP revenue grew "well into the 30% range" organically (just under 12% of revenue), and management is starting another round of HVP finishing-capacity investment in Europe and the US that takes 18–24 months to contribute — demand today exceeds qualified finishing capacity, and no competitor can qualify faster because the component is specified in the drug's FDA filing. Category leader at ~2.5x its nearest listed pure-play rival (Stevanato). Moat: regulatory qualification lock-in — a stopper worth cents on a drug worth thousands; Lilly and Novo cannot in-source it. Watch item: receivables \$748.2m at Sep 30, 2026 vs \$574.4m at Dec 31, 2025 (+30.3% in nine months) while nine-month FCF fell ~5% — management attributes it to shipment timing and expects Q4 improvement; re-test on Q4. West Vantage contract manufacturing is in a trough (−6.6% organic).

**2. MSI — Motorola Solutions (\$517.01).** Record ending backlog \$16.6bn at Q3 2026, +\$2.1bn / +14% YoY, ~1.27x guided 2026 revenue, built on record Q3 orders up >20% across all three technologies. Silvus (defence/drone MANET) is the surge driver — lead times were 20–22 weeks, now 12–14 weeks after capacity doubling; expected >\$900m revenue in 2026, +30% in 2027. Category leader: #1 in mission-critical LMR (28.4% North America, 24.5% global). Moat: standards-based, spectrum-licensed, life-safety systems with decade-long switching cycles; the D-Series refresh is a 5–7-year opportunity. Flag: INVENTORY_BUILD judged benign by research (backlog- and memory-pre-buy-driven, OCF still rising). Risk: defence demand is geopolitically cyclical; memory spend tripling then doubling again is a margin headwind.

**3. WAB — Wabtec (\$278.48).** Total multi-year backlog \$31.44bn at 30 Sep 2026, +22.9% YoY — about 2.5x guided FY2026 revenue — with 24–36 months of coverage on long-lead locomotive programmes (both verified CONFIRMED in Phase 3.5). Q3 orders included \$1bn international locomotive/service awards and >\$600m North American orders. Category leader in a North American duopoly for new high-horsepower freight locomotives, dominant supplier (the precise ~70%/30% split could **not** be verified as a current fact and is not cited). Moat: installed base, proprietary platforms, certification lock-in; no Class I railroad builds locomotives. Q3: adjusted EPS +22%, adjusted operating margin 22.9% (+1.9pts), FY2026 EPS guidance raised to \$10.85–\$11.05. Also the single pick — see `final_pick_2026-11-07.md`.

**4. CBOE — Cboe Global Markets (\$353.07).** 0DTE SPX options: index options ADV +24% YoY to 6.1m contracts in Q3 2026, 0DTE a record 65% of SPX activity; retail SPX 0DTE volume +46% YoY. Caveat: shares +32% in a month into the print — the surge is substantially priced; analyst targets straddle the price (Morgan Stanley \$450 Overweight, JPMorgan \$332 Underweight).
