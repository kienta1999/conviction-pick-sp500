# Final pick — momentum mode, 2026-09-30

## THE PICK

## One-paragraph thesis

Micron is the purest play on the worst memory shortage in 15 years: customers receive only 55–60% of what they ask for, DRAM prices are tracking +400% since 2024, and the industry has structurally changed — ~$22B in customer cash deposits and long-term agreements now cover ~20% of DRAM bits and ~30% of NAND bits, converting a spot-cyclical business into contracted multi-year revenue. With HBM sold out and only three firms on earth able to make leading-edge DRAM, Micron's pricing power is at a historic extreme (FQ3: 84.9% gross margin, record $18.3B FCF) while the stock still trades at 6.6x forward earnings — the market prices a cycle that the contract book says isn't coming back.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- IDC (Feb 28, 2026): worst memory crisis in 15 years; AI data-center buildout "completely depleting" HBM, DRAM and NAND capacity. Data centers will consume ~70% of all memory in 2026, up from 20–30% in 2022.
- Micron fills only 55–60% of core-customer asks (management range: "50% to two-thirds"); demand from key customers runs ~50% above committable volume (FQ3 FY26 call, June 2026).
- J.P. Morgan: DRAM prices +400%+ from start-2024 to end-2026. FQ3 confirmed it's a price story: DRAM revenue +67% QoQ on price +low-60s%, volume +low-single-digits.
- 16 strategic customer agreements tied to ~$22B in deposits/commitments (~$18B cash deposits) — the LTA shift that de-risks cyclicality.

## Why it's the category leader

#3 DRAM maker behind Samsung and SK Hynix (the two hold ~70% of DRAM combined); top-4 NAND. HBM is the highest-margin, most supply-constrained memory segment and Micron is a qualified Nvidia supplier. FQ3 FY26: record $41.5B revenue (+346% YoY, 5th consecutive record), non-GAAP EPS $25.11 (+106% QoQ, above the high end of guide).

## Why it's irreplaceable

Only three companies on earth make leading-edge DRAM (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron); a DRAM fab costs $20B+ and the process lead (1γ nodes, TSV stacking for HBM) is a decade of cumulative know-how. No hyperscaler can in-source memory the way it can in-source a chip design — Google designing its own DRAM is not a realistic threat. HBM consumes ~3x the fab capacity per bit of DDR5, so every HBM wafer tightens conventional supply further. Disintermediation risk: **low**.
