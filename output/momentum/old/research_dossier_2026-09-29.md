## Per-name compact dossiers

### MU — Micron (memory: DRAM/NAND/HBM)
- Shortage: tightest memory market since ~2017. Entire 2026 HBM output sold out under multi-year contracts, booked into 2027; can supply only ~50-66% of key customers' medium-term needs. DRAM contract prices +93-98% QoQ earlier in 2026. 16 take-or-pay strategic agreements, 14 carrying ~$100B minimum revenue, ~$22B customer financial commitments (~$18B cash deposits) through 2030. Q4 FY26 earnings **Sep 30, 2026** (tomorrow): consensus ~$51B rev, ~$31.50 adj EPS.
- Category: DRAM #3 (~24-25%), HBM #3 (~21%) behind SK Hynix (58%) and Samsung (21%); retakes solo #3 in NAND.
- Irreplaceability 7/10: no hyperscaler builds leading-edge DRAM; only 3 HBM makers. Real threat is China: CXMT 4%→~10% DRAM revenue share in one year, LPDDR6 mass production Sep 2026 — medium-term, commodity-DRAM-concentrated.
- Catalysts: HBM4 >$1B revenue shipped, ramping 2x faster than HBM3E; $250B investment plan pulled forward (Sep 2026); Morgan Stanley PT $1,050, BMO $1,300; crossed $1,000 (Sep 21), ~+280% YTD, ~6.6x forward earnings.
- Risks: DRAM/NAND price softening by 2027 (Citi trim rationale); CXMT capacity doubling; $1T+ cap on memory earnings.
- EQ flag RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN: benign — 10-Q attributes it to +203% revenue growth; OCF $45.7B vs net income $47.3B; cash is coming in.

### SNDK — SanDisk (NAND flash pure-play)
- Shortage: genuine physical NAND shortage. AI datacenters absorbing storage faster than production; bits "on allocation well beyond 2027" (Q4 FY26 call, Aug 5). Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron reallocated fab capacity from NAND to HBM — SNDK benefits as pure-play. 10 multi-year supply agreements (NBM), >4yr avg duration, $93.9B minimum revenue at floor pricing, $16.5B customer deposits. Q4 FY26: $8.97B rev (+372% YoY), 84.6% GM; FY26 $20.25B (+175%).
- Category: NOT #1 — Samsung ~28-29%, SK Hynix ~19%, Micron ~15%, Kioxia ~14%, YMTC ~14%, SanDisk ~11%.
- Irreplaceability 5/10: customers can't build NAND fabs, but NAND is a commodity; YMTC ~14% share, Samsung +50% capacity expansion 2026; Kioxia capped prices (Sep 2026: "prices have risen enough").
- Catalysts: investor day guided mid-to-high-teens rev growth FY28-30; $31B Kioxia joint fab investment (Aug 27); BiCS10 sampling; analyst avg target ~$2,155.
- Risks: -35% from late-June peak ($2,354 → ~$1,713); NAND price growth decelerating (+70-75% Q2 → +10-15% Q3 forecast); NBM ceilings cap supercycle upside; trailing P/E ~23 on PEAK earnings.
- Key tension vs MU: purest NAND leverage but first to break if cycle turns; weaker moat (commodity, not #1).
