## Why it's irreplaceable

- Only three firms on earth can manufacture HBM at scale (verified CONFIRMED); the moat is extreme capital intensity ($150B+ fab buildouts), process-tech lead, and qualification lock-in (HBM qualification with hyperscalers takes years; a fab cannot qualify before 2028).

- Earnings-quality flag (RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN) resolved as benign: receivables grew ~290% while revenue grew 256%; FY26 operating cash flow ($89.7B) exceeded net income ($85.0B) — cash conversion is superb, not deteriorating.
