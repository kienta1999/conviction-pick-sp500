## Per-name theses (top 5)

**NVDA (1).** Nvidia holds about 80-85% of AI accelerator revenue with a CUDA/full-stack moat no rival can copy in one step — a rival must replicate the whole stack, not just a chip. The verified correction matters: its 500B Blackwell+Rubin figure is management demand visibility through CY2026 (and the 1T figure a GTC projection through 2027), NOT a filing RPO — the actual filing RPO is 3.2B because hardware ships in weeks. Q2 FY27 revenue was 96.2B with Q3 guided to about 108B. The trap is live: Google TPU and AWS Trainium are confirmed shipping, though the OpenAI-Broadcom chip is still engineering samples, not shipping (Phase 3.5 correction to the dossier's framing). Both earnings-quality flags are live — earnings ran ahead of cash in Q2 FY27 on non-cash gains and deliberate customer financing (receivables +55% in a quarter, DSO 45 to 60).

**GOOGL (2).** Google Cloud backlog of 514B in Q2 2026, over 50% expected to convert within 24 months, on Cloud revenue +82% to 24.8B, with the business supply-constrained enough to raise 2026 capex to 195-205B. Search holds about 90% share and still grew +17%. Mandatory haircut: Q2 headline EPS of 9.11 was materially flattered by a 98.0B unrealised equity-securities gain — judge GOOGL on operating income and backlog conversion, not reported EPS. Panelist D's single pick: the full-stack winner still priced as a Search casualty.

**MU (3).** The shortage is contracted, not spot: HBM is sold out for CY2026 with the large majority of CY2027 already agreed at significantly higher prices, Micron can satisfy only 50-66% of key customers' HBM demand, and a 150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay strategic customer agreements with 32B of customer commitments floors revenue, covering over 35% of revenue through 2030. Micron is one of only three firms making HBM at scale. FQ4 FY26 revenue was 54.23B with FQ1 FY27 guided to 61.5B plus or minus 1.5B. It is the panel's single-pick winner (see If forced to ONE).

**APH (4).** Record orders of 10.7B at 1.23 book-to-bill, IT datacom at 43% of sales and +89% YoY, fibre lead times around 50 weeks, and connector content per TPU system more than doubling — the purest physical bottleneck in the field on this run's research (shortage 9/10). **SNDK (5).** The purest NAND price leverage: 93.9B of minimum revenue under floored New Business Model deals covering over 50% of FY2027 bits and about two-thirds of FY2028 bits, NAND on allocation beyond CY2027, and an 84.6% Q4 gross margin that NAND has never sustained.
