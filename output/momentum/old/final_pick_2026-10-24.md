# Momentum conviction pick — 2026-10-24

## THE PICK: MU — Micron Technology

Semiconductors (Information Technology), market cap about $993B, price $856.49 (shortlist_2026-10-24.json).

## Thesis

Micron is the doctrine's archetype, found again by an unanimous panel: the world's AI buildout runs on HBM that only three firms on earth can make at scale, demand exceeds supply with management guiding greater tightness in 2027 and 2028, and the shortage is not a forecast — customers have prepaid $12.747B in deposits and signed take-or-pay contracts inside a $150B RPO, with roughly three-quarters of 2027 output already booked. At 4.1x forward earnings the market is pricing a peak cycle; the contracts say the cycle has been pre-sold.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- $150B RPO across 26 strategic customer agreements, >$100B of it take-or-pay (verified, FYQ4 prepared remarks 2026-09-30).
- $12.747B of customer deposits received in FY26 (verified, cash-flow statement) — customers paying cash in advance for memory allocation.
- HBM sold out through 2027; management: demand exceeding supply, greater tightness in 2027 and 2028 (verified). Earlier FY26 calls quantified only 50–66% of key customer demand as satisfiable.
- FYQ1 2027 revenue guided at $61.5B ± $1.5B (panel ballots, from company guidance).

## Why it's the category leader

Micron is #3 in DRAM, HBM and NAND — #1 in nothing, and the panel weighed that honestly. What it holds is co-leadership of the only oligopoly that gates AI compute: SK hynix, Samsung and Micron together supply roughly 90–95% of DRAM and effectively all leading-edge HBM (verified). In a shortage, the #3 seat in a 3-seat market is a pricing seat, not a weakness.

## Why it's irreplaceable

The disintermediation trap cannot fire here in its usual form. Hyperscalers are in-sourcing chip *design* (TPUs, Trainium, custom ASICs) — and every one of those chips still needs merchant HBM stacked on it. Nobody can fab leading DRAM in-house: the process-tech lead, capital intensity (a leading fab costs tens of billions and years), and the 3-player structure mean the customer set has no realistic build-it-themselves path. Irreplaceability 8/10; the residual risk is share loss to SK hynix, not disintermediation. Earnings-quality flag RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN was investigated in research and judged benign: receivables grew, but DSO fell from about 75 to 65 days and FY26 operating cash flow of $89.7B was 1.06x net income.
