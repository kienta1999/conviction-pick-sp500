# Momentum final pick — 2026-11-02

## THE PICK

**CDNS — Cadence Design Systems, Technology / Application Software, market cap ~\$94.0bn.**
Screen price \$342.75, analyst mean target \$411.76 (+20.1% implied), forward PE 33.3x.
Next earnings 2027-02-18.

## One-paragraph thesis

Cadence is the toll road on the AI build-out that hyperscalers cannot route around: every AI
accelerator, GPU and chiplet must be designed and verified in EDA before tape-out, and
AI-driven design complexity is expanding verification iterations faster than engineering
headcount. It sits in a two-player EDA duopoly with Synopsys, with recurring revenue up 18%
year over year in Q3 2026 and a record backlog that already covers essentially all of next
quarter's guided revenue. Crucially, hyperscaler in-sourcing helps rather than hurts Cadence —
Google, Microsoft, Meta and Amazon design their own chips but buy Cadence tools to do it. The
explosive leg is a pricing-model shift: agentic-AI workflows increase parallel engine
iterations rather than cannibalising seat licences, so consumption, not seats, could re-rate a
toll road the whole industry must use — and the stock has de-rated to ~32x FY27 EPS versus
40–45x historically while delivering a third consecutive beat-and-raise.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- Record \$8.2bn backlog at 30 September 2026, of which \$4.2bn of remaining performance
  obligations is expected as revenue in the next 12 months — about 2.5x Q4 2026 revenue
  guidance (\$1.695–\$1.725bn), so essentially all of Q4 revenue was already covered in backlog
  at the print (Cadence Q3 2026 results, 26 Oct 2026; VERIFIED).
- Q3 2026 revenue \$1.633bn (+22% YoY) beat consensus ~\$1.61bn; non-GAAP EPS \$2.11 beat
  ~\$2.04–\$2.05. FY2026 guidance raised to \$6.387–\$6.417bn (~21% growth), the third raise in
  2026 (Q3 2026 earnings call, 26 Oct 2026; VERIFIED).
- Record Palladium/Protium emulation hardware year; 100+ AI Super Agent customer engagements
  (Maestro, ChipStack, InnoStack) — the consumption-pricing engine behind the bull case.

## Why it's the category leader

One of two firms in the EDA duopoly (with Synopsys) that every leading-edge chip design must
pass through; foundry-qualified flows integrated over 30+ years of PDK work. Recurring revenue
+18% YoY in Q3 2026; the backlog, not spot orders, is the revenue engine.

## Why it's irreplaceable

The disintermediation trap cannot touch this name — it inverts it. Hyperscalers designing their
own silicon are Cadence customers, not competitors: they buy Cadence/Synopsys tools to design
their chips and do not build competing EDA suites, because ripping out a foundry-qualified flow
mid-design risks tape-out failure. The genuine substitute threat is long-tail only: LLMs/agentic
AI commoditising parts of design — which Cadence is answering by selling the agents itself.
Irreplaceability score 9/10 in research; low disintermediation risk per both the quality and
contrarian panelists. Note: the precise 36%/38% market-share split and near-100% retention
figure cited in the dossier were NOT primary-confirmed in verification and are not relied on
here; the duopoly claim itself is directional only.

## Scenarios & expected value

Bottoms-up build on backlog conversion, not bare multiples. The \$4.2bn of RPO due within 12
months anchors the base: at ~21% FY2026 guided growth, revenue compounds toward ~\$7.7bn in
FY2027, and the analyst mean target of \$411.76 (+20.1%) is the base anchor. Bear = the cycle
turns or the trap's long tail fires: an LLM-driven design-automation scare plus a China
export-control reversal (China ~15% of FY2026 guide) plus Hexagon margin dilution compresses
the multiple back toward ~25x. Bull = agentic consumption pricing lands at scale and the market
re-rates Cadence back toward its historical 40–45x range on continued beat-and-raise execution.

- **Bear: \$270 (−21.2%) by ~2027-Q2** — de-rate on an LLM-disruption scare and/or China
  reversal; the bear target doubles as the stop.
- **Base: \$412 (+20.2%) by ~2027-Q4** — backlog converts at today's pricing, growth holds
  near guide, stock moves toward the analyst mean target.
- **Bull: \$480 (+40.0%) by ~2027-Q4** — agentic consumption pricing compounds on top of the
  backlog and the multiple re-rates toward history.

**Probabilities with reasoning:** bear 20 / base 55 / bull 25. Bear is weighted below base
despite the high multiple because the \$4.2bn of next-12-month RPO makes a near-term revenue
collapse arithmetically hard — the backlog has to be cancelled, not merely slowed. Bull is
weighted below base because agentic consumption pricing is real but unproven at scale; it is
an option, not yet a run-rate. These are not default weights: the backlog cover is the reason
bear is not the modal scenario.

**Expected value:** EV = 0.20 × 270 + 0.55 × 412 + 0.25 × 480 = \$400.60, which is +16.9% over
the current price of \$342.75 — above the +15% EV guardrail over 12–18 months, so this
publishes as an actionable pick, not a pass (narrowly — the guardrail margin is thin and the
reader should see it as such).

**Market-implied scenario:** the current price sits closest to base-minus — below the analyst
mean target (\$411.76) and well below the bull case, with a 12-month return of −4.0% in the
screen despite the beat-and-raise run. The stock is not priced for the bull case.

All scenario numbers are research estimates, not guarantees.
