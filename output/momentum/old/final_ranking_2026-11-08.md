## Per-name thesis

**1. MSI — Motorola Solutions (\$514.51).** The only name combining a shortage score of 8 with irreplaceability of 8, backed by a hard order book rather than a narrative: record ending backlog \$16.6bn at Q3 2026, +14% YoY (+\$2.1bn) and +\$1.0bn sequentially, with orders +20% YoY outrunning revenue +12% — demand demonstrably exceeding current supply. The backlog is ~1.3 years of revenue, and preliminary 2027 revenue of ~\$14.25bn is already guided. Category leader in mission-critical communications (#1, ~24.5% global share, 28.4% North America). Moat: P25/TETRA networks are multi-decade public-safety infrastructure with procurement lock-in; the \$12.4bn Software & Services backlog is mostly recurring multi-year contracts; agencies cannot in-source radios. Silvus (defence/drone MANET) is the surge driver — expected slightly more than \$900m revenue in 2026 (raised from ~\$850m on the Q3 call, 4 Nov 2026), +~30% in 2027. Watch item: INVENTORY_BUILD flag (+41.5pts divergence) judged benign-to-mixed by research (deliberate pre-build for backlog conversion and the Silvus/D-Fend ramp, but needs 10-Q confirmation); long-term debt \$9.36bn vs cash \$774m after the Silvus (\$4.4bn) and D-Fend (\$1.5bn) acquisitions.

**2. WST — West Pharmaceutical (\$399.81).** The purest shortage thesis in the field: high-value injectable components grew ~20% organically in Q3 2026 (now ~53% of revenue), GLP-1-related HVP grew "well into the 30s%" organically (just under 12% of revenue), and management is starting another round of HVP finishing-capacity investment in Europe and the US that takes 18–24 months to contribute — demand today exceeds qualified finishing capacity, and no competitor can qualify faster because the component is specified in the drug's FDA filing. Category leader at ~18–20% global share in rubber stoppers vs Datwyler #2 at 12–14%. Moat: regulatory qualification lock-in — a stopper worth cents on a drug worth thousands; Lilly and Novo cannot in-source it. Watch item: receivables outrunning revenue by ~11pts (flag judged benign/timing — CFO/NI 1.02, accrual ~0, late-quarter HVP surge) and a 38.5x forward multiple that already pays for much of the story. West Vantage contract manufacturing is in a trough (−6.6% organic).

**3. EMR — Emerson Electric (\$162.35).** The only true shortage thesis outside the top two: AI datacenter power and LNG demand is outrunning control-system delivery capacity. Ovation (power-plant controls) orders surged 74% in fiscal Q1 2026 and +26% in Q4 FY26; backlog \$8.2bn at Sep 30, 2026, +11% YoY, with a \$13bn project funnel. Category position: challenger overall in industrial automation, dominant in the niches that matter — DeltaV is a leading LNG automation platform and Ovation a leading power-generation control platform. Moat: qualification lock-in + installed-base switching costs — a DCS running an LNG terminal or power plant is safety-certified and embedded in operating procedures. Caveat: the share of backlog shipping within 12 months fell from 75% to 70%, so the FY27-shippable book grew only ~3–4%, and the FY27 sales guide is only ~5% — the payoff is gated by slow backlog conversion, and shares fell 4.6% on the print despite the beat.
