## One-paragraph thesis
KLA is the only name in this panel that a hyperscaler, a foundry, or a GPU maker fundamentally cannot build around: ~73.8% of the metrology/inspection market, defended by ~25 years of defect-classification data that even TSMC cannot replicate, with a **$12.57B backlog (+60% YoY)** that is growing because KLA's own supply constraints — not customer demand — are the binding bottleneck (lead times are *lengthening* on the supplier side). The stock sits **37.6% below its 52-week high** despite a record $3.66B quarter and H2 CY2026 guided ~20% above H1. You get the dossier's hardest-to-displace franchise, sold-out shipments, and a drawdown — the combination the momentum doctrine exists to find.

## Why it's the category leader
~73.8% of the metrology/inspection market (ainvest, May 2026), up from ~50% in 2010 — a near-monopoly that widened its lead during the AI buildout. Clear #1 vs Applied Materials, ASML, Onto Innovation in diagnostics/inspection; #1 in process control for advanced wafer-level packaging. Specialty Semiconductor Process + PCB/component inspection (Orbotech) expected to grow >25% in CY2026.

## Why it's irreplaceable
The moat is algorithmic, not optical: yield entitlement lives in defect-classification algorithms trained on ~25 years of cross-customer process data — newcomers can replicate the optics but not the algorithmic substrate (investmentvault thesis, Sept 2026). Every wafer passes through KLA tools repeatedly; swapping vendors means re-qualifying yield models, which no fab risks mid-production to save capex. Customers *cannot* in-source it — this is the one franchise in the dossier where the hyperscaler-in-sourcing threat (the doctrine's trap) structurally cannot apply, because KLA's customers are foundries, not hyperscalers, and the asset is data, not a design they can redraw. Irreplaceability 10/10 — the dossier's highest. **CONFIRMED with caveats** (verifier: 73.8% is metrology/inspection specifically; ~56–58% for total process control; "even TSMC cannot replicate" is analyst framing, noted as color).
