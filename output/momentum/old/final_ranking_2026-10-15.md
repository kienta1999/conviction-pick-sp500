## Per-name theses (top 5)

**MU (1).** The shortage is contracted, not spot: HBM is sold out for CY2026 with the vast majority of CY2027 already agreed at significantly higher prices, management sees 2027/2028 tighter than 2026 with no meaningful new cleanroom output until after late 2028, and a ~150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs (at minimum pricing where a pricing framework is defined, ~3/4 of SCA revenue) with 32B of customer commitments floors the revenue. Micron is #3 in a 3-firm HBM/DRAM oligopoly (~18% HBM share in Q2 2026) that customers prepay rather than in-source. FQ4 revenue was 54.23B with FQ1 FY27 guided to 61.5B ± 1.5B.

**KLAC (2).** The batch's cleanest verified file: backlog 12.57B at 30 Jun 2026, up ~60% from 7.86B in the FY26 10-K, ~3.4 quarters of sales, with Q1 FY27 guided to 4.0B ± 200M. KLA holds ~56-58% of process control (~7x its nearest rival), and process-control intensity rises with every node, HBM stack and advanced package — advanced-packaging process control is guided to ~1.1B in CY26, up over 70%. The contrarian kicker: the stock is -38.9% off its 52-week high while the backlog hardened. China (30% of FY26 revenue) and a receivables flag (CFO/NI 0.86) are the disclosed risks; earnings are company-confirmed for 2026-10-28.

**GOOGL (3).** Google Cloud backlog/RPO of 514B (from 106B a year ago), over 50% expected to convert within 24 months, on Cloud revenue +82% to 24.8B in Q2 2026, with the business described as capacity-constrained and 2026 capex raised to 195-205B. Search holds ~89.9% global share. Mandatory haircut: Q2 headline EPS of 9.11 was materially flattered by a 98.0B unrealised equity-securities gain in other income — judge GOOGL on operating income and backlog conversion, not reported EPS. Earnings are scheduled for 2026-10-28, the same day as KLAC.

**LLY (4).** The new name in the field: Q2 2026 revenue 23.0B (+48%), Mounjaro 9.94B (+91%) and Zepbound 4.93B (+46%), FY26 guidance raised to 85-87B, and a 60.9% US incretin share vs Novo's 38.8%. A customer cannot in-source a patented drug (irreplaceability 8/10); the risk is rival/next-gen substitution at dated readouts and capacity build-out easing the shortage, and it discloses no order book.

**PH (5).** Record backlog of 12.8B total / 8.5B aerospace (+16%/+15% YoY at 30 Jun 2026) against a 17,000+ aircraft industry queue, with clean earnings quality (CFO/NI 1.20). It is #1 in motion & control with certified, designed-in content. The skeptic's discount: pro-forma leverage from the 9.25B Filtration Group close plus the pending ~2.55B CIRCOR deal is not in the screen ratios — execution/deleveraging, not demand, is the risk.

One line each for the rest: SNDK — purest NAND price leverage, weakest moat in the top 10. AMAT — broadest WFE portfolio, revenue accelerating -2% to +25% to a record guide, no formal backlog disclosed. NVDA — 96.2B Q2 revenue and a 108B guide, discounted for live ASIC in-sourcing and weaker earnings quality (CFO/NI 0.70). LRCX — etch leader with deferred revenue of 2.43B as a backlog proxy; earnings 2026-10-21. VRTX — sole CFTR supplier for ~90% of CF patients with the nearest challenger discontinued in Aug 2026, but shortage score only 5/10.

## Risk lens & leverage-safety note

Sector concentration is high: 6 of the top 10 are semiconductor / semiconductor-equipment names (MU, KLAC, SNDK, AMAT, NVDA, LRCX), with GOOGL also an AI-capex proxy, so the list is largely one AI-capex cycle expressed several ways; LLY, VRTX and PH are the diversifiers. Highest-risk names are the cyclicals with price-led earnings (MU, SNDK) — memory names can draw down 50% or more fast when pricing rolls, which is brutal with any leverage; steadier compounders are KLAC, PH and VRTX. This system is cash-only per POLICY.md: no margin, no options as leverage substitutes. Treat any leverage discussion here as risk education, not a recommendation.
