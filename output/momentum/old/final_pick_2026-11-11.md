## One-paragraph thesis

Cadence sells the tools that design every AI chip, in a duopoly with Synopsys that no customer can bypass — and its order book has never been bigger. Backlog hit a record \$8.2bn at 30 Sep 2026 (with \$4.2bn of RPO converting in the next 12 months), Q3 was the third consecutive beat-and-raise of 2026, and hyperscaler in-sourcing — the trap that kills most AI suppliers — runs backwards here, because every customer that designs its own silicon buys more EDA. The stock is still 20.4% below its June high and up only 2.7% over 12 months, so the market has not repriced the backlog even though the backlog grew while the price fell.

## Why it's the category leader

Co-#1 in a duopoly: Cadence ~31–36% share vs Synopsys ~36–38%, Siemens EDA a distant #3 at ~13% (Phase 3.5 correction: cite the duopoly at ~62–67% combined — VMR Mar 2026: 67.2% — and the top-three total at ~70–80%, not 70–80% for the duopoly alone). Segment leadership in analog/custom layout (Virtuoso) and hardware emulation/verification (Palladium, the industry benchmark). **45% of revenue now comes from system companies, not chipmakers** — hyperscalers and auto/industrial firms designing their own silicon are a new, expanding customer class.

## Why it's irreplaceable

The moat is qualification lock-in: switching EDA tools mid-project is prohibitively costly, designs are qualified tool-by-tool with the foundry, near-100% retention rates, and an 80–85% recurring-revenue model. Only three firms on earth supply production-grade EDA at scale, backed by decades of accumulated IP (process-design kits, libraries, verification IP, signoff flows) that cannot be rebuilt quickly. The disintermediation threat is inverted: hyperscalers designing custom AI silicon *increase* EDA seat/IP demand — they do not build EDA stacks, and Cadence's system-company revenue share rising to 45% is the evidence. The live threats are second-order: agentic AI seat cannibalisation (Cadence's answer is to own the agents — Maestro, launched 26 Oct 2026) and China domestic EDA, a generational gap today. All four lenses rated the disintermediation risk low.
