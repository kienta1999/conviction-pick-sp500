## Per-name theses (top 5)

**GOOGL (1).** Google Cloud backlog of $514B in Q2 2026, over 50% expected to convert within 24 months, on Cloud revenue +82% to $24.8B, with Search still growing +17% — the AI-displacement thesis contradicted by the print again. It is the only name ranked top-3 by three of the four lenses, and Panelist D's single pick: the full-stack winner still priced as a Search casualty at 22.3x forward. Mandatory haircut: Q2 headline EPS of $9.11 was materially flattered by ~$98B of unrealised equity-securities gains (about $6.26 of the $9.11) — judge GOOGL on operating income (+30%) and backlog conversion, not reported EPS. Capex guided up to $195-205B for 2026 is the real downside variable.

**NVDA (2).** Nvidia holds about 80-85% of AI accelerator revenue with a CUDA/full-stack moat no rival can copy in one step — a rival must replicate the whole stack, not just a chip (supply/capacity commitments jumped to ~$279B in one quarter because HBM and CoWoS, not demand, are the bottleneck). Q2 FY27 revenue was $96.2B and Q3 is formally guided to $108.0B ± 2% (GlobeNewswire release, verified in Phase 3.5). The honest framing of the big number: its $500B Blackwell+Rubin figure is management demand visibility through CY2026 ($1T through 2027), NOT a filing RPO — the filing RPO is ~$3.2B because hardware ships in weeks. The trap is live: Google TPU and AWS Trainium ship in volume, Meta MTIA ships, Microsoft's Maia 200 is in production, and the OpenAI-Broadcom chip is taped out with volume ramp in 2027. Both earnings-quality flags are live — non-cash equity gains in accruals, and receivables +55% in Q2 FY27 to $63.1B (DSO ~45→60) on disclosed extended payment terms for large multi-quarter agreements.

**MU (3).** The shortage is contracted, not spot: HBM is sold out for CY2026 with the large majority of CY2027 already agreed at significantly higher prices, Micron can satisfy only about 50-66% of key customers' medium-term bit demand, and a $150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay strategic customer agreements floors revenue — over 35% of revenue through 2030, counting only the ~75% of SCA revenue with a defined pricing framework, at minimum pricing.
