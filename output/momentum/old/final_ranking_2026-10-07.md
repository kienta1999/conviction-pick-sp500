## Per-name thesis

**AVGO (#1):** The shortage is contracted, not hoped for — RPO ~$179.2B (Phase 3.5-corrected upward), ~3x book-to-bill on AI semis, Google/Anthropic/OpenAI locked through 2031. It collects the toll on the very in-sourcing trend the market fears. Mispricing: 23% off peak at 19.4x forward while AI revenue runs +143% YoY. Key caveat: $42B Anthropic vendor financing concentrates credit risk.

**MU (#2):** The purest shortage signal — $150B take-or-pay RPO, $12.7B customer cash received, meeting only half to two-thirds of demand, >75% of CY27 output committed. Only US-headquartered HBM supplier. The knock: $1.2T market cap already prices perfection and the price-increase slope is decelerating.

**KLAC (#3):** Near-monopoly process control (73.8% metrology share) riding rising per-wafer inspection intensity at the leading edge; backlog +60% YoY to $12.57B. The moat is algorithmic (decades of defect data) plus fab-recipe lock-in. Watch: 28% China revenue and stalled margin expansion.

**AMAT (#4):** Broadest WFE toolbox with 13 straight quarters of margin expansion, Q4 guide +51% revenue, two-year customer visibility, contracts to 2030. The China drag (30%→26% of revenue) and +102% YTD run temper the edge vs LRCX.

**LRCX (#5):** #1 in etch with the strongest guidance cadence in company history (Sep quarter +20% QoQ to $8.1B); NAND systems revenue doubled QoQ. Pure-play on the AI-memory capex supercycle; no formal backlog is the disclosure gap.

**NVDA (#6):** $1T order visibility through 2027 at 15.1x forward is the contrarian setup — but hyperscaler ASICs (TPU v7, Trainium 3, MTIA v3, Maia 200) are in production, the one live disintermediation in the field. Maturing shortage thesis.

**CAT (#7):** The most literal physical shortage — customers can't get gensets for ~2 years; $72B backlog (+92% YoY) booked to 2030. A 100-year-old industrial re-priced as AI power infrastructure at 34.5x trailing earnings.

**CDNS (#8):** The single best irreplaceability in the field (10/10) — a duopoly toll on every AI chip taped out on earth, $8.1B backlog. But it's a 70x-P/E compounder with export-control probation to 2028, not a shortage repricing.

**APH (#9):** Orders +94% YoY with non-cancelable AI commitments — the most order-backed demand signal in the batch. The multiple (42–55x) and the copper-to-optics technology arc are the debate, not demand.

**SNDK (#10):** The tightest structural NAND shortage in memory history, increasingly contracted ($93.9B NBM). Ranked last of the ten because the stock already ran 600–800% on it — momentum here is carry, not discovery — and NAND margins sit at historic peaks.
