# Momentum final pick — 2026-10-28

## THE PICK: MU — Micron Technology

Technology / Semiconductors. Frozen screen price $912.27. Market cap ~$992.9B (screen). Forward PE 4.41, analyst mean target $1561.91 (+71.2%).

## Thesis

Micron remains the doctrine's founding pattern: a structural HBM/DRAM/NAND shortage that customers have converted from spot exposure into contracted commitments. Roughly $150B of remaining performance obligations sit under 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements — verified in substance, with the definition caveat that the $150B is the priced-framework subset (about three-quarters of SCA revenue) at committed volumes times minimum pricing — backed by ~$32B of customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits. More than 75% of calendar-2027 output is committed (verified with the correction that the figure covers HBM/DRAM/NAND output broadly, not HBM alone), and Micron can currently satisfy only 50–66% of key customers' HBM demand. Every custom ASIC a hyperscaler builds to in-source compute still needs HBM from one of only three makers on earth — the disintermediation trap points elsewhere.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- ~$150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs, ~$32B customer commitments, >75% of 2027 output committed across SCA and non-SCA customers — fiscal Q4 2026 disclosure, 30 Sep 2026, verified in Phase 3.5 with the definition caveats above.
- Fiscal Q4 2026 revenue $54.23B verified; fiscal Q1 2027 guided to $60–63B revenue (~$61.5B midpoint), gross margin ~86%, EPS $42.13 ± $1.00 — verified.
- Screen: revenue growth +379.3% TTM (shortlist field), operating margin 80.7%, forward PE 4.41 at $912.27.

## Why it's the category leader

One of three leading-edge memory makers on earth (with SK hynix and Samsung); #3 in DRAM (~24%) and HBM (~18%), and the only US maker, with custom HBM co-design lock-in on top of process-tech scarcity. It is not the share leader — its claim is scarcity membership in a three-firm oligopoly, not dominance.

## Why it's irreplaceable

No hyperscaler operates a memory fab and none can build one inside the horizon ($20B+, 3–5 years, deep process IP). The honest weakness is share, not in-sourcing: Micron is #3 in HBM and customers can shift share to the other two makers at each qualification; CXMT presses conventional DRAM (10% share Q2 2026, still commodity-focused). Panel rated the trap low (A) to medium (B, D) — no lens rated it high.
