## Per-name theses (top 5)

**1. MU — Micron Technology.** The shortage archetype the doctrine was modeled on: HBM and DRAM supply can meet only roughly half to two-thirds of key customer demand, management guides tightness increasing in 2027–28, and customers have prepaid $12.747B in deposits against a $150B RPO (verified). It is the category co-leader in a 3-firm oligopoly, and it is irreplaceable in the specific way that matters — a hyperscaler designing its own ASIC still has to buy merchant HBM; nobody can fab leading DRAM in-house.

**2. NVDA — Nvidia.** $1T of Blackwell/Rubin visibility through 2027 (verified as visibility/purchase orders, not filed RPO) and a 70–80% AI-accelerator share make it the demand center of the AI buildout. The honest caveat is the trap: its own customers are shipping inference ASICs (TPU, Trainium, Maia, MTIA), and research flagged receivables outrunning revenue with DSO stretching 45 to 60 days — a real yellow flag, not a benign one.

**3. SNDK — Sandisk.** The purest NAND shortage trade: 2026 capacity sold out, $93.9B of contracted NBM minimums, and verification actually upgraded its backlog — the FY2026 10-K RPO is $59.8B ($91.1B pro-forma), not the $41.6B older figure in the dossier. The caveat is structural: #5 in NAND at 11.4% share (verified, TrendForce), a single-product cyclical whose shortage ends when industry capacity lands post-2027.

**4. LLY — Lilly (Eli).** The clear incretin #1 (60.9% US share) with Mounjaro and Zepbound at $27.7B in H1 sales and 2026 guidance raised to $85–87B. But the shortage phase has been manufactured away — this is now a volume and coverage-expansion story at 27.7x forward earnings with only +1.9% analyst upside, so it ranks on moat, not on shortage torque.

**5. META — Meta Platforms.** Ranked on growth and value, not doctrine fit: shortage score 3/10 because Meta is the squeezed buyer of scarce compute ($130–145B 2026 capex), not a seller into a shortage. What it has is ad pricing power (impressions +14%, price +12% in Q2 2026) at 18.6x forward earnings — the contrarian lens's #2.

## Risk lens & leverage-safety note

The top 3 are one trade in three wrappers: AI memory/compute cyclicality. A hyperscaler capex pause hits MU, NVDA and SNDK together, and memory names have historically drawn down 50%+ peak-to-trough — with leverage that is a margin call, not a drawdown. The steadier compounders on the list (ISRG, LLY, V-class moats further down) carry valuation risk instead of cycle risk. This system is cash-only per POLICY.md; treat the bear targets above as the real risk budget.
