## Per-name thesis

**1. LLY —** The purest volume engine in the batch: Q3 revenue \$24.4bn +39% on +53% volume, tirzepatide \$16.08bn in one quarter, ~62.8% of US branded GLP-1 prescriptions and still gaining, guidance raised three times to \$91–92bn, retatrutide BLA filing planned Q1 2027 (TRIUMPH-1: −25.0% ITT / −28.3% efficacy estimand at 80 wks, NEJM 29 Sep 2026). The moat is the molecule plus a \$50bn+ capacity build. The honest caveat is the whole story of this run: the shortage is over (FDA Dec 2024), the pen-gap claim could not be verified, realized prices fell −15%, and at \$1,392 with +5.6% to target the explosive upside is mostly in the rear-view mirror. See `final_pick_2026-11-26.md` for the full pass writeup.

**2. CBOE —** The only true monopoly in the batch: exclusive SPX options through 2051 (25-year S&P DJI extension signed 29 Sep 2026, confirmed), SPX ADV +26% to 4.9m contracts, 0DTE at a record 3.2m ADV (+34%), 65% of SPX activity, at ~\$0.95 index revenue per contract vs ~\$0.05 for multi-listed options, with a 71.4% adjusted operating margin. The discount: the license news (+6% on the announcement) is already in the price, analyst upside is ~3.2%, and the growth engine is a retail 0DTE flow (58% of 0DTE volume) that can reverse in a calm market or under regulatory scrutiny.

**3. VEEV —** The forced Vault CRM migration is the closest thing to a genuine demand surge in this batch: the Salesforce agreement expired 1 Sep 2025, Veeva CRM support ends 31 Dec 2029, and 14 of the top 20 biopharmas have committed to Vault CRM (confirmed at the 5 Nov 2026 Investor Day), with an ~80% win rate in decided top-20 deals and >80% biopharma CRM share. Q2 FY2027 revenue +18% with a +15.3% pop on the print. The catch, flagged by every lens that ranked it: Q3 FY2027 earnings land **Thu 3 Dec 2026 after close** — 7 days after this run — a binary event with an 8.3% average post-print move, and Salesforce + IQVIA is a real challenger for the remaining top-20 decisions.

**4. EMR —** The only name with a genuine, growing, quantified backlog: \$8.2B, +11% YoY, book-to-bill 1.01, underlying orders +14% in Q4 (accelerating from +7%), a \$13B project funnel (+17%), Ovation orders +26%, riding a documented transformer shortage (lead times ~128–144 weeks, prices +77% since 2019). The honest caveat: EMR sells the control systems around the scarce transformer, not the transformer itself — a second-order beneficiary — and only 70% of backlog ships within 12 months, which is why +14% orders support only ~5% FY27 sales guidance. Lens D's single pick (conviction 6/10) precisely because it is the least-priced-in name with a real shortage and a real backlog.

**5. ABNB —** Real demand re-acceleration: Q3 nights +16% (from +10% in Q2), GBV +21%, revenue +19% — the fastest in 3+ years — with FY2026 guidance raised a third time and a fresh \$12bn buyback. The "shortage" is regulation-created and city-specific (NYC listings cut ~90% by Local Law 18; Barcelona ends licensed short stays by Nov 2028), and that same regulatory creep caps the upside. Momentum has already partially faded from the post-earnings ~\$189 spike to \$172.15.

**6. CVX —** The best-executed version of the oil trade: Q3 adj EPS \$5.28 vs \$4.78 consensus, revenue \$67.8B +36%, record refinery throughput 1.09m bbl/d at ~98% utilisation, CFFO +77.8% in 9M, and a Venezuela growth option (to ~600k bbl/d by 2031, <$20/bbl costs).
