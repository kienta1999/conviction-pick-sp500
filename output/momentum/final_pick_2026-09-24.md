### One-paragraph thesis

Applied Materials sits on the one bottleneck every AI build must pass through. The semiconductor supply chain's binding constraint has migrated from foundry capacity to equipment supply itself: major-equipment lead times have stretched 1.5–2x and deposition-tool parts now take 4→10 months (TrendForce, 2026-09-18). That shortage converts into pricing power because AMAT's moat is fab qualification lock-in — no fab swaps a tool at the risk of yield on a $20B facility — and the gate-all-around transistor transition is multiplying process steps, compounding AMAT's content per wafer. It just printed a record quarter (Q3 FY2026: $9.12B revenue, +25% YoY, raised calendar-2026 outlook to >30% WFE growth) while trading 34% below its high, with next earnings 2026-11-12 and a fresh September analyst PT wave (UBS $695, TD Cowen $700, Susquehanna $900) as the re-rating fuel.

#1 wafer-fab-equipment vendor by revenue, sub-industry rank 2 of S&P 500 semicap (screen; leader KLAC at 1.02× market cap).
