## Per-name thesis

**1. MU —** Memory's worst shortage in 15 years meets a 3-firm HBM oligopoly: $32B in prepaid customer deposits (up $10B in one quarter), HBM sold out through 2027 with 2027–28 guided tighter, and a 5.1x forward multiple pricing a normal cycle against a contracted book. The only US-based HBM maker; no hyperscaler can in-source a fab.

**2. CAT —** A $72B record backlog (+92% YoY, ~1x trailing revenue) in a business that physically cannot build fast enough: gas-engine prime-power lead times run into H2 2028–2029, meeting only ~60% of 2026 genset demand. The AI datacenter power buildout turned a cyclical machinery name into a capacity-constrained compounder.

**3. KLAC —** The deepest moat in the batch (near-monopoly in process control, ~85%+ optical inspection, a 25-year cross-customer defect library no newcomer can rebuild) trading at a −31.4% drawdown while backlog surged 60% YoY to $12.57B. Advanced-packaging process-control revenue guided +70% in 2026; export controls cost revenue yet proved irreplaceability (customers couldn't substitute).

**4. MSFT —** $678B commercial RPO (+84% YoY) against Azure demand that "continued to outstrip available capacity," reinforced by Anthropic's $31.4B non-cancelable Azure commitment. The doctrine tension (hyperscaler = buyer side) is real, but power-constrained compute is a genuine demand>supply setup.

**5. APH —** 50-week fiber lead times and a 1.23 book-to-bill with hyperscaler LTAs pre-buying capacity. AI interconnect content per rack is the driver; the 5/10 irreplaceability (TE/Molex compete) caps it below the top tier.

**6. LRCX —** ~45% plasma-etch share into a memory build, WFE outlook raised twice to low-$150B, September quarter guided +52% YoY revenue. 60x P/E into the Oct 21 print is the contrarian's objection.

**7. SNDK —** Real NAND allocation into 2027+ backed by ~$94B of price-floor NBM deals. Kept in the ranking with the trap flag visible: 5th place in a commoditized market, +640% YTD, momentum decelerating — the weakest moat on the list.

**8. AMAT —** The broadest WFE toll-taker with customer fabs full and tool lead times doubled to ~12 months; first $10B quarter guided. Derivative of the memory build rather than the shortage itself.

**9. CDNS —** EDA duopoly toll booth on every AI tapeout with zero in-source risk. 53x forward earnings prices the quality; the contrarian seat from the 10/04 run holds.

**10. FCX —** The structural copper deficit in one ticker (~330 kmt 2026 deficit) plus its own mine outage; geology cannot be in-sourced. Only 1.9% analyst upside caps the near-term case.
