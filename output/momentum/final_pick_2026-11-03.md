## One-paragraph thesis

Cadence sells the picks and shovels nobody can replace: as one of two dominant EDA vendors, it sits on the verification bottleneck through which every advanced chip must pass, and that bottleneck is now priced as a backlog rather than a cycle — a record \$8.2bn at Q3 2026, with \$4.2bn converting within 12 months, a third consecutive beat-and-raise, and AI-driven design complexity (48-fold chip-size growth over five years per the dossier) lengthening rather than shortening its runway. The stock is flat on the year while the backlog compounds, so the panel's skeptic lens called it the real-but-underappreciated thesis in a field of crowded shortage rockets — the moat the disintermediation trap cannot touch, at a price that has not yet paid for the scarcity.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- Record backlog \$8.2bn at Q3 2026, reported 26 Oct 2026 (quarter ended 30 Sep 2026) — verified from the company release; ~1.28x guided FY2026 revenue; prior balance \$7.8bn at end-2025, so this is a genuine step-up, not a restatement.
- \$4.2bn of remaining performance obligations expected to be recognised within the next 12 months (CFO commentary, via the dossier and verifier).
- Q3 2026: revenue \$1.633bn, +22% YoY (vs \$1.595–1.625bn guide); non-GAAP EPS \$2.11 (vs \$2.04–2.05 consensus); recurring revenue +18%; all product groups double-digit (core EDA +20%, IP +24%, System Design & Analysis +30%).
- FY2026 guidance raised for the third consecutive quarter: revenue \$6.387–6.417bn (~21% growth, midpoint +\$102m), non-GAAP EPS \$8.37–8.43; board authorised an additional \$2.5bn buyback; Maestro AI models launched with >100 AI Super Agent engagements.
- Backlog booked in a "comparatively light renewal year" (panelist D) — expansion-driven, not renewal-driven.

## Why it's the category leader

Cadence and Synopsys each hold ~30% of the global EDA market (SemiAnalysis EDA Primer, May 2026, via the dossier and verifier); Siemens EDA holds ~13%. Together the top two command 70–80% of global EDA revenue, a concentration held for over a decade. Cadence leads custom/analog layout; Synopsys leads synthesis/simulation. No other vendor is close, and no customer runs a full flow on a single alternative.

## Why it's irreplaceable

The moat is full-flow integration embedded in foundry PDKs: switching a full flow mid-program is a multi-year, high-risk undertaking, and no source was found of any customer — hyperscaler included — building a full in-house replacement EDA flow. That inverts this doctrine's trap: hyperscaler in-sourcing *creates* Cadence customers (more chip designs to verify) instead of destroying them. Qualification carried from verification: "duopoly" is shorthand for a Big-3 market with a clear top two — Siemens EDA's Calibre runs physical verification on nearly every tape-out regardless of vendor — but that softens the label, not the barrier.

## Scenarios & expected value

Bottoms-up build on backlog conversion, not bare multiples. The \$4.2bn of RPO due within 12 months anchors the base: at ~21% FY2026 guided growth, revenue compounds toward ~\$7.7bn in FY2027, and the analyst mean target of \$411.76 (+25.6%) is the base anchor. Bear = the cycle turns or the trap's long tail fires: an LLM-driven design-automation scare plus a China export-control reversal (China ~15% of FY2026 guide per yesterday's file, carried as dossier context) plus margin dilution compresses the multiple back toward ~25x. Bull = agentic consumption pricing lands at scale and the market re-rates Cadence back toward its historical 40–45x range on continued beat-and-raise execution.

- **Bear (\$270, −17.6%, by 2027-Q2):** what breaks, quantified — a design-spending pause plus export-control tightening plus multiple compression to ~25x takes the stock to \$270. The bear target doubles as the **stop** — a price you would actually exit at.
- **Base (\$411.76, +25.6%, by 2027-Q2):** the most likely path — the \$4.2bn near-term RPO converts, FY2026 guidance of ~21% growth is delivered, and the stock moves to the analyst mean target.
- **Bull (\$480, +46.4%, by 2027-Q2):** what has to go right — verification capacity gets priced as a structural bottleneck rather than a software cycle, Maestro/AI Super Agent traction (>100 engagements) accelerates full-flow wins, and the beat-and-raise cadence continues. Bull needs repricing, not just execution — execution alone lands at base.
- **Probabilities with reasoning — 20 / 55 / 25 (bear / base / bull).** Base is heaviest because three consecutive beat-and-raises, a record backlog, and \$4.2bn of near-term RPO make delivery the modal path. Bear is held at 20, not higher, because the backlog is contracted and recurring revenue grew 18% — a pause hurts the multiple, not the revenue — but 20 is honest weight on a 33.3x multiple, export controls, and Synopsys (now with Ansys) contesting every deal.
