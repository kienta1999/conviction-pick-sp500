## Top-3 theses

**1. CF — CF Industries ($119.34).** The only verified physical shortage in the field, and the single pick — see `final_pick_2026-11-19.md`. The shortage is real but reversible — that reversibility is the bear case, priced at a one-in-three probability.

**2. MSFT — Microsoft ($506.24).** Azure grew 48% in Q1 FY2027 to $33.2B against a $677B commercial RPO (+73% YoY) that Microsoft cannot convert fast enough — capacity constraints through at least end-2026, capex $45.2B in the quarter and >$50B guided next. The RPO is flat sequentially and bookings fell 12% YoY, and OpenAI is 10.8% of revenue (~45% of RPO at Dec 2025) — a concentration asterisk on the biggest backlog in the dossier. The enterprise moat (M365/Windows/GitHub, OpenAI partnership, 60+ regions, own Maia silicon) is the hardest to leave in the set, and the stock is flat over 12 months at 20.9x forward — the market is pricing the AI-capex ROI question, not the growth.

**3. ANET — Arista Networks ($186.42).** The purest AI-networking shortage play: Q3 revenue $3.66B, +58.5% YoY (fastest since IPO), FY2026 guidance raised a fourth time to ~$13.3B, first 2027 guide ~$18.5B (+38–40%) vs ~$16.4B consensus, purchase commitments $11.4B with the beyond-12-months slice jumping to $2.1B from $0.3B, and Ullal's "2-year problem… until 2028". But the trap is live: Nvidia's Spectrum-X now leads Arista in datacenter Ethernet (20.4% vs 18.7%, IDC Q2 2026), deferred revenue fell sequentially for the first time, and insiders sold $1.56B in twelve months with no purchases. The growth case is the best in the field; the share contest is being lost.

**4. APH — Amphenol ($76.36).** Record Q3 orders $12.17B (+99% YoY, book-to-bill 1.19), IT datacom +117% YoY and 48% of sales, AI ~36% of total revenue. **5.
