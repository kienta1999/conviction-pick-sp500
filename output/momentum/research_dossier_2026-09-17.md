## 1) SNDK — Sandisk (NAND flash memory; pure-play NAND)

**1. Shortage thesis.** Yes — NAND in structural shortage, driven by AI datacenter buildout. Enterprise SSD (eSSD) demand is the engine: eSSDs were 43% of NAND market revenue in Q1 2026 (Counterpoint via SamMobile, ~June 2026) and ~48% of bits in Q2 2026 (Counterpoint via Club386, Aug 2026). Concrete evidence: (a) TrendForce via Zacks (2026-09-17): NAND flash prices up ~3x since Jan 2026; suppliers prioritized capex on DRAM/HBM, capping NAND supply; (b) Sandisk NBM contracted business running at gross margins >80% — Morgan Stanley's Joseph Moore called this "emblematic of a commodity in a shortage" (TheStreet, ~late June 2026); (c) Moore: "no line of sight to balance" from Sandisk management; models ASP increases through CY2026 and into 2027; (d) Sandisk pushed a 10% NAND price increase in late summer 2026 (TechRadar, early Sept 2026); (e) DRAM deficit spilling over — UBS's Nicolas Gaudois (Fool, 2026-07-06) forecasts DRAM supply in deficit until at least 2Q28, with demand +36.2% vs supply +19.3% in 2027; Sandisk's new high-bandwidth flash (HBF) positioned as a DRAM/HBM-adjacent alternative.
**2. Backlog / order book.** Sandisk's "New Business Model" (NBM) = multi-year supply contracts with firm financial commitments and locked pricing/volume. CFO Luis Visoso, Q4 FY26 earnings call (2026-08-05, MarketBeat transcript): total expected NBM revenue ≥$93.9B at floor pricing; remaining performance obligation (RPO) $59.8B at quarter end, $91.1B including two NBMs signed after the quarter closed; $16.5B in cash deposits/financial guarantees (mostly via third-party institutions) backstop customer obligations. Contracts expected to cover >half of FY27 bits and ~2/3 of FY28 bits (Zacks, 2026-09-04). Eight customers signed; management will stay "highly selective" on new NBMs.
**3. Category position.** NOT #1. NAND market (revenue share): Q2 2026 — Samsung 25–29%, SK Hynix 22%, YMTC 14%, Kioxia 14%, Micron 13%, Sandisk ~11% (Counterpoint via Club386, Aug 2026; TrendForce, 2026-08-18 puts Micron #3 by revenue ahead of Sandisk). Sandisk is a top-5/6 NAND maker and the only pure-play NAND company (post split from WDC; no DRAM overhang). Technology lead claim: BICS/Bixby leadership in TLC/QLC (CEO Goeckeler, Q4 call 2026-08-05) and first-mover HBF.
**4. Irreplaceability / disintermediation risk.** No hyperscaler fabricates NAND; commodity memory has no real "in-house" path. Threats: Chinese YMTC just overtook Sandisk in share (14% vs 11%, Q2 2026) with a $4.9B IPO planned; SSD controller + firmware could be in-sourced but the silicon itself cannot. **Irreplaceability score: 6/10** — Sandisk-the-company is replaceable in the NAND supply base; NAND-the-product is not substitutable, and shortage makes every supplier's bits sell.
**5. Recent catalysts (last ~3 months).** (a) Q4 FY26 earnings 2026-08-05: revenue $8.97B (+372% YoY, +51% QoQ, above guidance), GAAP EPS $43.97, gross margin 84.6% (26.2% a year ago); ~two-thirds of the sequential revenue growth from pricing (Fool, 2026-09-13). (d) Morgan Stanley Overweight — Moore: NBM "range of outcomes skewed to the upside." (e) HBF (high-bandwidth flash) development as HBM-adjacent alternative amid DRAM deficit. (f) Zero-debt balance sheet, FY26 operating cash flow $11.67B.

(d) Valuation: stock ~23x off its 52-week low (~$81 → ~$1,600–2,300), 12x trailing sales (Fool 2026-09-13); margin expansion is largely priced in. (e) NBM customer failures in a downturn (mitigated by $16.5B guarantees). **Shortage score: 9/10.**

## CF — CF Industries Holdings

### 1. Shortage thesis
The strongest genuine *commodity* shortage case of the three. Quantified: the Iran conflict has removed ~4–4.5 million metric tons of traded urea and ~1 million metric tons of ammonia from the market; China maintains strict urea export quotas for food security; Europe's gas-cost disadvantage persists (CF's US gas cost advantage vs Yara reached ~$200–400/ton of ammonia, per Wedbush, 2026). Nitrogen demand is structurally inelastic (food production; global grain stocks near decade lows). CF ran at >99% of available ammonia capacity in Q1 2026 to capture pricing. **Not applicable in the CAT sense** — fertilizer is sold on short-cycle spot/forward contracts, not multi-year backlogs.
