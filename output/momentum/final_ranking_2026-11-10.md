## Per-name thesis

**1. CDNS — Cadence Design Systems (\$333.73).** The purest toll-booth on the AI buildout: record \$8.2bn backlog at Q3 2026 (26 Oct 2026), \$4.2bn of RPO converting over the next 12 months (~65% coverage of guided FY2026 revenue), Q3 revenue \$1.633bn +22% YoY, FY2026 guidance raised a third time (revenue \$6.387–6.417bn, non-GAAP EPS \$8.37–8.43). Only three firms make production EDA (Big-3 >85% share, SemiAnalysis 22 May 2026), and hyperscaler in-sourcing *increases* Cadence demand. The stock is only +2.7% in 12 months and −19.9% off its 52-week high with a +23.4% analyst target — the one name where moat, backlog and price all point the same way. Single pick: EV +16.8% clears the guardrail. FLAG: ~40x FY2026 guided non-GAAP EPS; RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN + INVENTORY_BUILD flags judged mostly benign but unexplained in Q3 call summaries — watch the hardware.

**2. MSI — Motorola Solutions (\$525.11).** The best moat-plus-backlog combination in the field: record ending backlog \$16.6bn at Q3 2026 (+14% YoY, +\$1.0bn sequentially, ~1.27× guided FY2026 revenue), orders +25% YoY, Silvus guided slightly over \$900m in 2026 with ~+30% in 2027, D-Fend ~\$185m pro-forma full-year 2026 (+40–50% in 2027; only ~\$45m booked in 2026 — Phase 3.5 clarification). Customers (police/fire/federal agencies) cannot in-source mission-critical radio networks — irreplaceability 8/10. The drag: organic growth is only ~5% (the rest is acquisition-fuelled), the stock sits ~0.5% below its 52-week high with only +4.2% analyst upside, and memory-cost inflation (~\$150m in 2026, possibly doubling in 2027) is a direct margin threat. EV +10.7% on adjudicated scenarios (base \$590, bull \$675, bear \$440) — below the guardrail, flagged. A's single pick.

EV +7.6% on adjudicated scenarios (base \$139, bull \$163, bear \$90) — below the guardrail, flagged. D's single pick on duration mis-pricing (capacity under construction insufficient through 2030); C ranked it #10 on the same facts.
