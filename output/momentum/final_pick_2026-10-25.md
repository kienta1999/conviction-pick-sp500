## THE PICK: MU — Micron Technology

Information Technology / Semiconductors. Frozen screen price $877.55. Market cap $992.9B. Forward PE 4.24, analyst mean target $1561.91 (+78.0%).

## Thesis

Micron is the doctrine's founding pattern with the risk engineered out of it: a structural HBM/DRAM/NAND shortage that customers have converted from spot exposure into contracted, deposit-backed revenue. Roughly $150B of remaining performance obligations sit under 26 take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements, backed by ~$32B of customer financial commitments, with HBM sold out through 2026 and the vast majority of 2027 committed. Hyperscalers are pre-financing the fabs of one of only three firms on earth that can make HBM — and every custom ASIC they build to in-source compute still needs that HBM, which is why the disintermediation trap points at Micron's customers' other suppliers, not at Micron.

## The shortage + backlog evidence

- ~$150B RPO across 26 take-or-pay SCAs (up from ~$100B / 16 SCAs one quarter earlier), ~$32B customer commitments, mostly cash deposits — disclosed 30 Sep 2026, verified verbatim from the Q4 call.
- HBM sold out through 2026 with price/volume agreed including HBM4; over 75% of FY27 output committed; management sees no supply-demand balance in sight.
- Screen: revenue growth +157% TTM, operating margin 81%, latest quarter +379% YoY, forward PE 4.24 at $877.55.

## Why it is the category leader

One of three leading-edge memory makers on earth (with SK hynix and Samsung); #3 in DRAM (~23.3%) and HBM (~18%), co-designing custom HBM4E with Nvidia — design-in lock-in on top of process-tech scarcity.

## Why it is irreplaceable

No hyperscaler operates a memory fab and none can build one inside the horizon (decade-scale, deep process IP). The honest weakness is not in-sourcing but share: HBM share slipped 21% to 18% in 2Q26, and CXMT presses conventional DRAM from below. Panel ballots rated the trap low (B) to medium (A).
