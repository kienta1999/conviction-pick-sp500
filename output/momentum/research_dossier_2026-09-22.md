## Per-name condensed findings (from the batch dossiers)

**SNDK (Sandisk).** NAND shortage real: 2026–27 sold out industry-wide, contract prices +70–75% QoQ in Q2. But #5–6 globally (11–13% share), commodity product, price escalation decelerating (TrendForce Q3 +10–15%), stock +600–820% YTD then −35% from peak. Verdict: monetizes a genuine shortage at record prices but the cycle-top markers are visible.

**MU (Micron).** Fulfilling half to two-thirds of HBM demand; no new fab output until FY2028; $100B contracted floor, $22B prepaid. Q3 FY2026: $41.46B revenue (+346% YoY), GM 84.9%. Lead on HBM4 is temporal (Samsung will qualify; SK Hynix neck-and-neck). Stock +250% YTD. Flag RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN → BENIGN (record-shipment mechanics, $22B cash deposits). Verdict: sold out faster than it can build, but priced for perfection and the moat lead is temporary.

**NVDA (NVIDIA).** Supply chain "running flat out," meeting 70% of orders; ~$1T backlog; 80–85% share with CUDA moat; 21x forward EPS "undemanding if durable." Trap axis: hyperscaler custom-ASIC in-sourcing of inference in progress; share 92%→80–85%. Flags HIGH_ACCRUALS + RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN → BENIGN (supply-securement economics). Verdict: bottleneck owner; the risk is a capex digestion phase, not competition.

**ANET (Arista).** Demand surge, not shortage (shortage score 5): Q2 record $3.036B (+37.7%), FY2026 guide raised to $12.6B (~40% growth). NOT #1 in datacenter Ethernet (NVIDIA 20.4% > Arista 18.7%); merchant-silicon (Broadcom) = no chip moat; top-3 hyperscalers ~55% of revenue. Verdict: raised-40%-growth guide with sticky EOS, but the biggest threat is NVIDIA's integrated stack.

**NEM (Newmont).** World's largest gold producer; record Q2 FCF $2.2B (+29%); mine supply peaking (S&P: 110M oz 2026 → 103M oz 2028), central-bank demand strong. Irreplaceability 3: gold squeeze accrues to the metal, not the miner; zero pricing power; AISC crept $1,029 (Q1) → $1,621 (Q2). Verdict: pure gold-beta vehicle with operating leverage, not a shortage-pricing-power story.

**GOOGL (Alphabet).** Cleanest demand>supply print: Cloud backlog $514B, Cloud +82% YoY, record share-gainer (Synergy 15%, up from 13%). Binding constraint is capex: Q2 capex $44.9B (~2x YoY), FCF −$5.9B first negative quarter since 2004, buybacks paused. Flag HIGH_ACCRUALS → mostly BENIGN/optical (unrealized equity gains). Verdict: fastest-growing hyperscaler in the tightest infrastructure market, but momentum is capex-gated.

**KLAC (KLA).** #1 process control (55–56% share), $12.57B backlog (+60%), advanced-packaging process-control guide raised to ~$1.1B CY2026. Irreplaceability 9: in-sourcing threat ~zero; custom hyperscaler silicon increases inspection intensity. But the July beat-and-raise was "skinny" — stock fell 9% on the print; expectations priced. Flag RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN → BENIGN (milestone billing on booming backlog, $3.77B FCF). Verdict: the toll booth on AI chip complexity, but the explosive-move bar is set high.

**AMAT (Applied Materials).** #1 WFE by revenue; record Q3 ($9.115B, +25%); CY2026 Semi Systems growth guide raised to >30%; doubling quarterly output by 2028.
