## One-paragraph thesis

Cadence is the purest toll-booth on the AI buildout in this field: every advanced chip on earth is designed with EDA software from just three companies (SemiAnalysis EDA primer, 22 May 2026 — Big-3 hold >85% combined share), and the disintermediation trap runs backwards here — when hyperscalers build their own chips (Google TPU, Amazon Graviton, Meta MTIA, Microsoft Maia), they *become* Cadence customers rather than replacing it. Q3 2026 (26 Oct 2026) delivered revenue \$1.633bn (+22% YoY) and non-GAAP EPS \$2.11, with FY2026 guidance raised for the third consecutive quarter to revenue \$6.387–6.417bn (~21% growth) and non-GAAP EPS \$8.37–8.43. The growth is visible, not hoped for: a record \$8.2bn backlog at Q3, with \$4.2bn of remaining performance obligations converting over the next 12 months — a 65% coverage ratio against guided FY2026 revenue. At \$333.73 the stock is only +2.7% in 12 months and −19.9% off its 52-week high despite the acceleration — the one name in the field where the moat, the backlog and the price all point the same way.

## Why it's the category leader

Cadence is one of three production-EDA vendors on earth, and the duopoly-with-Siemens structure is the category: Synopsys + Cadence + Siemens EDA, with the Big-3 over 85% combined share (SemiAnalysis, 22 May 2026). Cadence's Q3 backlog (\$8.2bn) is the largest contracted book in the field relative to guided revenue, on >85% recurring revenue at 88.5% gross margin and near-100% retention. Operating margin 28.9%, ROE 22.9%, net debt/EBITDA 0.6x. It is the only name in the field whose customers' in-sourcing *increases* its demand.

## Why it's irreplaceable

The moat is decades of solver physics, foundry PDKs and signoff certification that no customer has replicated since the 1970s–80s. Hyperscalers building their own silicon run full EDA stacks; turnkey ASIC houses hold EDA licences on hyperscalers' behalf. All four lenses rated disintermediation risk low — C's pick rests on it, and A, B and D all made CDNS their runner-up on the same logic. The honest caveats: (a) AI-native chip design could eventually commoditise the tool layer — Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 reportedly designed a functional chip autonomously in 48 hours (Jul 2026), and CDNS fell 9–10% on that news; Cadence's defence is that its solvers are the ground-truth verifier AI models need; (b) China export controls (imposed May 2025, substantially eased Jul 2025) remain a live overhang, with 100+ domestic Chinese EDA vendors; (c) Synopsys+Ansys scale (\$34.9bn Ansys deal closed Jul 2025).
