## Risk lens & leverage-safety note

Concentration remains the headline risk: 7 of the top 10 are Information Technology and 6 touch the AI-capex chain directly, so this ranking is substantially a single-factor bet, with GOOGL, PH, and VRTX the only genuine diversifiers. Highest-risk names: SNDK (commodity pricing on peak 84.6% gross margins — the classic peak-cycle shape) and MU (ASP-led earnings). Steadiest: KLAC (hard backlog, recurring services mix) and PH (diversified industrial). Drawdown reality: MU fell ~28% in two months on narrative alone in a recent window and KLAC is ~37% below its high while its backlog grew 60% — these names move violently in both directions, and any leverage against them must survive a 50%+ peak-to-trough move. Education, not advice: no leverage multiple or position size is recommended, and POLICY.md is cash-only for this system.
