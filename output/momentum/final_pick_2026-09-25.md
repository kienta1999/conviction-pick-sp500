# Momentum conviction pick — 2026-09-25 (run 4/30)

## One-paragraph thesis

Micron is the most concrete pre-sold capacity story in the S&P 500 right now: all 2026 HBM supply is sold out, it can fill only half to two-thirds of customer demand with "no line of sight on when supply catches up" (CEO Mehrotra, FQ3 call Jun 24, 2026), and 16 strategic agreements carry ~$100B of minimum contracted revenue with ~$22B already collected as upfront customer deposits — customers are prepaying for capacity that doesn't exist yet. The HBM die penalty structurally drains ordinary DRAM supply while only three firms on earth can make leading-edge DRAM, so the shortage consensus (through 2028 or beyond) has both a demand and a supply wall behind it. At 6.85x forward earnings and $1,513 analyst mean (+41%), the price embeds cyclical skepticism, not super-cycle euphoria — the EV math says +27% expected over 12–18 months.

## The shortage + backlog evidence (verified)

- **$100B contracted / $22B in hand:** 16 strategic customer agreements, ~$100B minimum contracted revenue; ~$22B of upfront deposits already collected (~$18B cash + ~$4B letters of credit). Verified — 14 of the 16 agreements carry the floor.
- **Physically sold out:** "All 2026 HBM supply sold out"; filling "half to two-thirds" of HBM demand; no meaningful new fab output until mid-2027 (Idaho) then H2 2028 (Singapore) — slightly sooner than the dossier's "FY2028", still no relief this cycle.
- **HBM4 ramping ~2x HBM3E speed** — the next node arrives into a sold-out market.
- **Catalyst in 5 days:** FQ4 FY2026 earnings Sept 30, 2026, guided at ~$50B revenue and ~86% gross margin (verified). The immediate referendum.
- Recent context: Anthropic partnership (Sept 2026); UBS/Cantor PTs $1,500–1,600.

## Why it's the category leader

One of three scaled global DRAM producers (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) in a market where leading-edge HBM is made by effectively the same three. FQ3 FY2026: gross margin ~86%, EPS power at 6.85x forward — the market is pricing the earnings of a cyclical peak, while the contract structure says the earnings have utility-like visibility for the next 18–30 months.

## Why it's irreplaceable

Memory is the single input hyperscalers cannot build themselves — there is no "in-house HBM" project at any cloud vendor, and 2-year, $10B+ fab build cycles plus process-tech secrets mean no new entrant can appear. CXMT's DRAM share rose to ~9.5% in Q2 2026 — worth watching, not a moat breach. **Disintermediation risk: LOW.** The doctrine's trap does not touch this name; its risk is the cycle, not substitution.
