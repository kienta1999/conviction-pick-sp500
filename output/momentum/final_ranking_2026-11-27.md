## Per-name theses

**1. CBOE —** The only true monopoly in the batch: exclusive SPX options through 2051 (25-year S&P DJI extension signed 29 Sep 2026, confirmed), SPX 0DTE ADV at a record 3.2m contracts (+34% YoY), 0DTE at a record 65% of SPX activity, with Q3 net revenue +18% and adjusted EPS +36%. The discount: the licence news is already in the price, analyst upside is only +3.2%, the stock ran ~10% in 12 days on a VIX spike above 28, and the growth engine is retail 0DTE flow (58% of 0DTE volume) that can reverse in a calm market or under regulatory scrutiny. Three of four lenses picked it; D dissented on entry timing and picked EMR.

**2. EMR —** The only name with a genuine, growing, quantified backlog: \$8.2B, +11% YoY, book-to-bill 1.01, underlying orders +14% in Q4 FY2026, a \$13B project funnel (+17%), with semiconductor orders +59% (Test & Measurement), LNG sales +68% and power sales +24% — riding the AI-infrastructure power build-out into a supplier whose DeltaV systems cannot be ripped out of a running refinery or fab without years of re-validation. The honest caveat: FY2027 guidance is only ~5% sales growth — orders are long-cycle and slow to convert, and the backlog's within-12-months share already slipped to 70% from ~75%. Lens D's single pick (conviction 7/10) precisely because it is the least-priced-in name with a real shortage and a real backlog; it also pulled back ~7% from its post-earnings high while CBOE chased a vol spike.

**3. ISRG —** The best moat in the batch at the worst moment to pay for it: 12,094-system installed base (+12% YoY), 546 placements in Q3 (+28%, incl 315 da Vinci 5), procedures +15% (da Vinci +14%, Ion +31%), >10,000 procedures/day, 84% recurring revenue. But management is voluntarily cutting instrument/accessory revenue per procedure ~3–5% from H1 2027 (fully implemented basis), US procedure growth decelerated to +10%, and real competition has arrived for the first time in 20 years (Hugo FDA-cleared, Ottava approved, Versius pending). 34.3x forward for decelerating growth.

**4. VEEV —** The forced Vault CRM migration is the closest thing to a genuine demand surge in this batch: the Salesforce agreement expired 1 Sep 2025, Veeva CRM support ends 31 Dec 2029, and 12 of the top 20 biopharmas have committed to Vault CRM, with 180+ customers live. Q2 FY2027 revenue +18% with guidance raised. The catch, flagged by every lens that ranked it: Q3 FY2027 earnings land **Thu 3 Dec 2026 after close** — 6 days after this run — a binary event after a 13.3% one-month slide and eight straight down sessions into 19 Nov, and the 10-Q shows no multi-year RPO ("not significant") — revenue is re-underwritten every annual renewal. A post-print name, not a pre-print chase.

The steadier compounders are V, LLY and CBOE — but steadiness is the problem for this doctrine: their EV upsides are +7.9% to +11.3%, i.e. fairly priced. Drawdown reality: a high-beta cyclical can fall 50%+ fast, and even a large-cap monopoly whose growth engine is retail speculation can gap on a vol collapse or a regulatory decision; any use of leverage should be tempered by that. This is risk education, not a leverage recommendation — no specific leverage multiple or position size is suggested.
