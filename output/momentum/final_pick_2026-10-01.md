## The shortage (why this, why now)

Memory is in a structural shortage, not a cyclical upswing. All major NAND/DRAM makers sold out 2026 capacity; Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron were reported sold out of projected 2027 supply as of August 2026 (dossier research_batch1, sourced to company statements and TrendForce). Micron's fresh fiscal Q4 2026 print (Sept 30, 2026 — beat-and-raise): revenue $54.23B vs ~$51B consensus, non-GAAP EPS $33.42, and — the number that matters for the shortage thesis — **~$150B of contracted revenue / RPO from 26 take-or-pay customer agreements** (up from 16 in June), with **$12.3B of customer cash deposits in a single quarter**. Customers are funding Micron's balance sheet to secure supply through 2030; management called FQ1's lighter gross-margin guide "the floor" with supply-demand getting "even tighter through FY27–28."

## Why the category leader

Micron is one of only three firms that can produce HBM (with Samsung and SK Hynix). In DRAM/HBM it holds a genuine oligopoly seat: the verifier confirmed the "only ~3 firms" claim. TrendForce/Q2-2026 data puts the AI-memory stack — HBM and enterprise NAND — as the fastest-growing, highest-margin slice of the semiconductor market, and Micron's 87.0% non-GAAP gross margin (FQ4 beat) is the scoreboard.

## Why irreplaceable (honest disintermediation caveat)

The moat lens (Panelist C) judged this the hardest asset class in the dossier to disintermediate: building a leading-edge memory fab costs tens of billions and takes years of process qualification — hyperscalers are responding by signing take-or-pay contracts and prepaying cash, not by building fabs. **Caveat, stated honestly:** DRAM/NAND is still a commodity interface; within the oligopoly, customers can and do switch share between Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron. The shortage is the moat right now, not technology. The 2026-09-30 after-hours fade (margin-guide miss, inventory days 131 vs 122) is the live signal that the cycle has a ceiling.

## Scenario drivers (shortage math)

- **Units/capacity:** 2026 capacity sold out across makers; 2027 pre-booking underway. Supply grows ~low-teens% annually (litho-limited) vs AI-driven demand +25–30%.

- **Backlog conversion:** $150B RPO converts at floor/ceiling pricing — earnings are contract-walled through 2028 even if spot softens.
