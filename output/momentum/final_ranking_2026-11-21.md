## Top-3 theses

**1. MSFT — Microsoft (\$484.52).** The cleanest shortage signal in the field, and the single pick — see `final_pick_2026-11-21.md`. Commercial RPO \$677B (+73% YoY) with ~\$203B to be recognised in 12 months, Azure +48% against demand that "continues to exceed available supply" (Q1 FY27 call, 28 Oct 2026), and ~\$170B of calendar-2026 capex to close the gap. The moat is the enterprise stack plus an 80+ region footprint plus the exclusive OpenAI partnership — but the trap is live and runs from the customer side: OpenAI (11% of revenue) is building its own compute (Stargate) and multi-clouding. Shares are flat over 12 months while the backlog grew 73%, so the shortage is not being chased at a high.

**2. WST — West Pharmaceutical (\$390.30).** The GLP-1 tollbooth: HVP components \$466.7m in Q3 2026, +20.3% organically and 53% of sales, GLP-1 category organic growth over 30%, HVP for GLP-1 just under 12% of total revenue, and 200+ EU Annex 1 upgrade projects added year to date. The #1 position (~24% of elastomeric closures vs Datwyler ~18%, a single third-party estimate) is protected by qualification/regulatory lock-in — components are qualified into a drug programme years before launch. The constraint is the 18–24-month capacity lag. Caveats that cost it the top spot: the "demand exceeds supply" claim is unverified at primary-source level (management said current capacity should support near-term demand), and at 37.6x forward earnings the story is already paid for (+51.8% in 12 months).

**3. MSI — Motorola Solutions (\$520.21).** The strongest verified order momentum in the field: record backlog \$16.6B (+14% YoY, ~15 months of revenue under contract), Q3 orders up over 20% (25% per the call), record Q3 sales \$3.38B (+12%) and non-GAAP EPS \$4.69 (+16%, beat), guidance raised again. The moat is the best in the field (irreplaceability 9/10): switching a P25 system means replacing every handset, vehicle unit and base station at once on 15–20-year cycles, and governments cannot in-source radio infrastructure. Caveats: the ">80% of the US public-safety LMR market" claim rests only on a years-old Morningstar note — recent estimates say 60%+ for North American LMR — and the "shortage" is a procurement-driven order surge, not supply scarcity, so upside is durable compounding rather than an explosive squeeze. Leverage from the \$4.4B Silvus deal (long-term debt \$9.36B vs cash \$774m) is the balance-sheet watch item.

**4. EMR — Emerson Electric (\$150.93).** The best genuine shortage-adjacent thesis after MSFT: Ovation orders +74% in fiscal Q1 2026 on behind-the-meter datacenter projects, power business +25% in Q4 with control systems up ~45%, DeltaV running more than 50% of every LNG installation on the planet, backlog \$8.2B (+11% YoY) and a \$13B project funnel (+17%). Irreplaceability 8/10 — hyperscalers buy the power plant, not the control system, and cannot in-source safety-critical process control. The ceiling is conversion speed: only 70% of the backlog converts within 12 months and FY2027 sales growth is guided at ~5%.

**5. EW — Edwards Lifesciences (\$101.85).** Dominant TAVR leader (66% global share) with a genuine new patient pool opening via the PROGRESS trial (TAVR superior to surveillance in moderate AS, NEJM 2 Nov 2026) and a 44%-growing TMTT franchise (\$207m in Q3) on its way to a \$2B-by-2030 target. Q3 2026 beat + raise (4 Nov 2026): sales \$1.72B, +11.2% cc, adjusted EPS \$0.80 (+19%). Irreplaceability 9/10 — only ~4 firms on earth can make a transcatheter valve at scale. The ceiling is time: core TAVR growth is high-single-digits and moderate-AS revenue depends on guideline/coverage decisions years away.

**Names 6–10, one line each:** **VEEV** — #1 life-sciences cloud with a forced Vault CRM migration tailwind (13 of top 20 pharma committed) and an unproven AI option, growing mid-teens at a premium multiple, earnings 3 Dec. **MA** — irreplaceable payments duopoly compounding at mid-teens on a clean Q3 beat, but no shortage and ~33x trailing earnings. **EOG** — lowest-cost shale with a real gas/AI-power tailwind and a big Q3 beat, but oil is oversupplied and nothing is scarce or locked in. **COP** — largest independent E&P, +59% in 12 months on a price-driven Q3 beat, but volumes falling and no backlog or moat; only one ballot ranked it, at #3. **ABBV** — strong immunology share-gain compounder (Skyrizi/Rinvoq +23–24%) with three guidance raises this year, but two drugs are 48.7% of revenue in the most substitutable pharma category.
