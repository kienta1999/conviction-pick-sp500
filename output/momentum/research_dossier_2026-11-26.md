- **Backlog:** n/a. Nearest forward indicator: ~25% of payments volume comes up for client renewal in FY2027 (vs ~20% in FY2026) — a renewal wave, not a booked backlog. VAS revenue $14bn in FY2026, +30% constant dollars.
- **Category position:** clear #1 — 62.27% of combined Visa+Mastercard purchase volume ($15.34tn vs $9.29tn, 12 months to Jun 30, 2026); US share 70.96% in Q2 2026.
- **Irreplaceability (8/10):** two-sided network, 281bn transactions in FY2026 across 200+ countries. Threats: account-to-account/open-banking rails bypassing interchange; stablecoins as alternative settlement (Visa's answer is to co-opt them — settlement on 10 blockchains); FY2027 renewal wave with client incentives already +21% to $5.12bn, growing faster than revenue.
- **Recent catalysts:** Q4 FY2026 (27 Oct 2026) adj EPS $3.48 (+17%, beat $3.43); net revenue $12.14bn (+13%, beat $12.08bn); FY2026 revenue record $45.9bn (+15%), "strongest underlying growth in more than 15 years". - **Risks:** renewal/incentive squeeze; international transaction revenue +8% despite +14% cross-border volume (monetization lags volume); FY2027 guide steps down from FY2026's 15%; fwd PE 24.8 near highs.
- **Verdict:** best-in-class tollbooth with accelerating cross-border demand, but no shortage and no explosive-return setup — a compounder, not a momentum breakout bet.

## 11. ABNB — Airbnb ($172.15; rev +14.9% TTM; fwd PE 27.1; next earnings 2027-02-11)

- **Shortage thesis (5/10):** partial, demand-side. Q3 2026 (5 Nov 2026): Nights and Seats Booked +16% YoY to 155m (acceleration from +10% in Q2), GBV +21% to $27.7bn, revenue +19% to $4.87bn — fastest revenue growth in 3+ years. Management cited "lead times extended across major travel corridors" and North America nights at "their fastest pace in four years". Supply is not short in aggregate (~9m listings globally), but regulatory caps shrink legal supply in major cities, pushing prices up — ADR +4% YoY to ~$179. The shortage is city-level and regulation-driven.
- **Backlog:** no formal backlog. Unearned fees $1,536m at 30 Sep 2026 — down 16% YoY even as bookings grew 21%, because Reserve Now, Pay Later lets guests book further ahead without prepaying; RNPL added ~3% net lift to Q3 nights. Q4 guidance: revenue $3.22–3.28bn (+16–18%), FY2026 revenue outlook raised to "high teens" (third upward revision in 2026).
- **Category position:** clear #1 in short-term rentals — ~44% of global STR market (Skift 2026) vs Booking.com ~18%, Vrbo ~9%; US professionally managed STRs: 51% of reservations (KeyData Q2 2026). ~9m listings vs Vrbo ~2m.
- **Irreplaceability (6/10):** two-sided network effects; hosts multi-list but Airbnb delivers the majority of reservations. Threats: direct bookings (21% of US professionally managed reservations in Q2 2026; Airbnb piloting direct-booking links at 6–10% host fee vs standard ~15.5%); Booking.com/Vrbo; hotels and Google Travel.
- **Recent catalysts:** Q3 2026 beat-and-raise — EPS $3.16 (+43%, beat $2.85); net income $1.9bn (39% margin); adjusted EBITDA $2.5bn (+22%, 52% margin); shares surged ~12% on the print (to ~$188.61 intraday Nov 6). New $12bn buyback authorization (3 Nov 2026) on top of $2.7bn remaining. App nights +29% YoY, 66% of nights booked.
- **Risks:** regulation is the structural overhang — NYC Local Law 18 cut listings ~90%; Barcelona ends licensed short stays by Nov 2028 (10,101 licenses not renewed); Budapest, Athens, Paris caps; Maui County Bill 9 phases out apartment-zone vacation rentals. Founders sold ~$1.12bn of ~$1.14bn insider sales in the last 12 months (under trading plans). Price well below the post-earnings ~$189 spike — momentum has already partially faded.
- **Verdict:** real demand re-acceleration and a Q3 beat-and-raise with a fresh $12bn buyback, but the "shortage" is regulation-created and city-specific, and regulatory creep caps the upside.
