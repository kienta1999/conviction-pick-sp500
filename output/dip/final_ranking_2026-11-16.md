  (+28.9%) with Dec-qtr guidance ahead of consensus, yet the stock is ~54%

  process control, 42.6% operating margin, 91.4% ROE) is intact; AI raises

  WFE market expected to exceed $200B in 2027. Value-trap caveat: the
  WFE cycle is real and the sales multiple (29.1x) is rich.

  in Cisco's history (revenue $18.52B +24%, EPS $1.40 +40%) with FY27

## Risk lens & leverage-safety note

- **Sector concentration:** the top 6 are all AI-infrastructure proxies
  (equipment, data-center build, networking). A single AI-capex pause
  hits the whole list at once — the ranking is a theme bet as much as a
  stock list. SCHW/INTU/TPR are the only non-AI-capex names in the top 10.
- **Highest-risk vs steadiest:** deepest drawdowns (KLAC -49.5%, INTU
  -48.8%, AMAT -48.7%) are also the biggest snap-back candidates — and the
  most likely to keep falling if the Nov 5 fear proves right. HWM, SCHW
  and TPR are the steadier compounders; their upside is smaller because
  their dips are shallower.
- **Leverage:** a beaten-down name can keep falling — catching a knife
  with margin is brutal. This system's policy is cash-only (POLICY.md §2).
  No leverage multiple or position size beyond the policy formula in the
  final-pick file is recommended here. Education, not advice.
