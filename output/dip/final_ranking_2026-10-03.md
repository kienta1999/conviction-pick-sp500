**#2 IDXX — the compounder on sale (also the single pick).** Q2 EPS +18%, recurring CAG revenue +10.3% organic on volume *and* pricing, FY26 guidance raised — yet ~33% off its high at the cheapest forward multiple in years (~34.7x vs ~45–55x norm, verified), 0.5x net leverage, ~110% FCF conversion.
