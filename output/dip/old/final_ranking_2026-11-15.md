## Per-name thesis

**1. KLAC —** The purest quality compounder in the 14: near-monopoly process control (91% of Q1 FY2027 revenue), record Q1 revenue \$4.14bn (+29% YoY), guided December quarter to \$4.7bn, yet −49.5% off its high after a −13.3% fall on 5 Nov with no company news. The dip is temporary — multiple compression from 48.4x to 34.4x TTM in ~5 weeks while earnings rose — and the rebound catalyst is the December-quarter print (late Jan / early Feb 2027) plus NVDA's 17 Nov print. The moat holds: AI raises defect sensitivity, so inspection intensity per wafer rises; no model can physically inspect a wafer. Value-trap caveat: a genuine 2027 WFE air pocket would hit KLA hardest (memory 40% of the mix), and China is high-20s% of revenue under export controls.

**2. AMAT —** The cheapest WFE name (screen forward P/E ~15.1x) with the strongest print: record Q4 revenue \$10.41bn (+53% YoY), EPS \$4.16 vs \$4.06, Q1 FY2027 guidance above consensus, and the first quantified CY2027 WFE call above 30% — yet it fell 2.7% the next session on the NVDA leadership shock. The dip is temporary (sentiment, not the business) and the catalyst is the same WFE cycle plus the NVDA print on 17 Nov. The moat is the broadest equipment portfolio in the industry with net cash ~\$2.8bn. Value-trap caveat: 2026 WFE growth above 40% may have been pulled forward; the +92% non-GAAP EPS headline is flattered by a \$653m unrealised-loss add-back (GAAP +43%).

**3. HWM —** Best moat (9/10 irreplaceability) and cleanest momentum: Q3 revenue +29% YoY (strongest quarterly growth on record), adjusted EPS +54%, guidance raised — derated ~30% in 90 days on SpaceX/GE narratives years from revenue impact. The dip is temporary (sentiment), the catalyst is continued guidance delivery fading the narrative, and AI is a tailwind (gas turbines are the bottleneck for AI data-center power). Value-trap caveat: at ~33.2x forward earnings it is above its own 5-year median P/E — the margin of safety is growth delivery, not price.

**4. CAT —** Record backlog and +45% EPS growth sold off for a valuation excess committed in June; the data-center power cycle is the question — pause or peak. The Feb 2027 outlook is the proof point. Leverage (2.25x net debt/EBITDA) is the watch item.

**5. FIX —** Backlog \$17.81bn (+90%), operating cash flow ~2x net income, net cash \$2.11bn — the receivables flag is benign (customer advances \$3.87bn fund the build). The cap is 59% tech concentration and a sequential electrical-margin slip plus a CFO transition.

**7. CSCO —** Record beat-and-raise (Q1 FY27 revenue \$18.5bn, EPS \$1.40, FY27 guide raised, AI infra target \$9bn) that fell 6.2% on 13 Nov on order deceleration (+21% vs +35%) and margin compression. The inventory-build flag is benign (pre-build for \$9.3bn AI orders; OCF +36%).
