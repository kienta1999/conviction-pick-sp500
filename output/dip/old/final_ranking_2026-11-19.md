## Per-name thesis

**1. KLAC — the dip is temporary, the moat is intact.** Revenue growth accelerated three straight quarters to +28.9% while purchase commitments jumped to \$8.87B from \$2.50B a year earlier — customers queuing for a near-monopolist's tools while the stock halved on multiple compression alone (48.4x trailing P/E at end-September). Rebound catalyst: the December-quarter print in late January 2027, guided revenue \$4.7B (+42.5%). AI cannot disrupt it: at 3nm/2nm you cannot fabricate what you cannot measure, and AI chips are the most defect-sensitive products ever built. Base +35–50% (B/C/D range), bull +55–90% over 12–18 months. See final_pick_2026-11-19.md for the full scenario table (bear \$110 / base \$195 / bull \$245, EV \$190.50, +31.8%).

**2. LRCX — a record quarter the market sold anyway.** Revenue \$8.22B, +54% year over year, the fifth consecutive quarterly record, operating margin above 40% for the first time — sold off 6.82% after hours because December guidance only narrowly cleared consensus, then -16% in the 5 November rout. The rebound catalyst is the deferred December shipments landing in the March quarter plus the 2027 WFE outlook above \$200B, with a fresh \$25B buyback (roughly 9–10% of market value) as a floor. Moat: etch/deposition oligopoly — every AI accelerator passes through Lam's tools, and re-qualifying a vendor takes quarters. Honest caveat from verification: "duopoly" is overstated; it is a 3-player oligopoly (Lam/AMAT/TEL). Base +35–55%, bull +60–100% over 12–18 months; scenario table below.

**3. FIX — the most AI-proof name in the field, discounted on sentiment.** Record Q3 (revenue +53.7%, EPS \$13.91, backlog \$17.81B +90% year over year, the eighth consecutive quarterly increase) met with a -9.26% fall because it issued no forward guidance and the CFO announced retirement. AI cannot install a chiller or commission a data hall; skilled-labour scarcity plus 4.0M sq ft of modular capacity are the durable edge. Net cash ~\$2.16B, forward P/E 16.3x vs its five-year median 23.64x (verified). Catalyst: guidance resuming at the February print (screen: 18 February; listings conflict with 17 February — date unverified). Base +30–50%, bull +55–90% over 12–18 months; scenario table below.
