## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLA holds ~54-56% of semiconductor process control (verified), the highest-moat seat in WFE: its tools are qualified into customer process flows, so switching means re-qualifying yield learning on a live fab; services run at ~\$820M/quarter (+17% YoY) on contract-like recurring revenue. **Why AI cannot disrupt it:** software cannot inspect a physical wafer. AI *multiplies* the moat — smaller nodes, HBM stacks and hybrid bonding raise defect-detection steps per wafer, and advanced-packaging process-control revenue is guided to ~\$1.1B for CY2026 (+>70% YoY, verified). The honest weakness is mix, not disruption: China is ~26% of revenue under export controls (the dossier notes KLA states it gained share there in Q4 despite the bars), and that exposure caps the irreplaceability score at 9/10 rather than 10.
