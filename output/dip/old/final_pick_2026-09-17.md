### Why it stands above the rest

1. **The dip is provably temporary.** The Q2 2026 tape is the cleanest "price, not business" proof
   in the batch: revenue +19% ($2.89B vs $2.82B consensus), adjusted EPS $2.80 (vs $2.51 consensus),
   procedures +15–16% — and the stock collapsed 10.7% the same day purely because management
   *reaffirmed* rather than raised guidance. The drawdown is a ~51x → ~32x multiple derating on an
   intact compounder, not an impairment.
2. **The moat is untouched and AI-irreplaceable.** Surgeon lock-in (training, installed base,
   razor-and-blade instruments), patent/regulatory walls, and a physical procedure no model can
   perform. The GLP-1 panic is quantified at <3% of da Vinci procedures — a rounding error, not a
   thesis.
3. **It beat the value-trap filter.** Four independent verifier claims (Q2 beat-and-fall, <3%
   bariatric exposure, $8.6B gross cash, ~51x→~32x derating) all **CONFIRMED** from primary sources,
   with no red flags in the last 30 days. The trap-suspect names (ADBE — AI commoditization; VRSK —
   AI actuarial substitution; LVS — structural Macau margin reset; EFX — dead rate catalyst) are
   nowhere near this pick.
4. **The runner-up's dip driver is less temporary.** KLAC's −44% is priced on China export controls
   — a multi-year geopolitical overhang, not a one-quarter sentiment reset. ISRG's catalyst is
   simpler: procedure growth keeps compounding while the multiple re-rates.
