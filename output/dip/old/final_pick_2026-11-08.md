# Dip conviction pick — 2026-11-08: LRCX (Lam Research)

## THE PICK

**LRCX — Lam Research Corporation.** Information Technology / Semiconductor Materials & Equipment. Current price **\$200.44** (verified Fri 6 Nov 2026 close). Market cap ~\$250bn. ## One-paragraph thesis

Lam is the etch-and-deposition leader at the physical heart of the AI buildout — every HBM stack, 3D NAND layer and GAA transistor needs more etch/deposition steps per wafer, not fewer — that just printed a record quarter on every line (revenue \$8.22bn, +54% YoY; record 52.4% gross and 40.0% operating margins; record FCF \$3.24bn) and fell 54% anyway, to 16.4x forward earnings with an 82% Street-implied upside. It is net cash (~\$3.70bn), and management answered the rout with the largest buyback in company history — \$25bn, ~10% of market value — authorised 5 Nov. This is a record-printing, irreplaceable franchise priced as if the cycle just ended. All four lenses picked it #1 with conviction 8/10 — the first unanimous pick in this window.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The concrete, sourced evidence that the drop is about price, sentiment and cycle — not a broken business — verified in Phase 3.5 against Lam's 8-K and earnings call (21 Oct 2026):

- The Q1 FY27 print was a record on every line: revenue \$8.22bn (+54% YoY), non-GAAP EPS \$2.20 vs \$2.18 consensus, record 52.4% gross margin and 40.0% GAAP (40.2% non-GAAP) operating margin, record FCF \$3.24bn — the call itself called it "a record for our free cash flow".
- The December-quarter guide (\$8.6bn ± \$0.4bn revenue) was held back by supply-chain constraints, in CFO Doug Bettinger's words: the guide "would have been a bit higher if not for some near-term supply chain constraints". Shipments planned for December slip to the March quarter — deferred, not lost (secondary reporting, AIStockWire 24 Oct; the "not lost" reading is the dossier's reasonable inference from the guide language, not a company quote).
- The CXMT 4F² DRAM-glut scare (announced 10 Oct 2026) disclosed no yields, customer qualifications, speeds/capacities or shipment volumes — a headline, not a qualified competitor.
- The -16.36% fall on Thu 5 Nov was a market-wide political rout (Philadelphia Semiconductor Index -12%+, its worst since March 2020, on Democratic midterm gains and data-center moratorium / AI-regulation fears plus weak Qualcomm/Arm results) — not company-specific. The stock bounced +8.23% on Fri 6 Nov.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

Lam is the \#1 dry-etch supplier (~45–50% of dry etch; VLSI/KR ranking, Oct 2026), and its own newsroom says it "leads the industry in HBM manufacturing equipment". Etch leadership is what enables 3D NAND scaling toward 1,000 layers, and GAA raises etch/ALD value per wafer. AI cannot disrupt this moat — there is no software substitute for physically etching a wafer; AI *increases* demand for Lam's product, because every HBM stack and GAA transistor needs more etch/deposition steps. The moat's central factual claim was softened by verification from "required for every HBM stack / 3D NAND layer / GAA transistor" (unverifiable as phrased — Tokyo Electron and Applied Materials also supply etch/deposition, and Lam is not the ALD leader) to "etch leader (~45–50% of dry etch) whose tools are used across HBM, 3D NAND and GAA flows". Balance-sheet durability: net cash ~\$3.70bn (cash \$7.44bn, debt ~\$3.74bn at 27 Sep 2026, confirmed exactly from the 8-K balance sheet). **AI-disruption risk: low.**
