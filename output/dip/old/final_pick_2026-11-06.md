# Dip single pick — 2026-11-06

## THE PICK

**ANET — Arista Networks, Inc.** — Information Technology / Communications Equipment. Market cap about \$186bn (screen: 1.17bn shares). Verified close Thu 5 Nov 2026: **\$159.16**. Next earnings: 2027-02-09 (estimated).

## One-paragraph thesis

Arista is the cleanest "great quarter, falling stock" in the field: Q3 2026 revenue grew 58.5% year over year — the fastest since its 2014 IPO — with a 49.1% non-GAAP operating margin, and management raised guidance and gave a first FY2027 outlook of about \$18.5bn revenue (+38–40%), yet the stock fell 10.8% on 5 Nov 2026 in the broad AI-politics rout and sits 27% off its 52-week high. The moat is software — the EOS operating system plus multi-year hyperscaler qualification lock-in — which AI strengthens rather than threatens, since every GPU cluster needs high-speed Ethernet plumbing. With \$14.36bn cash, zero debt, and a forward multiple that has de-rated from about 53x trailing a year ago while earnings nearly doubled, this is a blowout business repriced as if its moat were cracking. It isn't.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The sequence is the evidence: on 3 Nov Arista printed its fastest growth since IPO (revenue +58.5%, beat its own \$3.3bn guide by over \$350m), raised Q4 guidance and issued a first-ever FY2027 outlook of about \$18.5bn (vs \$16.54bn consensus) — and two days later the stock fell 10.8% on the midterm / data-center-moratorium rout, with no company-specific negative event in the quarter and no moratorium law enacted (Congress does not take power until January). A business does not print its fastest growth since IPO and raise guidance the week its moat breaks. The drawdown is a multiple compression on a sentiment shock, not an operating break: fundamentals moved the opposite way from the price for the entire drawdown.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

Arista is the #1 player in cloud/AI Ethernet switching by the panel's read, with the moat in the EOS software and telemetry stack and the installed base, not the chip underneath: hyperscalers qualify vendors over multi-year cycles and rarely switch mid-build. Irreplaceability score 9/10 — the highest in the field. AI cannot route around Arista; AI *is* the demand driver, since every AI accelerator cluster needs high-speed Ethernet, and the 800G switch portfolio is the bottleneck hardware for AI fabrics. The honest second-order threats are Nvidia bundling Spectrum-X with its own GPUs (Nvidia took about 20.4% data-center Ethernet share vs Arista about 18.7% in Q2 2026) and white-box/SONiC commoditisation of the hardware layer — but neither displaces a qualified, software-locked-in fabric, and Arista still grew 58.5% while Nvidia rose. No model can write, qualify and operate a hyperscaler's network fabric.
