## One-paragraph thesis

Lam Research is the highest-quality dip in this field, bought on sale: one of the "big three" wafer-fab equipment oligopoly in plasma etch and deposition, it just reported a fifth consecutive record quarter — fiscal Q1 2027 revenue $8.219bn (+54% YoY), a record 52.4% non-GAAP gross margin, a first-ever 40%+ operating margin, record $3.24bn free cash flow — and still fell ~8–9% after the print and then −16.36% in a single day on 5 Nov, a day with no company news at all. The Dec-quarter guide was capped by supply-chain constraints, not by demand: delayed shipments slip to the March quarter, and preliminary CY2027 WFE spending is above $200bn (vs the mid-$150bn range for CY2026). On 5 Nov the board authorised a record $25bn buyback (~9% of shares) on top of ~$3bn remaining. At 18.2x forward earnings with net cash, the market is pricing a WFE peak, not a broken franchise.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

Lam is one of the "big three" wafer-fab equipment oligopoly (with Applied Materials and Tokyo Electron) in plasma etch and deposition — ALTUS tungsten, SABRE copper plating, VECTOR PECVD. Etch/deposition is physics-bound, capital-intensive, and qualified tool-by-tool into fabs; switching a qualified etch tool mid-node is prohibitively expensive and risky. A large installed base feeds the Customer Support Business Group (~$2.50bn in Q1 FY27, ~30% of revenue; guided >30% growth in CY2026, >15% in CY2027) — recurring spares/services/upgrades revenue. **AI cannot disrupt it** — AI *is* the demand driver: more complex devices mean more etch/deposition steps per wafer, and Q1 FY27 memory revenue was +120% YoY. The named competitive threat is Chinese domestic toolmaker substitution (CXMT's 4F² memory cell) — a real long-run share risk in China, not a product obsolescence threat. Research rebound score 8/10, irreplaceability 9/10. (Verification: the moat's central claims — record margins, oligopoly position, WFE outlook — confirmed; the 9/10 irreplaceability score is the research lens's judgment, not a verifiable fact.)
