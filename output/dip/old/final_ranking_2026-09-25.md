## Top-5 theses (transcribed from the ballots)

**1. IDXX.** Four straight EPS beats and two 2026 guidance raises against a −33%
drawdown is the signature of a sentiment de-rating, not a broken story — and the
moat is near-maximal (installed analyzers, reference labs, vet switching costs;

(Rebound 8; the skeptic lens's own #1 pick — the trap lens cannot touch it.)

**2. BR.** Arguably the most irrational price in the dossier: fwd P/E ~14x vs a
3-yr average of 33.6x while Q4 FY2026 printed recurring revenue +8% CC, adj EPS
+12%, record $305M closed sales, FY2027 guidance 6–8% recurring / 8–12% EPS
growth, FCF at 110% of adjusted earnings, 20th consecutive dividend hike. The
dip is AI/tokenization fear the earnings record rebuts — BR is building the
feared tech itself (DLR: $360B tokenized repo, June 2026; DLX launched Sept 9,
2026). Temporary, with receipts.

**3. CPRT.** The closest thing in the field to an uncopyable moat at a distressed
price — net-cash fortress ($4.4B, net debt/EBITDA −2.34x), national fenced-yard
toll road, record FY2026 revenue $4.7B while trading ~9.5% above its 52w low on
a cyclical insurance-volume trough. Cash-funded $1.9B ACV deal adds a second
growth leg, EPS-accretive from FY2028. No AI model can tow, store, or auction a
wrecked car. ⚠ The honest trap case: ADAS/safety tech structurally reduces
collision severity so total-loss frequency never mean-reverts.

**4. SYK.** Q2 organic sales +9% with double-digit medical/trauma/endoscopy growth
— demand intact — yet −30% at 16.1x forward P/E (trailing P/E 33% below its 5-yr
avg of 42.1) purely on a peripheral-vascular backorder and cyber-recovery costs,
both management-committed to resolve by end-Q3 2026. Mako-installed-base oligopoly
(#1 hip, #2 knee, robotics trends favor Stryker). Catalyst is the most
execution-dependent of the top 4.

**5. ISRG.** Zero debt, $5.2B cash, multiple cut from >60x to ~33x forward on
transitory hits (tariff ~1% margin drag, ACA-subsidy/GLP-1 procedure mix, one

>11,100 da Vinci systems, 3.1M+ annual procedures — the widest medtech moat.
Needs H2 procedure re-acceleration; Medtronic Hugo is a slow, real competitor.
