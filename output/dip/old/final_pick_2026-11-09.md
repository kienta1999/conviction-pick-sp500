## One-paragraph thesis

KLA is the purest quality compounder in this field, bought on sale: the dominant process-control monopolist (irreplaceability 10/10) that just reported a beat-and-guide-up — Q1 FY2027 revenue \$4.14bn, +28.9% YoY, a record 46.1% non-GAAP operating margin, record \$1.34bn free cash flow — raised its Q2 guide to \$4.70bn ± \$200m and its 2026 WFE outlook, pointed to WFE above \$200bn in calendar 2027, and still fell 5.69% after the print and sits ~54.9% off its 52-week high. Smaller nodes, 3D architectures and advanced packaging multiply the value of defect detection, so the AI buildout makes KLA more essential, not less. At 19.5x forward earnings (NTM) with \$8.81bn of buyback authorisation remaining, the market is pricing a cyclical peak, not a broken franchise.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence is the beat-and-guide-up that sold off anyway: Q1 FY2027 beat on revenue (\$4.14bn vs ~\$4.04bn consensus) and EPS, guided Q2 ahead of expectations, raised the 2026 WFE outlook, and management said momentum "should accelerate in the second half of calendar 2026 through 2027" — yet the stock fell 5.69% after the beat (30 Oct 2026) and was swept into the 5 Nov rout. Analysts trimmed targets (UBS to \$180, Needham to \$200) rather than cutting estimates: multiple compression, not a demand or share loss. No company-specific miss, no guidance cut, no lost customer is in the dossier. (Verification: confirmed from KLA's 28 Oct 2026 release/letter to shareholders and call transcript.)

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLA holds ~50–58% of process control, >85% of optical wafer inspection, and an effective monopoly/duopoly in reticle inspection, with an installed base of >50,000 tools and ROIC of ~34–44% in each of the five years to FY2026. Qualification lock-in is the moat: a fab cannot swap an inspection step without re-qualifying yield, and a single defect can scrap a \$30,000 chip. **AI cannot disrupt it** — AI cannot inspect wafers by itself; it needs KLA's optics, and smaller nodes, 3D architectures and advanced packaging (process-control revenue on track ~\$1.1bn in calendar 2026, +70%+ YoY, per the dossier) multiply defect-detection value. The only moat threat in the dossier is geopolitical: China is ~31% of TTM revenue and exposed to export controls — not substitution or commoditisation. (Verification: share and moat claims confirmed; the advanced-packaging revenue figure itself was not independently re-verified.)
