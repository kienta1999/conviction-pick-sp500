## Per-name thesis

**1. The \$25bn buyback authorised 5 Nov is the floor (the 27% dividend raise belongs to 27 Aug 2026, not 5 Nov — verification correction). The China cliff (China ~35% of revenue, guided down meaningfully) is the real risk. Moat: etch/deposition oligopoly, qualification lock-in; AI is the demand driver, not the disruptor. Rebound catalyst: the Jan print (~27 Jan 2027, estimated) plus WFE delivery through 2027. (Full writeup: `output/dip/final_pick_2026-11-12.md`.)

**2. KLAC — the purest quality compounder, repriced by a beat-and-fell pattern.** Revenue growth accelerated into a beat-and-raise: Q1 FY2027 revenue \$4.14bn (+28.9% YoY), record \$1.34bn FCF ("record" not independently verified), Dec-quarter guide \$4.70bn ±\$200m — yet the stock fell after 6 of its last 8 beats. Process control is the most defensible moat after Lam's etch/deposition (~55% of the whole process-control market, over 85% of optical inspection specifically) — AI complexity increases inspection intensity per wafer. It ranks second because the trailing multiple (~35x) still demands the CY2027 WFE boom above \$200bn on schedule. Rebound catalyst: the late-January print landing cleanly — note the date is contested (management said "late January", calendars estimate 4 Feb 2027; do not present 27 Jan as KLAC's date). The dip is temporary if WFE holds; the trap case is a WFE cut repricing a ~35x trailing multiple hard.

**3. FIX — the dip is a margin-optics discount on a record order book.** Record \$17.81bn backlog (+90% YoY) with Q3 revenue \$3.77bn (+54% YoY) and EPS \$13.91 beating consensus, while the share price fell roughly 44% from its high (not "halved" — verification correction). The RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flag is benign (customer pre-funding). Rebound catalyst: H1-2027 margin normalisation — management guided 2027 margins *within recent ranges*, not expansion — proven at the Q4 print on 18 Feb 2027. Moat: physical, skilled, site-specific contracting AI cannot replace (irreplaceability 8/10); modular prefab capacity expanding past 4.0m sq ft by end-2026.
