# Final pick — stock-pick-dip, 2026-09-28

**THE PICK: SYK — Stryker Corporation** (Health Care / Health Care Equipment), market cap ~$103B (screen 2026-09-28).

## One-paragraph thesis

Stryker is a premier medtech franchise trading at a derated multiple for operational, not franchise, reasons: a Q1 2026 cyberattack plus a supply disruption at the recently-acquired Inari peripheral-vascular unit — both fixable — knocked the stock ~30% off its high and compressed the forward P/E to ~17x against a 25–30x historical norm. Underneath the noise the business kept compounding: Q2 2026 sales +9.4% with adjusted EPS +17.9% and a +170 bps adjusted operating-margin inflection, and management *raised* the FY2026 EPS guidance midpoint. Three of four independent lens panelists nominated it (including the value-trap skeptic lens), the Phase 3.5 verifier confirmed the drawdown cause, valuation compression, and moat facts, and probability-weighted EV implies ~+26% over 12–18 months — clearing the +15% guardrail.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

- The two causes are one-off operations shocks: a March 2026 cybersecurity incident (per Q1 10-Q; facilities ran uninterrupted only from April) and a manufacturing backorder at one Inari plant (CEO Lobo on the 7/30/2026 Q2 call).
- The business grew *through* the events: Q2 sales $6.59B (+9.4% y/y), adjusted EPS $3.69 (+17.9% y/y, a beat), MedSurg, Neurotech and Ortho each growing high-single-digit organic. Companies with broken moats do not raise guidance mid-selloff — Stryker narrowed and slightly raised its FY2026 adjusted EPS midpoint ($14.90–$15.10 → $14.95–$15.10).
- No product obsolescence, no reimbursement collapse, no competitor winning Stryker hospital accounts. The Sept 8 disclosure scare (−8.8% session on the CFO's revised timeline) is the overreaction the dip thesis buys.

## The dip depth

- `dist_52w_high = -0.293` (52-wk high $392.55 → $272.36 = −30.6%), `dist_sma200 = -0.177`. Drawdown floor (−55%) nowhere near breached.
- The multiple did most of the damage: forward P/E ~17x vs a 5-year median ~25.9x — the derating, not an earnings collapse, created the entry.

## Why the moat is intact — and why AI cannot disrupt it

- Stryker is a close #2 in orthopedic implants (Zimmer Biomet leads knees/hips) and the **#1 in orthopedic surgical robotics**: the Mako platform has >2.5M cumulative procedures across 47 countries with record Q2 2026 installations (verified via Stryker's 2026-07-16 PR and 9/23/2026 Zacks). Note: the panel dossier's original "#1 in implants" claim was **contradicted** by the verifier and corrected here — the franchise strength claim rests on robotics + surgeon relationships, not implants leadership.
- FDA-regulated implants, deep surgeon relationships, hospital switching costs; net debt/EBITDA ~1.6x, no survival risk.
- AI-irreplaceability 9/10 (all four panelists): no model implants a hip. Stryker's own AI/AR work (e.g. SportSuite Vision) is additive product, not a displacement threat.

## The rebound catalyst & timing

- Q3 2026 earnings (estimated 10/29/2026): the print confirms the Inari recovery trajectory and continued margin inflection; Q4 then carries the actual recovery. **Correction vs the dossier:** management's "backorders manageable by end of Q3" guidance (7/30) was superseded on 9/8 — CFO Wells expects the issue to run "a little bit into the fourth quarter." The Q3 print confirms the path, not the completion.
- Buybacks resumed (~$1B authorization remaining) into the derated multiple.
