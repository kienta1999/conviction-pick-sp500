# Dip conviction pick — 2026-10-03

## One-paragraph thesis

Idexx is the dominant global companion-animal diagnostics franchise — a razor-blade compounder whose ~9,000+ inVue Dx installed instruments (plus ~80,000 Catalyst units globally) generate recurring consumables revenue that grew 10.3% organically in Q2 2026 even as US vet visits softened. The stock sits 32.5% off its high and near its 52-week low on a pet-spending pause, trading at ~34.7x forward — its cheapest forward multiple in years versus a ~45–55x historical norm — with a fortress balance sheet (0.5x net leverage, ~110% FCF conversion). This is a quality business on temporary cyclical sale, with an intact AI-proof moat and a clean falsifiable moment: Q3 earnings on ~Nov 2, 2026.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence: recurring CAG Diagnostics revenue — the flywheel that matters — accelerated +10.3% organic in Q2 2026 on volume (~4%) AND pricing, while management raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $4.7–$4.745B. The drawdown is about price/sentiment: soft US clinical visits (CFO guided ~1.5% visit declines in 2H 2026, concentrated in discretionary wellness) plus an expectation reset after the stock was priced for near-perfect execution. Verified against the company Q2 press release (BusinessWire, Aug 4, 2026): the business is still compounding; utilization per visit is rising even as visits dip. This is a cyclical pet-spend pause, not share loss or impairment.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

Global #1 in companion-animal diagnostics. The moat is the installed instrument base plus a proprietary reference-lab network and VetConnect data: switching costs (a vet clinic doesn't rip out its analyzers), pricing power (volume AND pricing grew), and international growth still double-digit. **Why AI or a substitute cannot disrupt it:** there is no substitute for a physical blood/urine assay run in-clinic — and AI is a tailwind here, not a threat, because Idexx embeds AI diagnostics itself (inVue Dx, Cancer Dx, Catalyst Cortisol). Irreplaceability 9/10 across the panel.

The falsifiable moment is **Q3 earnings ~Nov 2, 2026**: stabilization of 2H vet visits plus a further guidance raise would re-rate the multiple.
