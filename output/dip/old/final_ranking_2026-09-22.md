## Per-name thesis

**1. BR — Broadridge.** The −31% dip is a narrative derating of a legally-rooted
near-monopoly: FY26 delivered 8% recurring revenue and 12% adj EPS growth while the
market priced AI disintermediation that regulation (SEC Reg 14A proxy forwarding)
structurally blunts — verified: the moat is scale + broker integration + switching
costs, not a statutory monopoly, which the pick writeup states honestly. Q1 FY27
(~Nov 2026) closed-sales print adjudicates the one soft datapoint (−19% YoY in
Q3 FY26); ~15x fwd vs 25–35x history. Trap flag (visible, not vetoed): tokenization
of securities bypassing intermediaries over a decade.

**2. IDXX — IDEXX Laboratories.** The Q2 2026 re-acceleration (revenue +10%, EPS
$4.27 vs ~$3.94 est, organic growth back to 9%, second FY guidance raise) says the
vet-visit scare is already reversing while the price still discounts it. Razor/blade
fleet in ~175 countries, 63% gross margins, 9/10 irreplaceability — AI can only
assist interpretation, never draw blood. Caveat: 30.6x fwd is the richest multiple
in the top 5; the EV guardrail margin is thin (see scenarios). Trap flag: vet-visit
slump proves structural and the multiple compresses to ~20x.

**3. SPGI — S&P Global.** The purest "no model can copy this" moat in the field
(9.5/10): regulated ratings duopoly + S&P DJ Indices + Platts, Morningstar Wide
moat. Record Q2 2026 Ratings/Indices revenue against a −21% dip sourced from a Feb
guide miss and AI-research sentiment; >$7B 2026 buyback, Mobility spin completed
7/1/2026. Trap flag: 2024–25 issuance was pulled forward, not pent up — then it's
a low-teens grower at ~20x.

**4. BKNG — Booking Holdings.** Wide-moat #1 OTA at a narrow-moat 13.6x: Hormuz
guidance cut and the blocked €1.63B ETraveli deal are geopolitical/regulatory, not
demand; the AI-agent narrative has zero evidence in bookings, and the merchant model
(61% of revenue) makes Booking the payment layer an agent would have to displace.
Trap flag: AI agents genuinely become the booking interface in 2–3 years and take
rates compress — the one structural threat the skeptic took seriously.

**5. SYK — Stryker.** Backorder = demand exceeding supply: a one-plant Peripheral
Vascular (Inari) supply disruption — reportedly a cyberattack — crushed the premium
compounder to a rare 16.4x (vs 25–30x history). Fixable by Q4 2026 / Q1 2027; hip/knee
franchise intact. Trap flag: Hagens Berman disclosure investigation and any extension
of the backorder beyond Q4.

**6. CASY — Casey's.** Sept 8, 2026: EPS $7.37 beat, yet worst week in 17 years —
the market hated the *quality* of the beat (3.2% inside SSS, fuel-margin windfall)
and a reaffirmed-not-raised FY27 EBITDA guide. Resettable bar, fortress balance
sheet, December print is the catalyst. Trap flag: low-single-digit SSS the new
normal; multiple permanently re-rates to mid-20s.

**7. NFLX — Netflix.** Deepest drawdown (−42.2%) on growth deceleration, the
collapsed WBD deal ($2.8B termination fee inflates trailing earnings — strip it),
and a vocal Wells Fargo bear (PT $80→$57, 9/18/2026). FCF machine (~$12.5B 2026
target), cheapest earnings multiple in years, $4.7B buyback run-rate. Trap flag:
engagement decay is structural (hours drifting to YouTube), hit-rate falls — the
one name where the bear thesis is demand, not multiple.

**8. EFX — Equifax.** −38.4% on frozen mortgage rates (~6.6% 30-yr) plus the FHFA
bi-merge threat (9/4/2026, −8.6% that day — a proposal, not a rule). Coiled refi
pipeline (borrowers at 5–6%+ waiting on 25–50 bps) and ~18x fwd vs mid-20s–30
history make the risk/reward the best in the rate-sensitive names. Trap flag: the
skeptic's #1 policy casualty — bi-merge becomes rule and removes ~1/3 of mortgage
pull volume.

**9. TPR — Tapestry.** −16.5% in one session (8/13/2026) on essentially in-line
FY27 guidance after a record FY26 (Coach +24%); fwd P/E ~14 with a 16% dividend
hike and $1.6B planned returns. The panel's fastest re-rating candidate into the
holiday quarter. Trap flag: Coach fashion-cycle turn; Kate Spade (−7%) drag
persists — brand moat is the thinnest in the top 10.

**10. ISRG — Intuitive Surgical.** 11,700+ da Vinci systems, $5.22B net cash,
irreplaceability 9/10 — the best business, at 32.5x fwd the least attractive price.
Q2 2026 beat (revenue +18.5%) was sold on a held procedure-growth guide; multiple
compression, not operating collapse. Trap flag (price, not business): procedure
growth structurally decelerates to low teens and the premium multiple never re-rates.

"catching a knife" with margin is brutal. Education, not advice: no leverage
multiple or position size is recommended.
