# Final pick — stock-pick-dip, run 2026-11-19

## THE PICK

**KLAC — KLA Corporation** (Information Technology, Semiconductor Materials & Equipment). Screen price **\$144.56**; market cap ~\$188.4B; forward P/E 20.3x (screen/Yahoo basis — provider-dependent, see verification note); analyst mean target \$219.62 (+52%).

## One-paragraph thesis

KLA is the measurement layer of the fab — 56.8% of global process control and 85.9% of defect inspection — and the dip is a multiple reset on a business that got stronger while the stock fell: revenue growth accelerated three straight quarters to +28.9% year over year, operating margin hit 45.0%, ROE is 91.4%, and purchase commitments jumped to \$8.87B from \$2.50B a year earlier with ~12-month lead times because demand ran ahead of supply all year. The stock sits 52% off its June high because its trailing P/E was 48.4x at end-September, not because customers walked away. The December-quarter print in late January 2027 (guided revenue \$4.7B, +42.5% year over year) is the near-term proof point, with \$8.81B of buyback authorisation as a floor while it approaches.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence is the order book against the price action: purchase commitments of \$8.87B at 30 September 2026, up from \$2.50B a year earlier, with demand ahead of supply and 12-month lead times, while the stock fell 30.0% from 8 August to 6 November versus 4.7% for the S&P 500. A business losing its franchise does not see customers commit three-and-a-half times more money a year in advance. The two company-specific drags named on the call — persistently high memory costs trimming gross margin to a guided 62.5%, and CEO share sales of over \$10.7M — are a small input-cost headwind and a sentiment item, not a demand or share signal; KLA's process-control share is rising, not falling.

## The dip depth

-52.0% off the 52-week high; -20.3% below the 200-day SMA. The drawdown is a sharp recent correction off a very high peak (the stock was up +28% over 12 months even after the fall), driven by the 5 November 2026 SOX rout (-12.25% on the day) after the 3 November midterms raised data-centre permitting risk, plus the Nvidia CEO transition on 12 November. The cause is exogenous sentiment and multiple compression, not a KLA-specific deterioration.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLA holds 56.8% of the global process-control market in 2025, 6.5–7x its nearest competitor (Applied Materials at 11.2%), and 85.9% of defect inspection, the largest sub-segment. Its tools are the measurement layer of the fab: at 3nm/2nm and in HBM stacks, you cannot fabricate what you cannot measure, and decades of defect-classification data plus optics, laser and software integration make KLA the default reference platform at TSMC, Samsung and Intel. **AI cannot disrupt it — AI makes KLA more necessary, not less**, because AI chips are the most defect-sensitive products ever built; advanced-packaging inspection revenue is expected to reach \$1.1B in calendar 2026. No AI substitute exists for measuring a wafer. Irreplaceability: 10/10 in the dossier.

Hitting that guide confirms the acceleration and the gross-margin story, with management expecting to be back inside its 63–64% Investor Day gross-margin model by mid-to-late 2027 into 2028. The \$8.81B of buyback authorisation remaining at 30 September 2026 is a steady floor under the stock while the print approaches.
