## Per-name thesis

**1. KLAC — the catalyst already printed.** The dip is temporary because the business just proved it: fiscal Q1 2027 revenue 4.136B USD (+28.9%), a record 46.1% operating margin, and a December-quarter guide of 4.7B USD (about +42.5%) that beats consensus. The moat (9/10) is a near-monopoly in process control that AI accelerators strengthen rather than threaten, and the rebound catalyst needs no waiting — it is the December delivery and the January print, with a 2027 wafer-equipment market previewed above 200B USD behind it. The honest caveat is the premium multiple (verified about 27.0x forward, premium to every peer), which is why a 2027 WFE disappointment would hit KLAC hardest. Base case 225 USD by 2027-Q4; see Return scenarios.

**2. HWM — the best print, the worst margin of safety.** The dip is temporary because the vertical-integration fear has not cost it anything yet: Q3 2026 revenue 2.703B USD (+29%, +25% organic), adjusted EPS 1.46 USD versus 1.37 USD consensus, record quarterly FCF of 562M USD, raised FY2026 guidance, first FY2027 revenue guidance of about 11.5B USD, and a 5B USD buyback addition. The moat (9/10) is certification lock-in for turbine blades and castings that no AI tool bypasses, plus gas turbines +45% on share gains. The caveat is price: at 31.67x forward the dip repaired a premium rather than creating a bargain, and the screen's +57.6% upside is stale pre-print — treat it as a ceiling, not a base. Base case 250 USD by 2027-Q4; see Return scenarios.

**3. AMAT — record results, halved multiple, one binary date.** The dip is temporary in the dossier's reading: fiscal Q3 2026 revenue 9.12B USD (+24.8%) with a record non-GAAP EPS of 3.50 USD, while the stock fell on AI-capex financing doubts, a peer supply warning, and an investor day read as unambitious — a multiple story, with China mix (28% of revenue, down from 35%) as the durable headwind. The moat (8/10) is the world's largest semi-equipment franchise with installed-base switching costs, and AI chip complexity increases tool demand. The catalyst is the fiscal Q4 print after close Thu 12 Nov 2026 (guide 10.25B USD ± 500M USD, EPS 4.02 USD); a beat plus Q1 FY2027 guidance confirming 2027 growth re-rates it, while a flat/down guide re-opens the drawdown. Forward P/E compressed from about 49x at the June peak to the low-20s (verified). Base case 560 USD by 2027-Q4; see Return scenarios.

**4. CPRT — the veto lens's pick.** The dip is a margin-and-one-contract wobble, not a broken auction: Q4 FY2026 revenue still grew 2.4% while net income fell 17.4% and US insurance units fell 7.5% on one lost major account, with pricing power intact (global ASP +3.5% in Q4) and international units +10%. The moat (9/10) is zoning-scarce yards plus single-network insurer contracts plus two-sided bidder liquidity at a 32% operating margin, and the balance sheet is fortress (net cash about 4.1B USD, debt only about 88–93M USD). The 18–19 Nov 2026 print is the test of whether US volumes and cost-per-car (up 12.7%) have bottomed; the trap case is further contract losses or a structural shrinkage of total-loss supply as cars get safer.

**5. META — the strongest engine with a funding discount.** The dip is temporary on demand (Q3 revenue +26%, ad impressions +10% and price per ad +14% simultaneously) and about funding instead: Q3 capex 34.91B USD versus operating cash flow 32.79B USD (FCF −2.12B USD, first negative as a public company in that framing), a 135–145B USD 2026 capex range, a 9.89B USD legal charge, and a 95B USD equity programme launched 26 Oct 2026. The moat (9/10) is the planetary social graph plus ad-targeting data, with AI as an accelerant. The rebound is gated by a credible 2027 capex/payback framework and the Q4 print (screen: 2027-01-27); the equity overhang caps near-term per-share upside even if the business performs.

**6. GNRC — one line.** Contracted 4.5B USD data-center backlog (nearly tripled from 1.6B USD in July) plus a 2.4B USD Amazon 2027–28 schedule transforms a cyclical residential story into a data-center growth story; price already reflects part of it, so Q4/2027 execution is the bet.

**7. SCHW — one line.** Record franchise momentum (13.33T USD client assets, 190.4B USD core net new assets) on an 11.47x forward multiple, dipped on a high-bar Q3 print and lower-end NIM guidance; receivables flag verdict benign-with-a-watch.

**8. TPR — one line.** Coach genuinely winning with pricing power (handbag AUR up mid-teens, Greater China +30%) after a hated FY2027 guidance reset; the 5 Nov print is the imminent binary test, and concentration in one fashion cycle is the risk.

**9. NFLX — one line.** De-rated on an ad-execution miss and guidance reset (Q4 EPS guide 0.67 USD vs 0.73 USD, ad forecast cut to about 2.7B USD), not demand collapse; six straight post-print drops mean it must earn the re-rating at the January print.
