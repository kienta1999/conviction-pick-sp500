## One-paragraph thesis

KLA is the best business in the dip panel repriced as if its growth had broken: FQ1 FY27 was a record on every dimension that matters — revenue \$4.14bn (+29% YoY), record non-GAAP operating margin 46.1%, record FCF \$1.34bn — and Q2 guidance accelerates to \$4.7bn (+43% YoY), yet the stock sits 51.5% off its 52-week high because its trailing P/E halved from ~71x to ~31x in five months. Process control is the last budget a fab cuts — nobody ships uninspected wafers — and every node shrink, HBM stack and advanced-packaging step raises KLA's content per wafer, so AI's demand for leading-edge wafers widens the moat instead of threatening it. The rebound needs only the late-January print to confirm the guide, not a cycle turn, a legal win, or a Fed pivot.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The business accelerated into the sell-off. Record revenue, record 46.1% operating margin, record FCF, purchase commitments of \$8.87bn at 30 Sep 2026 (3.5x a year earlier — management's own bet on the ramp), and a Q2 guide of +43% YoY that was ahead of expectations. The dossier's verdict is explicit: "the multiple, not the model, broke." There is no share-loss evidence — revenue +29% with services at 20% of revenue (+14%) — and the post-print target trims (UBS to \$180, Needham to \$200) were, in the dossier's words, "tempered multiples rather than deteriorating fundamentals." The fall traces to the November 2026 AI-financing repricing (SOX down 12.25% on 5 Nov 2026), not to a KLA miss.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLA is the "eyes of the fab" — the dominant process-control franchise, scored 10/10 on irreplaceability in the dossier, the highest of all 15 names. Qualification is measured in years; the installed base plus defect-library and yield-analytics data creates switching costs no rival has replicated at scale. Advanced-packaging process control is ~\$1.1bn in CY2026, +70%+ YoY. KLA is the least exposed of the equipment names to Chinese equipment substitution (Bernstein). AI cannot substitute for physical inspection — a model can analyse defect data, but it cannot see a wafer; if anything, AI increases the value of the data KLA collects. The only genuine threat is cyclicality (fewer systems sold if WFE disappoints), not displacement.
