## THE PICK: **IDXX — IDEXX Laboratories, Inc. — $513.39 (screen price)**

Healthcare / Life Sciences Tools & Services. Market cap ~$41B (screen). The vet-diagnostics
compounder repriced as a broken growth story while the ledger kept compounding.

**One-paragraph thesis:** IDEXX is the cleanest "great business bought on sale" in the
field — a razor-and-blade vet-diagnostics franchise with 64% gross and 35% operating
margins, 74% ROE, and net debt/EBITDA of 0.55x, whose stock fell 33% purely on multiple
compression (trailing P/E from ~51x at end-2025 to ~36–38 now) while four straight EPS
beats and two 2026 guidance raises printed. The growth story never broke; only the
multiple did. At ~30.7x forward earnings vs its own 3–5 year history of 46–49x, it is
30–37% discounted against a franchise that has historically compounded in the
mid-teens, with the analyst mean target at $698.92 (+36%).

**Why the dip is temporary, not permanent:** the earnings ledger — EPS beats in each of
the last 4 quarters (avg surprise +5.8%; Q1 $3.47 vs $3.41, Q2 $4.27 vs ~$3.93,
verified), guidance raised twice in 2026 (EPS $14.45–14.90 → $14.69–14.94, verified),
record Q2 gross margin (64.0%) and operating margin (35.0%, +140 bps YoY) while the
stock is −33% off its high. A permanently impaired business cuts guidance; IDEXX raised
it twice and expanded margins. The drawdown is a growth-stock de-rating on soft US
vet-visit optics (~−1.3% Q2, ~−1.5% assumed for H2) plus healthcare-sector rotation —
price, not the business. (Verified: beats, raises, margins confirmed; Q2 FCF did miss
street — $322.5M vs $469.8M per TIKR — noted as a watch item, not a break.)

**The dip depth:** −33.0% off the 52-week high, −13.6% below its 200-day SMA.
Cause: soft US wellness-visit trends + healthcare multiple de-rating, not a business
deterioration.

**Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact:** razor-and-blade installed
base of in-clinic analyzers driving recurring consumables + reference-lab services with
real vet switching costs (workflow re-tooling), proprietary diagnostic data, and
practice-management software deepening lock-in. **AI/substitute risk: LOW.** AI can
assist test interpretation but cannot replace physical lab assays or wet-lab
infrastructure — and IDEXX itself embeds AI in its analyzers (Cancer Dx, inVue Dx
ramp). The verifier confirmed the razor-and-blade mechanics; one correction applied:
the "~75% US share" figure in the dossier is stale (FTC 2006–2011 in-clinic POC);
current analyses put total vet-diagnostics share at ~45% — still #1, with pricing
power (net price realization cited as a Q2 margin driver). (Ballots: moat lens
runner-up, skeptic lens top pick "zero AI-substitution risk".)

**The rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings on **2026-11-02** as the next
validation gate (a third beat-and-raise); stabilization of US wellness-visit trends
through Q4 2026; commercial expansion into four international countries in H2 2026;
Cancer Dx / inVue Dx new-product ramp. Multiple repair expected November 2026 → Q1 2027
if the print confirms.
