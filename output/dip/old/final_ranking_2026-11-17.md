## Per-name thesis

**1. KLAC — the pick.** Quasi-monopoly process control (50–55% share, 80%+ reticle) with revenue accelerating (+29% YoY, record Q1 FY27) while the price halved on a sector de-rating. The dip is multiple compression, not a broken business; the Dec-quarter guide (~$4.7B) is the falsifiable test. See `final_pick_2026-11-17.md`.

**2. AMAT — cheapest quality semi.** Record Q4 FY26 ($10.41B, +53% YoY, EPS $4.16) and a raised 2027 WFE call (>+30%) at 15.1x forward — the cheapest forward multiple in the field, with a +58.9% mean-target upside. The dip is a "market won't buy a beat" de-rating plus a stale 2025 share-loss narrative (20.0% → 17.9%) that the print rebuts but needs one more quarter to prove reversed. The beat was thin (+1% revenue vs consensus) and missed the $4.20 whisper.

**3. CSCO — cleanest "market won't buy a beat" setup.** Record Q1 FY27 ($18.52B, +24% YoY, EPS $1.40 vs $1.33 consensus), FY27 guidance raised (revenue $74.8–75.8B, EPS $5.47–5.53), AI infra target raised to $9B — sold off on a 250bp gross-margin slip to 65.6% (memory costs, hardware mix) and product orders slowing to +21% from +35%. Fairly valued on forward earnings (16.1x vs ~15.6x 5-yr average), not cheap; the rebound is earnings growth, and Nvidia's print tonight is the group trigger. The margin-mix question is the only thing standing between this and a fast snap-back.

**4. CAT — record backlog vs a half-corrected multiple.** $78B backlog, the strongest in the batch, with AI-proof heavy iron (irreplaceability 9/10). The dip is a transitory give-back after a run; the rebound needs the backlog to convert and the Nvidia print tonight to re-rate the group.

**5. HWM — best business, worst price.** The best operating momentum in the batch (29% revenue growth, 54% EPS growth, raised guidance) and certified castings no model can copy (irreplaceability 9/10) — but 33.2x forward is still the field's priciest multiple. The dip took it from absurdly expensive to merely expensive; the rebound is a grind on earnings delivery, not a snap-back.

**6. ODFL — best LTL pricing power, premium price.** A best-in-class franchise with a volume inflection that may have started, but 25.8x forward on trough-adjacent earnings leaves little room for the recovery to disappoint. Buy the freight recovery, not a bargain.

**7. FIX — fortress balance sheet, record backlog.** Net cash, record backlog, fastest growth in the field — but the margin path is the swing factor, and the receivables flag (benign: customers prepaying, CFO/NI 1.84x) bears watching if backlog conversion slows.

**8. VRT — biggest growth, least valuation cushion.** 61% revenue growth and a doubled order book, but inventory more than doubled ($3.29B) and the orders-disclosure ended — the most information risk in the top 10. D flagged it as a trap risk. Q4 conversion (Feb 10, 2027 print) decides whether the inventory build stays benign.

**9. INTU — deepest de-rating, still growing.** A high-quality franchise at a multi-year trough multiple because the market has priced AI disruption as a certainty; the business is still growing double digits. But Nov 19 is a live grenade — buy only after the print or sized for a ±3.3% move.

**10. SCHW — record asset-gathering at 12x forward.** A slow 2027 NIM story on a funding-mix wobble; the custody franchise is durable but the cheap-funding edge is the vulnerable layer. The shallowest dip in the top 10 (−17.9% off high).
