# Dip final pick — 2026-10-21

## THE PICK: SYK — Stryker Corporation

Health Care / Health Care Equipment. Market cap about 112.4 billion dollars. Screen price 293.38 (2026-10-20 close per shortlist_2026-10-21.json; see the price-date note under What was verified).

## One-paragraph thesis

Stryker is a quality medtech compounder whose dip was caused by two dated operational events — a March 2026 cyberattack that depressed Q1 and a supply disruption at one Inari plant that created a vascular backorder — while demand, share and guidance stayed intact: Q2 2026 organic growth was +9.0%, adjusted EPS rose +17.9% to 3.69 dollars, adjusted operating margin expanded to 27.4%, and the FY26 guide was narrowed-not-cut to 8.3–9.3% organic growth and 14.95–15.10 dollars of adjusted EPS (all verified in Phase 3.5). At 17.6x forward earnings versus roughly 26–27x historical forward averages, the market is refusing to pre-pay a guided EPS step-up it has no demand evidence against — that gap, not multiple heroics, is the rebound.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The damage is supply, not demand. The Inari shortfall was product unavailability from one plant; the Q2 capital order book was elevated with zero cancellations cited (Phase 3.5 clarified that quote covers the capital order book generally, not Inari orders specifically — the dossier's use is directionally fair but not Inari-specific). The honest caveat is timing: CFO Preston Wells said on 8 Sep 2026 the backorder was continuing into Q3 and into Q4, reversing the earlier expectation of a manageable level by end-Q3, and the stock fell about 8.8% that day. So the thesis is verified demand plus an unproven fix — 29 Oct is a verification point, not a formality.

## The dip depth

293.38 is -23.6% off the 52-week high and -9.5% versus the 200-day SMA (screen). The drawdown stacks the March cyberattack quarter, the Inari disclosure arc through the 8 Sep CFO update, and the October sector de-rating — no guidance cut sits anywhere in it.

## Why it is the category leader / why the moat is intact

Mako SmartRobotics holds about 75.5% of the orthopaedic robotic systems market (GlobalData basis, verified), with more than 3,000 installations pulling through proprietary implants, surgeon training and hospital capital lock-in. AI cannot disrupt this: regulated physical implants and procedures cannot be digitised away, and AI is a complement inside Mako navigation and robotics. Irreplaceability 9/10 in the dossier; the real threats are execution and rival robotics, not substitution.

## The rebound catalyst and timing

Q3 earnings on 2026-10-29 (verified date) — three checks decide it: (i) the US Vascular backorder stated as manageable or clearing, (ii) the FY guide of 8.3–9.3% organic / 14.95–15.10 dollars EPS held, (iii) margin at or above 27%. Follow-through: backorder shipments in Q4 2026–Q1 2027, the Mako RPS ramp into ambulatory surgery centres, and possible buyback resumption (about 1 billion dollars authorised remaining).
