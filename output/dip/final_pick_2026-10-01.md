## Why the dip is temporary (not permanent)

The drop is a multiple derating on fear, not a business break: every revenue line is growing double-digits, recurring mix is 85%, and the "competitor" is cleared but not at scale. That's a price dip on an intact moat.

## Moat / AI-irreplaceability

The irreproducible assets are the installed base, the surgeon training ecosystem, 20M+ patients treated, and FDA Class III clearances. The real substitute risk is rival robots (J&J Ottava, Medtronic) — competition, not commoditization.
