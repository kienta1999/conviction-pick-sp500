The stock is down 32% from its 52-week high on a narrative about declining US vet visits and a leadership transition — yet the business printed Q2 CAG Diagnostics recurring revenue up 10.3% organically while visits fell 1.3%, an 1,100bp "growth premium to visits" that proves pricing and mix power survive the soft patch.
