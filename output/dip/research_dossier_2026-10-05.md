Threat: YouTube's zero-marginal-cost creator model taking US TV share (14.2% vs 8% — Nielsen via simianx.ai) and fragmenting attention; AI-generated video could cheapen mid-tier content Netflix monetizes. But AI cannot replicate Netflix's owned-IP franchises or its global distribution/billing base — disruption risk is real but partial. 7/10.

### 3. Rebound catalyst
Q3'26 earnings 10/20/2026 (~1:01pm PT) — the single near-term catalyst; a beat-and-re-guide on engagement/ads could unwind the Wells Fargo thesis (ad-hoc-news.de, 10/4/2026).
