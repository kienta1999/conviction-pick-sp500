## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLA holds \~55%+ share of semiconductor process control — roughly 6x its nearest peer (VERIFIED, claim 5: 56.5% share, \~6.5x the nearest competitor) — plus a services annuity (\$820M in Q4 FY2026, +17% YoY, VERIFIED) that recurs regardless of the equipment cycle. AI cannot disrupt this moat because the product is physical: as nodes shrink toward 1.4nm, 1D DRAM and HBM stacks, defect tolerance falls and process-control intensity *rises* per fab dollar — AI makes KLA more necessary, and no model routes around wafer inspection. The dossier scores irreplaceability 9/10; the only deduction is Chinese indigenous inspection challengers over a multi-year horizon, not yet at the leading edge.
