## Per-name thesis

**1. LRCX — the dip is a sentiment repricing on record fundamentals.** Fiscal Q1 2027 (21 Oct 2026): revenue $8.219bn (+54% YoY), record 52.4% non-GAAP gross margin, first-ever 40%+ operating margin, record $3.24bn FCF — and the stock fell anyway, then −16.36% on 5 Nov with no company news. The Dec guide ($8.6bn revenue) is capped by supply, not demand; delayed shipments slip to the Mar quarter; CY2027 WFE above $200bn is the demand anchor. The $25bn buyback authorised 5 Nov is the floor. Rebound catalyst: the Jan print (~27 Jan 2027, estimated) plus WFE delivery through 2027. Moat: etch/deposition oligopoly, qualification lock-in, CSBG ~30% recurring revenue; AI is the demand driver, not the disruptor. (Full writeup: `output/dip/final_pick_2026-11-10.md`.)

**2. FIX — the most contracted thesis in the field, with a live margin debate.** Record $17.81bn backlog (+90% YoY) with 65–75% converting in 12 months, Q3 FCF $806m vs $489m net income, net cash ~$2.11bn, customers pre-funding the business by $1.69bn — growth that is booked, not forecast. The dip is a margin-uncertainty discount: gross margin fell 26.3% → 25.9% → 24.8% over three quarters and management gave no firm forward margin guidance. Lens D vetoed FIX as the single pick on that basis (the veto's facts were confirmed by verification, with the caveat that Q1's 26.3% included ~$43m of non-recurring closeout gains, ~25.2% underlying). In ranked mode the trap is a FLAG, not a veto — FIX stays #2 with the caveat visible. Rebound catalyst: H1-2027 margin normalisation, not new orders. Moat: physical, skilled, site-specific contracting AI cannot replace; the soft spot is pricing power, not replaceability.

**3. KLAC — yesterday's pick, still the purest quality compounder, repriced one day higher.** Revenue growth accelerated three straight quarters (7.2% → 11.5% → 15.2% → 28.9% YoY) into a beat-and-raise: Q1 FY2027 revenue $4.14bn, record $1.34bn FCF, Dec-quarter guide $4.70bn ±$200m.
