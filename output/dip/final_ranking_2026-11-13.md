The highest-risk names are the binary ones: KEYS (unseen 19 Nov print), VRT (policy beta, no backlog disclosure) and ADBE (value-trap suspect, unranked). Leverage-safety note: a beaten-down name can keep falling — catching a knife with margin is brutal; size for the possibility the dip deepens before it turns.
