### HWM-4. Moat: certified aerospace forgings oligopoly / certification lock-in
**CONFIRMED qualitatively; "oligopoly" is a characterisation, not a quantified share.**

  that AS9100 / FAA / EASA approvals are required before shipping
  safety-critical parts, and that one process change can trigger re-testing
  and re-approval; heavy capex, precision tooling and skilled labour keep new

  ConceptTicker (2026) describes the FAA-certified aerospace precision-parts

  certified for that specific aircraft configuration — switching to an
  uncertified equivalent is not legally permissible.
- Limit: no source found in this pass gives a quantified HWM market share in
  forgings/castings or a head-count of the "oligopoly." The lock-in mechanism
  is confirmed; any "oligopoly" share number would be UNVERIFIED and should
  not be added to the memo.
