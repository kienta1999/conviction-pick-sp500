## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** LRCX

- **Thesis (3–5 sentences):** Lam Research is the purest AI-irreplaceable dip in this field: one of three firms in the plasma-etch/deposition oligopoly, with tools qualified tool-by-tool into fabs so switching is prohibitively expensive, and a Customer Support Business Group of ~$2.50bn in Q1 FY27 (~30% of revenue) that recurs through spares, services and upgrades. AI does not route around Lam — it is the demand driver, with more etch/deposition steps per wafer and record memory revenue +120% YoY in Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 27 Sep 2026, reported 21 Oct 2026). The stock has halved (−48.6% off its 52-week high on the screen, −21.6% vs its 200-day SMA) while posting record revenue ($8.219bn, +54% YoY), a first-time-above-40% non-GAAP operating margin (40.2%) and record FCF of $3.24bn in the same quarter. At 18.2x forward P/E on the screen, with net cash (−0.37x net debt/EBITDA) and a record $25bn buyback authorised 5 Nov 2026, the dip is a de-rating of a record business, not a broken one.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The two legs are both dated and non-structural: the 21–22 Oct fall followed a record quarter whose Dec-quarter guide ($8.6bn ±$0.4bn) was capped by near-term supply-chain constraints — CFO Doug Bettinger said affected shipments slip to the Mar 2027 quarter, not lost — plus a guided decline in China revenue share (35.1% of Sep revenue); the 5 Nov leg (−16.36% in one day, close $185.20) had no company-specific news and was the midterm-driven data-centre-moratorium fear hitting the whole AI supply chain (SOX −12.3% that day). Demand signals point the other way: preliminary CY2027 WFE spending >$200bn (vs mid-$150bn range for CY2026), led by DRAM, then leading-edge foundry logic and NAND (21 Oct call). No lost product, customer or share is in the evidence; the 52.4% non-GAAP gross margin (highest in ~20 years per Sep reporting) says share is not being lost.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** **Low risk.** Etch/deposition is physics-bound, capital-intensive and fab-qualified; AI increases Lam's content per wafer rather than substituting for it. The only named long-run threat is Chinese domestic tool substitution (CXMT) — a share risk in China, not product obsolescence — which is why the dossier scores irreplaceability 9/10, not 10. No other name in the field pairs a 9/10 irreplaceability score with an 8/10 rebound score and a sub-19x forward multiple.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Near term (Dec 2026–Jan 2027): the Dec-quarter print (guided $8.6bn revenue, $2.33 EPS) and the Q2 FY27 report ~27 Jan 2027, when the slipped shipments should land in the Mar quarter. Medium term (2027): CY2027 WFE >$200bn, memory-capex-led growth, and CSBG compounding (>30% growth guided for CY2026, >15% for CY2027). The $25bn buyback (authorised 5 Nov 2026, ~9% of shares at the time, on top of ~$3.0bn remaining) is an already-fired capital-return floor, with Lam targeting ≥85% of FCF returned over time.
- **12–18 month return scenario:** Base — partial re-rating toward the $364.03 analyst mean target (+64.5% vs the Mon 9 Nov close of $221.31): roughly $310–330 by ~Q3 2027 (+~40–48% vs the $222.43 screen price) if CY2027 WFE >$200bn materialises and the Mar-quarter slip lands as guided. If AI-capex financing stress or data-centre moratoria cut CY2027 WFE, memory (the most cyclical customer segment, +120% YoY) reverses fastest, China (35% of revenue) is exposed to both export controls and domestic substitution, and component/cleanroom bottlenecks persist beyond one quarter.

**1. Base ~$310–330 by ~Q3 2027, bull ~$364 (analyst mean target), bear ~$185 (5 Nov close) — see the single-pick ballot above.

**2. FIX.** Comfort Systems is the most physically irreplaceable name in the field: 211 locations in 155 cities, 4.0m sq ft of modular/prefab capacity (+81.8% YoY), and bespoke, safety-critical hyperscale work awarded on track record and bonding capacity — on-site mechanical contracting AI cannot route around (dossier irreplaceability 9/10, rebound 8/10). The rebound engine is mechanical, not hopeful: a record $17.81bn backlog at 30 Sep 2026 (+90% YoY), with 65–75% to be recognised in the next 12 months (~$11.6–13.4bn), plus Q3 FCF of $806m and ~$2.11bn net cash. The dip is margin-uncertainty (gross margin 24.8%, second straight sequential decline, no forward margin guidance — "job maturity to normalize" in H1 2027), not demand; forward P/E is quoted as a range, 16.5x on the screen vs ~19–26x on sourced EPS bases. Base ~$1,450–1,600 by mid-2027 if margins stabilise ≥25% at the Q4 print (~18 Feb 2027); bull toward the $1,900–2,000 post-Q3 analyst targets if H1 2027 margin normalisation is confirmed; bear back toward ~$1,000 (6 Nov close $1,017.50) if 25% gross margin proves to have been the peak.

**3. KLAC.** KLA dominates process control — wafer inspection and metrology where AI complexity *increases* inspection intensity per wafer — with a data/knowledge advantage from inspecting the world's leading fabs and a fab that cannot swap inspection tools without re-qualifying yield (dossier irreplaceability 9/10). Revenue growth accelerated for three straight quarters (7.2% → 11.5% → 15.2% → 28.9% YoY) to $4.14bn in fiscal Q1 2027 (reported 28 Oct 2026), with record FCF of $1.34bn and a Dec-quarter guide up sharply ($4.7bn ±$200m).
