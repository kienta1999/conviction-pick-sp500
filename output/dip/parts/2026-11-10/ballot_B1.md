- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. FIX does physical, skilled, site-specific mechanical/electrical installation for data centres and commercial buildings — 211 locations in 155 cities, 4.0m sq ft of modular/prefab capacity (+81.8% YoY) — awarded on track record, safety and bonding capacity, with skilled labour itself the constraint. The moat's soft spot is pricing power, not replaceability: margins slipped even as demand boomed, so cost pass-through lags rather than a rival taking share.
