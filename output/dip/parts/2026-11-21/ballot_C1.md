## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** CAT
- **Runner-up:** FIX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Caterpillar roughly halved from its record \$1,064.90 high on 30 Jun 2026 to \$510.45 on 20 Nov 2026 while its business accelerated: Q3 2026 sales rose 17.3% year over year, adjusted EPS rose 45.5% and beat consensus, and backlog hit a record \$78.1bn, up 96% year over year, with power-generation sales up 30% and a large-engine backlog into 2029. That is the classic moat-lens set-up — a category-dominant, physically irreplaceable franchise repriced as if its end market had collapsed, when in fact data centres need its power-generation products and its dealer network, installed base and brand cannot be copied by a model. At 15.9x forward earnings, versus a 27.0x Industrials peer median and a historical average since 2016 of about 17x, the market is paying a cyclical price for a backlog that is already contracted.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the Q3 print was a beat on record backlog — sales up 17.3%, adjusted EPS up 45.5%, backlog up 96% — yet the stock fell 9.25% on earnings day, its largest earnings-day drop in 31 quarters, because management merely maintained rather than raised guidance and guided Q4 to only "moderate" growth; the fall was about guidance tone and the AI-power trade unwinding, not about orders, pricing or the backlog disappearing.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — AI does not substitute for bulldozers, mining equipment or gas turbines, and AI data centres are a demand driver for Caterpillar's power-generation products rather than a substitute for them. The dossier rates its moat 9/10, the second-highest in the batch, on the world's largest construction/mining equipment position, dealer network, installed base, brand and capital intensity. The real threat is cyclicality and a roll-off in large-engine orders after 2027-29, not disruption or commoditisation by AI.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q4 results on 2 Feb 2027 showing backlog conversion and dealer destocking ending, with consensus expecting about 20% EPS growth in 2027; any reaffirmation of the data-centre power story or a stabilisation in the AI-capex narrative would also re-rate the name, and two directors bought shares in November 2026.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +50%, toward the dossier's +58% analyst upside, as the backlog converts and the multiple normalises toward its own history. Bull case roughly +80% to +100%, back toward the June high, if backlog conversion is clean, dealer destocking ends and the market re-rates Caterpillar toward the Industrials peer median on the power-generation story.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The data-centre power boom was a one-off re-rating and large-engine orders roll off after 2027-29, dealer destocking deepens, tariff costs of about \$2.2bn for FY2026 permanently compress margins, and consensus 2027 EPS growth of about 20% is cut further — in that case the cheap multiple is cyclical cheapness, not a bargain.
