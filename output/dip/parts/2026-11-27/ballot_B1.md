## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** APH
- **Runner-up:** FIX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Amphenol is the highest-quality growth compounder in this field: TTM revenue growth +48.0% with +37% organic growth, a 28.7% operating margin, 40.1% ROE and 26.4% ROIC, on a record Q3 in which revenue rose 65% to \$10.23B and orders rose 99% to \$12.17B, a 1.19 book-to-bill. Management raised guidance into the sell-off rather than cutting it, and Q4 guidance of \$10.3–10.5B revenue with IT Datacom guided +100%+ for FY2026 shows the growth engine is still accelerating, not stalling. The dip is only -17.5% from the 52-week high and -3.3% versus the 200-day SMA, yet the multiple has roughly halved from its 5-year peak, so the sale is in the rating, not in a broken business. With a new \$4B buyback from 1 January 2027 and a 20% dividend increase, capital return now compounds alongside the growth.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that Q3 was a record on every operating line — revenue +65%, orders +99%, guidance raised — and the stock still fell about \$3.90 that Friday as analysts cut targets sharply (Redburn \$197→\$107; Baird \$190→\$105) amid profit-taking after a vertical run and the November AI sell-off. A business does not break on the same day it posts its best quarter and raises its outlook; the drop repriced the multiple, not the order book.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low disruption risk — and why: breadth plus custom engineering plus qualification lock-in in high-speed copper/optical interconnect, with AI as the demand driver (IT Datacom +117% YoY, AI about 75% of IT Datacom). AI does not substitute for Amphenol's connectors; it consumes more of them. The real threat is architectural, not obsolescence — a shift to optical-only / co-packaged optics or hyperscaler insourcing — which is a medium-term watch item, not a current break in the moat. Dossier irreplaceability 8/10.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q4 results around 27 January 2027 against raised guidance (\$0.81–0.83 EPS, \$10.3–10.5B revenue), with IT Datacom growth and order intake as the tell. A clean beat-and-raise there, plus the \$4B buyback starting 1 January 2027, should force the multiple back toward Amphenol's own history well before mid-2027.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +35–45% — multiple drift back toward the low-to-mid-20s forward P/E on continued 30%+ earnings growth, in line with the \$106.18 mean target (+45.3%). Bull case roughly +60–80% if IT Datacom sustains triple-digit growth and the market re-rates Amphenol back toward its 5-year peak multiple near 34x. Caveat: the screen forward P/E of 20.2x is disputed in the dossier versus contemporaneous coverage of about 35–41x on a different forward-EPS base, so the exact cheapness depends on which EPS base proves right; the direction — a halved multiple on record earnings — is the point, not the precise figure.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Amphenol is now an AI-concentration bet — about 75% of IT Datacom, which is 48% of sales, is AI — so a hyperscaler capex pause or an architecture shift to co-packaged optics hits the growth engine directly, and the \$10.5B CommScope deal has loaded \$17.9B of total debt (\$12.6B net, 1.1x net leverage). If AI interconnect demand stalls, the growth that justifies even the current multiple disappears and the "cheap for Amphenol" case collapses.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
