## ANET (Arista Networks) — tentative single pick

### 1. Why-it's-down cause — CONFIRMED

Q3 2026, reported 3 Nov 2026 (Arista IR press release, quarter ended 30 Sep 2026): revenue \$3.66bn, +58.5% YoY, described by the company as "the highest year-over-year revenue growth since our IPO"; non-GAAP operating margin 49.1% (GAAP 44.9%); non-GAAP diluted EPS \$1.20. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance raised for the fourth time, to about \$13.3bn (+48% YoY). First FY2027 outlook: revenue about \$18.5bn, +38–40% YoY, with AI fabrics doubling to about \$7bn (Zacks, 4 Nov 2026, reporting the 3 Nov call; Motley Fool transcript, 3 Nov 2026).

The fall was not on company news. ANET rose about 5% on 4 Nov 2026 after the print, then fell about 10.8–11% on 5 Nov 2026 in the broad AI-infrastructure sell-off after the US midterm results (TIKR, 5 Nov 2026; Wisevoter, 6 Nov 2026). The trigger reported that day was political: Democrats winning control of Congress and Texas governor-elect Gina Hinojosa's promised moratorium on new data-centre approvals — Texas holds about 27% of planned US data-centre capacity (Wells Fargo, via Motley Fool, 4 Nov 2026; Reuters via Evergreen Edit, 4 Nov 2026). No Arista-specific adverse news was cited in those reports; peers (Ciena −15%, Cisco −8% on a downgrade) fell the same day. The dossier's "post-midterm / moratorium sentiment, not company news" characterisation is therefore supported.

Caveat for the panel: deferred revenue was flat-to-down slightly (\$6.84bn total at 30 Sep 2026, down about \$26m sequentially — FourWeekMBA, 3 Nov 2026, from the release). That is a demand-visibility watch item, not a contradiction of the Q3 numbers.

### 2. Rebound catalyst and its date — UNVERIFIED (date only)

The Q4 2026 guide itself is CONFIRMED: revenue \$3.9–4.0bn, non-GAAP operating margin 48–49%, non-GAAP EPS \$1.26–1.30 (Arista IR release, 3 Nov 2026). The FY2027 about-\$18.5bn outlook is also confirmed as above, so the "hard checkpoint" framing is fair.

The February 2027 print date is NOT confirmed by the company. Third-party calendars disagree: TipRanks estimates 15 Feb 2027 (not confirmed); MyStockButler / Public.com estimate 11 Feb 2027 (explicitly "expected, not yet confirmed by Arista"); FXEmpire gives 10 Feb 2027; MarketChameleon gives a 12–17 Feb 2027 window. Arista's prior Q4 print was 12 Feb 2026, so mid-February is the right window, but no exact date can be asserted. The dossier should say "expected mid-Feb 2027, date not yet announced" rather than implying a fixed February date.

### 3a. Cash and debt — CONFIRMED

Q3 2026 release balance sheet (30 Sep 2026): cash and cash equivalents \$2,288.1m plus marketable securities \$12,070.9m = \$14,359.0m (about \$14.36bn). The balance sheet shows no separate borrowing line; total liabilities \$8,817.4m include \$6,839.5m deferred revenue. CEO Jayshree Ullal on the call: "0 debt, making our financials very pristine" (Motley Fool transcript, 3 Nov 2026). SiliconANGLE (4 Nov 2026) also reports "cash totaled \$14.4 billion with no debt". The \$14.36bn figure is cash + marketable securities, not cash alone — worth stating precisely, but the claim as written is confirmed.

### 3b. Forward P/E about 28.3x — CONTRADICTED

I could not reproduce 28.3x from any current source at the 6 Nov 2026 close (\$170.80). Checked 6–7 Nov 2026: Zacks (6 Nov 2026) 32.0x forward P/E; StockAnalysis 31.70x forward PE; GuruFocus 30.16x as of 6 Nov 2026; Investing.ly 29.6x; FinanceCharts 39.22x (different forward-EPS definition); MarketBeat 36.40x. Trailing P/E is about 45.9x on the same close. The 28.3x figure is below every forward-P/E source I found and is most plausibly stale (an earlier, higher price would not explain it — a lower price would; more likely a different screen date / EPS vintage). Do not use 28.3x as the valuation anchor; the defensible range at the 6 Nov close is about 30–32x on the common forward definitions, still below ANET's five-year median of about 40.5x (Zacks).

### 4. Moat: EOS plus hyperscaler qualification lock-in; AI as demand driver — CONFIRMED, with a material correction

Confirmed in direction. Arista's own 9 Jun 2026 release describes EOS differentiation for AI fabrics and names Meta, Microsoft and Oracle as validating customers; the Q3 call put AI fabrics doubling to about \$7bn in 2027 and cited 100+ Ethernet AI customers. Arista builds on merchant silicon — the 7060XE7 series runs on Broadcom Tomahawk 6 (NAND Research, 16 Jun 2026) — so the dossier is right that the moat is not the chip; it is EOS / CloudVision software, operational consistency, and decade-long co-development with cloud titans.

Correction — Nvidia Spectrum-X HAS taken material share, and the dossier must not present the moat as unchallenged. IDC Quarterly Ethernet Switch Tracker: in Q1 2026 Nvidia became the \#1 vendor by revenue in data-centre Ethernet switching for the first time — \$2.1bn, 21.5% share, +192.7% YoY — ahead of Arista at 20.7% (Arista \$2.2bn total Ethernet revenue, +37.3% YoY); IDC called Spectrum-X "the preferred network interconnect for large-scale AI training" (IDC, 17 Jun 2026). In Q2 2026 Nvidia maintained \#1 at 20.4% (\$2.5bn, +181.1% YoY) vs Arista 18.7% (IDC, 2Q26 tracker). I found no evidence of a named hyperscaler switching away from Arista entirely — Arista still grew data-centre Ethernet revenue 37–38% YoY in both quarters and retains Meta/Microsoft/Oracle validation — but the share-lead loss to Spectrum-X is real and is the single most important bear fact for the moat claim. The pick survives verification, but the memo should carry this correction, not a clean moat story.

### 2. UtilityInnovation acquisition closed 27 Oct 2026 — CONFIRMED, and the net-cash warning is confirmed

Vertiv press release and Form 8-K, both 27 Oct 2026: Vertiv Corporation completed the acquisition of Utility Innovation Holdings, Inc. (UtilityInnovation Group) for approximately \$1.45bn in upfront cash, plus up to \$1.15bn additional contingent cash consideration tied to EBITDA targets over 12- and 24-month periods (Vertiv.com, 27 Oct 2026; 8-K Item 2.01). Date and price confirmed exactly.

Net-cash effect: at 30 Sep 2026 Vertiv held cash and equivalents \$3,512.9m plus short-term investments \$198.8m against total debt of about \$3.36bn — net cash of only about \$355m (Trefis, 31 Oct 2026, from the 10-Q; StockAnalysis quarterly financials show net cash \$354.8m). The \$1.45bn upfront cash payment on 27 Oct 2026, on its own, exceeds that entire net-cash cushion and would flip Vertiv to roughly \$1.1bn net debt, before any Q4 cash generation and before the up-to-\$1.15bn earnout. The dossier's note that the net-cash position is "materially smaller" after the close is therefore confirmed — if anything it understates it: on a mechanical 30-Sep pro forma, the net-cash position is gone. The Q4 balance sheet (expected Feb 2027) is the next hard check.

Its load-bearing facts — the 58.5% Q3 growth (fastest since IPO), 49.1% non-GAAP operating margin, raised FY2026 guidance, first FY2027 outlook of about \$18.5bn, \$14.36bn cash with zero debt, and a fall driven by post-midterm moratorium sentiment rather than company news — are all confirmed from primary sources.
