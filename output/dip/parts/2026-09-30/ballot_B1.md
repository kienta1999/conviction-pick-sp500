Lens: revenue durability, margins, returns on capital, and the dip must not have broken the growth story. Great business bought on sale.

## Single-pick ballot

- Top pick: **IDXX**
- Runner-up: **ISRG**

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Idexx is the classic compounder-on-sale setup: a clear #1 franchise with razor/razor-blade economics and practice-software lock-in, printing 25% net margins and 72% ROE, whose stock fell 8% *after a beat-and-raise quarter* — EPS $4.27 vs $3.94, revenue +9.7%, FY26 guide raised to EPS $14.69–14.94. The decline is pure multiple compression driven by soft US vet visits (same-store −1.3%), not by anything wrong with the business model; the growth story is intact and management just told you so. At ~35x forward earnings it sits at the bottom of its own 40–55x historical range — a rare entry window into the best animal-health compounder ever built.

- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence: Q2 2026 was a beat-and-raise across EPS, revenue, and full-year guidance, yet the stock sold off — that is a price problem (multiple derating on transitory vet-visit softness and FX drag), not a business problem. Installed-base economics, data network effects, and share gains are all still pointing the right way.

- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability: LOW risk** — the installed-base diagnostic moat is reinforced by contributory data network effects; AI is a tailwind here (CoVetAI deal, Sep 2026), automating interpretation and pulling more work *into* the Idexx ecosystem rather than replacing it. Irreplaceability 9/10.

- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** InVue Dx / Cancer Dx instrument placements and vet-visit stabilization; the Q3 2026 print on 2026-11-02 is the proving ground where the raised guide starts converting into re-rating momentum.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +30–35% (multiple re-rates from ~35x toward ~40x on the raised ~$14.80 FY26 EPS base as visit trends normalize). Bull +50–60% (multiple back to ~45x, InVue/Cancer Dx placement cycle accelerates, share gains compound).

- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Pet-spending softness proves structural rather than cyclical; growth decelerates while the multiple de-rates from 35x to 25x — a classic premium-multiple trap where you pay for compounding that never arrives.

- **Conviction (1-10): 8**
