## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick: ISRG**
- **Runner-up: CPRT**
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical's −30% drawdown is pure multiple
  compression on an unbroken compounder, not business damage. The da Vinci installed
  base (11.7k systems) still throws off ~85% recurring revenue, the balance sheet is
  net cash, and da Vinci 5 is mid-rollout. Nothing in the GLP-1 bariatric overhang,
  China noise, or tariff chatter impairs the surgical-robotics franchise — the stock
  simply de-rated from a 58.2x 10-year median forward multiple to ~30x. As procedure
  volumes reaccelerate into 2027, the multiple has room to repair while earnings
  compound.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** the decline maps one-to-one to
  multiple compression (~34x fwd, ~30x on FY estimates vs 58.2x 10-yr median) on
  unchanged franchise economics — installed base still growing, 85% recurring
  revenue, zero debt, 4-for-4 quarterly EPS beats with ~16% avg surprise. No
  competitor, regulator, or technology has damaged the franchise; the "risks"
  (GLP-1s nibbling one procedure category, China/tariffs) are bounded noise, not
  impairment.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability: LOW risk —** AI cannot perform surgery. da Vinci is
  the entrenched standard of care with surgeon-training, hospital-capex, and
  instrument-recurring lock-in; robotics-assisted surgery is the direction of travel,
  and generative AI is at best a complement (planning/imaging), never a substitute
  for the instrument itself.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** da Vinci 5 rollout momentum + procedure-volume
  reacceleration; the 2026-10-20 Q3 print is the near-term proving ground, with the
  GLP-1 bariatric headwind lapping through 2027.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base +20–30% (procedure growth + partial multiple
  repair toward 38–42x); bull +40–55% (volume growth beats, multiple back toward
  45–50x, net-cash redeployment).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** GLP-1s structurally shrink bariatric and adjacent
  metabolic procedure volumes while hospital-capex constraints slow system
  placements — leaving ISRG a 30x stock grinding sideways on decelerating growth,
  i.e. the de-rating was half-justified and the multiple never returns.
- **Conviction (1-10): 7** — the impairment case is the weakest in the field, but
  30x absolute caps the explosiveness of the upside; this is the safest compounding
  rebound, not the deepest-value one.
