## INTU — Intuit (QuickBooks / TurboTax / Credit Karma)

1. **Why it's down — MIXED: AI disruption fear + deliberate growth reset.**
   YTD -47.2%, 1-yr -45.9% (Finnhub, Nov 16); 52w range $252.84–$682.27.
   FY2026 (ended Jul 31, 2026): revenue $21.45B +13.9%, net income $4.57B
   +18%, EPS +20.4%; Big Bets (assisted tax, money, mid-market) +34%,
   30% of revenue. But Q4 FY26 (Aug 25, 2026): EPS $4.03 beat $3.58
   (+12.6%) yet stock fell 6.5% next day (Gekko). Management flagged
   slowing new-customer acquisition (online paying customers +3%), DIY
   TurboTax losses at the price-sensitive segment, and guided FY2027 revenue
   growth of only 9–10% — a deliberate deceleration as it invests to rebuild
   top-of-funnel (QuickBooks Free/Lite, AI-native tax) and accepts lower
   initial ARPC (TipRanks call summary). The AI question: can AI-native tax/
   accounting agents disintermediate TurboTax/QuickBooks, or does Intuit's
   data + assisted network defend it? No single crash event — a grinding
   de-rate on growth-reset + AI fear. Verdict: **unresolved, leaning
   temporary** — the business is still growing double-digit with 8 straight
   EPS beats, but FY2027 is explicitly a reset year and the AI threat is
   unproven either way.
2. **Moat / AI-irreplaceability: 6/10.** Defenses: QuickBooks small-business
   data network, TurboTax brand + assisted expert network, Credit Karma
   distribution, switching costs in accounting workflows. Threats: AI agents
   that do taxes/bookkeeping end-to-end (Intuit's own answer is Intuit
   Intelligence / AI-native experiences; it just acquired AI-native revenue
   platform Sequence and launched Credit Karma Tax free federal filing).
   Intuit is embedding AI rather than being bypassed — so far.
3. **Rebound catalyst:** Q1 FY2027 print Nov 19, 2026 after close (3 days
   away) — consensus EPS $2.46 (vs $3.34 year-ago, -26% — seasonal mix),
   revenue $4.30B; options imply ~5.2% move (Gekko, Nov 13). A beat with
   credible FY2027 re-acceleration commentary re-rates; a soft print
   confirms the reset. Median of 15 price targets $400 (+18.3% vs $338).
4. **Balance sheet:** net debt/EBITDA 0.18x (screen); op margin 28.8%;
   ROE 23.6%; dividend $1.38/qtr, yield ~1.46%.
5. **Valuation:** fwd PE 12.7 vs trailing 20.8; +19.4% analyst upside
   (screen); StockAnalysis mean target $407.89 (+19.4%). Cheap vs its own
   history for a 14% grower — the market is pricing the AI/reset risk.
6. **Category position:** #1 in SMB accounting and consumer tax prep;
   share not demonstrably lost, but new-customer growth is slowing.
7. **Value-trap risk:** AI tax agents commoditize TurboTax; FY2027 reset
   extends into FY2028; DIY segment keeps bleeding; small-business
   sensitivity to a downturn.
8. **Rebound score: 7/10.** Verdict: deepest drawdown in the field on a
   still-growing franchise, but the Nov 19 print is a binary event 3 days
   out — the panel must price that in, not assume it.

3. **Rebound catalyst:** Q4 print Feb 18, 2027 (screen); Q4 guide revenue

5. **Valuation:** fwd PE 33.2 vs trailing 43.3 — the priciest kept name;

6. **Category position:** #1 in its niche; record engine-spare backlog.
7. **Value-trap risk:** low — the risk is overpaying, not impairment:

   growth normalizes.
8. **Rebound score: 6/10.** Verdict: best moat in the field, weakest dip
   and richest multiple — a quality compounder on sale, not a deep-value
   rebound.

8. **Rebound score: 8/10.** Verdict: the freshest beat-and-raise in the
