## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** FIX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLA is the highest-quality business in the
  field on the numbers that matter to a compounder: 42.6% operating margin,
  91.4% ROE, ~$4B of trailing free cash flow, net debt of 0.19x EBITDA —
  and it is still growing revenue 14.2% with the December quarter guided
  ahead of consensus. The 49.5% drawdown is a multiple de-rating from a
  June peak, not a broken growth story: fiscal Q1 FY27 revenue grew 28.9%
  year over year. At 21.5x forward earnings against that return profile,
  this is a great business bought on sale, and the WFE cycle gives it a
  multi-year demand tailwind.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** Revenue grew 28.9% in the
  latest quarter and guidance was raised — the growth story is intact; what
  broke was the multiple, after a sentiment capitulation on Nov 5 and
  margin worries about DRAM input costs that are a cost line, not a demand
  line.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low disruption risk — #1 in process
  control and yield management; AI and advanced packaging raise control
  intensity rather than replacing it.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q2 FY27 print Jan 28, 2027; continued
  WFE spending strength through 2027; margin recovery as memory costs
  normalize.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~$200–220 (+30–45% from $152.32)
  on earnings growth plus partial multiple recovery; bull ~$260–280 (+70–85%)
  if the multiple returns toward its June peak range and WFE beats.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The WFE cycle is real and KLA's 91% ROE
  flatters a capital-light model that still depends on fab spending; if the
  cycle turns, earnings fall faster than the multiple recovers, and the
  29x sales multiple leaves little room for error.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
