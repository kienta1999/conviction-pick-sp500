6. **Category position:** still #1 creative software, but share-loss signals

7. **Value-trap risk:** AI commoditizes creative tools; freemium pivot
   dilutes ARR growth; FY2027 guide disappoints Dec 9; leadership transition

8. **Rebound score: 6/10.** Verdict: cheapest name in the field, but the dip

   fell 47.5% to $558.63 by Nov 6 — giving back the AI-power rally
   (WhyMyStockMoved, Nov 7). Q3 2026 (Oct 29): record sales $20.7B +17%
   YoY, adj EPS $7.20 +45% beating $7.08 consensus, backlog record $78.1B
   (+96% YoY, +$6B sequentially), yet shares fell 9.3% that day: management
   kept full-year sales growth at "mid-to-high teens" instead of raising it,
   guided Q4 to only "moderate" growth, and flagged ~$600M Q3 tariff costs
   (full-year ~$2.2B) with adjusted operating margin near the bottom of its
   target range. Power Generation sales +30% to $3.42B (data centers);
   retail power-gen sales +75%. Q4 headwinds: dealer destocking ~$1.6B,
   capacity-constrained power growth ("no slack in the system" — CEO Joe
   Creed on the call), Asia/Pacific -2%.
   Verdict: **temporary** — a de-rating of a 46x peak multiple, not a demand
   break; backlog extends to 2029–2030 for large engines/turbines.
2. **Moat / AI-irreplaceability: 8/10.** Scale, dealer network, installed
   base, service; power-gen engines/turbines are capital-intensive and
   certification-heavy. AI is a demand driver here (data-center power), not
   a disruptor. Risk: power-gen was only ~1/7 of 2025 sales — the AI
   narrative outran the revenue mix at the peak.
3. **Rebound catalyst:** Q4 print Feb 2, 2027 (screen); 2027 outlook
   discussion in January (management deferred it); backlog conversion as
   capacity expands; tariff-cost normalization.
4. **Balance sheet:** net debt/EBITDA 2.25x (screen) — highest leverage among
   the kept names; op margin 18.8%; ROE 56.1%. Watch item, not distress.
5. **Valuation:** fwd PE 18.7 vs trailing 23.6; +35.8% analyst upside. At the
   June peak the stock was ~46x trailing earnings vs 12–19x at most prior
   year-ends — the de-rating has already happened.
6. **Category position:** #1 heavy equipment; power-gen demand visibility
   beyond the backlog per CEO.
7. **Value-trap risk:** construction/mining cycle turns; dealer destocking
   deepens; tariffs persist; data-center power orders slow; leverage
   amplifies a cyclical downturn.
8. **Rebound score: 7/10.** Verdict: record backlog + capacity-constrained
   demand is a good dip setup, but leverage and cyclicality cap the score.
