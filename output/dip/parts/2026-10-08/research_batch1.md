## 1) IDXX — IDEXX Laboratories (pet/vet diagnostics)

## Earnings-quality flag explanations

- **NFLX — RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN: benign.** Netflix's balance sheet carries effectively **zero traditional accounts receivable** (Zacks balance sheet: Receivables $0 at 6/30/2026; MacroTrends: change in AR $0 for Q1 2026). What looks like receivables growth in the screen is almost certainly content-amortization/working-capital mechanics: the 10-Q explains Q2 operating cash flow declined mainly because **payments for content assets rose $1.06B** while content amortization rose $8.5B vs $7.7B y/y (TradingView/Zacks, Sep 2026) — cash running ahead of amortization, i.e., the opposite of earnings-quality risk. No evidence of collection deterioration.
- **LVS — RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN: benign (casino markers).** Casino credit is the business model: net receivables $742M at Mar 31, 2026 (+77.9% y/y) are **$828M gross casino receivables** (2025 10-K; Q3 2025 10-Q showed Casino $636M gross) — markers extended to VIP/rolling players, standard in Macau/Singapore. The **provision for credit losses rose to $225M** (from $186M), with write-offs $51M in 2025 vs $31M in 2024 (2025 10-K) — worth watching, but provisions are ~27% of gross casino receivables and move with play volumes. No smoking gun of bad-debt blowout; this is casino working capital, not deteriorating collections.
