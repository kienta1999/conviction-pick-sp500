## BALLOT 1 — SINGLE-PICK NOMINATION

- **Top pick:** BKNG
- **Runner-up:** IDXX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Booking is the highest-ROIC business in this panel — 93.6% LTM ROIC, 87% gross margin, 33.8% FCF margin — and it is trading at a near multi-year valuation low (NTM P/E 15.8x, EV/EBITDA 12.1x) not because the engine broke but because the market is pricing AI disintermediation as a certainty. The fundamentals never flinched: Q2 beat on both lines, and AI is already *cutting* its cost to serve (Agoda service cost per booking down double-digits y/y). With an 8.5% buyback yield and $3.6B in Q1 repurchases, shareholders are being paid to wait while the AI narrative resets. When the multiple re-rates even halfway back toward history, this is a 50%+ mover on a business compounding through the cycle.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The −27.7% drawdown is regulatory (EU blocking eTraveli) plus AI-narrative plus macro — while Q2 revenue grew and EPS beat ($2.54 vs $2.43). The most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business: management is aggressively buying back stock at these levels ($3.6B in a single quarter, the largest ever) into a 33.8% FCF margin — that is a compounder's management team telling you the cash engine is intact.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Medium risk — and honestly stated. OTAs are exactly what AI travel agents threaten, which is why the multiple collapsed. But BKNG has the two-sided network (world's largest OTA by gross bookings), 87% gross margins to defend with, and is embedding *inside* the AI layer rather than fighting it — partnerships with OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Amazon, plus its own Lola assistant launched Sep 29, 2026. Disintermediation is a real tail risk, not the base case.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Lola launch reframes the AI narrative from "victim" to "offense" (already live since Sep 29); the binary reset is **Q3 earnings Oct 27, 2026**, where another beat-and-raise against a −27.7% depressed base forces the multiple question. The 8.5% buyback yield puts a floor under the wait.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35–40% — multiple drifts from 15.8x toward ~20x NTM on steady mid-teens earnings growth plus buyback accretion. Bull ~+55–60% — AI narrative flips positive (Lola traction), multiple re-rates toward low-20s, analyst mean target $236.98 (+52%) proves conservative.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** AI agents permanently disintermediate OTAs — travelers book via agent-direct inventory and BKNG degrades into a commoditized supply backend, in which case low-teens NTM earnings is the *new* fair multiple and the 93.6% ROIC was a backward-looking artifact. Watch eTraveli appeal outcome as a secondary hit to EU flight growth.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
