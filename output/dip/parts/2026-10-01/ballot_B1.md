## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** SYK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the highest-quality compounder in this dip set at its cheapest earnings yield since 2016 (fwd P/E 33.6 vs its 5-year mean ~50x) — and the growth engine never stopped: Q2 2026 revenue +18.5%, operating income +31%, 84% recurring revenue off an 11,106-system installed base, zero debt and $7.68B cash. The derating is a second-derivative growth scare (procedure growth 18% → 15%, a declined guidance raise, J&J Ottava clearance headlines) priced against a franchise whose lock-in — surgeon training, FDA-cleared instruments, 20M+ patients — competitors need years to dent. The da Vinci 5 rollout and new cardiac indications expand TAM while the stock still prices a permanently slower grower.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The scare is about the *rate of acceleration*, not a break in the story — Q2 still printed +18.5% revenue with procedure growth in the mid-teens, recurring revenue at 84%, and the balance sheet pristine; Ottava is cleared but not yet commercially at scale, so the dip is priced on a rival that hasn't taken a case, not on lost cases.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — installed-base lock-in (11,106 systems), surgeon training ecosystem, patents, FDA clearances, 84% recurring revenue from instruments/accessories/service. AI is Intuitive's own tailwind (AI-enabled da Vinci 5), not a threat; the substitute risk is rival *robots* (J&J Ottava, Medtronic), not obsolescence of robotic surgery.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings 2026-10-20 (consensus EPS $2.64, revenue $2.91B) with da Vinci 5 utilization data; da Vinci 5 rollout lifting utilization and cardiac indications expanding TAM through H2 2026–2027; Ottava's early commercial trajectory either validating or deflating the competition fear over the next 12 months.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+20–30% to the analyst consensus target $508.68 (31 analysts; Barchart avg $481.93) as 15–18% revenue compounding continues. Bull: procedures re-accelerate toward high-teens on da Vinci 5, the multiple re-rates toward ~45–50x on sustained 15%+ growth, ~+50% or better.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** If Ottava/Medtronic genuinely erode share, procedure growth permanently steps down to ~10%, hospitals defer capex, and tariff/ACA drags compound — then 33x fwd P/E is still expensive and the multiple keeps compressing; short interest rose to 8.12M shares and there was insider selling in August 2026.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
