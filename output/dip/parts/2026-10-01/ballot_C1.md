## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** SYK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the #1 robotic-surgery franchise by a wide margin — 11,106 installed systems, 84% recurring revenue, and surgeon training that makes switching nearly unthinkable — trading at its cheapest earnings yield since 2016 (EV/EBIT 25.6x vs historical mean 43.2x; fwd P/E 33.6 vs ~50x mean) because the market priced a growth scare as a franchise break. Procedure growth is still mid-teens (15% in Q2, guided 13–15% for FY2026), Q2 revenue was +18.5% with EPS +26%, and the balance sheet is pristine (zero debt, $7.68B cash + investments). The da Vinci 5 rollout, cardiac indications, and a China recovery give multiple operating levers over the next 12–18 months while the stock demands only a return to normal growth, not perfection.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence: the business beat and grew anyway — Q2 2026 revenue +18.5% ($2.89B), operating income +31%, EPS +26%, recurring revenue 84%, with 532 system placements in Q4 2025 — while the stock fell ~14% the next day purely because management declined to *raise* guidance, plus a newly FDA-cleared J&J Ottava that is cleared but not yet commercially at scale. That's a multiple derating on second-derivative fear, not on the business losing patients, surgeons, or share.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** low risk — AI cannot perform surgery, and here AI is a tailwind (AI-enabled da Vinci 5), not a threat. The irreproducible assets are the 11,106-system installed base, the surgeon training ecosystem, 20M+ patients treated, and FDA Class III clearances. The real substitute risk is rival *robots* (J&J Ottava, Medtronic) — competition, not commoditization — and that is the value-trap edge, not an AI one.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings 2026-10-20 (consensus EPS $2.64, revenue $2.91B) — a clean beat-plus-restored guidance re-rates the multiple immediately; behind it, da Vinci 5 utilization lifting per-procedure economics and new cardiac indications expanding TAM through H1 2027.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+20–26% (consensus analyst target $508.68 vs ~$405); bull ~+45–55% (~$590–630) if growth re-accelerates toward 18% and the fwd multiple re-rates toward ~45x, roughly the historical premium band.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** If Ottava/Medtronic genuinely erode share and procedure growth permanently steps down to ~10%, hospitals defer capex, and tariff/ACA drags compound — then 33.6x fwd P/E is still expensive and the multiple keeps compressing; short interest at 8.12M shares and August insider selling are the market's votes on exactly this.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
