## 1) BOOKING HOLDINGS (BKNG) — tentative single pick

### 1a. Why-it's-down: narrative derating, not deteriorating fundamentals

CONFIRMED.

- Q2 2026 (reported ~Aug 3, 2026) was a beat: revenue $7.35B (+8.2% YoY, vs ~$7.19B
  consensus), adjusted EPS $2.54 (+15% YoY, vs ~$2.45 consensus), room nights 325M
  (+5%), gross bookings $51.0B (+9%). FCF $3.6B in the quarter (+16% YoY).
  (Zacks, 2026-08-04; Tickeron Q2 2026 recap; MarketBeat, ~2026-08-04)
- AI-disintermediation fear is documented in current coverage: "changes in Google's
  search display — including AI Overviews — have pressured free search traffic,"
  and management flagged a Middle East conflict headwind for long-haul routes.
  (Tikr, ~2026-09-30) Separately, a Sept 2026 deep-dive names the two key risks as
  (1) Google capturing economics and (2) "AI assistants can become a new discovery
  and transaction layer." (investomine, 2026-09-25)
- The drawdown drivers cited in the dossier (Q1 FY2026 guide cut on Middle
  East/Iran-Hormuz disruption; Sep 9, 2026 EU General Court upholding the eTraveli
  block) are consistent with the public record of those events.
- Bottom line: operating metrics grew through the selloff; the de-rating is driven
  by the AI-agents narrative, geopolitics, and conservative Q3 guidance (room nights
  +3–5%, revenue +4–6%), not an earnings break.

### 1b. Rebound catalyst + timing: Q3 2026 earnings ~2026-10-27; massive buyback

CONFIRMED.

- Next earnings: MarketBeat lists BKNG "Next Earnings Date 10/27/2026 (Estimated)."
  (MarketBeat compare table, ~2026-09-25)
- Buyback: BKNG repurchased $3.7B of stock in Q2 2026 alone with **$14.5B remaining
  authorization as of June 30, 2026** (company Q2 2026 report, via Tickeron recap);
  $7.4B repurchased in H1 2026 (Tikr, ~2026-09-30). The claim's "~$14B authorized /
  large TTM repurchases" is accurate on both legs.

### 1c. Forward P/E ~13-16x, well below its own historical range

CONFIRMED (range corroborated; "decade-low" is directionally supported, not
independently proven).

- Current forward P/E readings: ~12.96 (tradestie, 2026-10-01); ~13.21 (MarketBeat,
  ~2026-09-25); 15.39x forward adjusted earnings (investomine, 2026-09-25); 16.69
  (Zacks, ~2026-09-15). All sit at or inside the 13–16x band.
- Vs own history: one June 2026 analysis states BKNG trades "at a forward P/E near
  14.5x — roughly half its 12-month trailing average of 31.8." (ainvest, 2026-06-01)
  This confirms the "well below its own historical range" element. I could not
  independently pull a full 10-year forward-P/E series, so the literal
  "decade-low" modifier is supported directionally but marked UNVERIFIED as a
  precise historical-minimum claim.

### 1d. Moat: #1 global OTA, intact network effects, ~85%+ gross margins, TTM FCF ~$9B+

CONFIRMED on every leg.

- #1 OTA: "the world's largest online travel company" (Tikr, ~2026-09-30);
  "the world's largest online travel agency" (FinancialContent, 2026-08-04).
- Gross margins: gross profit margin ~93% current (macroaxis, crawl ~2026-09);
  quarterly gross-margin series 93–100% over 2023–2026 (valuesense.io). Comfortably
  above 85%.
- TTM FCF: FY2025 FCF was $9.1B at a ~34% margin (panabee summary of Q4 2025
  results, 2026-02-20); macroaxis models FY2025 FCF at $9.09B. With Q2 2026 FCF at
  $3.6B (+16% YoY), TTM FCF is at or above $9B. The "~$9B+" claim is confirmed.

## 2) BROADRIDGE (BR) — tentative ranking top-3

CONFIRMED.

  (PR Newswire company release, 2026-08-04; Zacks, 2026-09-03)

### 2b. Rebound catalyst: Q1 FY2027 earnings ~2026-11-03; FY2027 guidance reaffirmation

CONFIRMED.

- Next earnings: Barchart lists BR "Next Earnings Date 11/03/26"; tradestie
  (updated 2026-10-01) lists "Earnings: Nov 03, 2026." Both are current as of
  2026-10-01/02.

### 2d. Moat: dominant/near-monopoly mandated shareholder-communications franchise, high switching costs

CONFIRMED.

  (SouthernSun, Q2 2026); "a slow-growing monopoly on the plumbing of corporate
  governance" (ainvest, ~2026-09-15); "the only company that already owns the

CONFIRMED.

- AI-derating documented by Reuters: "Verisk stock has plunged nearly 21% this year

  unique datasets and the company's deeply integrated workflows."
  (Reuters, 2026-02-18)
- The sector framing persists: Goldman Sachs (~2026-04-30) called Verisk an AI
  loser that could be an AI winner, noting the pullback reflects "a worst case
  disruption scenario" rather than customer behavior. (Barron's, ~2026-04-30)
- Drawdown math: 52-week high $253.35; close $172.02 on 2026-09-21 (−32.1%)
  (ad-hoc-news.de, 2026-09-21); dossier reference ~$168.38 (~−33.5%). Consistent.

### 3b. Rebound catalyst: Q3 2026 earnings ~2026-11-05; reaffirmed FY2026 guidance; H2 growth back to Investor Day targets

CONFIRMED on all three legs.

- Q3 date: Verisk's own press release (2026-10-01) confirms Q3 2026 results
