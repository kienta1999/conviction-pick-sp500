# Panelist B — growth/quality compounder ballot, 2026-10-10
Lens: revenue durability, margins, returns on capital, and that the dip hasn't broken the growth story. Looking for a great business bought on sale.

---

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** IDXX
- **Runner-up:** SYK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** IDEXX is the highest-quality business in the panel — ROE 74%, 35% operating margins, 62% gross margins, a razor-and-blade diagnostics monopoly with consumables lock-in and the world's largest vet reference-lab network. It's trading at a forward P/E of 30.9 versus a historical 45–60× premium multiple, sitting near its 52-week low, because the market is pricing a cyclical vet-visit decline as if it were franchise impairment. Meanwhile the business itself keeps beat-and-raising (8 straight EPS beats, FY2026 guidance raised twice, EPS growing ~12–13%), and the new CEO transition is complete. When visits stabilize — comps ease as the pet-adoption trough lapses into 2027 — the multiple re-rates and the compounder math takes over again.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** the single most compelling evidence is the "growth premium to visits": Q2 2026 CAG Diagnostics recurring revenue grew 10.3% organically *while* U.S. clinical visits fell 1.3% — an ~1,100bp premium. That's not a business losing its customers; it's a business gaining pricing power and per-visit share during a consumer-pressure dip. Revenue durability is proven, not promised.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** low risk. Proprietary analyzers (9,000+ inVue Dx installs) + consumables lock-in + reference-lab network = extreme switching costs; Zoetis is the only real peer and IDEXX outgrows it. AI is additive — IDEXX acquired CoVetAI (Sep 16, 2026) to bundle AI workflow with diagnostics. A generative model can't run a blood panel. Irreplaceability 9/10.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings Nov 2, 2026 — continued inVue Dx/Cancer Dx rollout (10,000+ ordering clinics) plus any stabilization in H2 visit comps re-rates the stock. Timing: 1–2 quarters.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base — visits stabilize, EPS grows ~12–15%, multiple drifts from 31× toward ~38–40× → ~$640–680 (+25–30% from ~$518). Bull — visits re-accelerate in 2027, another guidance raise, multiple back toward 45× → ~$750–800 (+45–55%). Analyst mean target $701 implies +35%.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** the visit decline is structural, not cyclical — younger-cohort pet ownership shrinking, wellness visits permanently eroding — capping the consumables flywheel that justifies any premium multiple. If H2 visits print worse than the guided −1.5 to −2%, another guidance cut follows, the new CEO's execution stays unproven, and 31× for low-single-digit growth derates into the low 20s — new lows, not a rebound.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 — compelling data point + scenario

**IDXX:** the single most compelling data point is the recurring-revenue decoupling from visits — 10.3% organic CAG growth with −1.3% visits in Q2, plus two guidance raises in FY2026 and 8 straight EPS beats. That's a growth story the dip hasn't broken; the price just moved ahead of it. Base 12–18mo: +25–30% to ~$640–680 on visit stabilization and a 38–40× multiple; bull: +45–55% to ~$750–800 if visits re-accelerate into 2027.

**SYK:** the dip is one plant in one sub-segment of one division — Peripheral Vascular from the Inari acquisition — while the rest of the business printed a Q2 beat (adj EPS $3.69 vs $3.49) and *raised* FY2026 organic growth guidance to 8.3–9.3% with zero capital-equipment cancellations. At 16.6× forward versus a historical ~30× for the #1 orthopedics/Mako robotics compounder, the discount is sentiment, not the franchise. Base 12–18mo: +30–35% to ~$360–380 as backorders clear at the Oct 29 Q3 print; bull: +45%+ toward $400+ if the Inari issue is fully resolved by Q4 and the 30× compounder multiple returns.

**BKNG:** eight straight EPS beats and Q2 revenue +8.1% with the stock at 13× forward versus its own mid-to-high-20s norm — the September AI-agent selloff (−18.5% in a month) repriced the model while the numbers kept compounding; the disruption fear hasn't shown up in any operating metric. Base 12–18mo: +40–50% toward the ~$233–238 consensus targets on a Q3 beat (Nov 3) and buyback support; bull: +60%+ if the market accepts AI agents as an OTA cost-of-customer-acquisition shift rather than disintermediation, restoring a 20× multiple.

- **CMG:** management deliberately sacrificing margins for flat 2026 comps, 25–35 cohort pullback may be secular, and 23× forward is still a premium price — "formerly premium brand" is the trap version.
- **BRO:** organic revenue fell 0.7%, mega-deal indigestion and a soft rate cycle mean the growth story is paused, not broken — but through this lens the compounder must *be compounding*; needs the Oct 26 Q3 turn first.
