## SPGI — S&P Global ($409.99)

### 1. Why-down cause — CONFIRMED, with a timeline clarification
- The Feb 2026 "SaaSpocalypse" is real and Anthropic-triggered: Reuters (5 Feb 2026, via multiple syndicated summaries) reported ~$1tn erased from software/services after Anthropic's Claude Cowork plugins (released 30 Jan 2026); Jefferies coined "SaaSpocalypse" on 3 Feb 2026. SPGI was caught in it — TipRanks earnings history shows SPGI fell from $417.25 to $376.76 (−9.7%) the day after its 10 Feb 2026 report.
- The "optical guidance cut" element is confirmed but belongs to **July, not February**: the Mobility spin-off **completed 1 Jul 2026** (S&P Global 10-Q; Morningstar company profile). On the Q2 call (28 Jul 2026, MarketBeat transcript), CFO Eric Aboaf introduced post-spin guidance excluding Mobility for the full year and stated it is "not directly comparable" to prior guidance that included Mobility. Trefis (8 Oct 2026) summarises the same: headline revenue growth cut to 5.9%–7.9% and adjusted EPS to $17.50–$17.75 from $19.40–$19.65 including Mobility, while the spin "may account for much of the cut."
- Organic guide unchanged + Ratings/Indices raised — confirmed verbatim from the Q2 call: organic constant-currency revenue growth guided 6%–8%, identical to the prior (incl.-Mobility) guide; Ratings raised +1pt to 5%–8%; Indices raised +2pts to 12%–14%; Market Intelligence and Energy unchanged.
- Caveat for the panel: the claim compresses two events (Feb AI scare = drawdown start; Jul spin recast = optical cut) into one sentence. Both elements check out; the chronology should not be read as the spin causing the February fall.

### 2. Catalyst + date: Q3 2026 earnings on 27 Oct 2026 — CONFIRMED

## IDXX — IDEXX Laboratories ($520.31)

### 5. Why-down cause — CONFIRMED
- Primary-quality source: Reuters, 2 Feb 2026 (via SRN News / WIXX syndications) — IDEXX forecast 2026 revenue and profit **slightly above** Wall Street estimates, but shares **fell 6% to $628.70** in morning trade after the company said the outlook includes a roughly **2% decline in U.S. same-store clinical visits** and modestly lower net price realisation. Guide: revenue $4.63–4.72bn, EPS $14.29–14.80; midpoints marginally above LSEG consensus ($4.66bn / $14.50). CEO Jay Mazelsky on the call attributed pressure to wellness visits (down 3.6% in Q4) amid pet-owner financial pressure, with non-wellness steadier.
- The Q4 2025 call transcript (MarketBeat, 2 Feb 2026) confirms the −2% figure in management's own words ("the 2% for 2026 is the baseline"). This is a visit-volume / multiple story on an above-consensus guide, exactly as claimed — no franchise break in the record.

### 6. Catalyst evidence: Q2 beat + guidance raised twice — CONFIRMED

- "Raised twice" checks out arithmetically: Feb initial $14.29–$14.80 → Q1 raise to $14.45–$14.90 (AInvest, May 2026: guidance updated to revenue $4.675–4.760bn, EPS $14.45–$14.90) → Q2 raise to $14.69–$14.94.
