## PART 1 — SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** NFLX
- **Runner-up:** SYK

**Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Netflix is the streaming winner bought at a loser's price. Q2 2026 printed revenue +13.4%, a 33.4% operating margin, and guided FY2026 FCF of ~$12.5B — while the stock sits ~43% off its high at ~20x forward earnings versus a 36x five-year average. The drawdown is driven by one analyst's engagement estimates (Wells Fargo Underweight, PT $57), not the reported numbers, and 382M paid memberships with demonstrated pricing power (an 8–12.5% price increase taken with no elevated churn) say the subscription engine is intact. The $3B ad-tier business doubling year-over-year is a fresh growth lever the market isn't paying for at this multiple.

**Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The earnings-quality flag check came back benign — receivables are flat/down while revenue grew 13–16%, Q2 operating cash flow exceeded net income, and FY26 FCF guidance is ~$12.5B. Cash conversion is the opposite of a broken growth story; a permanently impaired business doesn't guide a record free-cash year while buying back $4.7B of its own stock in one quarter. Ackman re-bought a ~$950M position arguing Netflix "won the streaming wars" — the fundamentals kept compounding while the narrative reset.

**Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW disruption risk. Global content scale + recommendation flywheel + production ecosystem; 382M memberships and ad-tier sign-ups at 45–60%+ of new adds. AI is a tailwind here — used in ~300 titles for production efficiency and across the ads lifecycle. Generative AI doesn't replace a library of must-watch content and a decade of personalization data.

**Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings October 20, 2026 (AMC) is the near-term trigger — a re-acceleration print plus a raised margin guide directly answers the engagement thesis. The definitive data point is Netflix's annual engagement report in January 2027, which could retire the Wells Fargo bear case entirely; ad-tier monetization ($1.5B → ~$3B) keeps repricing the growth algorithm through 2027.

**12-18 month return scenario:** Base — the engagement overhang fades on continued double-digit revenue growth, multiple re-rates partway from 20x toward history: ~+30–40% to ~$92–100 (in line with the $95.51 analyst mean). Bull — January 2027 engagement data kills the churn thesis, ad tier scales toward $4–5B, margins push past 32%: ~+50–65% to ~$107–117 (POEMS' $110 TP sits in this zone).

**Top risk (value-trap case):** If the engagement decline is real and persistent — Nielsen share already at post-May-2025 lows, thinner H2 slate — churn rises into 2027 and the multiple compresses from ~20x toward Cahall's 15x-on-2027-EPS case ($57, ~–20% from here). The $19B 2026 content budget is largely fixed, so a revenue shortfall hits margins straight down.

**Conviction (1-10):** 8
