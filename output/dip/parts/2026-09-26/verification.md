## NFLX (Netflix)

### Claim 1 — ~43% off the 52-week high, driven largely by Wells Fargo's Sep 18, 2026 Underweight downgrade (PT ~$57) built on estimated engagement data
**CONFIRMED.**
- Finnhub (2026-09-26): NFLX current price $71.145, 52-week high $124.86, 52-week low $65.08.
  Drawdown from the high = (124.86 − 71.145) / 124.86 = **−43.0%** — matches the "~43%" claim.
- Wells Fargo analyst Steven Cahall downgraded NFLX to Underweight from Equal Weight on Friday, September 18, 2026
  and cut the price target to **$57 from $80** — confirmed by InvestingChannel (Sep 2026), Insider Monkey (Sep 21, 2026),
  and American Market News (Sep 24 & 25, 2026).
- The bear case was built on **Cahall's own engagement estimates**, not company data: BestStocks' Sep 2026 FAQ cites his
  estimate of 2H engagement (hours viewed per subscriber per day) falling ~4% YoY, with Top-100 Originals viewing down
  ~21%; it explicitly notes the viewership figures are his own estimates, not data Netflix has reported.
- Note: the stock fell ~4–4.7% to ~$71.79 on the downgrade day (Sep 18), so the downgrade accelerated a slide that was
  already underway — "driven largely by" is fair as a framing, not an exact causal attribution.

### Claim 2 — Fundamentals intact: Q2 2026 revenue +~13.4%, operating margin ~33.4%, FY2026 FCF guided ~$12.5B
**CONFIRMED.**
- Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, reported July 16, 2026): revenue **$12.56B, +13.4% YoY**; operating income $4.19B
  (+11%); **operating margin 33.4%** (down from 34.1% a year earlier, slightly ahead of company forecast); diluted EPS
  $0.80 (+11%). — StockTitan summary via ad-hoc-news (Sep 13, 2026); Daily Newsfront (Jul 16, 2026).
- FY2026 revenue guided **$51.0–51.4B** (13–14% growth), full-year operating margin target held at 31.5%, and **FY2026
  free cash flow guided at ~$12.5B** — Yahoo Finance analysis via TimesNG (Sep 17, 2026); ad-hoc-news (Sep 4, 2026).
  (Note Q2 FCF itself was $1.53B, down from $2.27B a year earlier.)

### Claim 3 — Forward P/E ~20x vs ~36x 5-year average — deepest derating vs own history
**CONFIRMED** (corroborated by two independent analyst write-ups, though both are second-tier sources).
- blockchain.news (Sep 23, 2026): "The forward P/E sits at roughly 20x."
- nova.kapualabs (crawled recently): "the forward P/E ratio sits around 20x … That's a dramatic compression from the
  five-year average of 36x."
- Sanity check from Finnhub (2026-09-26): trailing P/E 21.80 — consistent with a ~20x forward figure, not contradicted
  by any primary source found.
- Caveat: I could not locate the ~36x figure in a primary or tier-1 source (Simply Wall St's historical table shows
  Netflix trailing P/E of 21.9x at 2026-09-21 rising to 48.8x in late 2025 — a 5-year average of ~36x is plausible from
  that history, but the exact average was not independently recomputed).

### Claim 4 — Q3 2026 earnings scheduled for Oct 20, 2026 (after market close)
**CONFIRMED.**
- foreignpolicyjournal.com (Sep 16, 2026): "Netflix's upcoming earnings disclosure, which is scheduled for October 20, 2026."
- ad-hoc-news (Sep 2026): "Netflix has circled 20 October on the calendar for its third-quarter 2026 financial report …
  the company will release the figures at roughly 1:01 a.m. Pacific Time, with a live management interview following
  at 1:45 a.m. Pacific Time." (That release-time convention corresponds to Netflix's usual after-market-close print.)

## SYK (Stryker)

### Claim 1 — March 2026 cyber incident caused ~$375M in deferred sales and a Q1 miss; also a Q2 Inari plant supply issue
**CONFIRMED** (with a timing nuance on the Inari issue).
- Cyber incident first reported **March 11, 2026** (Stryker 8-K) — confirmed via Stryker's own Q2 2026 10-Q (filed Jul 31,
  2026) and multiple press reports.
- **~$375M of deferred or lost sales**: stated in ainvest.com's June 2026 analysis ("A three-week production shutdown from
  the cyber incident caused about $375 million of deferred or lost sales"). Not found in Stryker's own filings verbatim,
  but consistent with the reported Q1 miss ($6.02B vs ~$6.33–6.35B expected).
- **Q1 2026 miss** (reported April 30, 2026): adjusted EPS **$2.60 vs $2.98 expected** (−12.8%), revenue $6.02B vs ~$6.33B
  expected, ~6.5% one-day decline — confirmed by MassDevice (Apr 30, 2026), ainvest, and Barchart.
- **Inari supply issue**: confirmed — but it surfaced more prominently in Q3 commentary than in Q2 results. MedTech Dive
  (Sep 10, 2026): "Stryker is still working through supply disruptions for its peripheral vascular business following a
  March cyberattack … CFO Preston Wells said at the Wells Fargo Healthcare Conference," noting the issues "may continue
  into the fourth quarter." The Inari manufacturing integration was disrupted by the cyberattack's "ripple effects" (William
  Blair analyst note, via MedTech Dive). Framing it as "a Q2 Inari plant supply issue" is approximately correct — the
  disruption is real and Inari-linked — but the public airing was September, not Q2.

### Claim 2 — Full-year 2026 guidance was HELD (adjusted EPS $14.90–15.10); Q2 2026 beat with adj operating margin ~27.4%
**CONFIRMED** (with a minor range discrepancy).
- Q2 2026 results (July 30, 2026 — Stryker's own press release via BioSpace, StockTitan 8-K filing, and investors.stryker.com):
  net sales **$6.6B (+9.4% reported, +9.0% organic)**; reported EPS $3.30 (+44.1%); adjusted EPS **$3.69 (+17.9%)**;
  **adjusted operating income margin 27.4%** (+170 bps). Consensus was ~$3.49, so it was a beat.
- Guidance: at Q2 the company **narrowed** (floor raised) to **adjusted EPS $14.95–$15.10** (vs $14.90–$15.10 at Q1) and
  organic net sales growth 8.3–9.3%. "HELD" is the right substance — guidance was not cut despite the Q1 miss — but the
  exact range the panel cites ($14.90–15.10) was the Q1-era range; post-Q2 it is $14.95–$15.10. Immaterial.

### Claim 3 — Q3 2026 earnings scheduled for Oct 29, 2026
**CONFIRMED.**
- TipRanks: "Stryker Corporation (SYK) is scheduled to report earnings on **Oct 29, 2026, After Close (Confirmed)**."
- Corroborated by Zacks ("expected on October 29, 2026") and MarketBeat ("Oct. 29 After Market Closes — Estimated").
- (TipRanks' earnings-history table also confirms the Q1 miss: Apr 30, 2026 forecast $2.98 / actual $2.60.)

### Claim 4 — Forward P/E ~21.6x, at the bottom of its historical 24–77x range
**PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — headline figure confirmed; historical range UNVERIFIED.**
- Forward P/E ~21.7x **confirmed**: Zacks fundamental ratios show **P/E (F1) 21.72** (crawled ~Aug 2026).
- The historical "24–77x range" could not be corroborated: stock-analysis-on.net's long-term P/E table shows Stryker
  P/E values like 68.67 (2015), 24.92 (2016), 57.72 (2021), 47.65 (2022), 39.24 (2020), 31.08 (2014) — a scattered
  history, not a clean 24–77 band. A separate source (valueinvesting.io, Sep 26, 2026) gives forward P/E multiples of
  26.1–29.2x — different methodology. Mark the "at the bottom of 24–77x" framing **UNVERIFIED**; the panel should not
  lean on that range as established fact.

## CPRT (Copart)

### Claim 1 — Drawdown drivers: cyclical US insurance-unit decline, loss of the Progressive account to IAA (partially admitted on the Sep 10, 2026 Q4 call), and CEO churn (Jeff Liaw departed June 29, 2026; Jay Adair returned)
**CONFIRMED** (with two nuances).
- **Insurance-unit decline**: Q4 FY2026 (reported Sep 10, 2026) — global insurance units −4.2%, domestic insurance
  assignments **−7.5%**, the **6th consecutive quarter of shrinking US insurance volumes** (Speedwell Research,
  Sep 2026). Confirmed in the earnings-call transcripts (Alphastreet; Motley Fool).
- **One major insurance customer loss**: on the call, Jay Adair disclosed losing "**one specific major insurance
  customer**" — excluding it, domestic insurance assignments would have been **+2.3%** instead of −7.5% (Alphastreet
  transcript; Speedwell). **Management did NOT name the customer on the call.** The identification as Progressive, lost
  to IAA/RB Global, comes from independent analyst/investor writing: Seeking Alpha (Sep 2026) lists "loss of exclusive
  Progressive business" among CPRT's risks, and investmentcase.substack notes "RB Global's locked multiyear carrier
  contracts (Progressive ~90% to IAA)." So "partially admitted on the call" is fair; the Progressive→IAA identification
  itself is market/investor attribution, not a company admission.
- **CEO churn**: **confirmed**. Copart's own 8-K/press release (via StockTitan): announced **June 29, 2026** that Jeff
  Liaw will step down as CEO and director **effective July 31, 2026**; Executive Chairman A. Jayson "Jay" Adair —
  previously CEO 2010–2024 — resumes the CEO role effective July 31, 2026. The panel's "departed June 29" should be
  read as the announcement date; the effective date was July 31. Morningstar, Simply Wall St, and ainvest corroborate.

### Claim 2 — $1.9B all-cash acquisition of ACV Auctions announced Sep 10, 2026, expected to close by end of 2026
**CONFIRMED.**
- Definitive merger agreement announced **September 10, 2026**: Copart to acquire ACV Auctions for **$10.50/share in
  cash**, ~**$1.9B** total equity value, a ~45% premium to ACV's unaffected Aug 10, 2026 close (AlphaStreet Sep 2026;
  ad-hoc-news citing the BusinessWire joint release; Simply Wall St; Medium/simianx citing the SEC-filed press release).
- **100% funded from cash on hand**, no financing condition; tender offer commenced Sep 17 (Apple Merger Sub),
  expiring Sep 30, 2026 (CollisionWeek, Sep 18, 2026).
- **Expected to close by end of calendar 2026**, subject to Hart-Scott-Rodino antitrust review and customary conditions
  (AlphaStreet). Largest acquisition in Copart's history.

### Claim 3 — Zero debt, ~$3.35B cash, forward P/E ~17.1x vs ~34x 5-year median
**CONFIRMED on balance sheet and forward P/E; the 5-year median figure is WEAK (partially contradicted).**
- **$3.35B cash + zero debt**: MarketBeat financials — at April 30, 2026 (Q3 FY26), Copart held **$3.35B in cash and
  equivalents plus $845.6M in short-term investments**, with total liabilities of only $857.6M and "no material
  long-term debt." Corroborated: chartsview.co.uk ("no funded debt… $4,199.7m of cash and held-to-maturity securities at
  30 April 2026" — note their figure includes short-term investments, consistent); marketshost (Debt/Equity 0.00x).
  (Q4 FY26 call reported $4.5B in cash equivalents + held-to-maturity securities at July 31, 2026 — blockonomi, Sep 2026.)
- **Forward P/E ~17.1x**: intellectia.ai (updated Jun 29, 2026): "current forward PE ratio of **17.15**." Seeking Alpha
  (Sep 2026) says forward PE 17.8 — close enough.
- **vs ~34x 5-year median**: **UNVERIFIED / soft CONTRADICTION.** intellectia.ai gives a **5-year average of 29.19**,
  not 34x. Tickeron's Sep 2026 write-up says "~20 times trailing EPS … well below its five-year median near 34 times"
  — i.e., 34x refers to the *trailing* 5-year median, not a forward-P/E comparator. The panel compares a forward P/E
  against a trailing median; the direction (multi-year-low valuation) is right, but the framing mixes metrics.

### Claim 4 — Drawdown of ~40% from 52-week high (~$27.59)
**CONTRADICTED on the 52-week-high figure; the ~40% drawdown itself is approximately right.**
- The actual 52-week high is **~$45.89–$50.11**, not $27.59: Barchart (Aug/Sep 2026) lists 52-week high **$50.11**,
  52-week low $26.81; StreetInsider lists $45.89; BeyondLink $48.96; blockonomi $49.05.
- **$27.59 is near the 52-week LOW ($26.81)** — the panel appears to have confused the high with the low.
- Drawdown math from a real high: e.g. ($48.96 → ~$28–32) = ~35–43%. So a "~40% drawdown" is a fair description of the
  peak-to-current move — but the stated $27.59 high is wrong and should be corrected to ~$46–50.
