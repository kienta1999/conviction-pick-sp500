**Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings Oct 20, 2026 (AMC) — the first clean rebuttal of the engagement thesis, <1 month out. The Jan 2027 annual engagement report is the definitive kill-shot for the bear case. Ad-tier monetization scaling ($1.5B → ~$3B in 2026) reprices the growth algorithm through 2027.

**12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35% (return to ~$96 analyst mean target on re-rating to ~24x forward as engagement data clears the overhang). Bull ~+60-90% (re-rating toward the $110-135 high-end targets if ad revenue re-accelerates membership growth and the multiple normalizes toward its ~36x history).

**Top risk (value-trap case):** If the engagement decline is real and persistent (Nielsen share at post-May-2025 lows, thinner H2 slate), 2027 churn rises and the multiple compresses toward Cahall's 15x-2027-EPS case → $57 PT is another -20% from here. The $19B content spend is fixed-ish, so any revenue-growth shortfall flows straight to margin disappointment, and there are few company-specific positive catalysts before the Jan 2027 engagement report.

**CPRT.** The single most compelling data point: a zero-debt, 32%-margin fortress was compressed to ~17x forward (vs ~34x 5-yr median) on a cyclical US-insurance-unit trough plus the confirmed loss of *one* major account to IAA — while total-loss frequency hit a second-quarter record 23.3% (a secular tailwind), international units grew +10%, and founder Jay Adair returned as CEO alongside a JPMorgan Overweight upgrade to $40 and the $1.9B all-cash ACV Auctions acquisition (funded from the $3.35B cash pile, breakeven year one, EPS-accretive from FY2028) opening the dealer-wholesale channel.
