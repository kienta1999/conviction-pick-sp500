## 1. GOOGL — Alphabet Inc. (Class A)

### 1. Why it's down — verdict: MOSTLY TRANSITORY (capex-bill / sentiment), with a real, slow-burn structural question on search monetisation

Source: Q3 release via The Bay Area Brief, 28 Oct; SEO Turtle / ValueAddVC, Nov 2026.
- **Sector washout.** The 16–17 Nov leg (−5.3% in two sessions) and the 20 Nov −2.7% were largely Nvidia-led AI deleveraging, not Alphabet-specific news.

The permanent-risk tail is real but slow: if AI answers structurally reduce ad clicks faster than Google can monetise AI Mode/Overviews, search economics erode over years, not quarters.

At $3,500 gold the spread is ~$2,050/oz — still enormous by historical standards, but ~28% narrower; earnings at $3,500 gold are materially lower than the $2.20/quarter run-rate implies. The 10x forward P/E is cheap *only if* gold holds near $4,000 and 2027 EPS $10.15 (consensus, range $9.34–$11.48) is achieved. If gold settles at $3,500, the "cheap" multiple is on earnings that no longer exist.
- **Verdict:** genuinely cheap vs history and vs peers on current earnings, with a fortress balance sheet — but this is a peak-cycle multiple, and the margin of safety is entirely a gold-price call, not a business call.

### 6. Category position — STILL #1, PRICING POWER = THE GOLD PRICE
World's largest gold miner; a price-taker by definition — "pricing power" is the gold price itself, and NEM's leverage to it is the thesis. No share loss: production fell on planned maintenance/resequencing, not competitive loss; peers (Barrick, Agnico Eagle) face the same gold price and the same cost inflation. Q3 AISC improvement (guide cut to $1,520/oz) was partly co-product credits (silver +57%, copper $6.91/lb) — co-product AISC actually *rose* for a fourth straight quarter to $1,963/oz, and Q4 silver halves with 2027 silver down ~30% (Aardvark). Cost discipline is real but flattered by by-products.

### 7. Value-trap risk — the honest bear case
Gold is a momentum/crowded trade unwinding: down ~30% from its record high, with the Dec hike still to come — if the Fed hikes in Dec *and* signals more, gold can fall further and stay down while real yields stay high. NEM's earnings are a spread, not a franchise: at $3,500 gold with Q4 AISC $1,900, the spread compresses fast, and 2027 costs are guided higher. The "Great Decoupling" (Wedbush, Mar 2026): record gold prices failing to translate into equity gains because AISC inflation eats the windfall — that pattern is now repeating at lower gold. Red Chris ($4.2–4.8bn) is a decade-long capital commitment. If gold enters a multi-year bear market (real yields high, dollar strong, no crisis bid), NEM grinds lower for years despite a low P/E — the classic miner value trap. Downside trigger: a Dec Fed hike + hawkish guidance, or Q4 AISC printing above $1,900 with gold <$3,800.

### 8. Rebound score: 6/10
**Verdict:** The purest mean-reversion bet of the three — fortress balance sheet, record FCF, single-digit P/E — but the rebound is a gold-price call, not a company call, and the cycle can stay against it longer than a dip-buyer expects.
