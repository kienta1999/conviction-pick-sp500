## FIX (Comfort Systems USA)

### Claim 1 — Q3 2026 revenue $3.77B, +53.7% YoY, EPS $13.91 beating consensus (~$12.81)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED (with a minor consensus-figure discrepancy)**

- Company press release (Business Wire / SEC 8-K Ex. 99.1, Oct 22, 2026): Q3 2026 revenue $3.77B vs $2.45B in Q3 2025 (+53.7%); net income $489.0M; diluted EPS $13.91 vs $8.25 a year earlier.
- Zacks (Oct 22, 2026): EPS $13.91 topped the Zacks Consensus Estimate of **$12.71** by 9.4%; revenue $3.77B beat the $3.35B consensus by 12.5%.
- **Flag:** the dossier's consensus figures are slightly off — Zacks puts the EPS consensus at $12.71 (not ~$12.81) and the revenue consensus at $3.35B (not $3.39B). The beat itself is confirmed and is, if anything, marginally larger than the dossier states. (A separate call summary cites an EPS estimate of $12.85, so ~$12.7–12.9 is the consensus range depending on the vendor.)

### Claim 2 — Record backlog $17.81B, +90% YoY, with +$3.8B of new bookings in Q3 alone
**Verdict: CONTRADICTED as worded — the $3.8B figure is the backlog build, not new bookings**

- Backlog confirmed: company press release (Oct 22, 2026): backlog $17.81B at Sep 30, 2026 vs $14.06B at Jun 30, 2026 and $9.38B at Sep 30, 2025 — +90% YoY, +26.7% sequentially. Motley Fool call transcript (Oct 23, 2026) confirms the same.
- The "+$3.8B" is the **sequential increase in backlog** ($17.81B − $14.06B = $3.75B), not new bookings. Implied new bookings = revenue recognised + backlog change ≈ $3.77B + $3.75B ≈ **$7.5B** in Q3 (book-to-bill ~2.0x). TipRanks (Nov 2026) states this explicitly: "The $3.8 billion sequential increase, combined with quarterly revenue of $3.77 billion, implies bookings of approximately $7.5 billion and a 2.0x book-to-bill ratio." DK Street Journal (Nov 6, 2026) likewise: "against $3.766bn of revenue recognised, that implies by subtraction roughly $7.5bn of new bookings."
- **Material difference:** the dossier understates Q3 bookings by roughly half. Directionally the demand story is even stronger than stated, but the claim as written mislabels the backlog build as bookings.

### Claim 3 — Net cash ~$1.78B (cash ~$2.16B vs ~$49.6M financial debt) and Q3 operating cash flow $1.01B vs $489M net income
**Verdict: CONFIRMED, with a material arithmetic flag on the net-cash label**

- Cash and debt confirmed: Zacks (Oct 22, 2026): "As of Sept. 30, 2026, Comfort Systems had cash and cash equivalents of $2.16 billion, up from $981.9 million at 2025-end. Total debt declined to $49.6 million from $145.2 million." The 10-Q balance sheet (filed Oct 2026) shows cash $2,158,027k; current maturities of long-term debt $5,710k + long-term debt $43,845k = $49,555k.
- OCF confirmed: company press release: "operating cash flow of $1.01 billion in the current quarter compared to $553.3 million in 2025" (precisely $1,010.1M); net income $489.0M. OCF is ~2.1x net income in the quarter.
- **Flag:** the parenthetical arithmetic does not produce $1.78B — $2.16B cash minus $0.05B financial debt = **~$2.11B net cash** on a financial-debt basis. The ~$1.78B figure is only reached by also deducting lease liabilities: total debt including leases was ~$374M ($2.158B − $0.374B = $1.784B), as the dossier itself notes elsewhere. TipRanks likewise reports "roughly $2.1 billion in net cash" on the financial-debt basis. So the $1.78B label is defensible only as a leases-included figure; the dossier's own parenthetical implies $2.11B. Either way the balance sheet is net cash and very strong — the disagreement is definitional, not directional.

## GOOGL (Alphabet Class A)

### Claim 7 — Q3 2026 revenue +25% YoY to ~$127.6B; Google Cloud revenue +103% YoY to ~$30.7B with a ~$561bn backlog
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

- Alphabet Q3 2026 earnings release / call transcript (Oct 28, 2026, via Motley Fool): consolidated revenue $127.6B, +25% YoY in both reported and constant currency; Google Cloud revenue $30.7B, +103% YoY, driven by AI infrastructure, AI solutions and TPU system sales; Google Cloud backlog $561B, with ~55% expected to be recognised as revenue over the next 24 months.
- Zacks (Oct 28, 2026): "GOOGL's Q3 revenues jumped 25% to $127.58B… Google Cloud revenues surged 103% to $30.71B, with a $561B backlog." Cloud operating income $11.1B vs $3.6B a year earlier (36.2% margin).

### Claim 8 — Search still holds ~90% global share with no evidence of share loss
**Verdict: CONFIRMED (on StatCounter referral share; with a measurement caveat)**

- StatCounter Global Stats, October 2026 (retrieved Nov 20, 2026): Google took **91.04% of worldwide search referrals**; September 2026 was 89.94%. Over the last 12 months the worldwide share moved only between 89.85% and 91.32% — "about 90%" is accurate for every month in the period (searchengines.net, Nov 2026; Colorlib, Sep 2026: 89.9%).
- No share loss is evident: the 12-month band is ~1.5 points wide and October 2026 (91.04%) is near the top of it.
- **Caveat:** StatCounter measures search *referrals* (clicks from results pages to websites), not queries performed. The dossier's separate point — that AI Overviews/AI answers reduce clicks per search — is a monetisation risk, not a share loss, and does not contradict the ~90% referral share.

### Claim 9 — Net cash ~$130bn+ (cash + marketable securities ~$265.1bn vs long-term debt ~$125.1bn)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED on the stated (long-term debt) basis, with a definitional flag**

- Alphabet Q3 2026 release / call (Oct 28, 2026): "We ended the quarter with $265.1 billion in cash and marketable securities, which includes $90.4 billion of marketable equity securities. Long-term debt was $125.1 billion." Motley Fool (Nov 21, 2026) and MarketBeat confirm both figures.
- On that basis net cash = $265.1B − $125.1B = **$140.0B**, i.e. above the dossier's "~$130bn+".
- **Flag:** on a *total* debt basis the figure is lower — Arincen (balance sheet, Sep 30, 2026) shows total debt $141.92B and net cash **$123.18B**. The dossier's "~$130bn+" sits between the two definitions; it is accurate vs long-term debt only. Note also the $265.1B includes $90.4B of marketable *equity* securities (e.g. SpaceX/Anthropic stakes), whose value moves with private marks — the cash pile is not all cash.

### Claim 10 — Forward P/E near the bottom of its 5-year range (trailing P/E ~13.6–14.7x vs ~23.7–23.9x median)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**

- Trailing P/E confirmed: Finnhub (Nov 20, 2026 close $265.29): trailing P/E **14.68x**; Arincen (Nov 20, 2026): P/E (TTM) **13.70**, flagged "Lowest in 5 years" against a 5-year range of 13.7–33.6; FinanceCharts: 14.81x on Nov 16, 2026 (price $283.72 / TTM diluted EPS $19.16), vs 12-month average 23.71 and 5-year average 24.04. All inside the dossier's 13.6–14.7x range.
- 5-year median/average confirmed: FinanceCharts 5Y average 24.04; Seeking Alpha (Nov 20, 2026) shows GOOGL P/E GAAP (TTM) 13.85 vs 5Y average 24.27. The dossier's "~23.7–23.9x median" matches the 12-month/3-year averages (23.71 / 23.90).
- Forward P/E is basis-dependent and should be quoted with its basis: Seeking Alpha (Nov 20, 2026) — P/E GAAP (FWD) 13.58 vs 5Y avg 22.54; P/E Non-GAAP (FWD) 20.27 vs 5Y avg 22.83; MarketBeat forward P/E 17.79. The GAAP forward ~13.5x is flattered by one-off investment gains in FY2026 EPS (per the dossier's own earnings-quality flag); the cleaner non-GAAP forward ~20x is still below its 5Y average but not at the bottom of the range. The trailing-P/E claim is solid; the forward-P/E claim holds only on the GAAP basis.
