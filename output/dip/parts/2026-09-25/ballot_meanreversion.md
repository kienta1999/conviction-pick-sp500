### Top 3 deep dives

**VRT.** The single most compelling data point: a $15B backlog growing 109% YoY with book-to-bill >1 for eight consecutive quarters and Q4 2025 organic orders +252% YoY — the stock fell 10.4% on a Q2 where management raised guidance on every metric, which is the definition of a price event, not a business event. AI is the moat's demand engine (liquid cooling content-per-rack rises with GPU density), not a disruption vector. 12-18mo: base ~+38% to the $338 analyst mean on backlog conversion; bull ~+53% to Evercore's $375 if Q3 lands clean and the AI-capex tape unwinds.

**BR.** The strongest mean-reversion data point in the whole dossier: forward P/E ~14x vs a 3-yr average 33.6x — at its historical multiple the stock would be $319 (+113%) — while the numbers never broke (recurring revenue +8% CC, adj EPS +12%, record $305M closed sales, FY27 guidance 6–8% revenue / 8–12% EPS growth). Both selloff narratives are rebutted on the record: proxy distribution is legally mandated and SEC-regulated (can't be in-sourced or AI'd away cheaply), and the SEC affirmed tokenized securities carry the same governance obligations — flowing through broker-dealers that are already BR clients. 12-18mo: base ~+31–40% as narrative repair re-rates a half-price compounder; bull toward $213–$319 if tokenization (DLR at $360B June volume, DLX rollout) reframes BR as infrastructure for the tokenized market rather than its victim.

**IDXX.** Four straight EPS beats (avg surprise +5.8%) and two full-year guidance raises in 2026, gross margin 64%, operating margin 35% (+140 bps YoY) — yet the stock is down ~24% YTD on soft US vet-visit optics (same-store visits −1.3% in Q2). Forward P/E 30.7x vs a 3-yr average 46.3x and 5-yr 48.9x: the multiple compressed while earnings quality (accrual ratio −0.068, CFO/NI 1.20) stayed pristine. Moat is near-max: installed analyzers → recurring consumables is razor-and-blade with real clinic switching costs; AI assists interpretation but can't replace wet-lab infrastructure. 12-18mo: base ~+36% to the $699 analyst mean on visit stabilization; bull ~+50–60% if Q3 (Nov 2) delivers a third raise and the multiple starts repairing toward 40x+.
