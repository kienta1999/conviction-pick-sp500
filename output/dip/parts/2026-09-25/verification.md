### 4. Moat: razor-and-blade installed-base lock-in; ~75% US share; AI risk near-zero — MOSTLY CONFIRMED, ONE FIGURE STALE/OVERSTATED
- Razor-and-blade lock-in: CONFIRMED. ainvest (May 2026): IDEXX "leverag[es] a
  razor-and-blade business model... and a robust installed base of diagnostic
  instruments"; 75,000+ Catalyst analyzers installed globally; consumables +

  evidence — PARTIALLY CONTRADICTED.** The 70%-plus figure traces to the FTC's 2013

  Aug 2025, cited twice). The dossier's "~75% US share of companion-animal
  diagnostics" overstates the current position; the defensible claim is dominant

- AI/substitute disruption near-zero: CONFIRMED in direction. Coverage frames AI as
  embedded in IDEXX's own platforms (inVue Dx is an AI-powered cytology analyzer;
  1,602 placements in Q2 2026) rather than a substitute threat; no credible
  replacement for physical assays/wet-lab infrastructure identified in any coverage.
