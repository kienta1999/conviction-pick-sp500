- Top pick: IDXX
- Runner-up: CPRT
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): IDEXX is a razor-and-blade diagnostics franchise repriced like a broken growth story while the ledger kept compounding — Q1 and Q2 2026 both beat with revenue +14% and +11% organic diagnostics growth, guidance was raised twice this year, gross margin sits at 64% and operating margin at 35% (+140 bps YoY). - Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence is the sequence — four straight quarterly EPS beats (avg surprise +5.8%) and two guidance raises in 2026 while the stock fell 33% off its high. A permanently impaired business cuts guidance; IDEXX raised it twice and expanded operating margins. The dip is about price, not the business.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: low risk. In-clinic analyzer installed base drives recurring consumables and reference-lab services with real switching costs (clinics re-tooling workflows); proprietary diagnostic data and practice-management software deepen lock-in. AI cannot replace physical lab assays or wet-lab infrastructure — it only improves test interpretation, which IDEXX itself embeds (Cancer Dx, inVue Dx). The "AI disruption" narrative that haunts BKNG and ADBE has essentially no purchase here.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: Q3 earnings on 2026-11-02 is the next validation gate; continued guidance raises plus international commercial expansion (four countries in H2 2026) and Cancer Dx / inVue Dx ramp. If US wellness-visit trends bottom, multiple repair runs through Q4 2026.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base — re-rating to ~37–40x forward (still well below its 46–49x 3/5-yr averages) with high-single-digit earnings growth gives ~$640–700, roughly +25–36% (analyst mean target $698.92, +36%); bull — visits stabilize, FY2027 guidance re-accelerates, multiple recovers toward ~44x, ~$750–800 (+45–55%). There is no leverage or dilution overhang to complicate the path.
- Top risk (value-trap case): US clinical-visit softness deepens from cyclical to structural — pet owners permanently trading down on vet care, wellness visits declining year after year, and organic growth slipping to low single digits. At ~30x forward P/E the multiple is only cheap relative to its own history; on 4–5% growth it is still dear, and the de-rating resumes.
