## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: IDXX
- Runner-up: BR
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): IDEXX is the cleanest "great business bought on sale" in the field — a razor-and-blade vet-diagnostics compounder with 64% gross and 35% operating margins, ROE of 74%, and net debt/EBITDA of 0.55x, whose stock fell 33% purely on multiple compression (trailing P/E from 51.3 at end-2025 to ~36–38 now). The growth story is unbroken: four straight EPS beats (+5.8% avg surprise), two guidance raises in 2026, Q1 revenue +14% YoY with 11% organic growth, and recurring diagnostics revenue +11%. It's trading at ~30.7x forward earnings vs its own 3–5 year history of 46–49x — a 30–37% discount on a franchise that has historically compounded mid-teens. Mean analyst target is $698.92 (+36%) and the business is accelerating, not stalling.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence is the earnings ledger — EPS beats in each of the last 4 quarters, guidance raised twice in 2026, record Q2 gross margin (64.0%) and operating margin (35.0%, +140 bps YoY) while the stock is down ~24% YTD. A permanent break would show up as margin erosion or guide-downs; instead pricing power ("net price realization" cited as a margin driver) and cash conversion (accrual ratio −0.068, CFO/NI 1.20) are intact. The −33% is a growth-stock de-rating on soft US vet-visit optics (~1.3% decline in Q2, assumed ~1.5% in H2) and healthcare-sector rotation — price, not the business.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: LOW risk. Razor-and-blade installed base (in-clinic analyzers → recurring consumables + reference labs), practice-management software, proprietary diagnostic data, real vet switching costs. AI can assist test interpretation but cannot replace physical lab assays, wet-lab infrastructure, or instruments — and IDEXX itself is embedding AI in its analyzers (Cancer Dx, inVue Dx ramp). This is the most AI-proof business in the dossier.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: Q3 earnings on 2026-11-02 as validation of a third beat-and-raise; stabilization/bottoming of US wellness-visit trends through Q4 2026; commercial expansion into four international countries in H2 2026; Cancer Dx / inVue Dx new-product ramp. Multiple repair expected November 2026 → Q1 2027 if the print confirms.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base — forward multiple re-rates from ~31x toward ~38–40x on sustained high-single/low-teens organic growth, plus ~12–15% EPS growth → roughly +30–40% total (in line with the $698.92 mean target, +36%). Bull — vet-visit trends stabilize, guidance keeps rising, multiple recovers toward ~45x its historical range → +55–70%.
- Top risk (value-trap case): US clinical-visit weakness deepens from cyclical softness to a structural trade-down in pet care; organic growth slips to low single digits while management keeps guiding optimistically; the de-rating resumes from here and even 30x forward proves too dear. Healthcare high-beta rotation persisting independently of fundamentals is the secondary drag.
- Conviction (1-10): 8

### Top 3 — the single most compelling temporary-dip + intact-moat point

**1. IDXX.** Four straight EPS beats and two 2026 guidance raises against a −33% drawdown is the signature of a sentiment de-rating, not a broken story — and the moat is near-maximal (installed analyzers, reference labs, vet switching costs, AI physically cannot replace lab assays). Base ~+30–40% (multiple 31x→38–40x + EPS growth); bull +55–70% toward historical ~45x if visit trends stabilize.

**2. BR.** This is arguably the most irrational price in the dossier: forward P/E ~14x vs a 3-yr average of 33.6x (AlphaSpread: $319.53 at the 3-yr average, +113%), while the business just printed Q4 FY2026 recurring revenue +8% CC, adjusted EPS +12%, record $305M closed sales, FY2027 guidance of 6–8% recurring growth and 8–12% EPS growth, FCF at 110% of adjusted earnings, a 12% dividend raise (20th consecutive), and $1.5B buyback authorization. The dip is AI/tokenization fear that its own earnings calls have already rebutted — SEC affirmed tokenized securities carry the same governance obligations, and BR is turning tokenization into a growth leg (DLR processed $360B in tokenized repo in June 2026). Base ~+30–40% (mean target $213.38 = +31% is conservative against history); bull +60–100% if multiple recovers halfway toward history.

**3. VRSK.** Forward P/E nearly halved from its 5-yr average (~34.95x → 19.4x) for a 45%-operating-margin analytics franchise whose core subscription engine (83% of revenue) still grows 8% organic with stated price realization — the drag is weather comps, a one-off federal contract stoppage, and P&C premium-normalization anxiety, all cyclical. Data moat is intact (proprietary actuarial datasets embedded industry-wide; AI is an accelerant — XactAI at 7,000 licensees). Base ~+30–40% (analyst upside +39%); bull +50–60% if the premium cycle stabilizes and Q3 beats on 10/28.
