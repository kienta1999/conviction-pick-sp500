## 1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Copart is the closest thing in the field to an uncopyable moat at a distressed price. A record FY2026 revenue print ($4.7B) proves the franchise is growing; only the multiple collapsed.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business: Q4 FY2026 (Sep 10, 2026) delivered record full-year revenue $4.7B with Q4 revenue beating estimates, yet the stock trades ~9.5% above its 52-week low — total-loss frequency and insurance auction volumes run in cycles, and rising repair costs historically push total-loss rates back up; the earnings-quality flag (RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN) was adjudicated benign (advance charges reimbursed at sale, CFO/NI 1.09), so there is no hidden deterioration — this is a cyclical trough priced as structural decline.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: LOW risk — and why: AI is an input, not a substitute. Management is deploying AI inside operations; no software entrant can replicate fenced acreage, insurer relationships, or auction liquidity, and Seeking Alpha (Sep 2026) describes a "wide moat via land ownership, network effects, and dominant US/international salvage auction operations." The ACV deal adds inspections/valuation/pricing data, deepening rather than diluting the data moat.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: ACV tender expected to close by calendar year-end 2026 (Barrington upgraded to Outperform, $40 target, on the deal); insurance-volume normalization as repair-cost inflation restores total-loss frequency; margin stabilization into FY2028 when ACV turns EPS-accretive. Near-term proof point: Q1 FY2027 print ~Nov 19, 2026.
- 12-18 month return scenario: base +35–40% to the $38.70 mean analyst target as volumes normalize and the multiple re-rates from ~16x toward its historical premium; bull +70–85% to $48–52 if ACV integrates cleanly, margins recover, and the cycle turns — the classic cyclical-compounder double (earnings growth + multiple expansion).
- Top risk (value-trap case): ADAS and vehicle-safety tech structurally reduce collision severity so total-loss frequency never mean-reverts, leaving revenue stagnant while land/AI capex keeps compressing margins — and the $1.9B ACV acquisition turns from growth vector into albatross (antitrust review could also delay/derail closing). In that world the "cheap" multiple is a rational repricing of a permanently slower business.
