- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Stryker is a category-dominant medtech franchise — implanted devices, surgical equipment and the Mako installed base create surgeon-training and hospital switching costs no competitor, and no AI model, can replicate — trading at a decade-low 16.6x forward multiple because of a fixable supply problem, not a demand problem. A moat-8/10 business does not lose its surgeons, its installed base or its procedure volumes because one acquired product line had a supply failure. At $277.33 against a $364.23 consensus target, the market is pricing an operational hiccup as franchise damage.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** Demand, margins and guidance all remained intact through the drawdown — the fall was caused by a transitory operational/disclosure event (Inari supply), and the Oct 29 print is a near, binary-ish catalyst on exactly the one problem that caused the fall.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Stryker sells physical implants and capital equipment embedded in operating rooms and surgeon muscle memory; AI assists surgery, it does not substitute for the implant, the robot or the rep in the room. There is no software layer here for a model to commoditize.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Oct 29 earnings — evidence the Inari supply issue is fixed, with guidance reaffirmed; re-rating off a decade-low multiple can follow immediately.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+31% (to the $364.23 target) on the Inari fix and multiple normalisation; bull ~+45-50% if supply fully resolves and the multiple re-rates toward its historical premium.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The Inari problem proves structural rather than a one-off supply failure, the disclosure-credibility discount sticks, and a "cheap" multiple stays cheap while growth is quietly rebased lower.
