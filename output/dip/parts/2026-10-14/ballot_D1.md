## Single-pick ballot
- **Top pick:** HSY
- **Runner-up:** SYK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Hershey is the one name in this field where the impairment is provably outside the business: a #1 US chocolate franchise (~45% share) whose earnings were crushed by a cocoa spike that has already reversed ~70%. Nothing in the dossier shows share loss, a guidance-driven demand collapse, leverage stress, or a broken model — the margin damage is an input-cost cycle, and the recovery is mechanically scheduled as cheaper cocoa flows through. At 16.6x forward, down 29.6%, you are not paying for the recovery in advance. The upside is less explosive than VRT or BKNG on paper (+24.0% to the $203.14 target), but as the veto lens I rank certainty that the business is intact above headline upside.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The cause is a commodity spike that has already reversed ~70% — the earnings hit came from cocoa costs, not from losing the franchise, the shelf, or the customer; a cost cycle that has already turned cannot keep impairing margins the way structural share loss does.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — a ~45%-share physical consumer franchise with brands, shelf placement and manufacturing scale; AI does not substitute for chocolate, and no AI-driven disintermediation channel exists here. The real substitution threat is pharmacological (GLP-1), not technological, and it is a slow headwind, not a cliff in the dossier evidence.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** The Oct 29 print, where the cocoa-driven margin recovery should begin showing mechanically, with the fuller recovery scheduled into FY27 as lower-cost cocoa works through the P&L.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+15-20% as margins normalise partway toward the $203.14 analyst target (+24.0%); bull ~+24-30% if the Oct 29 print and FY27 guide confirm full margin recovery and the multiple re-rates off 16.6x.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Volume fell -8% on +12% pricing — if that elasticity is actually permanent demand destruction compounded by GLP-1 adoption, margins recover into a shrinking volume base and 16.6x is fair, not cheap.
