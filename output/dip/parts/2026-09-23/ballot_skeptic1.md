## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** IDXX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the name in this field where the impairment evidence is thinnest and the price dislocation is most provable: five consecutive quarterly beats, Q2 2026 revenue +18.5% with EPS $2.80 vs $2.51 consensus — and the stock fell 10.8–11.6% after hours anyway, on guidance of "only" 13.5–15.5% procedure growth. The dip's stated causes are a tariff margin headwind (~1%), a Class II component recall, and ACA/GLP-1 mix effects on US bariatric procedures — all transient or mix-driven, none touching the installed base of 11,106 da Vinci systems, 84% recurring revenue, or a net-cash balance sheet. The da Vinci 5 upgrade cycle (303 of 532 Q4-2025 placements) plus 20% international procedure growth give the rebound a dated, mechanical engine. At 33.2x forward vs its own 60–95x history, this is a premium compounder repriced by a growth scare, not a broken franchise.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The numbers directly contradict the impairment thesis — five straight beats while the stock was sold on each, and the "slowdown" is still 13.5–15.5% procedure growth with international at 20%; a permanently impaired business does not beat five quarters running on a net-cash balance sheet.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** low risk (9/10) — AI can only enhance surgical robotics (guidance, planning); it cannot substitute a robot in the OR. Surgeon training lock-in, regulatory clearances, IP thicket. New competitors are "finally arriving after 20+ years" with no documented share loss.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** da Vinci 5 full rollout (higher ASP + upgrade cycle); procedure-growth re-acceleration as ACA/GLP-1 bariatric mix laps; tariff resolution; Q3 earnings ~10/20/26 as the dated re-rating event.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+25–30% (procedure growth re-accelerates to mid-teens, multiple repairs toward the low-40s on the da Vinci 5 cycle); bull ~+45–55% (tariff headwind resolves, international strength persists, multiple back toward 50x on 15%+ sustained growth).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** procedure growth permanently settles at low-teens (maturation, not a scare), competition compresses system pricing over time, and GLP-1 structurally shrinks the bariatric TAM — a slow de-rating to an industrial multiple on a no-longer-premium grower.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
