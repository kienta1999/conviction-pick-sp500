# Panelist B — growth/quality compounder — 2026-09-23

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** IDXX
- **Runner-up:** BKNG
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** IDEXX is the highest-quality compounder in the field: 35% operating margins, a razor/consumables installed base with recurring CAG diagnostics revenue (+14.1% yoy), and 9–11% organic growth that has genuinely re-accelerated across the last two quarters — with management raising the full-year outlook twice in 2026. Net debt is 0.55x EBITDA with 41x interest coverage, so there is no survival question — only a sentiment discount on a compounder that is still compounding, now sitting at the low end of its own historical multiple range.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-04): revenue +10% yoy to $1.22B, EPS $4.27 vs ~$3.94 consensus, organic growth back to 9% after 11% in Q1, and the full-year outlook raised for the second time this year. The business accelerated while the price stayed anchored to the February scare — this is price, not a broken business.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** low risk. Installed-base analyzer moat (Catalyst platform, inVue Dx, Cancer Dx) with workflow-integrated consumables and a reference-lab network — high switching costs. AI can assist interpretation but cannot replace in-clinic lab hardware. Irreplaceability 8/10 in the dossier.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 print 2026-11-02 — a third raise would force the re-rating; margin inflection already underway (Q2 operating margin 35.0% vs 33.6% a year earlier); new-product pull-through from Cancer Dx and Catalyst expansions.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+25% (multiple back toward the mid-30s on the raised trajectory, roughly in line with the analyst mean target implying 35.6% upside); bull ~+45–50% (third raise at Q3, U.S. vet-visit stabilization, multiple drifting back toward its historical 40x).
