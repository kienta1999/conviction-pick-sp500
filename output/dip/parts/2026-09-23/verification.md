## IDXX (Idexx Laboratories)

**Claim 1 — Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-04): revenue +10% yoy to ~$1.22B.**
CONFIRMED. Company press release (ir.idexx.com, 2026-08-04): "revenues of $1,217 million for the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 10% as reported and 9% organic."

**Claim 2 — Q2 EPS ~$4.27 vs ~$3.94 consensus.**
CONFIRMED. Company press release (ir.idexx.com, 2026-08-04): "Earnings per diluted share were $4.27, an increase of 18% as reported and 15% on a comparable basis." Consensus: MarketBeat recorded $4.27 vs $3.94 consensus (marketbeat.com); Zacks recorded $4.27 vs $3.95 consensus (zacks.com, 2026-08-04).

**Claim 3 — Organic growth back to ~9% (11% in Q1).**
CONFIRMED. Official Q2: 9% organic (ir.idexx.com, 2026-08-04). Official Q1 2026: 11% organic (ir.idexx.com 8-K, 2026-05-05; d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net Q1 snapshot).

**Claim 4 — Management raised the full-year outlook for the second time in 2026.**
CONFIRMED. Initial 2026 guide (2026-02-02): EPS $14.29–$14.80. First raise, Q1 print (2026-05-05): EPS raised to $14.45–$14.90 ("an increase of $0.13 compared to prior guidance midpoint"). Second raise, Q2 print (2026-08-04): EPS raised to $14.69–$14.94 ("an increase of $0.14 compared to prior guidance midpoint"); revenue outlook raised to $4,700M–$4,745M (ir.idexx.com, 2026-08-04). TIKR (Aug 2026) headline: "Guidance Raised Again."

**Claim 5 — Q4 2025 print (Feb 2026): EPS $2.92 missing by a penny.**
CONFIRMED with caveat. Multiple secondary aggregators report Q4 2025 EPS of $2.92 missing the $2.93 consensus by $0.01 on revenue of $1.09B (+14.3% yoy) (americanbankingnews.com, 2026-02-22/23; defenseworld.net; thestockobserver.com). Caveat: the company's official headline figure was EPS of $3.08, which beat consensus — the $2.92 is the non-GAAP "comparable EPS" after share-based-compensation, litigation and currency adjustments, per the official reconciliation (files.quartr.com, 2026-02-02). So the "penny miss" reading is aggregator-basis, not headline-basis.

**Claim 6 — Initial 2026 organic growth guided ~8% at midpoint.**
CONFIRMED. Initial 2026 outlook (2026-02-02): revenue $4,632M–$4,720M, "growth of 7.6%–9.6% reported and 7.0%–9.0% organic" — midpoint 8.0% (files.quartr.com; fool.com earnings call transcript, 2026-02-02).

## ISRG (Intuitive Surgical)

**Claim 1 — Net-cash balance sheet (negative net debt/EBITDA).**
CONFIRMED. Q2 2026 press release: company "ended the second quarter of 2026 with $8.63 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and investments" (files.quartr.com, 2026-07-16); no debt reported — described as "no debt" / "virtually no debt" with "$9B+ net cash" (panabee.com, 2026; momoview.com, 2026).

**Claim 2 — ~84% recurring revenue.**
CONFIRMED. Recurring revenue (instruments, accessories, service, some lease income) was 84% of 2025 sales (ts2.tech, 2026-09; panabee.com, 2026; compoundwithrene.com, 2026-09). Q2 2026 recurring revenue was $2.47B of $2.89B ≈ 85% (arabigin.com / barchart.com, 2026-07).

**Claim 3 — Dip causes: ~1% tariff headwind.**
CONFIRMED. Official Q2 2026 outlook: "Non-GAAP gross profit margin to be within a range of 68.0% to 69.0% of revenue in 2026. This range includes an estimated impact from tariffs of 1.0% of revenue" (files.quartr.com, 2026-07-16; stocktitan.net; massdevice.com, 2026-07-16).

**Claim 4 — Dip causes: a Class II recall.**
CONFIRMED. Intuitive voluntarily initiated a Class II recall in April 2026 of certain da Vinci components — an E-brake retainer pin issue affecting 454 identified units, which "could impede emergency braking or cause uncontrolled column motion" (en.cryptonomist.ch, 2026-07-17; simplywall.st, 2026-07, citing company announcement). Multiple Class II recall records across da Vinci systems/instruments also appear in FDA enforcement reports (simplywall.st, 2026-07).

**Claim 5 — Dip causes: bariatric procedure-mix headwinds, described as cyclical/temporary rather than share loss.**
CONFIRMED (facts); characterization is panel interpretation. Q2 2026 (reported 2026-07-16): adjusted EPS $2.80 vs $2.51 consensus, revenue $2.89B beat, but U.S. da Vinci procedure growth decelerated to 12% from 14% in Q1. CEO Dave Rosa attributed it to the expiration of enhanced ACA subsidies ("some patients may be postponing deferrable operations because of changes in insurance coverage and premiums") plus GLP-1 pressure on bariatric volumes (finance.biggo.com, 2026-07; ainvest.com, 2026-08/09). Full-year da Vinci procedure growth held at 13.5%–15.5%, "closer to the midpoint" (files.quartr.com, 2026-07-16). No documented da Vinci share loss found; competitors are arriving (J&J Ottava FDA-cleared summer 2026 — compoundwithrene.com, 2026-09; simplify.jobs) but no source evidences actual share erosion. The "temporary not structural" framing is consistent with the cited causes being policy/timing events, but remains the panel's judgment, not a company statement.

## VRT (Vertiv)

**Claim 1 — Q2 2026: revenue +24% yoy to ~$3.27B, missed ~$3.38B consensus.**
CONFIRMED. Reported 2026-07-29: net sales $3,274.3M, up 24.1% yoy (official non-GAAP reconciliation, cdn.prod.nntech.io; marketbeat.com earnings transcript). Zacks (2026-07-30): "Net sales increased 24.1% year over year to $3.27 billion but missed the consensus estimate by 3.41%" — implying consensus ≈ $3.39B, consistent with the dossier's ~$3.38B. Ainvest (2026-09): "came in below the roughly $3.39 billion Wall Street had modeled."

**Claim 2 — Miss attributed to timing shifts in large multiphase project execution + temporary supply-chain congestion.**
CONFIRMED. Zacks (2026-07-30): "Management said that minor timing shifts tied to temporary supply-chain congestion and multi-phased project execution affected second-quarter revenues, but expects the associated delays to resolve in the second half of 2026." Ainvest (2026-09) similarly: management "blamed supply-chain congestion and a 'learning curve' on bigger, more complex project rollouts."

**Claim 3 — Q2 adjusted operating margin ~22.6% (+410bps yoy).**
CONFIRMED. Official Q2 2026 non-GAAP reconciliation: "Adjusted operating margin 22.6%" vs 18.5% in Q2 2025 — exactly +410bps (cdn.prod.nntech.io presentation, 2026-07-29; github.com/winechord 10-Q/8-K filing notes, 2026-07-30).

**Claim 4 — Q3 2026 guidance: sales $3.65–3.85B, adjusted EPS $1.77–1.83.**
CONFIRMED. "For Q3 2026, Vertiv projects net sales of $3,650–$3,850 million… and adjusted diluted EPS of $1.77–$1.83" (stocktitan.net 8-K filing summary). The guidance was issued alongside the Q2 report (2026-07-29).
