## BKNG — Booking Holdings (OTA/travel platform)

**Screen:** dist_sma200 −8.4%, dist_52w_high −23.7%, fwdPE 13.6, TTM rev growth 8.4%, op margin 34.4%, analyst upside 41.7%.

**1. (c) EU General Court upheld the block of the €1.63B eTraveli acquisition on Sept 9, 2026 — stock fell ~4–5% that day (barchart/StockStory, 2026-09-09). The business kept executing through all of it.

**2. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — 7/10.** Merchant-of-record model, ~mid-fifties % direct bookings, Genius loyalty (L2/L3 = high-50% of room nights, 38% alternative-accommodation mix, Q1 2026 per alphastreet). The AI threat is real (JPMorgan's cut), but Booking's counter-evidence is concrete: "Penny" conversational booking at Priceline showed a "noticeable uplift" in engagement; Agoda cut customer-service cost per booking at a double-digit rate via AI; Fogel calls AI "an absolute positive" (Q1 2026). Medium risk: AI travel agents could commoditize comparison shopping, but inventory depth, payments, and loyalty are hard to replicate.

**3. Rebound catalyst.** Strait of Hormuz reopening (Tikr, Sept 2026: "the headwind that broke the stock is easing"; stock rallied 7% June 24 on repricing). Fogel at the J.P. Morgan TMT conference (May 20, 2026): in 2020, the worst travel year on record, Booking still earned ~$900M EBITDA because marketing spend is variable — "the business does not bleed when travel stalls and snaps back fast." Transformation program savings raised to $650M run-rate, mostly in 2027 (Q2 call). Connected Trip transactions growing low-double digits, 2x+ total transaction growth. eTraveli block removes some flight-platform optionality.

**4. Balance-sheet survival.** Bulletproof. Cash & investments $17.7B at June 30, 2026 (CFO Ewout Steenbergen, Q2 call Aug 3/4, 2026); gross debt ~$20.2B; Q2 FCF $3.6B (+16%), TTM FCF ~$9.5B (~32.6% FCF margin, ~6% FCF yield, ROIC 67.9% per ainvest); Q2 buybacks $3.7B (record, ~102% of FCF), $14.5B remaining authorization; H1 buybacks $7.4B at avg ~$173/share. Raised $3B debt, repaid $1B in Q2. No distress scenario.

**5. Margin of safety.** fwdPE 13.6 (screen) vs its own history of mid-teens to 20s — genuinely cheap because of the dip. Trailing P/E 18.18, price $164.22 on 9/22/26, 52-wk range $150.14–$225.00, street target $236.98 (marketbeat, 2026-09-22). ~44% to street target.

**6. Category position.** Clear #1 global OTA; 325M room nights in Q2 (+5%), gross bookings $51B (+9%), revenue $7.4B (+8%). No share loss — the dip is demand-shock, not share-shock.

**7. Value-trap risk.** The honest bear: OpenAI/Claude travel agents genuinely route around OTAs and Google's marketing toll is the real vulnerability; repeated Middle East exposure keeps recurring; and the eTraveli block shows regulators will cap inorganic optionality.

**8. Rebound score: 8/10.** War-shock dislocation on a cash machine buying back ~6% of itself annually with a variable cost base — the cleanest transitory-dip setup in the batch.
