## LRCX

### 1. Why-it's-down cause — record fiscal Q1 2027 (2026-10-21), supply-capped December guide, ~$300–400m deferred
Lam's own Q1 FY2027 press release (2026-10-21) and the earnings-call transcript (Motley Fool, 2026-10-21) confirm: revenue $8.22bn (+54% YoY, +22% QoQ), non-GAAP EPS $2.20 vs ~$2.18 consensus, December-quarter guide revenue $8.60bn ± $400m and non-GAAP EPS $2.33 ± $0.15. CFO Doug Bettinger said on the call the guide "would have been a bit higher if not for some near-term supply chain constraints" (clean-room space, components). Multiple reports (SF Inquirer, 2026-10-24; AI Stock Wire, 2026-10-23) confirm Bettinger said shipments that would have gone in December will ship in the March quarter — but he **declined to confirm** an analyst's $300–400m figure when asked. So the $300–400m is an analyst estimate, not a company-stated number. Precision correction: GAAP operating margin was exactly 40.0%; it was the **non-GAAP** operating margin of 40.2% that was the first above 40% (Lam press release via FX Broker Trust, 2026-10-22). The dossier's "40.0%, first time above 40%" conflates the two.
**Verdict: CONFIRMED — Lam Research Q1 FY2027 press release / earnings-call transcript, 2026-10-21 (with the two corrections above: $300–400m is an unconfirmed analyst estimate, and the first-above-40% margin was non-GAAP 40.2%, not GAAP 40.0%)**

## Overall verdict

The winner's and top-3's headline evidence stands: all three names reported genuine beats followed by guidance- or multiple-driven sell-offs rather than misses — Lam's record Q1 FY2027 with a supply-capped guide, KLA's Q1 FY2027 beat-and-raise followed by a post-print slide with no downgrades, and Netflix's Q3 EPS beat undone by a Q4 guide miss and a $300m ad-forecast cut — and the valuation anchors (LRCX ~19–23x forward with a ~$364 mean target; KLAC ~22–27x forward with a ~$219–221 mean target; NFLX ~17–19x forward vs a 37.8x five-year average with an ~$85–89 mean target) and moat rankings (#1 etch / #2 deposition for Lam; >50% process-control share and a 90.0% SPC revenue share for KLA; >325m subscribers for Netflix) all check out against independent sources. Three corrections are required before publishing: (1) NFLX's Q4 print date — use the January 19, 2027 estimate (MarketBeat/Zacks), not January 26, which contradicts the dossier's own table and is only one aggregator's estimate; (2) KLAC's "~18x historical average" P/E — either define the window and basis (it resembles the 2021–2023 trailing average, not a forward average) or drop it, since published 3Y/5Y trailing averages are 36–40x; and (3) two precision fixes — Lam's $300–400m deferred shipments is an analyst estimate Bettinger declined to confirm (the deferral to the March quarter is company-confirmed), the first-above-40% operating margin was non-GAAP 40.2% (GAAP was exactly 40.0%), and Netflix's current forward/trailing P/E reads ~16.8–18.7x / ~18–19x rather than 16.5x / 17–18x. None of these changes the ranking case, but all three should be fixed in the memo before it ships.
