## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** KLAC
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam is the cleanest "multiple compressed, estimates intact" case in the dossier: fiscal Q1 2027 was a record — revenue $8.22bn (+54% YoY) and operating margin 40.0%, the first time above 40% — yet the stock fell 11.76% on Oct 22 because the Dec-quarter guide merely met consensus. At the batch-verified Nov 4 close of $221.43, LRCX is 44.8% below its 52-week high and 15.8% below its 200-day SMA, at ~19.5x forward earnings against an analyst mean target of ~$364 (+52.2%). The dip is a supply constraint, not a demand failure: management said the guide would have been higher but for near-term supply-chain constraints, so ~$300–400m of shipments slip to the March quarter rather than disappearing. AI makes Lam more, not less, necessary — more layers, GAA and advanced packaging/HBM all raise etch and deposition intensity.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the Dec-quarter guide was supply-capped, not demand-capped — the CFO explicitly said it would have been higher without clean-room/component constraints, with no downgrades and only trimmed targets (Mizuho $365→$360), while the Customer Support Business Group grew 40% YoY to $2.49bn. A demand collapse does not produce a record quarter plus a deferred-shipment guide.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Lam is #1 in etch and #2 in deposition, with tools qualified fab-by-fab over years — a certification and process-knowledge moat no model can copy and no substitute technology replaces, since AI workloads increase the etch/deposition steps per wafer rather than bypassing them. The disruption vector is cyclical (a hyperscaler/neocloud capex pause rolling into WFE with a lag), not AI substitution.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Deferred Dec-quarter shipments landing in the March quarter, plus a calendar-2027 WFE view above $200bn (the first >$200bn print); the next hard test is the late-January 2027 print, with the March-quarter delivery as the confirmation.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~35–45% (toward ~$300–320) as deferred shipments convert and the multiple normalises part-way toward the analyst mean; bull ~55–65% (toward the ~$364 mean target) if CY2027 WFE clears $200bn and supply constraints ease on schedule.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The AI-financing chain breaks — hyperscaler/neocloud capex pauses, WFE orders roll over with a lag, and the >$200bn 2027 WFE view proves a pull-forward peak; China is 35% of revenue, adding export-control exposure. If that happens, a 19.5x multiple on falling estimates is not cheap.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
