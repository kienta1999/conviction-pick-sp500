## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** KLAC
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam fell 44.8% from its 52-week high to a verified $221.43 close on Nov 4, yet it just printed a record quarter — revenue $8.22bn (+54% YoY) and a first-ever 40.0% operating margin — with no downgrades and even the low analyst target ($255) above the price. The December-quarter guide merely met consensus because supply-chain constraints (clean-room space, components) capped shipments, not because demand faded. That is a deferral, not a loss: roughly $300–400m of shipments slip to the March quarter, while calendar-2027 WFE is viewed above $200bn for the first time. With net cash of ~$3.7bn, record quarterly FCF of $3.24bn, and a forward P/E of ~19.5x, this is multiple compression on intact estimates — the cleanest temporary dip in the field.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the guide shortfall was explicitly supply-capped — the CFO said it "would have been a bit higher if not for some near-term supply chain constraints" — on top of a record quarter, with no customer cancellations, no downgrade, and no earnings miss.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — #1 in etch, #2 in deposition, tools qualified fab-by-fab over years; AI increases etch/deposition intensity (more layers, GAA, advanced packaging, HBM), so AI is a demand driver, not a substitute. Irreplaceability 9/10 in the dossier.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** The deferred $300–400m ships in the March quarter and the late-January 2027 print confirms the WFE >$200bn view; a clean print then re-rates the compressed multiple, with the Customer Support Business Group ($2.49bn, +40% YoY) as a steadier second leg.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +35–50% (to roughly $300–330) if deferred shipments land and WFE holds above $200bn; bull +65–80% (to roughly $365–400, in line with the ~$361–367 analyst mean) if 2027 WFE beats and supply constraints clear early.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The AI-financing chain — if hyperscaler/neocloud capex pauses, WFE orders roll over with a lag and a >$200bn 2027 turns out to be a pull-forward peak; China is 35% of revenue, adding export-control exposure.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
