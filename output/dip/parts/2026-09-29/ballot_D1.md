## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** IDXX
- **Runner-up:** CBOE
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** This is the textbook sentiment derating, not a broken business: Idexx has posted four straight earnings beats while the stock fell double digits — Q2 8/4/26 was a beat AND a raised FY26 guide ($14.69–14.94 EPS), yet the shares fell on soft vet-visit chatter and a high-beta rotation. The business is a fortress: D/E 0.19, 25% net margins, ROE 72%, and a razor/blade vet-diagnostics installed base with 80%+ key-segment share locked in by retraining/recalibration/software switching costs. The bear case is "35x is expensive and vet visits are soft" — a valuation and cycle argument, not an impairment argument. I can find no structural mechanism by which this business shrinks: AI cannot replace a physical assay, and pricing power remains intact.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence: management raised guidance on 8/4/26 and the stock fell anyway, and it has beaten consensus on every print while falling. When the numbers go up and the price goes down four quarters running, the drop is about positioning and multiple compression, not the business.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Vet diagnostics are physical blood/urine assays run on proprietary analyzers with embedded software; 80%+ key-segment share and real switching costs. An LLM or model does not run a CBC. This is the most physically AI-proof business in the field alongside CBOE/CPRT.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings on 11/2/26 against a lowered sentiment bar — a fifth straight beat-and-raise forces multiple re-rate attention; plus the inVue Dx rollout (>1,900 placements) and Cancer Dx ramp compounding instrument placements through FY27.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +30–35% (consensus target implies +32–45% recapture as the de-rating reverses toward its 5-yr ~35x average, which it already sits at, so upside is earnings-driven); bull +45–50% if sentiment swings back to a premium multiple on continued beat-and-raise cadence.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Secular vet-visit softness proves structural rather than cyclical, and growth becomes price-led with eroding volume — at 35x trailing there is no room for a stumble, and a volume miss turns a "cheap dip" into a growth-scare rerating. I judge this unlikely (organic instrument placements and guidance raises contradict it) but it is the one mechanism that could break the thesis.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 9
