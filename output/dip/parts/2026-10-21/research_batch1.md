If AI capex financing (the CoreWeave signal) actually breaks, WFE is the highest-beta way to own that break, and a 22x forward multiple on peak-cycle forward EPS can be a mirage — on TTM GAAP EPS ($8.66) the stock is ~35–47x depending on source basis (sources differ; flagged, not resolved). **2. Trap case: two straight years of receivables + inventory + other assets absorbing >$1.6B/yr while reported EPS compounds is the classic pattern of revenue being pulled forward into a cycle peak — and it coincides with a WFE upcycle top in the stock. The Oct 28 print is the test: if Q1 FY27 CFO/NI recovers >1.0 and receivables flatten, it was timing; a third leg up in receivables on slowing orders would confirm the trap signature.
**One-line verdict on the flag: Mostly benign backlog/timing mechanics so far, but a genuine yellow flag — earnings have run ahead of cash for two consecutive years, so treat it as unproven until the Oct 28 cash-flow statement shows collection catching up.**

**Rebound score: 7/10.** Verdict: The best moat in the batch at a merely *less extreme* (not cheap) multiple, with Oct 28 earnings + cash conversion as a near-term binary that could re-rate it in either direction.
