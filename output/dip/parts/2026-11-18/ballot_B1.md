## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLAC is the highest-quality franchise in the field: a near-monopoly on semiconductor process control with a 42.6% TTM operating margin (the highest at any quarter-end in data back to FQ2 2017, per Trefis), 91.4% ROE, 62.7% gross margin, and a record $1.34B quarterly FCF in Q1 FY27. The business is still compounding — Q1 FY27 revenue was a record $4.14B (+29% YoY) with December-quarter guidance of ~$4.7B, above consensus, and a ~$12.5–12.6B backlog up ~60% YoY. At $144.56, −52% off its 52-week high, the stock has been de-rated from 48.4x to 34.4x trailing earnings on the same earnings, while the forward multiple is 20.3x — a great business bought on sale, not a broken one.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The fall was a sentiment de-rating plus margin timing, not a demand break: the record Q1 beat was followed by analyst target trims citing supply-chain concerns and a delayed return to the 63–64% gross-margin model (now framed as mid-2027/2028), and the Nov 5 leg was pure sector sentiment (SOX −12.2% on midterm data-center-permit fears, with nothing enacted). No lost franchise, no share loss — KLA is capacity-constrained with 12-month lead times and is raising prices on new capabilities.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. KLA dominates inspection and metrology; as nodes shrink and packaging gets more complex, inspection content per wafer rises — you cannot ship a 2nm chip or an HBM stack you cannot inspect. AI increases the need (more layers, more failure modes, tighter yields); there is no AI substitute for physical defect detection, and competition is effectively nil at the leading edge.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** The Dec-quarter print on Jan 28, 2027 ($4.7B revenue guide, +14% QoQ, with foundry/logic ~60% and memory ~40% of process-control systems revenue) is the direct read on AI logic + HBM capex; the 2027 WFE >$200B outlook and the gross-margin recovery toward the 63–64% model (mid-2027/2028) are the follow-on catalysts. Nvidia's Nov 17 print (Q4 guide $130B, memory procurement commitments $390B) already corroborates the demand leg.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35–45% (partial recovery toward the $219.62 analyst mean target, +51.9% upside, on the Dec/Jan prints confirming the backlog converts). Bull ~+55–70% (full recovery toward the old high area if 2027 WFE >$200B materialises and the margin inflection lands).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** China is ~34.5% of Q1 FY27 revenue and fading to the low-20s% in 2027 — tighter BIS rules could cut deeper than the guided mix fade; if memory costs stay elevated, the 63–64% gross-margin model slips past mid-2027/2028; and at 36.2x trailing earnings a WFE disappointment would re-rate the stock hard, as November showed.
