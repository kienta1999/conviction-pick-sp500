## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLAC is the highest-quality franchise in this field and the one whose moat AI strengthens rather than threatens: it holds a near-monopoly on semiconductor process control (inspection/metrology), and inspection content per wafer rises as nodes shrink, packaging gets more complex, and HBM stacks multiply. The stock has halved (−52.0% off its 52-week high, $144.56 at the Nov 17 close) on a record Q1 FY27 — revenue $4.14B (+29% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $1.22 vs ~$1.18–1.19 consensus, record FCF $1.34B — with a December-quarter guide of ~$4.7B that beat consensus. The multiple compressed from 48.4x to 34.4x trailing earnings between end-September and Nov 6 on the same earnings, and a ~$12.5–12.6B backlog (up ~60% YoY per one summary) gives the longest demand visibility in the batch. At 20.3x forward earnings with a 42.6% TTM operating margin, this is a sentiment de-rating of a monopoly, not a broken business.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the quarter was a record with an above-consensus guide and the stock still fell — analysts trimmed targets (Cantor $325 → $225, Stifel $250 → $230, Wells Fargo $245 → $210) citing supply-chain concerns and margin timing, not demand, and the Nov 5 leg (−13.3%) was pure sector sentiment (SOX −12.2%) with no company news. No lost franchise, no share loss: KLA is capacity-constrained with 12-month lead times and is raising prices on new capabilities.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — the lowest in the field. There is no AI substitute for physical defect detection; AI *increases* the need (more layers, more failure modes, tighter yields), and the moat is physics + optics + algorithms + decades of recipe libraries. China cannot easily replicate it: metrology is among the weakest areas for Chinese localisation (~25% domestic share per Yole/DigiTimes reporting). Competition is effectively nil at the leading edge.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** The Dec-quarter print on Jan 28, 2027 ($4.7B revenue guide, foundry/logic ~60% and memory ~40% of process-control systems revenue, DRAM ~84% of memory) is the direct read on AI logic + HBM capex; behind it, 2027 WFE >$200B (company-guided) and gross-margin recovery toward the 63–64% model as memory-cost mitigation lands (mid-2027/2028).
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+50% (toward the $219.62 analyst mean target, +51.9% upside from $144.56, on the guided earnings path with only partial multiple repair). Bull ~+80–110% (toward the ~$301 implied 52-week high if the multiple re-expands toward its September level and the margin inflection lands).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** China was ~34.5% of Q1 FY27 revenue and is guided to fade to the low-20s% in 2027 — tighter BIS rules could cut deeper than the guided mix fade; if memory costs stay elevated, the 63–64% gross-margin model slips past mid-2027/2028 and the margin-inflection catalyst evaporates; and at 36.2x trailing earnings a WFE disappointment re-rates KLA as fast as November did. A long backlog is also a liability if customers cancel or push out in a capex pause.
