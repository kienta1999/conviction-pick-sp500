## BALLOT 1 — Single-pick nomination

- **Top pick:** NFLX
- **Runner-up:** VRT

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Netflix is a −46% off-52w-high derating of the #1 global paid streamer while the business kept pricing power: H1 2026 price increases absorbed in line with past changes, double-digit revenue growth in every region in Q2, and 63% U.S. household penetration at a multi-year high. The multiple has roughly halved (forward P/E ~17.6 vs its own 30–50x history) on an engagement-narrative wobble, and the proving ground is 16 days away: Q3 earnings on Oct 20 (consensus revenue $12.88B +11.9% YoY, EPS $0.82 +39% YoY) followed by the Stranger Things final season in Nov/Dec and the ad tier doubling to ~$3B revenue. A record $4.7B buyback in Q2 with $27.1B authorization remaining is a mechanical bid under the dip. This is the dossier's clearest price-vs-business dislocation: subscribers are not fleeing; only hours are wobbling.

- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence: there is no subscriber flight — the drawdown is entirely about *engagement hours* (estimated viewing of Top 100 originals −21% YoY; Nielsen 7.8% share vs YouTube's 14.2%), yet pricing power is fully intact — two rounds of price increases performed in line with historical absorption, Q2 revenue grew double digits in *every* region, and Evercore's Sep 2026 survey showed U.S. household penetration at a multi-year high. You can't have a broken franchise and record penetration at the same time; the customer base is paying more, not leaving.

- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW disruption risk (dossier 8/10): global #1 paid streamer with 300M+ subs (325M in Q4 2025), $20B content budget scale, recommendation/personalization network effects, and live-sports acceleration (60% of users watched live sports in Sep 2026 vs 42% in March). AI is a cost-deflation tool for Netflix (Ads Suite targeting, content pipelines), not a substitute — and YouTube's creator model cannot replicate the premium scripted catalogue or the ad-inventory moat.

- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 2026 earnings Oct 20, 2026 — a beat with re-accelerating originals punctures the engagement bear case. Then Stranger Things final season (Nov/Dec 2026) and the annual full-year viewership disclosure ~Jan 2027 (Wells Fargo's own checkpoint). 16 days to the first trigger; a full catalyst stack through January.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+30–35% toward the analyst mean target (~$93–97 from ~$70) on a Q3 beat + Q4 slate. Bull: +55–70% toward $110+ if Oct 20 breaks the engagement narrative and ad revenue doubles to ~$3B as guided with the P/E re-rating back toward its own history.

- **Top risk (value-trap case):** If YouTube's attention shift is structural, not cyclical, Netflix loses engagement → pricing power → ad-tier economics atrophy; the $20B+ content treadmill against slower viewing compresses the margins the bull case depends on — and insiders have mostly been selling over the trailing 12 months.

- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
