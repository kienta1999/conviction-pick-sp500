(1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: AMAT
- Runner-up: IDXX
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): AMAT is the broadest materials-engineering franchise in wafer fab equipment — deposition, etch, implant, metrology, CMP and advanced packaging — the physical tools that build the transistor itself, with leadership in gate-all-around and backside power delivery ramping in 2026-27. The stock collapsed from ~49x forward to 21.1x, essentially its 5-year average of 21.18x, while fiscal Q3 2026 delivered record revenue $9.12B (+25% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $3.50 (+41%), 50.4% gross margin and record $3.04B operating cash flow. Guidance was raised to a record $10.25B ±$0.50B / ~$4.02 EPS for Q4, with Semiconductor Systems growth lifted to >30%. At $390.68 vs a $627.83 mean target, you are buying a net-cash, share-gaining picks-and-shovels franchise at a fair multiple on record, still-accelerating earnings — explosive upside if the multiple merely normalises toward peers.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): The single most compelling evidence is that there is no demand or share loss in the print that supposedly caused the fall — revenue, margins, cash flow and guidance all hit records, and the latest leg down on 2026-10-22 came with "no company-specific news," purely as Lam Research read-through on China and supply constraints. The real headwind is bounded and identified: China mix fell to ~28% from ~35% and a ~$600M FY2026 export-control hit was already flagged, while leading-edge foundry/logic and DRAM grow — a geography/policy digestion, not displacement of the core franchise.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: Low risk. AI cannot write software that substitutes for a deposition or etch tool qualified per-fab, per-node, per-recipe — swapping vendors mid-node risks yield, which is why the installed base compounds into Applied Global Services ($1.78B in Q3, +22% YoY, majority recurring subscription). AI is AMAT's demand driver, not its competitor: every AI capacity race must buy its tools. Residual risk is not AI but head-to-head competition from Lam/Tokyo Electron and subsidised Chinese domestic WFE in mature nodes — real, but not the AI-critical leading edge where AMAT claims share gains.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: Fiscal Q4 print on 2026-11-12 against management's own raised bar ($10.25B / ~$4.02, the highest single-quarter revenue in company history), plus any 2027 WFE framework and H2 calendar-2026 WFE acceleration customers have already signalled. An 80-100% FCF payout policy and $12.8B remaining buyback authorisation provide a floor while waiting.
- 12-18 month return scenario: Base ~+35-45% to ~$525-565 on earnings delivery and partial re-rating toward the mid-20s forward; Bull ~+60-70% to the $627.83 mean target and above ($660 JPM / $700 B. Riley targets) if the WFE cycle extends into 2027 and China stabilises.
- Top risk (value-trap case): The equipment cycle itself — 21.1x is fair, not distressed, and if hyperscaler/foundry capex digests in 2027 after a ~50% growth quarter, AMAT earnings fall faster than the multiple can cushion, making 21x on peak earnings a trap; compounded by further export-control tightening, Chinese domestic substitution, or a pause by either of its two ~19%/15% customers.
- Conviction (1-10): 8
