(1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: AMAT
- Runner-up: FIX
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): AMAT is the one deep dip in this basket where I can find no evidence the business itself is broken — only the multiple was. The stock round-tripped from roughly 49x forward earnings to 21.1x, essentially its five-year average of 21.18x, while fiscal Q3 2026 delivered record revenue of $9.12B (+25% YoY), non-GAAP EPS of $3.50 (+41%), 50.4% gross margin and record operating cash flow of $3.04B, with Q4 guidance raised to a record $10.25B. The latest leg down on 2026-10-22 had no company-specific news at all — it was Lam Research China/supply read-through. A skeptic buys when the impairment story has to be borrowed from a peer's headline because the company's own print contradicts it.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): The single most compelling evidence is that price and fundamentals moved in opposite directions at record levels: the multiple roughly halved while revenue, margins, cash flow and guidance all hit records, and no source in the dossier shows leading-edge share loss to Lam Research or Tokyo Electron — the China drag is bounded and quantified (mix ~28% vs ~35% a year earlier, ~$600M FY2026 export-control hit already flagged) while foundry/logic and DRAM grow.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: Low risk — AMAT's deposition, etch, implant, metrology and CMP tools physically build the transistor and are qualified per-fab, per-node, per-recipe, so swapping vendors mid-node risks yield; AI is its demand driver, not a substitute, and Applied Global Services ($1.78B in Q3, +22% YoY, largely recurring subscription revenue) compounds the installed-base lock-in. The real long-term substitute is subsidised Chinese domestic equipment in mature nodes, not AI.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: Fiscal Q4 print on 2026-11-12 against management's own raised bar ($10.25B revenue / ~$4.02 non-GAAP EPS, ~40-50% YoY growth), plus any 2027 wafer-fab-equipment framework and confirmation that H2 calendar-2026 WFE spending is accelerating as customers guided. The $12.8B remaining buyback authorisation and 80-100% FCF distribution policy provide a floor while waiting.
- 12-18 month return scenario: Base roughly +35-45% (re-rating toward the mid-$530s-$570s on Q4 delivery and a partial multiple recovery); bull roughly +60-70% toward the $627.83 screen mean target / $640-660 external consensus if the 2027 WFE cycle extends.
- Top risk (value-trap case): That 21.1x is being paid on peak cyclical earnings — if hyperscaler/foundry capex digests in 2027, equipment orders are cut first and earnings fall faster than the multiple cushions; further export-control tightening, a larger China step-down, or Lam's supply constraints binding AMAT's own shipments into 2027 would convert a de-rating into an earnings trap.
