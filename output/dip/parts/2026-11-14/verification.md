## AMAT — Applied Materials — screen $370.50

### 1. Q4 FY2026 print (12 Nov 2026): revenue $10.41B (+53% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $4.16 vs ~$4.06 expected — record quarter, beat-and-raise
**CONFIRMED.** Company earnings release (SEC Ex-99.1, 12 Nov 2026): "Record quarterly revenue $10.41 billion, up 53 percent year over year"; record non-GAAP EPS $4.16, up 92% YoY; GAAP EPS $3.40. Zacks (12 Nov): revenue $10.41B vs $10.30B consensus (+1.11% surprise); EPS $4.16 vs $4.06 consensus (+2.46%). Full-year revenue $34.45B (+21%), non-GAAP EPS $12.89 (+37%). "Beat-and-raise" is fair: Q1 FY2027 guide is revenue $11.1B ±$0.5B and non-GAAP EPS $4.35 ±$0.20, both above consensus (~$10.89B / ~$4.26) — confirmed in the release and by TradingKey/AP coverage. Caveats worth noting (do not change the verdict): EPS missed the $4.20 whisper number (Earnings Whispers, 12 Nov), and GAAP EPS was held back by a $653M unrealised loss on strategic investments — both already in the dossier.

### 2. Guidance raised and first quantified call for CY2027 WFE spending growth above 30%
**CONFIRMED.** Earnings call transcript, 12 Nov 2026 (Motley Fool): CEO Gary Dickerson — "We expect 2027 to be another record year for the market and for Applied. Our current view is that wafer fab equipment demand will be up more than 30% year-over-year, and we are in a strong position to outperform the market again." Multiple secondary sources (TradingKey, 13 Nov; aiagentupdate, 13 Nov) explicitly call this management's first explicit 2027 WFE call, noting the October investor day gave no 2027 outlook. CY2026 Semi Systems growth was also upgraded to >40% (from >30%). Note: the >30% call is for industry WFE *demand/spending*, not AMAT revenue, and management flagged cleanroom availability as the governing constraint — both already in the dossier.

### 3. Valuation: forward P/E ~21x NTM (screen says 15.1x) — which is right?
**CONFIRMED that ~21x is the right NTM figure; the screen's 15.1x is not reconcilable and should not be used.** TIKR (11 Nov 2026, pre-print): "Applied's forward P/E has fallen from 49x to 21x, right at its five-year average," with FY2027 normalised EPS consensus $18.62 (up from $16.40 at end-June). Longbridge/Zhitong (9 Nov): forward P/E "fallen from 49x in late June to around 21x (close to the five-year average)" at the 6 Nov close of $332.72. A second TIKR piece (12 Nov) puts it at 22x. Why they differ: the screen's 15.1x implies forward EPS of ~$24.5 ($370.50 / 15.1), far above the FY2027 consensus of ~$18.6 — no published consensus supports that EPS base; the screen is likely using a later-year (FY2028) or otherwise different EPS set, or a stale/wrong earnings figure. At the 13 Nov close of $370.50 against the $18.62 FY2027 consensus, the NTM multiple is ~19.9x — i.e. ~20–21x, not 15.1x. The dossier's own conclusion ("use ~21x") is correct. The de-rating story (49x → ~21x) is confirmed by both TIKR and Longbridge.

### 4. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA about -0.24x)
**CONFIRMED (net cash; the -0.24x ratio is approximately consistent).** Company earnings release balance sheet, 25 Oct 2026: cash and equivalents $7,239M + short-term investments $2,100M = $9,339M cash & investments vs short-term debt $1,299M + long-term debt $5,233M = $6,532M total debt → net cash $2,807M (stockanalysis.com quarterly financials show the identical figures: Cash & Investments 9,339; Total Debt 6,532; Net Cash 2,807). Net debt/EBITDA of about -0.24x was not independently recomputed to the decimal, but it is arithmetically consistent with ~$2.8B net cash against ~$12B of EBITDA (FY2026 non-GAAP operating income ~$12B+). Direction and magnitude confirmed.

On the like-for-like Zacks forward basis, the claim holds.

### 10. The -46% drawdown cause: AI-capex duration fears / sequential margin slip, not a demand collapse — confirm or contradict
**CONFIRMED.** The evidence supports both named drivers and contradicts a demand collapse:
- AI-capex duration fears: Tickeron (4 Nov, on the -14.66% session to $1,096.70): the decline reflects "mounting investor concern over the durability of data-center and AI-related construction demand"; technology customers are ~59% of 9M 2026 revenue (vs 42% a year earlier). StockStory (4 Nov): FIX fell 13.5% in the morning session after CNBC reported ballot rejections of data centres and a Democratic sweep raising regulatory headwinds; Texas (27% of planned US data-centre capacity per Wells Fargo) governor-elect campaigned on a moratorium. Macroplane (5 Nov): builders fell first on 4 Nov — FIX -15.1% that day — on the permitting/moratorium repricing.
- Sequential margin slip: Tickeron: gross margin slipped to 24.8% in Q3 from 25.9% in Q2 and 26.3% in Q1; electrical-segment gross margin fell to 24.3% from 26.4% on heavier equipment/materials mix, purchase-accounting adjustments and early-stage projects. Zacks (26 Oct) confirms Q3 gross margin 24.8%.
- Not a demand collapse: the same quarter that produced the selloff had revenue +53.7%, backlog +90% YoY to a record $17.81B, operating cash flow $1.01B, and 2026 EPS estimates *rising* (Zacks, 13 Nov). Trefis (via ad-hoc-news, 5 Nov) explicitly notes FIX "released no announcement, earnings report or filing around the sharp November 4 decline" and links the move to the broader data-centre selloff. The CFO transition (Bill George retiring after the 2026 annual report; Julie Shaeff to succeed) is a third, smaller driver also cited by Tickeron/Zacks — the dossier names it, correctly.
