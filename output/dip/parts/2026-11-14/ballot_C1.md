## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** KLAC

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLA is the purest monopoly in this field: 56.8% of the $15.7B process-control market in 2025, ~6.5–7x its nearest rival, 85.9% of defect inspection, with a wide Morningstar moat and ROIC of ~34–44% in each of the last five fiscal years. The stock is −49.5% off its 52w high while the business just printed a record fiscal Q1 2027 — revenue $4.14B, +28.9% YoY, record operating margin 46.1%, its 10th consecutive quarter of growth — and guided the Dec quarter above consensus. AI makes KLA more, not less, necessary: 3nm/2nm complexity and advanced packaging (already tracking ~$1.1B in CY2026, +70%+ YoY) increase the need for inspection at every step. At 21.5x forward earnings with net debt of just 0.19x EBITDA, this is a dominant compounder repriced on sentiment, not a broken business.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence: fundamentals went the opposite way from the price — a record quarter, a raised guide, and a raised 2026 WFE outlook on the same 28 Oct 2026 call, while the stock fell because its trailing P/E compressed from 48.4x at 30 Sep to 38.8x by 3 Nov and because memory costs are squeezing gross margin. The genuine wrinkle is a cyclical input-cost headwind (Dec-quarter gross margin guided ~62.5% vs 62.7% in Sep), not lost share — KLA actually gained share in 2025 while every major competitor lost share.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. AI is a tailwind, not a threat: no model can inspect a wafer, and every major foundry (TSMC, Samsung, Intel) must qualify KLA tools into the process flow, with yield data/software compounding with use. Chinese makers held 0.7% of the market in 2025 — no credible substitute exists. The real risks are geographic (China ~31% of revenue) and cyclical WFE, not disruption.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** NVDA's Q3 FY27 print/guide Tue 17 Nov 2026 is the near-term sentiment trigger for the whole semi-equipment group; the fundamental checkpoint is KLA's Dec-quarter print on 28 Jan 2027 (guided revenue ~$4.5–4.9B, gross margin ~62.5%), with WFE expected to exceed $200B in CY2027 and gross-margin recovery toward 63–64% in H2 2027–2028 as memory costs normalise.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +40–45% toward the mean analyst target of $219.62 (+44.2% upside) as the multiple stabilises and earnings grow into the forward multiple. Bull case roughly +70%+ if memory costs normalise, gross margin recovers toward 63–64%, and WFE beats the >$200B CY2027 call — a re-rating back toward a premium equipment multiple on a much larger earnings base.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Memory-cost inflation persists into 2027 and gross margin stalls below 62%, so the earnings growth the multiple depends on never arrives; or China export controls tighten further (China ~31% of revenue) — a hard restriction, not a cycle, would cut both revenue and the multiple. Note also that KLA has fallen after 5 of its last 6 earnings reports, so even good prints have not held.
