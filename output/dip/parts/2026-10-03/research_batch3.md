### 6. Category position
The leading pure-play in data-center power/thermal infrastructure with a $15B+ backlog signal (MarketBeat 2/26); benefiting from Nvidia/ecosystem partnerships. Not a monopoly (Emerson, ABB, Schneider compete), but no share-loss signal — the miss was execution timing, and guidance was raised.

### 7. Value-trap risk (bear case)

Execution on multi-phase mega-projects is a repeated stumble risk; UIG could prove an overpay at 13x forward EBITDA; tariffs/supply-chain volatility (cited by Zacks 9/1) compresses the margin story.

### 6. Category position
Still the #1 insurance-data franchise (ISO, Xactware, AIR) with pricing power (annual price increases tied to model enhancements, per Q2 call) and 97%-ish recurring economics. No evidence of share loss — transactional revenue softness is weather/volume-driven, not competitive.

### 7. Value-trap risk (bear case)
Secular growers don't re-rate if 5–6% OCC is the new ceiling: 5-yr revenue CAGR is only ~1.7% per StockStory (10/2/26). If AI genuinely commoditizes underwriting analytics, today's 20.5x could still be the *high* of a derating path; rising SG&A (+20.8% in Q2 on legal/advisory/acquisition costs) and personnel cost pressure (55% of opex) could cap margin expansion.
