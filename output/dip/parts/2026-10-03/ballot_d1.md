## A) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick: SPGI**
- **Runner-up: IDXX**

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** S&P Global is a wide-moat compounder trading at a multi-year-low multiple (19–20x fwd vs its historical mid-to-high-20s) because of issuance-cycle timing and a September AI-panic sentiment wave — not because the business broke. The dossier shows record Ratings and Indices performance in H1'26, pro-forma revenue +11%, and 500+ customers on LLM-ready APIs, i.e., the franchise is monetizing AI rather than being eaten by it. Over $7B in 2026 buybacks (~6% of market cap) post-Mobility-spin acts as a forced buyer under the stock. Q3 earnings in late October is the first clean post-spin quarter, and any dovish rate turn reviving debt issuance would hit the highest-margin segment directly — a cheap price on an intact business with a visible re-rating path.

- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is Q2'26 itself: adj EPS +23% ex-Mobility with record Ratings and Indices revenue — the core earnings engine posted one of its best quarters in the middle of a −24.8% drawdown. A genuinely impaired business doesn't set segment records while its stock slides; the price action is sentiment (AI panic) and cyclical (issuance overhang), while the numbers say the moat is still minting.

- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability: LOW risk — and why.** Morningstar reaffirmed the wide moat in March 2026 ($570 fair value): S&P 500 benchmark network effects, a regulatory-licensed ratings duopoly with Moody's, and covenant/mandate embedding create extreme switching costs. Nobody substitutes an S&P rating or index license with a chatbot; if anything, AI-driven data demand increases the value of authoritative, licensed data — and SPGI is already productizing 500+ LLM-ready API customers.

- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** (1) Q3 earnings late Oct 2026 — first clean post-spin quarter, low bar; (2) continued execution of the >$7B buyback (~6% of market cap) shrinking the float; (3) any dovish rate signal reviving debt issuance into the Ratings segment. Rough timing: 1–3 quarters for sentiment/AI-fear fade; the rate-turn leg is unknowable but the buyback floor is already running.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case ~+30–35% to ~$500–520 as the multiple drifts back toward ~22–23x on intact earnings, aided by the buyback. Bull case ~+55–65% to ~$600+ if issuance revives on rate cuts and the multiple re-rates to its historical 26–27x — Morningstar's own $570 fair value is already +47%.

- **Top risk (value-trap case):** If rates stay high, the issuance drought persists and the market discovers the post-Mobility portfolio is structurally a 4%-revenue-CAGR grower — then 19x on stagnant earnings isn't cheap, it's the new normal. The dossier's own caution: Morningstar may be overstating the AI-moat case, and if AI commoditizes Market Intelligence faster than the API monetization offsets, the wide moat becomes a narrow one in slow motion. This is a cyclical-recovery bet, not a secular-growth lock.

- **Conviction (1-10): 7**

**SPGI:** The most compelling temporary-dip data point is that the drawdown (−24.8%) happened *during* record Ratings and Indices performance — Q2'26 adj EPS +23% ex-Mobility, pro-forma revenue +11%, and the business is already monetizing the feared AI via 500+ LLM-ready API customers. That is price-vs-business decoupling in its purest form in this batch. 12–18mo: base ~+30–35% (~$500–520) on multiple normalization plus the >$7B buyback; bull ~+55–65% (~$600+) if issuance revives and the multiple re-rates to 26–27x (Morningstar FV $570 = +47%).

**IDXX:** The single most compelling business-intact data point in the dossier: CAG recurring revenue grew +10.3% organic and the inVue Dx base passed 9,000 instruments *while* vet visits were guided down −1.5% — the installed-base annuity is growing through the funnel weakness, which is utilization and share gain, not business breakage. Add 0.5x net leverage, ~110% FCF conversion, and the cheapest forward multiple in years on a 9/10-moat franchise. 12–18mo: base ~+30–35% (~$670–700) on visit recovery and bar-reset digestion; bull ~+45% (~$750) if the multiple re-rates back toward its historical 40x+ band.

**VRT:** The Q2'26 rev miss was explicitly "minor timing shifts" on multi-phase mega-projects — while adj EPS *beat*, operating margin expanded +410bp, FCF surged +234%, and management RAISED full-year guidance. The business signal improved while the stock fell 17%; that is the veto lens's favorite setup. AI is the demand driver (power/thermal), not a substitute, and the balance sheet is ~net cash. 12–18mo: base ~+35–40% (~$340–350) on proving the H2-weighted guide; bull ~+55% (~$390) if investor-day 20–22% CAGR-through-2030 and clean UIG disclosure reset the AI-infra narrative.

Sitting at the 52-week low with a −40.9% drawdown isn't a discount if the earnings base itself is legislated away; the regulatory wildcard is binary and unhedgeable.

Best-positioned AI monetizer in creative, yes — but if AI routes around the suite, this is a secular de-rating, not a dip.
- **BR — TEMP-TO-STRUCTURAL, OUT.** Tokenization is a *future* but *concrete* threat to the proxy franchise, and Q3 closed sales fell 19% YoY — the one leading indicator is already weakening. My lens cannot distinguish "market doesn't believe the guide yet" from "guide is wrong"; the 15x multiple has room to keep compressing, so it stays off the ballot.
