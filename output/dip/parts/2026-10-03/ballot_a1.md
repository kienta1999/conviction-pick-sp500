Customers pay, projects ship — they just ship in a different quarter. You do not lose demand and simultaneously raise full-year guidance while converting earnings to cash at 150%.

AI is the demand driver, not a substitute — VRT sells the physical mission-critical power and thermal infrastructure (UPS, liquid cooling, switchgear) that AI compute physically requires. The real risk is competition (Emerson, ABB, Schneider), not disruption.
