## Earnings-quality flags

- **CPRT RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN:** Receivables growing faster than revenue. Likely benign: Copart bills insurers in arrears and collects auction proceeds before remitting to sellers, so receivables swell with higher average selling prices per vehicle near quarter-end even when unit volume falls (revenue +2.4% on -2.9% units in Q4 FY26) — a timing/ASP-mix artifact, not channel-stuffing. **Verdict: mostly benign; watch only if the cost squeeze broadens.**
- **BRO RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN:** Benign and structural. Insurance brokers carry large "premiums and fees receivable" — fiduciary balances collected from clients and remitted to carriers — which scale with *gross premium throughput* and remittance timing, while reported revenue is only commissions/fees. After the Accession close (Aug 2025) these balances mechanically ballooned relative to organic commissions. **Verdict: benign business-model artifact; ignore unless reserve/allowance for doubtful accounts rises.**
