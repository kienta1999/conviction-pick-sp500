## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** IDXX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the purest growth-compounder in the batch on sale: 33.6% operating margin, net-cash balance sheet, ~75% recurring revenue from instruments/accessories, and an installed base of 11,710 da Vinci systems that locks in procedure volumes through surgeon training and workflow entrenchment. Q2 2026 printed +19% revenue, +16% procedure growth, 468 systems placed, and management *raised* gross-margin guidance to 68–69% — yet the stock sits 33.7% off its 52-week high on a 32.5x forward P/E vs a five-year average of ~54. That is a multiple compression on a maintained 13.5–15.5% procedure-growth guide, not a broken franchise. The dip is what quality investors wait for: a wide-moat, double-digit grower repriced as if its best years are over while it keeps beating.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the business keeps beating while the price falls — Q2 2026 beat revenue and EPS, raised margin guidance, and full-year procedure guidance was *held* (not cut), while demand indicators (468 placements, international procedures +20%, Q1 adjusted FCF +147%) stayed at record levels. The drawdown crystallized around a slower guide and tariff/surgery-volume *sentiment*, not an operating miss.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. No credible substitute for robotic surgery in the medium term; threats are margin-level (Chinese tenders, Medtronic's Hugo, hospital cost pressure) not franchise-level. AI *augments* the robot (case insights, remote collaboration) rather than replacing it — Intuitive is itself deploying it (My Intuitive Plus). Irreplaceability 9/10 per dossier.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 2026 earnings on **2026-10-20** — any procedure-growth beat vs the held 13.5–15.5% guide re-rates the stock; the da Vinci 5 rollout (246 installs in Q2, new cardiac clearances) and tariff relief already embedded in the raised 68–69% margin guide are the fuel.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: +25–35% — procedures print ~14–15%, margins keep expanding, forward multiple re-rates partway toward history (MarketBeat mean target $508.68 implies ~+29% from ~$393). Bull: +45–60% — da Vinci 5 accelerates placements, tariff relief lands, and the market re-prices to 40x+ on proven double-digit durability.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The H1 2027 Extended Use Program (longer instrument life) cannibalizes recurring instrument revenue faster than procedure volumes compensate — management itself says financial impacts are "unquantified and progressive from 2027" — and if 13–15% procedure growth is the new normal, 30x+ may never fully return. ACA-driven US procedure softness (HCA's warning) is the second leg of the trap.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
