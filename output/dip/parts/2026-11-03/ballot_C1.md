## 1. Single-pick ballot
- Top pick: KLAC
- Runner-up: HWM
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): KLAC is the name in this field whose product AI makes more necessary, not less: ever-more-complex AI accelerators require ever-more inspection and metrology, and no model inspects a wafer. Its fiscal Q1 2027 print was a record — revenue $4.136B, +28.9% YoY, non-GAAP operating margin 46.1%, above 46% for the first time — yet the stock fell 5.69% on 2026-10-30 to $157.91 on sector AI-capex fear and target cuts, a sentiment de-rating, not an impairment. Management then guided the December quarter to $4.7B ± $200M (+14% sequential at the midpoint) and raised the calendar-2027 wafer-fab-equipment outlook above $200B, giving the dip a dated, verifiable rebuttal. With qualification lock-in, an installed-base service annuity, and 32% free-cash-flow margin in the quarter, this is a compounder's dip, not a cyclical's hope.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence is that revenue, margins, guidance, and the 2027 market outlook all improved in the same report the market sold off on — the October fall was multiple compression on a stock still +26% year-to-date, with no lost customer, lost share, or failed product evidenced in the dossier.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: low risk — AI is a demand driver for process control, not a substitute; a fab cannot swap an inspection recipe mid-ramp without re-qualifying yield, and KLAC is #1 in process control. The deductions are cyclicality of customer capex and China/trade exposure, not substitutability.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: delivery against the December-quarter guide ($4.7B revenue, $1.44 ± $0.11 non-GAAP EPS), proven at the fiscal Q2 print in late January 2027; the raised 2027 WFE >$200B outlook frames the re-rating through 2027.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base +35–45% (toward the ~$221 analyst mean, +40–44% vs the $157.91 screen price, on guide delivery); bull +55–65% if the 2027 WFE >$200B call and 63–64% gross-margin target both hold and the multiple re-expands toward its late-2025 level.
- Top risk (value-trap case): an AI-capex pause, widening China restrictions, or a 2027 WFE print below $200B would expose a ~28x forward multiple — KLAC fell 39% in 2022 and 69% in 2008–09, so a cyclical air-pocket, not AI substitution, is the trap.
