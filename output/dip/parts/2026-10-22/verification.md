## AMAT — Applied Materials

### Claim 1 — Fiscal Q3 2026 results and fiscal Q4 2026 guidance
**Claim:** Record revenue ~$9.12B, +25% YoY, in fiscal Q3 2026; fiscal Q4 2026 guidance of
~$10.25B ± $0.5B revenue / ~$4.02 ± $0.20 non-GAAP EPS.
**Verdict: CONFIRMED.**
- Applied Materials reported fiscal Q3 2026 (quarter ended 2026-07-26) on 2026-08-13:
  record revenue $9.12B, up 25% YoY; non-GAAP EPS a record $3.50, up 41%; GAAP EPS $3.17.
- Q4 FY2026 guidance given with that release: revenue $10.25B ± $500M, non-GAAP diluted EPS
  $4.02 ± $0.20 (midpoint +51% revenue YoY, +85% non-GAAP EPS YoY).
- Sources: Applied Materials Q3 2026 earnings release / call reporting, 2026-08-13;
  Motley Fool Q3 2026 earnings-call transcript summary, 2026-08-20 (revenue $9.12B, +25%;
  Q4 guide $10.25B ± $500M, $4.02 ± $0.20); corroborated by multiple financial-press summaries
  of the 2026-08-13 release.

### Claim 3 — China revenue and the ~$600M FY26 export-control headwind
**Claim:** China revenue roughly flat in dollars at ~$2.5B in fiscal Q3 2026 (~28% of sales), and
a guided FY26 China/export-control revenue headwind of ~$600M.
**Verdict: CONFIRMED.**
- Fiscal Q3 2026 geographic mix: China $2.51B vs $2.55B a year earlier — essentially flat in
  dollars — falling to 28% of total revenue from 35% a year earlier purely because revenue
  elsewhere grew 25%. China remained the largest single market.
- The $600M figure: following the U.S. Commerce Department / BIS rule effective 2025-09-29,
  Applied Materials stated the expanded restrictions would reduce fiscal Q4 2025 revenue by
  ~$110M and fiscal 2026 revenue by ~$600M (~$710M combined).
- Sources: AMAT Q3 2026 geographic revenue reporting, Aug 2026 ($2.51B, 28%, vs $2.55B / 35%);
  Reuters reporting relayed Oct–Dec 2025 ("about a $600 million hit to fiscal 2026 revenue");
  company statement on the BIS affiliate rule, Oct 2025 ($110M Q4 + $600M FY2026).

### Claim 4 — Valuation, moat positions, and WFE share
**Claim:** AMAT trades at ~20.6x forward earnings, the cheapest of the large WFE peers
(Lam Research, KLA, ASML); holds #1 / widest-portfolio positions in leading-edge foundry-logic,
DRAM and advanced packaging equipment; WFE share slipped from ~20.0% (2024) to ~17.9% (2025).
**Verdict: CONFIRMED in substance, with two documented caveats (exact forward multiple is
screen-specific; exact WFE-share levels are source-dependent — see correction note).**
- Cheapest-of-peers ordering: confirmed by every provider checked, on both trailing and forward
  bases. FinanceCharts (Oct 2026): trailing P/E — AMAT 35.28 vs Lam Research 45.28, KLA 44.53,
  ASML 53.37; AMAT forward P/E listed at 23.42. MarketBeat comparison (Oct 2026): AMAT forward
  P/E 22.38 vs ASML 27.05, KLA 24.65, Lam 22.82 — AMAT lowest. StockAnalysis (close 2026-10-21,
  $408.94): forward P/E 23.19. TheStreet (Sep 2026, StockAnalysis data): AMAT "about 29 times
  forward earnings, the cheapest of the four" at pre-crash prices.
- Exact 20.6x caveat: 20.56x is the panel screen-of-record figure at the screen price of $380.10
  (2026-10-22, after a ~47% fall from the high). Public aggregators at the 2026-10-21 close
  ($408.94) show ~22–23x forward. The verifier could not replicate 20.6x exactly from a public
  provider; the figure is consistent in direction and ordering (cheapest peer, multiple compressed
  roughly in line with the price fall while estimates rose), but it should be cited as a screen
  figure, not as a provider-published multiple.
- #1 positions: confirmed as the company's stated position, repeated at its SEMICON West
  investor presentation (2026-10-13): Applied holds number-one process-equipment positions in
  leading-edge foundry-logic, DRAM and advanced packaging — the three areas expected to drive
  ~75% of WFE growth 2025–2030 — and describes its portfolio as the broadest/widest in the
  industry. Sources: AMAT SEMICON West investor-day reporting, Oct 2026; TIKR / Tickeron
  summaries of management statements ("holds number-one process-equipment positions in
  leading-edge foundry-logic, DRAM, and advanced packaging").
- **WFE-share CORRECTION NOTE (material):** The dossier's "20.0% (2024) → 17.9% (2025)" levels
  trace to The Information Network data as relayed by secondary reporting in Oct 2026
  (The Inference, 2026-10-16-ish; 24/7 Wall St relay) — they are *not* Gartner figures and the
  dossier does not source them to a primary publisher. Sourced Gartner WFE-share figures put
  Applied materially higher, at roughly ~21.7% for 2024 and ~20.2% for 2025. The two series
  differ in level (different market definitions / inclusions), but they agree on the direction
  and rough magnitude of the slip (~1.5–2.1 points) and on the implication the dossier draws:
  the company's 2030 target of >+2 points of share only recovers ground already lost. The panel
  should therefore cite the share levels as "per The Information Network" — or switch to the
  Gartner series (~21.7% → ~20.2%) — and must not present 20.0% / 17.9% as settled,
  source-neutral facts. The investment conclusion (share slipped; AMAT lagged peers' H1 2026
  equipment-revenue growth: AMAT +1.0% vs Lam +23.3%, KLA +12.6%, ASML +13.3%, same
  Information Network series) is not overturned by the correction.

## CPRT — Copart

### Claim 8 — Why-down decomposition: ex-lost-customer +2.3%; Progressive→IAA ~3 points
**Claim:** Excluding the one lost insurance customer, domestic insurance assignments would have
INCREASED +2.3% in Q4 FY2026 (Jay Adair, Q4 FY2026 earnings call, ~2026-09-10); the
Progressive-to-IAA reallocation is ~3 points of units.
**Verdict: CONFIRMED — first half as a direct management statement; second half confirmed only
as an independent analyst estimate (which is how the dossier presents it), not as a company
disclosure.**
- +2.3%: Jay Adair on the Q4 FY2026 call (2026-09-10): "With the exception of one single
  customer loss, domestic insurance assignments would be up 2.3%." Context in the same call:
  global units −2.9%, U.S. units −5.7%, domestic insurance units −7.5%, collision claim
  frequency −3.4% YoY, total-loss frequency 23.3% in Q2 FY2026 (record for a second quarter).
- Sources: Copart Q4 2026 earnings-call transcripts — Motley Fool, 2026-09-11; MarketBeat
  transcript, 2026-09-10; The Stock Observer call highlights, 2026-09-10 — all carrying the
  +2.3% ex-customer-loss statement verbatim or near-verbatim.
- Progressive ~3 points: no company source states this figure. It comes from an independent
  forensic decomposition (Undiscovered Compounders, 2026) of Copart's Aug 2025–Apr 2026 U.S.
  insurance-unit loss (−5.1 pts ex-CAT), which attributes ~2.7 pts to a shrinking industry
  wreck pool and ~1.2–1.9 pts to client mix, leaving the lost account / residual at ~0.5–1.2 pts
  and concluding the shift was "closer to a 3-point shift in Progressive's volume allocation
  than to a 15-point shift" (the sell-side scare of Oct–Nov 2025 had assumed Progressive moved
  its IAA allocation from ~75% to ~90%). Corroborating direction: RB Global reported +11%
  automotive units in Q2 2026 with "net market share gains," attributed to extending its largest
  insurance contract to all 50 states (RB Global results, 2026-08-04). The panel must keep the
  "~3 points" figure labelled as an analyst estimate.

### Claim 9 — Cash quality and valuation: OCF ~$1,604.5M vs net income ~$1,480.4M; ~18.5x trailing vs ~29x 10-year average
**Claim:** FY2026 operating cash flow ~$1,604.5M vs net income ~$1,480.4M (cash conversion
above 1.0); stock at ~18.5x trailing vs a ~29x 10-year trailing average multiple.
**Verdict: CONFIRMED.**
- Cash flow (primary source): Copart's FY2026 consolidated statements of cash flows (year ended
  2026-07-31), in the Q4/FY2026 8-K Exhibit 99.1 filed 2026-09-10 and the FY2026 10-K:
  net income $1,480,397K; net cash provided by operating activities $1,604,492K.
  Cash conversion = 1,604.5 / 1,480.4 ≈ 1.08 — above 1.0, as claimed.
- Valuation: MarketBeat (Oct 2026) lists CPRT trailing P/E of 18.49 on trailing EPS of $1.55;
  FullRatio lists the current P/E at 17.68 as of 2026-10-21 and — directly on point — "the
  average historical PE ratio of Copart for the last ten years is 29.18," with the 2026 annual
  P/E at 18.67, down 33.7% from 28.16 in 2025. Providers vary by date/price (17.7–18.7 across
  Oct 20–22 prints), so "~18.5x vs ~29x" is accurate within normal provider dispersion.
- Sources: Copart 8-K Ex-99.1, 2026-09-10, and FY2026 10-K cash-flow statements (SEC filings);
  FullRatio CPRT P/E history (accessed Oct 2026); MarketBeat CPRT earnings/valuation page.

### Claim 10 — Next earnings date: estimated 2026-11-19 (Q1 FY2027)
**Claim:** CPRT's next earnings (Q1 FY2027) are estimated for 2026-11-19.
**Verdict: UNVERIFIED — date is NOT company-confirmed, and aggregators do not agree on a
single date; the spread runs from Nov 19 to Dec 4.**
- No Copart press release, 8-K, or IR announcement confirming a Q1 FY2027 report date was
  found as of 2026-10-22. Copart historically does not pre-announce earnings dates far in
  advance, and at least one data provider states explicitly that "Copart has not yet confirmed
  a specific date for its next earnings release."
- Aggregator estimates disagree: MarketBeat, StockAnalysis, Quartr and several others estimate
  Thursday, November 19, 2026 (after market close) — so Nov 19 is the modal estimate and the
  dossier's date is the most common one — but TipRanks estimates November 18, 2026, and other
  aggregator calendars place the estimated Q1 FY2027 report as late as December 4, 2026.
  The full aggregator spread is therefore Nov 19 – Dec 4 (with Nov 18 also in the set).
- Sources: MarketBeat CPRT earnings page ("Nov. 19 — After Market Closes — Estimated");
  StockAnalysis CPRT statistics ("next estimated earnings date is Thursday, November 19, 2026");
  TipRanks CPRT earnings calendar (Nov 18, 2026, "based on past reporting schedules");
  provider statements that the date is unconfirmed / based on historical reporting patterns.
- Panel implication: any catalyst timing built on 2026-11-19 for CPRT must be labelled
  ESTIMATED, with a realistic window of mid-November to early December 2026. This contrasts
  with AMAT (Nov 12) and CAT (Oct 29), both of which are company-confirmed (claims 2 and 6).
