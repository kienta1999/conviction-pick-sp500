### Top-3 notes

**1. CPRT:** The most compelling data point is that ex-one-lost-account, domestic insurance assignments rose 2.3% in Q4 FY2026 — the "share collapse" is a ~3-point bounded reallocation plus a cyclical claims-frequency shrink, while pricing power proved itself with revenue per unit +5.7% and ASP +5.5% on falling volumes, and the RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flag is a near-zero-revenue-growth denominator artefact refuted by CFO/NI ~1.09–1.16 and a negative accrual ratio. The moat is physical and permitted — owned yards, a duopoly, and a global buyer flywheel no model can copy. 12–18mo scenario: base +30–40% (~$37–40), bull +60–75% (~$46–50) on a volume inflection plus multiple re-rating.

**2. CAT:** The dip is a valuation/rates/tariff repricing — 10-year at ~5.35%, ~$2.2B FY2026 tariff cost — of a business that just posted record results and a record $72.1B backlog (+92% YoY, 59% deliverable within 12 months, turbine orders into 2029–2030), with guidance *raised* on 2026-08-04 and no share loss found. Its moat is the >150-dealer / ~2,800-facility network built over a century, a 15–30-year installed base locked into Cat parts and service, and a product AI needs rather than replaces — there is no software substitute for a 2MW+ genset or a mining truck; the Q3 print on 2026-10-29 is the near-term proof point. 12–18mo scenario: base +25–35% (toward the $945.76 mean target), bull +50%+ if backlog converts and the AI-power premium partially returns — capped by the honest caveat that 21.47x forward on peak-cycle earnings is a reset, not a bargain.

**3. AMAT:** The drawdown stack is parabolic run-up, sell-the-news on a record Q3 ($9.12B revenue, +25% YoY), and a sector-wide October chip rout (AMAT -22.8% from 2026-10-06 closes, RSI 12.8) — not lost technology leadership, with Q4 guidance implying +51% YoY revenue and China revenue flat in dollars (~$2.5B, share down only because ex-China grew faster). The moat is real: widest WFE portfolio, tools qualified into fab process flows where switching risks yield, a $1.78B/quarter services installed base, and AI as its demand driver (GAA, backside power, hybrid bonding, eBeam) — but it is not an ASML-style monopoly, and WFE share slipped 20.0% (2024) to 17.9% (2025) on policy-imposed China access loss, so it ranks below the two physical-network moats. 12–18mo scenario: base +35–50%, bull +65%+ (the $627.83 mean target is +65.2%) if the 2026-11-12 print delivers guidance; highest beta (1.64) of the top three cuts both ways.

**Bottom names:** I deliberately left BKNG (5th) and COO (6th) at the bottom. BKNG's supply/payments infrastructure is genuinely hard to replace, but its consumer storefront is not — the September 2026 Meta Muse–Expedia partnership is a concrete AI-interface capture event, layered with EU DMA/DSA proceedings, so under an irreplaceability lens it fails the test the others pass despite +43.4% target upside and 8/8 EPS beats. COO is the name I most suspect is a value trap: legacy hydrogel decline is secular, PARAGARD has lost its 40-year uniqueness to Miudella (FDA-approved Feb 2025, the first new copper IUD in 40 years), the strategic-review/breakup catalyst has already fired and missed, estimates fell *with* the price through two FY2026 guidance cuts, management's "it's all destock" credibility is under investigation, and the analyst mean target offers only +8.6% — a low multiple on a possibly lower base. CPRT's bounded lost account is a watch item, not a trap call, given its net-cash fortress and demonstrated pricing power.
