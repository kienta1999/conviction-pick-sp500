**6. Category position — still #1 in professional creative software and in CX, losing the layer below.**
Adobe remains the category leader: Creative & Marketing Professionals subscription revenue $4.65bn in Q3 (+13%), Business Professionals & Consumers $1.91bn (+16%), total ARR $27.5bn (+11.2%), >1bn MAU. No evidence of professional-tier share loss in the numbers — revenue grew +12.9% in Q3 with 4/4 EPS beats (avg +2.1%) on the screen. The share loss is at the edges and in growth rates: Figma +48%, Canva taking prosumer/SMB, AI-native tools taking task volume, and Adobe's own net new ARR falling ~38% YoY is the internal proof that the funnel's paid conversion is weakening even as free users surge +70%. Pricing power at the top tier is intact (it just raised guidance); pricing power over the *next* cohort of creators is what is being contested.

**7. Value-trap risk (bear case) — the most credible falling-knife case in the batch.**
This is the textbook trap shape: 9–10x forward, $9bn+ FCF, buybacks — and a product category whose unit value generative AI is actively deflating. The knife scenario: (a) AI-first ARR stays ~2–3% of the base while Creative net new ARR keeps falling, proving Firefly is a defensive feature, not a growth engine; (b) the freemium funnel (100m+ free users) never converts at historical rates because free AI tools elsewhere are "good enough," forcing price/mix cuts; (c) Figma/Canva/AI-clone competition moves up-tier into team/enterprise seats, as HSBC's 13 Oct ArtCraft note fears; (d) the CEO/CFO double transition (1 Dec, CFO still interim) produces a kitchen-sink FY27 guide on 9 Dec that resets growth to high-single digits — at which point 9x is not cheap, it is fair for an ex-growth software name, and the stock can sit there for years (the Oracle/IBM precedent). Semrush integration and rising AI infrastructure/model spend pressuring the 44% non-GAAP operating margin (already down from 46.3% in Q3 FY25) would compound it. The tell to watch is not EPS — Adobe will likely keep beating EPS on buybacks — it is net new ARR and cRPO.

**Rebound score: 6.0/10 — The batch's only genuine statistical bargain (~9–10x verified non-GAAP forward vs ~37x own median, $9bn+ FCF, net debt 0.12x) — discounted half a point below KLAC because its catalyst is 52 days away, its mean target implies only +11%, and it is the one name here where the AI-impairment bear case could be right.**
