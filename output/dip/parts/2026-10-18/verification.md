### Claim 1 — WHY-DOWN / MOAT: <1% of room nights via AI channels (Skift summit, 11 Oct 2026); $100m Vayo investment disclosed the same day
- **1a — <1% AI-channel share: CONFIRMED (figure); summit date only loosely pinned.**
  Multiple secondary summaries of the Skift coverage state verbatim: "At the Skift Data and AI Summit on October 11, 2026, the company disclosed that fewer than 1% of its room nights are currently booked through AI channels" (GPTs24 / TravelersToday syndication of Skift, 17 Oct 2026). Independent corroboration of the figure itself: TravelExtra's summit takeaways (Oct 2026) — "AI agent referral traffic still accounts for less than 1pc of direct room nights at major sites like Booking.com." The substance (<1%) holds; treat the exact "11 Oct summit" dating as approximate.
- **1b — $100m Vayo investment "disclosed the same day": CONTRADICTED on date (amount CONFIRMED).**
  The $100m figure is real, but the primary disclosure is dated **14 Oct 2026, not 11 Oct**: Booking Holdings PR Newswire release, "Booking Holdings Launches Vayo with Planned $100M Investment," SEATTLE, Oct. 14, 2026 (via Morningstar PR Newswire feed, 14 Oct 2026); Skift, "Booking Holdings Plans to Fund New AI Startup Vayo With $100 Million," published 2026-10-14T13:00Z — Vayo "launched Wednesday in private beta." Two further honest caveats the dossier understates: (i) it is a **planned** investment, "subject to continued advancement of the product and market conditions" (Skift, 14 Oct 2026; PR release asterisk) — not cash already committed; (ii) the dossier's wording "disclosed at the same 11 Oct 2026 Skift summit" conflates the summit coverage (11 Oct) with the launch release (14 Oct).
  **Verdict for Claim 1 as written: CONTRADICTED** — the moat datapoint (<1%) is confirmed, the same-day Vayo disclosure is not; correct date is 14 Oct 2026.
