## AMAT — Applied Materials

### Claim 1 — Fiscal Q3 2026 (reported 2026-08-13): record revenue $9.12B (+25% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $3.50 (+41%), non-GAAP gross margin 50.4%
**CONFIRMED**
- Source: Applied Materials Q3 FY2026 earnings release (Exhibit 99.1), SANTA CLARA, Aug. 13, 2026, quarter ended July 26, 2026.
- Verbatim from the release: "Record revenue $9.12 billion, up 25 percent year over year • GAAP gross margin 50.3 percent and non-GAAP gross margin 50.4 percent • GAAP EPS $3.17 and record non-GAAP EPS $3.50, up 43 percent and 41 percent year over year, respectively." Results Summary table: revenue $9,115M vs $7,302M (+25%), non-GAAP diluted EPS $3.50 vs $2.48 (+41%), non-GAAP gross margin 50.4%.

### Claim 2 — Q4 FY2026 guidance $10.25B ± $0.50B revenue and ~$4.02 non-GAAP EPS; raised calendar-2026 semiconductor equipment growth outlook to >30%; Q4 earnings date 2026-11-12
**CONTRADICTED (in part — guidance and date confirmed; the >30% raise is mis-timed / mis-attributed to this print)**
- Q4 guidance: CONFIRMED in the primary release, "Business Outlook" section: "Total revenue $10,250 +/- $500" (millions) and "Non-GAAP diluted EPS $4.02 +/- $0.20" for Q4 FY2026.
- Earnings date 2026-11-12: CONFIRMED — multiple secondary sources citing the company's Oct. 22, 2026 announcement (e.g., Morningstar / Tickeron / Kalkine, Oct. 2026): Applied Materials will report fiscal Q4 2026 results on November 12, 2026, after market close, call 4:30 p.m. ET.
- ">30%" element: CONTRADICTED as a Q3 event. The raise of the calendar-2026 semiconductor equipment / Semiconductor Systems growth outlook to "more than 30%" from "more than 20%" occurred at **fiscal Q2 (May 2026)**, not at the Aug. 13 Q3 print — see TechTimes, May 31, 2026 ("CEO Gary Dickerson raised the company's forecast for semiconductor equipment market growth to more than 30% in calendar 2026, up from a prior estimate of more than 20%") and the AInvest Q2 earnings write-up ("Management also raised its calendar 2026 semiconductor equipment growth outlook to more than 30%, up from its prior view of more than 20%"). The Q3 release contains no "30%" figure at all; its only forward language on this point is Dickerson's unquantified "we are further raising our Semiconductor Systems revenue expectations for calendar 2026." Treating ">30%" as new/raised at Q3 double-counts a Q2 raise. The underlying >30% outlook itself was real — it is the timing/attribution in the claim that fails.

### Claim 3 — Balance sheet at Q3-end: liquid assets (cash + short- and long-term investments) ~$14.5B vs total debt ~$6.5B (net cash); China ~28–30% of revenue
**CONFIRMED**
- Source: same Q3 FY2026 release, Unaudited Consolidated Condensed Balance Sheets at July 26, 2026 (primary):
  - Cash and cash equivalents $7,037M + short-term investments $2,196M + long-term investments $5,268M = **$14,501M ≈ $14.5B**.
  - Short-term debt $1,299M + long-term debt $5,245M = **$6,544M ≈ $6.5B**. Liquid assets exceed total debt by ~$8.0B — net cash, as claimed.
- China: CONFIRMED in the same release, "Revenue by Geography": China $2,506M = **28% of total** in Q3 FY2026 (vs 35% a year earlier) — at the low end of the claimed ~28–30% range. Note for precision: on the earnings call management separately cited China as 26% of *Semiconductor Systems + AGS* revenue (a different denominator, call transcript via MarketBeat, Aug. 13, 2026); the 28% total-revenue figure is the release figure and supports the claim as written.

## IDXX — IDEXX Laboratories

### Claim 6 — Q2 2026: revenue ~$1.22B (+~10% YoY), EPS $4.27 vs ~$3.96 consensus, and management RAISED FY2026 guidance to revenue $4,700–4,745M and EPS $14.69–14.94
**CONFIRMED (with a minor consensus-source variance noted)**
- Revenue / EPS: CONFIRMED — IDEXX Q2 2026 Earnings Snapshot (company, quarter ended June 30, 2026; reported Aug. 4, 2026) and earnings coverage: revenue $1,216.6M (+10% reported, ~9% organic), diluted EPS $4.27 (+18% reported / +15% comparable), operating margin 35.0%.
- Consensus: most sources put Q2 consensus EPS at **$3.94** (MarketBeat: $4.27 vs $3.94 consensus, +$0.33; revenue consensus $1.20B) or $3.93 (TIKR) — the claimed "~$3.96" is $0.02–0.03 above the commonly cited figure. This is within vendor/rounding variance (a Zacks-derived 8.1% beat implies ~$3.95) and does not change the beat, but the precise consensus on record is $3.93–3.94, not $3.96.
- Guidance raise: CONFIRMED — company Q2 2026 Earnings Snapshot: "Updates revenue guidance to $4,700 million – $4,745 million, 9.1%–10.3% growth as reported and 8.5%–9.7% organic, raising organic growth guidance at midpoint by 40 basis points vs. prior guide" and "Increases EPS outlook to $14.69 – $14.94, reflecting an increase of $0.14 compared to prior guidance midpoint." Call transcript (CFO Andrew Emerson, Aug. 4, 2026, via MarketBeat) confirms the same ranges. Precision note: the revenue raise is a **midpoint** raise (+$5M at midpoint, +$20M operational improvement partly offset by a $15M FX headwind) with the range narrowed — the top end came down vs the prior $4,680–4,760M-style range cited by Zacks — whereas the EPS raise is clearer (+$0.14 at midpoint). "Raised" is accurate at midpoint for both, not for every bound.
