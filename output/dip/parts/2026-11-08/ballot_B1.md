## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** APH
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam is the highest-quality business in this field bought at the deepest genuine discount: TTM revenue growth 29.6% with Q1 FY27 revenue +54% YoY to a record $8.22bn, a record 40.0% operating margin and 52.4% gross margin, ROE of ~70%, and a record $3.24bn of FCF in a single quarter — all while sitting on ~$3.7bn of net cash. The stock is −53.7% off its 52-week high at 16.4x forward earnings, a multiple that only makes sense if the FY27 EPS step-up (~$9.60) collapses. Management just put its own money where its mouth is with a record $25bn buyback authorisation (~10% of market value) and a 27% dividend hike. This is a great business on sale, not a broken one repriced.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** the single most compelling evidence is that the record quarter printed three weeks before the rout and the December-quarter guide was capped by supply, not demand — CFO Doug Bettinger said the guide "would have been a bit higher if not for some near-term supply chain constraints," with delayed shipments slipping to the March 2027 quarter, not lost. A business whose problem is that it cannot ship fast enough has a price problem, not a business problem.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** low risk. Lam is the etch leader; its tools are qualified into fab process flows over years, a wrong etch step scraps wafers, and customers cannot swap a qualified fleet without re-qualifying. AI increases, not decreases, etch/deposition steps per wafer (taller 3D NAND stacks, GAA transistors, advanced packaging). The credible threat is not AI substitution but a CXMT-driven DRAM glut compressing memory capex — a demand-cycle risk, not a product-substitution risk.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** immediate — the $25bn buyback is already authorised and the stock bounced +8.2% on Fri 6 Nov on it; fundamental — the Q2 FY27 print (~27 Jan 2027) showing December-quarter delivery plus the deferred March-quarter shipments landing, and confirmation of the >$200bn CY2027 WFE outlook.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base case roughly +45–60% (re-rating toward ~$290–320 as the WFE step-up and shipment recovery print, with buyback support underneath); bull case roughly +80–100% toward the $364 mean analyst target if 2027 WFE lands above $200bn and margins hold at record levels.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** the memory supercycle peaks and CXMT's 4F² DRAM scales at yield, flooding commodity DDR5 and cutting memory capex — then the >$200bn 2027 WFE outlook evaporates, FY27 EPS ~$9.60 gets cut ~20%, and 16x forward becomes ~$155, not a rebound. A real, enacted data-center moratorium regime would hit the same way, a year later.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

I would rather own the same cycle through LRCX (no imminent print, buyback already firing) and revisit AMAT after 12 Nov proves the 2027 framework.
