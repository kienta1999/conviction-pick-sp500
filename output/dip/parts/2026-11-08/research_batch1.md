### 2. Moat / AI-irreplaceability
- Lam is the etch-and-deposition specialist: plasma etch and deposition tools (incl. deep etch for 3D NAND stacking and HBM-adjacent memory steps) used to form layers of memory and logic chips. Moat = **process-tech/patents + switching costs + certification lock-in + capital intensity**: tools are qualified into a fab's process flow over years; a wrong etch step scraps wafers; customers (TSMC, Samsung, SK hynix, Micron) cannot swap a qualified etch/deposition fleet without re-qualifying the process. Memory revenue grew +120% YoY in Q1 FY27 — Lam is levered to exactly the HBM/DRAM capacity build AI needs, and more etch/deposition steps per wafer as nodes shrink and stacks get taller.
- **Can AI route around it? No.** AI increases, not decreases, the number of etch/deposition steps (3D NAND layers, GAA transistors, advanced packaging). There is no software substitute for physically etching a wafer. The credible disruption vector is not AI but **Chinese domestic equipment substitution** in mature nodes and CXMT-led DRAM oversupply compressing memory capex — a customer-demand risk, not a product-substitution risk.
- **Irreplaceability score: 9/10.**
