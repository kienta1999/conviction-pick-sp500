The moat is scale manufacturing plus the Cat dealer network, parts/service ecosystem, financing via Cat Financial, brand and resale value, and installed-base switching costs. The only partial threats are Bloom Energy fuel cells competing with CAT gas gensets for data-centre power and electrification/automation over a decade — neither commoditises the franchise today.
