## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: ANET
- Runner-up: FIX
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Arista is the cleanest case in the field where the business got better while the stock fell — Q3 2026 was the best quarter in company history (revenue $3.66bn, +58.5% YoY, the highest growth since its 2014 IPO), yet the stock fell ~22% from its $217.41 record to ~$170 before the 20 Nov rout took it to $167.62, only −0.6% vs its 200-day SMA. The 2027 framework (revenue ~$18.5bn, +38–40%, AI fabrics ~$7.0bn, roughly double 2026, operating margin ~49%) is a doubling of the exact product line the market is supposedly repricing. With zero debt, $14.36bn cash, and a 49.1% non-GAAP operating margin, there is no survival question at all — the only question is the multiple. If the Feb 2027 print lands at or above the $3.9–4.0bn Q4 guide, a 27.8x forward stock on mid-30s growth re-rates fast.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business
- The Q3 print beat Arista's own $3.3bn guide by ~11% and consensus by ~10%, and the Q4 guide ($3.9–4.0bn) was itself *above* the ~$3.6bn consensus — a company guiding up while its stock falls 22% on no company-specific news is a multiple compression, not an impairment.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: can AI or a substitute disrupt it? (low / medium / high risk — and why)
- Medium risk. AI is Arista's demand driver, not its substitute — in large training clusters networking is 20–30% of build cost, and Ethernet is displacing InfiniBand — but the threat is real and named: Nvidia's Spectrum-X overtook Arista in data-centre Ethernet switching in Q2 2026 (20.4% vs 18.7%), and Microsoft + Meta are ~40–42% of revenue, so a single customer shifting volume to bundled Nvidia networking is a genuine concentration risk. EOS switching costs and vendor-neutrality (customers building around non-Nvidia accelerators need open Ethernet) are the defence, but this is a three-way fight, not a monopoly.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: the concrete trigger and rough when
- Q4 2026 print ~9 Feb 2027 (screen date; TipRanks says 15 Feb, unconfirmed) landing at/above the top of the $3.9–4.0bn guide with operating margin ≥48%, plus delivery checkpoints on the 2027 framework (AI fabrics doubling, >10 GW deployed across 10+ accelerator types by end-2027). Any share Nvidia loses in networking after its CEO ouster is a secondary tailwind.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case
- Base: +30–40% (toward the $249.65 analyst mean target, +48.9% upside, as the multiple normalises toward its ~36x 5-year average forward PE on delivered 2027 growth). Bull: +60–70% if the 2027 AI-fabrics doubling lands and a new 10% customer de-concentrates the story.
- Top risk (value-trap case):
- Customer concentration is the trap: if Microsoft or Meta pauses AI-networking spend or shifts volume to Nvidia Spectrum-X, growth falls off a cliff in lumps, and at 27.8x forward earnings with a ~2.4% FCF yield a single guide-down re-rates the stock 20%+ — October/November demonstrated exactly that twice. Gross margin is already drifting down (64.6% → 63.6% YoY) as AI hardware mixes in.
