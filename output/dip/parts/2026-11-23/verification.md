## ANET — Arista Networks

### Claim 1 — Q3 2026 best quarter ever: revenue $3.66bn, +58.5% YoY, beating its own $3.3bn guide by ~11%; non-GAAP operating margin 49.1%
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**
- Source: Arista Q3 2026 earnings release, filed as SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1, 3 Nov 2026 (investors.arista.com / sec.gov): "Delivered total revenues in Q3 2026 of approximately $3.66 billion, exceeding guidance of $3.3 billion while growing 58.5%, the highest year-over-year revenue growth since our IPO." Revenue $3,657.6m vs $2,308.3m in Q3 2025; GAAP and non-GAAP operating margin 44.9% and 49.1% respectively (vs 42.4% / 48.6% a year earlier); non-GAAP diluted EPS $1.20 vs $0.77.
- Arithmetic check: $3.66bn vs $3.3bn guide = +10.9%, i.e. "~11%" as claimed. CEO Jayshree Ullal called it "record profitability" on the same release. "Best quarter ever" is a fair characterisation of the release's own language (highest YoY growth since IPO, record profitability); the hard figures are exact.

### Claim 2 — 2027 framework given 3 Nov 2026: revenue ~$18.5bn (+38–40%), AI fabrics ~$7.0bn (roughly double 2026), operating margin ~49%
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**
- Source: Arista Q3 2026 earnings call, 3 Nov 2026, as transcribed by Motley Fool / Nasdaq and reported by Zacks (4 Nov 2026): "For fiscal year 2027, the revenue growth outlook is between 38% and 40%, equating to approximately $18.5 billion at the midpoint. Gross margin continues to be in the range of 62% to 64% and an operating margin of approximately 49%." AI fabrics "expected to double to approximately $7 billion" from a ~$3.5bn 2026 target; cognitive campus +60% to ~$2bn. All three numbers match the claim exactly.

### Claim 3 — Balance sheet: zero debt, ~$14.36bn cash at Q3 2026
**Verdict: CONFIRMED (with a definitional correction)**
- Source: Arista 10-Q for the quarter ended 30 Sep 2026, as summarised by SEC-API / MarketChameleon / TipRanks (Nov 2026): cash and cash equivalents $2.29bn + marketable securities $12.07bn = **$14.36bn combined**; total debt $0. Multiple sources state "net cash $14.36bn and no debt."
- Correction: the $14.36bn is cash **plus marketable securities**, not cash alone — cash and equivalents were $2.29bn. The "zero debt" and the $14.36bn total are exact; the claim's wording ("~$14.36bn cash") should read "cash and marketable securities."

### Claim 4 — Q4 2026 guide $3.9–4.0bn revenue (above ~$3.6bn consensus) and next earnings ~9 Feb 2027
**Verdict: CONTRADICTED (on the earnings date; the guide itself is confirmed)**
- Guide — CONFIRMED. Source: Arista Q3 release / earnings call, 3 Nov 2026, corroborated by Benzinga via TradingView, FinanceFeeds and Tickeron (3–4 Nov 2026): Q4 revenue guided $3.9–4.0bn, non-GAAP EPS $1.26–1.30, non-GAAP operating margin 48–49%, vs consensus ~$3.6bn revenue / ~$1.15 EPS (LSEG / Newsquawk figures cited by the press).
- Earnings date — CONTRADICTED. No source found supports ~9 Feb 2027. Zacks earnings calendar and MarketBeat both put the estimated Q4 report date at **11 Feb 2027** (after market close, consensus EPS $1.29, revenue ~$3.97bn); TipRanks estimates **15 Feb 2027** (explicitly "TBA, not confirmed"). The date is in any case an estimate — the company has not confirmed it — but the dossier's 9 Feb date matches no provider found. The panel should use ~11 Feb 2027 (Zacks/MarketBeat) with TipRanks' 15 Feb as the outlier, and treat the date as unconfirmed until Arista announces it.

### Claim 5 — Moat claim: Nvidia's Spectrum-X overtook Arista in data-centre Ethernet switching in Q2 2026 (Nvidia 20.4% vs Arista 18.7%, IDC)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED**
- Source: IDC Q2 2026 Ethernet switch tracker, as reported by TIKR (Evercore note, ~16 Nov 2026) and corroborated by The Next Platform's analysis of the same IDC data (20 Sep 2026) and Gaugius' IDC summary: "NVIDIA held 20.4% of the data center Ethernet switching market in the second quarter of 2026, compared with Arista's 18.7%," Cisco 18.2%; datacentre Ethernet revenue $12.3bn, +64.5% YoY; Nvidia's datacentre Ethernet revenue +181.1% YoY vs Arista's +37.6%. The Next Platform confirms the underlying revenues (Nvidia $3.86bn total Ethernet, Arista $2.53bn, +37.6%).
- Caveat: the IDC tracker itself is paywalled; verification rests on three independent secondary reports of the same IDC figures, which agree exactly. The "Spectrum-X" attribution is the press/analyst framing of Nvidia's Ethernet business (Spectrum-X switches for DGX/NVL72 scale-out); IDC's share figures are for branded datacentre Ethernet switching as a whole, not a Spectrum-X-only line.

### Claim 11 — Net cash position (~$1.85bn net cash) and forward PE ~15.5x vs its own 5-year median ~23.6x
**Verdict: CONFIRMED (with definitional notes on both halves)**
- Net cash: the company's Q3 presentation / 10-Q (30 Sep 2026) reports **$2.16bn cash** ($2,158.0m) and **$49.6m of interest-bearing debt** ($5.7m current maturities + $43.8m long-term), i.e. $2.11bn of cash over reported debt. The screen's ~$1.85bn net-cash figure is consistent once lease liabilities are included in "total debt": total debt including leases was $374.4m at 30 Sep 2026, giving net cash of ~$1.78bn (Arincen). So "~$1.85bn net cash" is a fair rounded figure on a total-debt-including-leases basis; on the company's own narrow debt definition the figure is ~$2.11bn. Either way the substance — a large net-cash position — is confirmed.
- Valuation: the **5-year median forward P/E of 23.64x is confirmed** — Zacks, 13 Nov 2026: "FIX trades at 17.9X forward earnings, below the industry average of 18.75X and five-year median of 23.64X." The "~15.5x" forward P/E is provider/basis-dependent: the screen's 15.49x and MarketBeat's 15.66x forward P/E (at the 20 Nov close, $1,042.47) support it, but Zacks' forward-12-month P/E on 13 Nov was **17.9x**. The gap is methodology/vintage (which forward year, which price date), not an error in either cut — but the panel should not cite "15.5x vs 23.6x" as if both numbers came from the same provider on the same basis. On any cut, FIX trades below its own 5-year median; the discount is ~25% on Zacks' basis and ~35% on the screen's basis.
