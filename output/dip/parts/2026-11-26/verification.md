## KEYS — Keysight Technologies ($267.69)

### 1. Why-it's-down cause
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (one sub-element approximate — see note).

- Q4 FY26 reported 19 Nov 2026: Keysight's own press release (investor.keysight.com, 19 Nov 2026) — revenue $1,994m vs $1,419m a year earlier (+41% reported), orders $2,339m vs $1,533m (+53%), non-GAAP EPS $3.61 vs $1.91. FY26 revenue $7,157m (+33%), EPS $11.72 (+64%). The earnings-call transcript (Motley Fool / AlphaStreet, 19 Nov 2026) has management calling it a record fourth quarter and the strongest year in its history; operating margin 35.8%, up 950bp.
- Consensus: MarketBeat (19 Nov 2026) — EPS $3.61 beat the $3.37 consensus; Zacks put the consensus at $3.36, revenue consensus $1.94bn. The $3.37 figure is confirmed within rounding; Zacks' $3.36 is a $0.01 variant.
- The fall: MarketWatch, 20 Nov 2026 — KEYS slid 7.07% to $261.51 on Friday 20 Nov; FinanceCharts price history confirms close $261.51, −7.07% from $281.42. Tickeron confirms the Nasdaq fell 5.28% the same session (S&P 500 −3.70%).
- Peak / drawdown: Finnhub — 52-week high $389.93 on 6 Oct 2026; $267.69 is −31.3% below that high, vs the claimed −31.0% (a 0.3pp rounding difference, immaterial). Tickeron also notes the peak "near $388 in early October" and the $341.35 close on 21 Oct → $261.51 on 20 Nov (−23% in 30 days).
- Implication if not confirmed: n/a — confirmed.

### 2. Rebound catalyst + date
**Verdict: CONFIRMED.**

- FY27 base case ~20% revenue growth: earnings-call transcript (19 Nov 2026) — "our base case assumption for fiscal 2027 is revenue growth of 20%"; Webull/Zacks summaries put guided FY27 revenue at $8.588bn vs $8.187bn consensus (20% on FY26's $7.157bn = $8.588bn — arithmetic checks).
- Q1 FY27 revenue $2.04–2.06bn: call transcript — "we expect revenue in the range of $2.040 billion to $2.060 billion"; Webull notes consensus was $1.925bn (the brief's $1.98bn consensus figure was not independently reproduced — the sources I found cite $1.925bn, so treat $1.98bn as UNVERIFIED, though the guide still beats either number).
- Book-to-bill 1.17: orders $2,339m ÷ revenue $1,994m = 1.173; Atlas Peak Research (Q4 FY26 note) states "approximately 1.17 book-to-bill".
- Capex $275m FY27: call transcript — "We expect capital expenditures to be approximately $275 million".
- Q1 FY27 earnings due Feb 2027: Finnhub lists Q1 FY2027 estimated 2027-02-22; TipRanks lists next earnings 23 Feb 2027. Month confirmed; exact day differs by source (22 vs 23 Feb) — the brief only claims February, so this stands.
- Implication if not confirmed: n/a — confirmed, except the $1.98bn Q1 consensus sub-figure.

### 3. Key valuation claim
**Verdict: CONFIRMED, with a vendor-dependence caveat.**

- Forward P/E ~15.5x: TheMarketGist lists forward P/E 15.14x and 1y target $391.08; TrendMatrix lists P/E (Fwd) 15.8x; RockFlow cites "15.14x forward PE". The ~15.5x figure sits inside this 15.1–15.8x range.
- Caveat: FinanceCharts (25 Nov 2026) reports a forward P/E of 22.42x on a different EPS base — forward P/E for KEYS is vendor-dependent, so the 15.5x figure should be read as the dossier's data-vendor basis, not a universal number.
- Trailing P/E collapse from ~48–60x: FinanceCharts historical P/E table — Mar 2026 ~49–50x, May–Jun 2026 54–60x (e.g. 59.72x on 18 Jun 2026), Sep 2026 ~46–50x, Oct 2026 42–53x, falling to 30.56x on 25 Nov 2026. Beanvest monthly closes corroborate (Mar 2026 50.78x, Jun 2026 57.46x, Sep 2026 49.98x). The "48–60x in Mar/Sep 2026" range is confirmed.
- Mean analyst target $391.08: TrendMatrix — "Analyst consensus $391.08 +43.7%, 12 analysts"; RockFlow — "consensus price target around $391.08… 12 analysts… range $345–$464". Confirmed. Note MarketBeat's consensus is lower ($382.17, 14 analysts) — the $391.08 figure is the 12-analyst set, not every vendor's.
- Implication if not confirmed: n/a — confirmed; only flag the forward-P/E vendor spread (15x vs 22x) when citing cheapness.

### 4. Moat's central factual claim
**Verdict: CONFIRMED in substance; the $1,564m price is CONTRADICTED.**

- Largest pure-play / market shares: Verified Market Research (Feb 2026, "Test & Measurement Equipment 2026") states Keysight holds "a 29.87% market share within the electronic measurement segment" and its comparison table lists Keysight 29.8%, Rohde & Schwarz 14.5%, NI (Emerson) 11.2% — an exact match to the claimed figures. Flow Partners independently calls Keysight the "Largest pure-play electronic test and measurement vendor". Caveat: the share figures trace to a single market-research vendor (VMR), not a company filing — the numbers are confirmed as published, but the source is not a primary one.
- Spirent acquisition closed Oct 2025 after a DOJ suit: CONFIRMED on timing and the suit, CONTRADICTED on price. BusinessWire (15 Oct 2025) — Keysight completed the Spirent acquisition on 15 Oct 2025; aggregate consideration "approximately £1.16 billion (around US $1.46 billion)", not $1,564m. DOJ press release — the Antitrust Division "filed a civil antitrust lawsuit… to block the proposed transaction" and required divestiture of Spirent's high-speed ethernet, network security and RF channel emulation businesses to Viavi; the divestiture completed 16 Oct 2025 for $399m (per Keysight's filing, via iTechFAQs/TheFinanceBase).
- Implication: the $1,564m figure overstates the deal price by ~$100m (~7%); it does not change the moat conclusion (the deal closed, with divestitures), but the dossier's price should be corrected to ~$1.46bn.

**Overall verdict — KEYS: Headline evidence stands.** The record quarter, the 7.07% post-print fall into a −5.28% Nasdaq session, the 20% FY27 guide, the ~15x forward multiple and the $391.08 mean target are all confirmed from primary or near-primary sources. Only corrections: deal price $1.46bn (not $1,564m), Q1 consensus $1.925bn in the sources found (not $1.98bn), and forward P/E is vendor-dependent (15x vs 22x on another base).
