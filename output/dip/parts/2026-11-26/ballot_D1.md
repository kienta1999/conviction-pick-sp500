- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Keysight is the one name in this dossier where the dip looks like pure sentiment repricing on top of accelerating fundamentals, not a business breaking. It reported its strongest quarter in history on 19 Nov 2026 — Q4 FY26 revenue $1.994bn (+41% YoY), orders $2.339bn (+53%), non-GAAP EPS $3.61 vs $3.37 — and still fell 7.07% the next session as the Nasdaq fell 5.28%. Demand exceeds shipment capacity on ~12–24 AI-related SKUs, with a $200–300m excess backlog, so the constraint is Keysight's ability to ship, not customers' willingness to buy. At 15.5x forward earnings with net cash, FY26 FCF of $1.75bn, and FY27 base-case guidance of ~20% revenue growth, the market is pricing a cyclical peak that the order book does not yet show.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is the order book: FY26 orders rose 49% against revenue up 33%, Q4 book-to-bill was 1.17, and the Spirent acquisition removed the closest rival — a demand-led backlog, not a share-loss or pricing-collapse print. The only company-specific negative in the dossier is supply — demand outrunning shipments — which is the opposite of a value-trap signature.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — AI is a demand driver, not a substitute. Keysight is the largest pure-play electronic test & measurement vendor (~29.9% share vs Rohde & Schwarz ~14.5%), with HP/Agilent-lineage precision RF/microwave IP, calibration/certification lock-in, PathWave workflow lock-in, and instrument-specific test programs/fixtures. AI increases the need for test equipment (AI bookings ~2.5x prior year); no AI model can test a 1.6T optical link or certify 6G hardware.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q1 FY27 earnings in Feb 2027 — guided revenue $2.04–2.06bn vs $1.98bn consensus — confirming the ~20% FY27 growth guide and showing supply constraints easing (guided backlog extension under 2 weeks, $275m FY27 capex to expand capacity). A clean Q1 print should re-rate the stock within one quarter; the fuller re-rating follows if the record backlog converts through H1 2027.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: FY27 guidance holds and the multiple recovers partway toward the mean target of $391.08 (+46.1% from $267.69) — roughly +35–45%, to ~$360–390. Bull: supply resolves, orders stay above 1.1x book-to-bill, and the market pays up for a 20% grower at ~15x — roughly +60–70%, to ~$430–455. Downside case is a genuine AI-capex peak cutting test budgets, which would leave a ~15x stock with net cash rather than a leveraged trap.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The AI-infrastructure capex cycle peaks and hyperscaler test budgets are cut just as Keysight expands capacity — the $200–300m excess backlog evaporates, FY27's guided 20% growth becomes a one-off, and the 15.5x multiple proves to be peak-earnings pricing rather than a bargain. Secondary risks: supply constraints persist past guidance, the CFO transition (effective 18 Dec 2026) disrupts execution, and tariff refunds that flattered FY26 earnings do not recur.
