# Panelist B (growth/quality compounder) — Ballot, 2026-11-26

- **Top pick:** KEYS
- **Runner-up:** FIX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Keysight is the purest compounder in this batch bought on a sentiment sale: it reported the strongest quarter in its history on 19 Nov 2026 — revenue $1.994bn (+41% YoY), orders $2.339bn (+53%), non-GAAP EPS $3.61 vs $3.37 consensus — and still fell 7.07% the next session as the Nasdaq fell 5.28%. FY26 revenue grew 33% to $7.157bn with EPS up 64% to $11.72, Q4 operating margin expanded 950bp to 35.8%, and management guided FY27 to ~20% revenue growth ($8.588bn vs $8.187bn consensus) with a 1.17 book-to-bill and record backlog. At 15.5x forward earnings with net cash ($2.916bn cash at 31 Oct 2026) and FY26 FCF of $1.75bn, the market is pricing a cyclical test-equipment vendor while the business is printing software-like margins and consolidating its industry via the Spirent acquisition. If FY27 guidance holds, the re-rating from ~15x toward even a low-20s multiple on ~20% growth is the explosive upside.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the dip happened on the best print in company history: orders grew faster than revenue (+53% vs +41% in Q4), orders were +49% vs revenue +33% for the full year, and the only company-specific negative was that demand exceeds shipment capacity on ~12–24 AI-related SKUs ($200–300m excess backlog) — a supply constraint, not a demand break. The drawdown (-31.0% off the 52-week high, peaked near $388–390 early Oct 2026) tracks the AI-trade unwind, not a lost customer, a lost share position, or a guidance cut.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Keysight is the largest pure-play electronic test & measurement vendor (~29.9% share vs Rohde & Schwarz ~14.5%, NI ~11.2%), with precision RF/microwave and high-speed digital test IP (HP/Agilent lineage), calibration/certification lock-in (5G-Advanced, 6G, 1.6T optical), PathWave software workflow lock-in, and instrument-specific test programs/fixtures. AI increases the need for test equipment (AI bookings ~2.5x prior year) — AI is a demand driver, not a substitute — and the Spirent deal removed its closest rival. The real threat is cyclical (semi/telecom capex) and low-end Chinese competition, not disruption.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q1 FY27 earnings (Feb 2027) is the next proof point: guided revenue $2.04–2.06bn vs $1.98bn consensus, with receivables normalisation and the $275m FY27 capex capacity expansion easing the shipment backlog. A print confirming ~20% growth and a book-to-bill above 1.0 should start the re-rating within one quarter; full re-rating follows through FY27 as the backlog converts.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case ~+35–45% (earnings growth ~20% plus a modest multiple move from 15.5x toward ~18–19x, in line with the $391.08 mean target, +46.1%). Bull case ~+60–75% if FY27 growth holds at or above 20%, margins stay near 35%+, and the multiple re-rates toward the low-20s on sustained AI test demand.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** AI-infrastructure capex peaks and hyperscaler test budgets are cut, turning the record order book into a one-off spike; supply constraints persist past the guided sub-two-week backlog extension; or the tariff refunds that flattered FY26 earnings do not recur and the multiple never recovers from ~15x, leaving a good business flat for years.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
