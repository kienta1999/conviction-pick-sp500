# Ballot C1 — Moat & AI-irreplaceability — 2026-11-26

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** KEYS
- **Runner-up:** CAT
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Keysight is the largest pure-play electronic test and measurement vendor, with ~29.9% share of the electronic-measurement segment versus ~14.5% for the next rival, and it just printed its strongest quarter in history — Q4 FY26 revenue $1.994bn (+41% YoY), orders $2.339bn (+53%), non-GAAP EPS $3.61 versus $3.37 expected — yet fell 31% from its early-October peak on a sentiment unwind in the AI trade. AI does not disrupt Keysight; it multiplies the need for its product, with AI bookings ~2.5x the prior year, because every faster chip, 1.6T optical link and 6G radio must be tested, calibrated and certified before it ships. At ~15.5x forward earnings with FY27 base-case guidance of ~20% revenue growth, net cash of $2.916bn and a record backlog, the market is pricing a cyclical test vendor while the business is behaving like a toll-taker on the AI build-out.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that Keysight reported record revenue, record orders and a 1.17x book-to-bill on 19 November 2026 and still fell 7.07% the next session — on a day the Nasdaq fell 5.28%. The only company-specific negative was that demand exceeds shipment capacity on ~12–24 AI-related SKUs ($200–300m excess backlog), i.e. too much demand, not too little. There was no miss, no guidance cut and no share loss; the Spirent acquisition even removed the closest rival.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. AI is a demand tailwind, not a substitute: precision RF/microwave and high-speed digital test IP (HP/Agilent lineage), calibration and certification lock-in for 5G-Advanced, 6G and 1.6T optical, PathWave software workflow lock-in, and instrument-specific test programs and fixtures create switching costs no model can code around. No AI system can certify a physical radio or optical transceiver without the instrument. The residual risks are cyclical (semi/telecom capex) and low-end Chinese competition, not displacement.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q1 FY27 earnings (February 2027) confirming the guided ~20% FY27 revenue growth ($8.588bn base case versus $8.187bn consensus) and Q1 revenue of $2.04–2.06bn versus $1.98bn consensus, with the $275m FY27 capacity expansion easing the shipment backlog. Any stabilisation in AI-infra sentiment re-rates the multiple first, well before February.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case ~+40–50%: FY27 guidance holds, earnings grow ~20%, and the forward multiple recovers only part-way from 15.5x toward ~20x, roughly in line with the $391.08 mean target (+46.1%). Bull case ~+70–90%: capacity expansion clears the backlog, AI test demand stays at 2.5x, and the multiple re-rates toward its pre-drawdown range on sustained 20%+ growth.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The AI-infra capex cycle peaks and hyperscaler test budgets are cut just as Keysight expands capacity — then the record backlog proves to be a pull-forward, FY27 growth disappoints, supply constraints persist past the guided sub-two-week backlog extension, and a 15x multiple on falling estimates is no bargain at all.
