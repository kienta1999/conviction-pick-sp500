**Single-pick ballot:**
- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLAC is the purest quality compounder in this field: the dominant process-control monopolist (irreplaceability 10/10) that just reported a beat-and-guide-up — Q1 FY2027 revenue $4.14bn, +28.9% YoY, record operating margin 46.1% — and still fell ~54% from its 52-week high. Smaller nodes, 3D architectures and advanced packaging (on track ~$1.1bn in calendar 2026, +70%+ YoY) multiply the value of defect detection, so the AI buildout makes KLA more essential, not less. At 19.5x forward earnings, with record Q1 free cash flow of $1.34bn and $8.81bn of buyback authorisation remaining, the market is pricing a cyclical peak, not the franchise. This is a great business on sale, not a business in decline.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that KLA beat, raised its guide (Q2 FY2027 to $4.70bn ±$200m revenue and ~$1.44 non-GAAP EPS, both ahead of expectations) and raised its 2026 WFE outlook — and the stock still fell 5.69% after the print. Analysts trimmed targets (UBS to $180, Needham to $200) rather than cutting estimates: a multiple compression, not a demand or share loss. No company-specific miss, no guidance cut, no lost customer is in the dossier.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. KLA holds ~50–58% of process control, >85% in optical wafer inspection, essentially a duopoly/monopoly in reticle inspection, with an installed base of >50,000 tools, qualification lock-in (a fab cannot swap an inspection step without re-qualifying yield), and ROIC of ~34–44% in each of the five years to FY2026. AI cannot inspect wafers by itself; it needs KLA's optics, and a single defect can scrap a $30,000 chip. The threat is geopolitical (China ~31% of TTM revenue, export controls), not substitution.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q2 FY2027 delivery against the already-raised $4.70bn guide at the next print (late Jan / early Feb 2027; brief says 28 Jan 2027), plus confirmation of calendar-2027 WFE >$200bn. No single product catalyst is needed — execution against the raised guide plus multiple normalisation once the AI-valuation panic fades is the path.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +30–45% (partial multiple re-expansion toward its median on delivered earnings, to roughly $180–200). Bull case roughly +59% to the analyst mean target of $219.62, if the Jan/Feb print confirms the guide and WFE >$200bn holds. Both are rough, anchored to the dossier's target; no price is guaranteed.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** WFE is cyclical — if the 2027 >$200bn forecast is cut, the record quarter is the peak and the "cheap" 19.5x forward P/E is an earnings peak in disguise. China exposure (~31% of TTM revenue) and the June 2026 run-up (including the 10-for-1 split peak) mean the trailing ~42x multiple may simply have been a bubble that never re-rates.
