## ISRG — Intuitive Surgical (tentative rank 1)

### ISRG-1. Q2 2026 beat on revenue (+~19% YoY) and adjusted EPS (~$2.80 vs ~$2.51 consensus), yet the stock fell ~10.7% in a single day because guidance was merely reaffirmed
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (minor rounding/wording nuances)

- Revenue $2.89B, +19% YoY vs Q2 2025 ($2.44B); adjusted (non-GAAP) net income $1.00B / **$2.80 diluted EPS** — Intuitive Surgical Q2 2026 results, reported via TradingView News, July 16, 2026.
- Consensus check: Zacks (July 16, 2026) reports EPS $2.80 vs Zacks Consensus **$2.48** (+12.9% surprise); Motley Fool (July 18, 2026) says Wall Street called for **$2.50**. The dossier's "~$2.51" sits inside this range; exact consensus varies by source (Zacks $2.48, others ~$2.50).
- Revenue surprise: Zacks (July 16, 2026) — $2.89B vs $2.81B consensus (+3.08%); YoY +18.5% per Zacks vs "up 19%" per the company headline. ~19% is fair rounding.
- Drop: Hargreaves Lansdown (July 16, 2026) — "The shares fell **10.8% in after-hours trading**." Reported July 16, 2026 after market close; the selloff was the immediate reaction to the print.
- Guidance: HL confirms management **kept full-year procedure growth guidance at 13.5–15.5%** (not raised), expecting outcome near the 14.5% midpoint; Fool (July 18, 2026): "By besting expectations in the second quarter and not raising its full-year guidance, Intuitive is essentially forecasting slower growth for the rest of the year." Multiple price target cuts followed.

### ISRG-2. GLP-1 drugs affect less than 3% of ISRG's procedure volume
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (with scope nuance)

- Zacks (Dec 11, 2025): "Management confirmed that **bariatric now represents less than 3% of U.S. da Vinci procedures**," after six consecutive quarters of high-single-digit U.S. bariatric declines tied to GLP-1 adoption.
- Nuance: the <3% figure is specifically **U.S. da Vinci procedures**, not total global procedure volume (which includes Ion and international procedures — where bariatric is even smaller as a share). The dossier's broader phrasing is directionally accurate but slightly imprecise.

### ISRG-3. Roughly $8.6B in net cash (no meaningful debt) as of mid-2026
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (with gross-vs-net nuance)

- Intuitive Q2 2026 results (July 16, 2026): **$8,625.5M** in cash, cash equivalents and investments as of June 30, 2026. The dossier's "$8.6B" is this gross-cash figure.
- Debt: Simply Wall St (health check, current) lists debt at $0.0 — no interest-bearing debt. However, the balance sheet carries ~$656M in long-term liabilities (MarketBeat financials, Q2 2026; stockanalysis.com balance sheet). True *net* cash ≈ $7.97B. "No meaningful debt" holds; "$8.6B net cash" slightly overstates net (it is gross).
- Supplementary: Q2 operating cash flow $1.06B, +48.3% YoY (MarketBeat financials).

### ISRG-4. Forward P/E compressed from ~50x+ to ~31-32x during the drawdown
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (in substance)

- Finnhub (Sep 17, 2026): ISRG current price **$383.54**; 52-week high **$603.88**. Zacks (July 14, 2026, pre-Q2): 2026 EPS estimate $10.41, 2027 estimate $11.70 (post-Q2 estimates likely modestly higher). At the 52-week high, forward P/E ≈ 52x (603.88 / ~11.6); at current price, forward P/E ≈ 32–33x (383.54 / ~11.7–12). The "~50x+ → ~31-32x" compression is directionally confirmed.

## SYK — Stryker Corporation (tentative rank 2)

### SYK-1. March 2026 "Handala" cyberattack disrupted 200k+ systems and, together with vascular product backorders, caused Stryker's Q2 2026 revenue shortfall — inability to ship, not lost demand
**Verdict: CONTRADICTED as stated** (the "Q2 revenue shortfall caused by cyberattack" framing fails; the vascular-backorders half is confirmed)

- The attack happened: March 11, 2026, claimed by Iran-linked Handala; disrupted ordering, processing, shipping and manufacturing (TechTarget, Mar 20, 2026).
- "200k+ systems" is **Handala's own claim**, not independently verified. A source familiar with the investigation told BleepingComputer (cited in a LinkedIn security review, Mar 18, 2026) the actual number was **~80,000 devices**. Stryker itself stated there was no malware or ransomware and the incident was contained to its internal Microsoft environment.
- Q2 2026 was NOT a headline revenue shortfall: Morningstar/Dow Jones (July 30, 2026) — Q2 sales **$6.59B beat analyst expectations of $6.58B**; adjusted EPS $3.69 beat $3.49. StockStory noted organic revenue +9% "missed" (organic consensus), but the quarter was characterized by management and press as a recovery quarter, not a shortfall.
- Timing error: Stryker's 8-K/A (April 9, 2026, via MassDevice) said the cyberattack had a **material impact on Q1 2026** results but "no material impact" expected on 2026 full-year guidance; by Q2 the company was "fully operational" and ramping production.
- The vascular half is confirmed: CEO Kevin Lobo on the Q2 2026 call (transcript via Motley Fool/MarketBeat) — "supply disruptions in our Peripheral Vascular business... resulted in a **meaningful backorder situation with lost sales** in the quarter"; backorders expected manageable by end of Q3. That is inability-to-ship, not demand loss — but it is a manufacturing/supply issue, not the cyberattack.

### SYK-2. Stryker guided that the elevated backlog converts in H2 2026, and raised (or at least maintained) full-year guidance
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (with the "raised" being a midpoint nudge, plus noted skepticism)

- Q2 2026 report (July 30, 2026): full-year organic net sales growth **narrowed to 8.3%–9.3%** (from 8%–9.5%); full-year adjusted EPS **narrowed to $14.95–$15.10** (from $14.90–$15.10) — low end and midpoint raised slightly (StockStory: midpoint to $15.02). Sales midpoint 8.75 → 8.8.
- Backlog conversion: leadership pointed to "order backlog, procedural stability and increased manufacturing output" for H2 confidence (MassDevice, July 30, 2026); Zacks (Aug 13, 2026): "Stryker must convert elevated backlog" into shipments in H2.
- Counterpoint to log: BTIG (cited by MassDevice and Zacks, early Aug 2026) — the implied H2 step-up "is significant," requiring double-digit organic growth after ~5.7% in 1H26; "investors may remain skeptical."

### SYK-3. Forward P/E (~16.7x) at or near a multi-year low vs its own history
**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (number is current post-selloff; "multi-year low" supported)

- Zacks (Aug 13, 2026): "SYK trades at **20.4X forward 12-month earnings, below its five-year median of 26.0X**" — already at a historic discount to own history before the latest leg down.
- Current: SYK fell to a 52-week low of $270.01 on Sep 10, 2026 (Trefis, Sep 11, 2026; Finnhub Sep 17, 2026: 52-week low $267, current $280.13, 1-yr return -26.9%). At ~$270 with NTM adjusted EPS ~$16.2, forward P/E ≈ **16.7x** — consistent with the dossier's figure and below the five-year median.
- Peer context (Zacks, Aug 13, 2026): sub-industry trades at 16.9x — SYK's discount to history is real, but it is not cheap vs its industry.

### SYK — material developments in the last 30 days (weigh on temporary-vs-permanent verdict)
- **Selloff continued after Q2**: 8-day losing streak, -18%, to the 52-week low $270.01 (Trefis, Sep 11, 2026); -22.4% over the trailing month.
- **Analyst target cuts** (MarketBeat, Sep 15, 2026): Wells Fargo $418→$348, Citigroup $399→$360, BMO $317 — ratings kept at Buy/Overweight, but targets cut after the quarter.
- **Estimate revisions negative**: Zacks (Aug 26, 2026) — Q3 consensus cut from $3.73 to $3.63; seven analysts revised down over 60 days.
- **Insider selling**: $118.2M of insider sales in the last 90 days (MarketBeat, Sep 15, 2026); GuruFocus also flagged $100.7M of insider selling with no buying.
- Net: the dossier's "temporary" thesis (demand intact, backlog converts) is management's stated view, but the market, analysts, and insiders are all signaling execution risk. The panel should not present SYK as a clean consensus dip — it is a show-me story.

## Cross-cutting flags

1. **SYK claim 1 must be rewritten before publication.** The dossier's framing — cyberattack + vascular backorders caused a Q2 *revenue shortfall* — is wrong on two counts: (a) headline Q2 revenue *beat* ($6.59B vs $6.58B), and (b) the cyberattack's material financial impact hit Q1, not Q2. Only the vascular-backorders/lost-sales half is accurate. The 200k+ figure is the attacker's unverified claim (~80k per independent sourcing).
2. **No fatal contradiction kills any of the three picks**, but SYK's post-Q2 price action (continued -18% slide to 52-week lows, target cuts, estimate cuts, $118M insider selling) materially tempers the "temporary" verdict — the panel should disclose the show-me H2 execution risk (BTIG: H2 needs double-digit organic growth).
3. **ISRG claims are the cleanest** of the three; the dossier is accurate on the beat, the drop, GLP-1 exposure, and balance sheet, with only rounding/scope nuances.
4. **KLAC claims verified**, but the panel should note the August China-restriction selloff, the Sep 14 AI-capex scare, and the CEO's $17.4M August share sale as sentiment overhangs, plus that TTM valuation (~45–49x) is elevated, so the "dip" is a multiple-compression story on still-strong fundamentals.
