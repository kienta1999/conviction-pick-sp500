# Panelist C ballot — Moat & AI-irreplaceability lens — 2026-09-17

Through this lens the veto is simple: any name AI or a substitute could plausibly eat gets rejected no matter how deep the dip. I favor switching costs, network effects, process-tech/patents, certification lock-in, brand, and capital intensity that AI cannot erode — and I treat "dip reveals moat erosion" as a second veto (LVS's repeating Macau margin compression, Kate Spade's brand decay, VRSK's AI-elongated sales cycles are all marks against, not neutral).

=== BALLOT 1: SINGLE-PICK NOMINATION (exact structure) ===

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** ISRG
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLA is a near-monopoly in a physical inspection step that every advanced chip must pass through — ~73.8% of the global metrology & inspection market, ~7x its nearest rival, and *gaining* share (+360 bps since 2021). The -44% drawdown is priced on China export-control fear and AI-capex-cooling sentiment, while the business is compounding FCF at ~20% CAGR, growing services at ~$775M/quarter recurring, and forecasting FY27 EPS +23.5% (to ~$43.96). AI cannot route around physical defect inspection — it *raises* process-control intensity (GAA, hybrid bonding, HBM), and KLA raised its advanced-packaging process-control outlook from ~$635M (2025) to ~$1B (2026). This is a dominant franchise de-rated on geopolitics, not competition, with explosive operating leverage when the WFE cycle re-accelerates into 2027.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** the single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW disruption risk — ~73.8% share in metrology/inspection (up from ~50% in 2010), process-tech + switching costs + a decades-deep defect-classification data moat that makes KLA the reference platform fabs won't break compatibility with, plus a recurring services floor (~24% of revenue). AI is a beneficiary, not a threat: chip complexity increases inspection intensity. The only share nibble is Onto Innovation's standalone-OCD Atlas V — a niche, not a moat breach. No model can replace physical defect inspection.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** H2-2026 WFE re-acceleration (management signaled revenue growth continuing into 2027; WFE >$140B expected in 2026); the dated checkpoint is **Q1 FY27 earnings on 2026-10-28**, where any WFE-upturn evidence re-rates the name. China export-control clarity is pure upside optionality (guided impact $300–350M through 2026, already priced in).
- **12-18 month return scenario:** base **~+35%** (to ~$228, near the panel's mean analyst target of $233.77, +38%); bull **~+60%** if FY27 EPS (+23.5% to $43.96) compounds and the multiple re-expands from today's trough ~25–30x forward toward KLA's historical premium.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** export controls expand further (China revenue loss permanently larger than $300–350M); a global memory downturn delays the H2-2026 turn; AI capex pivots to inference with lower fab intensity; and at ~25–30x forward there is little room if FY27 growth disappoints.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

*(Note on the above "why the dip is temporary" field: the single most compelling evidence is share expansion — KLA has GAINED 360 bps of process-control share since 2021 per Zacks, including during the selloff window; a genuinely impaired business loses share. The selloff drivers are geopolitics (BIS export controls, $300–350M guided China impact) and cyclicality, with FY2026 FCF still $3.77B and the dividend raised 21% — a franchise returning >90% of FCF is not a business in distress.)*
