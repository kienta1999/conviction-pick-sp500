### 6. Category position
Still #1 in professional creative software, document (PDF/Acrobat) and a top-tier CX/marketing suite, with 97% subscription revenue — but it is **losing the growth edge and the entry-level**: Canva owns prosumer/new creators, AI natives own generation-first workflows, Adobe Stock is being cannibalised by its own Firefly. Enterprise pricing power holds (indemnification, workflow lock-in); prosumer pricing power does not — >35% of Digital Media revenue is prosumer/small-agency, the most price-sensitive, lowest-switching-cost segment (Medium/Gilad Barilan research, Sep 2026).

### 7. Value-trap risk — honest bear case
The strongest bear case in the batch (Morgan Stanley's Adam Wood, via DailyStocks summary): three simultaneous transitions — freemium pivot, leadership vacuum/turnover, AI-spend pivot — compound rather than add. If ARR growth drifts to single digits, ~10x can persist or compress further without any earnings collapse (QuantAbundance). Add: discontinued net-new creative ARR disclosure (less transparency exactly when scrutiny is highest), a CEO whose expertise is not in the threatened business, an interim CFO, and a new-CEO bar-reset risk at the Dec print. Cheap + decelerating + disrupted is the classic trap signature; the $10.6B FCF and buyback are the mitigants, not a refutation.

### 8. Rebound score: 5/10
**Verdict: Statistically the cheapest name here (~9-10x, 10%+ FCF yield, fortress balance sheet) but with the weakest catalyst, a -38% net-new-ARR trend and a genuine AI seat-compression threat — a value candidate, not yet a dip-rebound candidate.**
