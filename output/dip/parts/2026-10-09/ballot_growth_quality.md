## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** IDXX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the textbook "great business bought on sale": 12,000+ da Vinci systems across 70+ countries, ~75% recurring revenue from instruments/accessories/service, gross margin guidance raised to 68–69%, debt-free with $5.2B+ cash and ~$3.2B annual FCF — and a 55x→35x forward-P/E derating happened *while* fundamentals kept beating (Q2 CY2026 revenue +18.5%, EPS beat +11.9%). Procedure growth is still guided at 13.5–15.5% for FY26, da Vinci 5 has cleared 1,700 installs, Ion procedures grew 36%, and international grew 20% — the growth story is dented by sentiment, not broken. Every prior 30%+ ISRG drawdown has been a buying opportunity; this one has the same anatomy.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is the disconnect between the multiple and the numbers: management *beat* four straight quarters while the multiple fell from ~55x to ~35x forward. The selloff drivers — GLP-1 bariatric softness (one procedure category, confirmed only as high-single-digit bariatric volume decline in Q2), China softness, and Hugo/Ottava's *first* U.S. cases — are future competitive risks, not present share loss; no data shows a rival taking a procedure from da Vinci. That's a price event on a still-growing franchise, not a broken one.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. AI cannot bypass the FDA, the surgeon training ecosystem (the installed skill base is the network effect), or hospital capital integration. The only genuine competitive threat is J&J's Ottava and Medtronic's Hugo — real companies with real robots, but they must climb a decade-long regulatory/training curve against 20M+ lifetime da Vinci procedures. AI is a tailwind (imaging, planning), not a substitute.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings Oct 20, 2026 — the fifth straight beat with da Vinci 5 ramp and Ion growth would re-rate the story; Barclays' Oct 7 Strong-Buy initiation ($485 PT) and the cluster of October buys (Piper $470, Citi $500, RBC $575) show the Street is already re-engaging. Over 2–3 quarters, procedure-growth stabilization above 13% plus international momentum clears the GLP-1/competition narrative.
- **Base/bull 12-18mo scenarios:** Base ~$485 (+17%): Q3 beat confirms the guide, multiple holds ~35x on $13.50+ FY27 EPS — in line with Barclays/Piper/Citi targets. Bull ~$575–600 (+38–44%): procedure growth re-accelerates into the mid-teens, Hugo/Ottava rollout proves slower than feared, and the multiple re-rates partway toward the historical ~50x+ average on derisked long-term growth (RBC's $575 implies this path). Honest caveat: 35x is still a full multiple, so this is quality-on-sale, not deep value.
- **Key risks:** If J&J's Ottava or Medtronic's Hugo scale faster than the training curve suggests, the competitive premium erodes structurally; GLP-1 drugs could permanently remove the bariatric growth driver rather than dent it; China could be lost to subsidized local OEMs; ACA subsidy expirations may crimp U.S. procedure volumes; and at 35x forward, any further procedure-growth deceleration (below the 13.5% guide) gets punished hard. Insider selling and ~8.1M shares short flag fragile sentiment.
