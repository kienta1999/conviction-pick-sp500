# Panelist C — Moat & AI-irreplaceability — Run date 2026-11-06

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: ANET
- Runner-up: HWM
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Arista is the one name in this field whose moat is software, not just scale — EOS runs a single operating system across cloud, AI fabrics, campus and routing, and hyperscalers qualify vendors over multi-year cycles and rarely switch mid-build. Q3 2026 was the fastest revenue growth since its 2014 IPO (+58.5% to $3.66bn, non-GAAP operating margin 49.1%), with FY2027 guidance raised to ~$18.5bn (+38–40%) — yet the stock fell 10.8% on 5 Nov and sits 27% off its high on pure AI-politics sentiment. AI does not threaten Arista; every GPU cluster needs its high-speed Ethernet plumbing, and the 800G switch portfolio is the bottleneck hardware for AI fabrics. With $14.36bn cash, zero debt and a ~30x forward P/E that has de-rated from ~53x trailing a year ago while earnings nearly doubled, this is a blowout business repriced as if its moat were cracking — it isn't.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business
- The Q3 print landed 3 Nov — revenue beat its own $3.3bn guide by over $350m, EPS beat, and management raised FY2027 guidance to ~$18.5bn (vs $16.54bn consensus) — and the stock still fell 10.8% two days later on the midterm/data-center-moratorium rout, with no company-specific negative event in the quarter. A business does not print its fastest growth since IPO and raise guidance the week its moat breaks.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: can AI or a substitute disrupt it? (low / medium / high risk — and why)
- Low risk. AI is the demand driver, not the disruptor: every AI accelerator cluster needs high-speed Ethernet, and Arista's moat is the EOS software/telemetry stack and installed base, not the Broadcom chip underneath. The genuine threats are second-order — Nvidia bundling Spectrum-X with its own GPUs (Nvidia took ~20.4% data-center Ethernet share vs Arista ~18.7% in Q2 2026) and white-box/SONiC commoditisation of the hardware layer — but neither displaces a qualified, software-locked-in fabric, and Arista still grew 58.5% while Nvidia rose. No model can write, qualify and operate a hyperscaler's network fabric.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: the concrete trigger and rough when
- Q4 2026 print (~Feb 2027) against the $3.9–4.0bn revenue guide and confirmation of the ~$18.5bn FY2027 outlook; nearer-term, stabilisation in the AI tape as the midterm moratorium noise fades without any enacted law (Congress does not take power until January) lets the multiple re-rate toward the raised guide. Timing: 1–3 months for sentiment reset, Feb 2027 print as the hard confirmation.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case
- Base: ~+40–55% (toward the $240–245 analyst mean target, +51–54% vs the $159.16 close) as the FY2027 guide is delivered and the forward multiple holds ~30x on ~$6 FY2027 EPS. Bull: ~+80% (toward the $290 top of the analyst range) if AI networking demand stays at the Q3 pace and the market restores a premium multiple for 40% growth.
- Top risk (value-trap case):
- Hyperscaler concentration plus a real capex pause: if data-center moratoria or a capex ROI reckoning actually delays builds, a +58% grower decelerates violently and a 30x forward multiple compresses further; Nvidia's vertical integration could also cap Arista's share in AI fabrics, the fastest-growing slice. Margin normalisation has already begun (non-GAAP gross margin 64.0% vs 65.2% a year ago).

**3.
