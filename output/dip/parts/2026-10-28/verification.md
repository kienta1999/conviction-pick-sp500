### 3. Balance sheet at Q3-end: liquid assets ~$14.5B vs total debt ~$6.5B (net cash); China 28% of Q3 revenue, down from 35% a year earlier — **CONFIRMED**

- Source (primary): Same Q3 Exhibit 99.1, Unaudited Consolidated Condensed Balance Sheets at July 26, 2026 (vs Oct. 26, 2025):
  - Cash and cash equivalents $7,037M; Short-term investments $2,196M; Long-term investments $5,268M.   - Short-term debt $1,299M; Long-term debt $5,245M. Sum = **$6,544M ≈ $6.5B**. Implied net cash ≈ $14,501M − $6,544M = ~$7,957M (~$8.0B) on the broad definition.
  - Note: a stray secondary source cited in the dossier research ("$17.9B total debt") is contradicted by this balance sheet — $17,896M is **total liabilities**, not debt. The dossier correctly disregarded it.
- China mix — CONFIRMED (primary): Same release, "Additional Information — Revenue by Geography": China $2,506M, **28% of total** in Q3 FY2026 vs $2,548M, **35% of total** in Q3 FY2025.
