## BALLOT 1 — Single-pick

- **Top pick:** AMAT
- **Runner-up:** CPRT
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Applied Materials is the broadest, hardest-to-copy franchise in the set — dominant across deposition, CMP, ion implant, process diagnostics, DRAM/HBM and advanced packaging, with qualification lock-in measured in process nodes and an installed-base services business attached. AI cannot substitute for its physical tools; AI is the demand driver, and the segments AMAT leads are expected to drive ~80% of WFE growth in 2026–27, with advanced packaging alone guided up >70% this year. The stock has round-tripped from ~49× to 21.4× forward — its 5-year average — while consensus EPS is still forecast to grow ~+36% in FY26 and ~+45% in FY27. That combination of a 9/10 moat, a net-cash balance sheet and an unrevised-up earnings path is the most explosive irreplaceability dip here.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** It fell for *not giving a number*, not for missing one — at SEMICON West on 2026-10-13 management withheld a specific WFE forecast, triggering −24% in eight sessions, weeks after a record fiscal Q3 ($9.12B revenue, +24.9% YoY, EPS $3.50 beating $3.40, 13th straight quarter of gross-margin expansion) and a fiscal Q4 guide whose midpoint sat above consensus.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk — no model or substitute technology deposits, etches or packages a wafer; rivals LRCX/KLAC contest only parts of the portfolio and nobody replicates the breadth, and management is targeting +2 points of WFE share by 2030. The real chink is China export controls (revenue share already 35% → 28%), which is policy, not technological disruption.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Fiscal Q4 print on 2026-11-12 — delivery against the ~$10.25B ± $0.5B guide plus any quantified 2027 WFE commentary, the exact item withheld on 10-13, should close the disclosure gap; Lam's marker of >$200B 2027 WFE demand frames the upside.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+35–45% (re-rating toward the mid-$500s as FY27 EPS of ~$18.49 is delivered); bull ~+59%+ to the $630.90 mean analyst target, more if the FY28 ~$23.65 EPS path holds.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** WFE cyclicality — if AI capex digests in 2027, orders can fall 30%+ and 21× forward becomes 21× peak earnings; the withheld forecast may also reflect genuine cleanroom/power bottlenecks that cap shippable 2027 revenue regardless of chip demand.
