## Bottom line

**Which rendition is reliable: the alternative, on every material figure checked.** The disk rendition is a stale-vintage composite: KLAC's figures are the Q4 FY2025 pre-split release (31 Jul 2025) relabelled as the June 2026 quarter; VRT's guide is FY2025 actuals / FY2025 guidance relabelled as FY2026, its backlog figure is Q4 2025, its book-to-bill is Q3 2025, and its "Q3 2025 miss" is the Q2 2025 margin event moved by a quarter; IDXX's revenue, recurring-growth, and guide ranges appear in no IDEXX release. The disk is also internally inconsistent — `research_batch1.md` in this same parts folder already carried the correct split, KLAC Q4 FY2026, and VRT FY2026 figures, while ballots A1/C1 and `verification.md` used the stale set. `verification.md`'s verdict of "all 11 claims CONFIRMED, none contradicted, no re-adjudication required" is overturned: of the disputed load-bearing figures, only the three earnings dates and VRT's FCF midpoint survive as stated.

**The IDXX-vs-KLAC contest (IDXX #1, Borda 31; KLAC #2, Borda 29): the outcome survives, and the correction widens IDXX's valuation edge while narrowing KLAC's fundamental gap.** KLAC's correction cuts both ways: its fundamentals are stronger than the disk credited (revenue $3.66B growing +15.2%, a September-quarter guide stepping up to $4.0B, an Oct 28 catalyst against that raised guide — not the flat $3.15B guide ballot C1 used), but its headline valuation argument collapses (forward P/E is ~37.7x at $174.46, not a "cyclical-low" 25.9x, and Q4 FCF was $817M, not a record $1.47B). The adjudication rationale actually used for IDXX #1 — best average ballot placement, no lens veto, a dated near-term catalyst — does not depend on KLAC's stale figures, so the ranking stands; on corrected numbers KLAC is a fundamentally stronger but materially more expensive #2 than the panel believed.

**The IDXX pick's load-bearing figures: the thesis survives, the stated numbers do not.** Corrected, Q2 2026 still shows double-digit recurring growth (CAG Diagnostics recurring +11% reported / +10% organic on revenue of $1,217M, +10%) and a guidance raise (EPS midpoint +$0.14 to $14.69–14.94; revenue range narrowed to $4,700–4,745M, midpoint +$5M), with the 2 Nov 2026 print date exact. So the pick's direction — franchise intact, guide raised mid-dip, recurring base compounding — holds on primary sources. But every load-bearing number printed in `final_pick_2026-10-16.md` and `final_ranking_2026-10-16.md` is wrong and overstates the raise: revenue $1.173B (actual $1,217M), growth +11% / recurring +12% (actual +10% / +11%), guide $4.74–4.80B / $14.62–15.12 (actual $4.70–4.745B / $14.69–14.94; the disk's top ends were never guided). The scenario/EV block is built on price ($547.18) and the analyst mean target, not on the wrong guide ranges, so the EV arithmetic itself is unaffected — but the pick as published cites a larger, cleaner guide raise than IDEXX actually delivered, and it should not be relied on until those figures are corrected.
