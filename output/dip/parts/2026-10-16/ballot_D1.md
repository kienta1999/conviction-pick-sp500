# Ballot D1 — Falling-knife / value-trap skeptic

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** HSY
- **Runner-up:** IDXX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Hershey is the one name in this field where the dip's cause is not a matter of interpretation: a commodity input spiked to ~$12,000/t (Dec 2024–Jan 2025) and has since collapsed back toward ~$5,000–6,000/t, and that reversal is already inside the hedge book — the margin recovery is dated, mechanical, and guided (Q2 2026 reiterated the rebuild through 2026–27 as hedges roll). Everything else in this panel asks me to believe a narrative (backlogs convert, headlines fade, deals clarify); Hershey asks me to believe arithmetic. The franchise never wobbled: #1 US chocolate at ~30%+ share, double-digit cumulative pricing absorbed in 2024–25, AI-irreplaceability 8/10 because no model can eat a Reese's. At 17.1× forward against a 20–28× own-history range, the multiple already prices volume pessimism, and the balance sheet (~1.9× net debt/EBITDA) is uncomfortable only by staple standards, with FCF recovering mechanically as cocoa costs fall.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is the cocoa futures curve itself: the cost that crushed margins has halved from its peak, and because HSY hedges, that tailwind is contractually scheduled to arrive in COGS through 2026–27 — a temporary input cycle with a visible end date, not a demand verdict.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low disruption risk — brand plus shelf-space plus seasonal dominance; AI cannot disrupt a physical impulse purchase. The genuine erosion vectors are private label and health trends, which are slow.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Gross-margin inflection proof at the Q3 print 29 Oct 2026, then Halloween/Christmas volume showcase in Q4; re-rate window 2–3 quarters of margin delivery.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base toward the analyst mean ~$202 (+19.5%) as margins rebuild; bull +30–40% if volumes stabilize and the multiple re-rates toward the low-20s on recovered EPS.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** GLP-1 structurally shrinking US chocolate volumes while cocoa re-spikes — a melting ice cube at 17×; plus the possibility that 2024–25 pricing permanently traded consumers down to private label.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
