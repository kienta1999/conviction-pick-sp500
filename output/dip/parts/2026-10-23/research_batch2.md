### 8. Rebound score: 8/10
**Verdict:** The only name in the batch priced at a market multiple while growing like a hyper-growth name — the dip is a capex-fear discount on the AI *beneficiary* with the strongest catalyst (Oct 28) and target corroboration, provided you underwrite operating earnings, not SpaceX-inflated GAAP EPS.
