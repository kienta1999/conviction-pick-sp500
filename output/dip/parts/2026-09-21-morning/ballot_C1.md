## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** IDXX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the single most irreplaceable franchise in this batch: 11,710 installed da Vinci systems, surgeons trained on its platform, hospital workflows and accreditation built around it, and ~75% recurring revenue from instruments, accessories, and service. The 33.7% drawdown is a forward-expectations de-rating — full-year procedure-growth guidance held at the 13.5–15.5% midpoint while the quarter itself beat on revenue (+19%), EPS, and margins — not a franchise break. A 40% multiple derating (54x → 32.5x forward vs its own history) on intact operations is exactly the moat-investor entry point.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The quarter that triggered the selloff was operationally strong — revenue $2.89B (+19% y/y, beat), non-GAAP EPS $2.80 vs $2.51 expected, operating margin 33.6% up from 30.5%, 468 systems placed (+18%) — and management raised the 2026 pro-forma gross margin guide to 68–69% while holding procedure-growth guidance; nothing in the print suggests the installed base or recurring stream is impaired.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW risk. No substitute for robotic surgery exists or is credibly near; AI augments the platform (My Intuitive Plus, AI-powered case insights) rather than displacing it. Chinese low-end competitors and hospital cost pressure are pricing questions at the margin, not franchise displacement — Intuitive is answering with XiR, refurbished systems, and the Extended Use Program.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 2026 earnings on 2026-10-20 — any procedure-growth beat vs the held 13.5–15.5% guide, plus the da Vinci 5 rollout (246 installs in Q2, new cardiac clearances) and margin relief already in the raised guide, re-rates the stock. International procedures growing ~20% is the bright spot.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +25–35% — procedure growth beats the held guide, tariff relief flows through, and the multiple partially repairs from ~32x toward historical ~40x+ on 2027 EPS growth. Bull +50%+ — da Vinci 5 ramp accelerates placements, the Extended Use Program lowers per-procedure cost and wins back price-sensitive hospitals, and the stock reclaims the analyst mean target band (~$501–509, +28–30% from here) with 2027 earnings momentum behind it.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The Extended Use Program (launching H1 2027) cannibalizes the recurring instrument stream faster than procedure volumes compensate — Intuitive itself says the financial impacts are "unquantified" and progressive from 2027 — and if 13–15% procedure growth is the new normal, the premium multiple never fully returns.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
