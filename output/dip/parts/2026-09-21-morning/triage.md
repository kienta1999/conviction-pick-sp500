## High-composite drops, explained honestly
- **CASY (#9, 0.599)** — dropped despite top-10 composite because the doctrine
  is about *cheap because of the dip*: 25.5x forward PE on 6.5% operating
  margins is a quality-growth price, not a dip price.
- **GS (#10, 0.569)** — dropped because the dip shape fails: -0.7% below the
  200-day SMA is noise, not a dislocation; the bank's LOW_CASH_CONVERSION /
  RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN flags are balance-sheet-model noise, not a value-trap
  signal worth research budget.
