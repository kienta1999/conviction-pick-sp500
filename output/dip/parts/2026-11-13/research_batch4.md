### 7. Value-trap risk
- **Margin dilution is structural, not one-off.** If hyperscaler hardware keeps growing faster than high-margin software/security, gross margin grinds from 68% toward the low-60s and EPS growth lags revenue growth into FY28 (Morgan Stanley's core concern). Q1 non-GAAP gross margin 65.6% (vs 68.1% YoY) shows it is still happening.
- **Pull-forward / double-ordering.** Hyperscalers may have pulled orders forward ahead of price increases or on AI euphoria; the $17.17bn of purchase commitments and $6.38bn inventory could become excess if AI capex pauses — the 5 Nov data-centre moratorium scare is the live version of this risk.
- **Customer concentration:** a handful of hyperscalers now drive the growth; a single capex cut (or a shift to in-house silicon) hits disproportionately.
- **The stock already ran once:** +57% YTD into August, then −28% — momentum buyers are gone and the chart is technically damaged (below 50d ~$108.82 and 200d ~$101.6 at the lows).

### Earnings-quality flag — INVENTORY_BUILD
**Verdict: mostly benign business-model reason, with a genuine watch item.** Inventories $5.69bn at FY26 end (25 Jul 2026), +80% YoY from $3.16bn, and $6.38bn at 24 Oct 2026; inventory purchase commitments $17.17bn, +126% YoY from $7.60bn.
