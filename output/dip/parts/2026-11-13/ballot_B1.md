## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: KLAC
- Runner-up: LRCX
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): KLAC is the highest-quality business in this field bought on a multiple-compression dip: a process-control quasi-monopoly with >56% worldwide share, gross margins consistently above 60% and a record 46.1% non-GAAP operating margin in Q1 FY27, revenue growth accelerating for three straight quarters (7.2% → 11.5% → 15.2% → 28.9%) on record revenue of $4.14bn (+29% YoY). At 22.7x forward earnings with record Q1 free cash flow of $1.34bn and $8.81bn of buyback authorisation remaining, the dip offers a great compounder at a repaired price, with CY2027 wafer-fab equipment spending above $200bn as the demand tailwind.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business: Q1 FY27 was a record on revenue, EPS ($1.22 vs $1.19 expected) and operating margin (first time ever above 46%), the stock rallied the day after earnings, and the -13.3% leg on 5 Nov came with no company news — a policy-panic de-rating, not a demand or share-loss event.
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: low risk — KLAC's >56% share of semiconductor process control is comparable to ASML in lithography; as nodes shrink and HBM stacks grow, inspection intensity per wafer rises, so AI is the demand driver, not a substitute. No software or AI tool can replace physical wafer inspection and metrology.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: the Dec-quarter print in late Jan / early Feb 2027 (next-earnings date contested: ~28 Jan 2027 vs 4 Feb 2027) delivering on the $4.7bn ± $200m revenue and $1.44 ± $0.11 EPS guide, plus confirmation that CY2027 WFE stays above $200bn; a clean print should re-rate the compressed trailing multiple (48.4x at end-Sep → 34.4x on 6 Nov) back toward its growth rate.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case: base ~+35–45% (toward the +36.4% analyst upside, on delivered Dec-quarter earnings and a modest multiple repair); bull ~+60–75% if WFE above $200bn holds, gross margin moves back toward 63–64% and the market pays up again for a quasi-monopoly compounder.
- Top risk (value-trap case): memory-cost inflation caps gross margin for several quarters and, if CY2027 WFE comes in well below $200bn, a ~35x trailing multiple reprices hard — the growth story would then be a cyclical peak, not a compounder on sale.
