## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: KLAC
- Runner-up: LRCX
- Thesis (3-5 sentences): KLA is the one name in this field whose moat no model and no substitute can copy: >56% worldwide share of semiconductor process control, a quasi-monopoly comparable to ASML in lithography, with gross margins consistently above 60% and a record 46.1% non-GAAP operating margin in Q1 FY27. The dip is pure multiple compression on a margin-cost quibble (memory input costs capping gross margin at 62.5% vs a 63–64% Investor Day target), not demand — revenue grew 29% YoY to a record $4.14bn, growth has accelerated three straight quarters (7.2% → 11.5% → 15.2% → 28.9%), and lead times average ~12 months because KLA is supply-constrained. AI is the demand driver, not the disruptor: as nodes shrink and HBM stacks grow, inspection intensity per wafer rises, so every AI chip built makes KLA's toll booth more valuable.
- Why the dip is temporary (not permanent): the single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business — the stock fell −13.3% on 5 Nov with no company news, days after a record quarter in which it beat on revenue and EPS, while the Dec-quarter guide ($4.7bn ± $200m revenue, $1.44 ± $0.11 EPS) was strong; nothing about the business broke, only the multiple did (trailing P/E compressed from 48.4x at end-Sep to 34.4x by 6 Nov on rising earnings).
- Moat / AI-irreplaceability: can AI or a substitute disrupt it? Low risk — process control is measurement science plus qualification lock-in: KLA's tools are specified into customers' design flows, switching costs are extreme, and no AI model can inspect a wafer. AI increases the need for KLA's product rather than replacing it.
- Rebound catalyst & timing: the Dec-quarter print in late Jan / early Feb 2027 (next-earnings date contested: ~28 Jan 2027 vs 4 Feb 2027) delivering on the $4.7bn guide, plus CY2027 WFE above $200bn (preliminary outlook) — either should re-rate a stock trading at 22.7x forward earnings with $8.81bn of buyback authorisation remaining.
- 12-18 month return scenario: rough base / bull case — base: +30–40% to roughly $210–225 (in line with the +36.4% analyst upside / ~$219.62 mean target from the screen), as the multiple normalises on delivered earnings. Bull: +55–65% to roughly $250–265 if WFE >$200bn is confirmed and the market pays a monopoly multiple again for record margins.
- Top risk (value-trap case): memory-cost inflation caps gross margin for several quarters and a CY2027 WFE cut reprices a ~35x trailing multiple hard — if the WFE outlook inverts, KLA is a cyclical at a growth multiple, not a monopoly at a fair one.
- Conviction (1-10): 9
