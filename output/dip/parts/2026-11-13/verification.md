- No primary company filing states the >56% figure directly; the number comes from KLA-cited Gartner data and third-party TechInsights/CSET counts, which agree.

### LRCX 2 — A $25bn buyback authorisation was announced 5 Nov 2026 (largest in company history) plus a 27% dividend raise to $0.33/qtr
**CONTRADICTED** on the dividend timing; the buyback half is confirmed.
- Buyback: **confirmed.** Lam press release / Form 8-K, 5 Nov 2026: "its Board of Directors approved a $25 billion share repurchase authorization and a quarterly dividend of $0.33 per share." - Dividend raise: **the 27% raise to $0.33 did NOT happen on 5 Nov.** Lam's newsroom lists a separate release dated **27 Aug 2026**: "its Board of Directors has approved a $0.07, or 27%, increase in its quarterly dividend, from $0.26 to $0.33 per share." The 5 Nov release merely *declared* the $0.33 quarterly dividend (payable 6 Jan 2027 to holders of record 2 Dec 2026) at the rate already set in August.
- **Correction: the 27% dividend raise to $0.33/qtr was announced 27 Aug 2026, not 5 Nov 2026.** The raise itself is real; only its date was misattributed. This does not change the capital-return thesis.
