## BALLOT 1 — Single pick

- **Top pick:** BR (Broadridge Financial Solutions)
- **Runner-up:** APP (AppLovin)

**Thesis (5 sentences).**
Broadridge is the only name in this field that **raised guidance through the entire 40% decline** —
Q3 FY26 recurring revenue $1.29B (+6% cc), adjusted EPS +11% to $2.72, and FY26 guidance *raised* to
≥7% cc recurring growth and 10–12% adjusted EPS growth — while the stock fell from a high to $157.34.
That is a pure multiple event, not an earnings event: 15.1× forward against a 10-year average P/E of
**30.85** and a 10-year *trough* of **21.93**, meaning BR trades **below its own decade-low multiple
while compounding EPS at a double-digit rate**, and it was at 23.2× as recently as **Jan 8 2026** — the
entire de-rate is seven months old. The market is short one falsifiable claim (closed sales cut from
$290–340M to $240–290M, YTD $147M, −16% y/y) and long two unfalsifiable ones (AI erodes proxy
processing, tokenization disintermediates governance), and **tomorrow's print marks the falsifiable one
to market** while initial FY27 guidance tests the unfalsifiable ones with numbers. The explosive part
is the mechanics of a multiple that has no historical support below where it sits: on ~$10.4 forward
EPS growing 10–12%, simply returning to 19× — *still under the decade trough* — is +40%, and Needham,
which cut its target to $230, **maintained Buy**, i.e. the bear on the desk carries a +46% target.
I am taking the fastest clock in the field, and I am explicit that the same clock is how I get proven
wrong by 9:30am.

**Why the dip is temporary (not permanent).**
Guidance was **raised, not cut, during the drawdown** — a company being structurally disintermediated
does not raise recurring-revenue and EPS guidance mid-disintermediation. And the specific disruption
narrative is contradicted by primary evidence rather than by management assertion: the **SEC has
affirmed tokenized securities carry identical governance obligations**, most tokenized equities route
through broker-dealers that are *already BR clients*, and **BR went live with on-chain governance for
tokenized equities**, extending proxy voting to all tokenized-security models. It is monetizing the
thing it is being sold for.

**Moat / AI-irreplaceability — LOW risk (9/10 irreplaceable).**
The moat is a **regulatory mandate**, not a product advantage: SEC rules *require* proxy distribution to
beneficial owners at NYSE-regulated fees. An LLM cannot deregulate that, and in-sourcing it invites
compliance exposure and regulatory review — the cost of switching is not price, it is liability. The
honest deduction is that a mandated toll-booth can still be re-priced by regulators over a decade; that
is a 2030s risk, not a 2027 one.

**Rebound catalyst & timing — TOMORROW, Aug 4, pre-open (~18 hours).**
FY26 full-year results plus **initial FY27 guidance**. Two things re-rate the stock: (1) full-year
closed sales landing at or above the cut $240–290M band, converting "demand" back into "sales-cycle
timing" — supported by **pipeline >$1B, +20% y/y and deal origination +25%**; and (2) an FY27 adjusted
EPS guide in the historical 8–12% range, which is arithmetically incompatible with the erosion thesis.
Secondary, slower catalyst: a **~6.6% FCF yield** ($1.19B FCF) at 1.76× leverage funds buybacks that
compound the re-rate through 2027.

**12–18 month return scenario.**
- Base (+35–45%, ~$215–225): closed sales land in band, FY27 guided ~9–11% EPS growth, multiple recovers
  to ~19× on ~$11.6 FY27 EPS. Still below the decade trough of 21.93.
- Bull (+75–85%, ~$275–290): closed sales beat with pipeline conversion visible, FY27 guided ≥11%, and
  the AI-erosion narrative breaks — multiple to 23–24×, i.e. merely back to January 2026.
- Bear (−10 to −20%): see below.

**Top risk (value-trap case).**
Unfalsifiable long-dated fears can hold a multiple down for years regardless of delivered EPS — you can
be right on the business and dead money to 2028. The specific trigger: if **closed sales miss again
tomorrow**, "timing" becomes "demand," the one real negative stops being one-off, and 15× becomes the
new ceiling instead of the floor. There is no valuation cushion left to *discover*; the entire thesis is
that the cushion is already there and the market disagrees.

**Conviction: 8/10.**
