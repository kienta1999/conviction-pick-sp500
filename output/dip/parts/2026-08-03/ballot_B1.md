# Ballot B1 — Agent B: Growth / quality compounder lens
Dip run 2026-08-03. Prices are the 2026-08-03 close.

**Framing rule I applied to all 14:** I am not paying for cheapness. I am paying for a business whose
2029 earnings power is *provably* higher than today's, temporarily marked down. So for every name the
first question is: **did the growth algorithm survive the quarter that caused the drawdown, or did it
quietly step down to a lower rate the multiple hasn't finished repricing?** Four names in this field
failed that test outright (ADBE, IDXX, GNRC, ROL) and two passed only on a technicality (NFLX, VRSK).

## BALLOT 1 — Single pick

- **Top pick:** APP
- **Runner-up:** ISRG

**Thesis (3-5 sentences):**
AppLovin is the only name in this field where the growth rate is *accelerating* into the drawdown —
**+59% YoY like-for-like** revenue, consensus for Aug 5 of **$1.94B (+54.0%)** and **EPS $3.72
(+64.6%)** — while the multiple has collapsed from a **50–70× historical range to ~26× 2026E / 18.8×
forward**. Everything else on this list is a business whose growth decelerated and whose multiple
decelerated with it; APP is a business whose growth did not decelerate and whose multiple fell 44.6%
anyway, which is the exact definition of a price event rather than a business event. The margin
structure is the tell that this is a genuine compounder and not a boom: **78.2% operating margins**
with guidance implying **84–85% adjusted EBITDA margins** on $1.915–1.945B of revenue, i.e.
incremental margins near unity — every incremental ad dollar routed through Axon drops to cash with
almost no incremental cost, which is why FCF is **$3.18B against 0.22× ND/EBITDA**. The reinvestment
runway is the least appreciated part: APP holds **11.1% of e-commerce ad budgets and is *gaining*
169bps**, meaning it is roughly a tenth penetrated into an adjacency it entered two years ago, having
already won mobile gaming. Wells Fargo raised its PT to **$575 on Jul 7 2026** and the mean target
moved *up* to **~$688.75** against a $406.16 price — the sell side did not follow the stock down.

**Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):**
The single most compelling fact is the *shape* of the July decline: **−12.65% on Jul 13 2026 on a
BofA e-commerce channel note** — a third-party read-through, not a company disclosure, not a guidance
change, not a print. No quarter has missed. Guidance for the quarter being sold ($1.915–1.945B rev,
$1.62–1.65B adj EBITDA) has never been walked back. A −44.6% drawdown driven by a brokerage channel
check plus two unresolved legal overhangs, sitting on top of +54% guided growth and 85% EBITDA
margins, is a sentiment de-rate. The Aug 5 print either confirms or destroys that in 48 hours, which
is unusually cheap falsifiability for a thesis this size.

**Moat / AI-irreplaceability — MEDIUM risk (I score it 6.5/10, near the analysts' 6):**
The moat is an algorithm, not a licence — that is real and I will not argue it away. But it is an
algorithm with a compounding data flywheel: Axon's ranking quality improves with ad spend routed
through it, and share gains (+169bps in e-commerce) are the observable proof the flywheel is turning.
Critically, **AI is the input here, not the substitute** — the same dynamic as META's +12% price per
ad. The thing that can hurt APP is not a model replacing it, it is a bigger first-party data owner
(Amazon, Meta) undercutting it in e-commerce, or the regulator constraining the data collection that
feeds the model. That second one is the risk, and it is legal, not technological.

**Rebound catalyst & timing:**
**Aug 5 2026, 5pm ET (two days).** Consensus rev $1.94B / EPS $3.72; the bar is a +52–55% growth
guide, and two soft channel checks (BofA on e-commerce, Wells on mobile-game ROAS from cost-per-
install inflation) have already reset expectations into it. A clean print with e-commerce share
holding re-rates the stock toward the $575 PT within weeks. The second, unpredictable catalyst is a
ruling on the **securities class-action motion to dismiss, pending since Nov 14 2025** — nine months
with no ruling means it can land any day, and a dismissal removes one of the two overhangs outright.

**12-18 month return scenario:**
- **Base (~55%): $530–570, +30% to +40%.** Aug 5 lands in-line to modestly above; growth normalizes
  to +35–40% through 2027; multiple recovers to ~28–30× 2027E. Plus buybacks against $3.18B FCF.
- **Bull (~25%): $700–760, +72% to +87%.** Aug 5 beats and guides e-commerce ahead; MTD granted or
  SEC probe closed without action; multiple returns to only the *low end* of its 50–70× history on a
  larger earnings base. Note this still does not require the old peak multiple.
- **Bear (~20%): $230–260, −36% to −43%.** See below.

**Top risk (value-trap case):**
The SEC data-collection probe — **"active and ongoing" as of Feb 20 2026, no update in five months** —
targets the *mechanism* that produces the 85% EBITDA margin. This is not a fine risk; it is a risk to
the input that makes the model work, and it is uncapped. Layer on the genuine growth-quality question
the channel checks raise: **cost-per-install inflation weakening mobile-game return-on-ad-spend** would
mean the legacy half of the business is being harvested to fund the e-commerce land-grab, and
consolidated +54% would be masking a decelerating core. Two *independent* soft checks on *both* sides
of the business going into a quarter guided +52–55% is the one piece of evidence in this brief that
genuinely worries me. Sizing, not conviction, is the right answer to it.

**Conviction: 8/10.**

### Top 3 — detail

**1. APP — AppLovin ($406.16)**
The decisive data point for this lens is that **guidance implies 84–85% adjusted EBITDA margins**
($1.62–1.65B on $1.915–1.945B) while revenue is guided +52–55% — that combination of rate and
incremental margin does not exist anywhere else in this field, and it is being priced at **~26×
2026E against a 50–70× history**. The dip was triggered by a **third-party channel note on Jul 13**,
not a company event; guidance has never been cut, and the mean target *rose* to ~$688.75 as the stock
fell. **12-18mo: base $530–570 (+30–40%); bull $700–760 (+72–87%).**

**2. ISRG — Intuitive Surgical ($375.41)**
Q2 (Jul 16) was **revenue $2.89B +19%, non-GAAP EPS $2.80 +28%**, and the stock fell **11.6%
entirely on a procedure-growth guide** of 13.5–15.5% vs consensus 15.3% — a ~100bp quibble on a
forecast, not a reported number. Everything that defines compounding quality got *better*: recurring
revenue is **85% of total and grew 19%**, instrument pricing rose to **~$1,830 per procedure from
$1,800**, FY26 non-GAAP gross margin was **raised to 68–69%**, and procedures grew 17% against a 12%
installed-base increase — **utilization per system is rising**, which is the purest evidence a moat is
deepening rather than being defended. The reinvestment runway is unusually visible and unusually
long: the **dV5 refresh is ~15% complete (~1,700 of 11,710 systems)**, a capital annuity barely
started, funded from **$8.63B of cash and zero debt**. At **31.1× forward vs a 45–60× history and a
10-year low of 32.93×**, I am buying the best AI-immune franchise here at close to its decade-trough
multiple; the Ottava De Novo (Jul 22) is a real 3-5 year share risk but Intuitive can fund a five-year
price war out of the bank account and keeps **86% of accessories**, the stickier annuity.
**12-18mo: base $460–490 (+23–31%); bull $570–610 (+52–63%)** if Oct 20 lands at the high end of the
procedure range and Ottava's commercial ramp proves slow.

**3. META — Meta Platforms ($590.24)**
The one number that settles whether the moat is intact: Q2 ad revenue **+27% to $59.4B with
impressions +14% AND average price per ad +12%** — accelerating *price* is the opposite of a demand
problem, and it is direct evidence that better AI ranking is accretive to Meta rather than
substitutive. The drawdown is entirely a capital-allocation de-rate: **$130–145B of 2026 capex**, FCF
down 91% to $784M, operating margin 31% vs 43%. I am not pricing that as broken — the Q2 EPS miss was
$2.4B of legal charges plus $1.2B of severance, both Q2-specific, so Q3 optics improve mechanically —
but I am docking it to #3 because **returns on the incremental capital are unproven and Susan Li
refused to give the 2027 capex number**, and because the depreciation-life change (~$2.9B benefit in
2025) means "16.9× forward" may really be 20–21×. **12-18mo: base $720–760 (+22–29%); bull $880–930
(+49–58%)** if the Oct 28 print contains 2027 capex and shows AI revenue scaling into the spend.

### Deliberately left out of my top 10 — and why

**ADBE, IDXX, GNRC, ROL — all four fail the growth-quality test, and I flag ADBE and IDXX as the two
genuine value traps in this field.** **ADBE** is the field's defining trap and I would not own it at
any multiple here: total ARR growth is decelerating to **10.2% guided *including* ~$480M of acquired
Semrush ARR**, so organic is meaningfully below 10% — buying revenue while organic decelerates is the
late-stage tell, the first impairment already appeared (**$70M goodwill write-down in Q2**), the moat
is the lowest in the field (5/10) against **Canva at ~$4B ARR growing 30–35% and Figma at ~$1.2B
growing ~40%** — competitors compounding 3–4× faster — and **both the CEO and CFO seats are vacant
mid-pivot**. 9.1× is a forecast of decline, not a discount; and the dip is largely harvested anyway
(**+32% off the June low, +22.1% in July**). **IDXX** is the mirror-image trap: the **highest multiple
in the field (34.4×) for the lowest growth (7.5%)**, on the shallowest kept drawdown, into an end
market where **US clinical vet visits have run −1% to −2% for three straight quarters** — if that
decline is structural, 34× on 5-6% growth is a 40% air pocket, and earnings are tomorrow pre-open.
**GNRC** is disqualified on the corrected numbers alone: **revenue growth +0.6% TTM**, TTM GAAP
operating margin **~9.5%** not 17.9%, the "beat" carried by a **non-recurring $71M tariff refund
(~$0.90 of $2.40 EPS)**, gross margin *fell* YoY ex-refund, residential guided down, and the data-
center vector trades 34.7% residential margins for 14.6% segment margins — it is winning revenue that
is half as profitable as what it is losing. **ROL** is the painful one: a 9/10 business (AI cannot
kill a termite, 37% ROE) at the wrong price — **29.1× forward after a 41% fall**, with **organic
guidance CUT to "at least 6%"** and the lowest implied upside in the field (+19.4%). A de-rate to 20×
is another ~30% down with nothing operationally new going wrong. Great business, unfinished multiple.

**On NFLX (kept at #7, but with a warning):** this is the clearest example in the field of a
compounder that has *quietly stepped down* — **+17.6% → +16.2% → +13.4% → +12% guided**, four
consecutive quarters — while **H1'26 view hours grew only ~2%**. Management then moved engagement
reporting to **annual from 2027**, removing the metric that would let you check the input to both
pricing power and ad CPMs. The $12.5B FCF and the **$27.1B buyback (~9.1% of shares)** earn it a top-
10 slot; the disclosure withdrawal is why it is not top-5.

**Diversification note:** I have taken the brief's correlation warning seriously. My top 3 (APP, ISRG,
META) sits deliberately *outside* the "AI commoditizes the toll-booth" cluster (BR/VRSK/ROL/ADBE) —
BR is my #5 and VRSK my #9 precisely so a single wrong macro thesis cannot take out the top of my
ranking. ISRG is one of the four genuinely uncorrelated drawdowns.
