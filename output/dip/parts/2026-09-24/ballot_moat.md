## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** BR
- **Runner-up:** ISRG
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Broadridge is a de facto monopoly on the plumbing nobody can copy — U.S. proxy voting and fixed-income processing that is legally mandated, mission-critical, constantly audited, and locked in by multi-year contracts. The stock is down ~33% purely on a fear of the future (AI disintermediation, tokenized equities bypassing governance), while every reported number kept beating — four straight quarterly EPS beats, Q4 FY26 +8% recurring revenue and +12% adjusted EPS with record $305M closed sales. Most damning to the bear case: BR is building the feared technology itself — its DLR platform processed $360B in tokenized repo transactions in June 2026, and it launched DLX on Sept 9, 2026 to bridge on-chain and off-chain markets, with the SEC affirming tokenized securities carry the same governance obligations BR already services. The disruption is priced; its arrival is BR becoming the infrastructure.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The drawdown drivers were all sentiment about a future that never arrived — Jensen's Q1 2026 letter and SouthernSun's Q2 update both describe the same story: shares fell on "AI will erode BR's franchises" and "tokenization will disintermediate governance" fears *while 7% recurring-revenue growth, 11% adjusted-EPS growth, and raised full-year guidance printed*. A permanently impaired moat doesn't produce four consecutive beats with a $470M sales backlog and FY27 guidance of 6–8% recurring-revenue growth.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW risk. AI is a $25M cost-save productivity lever, not a substitute — you cannot automate away legally mandated shareholder governance, regulated audits, and the switching costs of multi-year contracts with the entire brokerage industry. Tokenization, the one substitute technology, is being co-opted (DLR/DLX), and regulators affirmed the governance layer survives on-chain.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q1 FY27 earnings ~Nov 3, 2026 (next clean checkpoint after the Aug 4 beat that rose 7%); continued DLX/DLR volume growth (DLR guided +50% by December) proving the tokenization hedge; RBC's $226 target (Aug 31, 2026) re-rating the AI-fear discount. Real re-rating unfolds over 2–4 quarters as closed-sales momentum compounds.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base: back to consensus $216 (~+32% from ~$163) on a recovered ~20x multiple for double-digit EPS growth. Bull: DLX traction + buyback execution ($1.5B authorization, $600M done in FY26) drives a re-rating toward RBC's $226 and the Street high $257 (~+39–58%).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Over a 5–10 year horizon, a fully tokenized equity market with direct on-chain shareholder registers structurally shrinks the registered-shareholder communications base faster than BR's DLX hedge can offset; closed-sales volatility (Q3 FY26 −19% y/y) resurfaces, showing the fear was early, not wrong.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
