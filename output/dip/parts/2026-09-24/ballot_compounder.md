# Panelist B — Growth/Quality Compounder Ballot — 2026-09-24
Lens: revenue durability, margins, returns on capital, growth story intact. A great business bought on sale — not a cheap business with a broken one.

---

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** IDXX
- **Runner-up:** ISRG
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** IDEXX is the textbook compounder in this set: 35% operating margins, 66%+ ROE, $1B+ annual FCF, net debt trivial ($0.77B), and a razor/razorblade model where consumables pull through a growing installed base (+14.7% organic consumables on an 11% larger premium base). The dip is pure multiple compression — both 2026 quarters beat and raised, yet the stock sits -32% off its high near the low end of its 5-year multiple band — with zero guidance cuts, zero credibility events, and zero structural questions. It is the only name here where the "still compounding" test passes on every line item.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The selloff is documented as sentiment — a March insider-selling recalibration, a May sector-wide healthcare rotation with no company news, and a tariff overhang from Section 232 investigations — while Q2 delivered EPS $4.27 (+18% YoY, +8% vs consensus), revenue +10%, and raised FY26 guidance. Nothing in the drawdown is a customer, revenue, or margin event.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW risk. Proprietary instrument installed base + consumables lock-in + reference-lab network + cloud software; no AI substitutes for in-clinic blood work — AI actually helps IDEXX (diagnostic utilization algorithms). 63% gross margin confirms pricing power.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 print ~Nov 2, 2026 with another beat-and-raise on double-digit CAG recurring growth; continued premium-instrument placements expanding the consumables flywheel. Timing: 1-2 quarters.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +30-40% as the multiple drifts back toward the middle of its 35-75x history (mean target $698 = +34% from ~$522). Bull +50%+ if pet-health spending re-accelerates and the market re-prices the compounder premium on record recurring-revenue prints.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** PEG >2 means this is a growth-scare discount, not deep value — the re-rating is capped if EPS growth stays high-teens; heavy buybacks (~$347M/quarter ≈ operating CF) mask organic EPS growth while the cash cushion stays thin (current ratio 1.17); and the one genuine soft spot — a consumer pullback in vet visits — would hit the recurring consumable stream the whole thesis rests on.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
