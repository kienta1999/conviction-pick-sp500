# Panelist D ballot — dip run 2026-10-12 (Falling-knife / value-trap skeptic)

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** SYK
- **Runner-up:** AZO
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The SYK drawdown is a named, bounded operational event — an Inari supply disruption the CFO himself admitted runs into Q4 — hitting a compounder that is otherwise still compounding: revenue +9.4%, EPS +44%, and FCF of $4.7B running well above net income of $3.73B. This is precisely what a temporary dip looks like: the business is generating cash faster than it earns, surgeon lock-in and Mako robotics are untouched, and the cause is fixable with a visible timeline rather than a structural impairment. At ~18.5x forward vs a historical mid-20s multiple, the price is doing all the punishing while the business keeps growing.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** Cash conversion tells the truth: FCF $4.7B > NI $3.73B in the same period the stock fell 30% on supply/backorder noise — a structurally broken business does not print cash like that. The two named causes (Inari backorder, CEO transition) are both time-limited and neither touches the installed Mako base or surgeon relationships.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** low risk. Mako robotics + surgeon lock-in and implant-switching costs are among the hardest moats in the list; AI is a beneficiary (robotics planning, workflow), not a substitute.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q4 2026 normalization of Inari supply confirmed at the Oct 29 Q3 print, plus clean execution from the incoming CEO (Stiles, starts Jan 1 2027) — a supply-resolved + transition-completed print by early 2027.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +31–34% to ~$364–371 (analyst target, multiple drifts back toward low-20s on FY27 estimates); bull +40–45% if supply resolves cleanly and the new CEO accelerates the compounder re-rating.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The supply disruption drags beyond Q4 or recurs (systemic ops issue rather than one-off), and/or the CEO transition coincides with share losses in extremities/robotics — in which case mid-20s multiple expectations never return and 18.5x proves fairly-priced, not cheap.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
