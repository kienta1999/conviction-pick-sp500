## Material facts that could bear on the temporary-dip reading (searched 2026-11-04)

No guide cut, demand warning, or cancelled catalyst for any of the three names was found since the dossier was written. The following context points do **not** contradict the dip reading, but the panel should keep them attached to it:

- **LRCX — supply constraint is a timing shift, but it persists into 2027:** management said clean-room/component constraints will continue to limit the industry's ability to fully meet demand into calendar 2027, and China's share (35% of September-quarter revenue, up from 26%) is expected to fall significantly in the December quarter. Shares fell ~6% after hours on 2026-10-21 and >10% on 2026-10-22 despite the record quarter — the market read the capped guide as a setback (Morgan Stanley, cited by Catenaa, 2026-10-22), not as a demand break. Delayed shipments moving to March is the key test at the January print.
- **KLAC — margin headwind and target trims, not a guide cut:** management flagged persistent memory-cost inflation pressuring gross margins (December gross margin guided to 62.5%, slightly below the September 62.7%), and several firms lowered price targets after the beat (e.g. Wells Fargo $245 → $210, per MarketBeat, 2026-11-01) while keeping positive/neutral ratings; shares fell 5.69% on 2026-10-30 despite the beat. KLA's own 10-K also cautions that backlog "does not provide meaningful information about the timing of future revenue recognition". None of this is a demand warning.
- **AMAT — the dip is substantially a valuation reset, and the November 12 print is the imminent test:** AMAT fell ~24–29% over the 30 days to 2026-10-30 (Tickeron; Pluang, 2026-11-03) on AI-capex-return doubts, Lam's peer supply warning, and target cuts (Needham $740 → $580 on 2026-10-16; Morgan Stanley cut to $538 on 2026-11-03, Equal Weight). The Q4 print on 2026-11-12 (guide $10.25bn / $4.02) is therefore a live binary for the dip thesis: a miss or soft fiscal Q1 2027 guide would undermine it.
