## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** KEYS
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam is the fastest-growing, highest-returning franchise in the set that is also genuinely cheap: Q1 FY2027 revenue rose 54% YoY to $8.22bn with non-GAAP EPS up 75% and a record 52.4% gross margin, yet the stock sits 45.5% below its 52-week high at 19.3x forward earnings — the cheapest of the three US equipment names despite the fastest current growth. It holds ~45–50% of the global etch market, earns a 70.1% ROE, sits on $3.70bn of net cash, and generates $4.55bn of FCF, so it can fund the growth and the capital return through the drawdown. Management expects WFE spending in the mid-$150bn range in CY2026 and above $200bn in 2027, with Lam's systems revenue growing over 40% in CY2026 — the growth story accelerated into the sell-off, it did not break.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that fundamentals accelerated into the fall: revenue +54%, EPS +75%, record margins, EPS beats in 4 of the last 4 quarters — while the stock fell 16.4% in a single session on 5 Nov on the data-center moratorium scare, the same day Lam announced a $25bn buyback. No customer was lost, no fab was cancelled, no share was lost; the drawdown is multiple compression plus a supply-constrained guide plus a political sentiment shock.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. AI increases Lam's content per wafer — HBM production needs 2–3x more etch/deposition steps than standard DRAM, and ever-deeper 3D NAND holes are Lam's home turf. There is no software substitute for physically carving silicon, and each tool must be qualified fab-by-fab over months, so switching costs are extreme. The moat is scored 9/10 in the dossier, docked only for deposition being a four-firm race and cyclical memory-capex timing.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Already firing — the $25bn buyback authorisation plus a 27% dividend raise to $0.33/share announced 5 Nov 2026; the stock rose 8.2% on 6 Nov and ~10%+ on 9 Nov on the news. Next proof point is Q2 FY2027 earnings on 27 Jan 2027 against the $8.6bn revenue / $2.33 EPS guide, which is beatable if supply-chain and clean-room constraints ease; beyond that, NVDA's 17 Nov print and any clarification that moratoriums exempt projects already under construction would de-risk the 12–18 month delay fear.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +40–55%, toward the $364.03 analyst mean target (+54.3% upside from $235.88), as the 19x forward multiple re-rates part-way back toward the ~28x forward level of the summer peak while FY2027 EPS grows an estimated +65.1% (Zacks). Bull case roughly +70–80%, toward $400–430, if supply constraints ease, the Q2 guide is beaten, and WFE above $200bn in 2027 holds — earnings growth plus a fuller multiple recovery. Downside case is a WFE push-out (see Top risk).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** If the moratorium fear proves to be a real demand delay rather than a permitting delay, hyperscaler builds slip 12–18 months and WFE above $200bn in 2027 slips with them; Lam's memory-heavy mix gets hit first and hardest, the supply-constraint guide could be masking softening order intake, and the "cheap" 19x forward P/E would be priced on earnings that get cut. High beta (2.03) means it falls hardest in any further risk-off leg, and China export controls remain a standing overhang.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top 3 — single most compelling data point + 12–18mo scenario

**1. LRCX:** The single most compelling point is the combination of a record quarter and a $25bn buyback announced into the panic: Q1 FY2027 revenue $8.22bn (+54% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $2.20 (+75% YoY), record 52.4% gross margin, net cash $3.70bn — and the board authorised $25bn of buybacks on 5 Nov, the day the stock fell 16.4%. Base case +40–55% toward the $364.03 target; bull case +70–80% toward $400–430 if supply constraints ease and WFE above $200bn in 2027 holds.

**2. The 19 Nov print (7 days out) is the immediate binary. Base case +30–40% toward the $419.67 analyst mean target (+37.3% from $305.64); bull case +50% or more if FY2027 guidance on that call holds double-digit growth and the ~$100m of supply-pushed revenue flows back.

**3. KLAC:** The single most compelling point is the quality of the franchise at a reset price: a quasi-monopoly with ~55%+ of process-control / defect inspection, 61.5% gross margin, 45.0% operating margin, 91.4% ROE, revenue growth accelerating for three straight quarters to +28.9% YoY, and a Dec-quarter guide of $4.7bn ± $200m on leading-edge foundry logic tied to AI infrastructure — after the trailing multiple fell from 48.4x at end-September to ~35x on unchanged trailing earnings. Base case +25–35% toward the $219.62 target (+36.4% from $161.01); bull case +45–50% if the Dec-quarter gross margin holds at or above 62.5% and the 63–64% Investor Day margin model stays credible into 2027–28.
