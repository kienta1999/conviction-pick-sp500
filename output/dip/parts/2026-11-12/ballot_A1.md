## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** KEYS
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam Research is the cleanest rebound in the whole panel: it just printed a record quarter — Q1 FY2027 revenue $8.22bn (+54% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $2.20 (+75% YoY), record 52.4% gross margin — and the stock fell anyway, first 12.64% on a supply-capped guide, then 16.4% in a single session on the 5 Nov moratorium panic, a ~57% peak-to-trough fall. At $235.88 it trades at 19.3x forward earnings (vs ~28x at the June peak) while FY2027 EPS is expected to grow +65.1%, and the board answered the panic with a $25bn buyback plus a 27% dividend raise announced on 5 Nov itself — the stock already snapped +8.2% on 6 Nov and ~10%+ on 9 Nov. With WFE spending guided to >$200bn in 2027 and Lam's systems revenue growing >40% in CY2026, this is multiple compression on an accelerating franchise, not a broken business.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that fundamentals accelerated *into* the sell-off: revenue +54%, EPS +75%, record margins, 4/4 EPS beats (avg surprise +6.4%), and net cash of $3.70bn — while no customer was lost, no fab was cancelled, and no share was lost (Lam has outperformed WFE for three straight years). The fall was a supply-constrained guide plus a political sentiment shock about *future* permits, not a demand print.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Lam holds ~45–50% of the global etch market in a 3–4 firm oligopoly, with fab-by-fab qualification lock-in (a failed etch step scraps whole wafer lots). AI *increases* Lam's content per wafer — HBM needs 2–3x more etch/deposition steps than standard DRAM, and ever-deeper 3D NAND is Lam's home turf. There is no software substitute for physically carving silicon.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Already firing — the $25bn buyback (announced 5 Nov, no termination date) and dividend raise are live now. Next sentiment reset: NVDA reports 17 Nov, plus any clarification that moratoriums exempt projects already under construction. Company-specific proof point: Q2 FY2027 earnings 27 Jan 2027 against a beatable guide ($8.6bn revenue, $2.33 EPS).
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+50% (toward the $364.03 analyst mean target, +54.3% upside) as WFE >$200bn in 2027 materialises and the buyback shrinks the float on a re-rating from ~19x toward the low-20s forward. Bull ~+75–100% if supply constraints ease, the Q2 guide is beaten, and the forward multiple re-rates back toward the ~28x peak level on +65%/+24% FY2027/FY2028 EPS growth.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Memory capex is cyclical and Lam is memory-heavy — if the moratorium fear proves to be a real 12–18 month hyperscaler build pause rather than a permitting delay, WFE >$200bn in 2027 slips, the supply-constraint guide may be masking softening order intake, and the "cheap" 19x forward P/E is on earnings that get cut. High beta (2.03) means it falls hardest in any further risk-off leg.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
