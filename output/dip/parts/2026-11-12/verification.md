1. **LRCX dividend timing (material):** the 27% dividend raise belongs to 27 Aug 2026, not the 5 Nov buyback announcement. Any memo language tying the raise to 5 Nov should be corrected.
2. **FIX "halved" (material):** −44% from the 52-week high, not −50%. Correct to "~44% off its high".
3. **KLAC next-print date (material):** "~27 Jan 2027" is contested — management said "late January", but TipRanks/Zacks/FMP estimate 4 Feb 2027. Do not present 27 Jan as KLAC's date; LRCX's estimated date is 27 Jan, KLAC's is not confirmed.
4. **FIX margin "normalisation" (framing):** management guided 2027 gross margins *within recent ranges*, not a return to higher margins. The H1-2027 test is real (job-maturity normalisation), but the upside case should not assume margin expansion.
5. **FIX Q3 FCF quality:** $806m FCF includes a $166m one-off tax deferral into Q4; underlying FCF ex-deferral is ~$640m — still above operating income, but the beat is smaller than the headline suggests.
6. **LRCX "fell anyway" (minor omission):** the post-earnings fall was not just sentiment — the Dec guide barely beat consensus ($8.60bn vs $8.57bn FactSet) and China mix drove the de-rating; the dossier's "fell anyway" framing is fair but incomplete without the guide/China context.
7. **KLAC "record FCF" (minor):** $1.34bn confirmed; "record" not independently verified from a primary source.
8. **Analyst targets are stale-sensitive:** all three names' consensus targets sit 36–63% above price after a −35% to −57% drawdown, with several firms cutting targets in late Oct / early Nov (LRCX: B. Riley, UBS, Needham; FIX: UBS, Oppenheimer). Treat mean targets as lagging indicators, not price anchors.
9. **LRCX June-peak multiple (unverified):** the "~28x forward P/E at the June peak" claim has no supporting source found; trailing P/E peaked at ~54x (quarter-end) to ~77x (daily) in June 2026. If the memo keeps a peak-multiple comparison, it needs a sourced forward-EPS basis.
10. **LRCX HBM step-count (unverified):** the "2–3x more etch/deposition steps" ratio is not sourced; the supportable formulations are "~3x wafer area per bit (≈4x+ for HBM4E)" and "200–300 process steps per HBM build".
