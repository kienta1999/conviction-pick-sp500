## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** KLAC
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam is the largest plasma-etch supplier in a 3–4 firm oligopoly, with ~45–50% of the global etch market, and its Q1 FY2027 was a record — revenue $8.22bn (+54% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $2.20 (+75% YoY), record 52.4% gross margin — yet the stock sits 45.5% below its June high at 19.3x forward earnings, the cheapest of the US equipment trio. AI is a demand multiplier, not a threat: HBM needs 2–3x more etch/deposition steps than standard DRAM, and there is no software substitute for physically carving silicon. Management guides WFE spending to >$200bn in 2027 and Lam's systems revenue to grow >40% in CY2026, its third straight year outperforming WFE. The dip is a multiple compression plus a supply-capped guide plus the 5 Nov moratorium panic — the business accelerated into it.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence: Lam beat and grew on every line — revenue +54%, EPS +75%, record margins, EPS beats 4/4 quarters — and fell 12.64% the day after that beat, then 16.4% on 5 Nov, the same day it announced a $25bn buyback. The market sold a capital-return announcement into a political sentiment shock; no customer was lost, no fab cancelled, no share lost. Orders and earnings are still accelerating; only the multiple fell.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW risk. Etch/deposition tools must be qualified fab-by-fab, recipe-by-recipe, over months, and a failed etch step scraps whole wafer lots — switching costs are extreme. AI cannot route around etch or deposition; it increases Lam's content per wafer (HBM, deeper 3D NAND). The only dent: deposition is a four-firm race and Lam is cyclically exposed to memory capex timing, so the moat is 9/10, not 10/10.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Already firing — the $25bn buyback authorisation (5 Nov 2026) plus a 27% dividend raise to $0.33/share; the stock rose 8.2% on 6 Nov and ~10%+ on 9 Nov on it. Next hard proof point: Q2 FY2027 earnings 27 Jan 2027 against a beatable $8.6bn revenue / $2.33 EPS guide. Sentiment reset: NVDA reports 17 Nov, and any clarification that moratoriums exempt projects already under construction de-risks the 12–18 month delay fear.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+50–55% — re-rating toward the analyst mean target of $364.03 (+54.3% upside) as the forward multiple normalises toward the low-20s on FY2027 EPS growth of ~+65%. Bull ~+80–85% — a return to the June 2026 high of ~$433–438 if WFE >$200bn in 2027 materialises and the buyback compounds the share-count shrink.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** If the moratorium fear is a real demand delay rather than a permitting delay, hyperscaler builds slip 12–18 months and Lam's memory-heavy mix gets hit first — the 19x forward P/E is then priced on earnings that get cut. China export controls remain a standing overhang, and a 2.03 beta means it falls hardest in any further risk-off leg.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
