## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** LRCX
- **Runner-up:** FIX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Lam Research is the cleanest "price broke, business didn't" name in the set: Q1 FY2027 revenue rose 54% YoY to $8.22bn and non-GAAP EPS rose 75% to $2.20 with a record 52.4% gross margin, yet the stock sits 45.5% below its 52-week high at 19.3x forward earnings — the cheapest of the three US equipment names despite the fastest current revenue growth. The drawdown is a multiple compression plus a supply-constrained guide plus the 5 Nov data-center moratorium panic, not a lost customer, cancelled fab, or share loss. A $25bn buyback authorised on 5 Nov, a 27% dividend raise, and $3.70bn of net cash mean the company is buying its own dip from strength, not survival mode.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that fundamentals accelerated into the sell-off: revenue +54%, EPS +75%, record margins, EPS beats in 4 of the last 4 quarters — and the stock fell 12.64% the day after that beat, then another 16.4% on 5 Nov, the same day the buyback was announced. A business that is permanently impaired does not print its best quarter ever while the market sells it on politics.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Lam holds ~45–50% of the global etch market in a 3–4 firm oligopoly, with fab-by-fab, recipe-by-recipe qualification that takes months and where a failed etch step scraps whole wafer lots. AI increases Lam's content per wafer — HBM needs 2–3x more etch/deposition steps than standard DRAM — and there is no software substitute for physically carving silicon.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Already firing — the $25bn buyback and dividend raise (5 Nov) plus the bounce from ~$185 in early Nov to $235.88 by 11 Nov. Next proof points: NVDA's 17 Nov print and any clarification that moratoriums exempt projects already under construction, then Q2 FY2027 earnings on 27 Jan 2027 against a beatable $8.6bn revenue / $2.33 EPS guide. Medium-term: WFE spending guided to >$200bn in 2027.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +35–45% (partial re-rating toward ~$320–340 as the forward multiple normalises toward the low-20s on growing earnings); bull case roughly +54%+ toward the $364.03 analyst mean target (and the $360–385 target range cited in the dossier) if WFE >$200bn in 2027 materialises and the buyback compounds.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Memory capex is cyclical and Lam is memory-heavy: if the moratorium fear proves to be a real 12–18 month demand delay rather than a permitting delay, WFE >$200bn in 2027 slips, the supply-constraint guide may be masking softening order intake, and the "cheap" 19x forward P/E is priced on earnings that get cut. High beta (2.03) means it falls hardest in any further risk-off leg, and China export controls remain a standing overhang.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
