### Top 3 detail

**KLAC:** The temporary-dip evidence is already in print, not promised: record Q1 FY27 revenue of $4.136bn, a record 46.1% operating margin, a $12.5bn backlog (+58.2% year-on-year at end-FY2026), and a December guide roughly 4.8% above consensus. Base case is a re-rating toward the dossier's pre-print median target of $227 (roughly +36% from $167.43); bull case is a move beyond that if the over-$200bn 2027 WFE preview and advanced-packaging strength hold.

**HWM:** The catalyst is also delivered and unusually complete: Q3 2026 revenue $2.703bn (+29% year-on-year, +25% organic), adjusted EPS $1.46 versus $1.37 consensus, record quarterly FCF of $562m, raised FY2026 guidance (adjusted EPS $5.49–5.51), first FY2027 revenue guidance of about $11.5bn, and a $5bn buyback addition. Base case is a continued grind back from still -29.6% off the high as Q4 confirms the print; bull case is a fuller multiple re-rating if gas-turbine share gains and the plan to double industrial gas-turbine sales within three years stay on track.

**GNRC:** The catalyst is contracted, not just guided: a data-center backlog of about $4.5bn, nearly tripling from about $1.6bn in July, an Amazon long-term supply agreement with initial deliveries of $2.4bn in 2027–2028, and 2027 data-center revenue guided to well exceed $2bn versus nearly $500m in 2026. Base case is that Q4 shipments and the Q4 print (Feb 2027) validate the backlog and the stock holds its post-Amazon repricing with further upside; bull case is a second hyperscaler (about $700m expected in 2027) plus a third in negotiation adding a further leg.
