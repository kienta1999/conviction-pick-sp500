# Phase 3.5 Independent Verification — stock-pick-dip, RUN_DATE 2026-10-13

Scope: the 12 sub-claims (a–d for each of CPRT, HWM, IDXX) as briefed. Checked 2026-10-13 against company releases / earnings-call transcripts / SEC-filed figures where available, plus reputable financial press and named data vendors (StockAnalysis, MarketBeat, Zacks, Public.com) for consensus/valuation figures, which are vendor-dependent by construction. Verdicts are per sub-claim; where a sub-claim bundles a correct narrative with a wrong figure, the verdict flags the figure.

**Headline finding: one material error in the panel's tentative single pick.** CPRT's "operating margin compressing from ~35.1% to ~28.5%" is mislabelled and mis-scoped — those numbers are (approximately) Copart's **Q4 net** margin, not its full-year operating margin. FY26 operating margin was ~35.4%, down only ~110bp from ~36.5% in FY25. The dip thesis for CPRT does not depend on a ~660bp operating-margin collapse, because that collapse did not happen at the stated level.

## 1. CPRT — Copart (tentative single pick)

### 1(a) Why-down cause — **CONTRADICTED** (on the margin figure; the volume/revenue part is confirmed)

- FY26 revenue growth of only +0.4%: **CONFIRMED.** Company Q4/FY26 release (Business Wire, 2026-09-10): FY26 revenue $4,666.2M vs $4,647.0M in FY25 (+0.4%); Q4 revenue +2.4% to $1,152.4M. Press coverage (Financial News, 2026-09-10/11) attributes the flat year in part to the tough comparison against FY25 hurricane (Helene/Milton) volume.
- Operating margin "~35.1% → ~28.5%": **CONTRADICTED.** From the same company release (segment table): FY26 operating income $1,652.6M on $4,666.2M revenue = **35.4% operating margin**; FY25 was $1,696.7M / $4,647.0M = **36.5%** — a compression of ~110bp, not ~660bp. Q4 alone: $368.9M / $1,152.4M = 32.0% vs 36.7% a year earlier. The panel's pair of numbers instead matches Q4 **net** margin: $327.4M / $1,152.4M ≈ 28.4% vs $396.4M / $1,125.1M ≈ 35.2% a year earlier. So the figure conflates net with operating margin and a single quarter with the full year.
- Volume trough, not share loss: **largely CONFIRMED, with one disclosed exception.** Q4 call (Motley Fool transcript, 2026-09-11): U.S. insurance units fell 8% for FY26 and total units fell for the year, driven by industry collision-claim frequency down 3.4% YoY and the lapse of FY25 catastrophe volume; revenue per unit rose ~5.7% for the year and international revenue grew double-digit. Caveat the panel should carry: management also disclosed the loss of one U.S. customer — U.S. insurance assignments would have risen 2.3% excluding that loss — so "not share loss" is true in aggregate but not literally complete.

### 1(b) Catalyst / timing — **UNVERIFIED** as a forecast; the earnings date is **CONFIRMED** (as an estimate)

- "Volume/margin normalisation visible in coming quarterly prints" is a forward prediction; by definition it cannot be confirmed from sources today. Supporting context exists in the Q4 call — claim-frequency declines moderating versus FY25, total-loss frequency rising (23.3% in Q2 FY26), non-insurance/dealer volumes inflecting, and management's stated focus on cutting facility cost per unit — but none of that guarantees normalisation in the next prints.
- Next earnings 2026-11-19: **CONFIRMED as the estimated date.** MarketBeat, Zacks, StockAnalysis and Tradestie all list Thu 2026-11-19 (after close) for Q1 FY27, explicitly as estimated/not yet company-confirmed (MarketBeat, accessed 2026-10-13). One vendor differs: TipRanks lists Nov 18 as "confirmed". Treat Nov 19 as the working date, not a company-announced one.

### 1(c) Valuation — **CONFIRMED** (with vendor variance noted)

- Forward P/E ~15.1: **CONFIRMED in one vendor, lower than others.** Tradestie (updated 2026-10-12) prints Forward PE 15.1 for CPRT at $26.48. StockAnalysis (2026-10-12 close) prints 16.33 and MarketBeat 16.25–16.76 — same order of magnitude, definition/timing differences. No source contradicts the "mid-teens forward multiple" substance.
- ~31x 5-year average P/E: **CONFIRMED.** Public.com (accessed 2026-10-13): 5-year average P/E 31.15 (3-year 31.5). Other vendors put the 5-year average at 29.1–30.4 (Wisesheets, FullRatio, FinanceCharts) — "~31x" sits inside the vendor range.
- Analyst target $39.00: **CONFIRMED.** StockAnalysis (S&P Global poll of 12 analysts, 2026-10-12): average price target exactly $39.00 (median $37, range $25–$55), consensus Buy. TipRanks' 7-analyst average is lower ($37.67), so the $39.00 figure is vendor-specific but exactly sourced.

### 1(d) Moat / balance sheet — **CONFIRMED**

- Zoning-scarce yards / two-sided network: **CONFIRMED qualitatively.** Insider Monkey moat analysis (2026-10-11) describes salvage yards as large sites near major cities that municipalities are not zoning anew at workable cost, plus single-network insurer routing where volume attracts bidders and bidders win the next contract. Corroborated by Columbia Business School case material (duopoly with IAA, IOS land constrained by zoning/NIMBY) and Ad-hoc News (2026, infrastructure/network-effects moat, zoning and permitting barriers).
- ~$5.7B liquidity and no debt: **CONFIRMED, in management's own words.** CFO Leah Stearns, Q4 FY26 call (2026-09-10, via MarketBeat transcript): ~$5.7B of liquidity at end-July 2026, comprising ~$4.5B in cash equivalents and held-to-maturity securities plus ~$1.25B of revolver capacity, with no debt outstanding. Balance sheet in the company release agrees: cash/restricted cash $1,907.9M + held-to-maturity securities $2,581.9M; no borrowings line; total liabilities $913.9M (payables, leases, taxes).
- FY26 operating cash flow $1.604B exceeding net income $1.480B: **CONFIRMED, primary source.** Consolidated cash-flow statement in the company release (Business Wire, 2026-09-10): net cash provided by operating activities $1,604,492k; net income $1,480,397k. Exact match to the panel's figures.

## 2. HWM — Howmet Aerospace

### 2(a) Why-down — **CONFIRMED** (one date nuance)

- SpaceX in-house foundry news: **CONFIRMED.** Elon Musk posted on **Aug 30, 2026** (not September) that SpaceX would cast its own gas-turbine blades/vanes at a Texas foundry, claiming turbines up to 18 months sooner; HWM fell ~8% in one session (Webull/Barron's-syndicated piece) and ~10–11% around Sep 4 by other accounts (Webull, Money Morning, 2026-09-09). The trading damage therefore landed in the first September sessions — "September selloff" is substantially right, the originating post was Aug 30.
- GE's $11.75B CPP deal (Sep 8): **CONFIRMED, primary source.** GE Aerospace's Sep 8, 2026 release / Form 8-K: agreement to acquire Consolidated Precision Products from Warburg Pincus and Berkshire Partners for $11.75B ($7B cash, balance new debt), closing expected H2 2027. HWM fell ~10.7% on the deal (Substack deep-dive citing the move; Insider Monkey, 2026-09-14; AInvest, Sep 2026) on fears GE would insource castings.
- Not Howmet's own results: **CONFIRMED.** The immediately preceding print (Q2, Aug 6) was a beat-and-raise (see 2(b)); no company-specific bad news preceded the falls. Management publicly said it was comfortable with the GE deal (Reuters via Stockopedia news log, 2026-09-09), and JPMorgan/Bernstein commentary framed capacity scarcity, not lost demand, as the read-through.

### 2(b) Catalyst — **CONFIRMED**

- Q3 earnings Oct 29, 2026: **CONFIRMED.** Company announcement Oct 1, 2026: Q3 results Oct 29, 2026, 7:00 a.m. ET (via Ad-hoc News, 2026-10; StockAnalysis lists Oct 29 as the confirmed date).
- Following a Q2 beat-and-raise (+24% revenue, +46% EPS): **CONFIRMED.** Q2 2026 (reported Aug 6, 2026): revenue $2.55B, +24.1% YoY, above ~$2.43B consensus; adjusted EPS $1.33, +46% YoY, above ~$1.24 consensus; FY26 guidance raised to revenue $10.0–$10.1B (from $9.575–$9.725B) and EPS $5.23–$5.31 (from $4.88–$5.00) (Zacks, 2026-08-11; StockTitan/SEC-filing summary; company call transcript via MarketBeat).

### 2(c) Valuation — **CONFIRMED** on the target; **UNVERIFIED** on the forward P/E

- Analyst target $332.25: **CONFIRMED as a vendor figure.** Finquota and Deepscope both print a $332.25 target price for HWM (22 analysts, accessed 2026-10-13); Stockopedia's consensus is $332.68. Note real vendor dispersion: StockAnalysis (S&P Global, 22 analysts, Oct 12 close $220.81) prints $328.70 and MarketBeat (23 analysts) $312.67 — the $332.25 figure is sourced, but it is the high end of the vendor range.
- Forward P/E ~34.0: **UNVERIFIED.** No reputable source located prints 34.0. StockAnalysis prints a (NTM) forward P/E of 37.39 at the Oct 12 close; Zacks prints a forward 12-month P/E of 41.75 in recent coverage. A ~34x figure is arithmetically consistent with price ÷ FY27 consensus EPS ($220.81 / ~$6.46 ≈ 34.2), i.e. it is probably a second-year-forward multiple — but as stated, without that definition, it does not match the standard forward P/E prints and must be marked unverified.

### 2(d) Moat / leverage — **CONFIRMED**

- Certification/qualification gating for flight-critical castings/forgings: **CONFIRMED in substance.** Sources consistently describe slow, expensive qualification as the barrier: AInvest's CPP analysis (Sep 2026) notes only a handful of foundries can cast flight-safety blades with regulator-grade paperwork and that a new foundry cannot be casually certified; industry research (GII/METI, 2026) notes AS9100/Nadcap/FAA/EASA standards and multi-year qualification for critical engine parts. Precision note: the gating is customer/regulatory qualification of each part and process, not a single "FAA certificate" for Howmet — the panel's shorthand is acceptable but should not be read literally.
- Net debt/EBITDA ~1.4x: **CONFIRMED, primary source.** CFO Patrick Winterlich, Q2 2026 call (2026-08-06, via MarketBeat transcript): net debt to trailing EBITDA finished Q2 at 1.4x following completion of the CAM acquisition (cash $564M; $450M commercial paper drawn for CAM).

## 3. IDXX — IDEXX Laboratories

### 3(a) Why-down — **CONFIRMED**

- Vet-visit softness (Q2 visits −1.3%, wellness −3.4%): **CONFIRMED.** CFRA stock report (2026-09-27, summarising the Q2 report of Aug 4, 2026): U.S. same-store clinical visits declined an estimated 1.3%, with wellness visits down 3.4% the primary constraint. The Q2 call (via BigGo/TIKR summaries) carries the same −1.3% figure and management guided to ~−1.5% to −2.0% H2 visit declines at the Sep 14 Morgan Stanley conference.
- Not share loss: **CONFIRMED on the evidence available.** In the same Q2, CAG Diagnostics recurring revenue grew 10.3% organically — a ~1,100bp growth premium over U.S. clinical visits, in management's own framing — with double-digit U.S. and international recurring growth and 11% growth in the global premium instrument installed base. No located source reports share loss to Zoetis/Antech in the period.
- Multiple compression: **CONFIRMED qualitatively.** The stock was down ~22.7% YTD at early October (Simply Wall St, Oct 2026) and ~15% over 52 weeks at Oct 12 (StockAnalysis) despite EPS beats — i.e. the drawdown is a de-rating against rising earnings, consistent with the panel's framing.

### 3(b) Catalyst — **CONFIRMED**

- FY guide raised in Q2: **CONFIRMED.** Q2 2026 (Aug 4, 2026): FY26 EPS guide raised to $14.69–$14.94 (from $14.45–$14.90) and revenue guidance set at $4.70–$4.745B (Zacks via Ad-hoc News; BigGo call summary; company guidance reiterated in MarketBeat coverage).
- Next earnings 2026-11-02: **CONFIRMED.** StockAnalysis lists Nov 2, 2026 as the **confirmed** date (before open); Zacks, Barchart and Public.com agree (Nov 2, 2026).

### 3(c) Valuation — **CONFIRMED** on the target; **UNVERIFIED** on the forward P/E

- Analyst target $701.33: **CONFIRMED, exact.** StockAnalysis (S&P Global poll of 14 analysts, Oct 12 close $523.94): average price target exactly $701.33 (median $705, range $470–$805), consensus Buy. Other vendors are higher (MarketBeat $747.00 on 10 analysts), so again the figure is vendor-specific but exactly sourced.
- Forward P/E ~31.3: **UNVERIFIED.** No reputable source located prints 31.3. StockAnalysis prints a forward P/E of 33.49 (Oct 12); ValueInvesting.io prints 33.9 as of 2026-10-10; MarketBeat prints 34.68; Barchart 37.60. As with HWM, ~31x is arithmetically consistent with price ÷ FY27 consensus EPS ($523.94 / ~$16.62 ≈ 31.5) — a second-year multiple, not the standard forward print. As stated, unverified.

### 3(d) Moat / leverage — **CONFIRMED**

- #1 pet diagnostics, razor/blade, installed-base/cloud switching costs: **CONFIRMED qualitatively.** Multiple sources describe IDEXX as the clear market leader in veterinary diagnostics with an estimated ~45% market share, a razor-and-blade model (instruments placed, ~79% recurring revenue from consumables, reference labs and software) and an integrated ecosystem creating retention (AInvest, 2025; TS2/GuruFocus summary, 2026). The Q2 2026 numbers support the mechanism in-period: 5,265 premium instruments placed in Q2 including 1,602 inVue Dx, recurring diagnostics +10.3% organically.
- Net leverage ~0.5x: **CONFIRMED, primary source.** CFO Andrew Emerson, Q2 2026 call (2026-08-04, via MarketBeat transcript): IDEXX finished the period with leverage of 0.6x gross and 0.5x net of cash. (Q1 call gave the same 0.6x/0.5x pair; the 10-Q leverage calculation is of the same order.)
