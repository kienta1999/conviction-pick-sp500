## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLA is the best business in this panel on quality metrics: a dominant process-control franchise with a record 46.1% non-GAAP operating margin and 62.7% gross margin in FQ1 FY27, revenue +29% YoY to a record $4.14B, and a Q2 guide of $4.7B (+43% YoY) — growth that is accelerating into the sell-off, not decelerating. Returns on capital are exceptional (ROE 91.38% per screen; FCF $1.34B in the quarter, 32.3% of revenue), and process-control intensity rises with every node shrink, so AI's demand for leading-edge wafers directly increases KLA's content per wafer. The stock fell ~57% from its June high because its trailing P/E de-rated from 71x to ~31x, not because the franchise weakened — a premium-quality compounder repriced as if its growth had broken, when the print says the opposite. At fPE 20.53 it is the priciest of the equipment trio, but it is the only name here with a 10/10 moat and the last budget cut in a fab.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is the sequence: KLA reported its strongest quarter in margin terms (record operating margin, record FCF), guided revenue +43% YoY for the next quarter, holds purchase commitments of $8.87B (3.5x a year earlier), and the stock still fell 8.4% on 24 Nov alone — the multiple broke, the model did not. The dossier's own verdict is that the earlier target trims (UBS to $180, Needham to $200) were "tempered multiples rather than deteriorating fundamentals."
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. KLA is the "eyes of the fab": no fab can yield a 2nm / HBM4 line without KLA-class inspection, qualification is multi-year, and the installed base plus defect-library data creates switching costs no rival has replicated at scale. AI cannot substitute for physical inspection — an AI model can analyse defect data, but it cannot see a wafer; if anything AI increases the value of the data KLA collects. Least exposed of the equipment names to Chinese substitution (Bernstein).
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q2 FY27 earnings ~27 Jan–4 Feb 2027, on a guide of $4.7B revenue (+43% YoY) — a beat there plus a CY2027 WFE/services update is the natural re-rating trigger; the March analyst day is the next formal catalyst after that. The $8.81B buyback authorisation remaining (with $936m bought back in FQ1, the second-highest quarter on record) provides a floor in the meantime.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base case roughly +50–65% — a recovery toward the MarketBeat consensus target of ~$220.35 (+65% at $133.58) as the market re-underwrites guided +43% growth at something closer to a historic multiple. Bull case roughly +80–100% — a re-rating toward the Bernstein $225 target and beyond if KLA outgrows a >$200B WFE year in 2027 and the multiple moves back toward its five-year norms (not found as a precise forward series; trailing P/E range 11x–71x). Both scenarios assume WFE is delayed, not cancelled.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Paying 19–20x forward for a cyclical whose multiple was 71x five months ago — if WFE disappoints, the de-rating has further to run, and a return to the low end of its 5-year trailing range (~11x) would be another ~40% down even with earnings flat. China is 34% of Sep-quarter revenue with export-control risk, and capital returns already consume ~93% of FCF, so a downturn leaves little buffer to increase buybacks when they would matter most.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
