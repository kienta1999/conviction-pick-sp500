## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** KLAC
- **Runner-up:** LRCX
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** KLAC is the one name in the 15 where the dip is a multiple breaking, not the business breaking: FQ1 FY27 (reported 28 Oct 2026) was a record — revenue $4.14B, +29% YoY, non-GAAP operating margin a record 46.1%, FCF a record $1.34B — and Q2 guidance accelerates to $4.7B (+43% YoY) with EPS $1.44. The stock still fell ~57% from its $307.37 June high to $133.65 on 24 Nov, as its trailing P/E halved from 71x at the June quarter-end to ~31x, while earnings grew. That is a de-rating of the highest-moat business in the panel, not an impairment of it, and the rebound needs only the Jan–Feb 2027 print to confirm the guide — not a cycle turn, a legal win, or a Fed pivot.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The single most compelling evidence is that the business accelerated *into* the sell-off: record revenue, record 46.1% operating margin, record FCF, purchase commitments $8.87B at 30 Sep (3.5x a year earlier), and a Q2 guide of +43% YoY — with the dossier attributing the fall to "a sector-wide sell-off and investor rotation out of high-momentum semiconductor and AI-related stocks, rather than weakening company fundamentals" and to post-print target trims (UBS to $180, Needham to $200) that were "tempered multiples rather than deteriorating fundamentals".
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk (10/10 in the dossier — the highest score in the panel). KLA is the "eyes of the fab": dominant process-control (inspection + metrology), with qualification measured in years, an installed base plus defect-library/yield-analytics data no rival has replicated at scale. AI cannot substitute for physical inspection — an AI model can analyse defect data but cannot see a wafer — and shrinking nodes, HBM stacks and advanced packaging *raise* process-control intensity per wafer. Advanced-packaging process control is ~$1.1B in CY2026, +70%+ YoY.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q2 FY27 earnings ~27 Jan–4 Feb 2027 (Finnhub 27 Jan; MarketBeat est. 4 Feb) on the $4.7B (+43% YoY) guide, plus a CY2027 WFE/services update; the March analyst day is the next formal catalyst after that print. KLA has already corroborated WFE >$200B in 2027, expects to outgrow WFE, and returned $1.24B in FQ1 ($936m buybacks — second-highest quarter on record — + $305m dividends) with $8.81B authorisation remaining.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Rough base / bull case: base — the multiple stabilises rather than re-rates fully, earnings compound on the +43% guided quarter, and the stock recovers roughly half the drawdown toward the lower analyst targets (UBS $180 / Needham $200), i.e. roughly +35–50% from $133.65. Bull — a clean Q2 beat plus a 2027 WFE endorsement re-rates it toward the MarketBeat consensus $220.35 (+65% at $133.58) / Bernstein $225, i.e. roughly +60–70%. No precise 12–18mo price target is in the dossier — the figures above are the dossier's cited analyst targets, and the dossier warns they are stale / being marked down even as estimates rise.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Paying 19–20x forward (fPE 20.53, the highest in the equipment batch) for a cyclical whose trailing P/E was 71x five months ago: if WFE disappoints, the de-rating has further to run — a return to the low end of its 5-year P/E range (~11x) would be another ~40% down even with earnings flat. China is 34% of Sep-quarter revenue with export-control risk; rising DRAM costs in shipped processing computers hold gross margin near 62.5% until mid-to-late 2027 pricing resets; and capital returns consumed ~93% of FCF, so a downturn leaves little buffer to increase buybacks when they would matter most.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
