Instrument installed base (VetLab, inVue Dx — ~5,500 placements targeted in 2026 after a record 6,000+ in 2025) drives high-margin recurring consumables; 10,000+ clinics run IDEXX cloud practice-management software; proprietary assays (SDMA/Catalyst integration); acquired CoVetAI (AI ambient listening for vet workflows) on Sep 16, 2026. **4. Balance-sheet survival:** Fortress. Cash $196.9M at Jun 30, 2026 (H1 operating cash flow $613.4M vs $423.7M prior year); H1 2026 free cash flow $556.8M (+55% y/y), Q2 FCF $322.5M vs $151.6M; debt/equity 0.19 (MarketBeat, Sep 2026); repurchased $697.8M of stock in H1 2026 — a company returning cash, not raising it. **5. Valuation:** fwd P/E ~30.9 (brief) / MarketBeat fwd 34.68, trailing 35.92 — a real discount to its historical 40–45x premium multiple. Consensus target $747 (MarketBeat, Oct 5, 2026), +44–45% above ~$518 (UBS/Citi $670; Barclays Overweight $710, cut from $800; Piper Sandler Overweight $750). Low of targets $670.

**6. Category position:** #1 in companion-animal diagnostics with demonstrated pricing power (~4% net price realization guided); no evidence of share loss — the issue is visit volume, not competitive displacement.

**7. Value-trap risk:** The bear case: US pet-ownership normalization continues, discretionary wellness visits keep declining faster than the guided −1.5–2%, and Zoetis/Thermo Fisher point-of-care competition compresses the 63%-gross-margin/32%-operating-margin structure. A 30x+ multiple on decelerating organic growth is the knife — if 2026 organic growth misses the 7.7–9.7% guided range, the de-rate has another leg.

**8. Rebound score: 7/10.** Verdict: Best-in-class diagnostics compounder de-rated on macro visit fears; fortress balance sheet and raised guidance make the dip buyable, but the multiple demands execution on inVue placements and price realization.
