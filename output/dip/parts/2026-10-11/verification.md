### Claim 2: SEC affirmed tokenized securities carry the same governance/compliance obligations as traditional equities
**CONFIRMED** (with caveat). The SEC staff's **joint statement on tokenized securities, issued January 28, 2026**, described custodial models and confirmed that tokenization does not alter a security's legal status or remove its obligations (CoinDesk research summary, Oct 2026; adbytes.media, Oct 2026; financialinvestor24, Jul 2026 all date the statement Jan 28, 2026). The Ondo Finance/Broadridge partnership PR cites it as the basis for extending existing compliance (proxy voting via Broadridge's ProxyVote.com) to tokenized holders. Caveat: it is **staff guidance with no legal force** (Columbia Law Blue Sky blog, Oct 2026, on the SEC Innovation Exemption); a later SEC Innovation Exemption Order additionally requires tokenized NMS stock to preserve shareholder rights (dividends, voting, liquidation distributions). The dossier's SouthernSun quote ("the SEC has affirmed…") is the letter's phrasing of this same January statement.
- https://www.coindesk.com/research/the-future-of-equity-ownership-an-issuer-s-guide-to-tokenized-equities
- https://www.financialinvestor24.com/2026/07/05/ondo-tokenized-us-securities-debut-with-full-shareholder-voting-rights/
