## Single-pick ballot — Agent B (Growth/quality compounder)

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** SYK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the purest compounder in the field — ~11,700 installed da Vinci systems generating 85% recurring revenue, a 20M+ procedure dataset no competitor can replicate, and a fortress balance sheet (~$9B cash, zero net debt). The dip is demand-side softness (GLP-1 bariatric cannibalization, ACA subsidy expiry, tariffs), not share loss — the Q2 beat was genuine and da Vinci 5 is mid-cycle. It now trades at 33.5x forward, its cheapest multiple in 7 years, for a business still compounding procedures at double digits. When the GLP-1 headwind laps and procedure growth re-accelerates, both the earnings line and the multiple have room to expand — the classic two-engine rebound a compounder dip offers.
- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The installed base and recurring-revenue flywheel are untouched — 85% recurring revenue and surgeon lock-in don't disappear because GLP-1s cut bariatric cases for a few quarters. The Q2 print was a real beat; the only thing that changed is the guide (13.5–15.5%, slowest since 2020) on explicitly temporary headwinds. Demand is deferred and macro, not lost to a rival.
- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW disruption risk — AI is a tailwind (NVIDIA partnership), not a threat. Robotic surgery requires FDA-cleared hardware, surgeon training, and a procedure dataset accumulated over 20M cases; no LLM replaces a scalpel. Surgeon lock-in plus capital-equipment certification barriers make this the hardest moat in the panel to attack.
- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 earnings 10/20 confirms procedure-growth stabilization; the da Vinci 5 rollout cycle and GLP-1 headwind lapping over the next 2–4 quarters restore double-digit procedure growth visibility.
- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base +20–25% (~$500–520 on procedure growth re-accelerating to mid-teens and a modest re-rating toward the historical multiple); Bull +40% (~$580, earnings beat cadence returns plus a da Vinci 5 upgrade super-cycle and multiple expansion back toward the 40s).
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The 13–14% procedure-growth guide is structural, not cyclical — if GLP-1s and ACA subsidy loss permanently remove the bariatric leg and 50x never returns, the stock is a fair-weather grower bought at a still-premium 33.5x, grinding sideways as the multiple de-rates further.
