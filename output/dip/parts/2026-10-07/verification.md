## NFLX (Netflix) — top-3

### 1. Lost the ~$83B Warner Bros. bidding war to Paramount Skydance in early 2026; kept a ~$2.8B termination fee booked in "interest and other income" H1 2026

**CONFIRMED** — from primary/press sources:

- Bidding war: Netflix signed a definitive agreement 12/4/2025 to buy WBD's studios/streaming assets for ~$82.7B enterprise value; Paramount Skydance countered with an all-cash $31/share bid for all of WBD (~$110–111B); on 2/27/2026 WBD terminated the Netflix agreement and merged with Paramount Skydance instead; Netflix declined to match. The combined deal (renamed Skydance, ticker SKYD) closed 10/6/2026. (multiple outlets; talkesport.com timeline; brandcom.ng; colitco.com)
- Termination fee: Netflix's Q1 2026 10-Q (SEC filing, via StockTitan): "PSKY, on behalf of WBD, paid a $2.8 billion termination fee owed to Netflix… The $2.8 billion termination fee received was recorded in 'Interest and other income (expense)' … during the three months ended March 31, 2026." (stocktitan.net; simianx.ai analysis confirms H1 2026 interest-and-other-income of $2,903.8M vs $90.5M a year earlier, essentially all booked in Q1.)

### Cash-collected question: has the $2.8B been collected, or is it still a receivable?

**Collected — CONFIRMED.** The 10-Q language is explicit: the fee was "paid" (by PSKY on WBD's behalf) and "received" (by Netflix) in Q1 2026 — i.e., cash in hand, not a receivable. Corroborating: (a) WBD booked the $2.8B outflow in its Q1 2026 results (democrata.es, 5/2026, citing WBD's statement); (b) Netflix raised its 2026 free-cash-flow forecast to ~$12.5B from ~$11B on the fee (Motley Fool, 9/18/2026); (c) H1 2026 interest-and-other-income of $2,903.8M sits in the cash-flow statement's income line. One technicality: the WBD statement notes the $2.8B could theoretically be reimbursed to PSKY in narrow scenarios if the Paramount deal fell through — the Paramount deal closed 10/6/2026, so that scenario is moot.

### 2. YouTube hit a record ~14.2% share of US TV viewing time in July 2026 while Netflix slipped to ~7.8%

**CONFIRMED** — Nielsen's July 2026 monthly Gauge report, widely reported: YouTube took a record **14.2%** of US TV viewing time in July 2026; Netflix held **7.8%**, down from its then-record 8.8% in July 2025; gap widened from 4.6pp to 6.4pp. (fool.com, 10/3/2026; thestreet.com, 9/2026; cordcuttersnews.com citing Nielsen's Media Distributor Gauge; ad-hoc-news.de, 9/2026)

### 3. Q3 2026 earnings scheduled for Oct 20, 2026

**CONFIRMED** — Netflix Investor Relations (announced 9/14/2026): Q3 2026 results and business outlook on **10/20/2026** at ~1:01 p.m. PT, followed by a management video interview at 1:45 p.m. PT.
