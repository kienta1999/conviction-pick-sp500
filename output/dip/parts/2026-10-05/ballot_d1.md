## Single-pick ballot

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Deckers is the only name in the dossier where the cheapness is about the growth *rate*, not the business: HOKA grew +11.1% and UGG +10.1% in the latest quarter, international +29%, gross margins 56.2%, and the company is net-cash (~$1.6B cash vs ~$472M debt) trading at ~10x forward earnings with $2.2B of buyback authorization still live. Wholesale discipline (Dick's, REI, Nordstrom — protected full-price sell-through) and the DTC engine make this a brand moat, 6/10 per dossier, and the AI angle is near-zero.

Earnings: FY26 guide EPS $6.30–6.39, so even modest multiple repair does the work.
- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Hoka is a fad cycle and the deceleration doesn't stop at mid-teens — footwear fads (Crocs 2022) can collapse past the point of cheap-looking multiples, and tariff-driven price hikes on $150+ shoes could structurally shrink the addressable market.
