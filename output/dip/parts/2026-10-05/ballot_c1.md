# Panelist C ballot — Moat & AI-Irreplaceability — RUN 2026-10-05

Lens: category dominance, balance-sheet durability, irreplaceability. AI/substitute
disruption = veto-level criterion. Score basis: the research dossier dated 2026-10-05.

## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** ISRG
- **Runner-up:** SYK

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Intuitive Surgical is the closest thing to an unassailable
  moat in the entire dossier: 11,710 da Vinci systems installed, 20M+ procedures,
  surgeon training/certification lock-in, and 85% recurring revenue from instruments
  and service contracts — all behind a patent and regulatory-clearance wall no
  competitor has cracked in 20 years. The 35% drawdown is a growth-rate derate
  (procedure growth 12% vs 17% last year, GLP-1 bariatric softness, ACA noise), not
  a franchise crack — management even raised the margin guide, and ISRG has
  recovered from eight prior 30%+ drawdowns. AI here is a complement that makes
  the robot more valuable, not a substitute that removes it. At ~44x trailing vs a
  95x peak and with zero debt and $8.6B cash, this is the rare moment the best
  medical-device franchise trades at a merely expensive multiple.

- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The deceleration inputs are all
  cyclical/one-off — bariatric weakness from GLP-1 drugs, ACA subsidy expiry noise,
  tariff drag — while demand-side signals never broke: 468 system placements in
  Q2, international procedure growth +20%, da Vinci 5 ramp just starting, Ion
  procedures +36%. Even the "first real competition" (Medtronic Hugo, J&J Ottava)
  has yet to convert a single meaningful share point.

- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** LOW risk (9/10 irreplaceability). No AI model
  removes a surgical robot, a sterilized instrument set, or the surgeon's hands.
  The disruption arrow points the other way: AI-enhanced vision/planning raises
  the value of the console, and Intuitive is absorbing it into its own digital
  offerings.

- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q3 2026 earnings 10/20/2026 — the procedure-growth
  checkpoint. Re-acceleration toward the top of the 13.5–15.5% guide, or da Vinci 5
  placement beats, is the multiple-restoring event. RBC's $575 target sits ~47%
  above the current price.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** base ~+25-35% (multiple re-rating toward
  ~55x on restored high-teens procedure growth); bull ~+50-70% (procedure growth
  re-accelerates to 16%+, da Vinci 5 replacement cycle confirmed, Hugo/Ottava
  narrative dies).

- **Top risk (value-trap case):** Procedure growth breaks below 12% and stays
  there (ACA uninsured surge, bariatric collapse deepens, China tenders award
  share to Hugo), and the 44x multiple keeps compressing toward 25x on mid-teens
  growth — an expensive derate on a decelerating compounder.

- **Conviction (1-10):** 8

### Top-3 extended notes

**1. ISRG.** The single most compelling data point: 85% of Q2 revenue was
recurring (instruments, services, leases) against an $8.6B net-cash fortress —
the dip came from a procedure-growth deceleration (12%) and a held guide, yet
op margin expanded 33.6% and eight prior 30%+ drawdowns all proved transitory.
AI is a complement (digital/learning offerings), not a substitute. Base ~+25-35%
/ bull ~+50-70% over 12–18 months (10/20 earnings is the gate).

**2. SYK.** Most compelling: #1 global knee arthroplasty with the Mako robotics
ecosystem (2.5M+ procedures, record Q1 installations, Mako 4/Shoulder/RPS
positioning on both hospital and ASC tracks) trading at ~21.6x forward — roughly
half its historical 25–30x multiple and a fraction of its ~95x peak — while Q2
op margin already rebounded to 25.2%. The Inari backorder and cyberattack are
one-plant/one-off damage, not a franchise crack. Base ~+30% / bull ~+45-55% if
the Oct 29 print confirms manufacturing resolution.

**3. SPGI.** Most compelling: the core engines are at records — Ratings +17% on
strong debt issuance, S&P DJ Indices at a 13th straight record quarter — while
the stock sits −26% off its high on spin-off reporting noise and estimate
resets. A bond cannot be issued without a rating and the S&P 500 benchmark
cannot be routed around; AI is a distribution upgrade, not a substitute.
Base ~+30-35% / bull ~+45% on clean comparable quarters plus the >$7B buyback
firehose (53rd straight dividend year).

### Deliberately left out of the top 10 (and why)

- **ADBE — VETOED on this lens.** The generative-AI commoditization fear is not
  sentiment, it is the business-model question: net new ARR −38% y/y in Q3
  FY2026 under the freemium pivot, paid conversion unproven, management itself
  admitting Firefly cannibalizes its own stock library. 1B MAU must convert, not
  cannibalize. Value-trap risk is real: AI permanently compresses willingness
  to pay for Creative Cloud.
- **BKNG — left out.** AI-agent disintermediation (Meta Muse and peers capturing
  trip origination, Booking reduced to a commoditized inventory pipe at
  compressed take rates) is the single most credible business-model threat in
  the dossier. Growing bookings 9% today does not disprove it.
- **EFX — left out, flagged value trap.** The FHFA bi-merge campaign is a
  genuine moat repricing of the tri-merge scoring margin, not a cycle — if the
  regime changes, the old 25–30x multiple never comes back, and the +48% analyst
  upside assumes a benign regulatory outcome not yet known.
- **BRO — left out.** Soft CAT-property pricing cycle plus $17.3B debt on
  ~0% organic growth; cheapness is earned until organic growth returns. AI fear
  here is overstated (Barclays agrees it's a tailwind), but simple-lines
  placement is the real long-term exposure.
- **TPR — left out.** A leather handbag brand has zero AI threat but a moat
  made entirely of sentiment — Kate Spade's $855M impairment is what Coach's
  fashion cycle looks like when it turns, and Coach is now 86% of revenue.
