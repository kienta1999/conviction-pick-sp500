AI literally drives its demand; it cannot be routed around the chemistry and power of a rack.

UIG acquisition at 13x *projected* 2027 EBITDA with undisclosed financials adds capital-destruction risk if hyperscaler capex is pulled forward into 2026.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7.5
