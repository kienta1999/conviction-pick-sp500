# BALLOT A1 — 2026-09-22 (Panelist A: Mean-reversion / catalyst analyst)

## 1. Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** BR
- **Runner-up:** BKNG

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Broadridge fell −31.2% on a narrative, not on numbers: the stock was repriced as an AI-disintermediation casualty while FY26 reported fundamentals were fine (8% recurring revenue growth, 12% adj EPS growth on 8/4/2026) and FY27 guided to 6–8% recurring / 8–12% adj EPS growth. Its core franchise is a legally-mandated near-monopoly in proxy and regulatory communications — regulation is the moat, and AI cannot vote shares or file with the SEC. At ~15x forward vs a historic 25–35x multiple, the de-rating is pure sentiment compression with a dated catalyst (Q1 FY27 print ~Nov 2026) to unwind it. A closed-sales rebound on that print, layered with a 12% dividend hike and a new $1.5B buyback, gives this the panel's cleanest setup for a fast snap-back.

- **Why the dip is temporary (not permanent):** The only real fundamental datapoint bears cited — Q3 FY26 closed sales −19% YoY — is being treated as secular decline when the business says pipeline timing; meanwhile actual revenue and EPS growth kept compounding and the company guided forward with confidence. Nothing about AI changes the regulatory requirement that every shareholder gets proxy materials and every issuer files through compliant rails. The dip is priced as disruption of a business that is legally un-disruptable in its core franchise.

- **Moat / AI-irreplaceability:** Low risk. Proxy delivery and regulatory communication are compliance obligations, not workflows an AI agent can absorb — the SEC filing and shareholder-vote rails are the moat. The tokenization-bypasses-intermediaries tail risk is a decade-scale thesis, not a 2026 earnings problem.

- **Rebound catalyst & timing:** Q1 FY27 earnings (~early November 2026): a closed-sales rebound vs the −19% comp. Consensus target $216 (+32.5%) gives the sell-side room to mark up on confirmation.

- **12-18 month return scenario:** Base ~+30–35% (multiple partially repairs to ~20–22x on recurring growth holding 6–8%). Bull ~+50%+ (multiple re-rates toward 25x historic mid as the AI narrative dies on two consecutive closed-sales prints).

- **Top risk (value-trap case):** The closed-sales decline IS secular — issuers move proxy work to cheaper/embedded rails and FY27 guidance proves optimistic, in which case 15x is the new fair price and the stock grinds sideways as recurring growth decelerates.

- **Conviction (1-10):** 8
