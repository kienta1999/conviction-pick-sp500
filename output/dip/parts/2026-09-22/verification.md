**Claim 4 — Moat: proxy distribution and SEC filing composition are legally mandated functions requiring a registered/auditable intermediary (regulation as moat against AI disintermediation).**
**CONFIRMED (qualified — contains material overstatement).** Factual core holds for proxy distribution: SEC Regulation 14A rules (Rule 14a-13, Rules 14b-1/14b-2, Rule 14a-16) legally obligate brokers/dealers to forward proxy materials and requests for voting instructions to beneficial owners, promptly and impartially — Broadridge is the agent chosen by most banks/brokers for this process. Qualifications: (a) the law mandates the *function* (forwarding) be performed by brokers/dealers, not specifically by Broadridge or any single "registered/auditable intermediary" — competitors exist ("The remaining banks and brokers engage other service providers"); (b) SEC filing composition via EDGAR is NOT legally mandated to go through an intermediary — issuers can file directly, so that half of the claim is inaccurate; (c) "regulation itself is the moat against AI disintermediation" is an analytical inference, not a verifiable fact. The moat is real but rests on network scale + switching costs + regulatory plumbing, not a statutory monopoly.
Sources: 17 CFR §240.14b-1/14b-2 (govinfo.gov); SEC Rule 14a-16 final release 34-55146 (sec.gov); Broadridge issuer guidebook (broadridge.com)

## Flags
1. **BR-4 overstatement risk:** the dossier's "legally mandated functions requiring a registered/auditable intermediary" is only half-true. Proxy forwarding has SEC-mandated mechanics (Reg 14A), but the law does not mandate Broadridge (or any intermediary) specifically, and EDGAR filing composition has no mandatory-intermediary rule at all — issuers can file directly. The "regulation is the moat" conclusion is analysis, not fact; the real moat is scale + bank/broker integration + switching costs.
2. **SPGI-2 currency:** Morningstar's $570 FV is from ~March 2026; their later company report shows $589. The claim is accurate as stated but dated.
3. **BR-5 timing nuance:** Board actions (dividend hike, $1.5B buyback authorization) were taken 2026-08-03 and *announced* with earnings 2026-08-04 — consistent with the claim.
4. **IDXX-1 "second raise":** supported by guidance progression ($14.29–14.80 initial → $14.69–14.94 at Q2) but the Q1 intermediate raise was inferred, not separately verified in this pass.
