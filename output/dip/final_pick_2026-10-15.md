# Dip conviction pick — 2026-10-15

## THE PICK: SPGI — S&P Global Inc.

Financials / Financial Exchanges & Data. Price 409.99; market cap about 120.9bn; forward P/E 20.3; analyst mean target 516.19 (+25.9%). Next earnings 2026-10-27 (12 days out).

**Thesis.** S&P Global is a licensed ratings duopoly and benchmark-index franchise that the market has de-rated twice for reasons verification showed are not franchise damage: a February 2026 AI scare that prices substitution the numbers do not show, and a July post-spin guidance recast that looked like a cut but left the organic guide unchanged while Ratings and Indices were raised. It trades at 20.3x forward — the cheapest of the SPGI/MCO/MSCI trio — on a 45% operating margin, about 5.6bn of trailing FCF and a business (credit ratings, indices, data) whose core is licensed, regulated and embedded. The dip buys the toll road, not the traffic jam.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

Two stacked, dated causes, neither a demand loss: (1) the Feb 2026 Anthropic-driven "SaaSpocalypse" sold data/software names indiscriminately — the dossier estimates under 7% of SPGI revenue is genuinely AI-exposed (verifier note: that figure is a Seeking Alpha analyst estimate, not a company disclosure, and is treated here as indicative, not proven); (2) the Mobility spin-off completed 1 Jul 2026 forced a guidance recast that screens as a cut — the verifier confirmed from the call transcript that the organic guide held at 6–8%, Ratings was raised to 5–8% and Indices to 12–14%. Q2 revenue grew 10.4%. A guidance artefact plus a sector scare is price, not business.

## The dip depth

Price is -20.1% off its 52-week high and -2.3% below its 200-day SMA (dist_sma200 -0.023) — the shallowest SMA dip in the kept set, which is the honest weakness of this pick: the dislocation is in the multiple (trailing P/E compressed to about 25x, forward 20.3x vs peers at about 28–30x), not in a capitulated price. You are buying a multiple gap, not a crash.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

Credit ratings are a licensed duopoly (SPGI/Moody's) embedded in bond covenants and regulation; the S&P 500 index franchise collects a toll on global equity assets that cannot be replicated by a model; Market Intelligence and Commodity Insights are workflow-embedded data with high switching costs. AI can summarise filings — it cannot issue a regulated rating, license the benchmark, or manufacture the historical datasets. Irreplaceability 9/10 (panel lens C's #1). The honest disruption statement: generative AI plausibly pressures the commoditised edges of Market Intelligence (desktop data, research distribution) over years — that is the bear case's engine, and it is a margin story, not a franchise-ending one on any evidence in hand.

## The rebound catalyst & timing

Q3 2026 earnings on **27 Oct 2026** (company-confirmed via PR Newswire, 29 Sep) is the proof point: a clean print on the raised Ratings/Indices guides re-rates the multiple toward peers. Beyond it: debt-issuance recovery lifts Ratings volumes, index inflows compound Indices revenue, and continued buybacks on about 5.6bn FCF shrink the share count into the re-rating. Base case lands by 2027-Q2.

## Scenarios & expected value

Bottoms-up builds on the depressed driver (the multiple and the optics) recovering toward baseline. Research scenarios, not guarantees.

- **Bear — 320 (-21.9%) by 2027-Q2:** the AI case turns real in Market Intelligence at the same time issuance slumps; organic growth halves, earnings power is impaired at a de-rated about-17x multiple. This price is the stop.
- **Base — 516 (+25.9%) by 2027-Q2:** the raised guides are delivered (Ratings 5–8%, Indices 12–14%, organic 6–8%), the spin-off optics wash out, and the multiple mean-reverts partway toward peers — landing exactly on the analyst mean target of 516.19.
- **Bull — 590 (+43.9%) by 2027-Q3:** an issuance upcycle plus index inflows push growth above guide and the multiple fully re-rates to about 27x; this needs the cycle and the re-rating to fire together, which compounds the risk.

**Probabilities — bear 0.25 / base 0.50 / bull 0.25.** Base carries half the weight because the guide was already raised mid-year and Q2 delivered double-digit growth — the base case is the company's own trajectory, not a hope. Bear gets a full quarter (above a token weight) because the AI-exposure estimate is unverified analyst work and issuance is cyclical. Bull gets a quarter because it needs two things at once. Honesty clause: the bear case is not the most probable scenario, but it is a live one.

**EV** = 0.25 × 320 + 0.50 × 516 + 0.25 × 590 = **485.50**, +18.4% vs the 409.99 price — clears the +15% guardrail, so this publishes as an actionable pick, not a pass.

**Market-implied scenario:** the price sits between bear and base, closer to bear — the market is paying for the scare, not the guide. The stock trades below (not above) its analyst mean target.
