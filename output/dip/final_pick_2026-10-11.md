## THE PICK

**BR — Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc.** — Industrials / Data Processing & Outsourced Services. Market cap ~$18.8B. Entry price **$165.45**.

## One-paragraph thesis

Broadridge is a near-monopoly piece of SEC-regulated financial plumbing — proxy distribution, shareholder communications, and capital-markets infrastructure for US broker-dealers — that has been de-rated 28% off its 52-week high on a narrative that AI and tokenization will disintermediate the "middleman." The fundamentals contradict the narrative: Q4 FY2026 (Aug 4, 2026) beat ($3.82 adjusted EPS vs $3.76 consensus), FY2027 EPS guidance was raised ($10.37–10.75), and FY2026 closed sales hit a record $305M — while the SEC's own staff (Jan 28, 2026 joint statement on tokenized securities, verified) affirmed that tokenization does not alter a security's legal status or remove its governance obligations. You buy a 40%-ROE near-monopoly at 14.3x forward earnings with a 2.6% dividend, not a distressed turnaround.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence: the dip's entire cause is a *story* about future disintermediation, while every reported number is accelerating. Barchart (Sep 16, 2026) attributed the underperformance explicitly to "concerns that artificial intelligence could disrupt its technology and business-services operations"; Motley Fool (Aug 4, 2026) cited the same over the prior year's 32% decline. Against that, the SouthernSun Q2 2026 letter — quoted in full — documents 7% recurring revenue growth, 11% adjusted EPS growth, raised guidance, and the counter-narrative: (a) the SEC affirmed tokenized securities carry the same governance obligations, (b) clients in-sourcing proxy face asymmetric compliance/liability risk, (c) multi-year contracts make migration gradual. Broadridge itself is building governance for tokenized assets ("enabling Governance solutions for tokenized assets… integrating tokenized assets into our Wealth and Capital Markets platforms" — CEO Tim Gokey, Aug 4, 2026 call). Nothing in the actual business broke.

## The dip depth

−28.0% off the 52-week high ($237.98), −1.3% below the 200-day SMA, −28.5% trailing 12 months. The drawdown is a pure multiple de-rating on narrative — no operational event, no guidance cut, no share loss.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

#1 in proxy processing and fixed-income trading infrastructure in the US. The moat is regulatory plumbing: SEC-regulated intermediary status, deep switching costs, multi-year contracts, and the asymmetric compliance risk any client faces by in-sourcing. AI digitizes the workflow — which Broadridge is itself doing via agentic AI — but does not remove the regulated intermediary. AI cannot disrupt it because the moat is legal, not computational. Irreplaceability: 9/10 (moat lens).
