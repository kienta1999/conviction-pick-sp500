## One-paragraph thesis

Intuitive Surgical is the closest thing to an unassailable medical-device
franchise trading at a discount: 11,710 da Vinci systems installed, 20M+
cumulative procedures, 85% recurring revenue, zero debt and $8.6B cash — derated
35% off its high on a procedure-growth deceleration (12% vs 17%) and a first
real competition narrative, not a franchise crack. The deceleration inputs are
all cyclical or one-off (GLP-1 bariatric softness, ACA subsidy noise, tariff
drag) while demand signals never broke (468 Q2 system placements, international
procedure growth +20%, da Vinci 5 ramp on track with 246 placed in Q2), and the
Q3 print on 10/20/2026 is the dated checkpoint for re-acceleration.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence: eight prior 30%+ drawdowns since IPO, every
one transitory — and this one came from a *held* guide, not a cut, with the
operating margin expanding to 33.6% on the same quarter the price fell. The
CFO attributed the deceleration to GLP-1 bariatric softness and ACA subsidy
expiry on the Q2 call (cyclical/one-off), management raised the margin guide,
and the "competition" (Medtronic Hugo's first US case Feb 2026, J&J Ottava
FDA clearance) has yet to convert a single meaningful share point. This is a
growth-rate derate — price, not business.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

da Vinci owns the installed base, the surgeon-training/certification pipeline,
and the patent/regulatory-clearance wall no competitor has cracked in 20 years.
Instruments and service contracts make revenue 85% recurring — the business
compounds even when system placements pause. **Why AI or a substitute cannot
disrupt it:** no AI model removes a surgical robot, a sterilized instrument
set, or the surgeon's hands. The disruption arrow points the other way:
AI-enhanced vision and planning raise the console's value, and Intuitive is
absorbing it into its own digital offerings (irreplaceability 9/10). The honest
caveat: if Hugo/Ottava start winning major tenders, the multiple story changes
— but that is years ahead of any share reality today.

## The rebound catalyst & timing

Q3 2026 earnings **10/20/2026 (after close)** — the procedure-growth checkpoint.
Re-acceleration toward the top of the 13.5–15.5% full-year guide, or da Vinci 5
placement beats, is the multiple-restoring event. RBC's $575 target sits ~47%
above the current price.
