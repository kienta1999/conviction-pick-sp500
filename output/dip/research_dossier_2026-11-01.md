## SNDK — Sandisk (rebound 6/10, irreplaceability 7/10)

- **Why it's down — TEMPORARY/cyclical with cycle-timing risk.** Fiscal Q1 2027 (reported 29-30 Oct 2026) was a record beat — revenue \$11.03B (+378% YoY), data-centre revenue \$4.8B (+1,682% YoY) — yet the stock fell 13.47% on 30 Oct 2026 to the screen price on peak-pricing fears: crowded positioning, AI-ROI de-rating, and the question of how long NAND pricing holds.
- **Moat:** scale NAND manufacturing + contracted price floors; AI does not replace storage, it consumes it — but new capacity (2027-2029) and Chinese supply cap pricing power.
- **Catalyst:** contracted floors holding through 2027; next prints confirming data-centre demand durability.
- **Balance sheet:** zero debt, \$5.14B cash — survives any drawdown; the risk is earnings, not solvency.
- **Valuation:** screen 4.0x forward P/E annualises peak-cycle earnings (85.1% non-GAAP gross margin in FQ1 2027 vs 29.9% a year earlier) and is optically absurd; Morningstar cut fair value to \$870 (30 Oct 2026), ~24% below screen price, vs the \$2105 screen target — the widest bull/bear dispersion in the panel. Margin of safety is the contracted floors, not the multiple.
- **Trap case:** a NAND pricing downcycle arriving before contracted floors roll off turns peak earnings into trough earnings and the "cheap" multiple evaporates.
- **Verdict:** a high-volatility cyclical rebound bet, not a quality-compounder dip.
