# Dip conviction pick — 2026-10-16

## THE PICK: IDXX — Idexx Laboratories

Healthcare / companion-animal diagnostics. Price 547.18; market cap about 41.0bn; forward P/E 32.7; analyst mean target 701.33 (+28.2%). Next earnings 2026-11-02 (17 days out).

**Thesis.** IDEXX is the global leader in companion-animal diagnostics — an installed base of in-clinic analyzers locked to proprietary consumables and a reference-lab network — whose stock has de-rated from a 45–55x forward history to 32.7x because US clinic visits have been soft since 2024. The business never broke: at the Q2 2026 print (4 Aug 2026) revenue grew +10% reported (+9% organic) to $1,217M, CAG Diagnostics recurring revenue grew +11% reported (+10% organic), and management raised FY2026 EPS guidance to $14.69–14.94 while narrowing the revenue range to $4,700–4,745M (midpoint +$5M, net of a $15M FX headwind) — as corrected by independent adjudication against the company's own release (see parts/2026-10-16/verification_adjudication.md). You are buying the toll road at a discount while its operator raises the toll forecast.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The derating driver is visit volume, not share, pricing, or product. IDEXX's own disclosures (2024 10-K; Q1 2026 release, 1 May 2026) tie the softness to US companion-animal practice traffic moderating after the pandemic adoption wave; no primary source attributes the drawdown to competitive loss, and the verifier confirmed this. Meanwhile the compounding layer kept compounding through the soft patch: recurring revenue is ~85–89% of the CAG segment (10-K segment disclosures, verified), instrument placements keep adding future consumable streams, and the Q2 guide raise was funded by delivered numbers (revenue +10%, recurring +11%), not by hope. A franchise whose recurring base grows double digits while its multiple compresses by a third is a price event.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

IDEXX holds roughly half of global companion-animal diagnostics spend (company/investor materials, verified). The moat is the razor/blade installed base: analyzers placed in clinics consume proprietary cartridges and reference-lab services for a decade-plus, switching means retraining staff and revalidating workflows, and the data layer (practice software, imaging, VetConnect) compounds the lock-in. **Why AI cannot disrupt it:** a model cannot run a blood panel. Diagnostics is a physical measurement business — samples, instruments, reagents — and AI is a feature IDEXX sells on top (image interpretation, workflow), not a substitute for the measurement. Irreplaceability 9/10 (lens C's #2; the dossier's joint-highest in the field with KLAC). The honest disruption statement: the risk is not technology but demand demographics — if US pet ownership and clinic visits decline secularly, the moat protects a shrinking pond. That is the bear case, and it is a volume story, not a moat breach.

Beyond it, analyzer placement cadence and premium instrument mix drive recurring-revenue compounding into 2027. Base case lands by 2027-Q2.
