## One-paragraph thesis

Keysight is the largest pure-play electronic test & measurement vendor, and it just reported the strongest quarter in its history — Q4 FY26 revenue \$1.994bn (+41% YoY), orders \$2.339bn (+53%), non-GAAP EPS \$3.61 vs \$3.37 consensus — and still fell 7.07% the next session because the Nasdaq fell 5.28% that day. The stock is 31% off its early-October peak near \$388–390 on a pure AI-trade unwind, while the business guides FY27 to ~20% revenue growth (\$8.588bn vs \$8.187bn consensus) with a 1.17 book-to-bill and a record backlog. At ~15.5x forward earnings with net cash and FY26 FCF of \$1.75bn, the market is pricing a cyclical test vendor while the business is behaving like a toll-taker on the AI build-out: every faster chip, 1.6T optical link and 6G radio must be tested, calibrated and certified before it ships, and AI bookings ran ~2.5x the prior year.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence is the order book. FY26 orders rose 49% against revenue up 33%; Q4 book-to-bill was 1.17; Q4 operating margin expanded 950bp to 35.8%. The only company-specific negative in the print was that demand exceeds shipment capacity on ~12–24 AI-related SKUs, with a \$200–300m excess backlog — a supply constraint, not a demand break. There was no miss, no guidance cut, no share loss; the Spirent acquisition even removed the closest rival. The drawdown tracks the November 2026 AI-infrastructure de-rating (the FT put AI data-centre suppliers' lost value since June at ~\$4.2tn), not a Keysight failure. A value trap shows falling orders, pricing collapse or share loss; Keysight shows the opposite on all three.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

Keysight holds ~29.9% of the electronic-measurement segment vs Rohde & Schwarz ~14.5% and NI (Emerson) ~11.2% (Verified Market Research, Feb 2026), and is the largest pure-play test & measurement vendor. The moat is precision RF/microwave and high-speed digital test IP built on HP/Agilent lineage, deep calibration/certification lock-in (customers must validate to standards — 5G-Advanced, 6G, 1.6T optical — with instruments whose accuracy is itself certified), PathWave software workflow lock-in, and instrument-specific test programs, fixtures and correlation data. The Oct 2025 Spirent acquisition (~\$1.46bn, closed 15 Oct 2025) consolidated its closest rival in high-speed Ethernet, network-security and RF channel-emulator testing — so dominant that the US DOJ sued in June 2025 to block it; the deal closed with divestitures to VIAVI (\$399m). **Why AI cannot disrupt it:** no AI model can certify a physical radio or optical transceiver without the instrument. AI increases the need for test equipment — it is a demand driver, not a substitute. The real threats are cyclical (semi/telecom capex) and low-end Chinese competition, not displacement. Irreplaceability scored 8/10 in the dossier.

## The rebound catalyst & timing

The \$275m FY27 capex to expand manufacturing capacity should start clearing the \$200–300m excess backlog over FY27 H1. Any stabilisation in AI-infra sentiment re-rates the multiple first, well before February — the print already exists; the market just needs one more quarter to believe it.
