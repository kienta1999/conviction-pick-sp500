### 1. Why it's down — verdict: TEMPORARY, with one genuine tail risk

- **Jul 13, 2026:** a **BofA note reported softer-than-expected e-commerce ad growth for the month of June**, questioning the AXON self-serve merchant rollout. Stock fell ~12.6% that day. Pomerantz opened an investor "investigation" Jul 16, 2026.
- Overlay: a June 2026 oil-price spike / US-Iran risk-off episode that hit high-multiple AI names hardest. Stock −24% in H1 2026, ~−34% YTD by mid-July.

**The "36.1% TTM growth" screen figure is misleading.** APP divested its Apps
(first-party games) segment; the screen compares TTM continuing-ops revenue
($6,164M) against a base year still *including* the divested Apps segment
(~$4.53B). Underlying advertising growth: **Q1'26 revenue +59% YoY**, FY2025

multiple compression plus narrative/legal risk.

**−45% from high vs +7.7% over 12 months:** the 52-week high (~$733 implied) was
set mid-window (Jan/Feb 2026); a year ago the stock was ~$371. It doubled, then

**The short-seller / SEC thread (the non-transitory item).** Fuzzy Panda and

**The model under investigation is the source of the 85% EBITDA margin.**

## VST — Vistra Corp.
**$142.81 · $48B · −34.2% off 52w high · −12.9% vs 200d SMA · −26.7% 12m**

### 1. Why it's down — verdict: TEMPORARY RE-PRICING, not a broken thesis (but the payoff *timing* genuinely slipped)
The AI-power thesis is not breaking; what broke is willingness to pay 2027–2030
earnings today.
- **Peak and unwind.** All-time closing high **$217.32 on Sep 22, 2025**; 52w high $219.82; 52w low near **$132.66**. **US IPPs have lagged the AI basket for 6+ months**, investors citing "underwhelming pace of power purchase agreement deals, distributed behind-the-meter solutions, and pressure for data centers to bring their own generation."
- **Feb 26, 2026 — the Q4'25 miss.** EPS **$2.13 vs $2.33 est (−8.6%)**, revenue **$4.58B vs $5.3B est (−13.6%)**; shares −5.3% pre-market. Wedbush headlined it *"Reality Check for the AI-Power Trade."* FY25 carried an **$808M unrealized pre-tax hedge loss** (non-cash MTM) that made reported numbers look bad. Q3'25 had also shown a "significant earnings shortfall."
- **Commodity/weather softness.** Raymond James flagged **softer ERCOT weather, lower load and weaker power prices**. Gas forecast in the **mid-to-upper $3s for four years**; **gas swings alone move ERCOT EBITDA by $500M–$1B annually**.
- **Sector beta.** On **Jul 27, 2026** VST fell alongside Constellation, Talen and NRG with **no company-specific news** — a group move on overbuilding/slower-payback fears.

**The regulatory and market signals are actually bullish — hence "re-pricing."**
- **PJM Base Residual Auction (held Jun 30–Jul 7, 2026) cleared at the $325/MW-day price cap** for delivery Jun 1, 2028–May 31, 2029, coming in **6.8 GW short of the reserve-margin target** while attracting only ~525 MW of new resources — the first auctions in PJM history where the entire RTO fell short of the reliability requirement. PJM will run a **one-time backstop capacity auction starting September 2026**; new large loads not bringing their own generation will be **curtailed near emergency conditions**. Data centers were **$6.5B of the $16.4B (40%)** of the December auction's cost.
- **Co-location resolved in generators' favor.** FERC issued a **unanimous order Dec 18, 2025** directing PJM to write clear co-location rules — **three new transmission service options** (incl. Firm and Non-Firm Contract Demand service) and reformed behind-the-meter rules, compliance from January 2026, **requested effective date Jul 31, 2026**. This removes the overhang from FERC's late-2024 rejection of the original Talen/AWS Susquehanna ISA — precedent since re-established by Talen's restructured **17-year, $18B, up-to-1,920 MW** AWS PPA (Jun 11, 2025).
- **Vistra's own contracting is landing.** Long-term PPAs totaling **~3,800 MW with Meta and AWS**, incl. **~2,600 MW with Meta at its PJM nuclear sites**; plus the **~$4.0–4.7B Cogentrix acquisition (5,500 MW of gas)**, expected to close 2H 2026.

**So why isn't the stock up? Vistra hedged away its own near-term upside.**
**~96% hedged for 2026 as of Oct 31, 2025** (~98% at Q1'26), **~89% for 2027,
~65% for 2028**. Correct risk management, wrong equity narrative in a rising-price
world: the scarcity in the 2028/29 capacity auction cannot reach the P&L until
2028+. **Critically, 2026 guidance excludes both Cogentrix and the Meta PPAs**,
whose contribution begins 2027.

### 2. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — **9/10** (highest in batch)
Vistra sells **electrons from NRC-licensed, already-interconnected, dispatchable
capacity** — the scarcest input to AI. AI is a *demand driver*; there is no
software substitute for a megawatt. Moat = **regulatory/permitting + capital
intensity + irreplaceability of the asset base**: Comanche Peak's 2,400 MW is
"one of the lowest-cost and highest-performing nuclear plants in the country,"
**NRC-relicensed in 2024 through 2053**; interconnection is multi-year queued and
**PJM attracted only ~525 MW of new supply at a $325/MW-day clearing price** —
definitive proof the asset base cannot be replicated on demand. Plus **~$15/MWh
nuclear PTC as a revenue floor through 2032** (~$100M/yr), a retail book (TXU) as
a natural hedge, and investment-grade ratings.

Deduction to 9: Vistra is ultimately a **commodity price-taker** — pricing power
is market-set, not firm-set; gas-on-gas competition sets ERCOT spot. It cannot
raise price, only capture scarcity when scarcity arrives.

### 3. Rebound catalyst
- **Aug 7, 2026 Q2 print** (10:00am ET) — nearest event.
- **FERC/PJM co-location tariff effective ~Jul 31, 2026** — removes the biggest structural uncertainty for behind-the-meter data-center deals; **days away**.
- **PJM backstop capacity auction beginning September 2026** — a second scarcity price signal inside 60 days.
- **Cogentrix close in 2H 2026 (5,500 MW)** + **Meta/AWS PPA contribution from 2027** — both *excluded* from guidance, so pure upward revisions when they land. Management has framed **2027 adj EBITDA of $7.4–7.8B ex-Cogentrix and ex-Meta**.
- **The hedge roll itself:** 2028 is only ~65% hedged, so the $325/MW-day cap flows through as the book opens.
- **Buyback:** $372M in Q1'26; ~**$6.3B repurchased since Nov 2021**, share count down to ~337M (**~30% reduction**); ~**$1.5B remaining** through YE2027, funded from FCF. Plus a completed $6.13B program and **investment-grade upgrades from two agencies**.
- ERCOT load growth **+6.1% in 2025**, up from 5.2% in both 2023 and 2024.

### 4. Balance-sheet survival — adequate; weakest of the three but not distressed
Screen net debt/EBITDA **2.93×** — genuinely levered vs APP (0.22×) and VRT (net
cash), but normal-to-conservative for an IPP with hedged, contracted cash flows,
and it carries **investment-grade ratings**. Q1'26: cash **$634M**, **total
liquidity $4,173M**. FY26 **adj FCF before growth guided $3.925–4.725B** against a
$48B market cap — a **~8–10% FCF yield**. Dividend $77M in Q1. Funding a $4B+
acquisition and a $1.5B buyback out of internal cash flow. A sustained gas-price
collapse would matter more here than at the other two. Total debt and stated
net-leverage ratio at Mar 31, 2026: **not found** (SEC 8-K exhibit returned 403).

### 5. Margin of safety / valuation — cheapest of the three
Mean target **$222.83 (+56.0%)**; a second source puts the Street mean at
**$225**, consensus **"Strong Buy" among 17 analysts** despite underperformance.
TD Cowen (Shelby Tucker) **cut to $230 from $253 on May 4, 2026, maintaining
Buy** — a trim, not a thesis change. **One quant model marks fair value far lower
at $130.74 (from $133.54)** — dispersion is wide; do not treat $223 as
consensus-safe. Screen forward P/E **13.6×**, trailing 24.9×. At $142.81 with
~337M shares, ~$48B cap against **FY26 adj EBITDA guidance $6.8–7.6B**
(EV/EBITDA high single digits incl. debt) and **management's 2027 EBITDA range of
$7.4–7.8B excluding Cogentrix and the Meta PPAs**. **The one name where the dip
produced a genuinely low absolute multiple and where 2027 is likely to be revised
*up* mechanically rather than earned.** The honest catch: 98% hedged for 2026
means you are underwriting 2027–2029, and 13.6× on a leveraged commodity
generator is not the same quality of 13.6× as a contracted utility.

### 6. Category position — top-tier, not losing share
One of three default AI-power vehicles (with Constellation and Talen); largest
competitive generator in ERCOT, **mixed fleet weighted to Texas gas plus
nuclear** — more diversified than Talen (single PA nuclear site), more
gas-levered than Constellation. **Added 4.5 GW of capacity** in line with
PJM/ERCOT forecasts and signed ~3,800 MW of hyperscaler PPAs. Some analysis
argues **Vistra and NRG are better positioned than the pure nuclear plays**
because dispatchable gas is what the grid needs at the margin.

### 7. Value-trap / bear case
(a) **Data centers bring their own generation** — the dangerous scenario isn't
less demand, it's demand that **bypasses Vistra entirely** via on-site gas
turbines, fuel cells and distributed behind-the-meter solutions. Already the
stated investor concern; the **KKR–Nvidia AI infrastructure partnership** is the
template for capital going around merchant generators. If hyperscalers
self-supply, Vistra captures tightness in *spot* but never gets the contracted,
re-rating PPAs. (b) **Gas prices** — mid-to-upper $3s; a $500M–$1B annual ERCOT
EBITDA swing on gas alone means a warm winter plus cheap gas wipes out a year of
thesis. (c) **The hedge book cuts both ways** — 98%/89% hedged means late
participation if power rips, and MTM losses ($808M in FY25) keep making GAAP
prints ugly. (d) **Political/regulatory backlash** — PJM capacity at the cap for a
third straight cycle has consumer advocates (CUB, Jul 15, 2026) demanding
data-center cost-allocation reform; **price caps were extended for two more
auctions through 2030 rather than raised**, and states can legislate away the
scarcity rent. That is the specific mechanism by which an AI-power winner becomes
a falling knife. (e) **2.93× leverage** in a rate-sensitive, capital-intensive
business. (f) **A quant fair value of $130.74 vs a $142.81 price** says at least
one credible model thinks it's already fully valued.

### 8. Rebound score **7/10**
*The AI-power thesis is re-pricing, not breaking — PJM cleared at the $325 cap
with a 6.8 GW shortfall and FERC settled co-location in generators' favor — but
Vistra hedged 98% of 2026 away, so this is a cheap, IG-rated, 8–10% FCF-yield
option on 2027–2029 that needs patience rather than a fast snap-back.*
