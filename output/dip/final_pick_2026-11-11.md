FIX is one of the largest US mechanical/electrical contractors serving data centers (verification note: "the largest" is unverified — sources say "one of the largest"). AI cannot do this work — licensed skilled craft installing HVAC, pipefitting and switchgear on congested job sites — and AI data centers are the demand driver, not the disruptor.
