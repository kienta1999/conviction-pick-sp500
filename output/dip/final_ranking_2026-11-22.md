## Per-name thesis

**1. FIX —** The dip is a pure multiple compression on a business accelerating into the selloff: Q3 revenue +53.7% YoY, EPS \$13.91 beating consensus, a record \$17.81bn backlog (+90% YoY, implied new bookings ~\$7.5bn, book-to-bill ~2.0x), and FY2027 estimates revised up to \$65.30 while the stock halved. Cash runs ahead of earnings (Q3 operating cash flow \$1.01bn vs \$489m net income), the balance sheet is net cash (~\$2.11bn on a financial-debt basis), and the work — physical HVAC/mechanical installation for data centers — is the most AI-irreplaceable in the batch (dossier score 9/10). Catalyst: H1 2027 margin normalisation, first tested at the Q4 print (expected mid-to-late Feb 2027, date unconfirmed).

**2. GOOGL —** The drawdown is a capex-financing shock, not a franchise break: Q3 revenue +25% to \$127.6bn, Google Cloud +103% to \$30.7bn with a \$561bn backlog, search still ~91% referral share with no share loss, and net cash ~\$140bn vs long-term debt. Trailing P/E of ~13.7–14.7x is the lowest in five years against a ~24x average. The watch item is the capex bill — two straight quarters of negative FCF — and whether AI Overviews dilute search monetisation even as share holds.

**3. CAT —** A guidance-gap dip on a record \$78bn backlog (+96% YoY): Q3 sales \$20.7bn (+17%), adjusted EPS \$7.20 (+45%), but management held full-year sales growth at "mid to high teens" and guided Q4 to only "moderate" growth with no 2027 number until January — and the stock fell 9.25% on Oct 29, then another -15.7% in the Nov 16–20 week. CEO Joe Creed identified manufacturing capacity as the primary constraint on Power & Energy growth: the dip is a capacity story, not a demand story, which is the temporary-dip shape — provided the January 2027 guide confirms the backlog converts.

**4. BKNG —** The business is still growing through the shock that supposedly broke it: Q3 room nights +6.7% (accelerating from +5.3% in Q2), gross bookings \$54.5bn (+9.6%), adjusted EPS \$4.66 (+17%, beating consensus), while the reported revenue decline was a \$900m FTC one-off at Priceline, not an operating collapse. The \#1 OTA globally, with a \$9.5bn buyback in 9M 2026 (\$12.4bn authorisation remaining) retiring ~8–9% of the float a year at this price, at ~12x forward earnings vs ~20x+ history.

**5. MU —** The fastest re-rating candidate and the riskiest: down 51% from its high on sentiment contagion while the business printed a sixth straight quarterly revenue record and guided Q1 FY27 even higher, with 2026 HBM capacity sold out and >75% of 2027 output committed under multi-year agreements. The Dec 17 print is the test. ⚠️ Value-trap flag: a 2.8x forward P/E on FY27 EPS of ~\$176.50 that could fall 50–80% if 2027 NAND/DRAM pricing rolls over — exactly as EPS fell 79% after the 2017–18 peak. The skeptic lens left MU out of its top 10 entirely.

**6. VST —** AI-power name derated with the AI trade: 2027 cash flows hedged, Meta nuclear PPAs contracted, analyst upside +71%. The watch item is leverage — net debt 2.4x EBITDA — in a financing-sensitive tape.

**7. NEM —** A fortress gold miner (net cash ~\$3.2bn, Q3 FCF \$1,912m, +22% YoY) whose dip follows gold, not its franchise. This is a gold-price call, not a company call: at \$3,500 gold the Q3 earnings spread narrows ~28%.

**8. APP —** The strongest operating momentum in the batch (Q3 revenue +55% YoY, 66% net margin, 84% adjusted EBITDA margin) with AI as its weapon, not its killer — capped by the open SEC probe and the Sep 2026 class action, a binary overhang that kept it out of every lens's top 2.

**9. ADBE —** The cheapest Adobe has been in a decade (7.9x forward P/E vs a ~23x 5-year average) with revenue still growing 13% YoY — but the market may be right: generative AI directly commoditises Creative Cloud, pricing power is being traded away for MAU growth, and the Dec 9 Q4/FY2027 guide under the new CEO is a binary event (Citi Sell, \$207, warning of a FY2027 "hard-landing-style reset"). ⚠️ Suspected value trap per lenses C and D.

**10. GS —** Near-record Q3, the \#1 deal franchise, at 10.6x forward earnings. A market-beta dip with no distinctive rebound catalyst — ranked last by three of four lenses, but ranked by all four, which is consensus of a different kind: nobody hates it, nobody loves it. LOW_CASH_CONVERSION flag is bank balance-sheet mechanics, per the dossier.
