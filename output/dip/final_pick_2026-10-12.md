# Dip conviction pick — 2026-10-12

## THE PICK
**SYK — Stryker Corporation**, Health Care Equipment (orthopedics #1 globally). Market cap ~$105B. Price at pick: **$277.33** (Oct 9, 2026 close).

## One-paragraph thesis
Stryker is a high-quality medtech compounder repriced ~30% for an operational hiccup, not a broken business: a supply disruption at the small Inari peripheral-vascular unit plus a CEO transition headline have compressed the forward multiple to ~18.5x (vs its typical mid-20s) while the underlying machine printed +9.4% revenue, +44% EPS and $4.7B FCF against $3.73B net income in Q2. The drawdown traces to one plant and disclosure timing — the Oct 29 Q3 print is a near-term clearing event, and even the value-trap skeptic on the panel picked it.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent
The entire -30% traces to two bounded events: (1) a "meaningful backorder situation with lost sales" at Inari (CEO Kevin Lobo, Jul 30, 2026) — a niche peripheral-vascular business, low-single-digit % of revenue — and (2) the Sep 8 admission that the disruption runs into Q4, after Q2 assurances of a Q3 fix. Revenue still grew 9.4%, gross margin is 65.6%, and FCF exceeds net income ($4.7B vs $3.73B) — a business in structural decline does not convert cash at 126% of earnings. Plaintiff-firm investigations (Pomerantz, Hagens Berman) are post-selloff noise, typical and usually fading.

## The dip depth
-27.8% off the 52-week high ($392.55), ~-15% below the 200-day SMA area (~$310), -25% over 12 months. Forward P/E ~18.5 vs own-history mid-20s; analyst consensus target $364–371 (+31–34%).

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact
#1 in orthopedics globally; Mako robotic joint-replacement platform with surgeon-training switching costs; dominant Instruments and Medical/Surgical equipment. **AI cannot disrupt it** — no model routes around a physical knee implant; Stryker's robotics make it an AI/robotics beneficiary. Irreplaceability 9/10. The dip revealed no moat erosion — Inari is a manufacturing execution issue, not competitive share loss.

## The rebound catalyst & timing
