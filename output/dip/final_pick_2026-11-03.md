## One-paragraph thesis

KLAC is a near-monopoly compounder on sale for sentiment reasons: it just printed a record quarter, raised its December guide and its 2027 wafer-fab-equipment outlook, and still trades 47.6% below its 52-week high because an AI-spending scare swept semiconductors in late October. The business did not break — shipments, backlog, and margins all made records — so the dip is a price dislocation to buy, with the December-guide delivery and the late-January 2027 WFE confirmation as dated re-rate triggers.

## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence the drop is about price, not the business: Q1 FY2027 (quarter ended 2026-09-30, reported 2026-10-28) set records — revenue \$4.14bn (+28.9% YoY), 46.1% non-GAAP operating margin, \$1.336bn quarterly free cash flow — while the shares fell, including -5.69% on 2026-10-30 alone, on sector sentiment. No lost customer, cancelled fab programme, or demand break was cited anywhere in the dossier; the selloff drivers were a CXMT DRAM headline, a Tesla capex trim, and a cautious Lam Research outlook — other companies' news, not KLAC's.

## The dip depth

Dist to 200-day SMA: -13.1%. Dist to 52-week high: -47.6%. Cause: a sector-wide AI-spending scare plus profit-taking after a strong run (the stock was still +30% over 1 year at the screen date despite the drawdown), not a company-specific break.

## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLAC holds 58% of the semiconductor process-control market (Gartner, via KLA's 2026-03-12 Investor Day deck; 2024 share 56%), roughly 6.5x the nearest competitor, and is effectively a near-monopoly at the most advanced nodes (N5/N3/N2/sub-2nm) and advanced packaging. The moat is process technology, patents, and qualification lock-in at leading-edge fabs. AI cannot disrupt it — AI-driven fab complexity is a demand tailwind, because more complex chips need more inspection, not less. The verified caveat: the 58% figure is for semiconductor process control as Gartner/KLA define it, not for all metrology broadly defined.

## The rebound catalyst & timing

Two dated triggers: delivery against the December-quarter (fiscal Q2 2027) revenue guide of \$4.7bn ± \$200m, confirmed at the next earnings print on 2027-01-28; and fuller 2027 market/segment expectations on that same late-January call, following the preliminary outlook that calendar-2027 WFE will exceed \$200bn. Backlog conversion through 2027 is the follow-through.
