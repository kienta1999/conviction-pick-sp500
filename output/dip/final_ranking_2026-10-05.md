## Per-name thesis

**1. ISRG.** The cleanest quality drawdown in the field: an 11,710-system
installed base, 20M+ cumulative procedures, 85% recurring revenue, zero debt
and $8.6B cash — derated 35% because procedure growth decelerated from 17% to
12%, not because anything broke. The deceleration inputs are cyclical/one-off
(GLP-1 bariatric softness, ACA subsidy noise, tariff drag; all CFO-attributed
on the Q2 call), the margin guide was raised, and eight prior 30%+ drawdowns
all proved transitory. AI is a complement here (enhanced vision/planning), not
a substitute — 9/10 irreplaceability. The 10/20 Q3 print is the falsifiable
checkpoint: re-acceleration toward the top of the 13.5–15.5% guide re-rates
the multiple; growth breaking below 12% breaks the thesis.

**2. SPGI.** A spin-off-noise discount on a benchmark monopoly: the July 2026
Mobility spin (MBGL) created pro-forma reporting confusion that made a real
beat look like a miss, while underneath, Ratings grew +17% on strong debt
issuance and S&P DJ Indices posted its 13th straight record quarter. A bond
cannot be issued without a rating and the S&P 500 benchmark cannot be routed
around (9/10 irreplaceability); AI is a distribution upgrade here. >$7B of
buybacks targeted this year mechanically shrink the float while the 53-year
dividend streak continues. At ~21-22x forward vs a historical high-20s
multiple, this is a compounder mispriced as a confused quarter.

**3. SYK.** A top-tier knee franchise (corrected: not the outright #1 in total
knee — Zimmer Biomet leads at 30.1% vs Stryker's 22.4% — but the leader in
robotic-assisted knee with the Mako ecosystem at 2.5M+ procedures) trading at
~half its historical 25–30x multiple after a March 2026 cyberattack and a
persistent Inari Medical peripheral-vascular manufacturing issue the CFO owned
up to on Sep 8 (−8.8% that day). The wounds are one-plant/one-off, not
franchise cracks; Q2 op margin already rebounded to 25.2%. The 10/29 Q3 print
is the defined status checkpoint — note the CFO pushed expected resolution
into Q4 2026, so this is a two-step re-rate, not a single binary.

**4. NFLX.** The deepest derate in the field (−46%) on a fortress: 33%
operating margins, pricing power intact, a huge buyback running. The dip is
sentiment + content-spend-cycle, with the ads tier as the re-rate engine and
the 10/20 Q3 print as the reset. Watch-list caveat from lens D: if the −8%
viewing decline is structural rather than a measurement artifact, content
costs rise structurally and margins stall.
