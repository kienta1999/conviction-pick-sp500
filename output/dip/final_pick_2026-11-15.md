## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

KLA holds ~56–58% share of process control and >85% of reticle inspection (verification: dominance confirmed; the re-qualification mechanism itself was not citable as a sourced fact, so it is stated here as the dossier's characterisation, not a verified claim). AI cannot disrupt it — AI *is* the demand driver: smaller nodes, 3D packaging and HBM raise defect sensitivity, so inspection intensity per wafer rises, not falls. No AI model can physically inspect a wafer, and KLA's own AI sits inside its tools (defect classification), strengthening the product. The moat score is 9/10 in the dossier; the one point off is customer concentration and China at high-20s% of CY2026 revenue under export-control exposure.
