2. **SYK** — Three transitory shocks (March cyber incident ~$375M deferred sales, Q2 Inari plant supply issue, June Russell growth-index removal) hit a business whose full-year guidance was *narrowed upward* to $14.95–15.10 at Q2. Installed-base medtech moat (9/10); 21.7x forward is near the bottom of history. Catalyst: Q3 Oct 29 — backlog conversion is the proof point. Skeptic's watch: one more guide wobble and the "serial disappointer" discount becomes deserved.
3. **CPRT** — The −38.7% drawdown is a cyclical insurance-unit trough plus one lost account (Progressive→IAA) plus CEO churn, against a zero-debt, 32%-margin salvage-auction exchange monopoly trading at ~17x vs ~29x forward history. Catalyst: the $1.9B all-cash ACV Auctions deal (announced Sep 10, closing by EOY 2026) accretes FY28 and extends the flywheel into dealer wholesale. Honest caveat: if the lost account was share loss rather than mix, the moat's pricing power is in question.
4. **CBOE** — The skeptic lens's own pick: −26.7% is pure multiple compression while Q2 set records (net revenue +25%) and guidance was raised; dividend +19% in August. Net-cash-like (0.7x leverage), exclusive SPX/VIX licenses, low AI-disruption risk (8/10). Watch: structural vol compression could make the sector derating permanent — that's the only trap vector.
5. **VRSK** — 45%-margin insurance-analytics duopoly derated >30% on AI-commoditization fear and a soft 2026 guide. The moat lens's runner-up: AI needs Verisk's regulatory-embedded dataset (39B records, statistical-agent status in all 50 states), it doesn't replace it (9/10). 22x vs ~36x history; reaffirmed guide. Slower rebuild — issuance trough, not erosion.

One line each for the rest: **IDXX** — strongest moat in the field but 34x forward leaves no margin of safety; **ADBE** — historic cheapness vs a genuine AI-seat-model question the moat lens won't forgive; **TPR** — cheap luxury turnaround capped by fashion-cycle durability; **LVS** — FCF fortress at the cyclical trough, but margin compression and $19B debt make the trap lens watchful; **BR** — fear-discounted governance plumbing whose disruption vector (tokenization) touches its core.
