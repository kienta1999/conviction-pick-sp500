**ADDITIONAL — the [RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN] flag, explained.** Netflix's annual accounts receivable were $2.031B at 12/31/2025, up 52.14% YoY (financecharts, Oct 2026) — well ahead of ~16% revenue growth. AR is still only ~4% of TTM revenue (~$48.4B), and TTM FCF (~$11B, +31%) confirms earnings are converting to cash. One-line verdict: **flag explained — the receivables build tracks the ad-business mix shift to arrears billing, not deteriorating collection; watch it only if AR keeps outrunning revenue after ad growth normalizes.**
