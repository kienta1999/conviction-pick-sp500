## Why the dip is temporary, not permanent

The single most compelling evidence is the shape of the fall versus the shape of the business: AMAT fell a further 2.7% on Fri 13 Nov *despite* the 12 Nov beat-and-raise, as analysts cut targets en masse (e.g. Jefferies \$770 → \$580, still Buy), and the whole semiconductor complex sold off that day (SOXX −4%, AMD −10%) after NVIDIA announced Jensen Huang's resignation on 12 Nov (CFO Colette Kress interim CEO) plus a 4-week high in the 10-year yield. No company-specific bad news: Q4 FY2026 was a record on every line, Q1 FY2027 guidance (\$11.1bn ± \$0.5bn revenue, \$4.35 ± \$0.20 EPS) came in above consensus, and management put a number on CY2027 WFE growth (>30%) for the first time. The dip is multiple compression on macro/AI-sentiment fears, not a broken franchise — the same pattern as KLAC, its closest peer, which fell −13.3% on 5 Nov with no company news at all.

## The dip depth

AMAT is −49.9% off its 52-week high (\$739.67) and −16.9% below its 200-day SMA (verification: the screen's −48.7% understates the drawdown; −49.9% is the arithmetic). ## Why it's the category leader / why the moat is intact

AMAT is the #1 WFE vendor by revenue, the broadest equipment portfolio in the industry (deposition, etch, inspection-adjacent process control, packaging), with switching costs that are structural: a fab's process recipes are qualified on AMAT tools, re-qualification takes quarters, and the installed base generates a growing service annuity. AI cannot disrupt it — AI *is* the demand driver: leading-edge logic, HBM and advanced packaging all need more process steps per wafer, and the CY2027 WFE >30% call is the company telling the market the capex cycle is accelerating, not digesting. Net cash of \$2.8bn (cash + investments \$9.34bn vs debt \$6.53bn) means it can fund the cycle and buy back stock through any digestion. The verified FY2026 free cash flow is \$6.20bn (\$2.62bn in Q4 alone, a record) — the screen's \$1.80bn TTM FCF figure is wrong by ~3.4x and must not be used.
