## Per-name thesis

**1. BNY — the unanimous pick.** Record: 4/4 beats, surprises +5.6% to +16% and widening, revenue accelerating (+7% → +13% → +13.3%), Q2'26 a record $5.70B print. Reaction record: 87.5% beat-to-up, last four report-day reactions +5.1/+2.2/+1.9/−2.0 — beats are genuinely rewarded here. Revenue direction: accelerating into the print with the FY guide raised twice (now revenue ex-notable +10–11%, NII +12–13%). Guide risk (5/10): the forward bar is a run-rate continuation, not an acceleration — but a reaffirm-only after two raises reads as a stall. Priced-in verdict: low — derisked, no run-up, 15.7% below the analyst target. Fallback: the world's largest custodian, $62.6T AUC/A, ROTCE 31.3% — a bad print is an event, not a business break. Plan: B (checklist overruled the unanimous lens Plan-A vote — see the pick writeup).

**2. BLK — the clean runner-up.** Record: 8/8 beats with the serial in-raise momentum of a $15.3T-AUM franchise. Reaction record: 87.5% beat-to-up, last four +6.6/+3/+5.9/−5.9 — beats are rewarded, and options are cheap. Revenue direction: accelerating (+30.6% YoY in Q2'26) with margin expansion. Guide risk (5/10): no formal guide — the "guide" is inflow momentum, which has been serial-positive; the forward ask is a leverage-acceleration debate, not a number. Priced-in verdict: low-medium — 12.6% below the high, fair 16.9x multiple, cheap options. Fallback: a 12–18 month hold is a quality asset-management franchise, not a punt. Plan B (researcher: Plan A conditional on the 10/13 money-center prints not showing a September vol collapse).

**3. BAC — winnable quarter, dangerous guide.** Record: 4/4 beats, 8-quarter streak. Reaction record: 75% beat-to-up, last four +2/+2/−4/+4 — thin but mostly rewarded. Revenue direction: modest (TTM +2.2%) but NII momentum intact. Guide risk (7/10): NII already guided to the top of 6–8% with nothing left to raise, and FY EPS implies a Q4 acceleration the run-rate hasn't shown — the market is carrying the raise, not the number. Priced-in verdict: low — the bar was cut 6.7% in a month ($1.19→$1.11), the stock is washed out (−13%), and Moynihan's IB-fees pre-warning (−10–20%) is already in the price. Fallback: the second-largest US bank deposit franchise — holdable. Plan B: needs an IB-fees beat plus something new on guidance.

**4. GS — a great record priced for perfection.** 8/8 beats at +20.5% average surprises — the largest in the field — but the reflex is beat-and-fade: the last two non-blowout beats fell (−2.2%, −1.9%), only the Q2 blowout rallied (+8%). Management pre-warned softer FICC and +$500M non-comp expenses. Guide risk (8/10): the implied Q4 (~$15–16 on a ~$68 FY) is the most demanding forward bar in the field, and estimates were cut into a rising bar. The stock is derisked (−12% month), but the implied 4.1% move exceeds the 3.5% historical proxy — you are paying for the event. Fallback is fine (premier advisory franchise), but this name needs a blowout, not a beat.

**5. JPM — serial beater that sells off on beats.** 3/4 beats and a 57.1% beat-to-up rate — barely above a coin flip, with three of the last four falling despite beats. Estimates were revised UP 8.4% over 90 days into a record Q2 comp, and implied 3.58% exceeds the 2.7% historical proxy. The guide trajectory (NII ex-Markets ~$96.5B, adj. expense ~$107.5B) is already raised, so the print has nothing new to say. Quality fallback, poor event record.

**6. MS — the classic beat-and-fade.** A record Q2 print (EPS $3.46 vs $2.89, equities +69%) moved the stock +0.4%; a +36% Q3'25 beat printed −2.6%. The name's reflex is to sell its own good news (beat-likelihood 6, priced-in 7). The bar was cut ~7% in 30 days, which helps the quarter, but the event evidence is damning. Fine wealth-management franchise underneath; terrible earnings vehicle.

**7. ISRG — the field's pure trap.** The only non-financial, and the only run-into-print flag (+17.6% in 21 sessions) at 35x forward. Q2 beat big and fell −14% on a *reaffirmed* procedure guide; the guide, not the beat, decides here, and the run-rate (US 12%, H1 16% vs 13.5–15.5% FY guide) supports a reaffirm at best — the same print that dumped −14% last time. Quality company, pristine balance sheet, worst setup in the field.

**8. FAST — falls on everything.** Fell on all 4 of the last 4 prints (the screen's +5% Q4'25 figure was corrected to −2.6% at Phase 2), with an average EPS surprise of +0.07% — it manages to the decimal and gets punished for it. 36x forward with a Hold consensus and a mean target *below* the price. Margins eroding three straight quarters. Serial in-liner at a growth multiple; the market needs margin recovery, not revenue growth. Plan B by default, with a low bar to clear — but nothing here clears it.
