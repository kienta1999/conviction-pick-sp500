## The fallback

Why this is a business worth owning for 18 months if the exit is ugly: corporate payments, cross-border, fleet/fuel and lodging spend controls embedded in ERP/accounting workflows, with network effects in fuel/lodging acceptance and card issuance. A large customer could in-source generic AP payments, but replicating fuel-card acceptance, cross-border rails, tax/rebate handling and spend controls is a multi-year build, and switching costs are high (93% retention cited Q2). Irreplaceability score 7/10 (dossier). At roughly 14–15x forward adjusted earnings for a double-digit organic grower with buybacks, a gap down is a valuation opportunity — provided the FTC matter does not expand beyond the \$100m preliminary charge. Balance sheet (screen): net debt/EBITDA 2.70x, operating margin 46.9%, ROE 29.2%. The honest caveat: the fallback EV below does not clear the guardrail, so this section is insurance reasoning, not a recommendation to hold through a bad print.
