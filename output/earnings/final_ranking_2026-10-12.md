## Per-name thesis (top 5)

**CME (#1).** The world's leading derivatives marketplace goes into Oct 21 with
~70% of the quarter pre-disclosed: Q3 ADV 29.4M +16% y/y, September record
31.8M +22% (Oct 2 company PR). Perfect 1.000 beat_up rate, no forward guide to
disappoint, 65% operating margins, Hold consensus, −11% off highs. The reaction
record is pristine — but the edge is thin (1.6% avgabs) and September's record
is public, so this is a moat-and-certainty pick more than an event edge. Plan B.

**BAC (#2).** See final_pick_2026-10-12.md — the panel's single-pick winner
(5 pts: B top, D top, A runner-up). Bar cut 6.7% in 30 days while Q2 printed
+34% y/y EPS; serial NII-guide raiser; priced-in risk 3/10, the lowest in the
field. The trap filter does not veto it. Plan B on the ticked guide-raised box.

**BNY (#3).** Four straight beats (+5.6% to +16.0%), four straight ~13% y/y
revenue quarters, the only name with an explicitly raised revenue guide
(Jul 15: 10–11% growth, NII +12–13%). −13.4% off highs. The Q3'25 precedent
(beat +6.8%, stock fell 2%) says "good" is expected — upside needs another
raise. Plan B.

**GS (#4).** Twelve straight beats, −22% off highs, 4.27% implied move — the
most de-risked bank setup. But Q2 was a record ($20.98 EPS) and GS self-flagged
softer FICC + $500M sequential non-comp expense growth. A show-me print. Plan B.

**MS (#5).** The most de-risked setup on price (−18% from highs, bar cut 7%),
but the tape's clearest "sells its strength" precedent: faded −4.4% after a
record Q2 with an +18% EPS beat. If trading normalized gently, relief rally;
if the estimate cut was right, dead money. Plan B.

**BLK (#6).** Best business, most reliable beater, accelerating inflows — and
the most priced for perfection (25.8x P/E, Strong Buy herd, Wells Fargo
initiated Oct 9). Base case is a beat that does nothing. Plan B; entry is a
post-print dip on an in-line quarter.

**ISRG (#7).** Eight straight beats, but July's −13% on a held guide is the
template: the stock trades on the procedure guide, which needs to go UP to
justify the last month's +15% rally. Date still unconfirmed. Plan B.

**VRT (#8).** Backlog covers H2 and the stock is −35% off highs, but consensus
$1.83 sits at the top of the company's own guide and the last two raises were
both sold (−2.3%, −17.3%). Execution-jittery. Plan B.

**WAB (#9).** Best operator ($30.9B backlog, Oct 1 $700M+ Africa deal, raised
twice) at the fullest price (+31% YTD, bar at guide top after a +12% earnings
day). You're paying for the sixth straight year of mid-teens growth. Plan B.

**LVS (#10).** −49% from highs, on the 52-week low, priced-in 2 — expectations
are on the floor, which is the entire bull case. But Macau September GGR was
the weakest month of 2026 (−1.2%, 4th straight decline) and FY $3.12 needs an
H2 step-up. "Less bad" plus stable hold is the trade. Plan B.
