across business models (asset manager, custodian ×2, money-center ×2,

STT (repeat-the-blowout bar). Steadiest compounders: BLK, BNY, MS (annuity-like
fee bases). Drawdown reality: single-session gaps of 10–20% are normal here —
this is the mode where leverage is most obviously destructive. Education, not
advice: no leverage multiple or position size is recommended.
