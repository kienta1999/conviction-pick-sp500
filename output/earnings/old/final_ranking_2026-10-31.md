Guide risk 7/10 is the whole trade; priced-in risk is low on price, high on consensus. Plan B: enter Friday 2026-11-06 only on EPS beat vs ~\$8.21 + revenue beat vs ~\$5.81bn + guide raised above \$35.72 + November 5 session up.

**2. IDXX — the consensus name nobody picked.** Ranked #4 by all four lenses (7 pts each) without a single-pick vote — the ranking's purest consensus pick. 8 consecutive beats (verified Phase 3.5), Q3 consensus ~\$3.71 (+9.1% year over year), premium installed base +12% with inVue Dx past 9,000 instruments, but H2 vet visits guided -1.5% to -2.0% and a 10.4% swing at 34.4x forward. The Q1 2026 reaction (+3% screen) is UNVERIFIED — only a +0.3% pre-market gain was found — so do not quote it as researched. Guide risk is the H2 visit decline capping the multiple; priced-in risk is moderate on a +6.5% one-month move into a premium multiple. Plan B: the print needs a beat that reaffirms without a visit-guide cut.

**3. AME — the momentum pick at a premium.** 8/8 beats, two straight quarters above its own guide top with two full-year raises, orders +28% year over year, backlog \$4.11bn (verified), calmest reaction record in the field (worst -2.0%, abs move 4.1%).
