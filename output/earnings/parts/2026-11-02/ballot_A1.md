## 1. SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- Top pick: CPAY
- Runner-up: PH

- Thesis (3-5 sentences): Corpay is the only name in the field with the full earnings-momentum combination — a 4/4 recent beat streak with large surprises, a serial-raising full-year guide in 2026, and a flat month into the print after a strong six months, so the momentum is in the numbers rather than in a last-minute run. Consensus sitting at the top of the Q3 guide range is the real hurdle, but a beat of the size Corpay has delivered recently clears it, and a raise would then be the third step in the 2026 sequence rather than a break in pattern.

- Key evidence from the record: 4/4 EPS beats — Q2 2026 +6.4%, Q1 +12.51%, Q4 2025 +11.56%, Q3 2025 +6.23% — with 4/4 positive reactions, plus a serial FY guide raise in 2026 (Feb $26.00 → May $26.70 → Aug $27.15–$27.55).

- Priced-in risk (THE TRAP): medium. The stock is ~6% below its high after a +30.5% six-month run, which is crowded enough to punish a mere reaffirmation, but the 1-month move is only +1.6% — flat into the print, not a melt-up — and the options-implied move was not found in the dossier, so there is no verified straddle premium to point to as a trap. The trap here is guide-shaped, not run-shaped: a reaffirmation on Nov 4 reads as a de facto cut after three raises.

- The guide: the market is carrying Q3 consensus EPS $7.20 and revenue $1.36B, against company Q3 guidance of revenue ~$1.355B and adj. EPS $7.05–$7.25 — consensus is at the top of the EPS range and a touch above the revenue guide. For the full year, the Aug guide of $27.15–$27.55 implies Q4 of roughly ~$7.0 at the midpoint, which the run-rate supports if organic growth holds ~10%. "Beat but guided soft" is the loss case here, and it is explicit: a Q3 beat paired with a mere reaffirmation of $27.15–$27.55 (or a Q4 guide that implies no raise) would likely be sold, because the 2026 pattern has trained the market to expect a raise. No between-quarter raise was found in the dossier, so the raise, if it comes, must come on this call.

- Plan A or Plan B, with one sentence of justification: Plan A (hold through the print) — Corpay is the dossier's only Plan A case, and its large, consistent beat sizes give a margin over a top-of-range consensus bar that the other serial raisers (AME, PFE) do not have, since their consensuses sit above their guides.

- Fallback if the print disappoints: yes, hold it 12–18 months. The case would then rest on the payments-network moat (dossier 7/10), steady ~10–11% organic growth, and a forward P/E of 12.7x — the cheapest multiple in the field alongside PFE — rather than on one quarter's guide call; the $100m preliminary FTC charge flagged in the dossier is a headline risk to monitor, not a thesis-breaker on its own.

- Event scenario: beat-and-raise roughly +4% to +7% (screen average move +4.4%, average absolute 5.4%, last four reactions +1%, +13%, +12%, +6%); in-line / beat-with-reaffirm roughly flat to −3%; miss roughly −5% to −8%.

- Top risk: a Q3 beat that is not followed by a FY raise — reaffirmation reads as a cut after three 2026 raises — compounded by any fuel-price or FTC-charge commentary on the call.

- Conviction (1-10): 7
