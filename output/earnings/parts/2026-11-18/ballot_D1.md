## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick: ADSK**
- **Runner-up: AZO**
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Autodesk is the cheapest growth in the field at 18.0x forward for +15% guided revenue growth, down 13.8% YTD and 20.3% below its 52-week high, after a stock that has fallen on its last two beats — the market is grading the guide and the MaintainX deal, not the quarter, and has now priced in the doubt. Management set the Q3 EPS guide ($3.04–3.09) below the then-Street $3.14 precisely to be beaten, and the 8-straight EPS beat streak plus a Q2 beat of +5.8% say the quarter itself is the reliable part. The mis-modelling is treating billings deceleration as a broken model when H1 run-rate already covers the full-year guide. What is not in the price is a clean print — whisper-clearing EPS plus billings re-accelerating toward revenue — because the last two beats were sold on deal/guide noise, not on the numbers.
- **Key evidence from the record:** 8 straight EPS beats (4/4 in the screen, +6.37% average surprise); Q2 FY27 EPS $3.30 vs $3.12 (+5.8%) and revenue $2.046bn vs $2.01bn (+16.1% YoY), with the FY27 guide raised twice in FY27 — yet the stock fell ~4% after hours on Q1 and −3.67% close-to-close on Q2.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP): Medium.** The stock is cheap and faded — YTD −13.78%, −5.7% since the 27 Aug pre-print close of $270.58, −3.88% on 17 Nov alone — so the equity is not crowded. But the number is priced above consensus: the Earnings Whispers bar is $3.16, above both consensus $3.07 and the top of the guided range ($3.09), so a mere consensus beat reads as a miss. Net: low priced-in on the stock, high priced-in on the print.
- **The guide:** The market is carrying full-year FY27 EPS of $12.62 (Zacks) on revenue $8.32bn — above the company's guided $12.52–12.60 / $8.295–8.345bn — implying Q4 EPS of ~$3.17–3.25. The run-rate supports the guide itself: H1 non-GAAP EPS was $6.29 on revenue $3.98bn, annualising to ~$12.58 before Q3/Q4 seasonality, and H2 revenue needs ~$4.32–4.37bn vs H1 $3.98bn, which is normal H2 seasonality plus ~$60m of MaintainX H2 revenue. What the run-rate does not support is the Street's extra: the $12.62 needs a modest Q4 beat on top of a guide the company has already raised twice. The loss case is explicit — **beat but guided soft**: Q3 EPS prints $3.07–3.10 (a consensus beat that misses the $3.16 whisper) while Q4 guidance is set low on "nonlinear" transaction-model headwinds, the FCF midpoint is cut again (it was already cut ~$25m in August), or MaintainX shows up dilutive — and the stock sells off a third straight beat, as it did after Q1 and Q2.
- **Plan A or Plan B, with one sentence of justification:** Plan B — the last two beats both fell on guide/deal news, so the burden of proof for holding through the print is not met and the edge, if any, is buying the post-print dip on a whisper-clearing print.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, I would hold it 12–18 months — a ~97%-recurring-revenue franchise at ~15x forward non-GAAP earnings after a 15% gap-down, with 39–41% non-GAAP operating margins, does not stay down on a one-quarter guide wobble, unless the sales reorganisation or the MaintainX integration is genuinely broken.
- **Event scenario:** Beat-and-raise (EPS ≥$3.16, billings re-accelerating toward revenue growth, FY27 raised again): +6% to +9%. In-line (EPS ~$3.07, guide merely reaffirmed, whisper missed): −3% to −5%. Miss (EPS at/below the $3.04–3.09 guide, billings weakening further, Q4 guide soft or FCF cut): −7% to −10%. Context: Gekko implied move ~±7.2% vs a 4.9% historical average absolute move (other prints disagree, ±3.1–4.5%), and the screen's last-4 reactions (+6%, −4%, +5%, +2%) cannot be mapped 1-to-1 to quarters — do not treat them as a per-quarter record.
- **Top risk:** The whisper-plus-billings trap — EPS clears $3.07 but misses $3.16 while billings (+10% in Q2 vs revenue +16%, total RPO only +2%, unbilled deferred revenue −8%) decelerate again, confirming revenue is being recognised faster than new business is booked and repricing the $3.6bn MaintainX deal.
