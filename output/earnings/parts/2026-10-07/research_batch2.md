## EARNINGS-QUALITY FLAG appendix

- **BAC — RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN → benign, balance-sheet artifact.** The "receivables" of a bank are its loan book. Average loans grew 8% (9-quarter streak) and deposits grew for 12 straight quarters; interest and fee receivables mechanically accrue ahead of cash collection on a growing book. Q2 provision actually *fell* YoY ($1.4B vs $1.6B). This is loan growth, not earnings running ahead of cash.
- **JPM — LOW_CASH_CONVERSION + RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN → benign, accrual-accounting artifacts.** NII accrues on a growing loan book (average loans +7% YoY to ~$1.4T; deposits +6%); in a blowout capital-markets quarter, trading assets and IB fee receivables also swell the balance sheet faster than cash settles. Banks are deposit-funded — operating cash flow is structurally uninformative for them. The credit signal that matters (card NCO guide *lowered* to 3.2%) points the other way.
- **STT — none.** No flag to explain.
