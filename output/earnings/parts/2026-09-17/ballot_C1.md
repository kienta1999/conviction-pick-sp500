# BALLOT 1 — Single-pick nomination

- **Top pick:** CTAS
- **Runner-up:** BLK

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Cintas is the most irreplaceable business in the field (dossier moat 9: route-based service density, contractual recurring revenue, no-bid switching costs; AI cannot replace physical services, only optimize routes). The print setup is good-not-great but underpriced relative to the business quality: four straight tracked beats, organic growth re-accelerated to 8.4% with 51.0% gross margin, the FY2027 guide was *raised* at the Q4 print ($12.10–12.25B revenue / $5.36–5.50 EPS), and the stock has derated -9.7% off its July high on a calm -0.78% month. That isn't in the price because the market is anchored on the March 2026 beat-that-sold (-4.52%) and the 40x headline multiple — yet the most recent print (the guide-setting Q4) was rewarded +7.22%, proving this market still pays for clean beats with guide support, and near-term expected move was only ~2.1–2.4% (caveat: not a clean earnings straddle), i.e. no crowded event premium.

- **Key evidence from the record:** Q4 FY2026 (2026-07-15): EPS $1.29 vs $1.24 consensus, revenue $2.905B, +8.4% organic growth, FY2027 guide raised — next-session reaction **+7.22%**.

- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP): HIGH.** 40x trailing P/E (~37x forward); only -9.7% off the July high; consensus FY2027 EPS ~$5.49 already sits at the *top* of the $5.36–5.50 guide, so the market demands a raise later in the year, not just a beat; and the March 2026 precedent (a $1.24-vs-$1.23 thin beat sold off -4.52%) is the template — this stock is driven by expectations, not numbers. The UniFirst acquisition chatter is a wildcard that can move the stock regardless of the print (guide explicitly excludes UniFirst).

- **The guide:** FY2027 company guide: revenue **$12.10–12.25B**, adjusted EPS **$5.36–5.50** (constant FX, excludes UniFirst, one extra workday). The market is carrying Q1 consensus **$1.35 EPS / $2.9777B revenue** and FY consensus ~$5.49. Run-rate check: Q1 $1.35 × 4 = $5.40 sits *inside* the guide, but revenue annualized ($2.9777B × 4 ≈ $11.91B) sits slightly *below* the guide's low end — so the run-rate supports the EPS bar but requires back-half acceleration (extra workday + seasonality) to clear the revenue bar. **"Beat but guided soft" loss case (explicit):** Q1 beats $1.35 but management merely *maintains* (does not raise) the FY guide → the March 2026 template repeats, -2% to -5%, because the stock is priced for the raise, not the beat.

- **Plan A or Plan B:** **Plan B — wait for the number.** The burden of proof for holding through the print is not met: priced-in risk is high (40x, consensus at guide top-end, March beat-sold precedent), so the edge is in buying the print's dislocation, not in taking event risk.

- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes — I would happily hold 12–18 months. Moat specifics: route density economics (denser routes = structurally lower cost per stop, unreplicable without the route base), contractual recurring revenue in uniform rental and facility services, and no-bid renewal switching costs with embedded customer workflows. AI bypass risk is low — physical services cannot be substituted by software; AI only helps route optimization. Balance-sheet detail: not found in the dossier, and no leverage red flag was evidenced. The 18-month risk is multiple compression from 40x, not business impairment: at 8.4% organic growth with 51% gross margins and a raised guide, a sell-the-news gap is a compounding entry, not an exit.

- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise: **+4% to +7%** (Q4 template +7.22%); in-line / thin beat with guide maintained: **-2% to -5%** (March template -4.52%); miss or soft guide: **-6% to -10%**.

- **Top risk:** The March 2026 dynamic — a beat without a guide raise sells off — compounded by the UniFirst deal overhang: any acquisition commentary moves the stock independent of fundamentals, and consensus at the guide top-end leaves zero room for a "maintained" guide to be read positively.

- **Conviction (1-10):** **7** — business conviction is a 9 (best fallback in the field); capped at 7 by the priced-in trap and the UniFirst wildcard. Report date: **2026-09-23, confirmed (d=5)**; BMO corroborated by third party.
