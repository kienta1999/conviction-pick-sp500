## Ballot 1 — Single-pick nomination

- **Top pick:** FSLR
- **Runner-up:** MS
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** FSLR is the only name in the field where the street has *lowered* expectations into the print: after analysts overshot Q2 (+43% EPS beat), Q3 consensus was cut from $5.29 to $4.56, the stock sits 37.3% below its 52-week high and −10.8% over the past month, and the company reaffirmed all full-year guidance on the Q2 call. At ~11.8x forward EPS with a PEG of 0.45, the print is priced for disappointment, so a mere confirmation of the stated Q3 volume cadence (3.9–4.5 GW) is enough to work. The reaction record backs it: this is the one name where good news has never been sold.
- **Key evidence from the record:** The perfect reaction record — beats have been rewarded 100% of the time (up=1.00, the best in the 48-name triage field, with 7.5% average absolute moves) — while the stock sits 37% below its high: good news gets paid here, and almost none of it is currently in the price.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** LOW. Consensus was revised *down* into the print (not up), the stock is at its most derated point in the batch (−37.3% from high, −23% YTD), and the multiple is the cheapest in the dossier (forward ~11.8x, PEG 0.45). This is the mirror image of the trap: fear is priced, good news isn't.
- **The guide:** The market is carrying the company's own Q3 cadence — adjusted EBITDA **$625–775M** and volumes sold **3.9–4.5 GW** (incl. 3.2–3.7 GW U.S.) — inside a reaffirmed FY2026 frame (volume 17.0–18.2 GW, EBITDA $2.6–2.8B, revenue $4.9–5.2B). Run-rate check: H1 EPS $7.14 vs FY consensus $17.77 implies H2 ~$10.63; Q3 consensus $4.56 implies Q4 ~$6.2 — steep, but consistent with the back-half-weighted cadence management affirmed in July. The "beat but guided soft" loss case, stated explicitly: Q3 beats on shipments but management trims the FY volume or EBITDA range, backlog slips below 45 GW on deferrals/cancellations (a post-OBBBA demand air-pocket signal), or tariff/freight costs land above the $60–80M assumption — any of those turns a beat into the kill shot.
- **Plan A or Plan B:** **Plan A — hold through the print.** Justification: this is the one setup in the field where expectations sit below the company's own reaffirmed bar, so the pre-print risk is compensated by the discount — you are buying the fear that analysts already priced in via the lowered bar.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, hold 12–18 months — a 45.1 GW backlog through 2030 (~$13.6B), ~$1.7B net cash, and a domestic CdTe chain insulated from China/FEOC exposure mean a shipment-timing miss is a quarter problem, not a thesis problem; the reaffirmed guide gives you an anchor to average into.
- **Event scenario:** Beat-and-reaffirm/raise: **+7–10%** (last two prints went +4.9% and +2.5%-then-+18.3%); in-line on cadence: **+1–3%** relief drift; miss (shipments <3.9 GW or any guide cut): **−8–12%**.
- **Top risk:** Policy-driven demand air-pocket — post-OBBBA backlog deferrals or tariff/freight leakage beyond $80M converting a volume beat into a guidance cut.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
- **Report date:** 2026-10-29 (estimated, AMC)
