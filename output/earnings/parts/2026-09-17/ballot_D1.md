## BALLOT 1 — Single-pick nomination

- **Top pick: NO TRADE**
  Reasoning: the only two names actionable this week (both reporting 2026-09-23, 5 days out) are both consensus-above-guide setups where the market has pre-carried a raise that management has not promised. There is no mis-modelled-low bar in the actionable set — the mis-modelling runs the *wrong way* for a buyer. The genuine edge in both names is post-print (buy the fade of a quality business), not holding through the event. Passing on the event is the highest-conviction call in this field.

- **Runner-up: FSLR** (reports 2026-10-29, AMC, estimated)

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** This week's actionable tape offers no buyer's edge: CTAS's FY2027 consensus EPS of $5.49 sits at the very top of the company's $5.36–$5.50 guide, demanding an unpromised later raise, and its own March 2026 print proved exactly what a beat-without-a-raise earns — −4.52% the next session. PAYX is the same shape, milder: FY2027 consensus EPS $5.96 exceeds the implied guide range ($5.62–5.73), its beats are mechanically managed at +1.3%, and its last clean print faded −5% premarket. The one true "bar too low" mis-model in the field is FSLR — consensus cut from $5.29/$1.43B to $4.56/$1.32B into September, all FY guidance reaffirmed, stock −37% off its high — but it reports 10/29 (42 days out) with solar-policy binary risk, so it is a watchlist name, not a hold-through-print nomination today.

- **Key evidence from the record:** CTAS consensus FY2027 EPS **$5.49 vs company guide $5.36–$5.50** (the bar demands the top of guide *plus* a later raise), paired with the March 2026 precedent: EPS $1.24 vs $1.23 beat and revenue +0.86% over consensus → **−4.52% next-session close**.

- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP): HIGH (CTAS) / MEDIUM (PAYX).** CTAS: only −9.7% off its 52-week high, +16% in 3 months into the print, 40.3x P/E (~36.8–37.2x forward) — priced for perfection with a guide that has no room to give. PAYX: −14.3% off its high and a weak month give it slightly less trap risk, but 4 straight managed beats at +1.3% mean the market expects a mechanical beat and prices it as a non-event. The trap on both is identical: a clean beat that the bar already assumes.

- **The guide (the owned guide-down case):**
  - **CTAS:** the market is carrying FY2027 revenue $12.10–12.25B and adjusted EPS $5.36–$5.50 (company guide), with consensus EPS $5.49 — the run-rate does *not* support it: Q1 consensus $1.35 × 4 = $5.40 sits inside the guide, and the revenue run-rate ($2.9777B × 4 ≈ $11.91B) trails the guide's low end, requiring an acceleration that is asserted, not evidenced. **The "beat but guided soft" loss case:** Q1 FY27 beats by a penny on organic growth ≥8%, the company *maintains* (does not raise) FY guidance, and/or adds cautious UniFirst commentary — the stock repeats March 2026 and sells the beat, −4% to −6%.
  - **PAYX:** the market carries FY2027 EPS consensus $5.96 against an implied company guide of ~$5.62–5.73 and revenue growth +5–6%; the Q1 margin guide (41–42%) sits below the FY ~44% guide. **Loss case:** in-line Q1, guide maintained (the company rarely raises mid-stream), rate-cut commentary deepens the client-funds-interest hole ($195–205M guide already down from $200–210M) — the Q4 FY26 template: good numbers, −5% premarket fade.

- **Plan A or Plan B: Plan B for both actionable names** — do not hold through the 2026-09-23 prints; the consensus-above-guide tension only resolves at the print, and reaction history shows beats don't get paid in this setup. The tradeable edge is the day-after fade.

- **Fallback if the print disappoints: Yes, I would hold either 12–18 months after a print-driven selloff — that is precisely the fallback.** CTAS: moat 9 (route-based service density, contractual recurring revenue, physical services no software can disintermediate); a March-style fade into the low-$180s would be a buy, not a warning. PAYX: Morningstar Wide moat, ~800k embedded clients, 40% ROE; the guide is conservative by habit, and a fade toward $105–110 would be the entry. Both are compounders the print can't break.

- **Event scenario (rough % move):** CTAS — beat-and-raise +4–6% / in-line-beat with maintained guide −3–5% (March template) / miss −8–10%. PAYX — beat with flat guide −2–4% (Q4 template) / in-line −3–5% / miss −7–9%.

- **Top risk:** CTAS posts organic growth ≥9% *with* a genuine FY guide raise and reprices +6–8% without me; or PAYX prints Q1 margin at/above 42% (Paycor synergies arriving early), forcing the guide-above-consensus tension to resolve upward and gapping the stock.

- **Conviction (1-10): 7** — high conviction that holding either actionable name through 2026-09-23 is negative-expectancy; moderate conviction that the post-print fade is the buyable side.
