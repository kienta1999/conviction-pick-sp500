## 7. MOAT / AI-IRREPLACEABILITY

Cintas is a physical, route-density business: 1M+ customer locations across North America, recurring uniform-rental and facility-services contracts, 40%+ ROE, 17.8% net margin, 42-year dividend-increase streak, revenue growth in 55 of the last 57 years. Only ~1M of 16–20M North American businesses are customers — the moat is route density (a truck already on your block) plus service integration (uniforms, mats, restrooms, first aid, fire safety on one invoice) plus switching friction (change-outs disrupt operations).

- **Can big clients in-source?** In theory; in practice Cintas's scale makes its unit cost lower than a client-run laundry fleet, and garment-sharing/auto-sortation/SmartTruck tech keeps widening that gap. Large-client self-operation is the main structural risk, but churn data ("record retention" cited at Q4) says it isn't happening.
- **Can AI-native competitors route around it?** AI can optimize routes — Cintas is doing that itself (SmartTruck, SAP). But no software routes around picking up dirty uniforms and delivering clean ones. Physical fulfillment is the moat.
- **Score: 8/10.** A 15% gap-down here with an 18-month hold is **opportunity, not disaster** — this business has compounded through every cycle, and its drawdowns have been bought (May 2026: ~$164 → $219 in ~2.5 months). The risk is paying 37x and waiting, not permanent impairment.

**PLAN B — wait for the number.**
