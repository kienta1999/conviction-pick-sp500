Unlike the other moat names in this field, the street is not sitting above management's guide, so a good quarter does not need a heroic raise to avoid printing as a disappointment. This lens therefore ranks IDXX first even though its setup is less clean than Q's — Q's fallback carries a single-region thesis-break tail (China) that IDXX's gradual visit/placement risks do not.
