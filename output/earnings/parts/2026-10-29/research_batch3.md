### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10
- IDEXX owns the vet clinic's diagnostic workflow: reference labs, in-clinic analysers (Catalyst, ProCyte, inVue Dx),
  consumables (razor-and-blades), VetConnect PLUS, and cloud PIMS across 10,000+ clinics. Switching analysers
  means retraining staff, re-validating results, and breaking the patient-history record — high switching costs,
  recurring consumables, and a data network effect. AI is a tailwind here, not a threat: IDEXX is buying it
  (CoVetAI) and embedding it in workflow, and an AI-native entrant still needs the analyser, the assay menu,
  the lab network, and clinic trust to route around IDEXX. The moat's soft spots: reference-lab competition,
  point-of-care entrants, and pricing pressure if clinic economics worsen — all gradual, not cliff risks.
- If it gaps down 15% and you must hold 18 months: **opportunity, with a patience requirement** — at ~$478
  (15% below $563) the stock would be near its 52-week low on a business still growing CAG recurring at
  ~10% organic with expanding margins; the disaster case is only if the gap is caused by a structural visit
  collapse or a Cancer Dx/inVue Dx failure, neither of which is in the current data.
