### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 6/10
SanDisk owns NAND IP, a Kioxia JV fab base, and now multi-year contracted volumes with floor prices — real, but it sells a **commodity input**. Its biggest customers (hyperscalers, device OEMs) cannot easily in-source NAND fabrication, but they *can* dual-source (Samsung, SK hynix, Micron, Kioxia, YMTC/CXMT) and they set the price when supply loosens. No AI-native competitor routes around NAND — AI *increases* NAND demand (KV-cache, inference storage, enterprise SSD) — but nothing about SanDisk's NAND is un-replicable; the moat is scale, contracts, and cycle position, not uniqueness. **If it gaps -15% and you hold 18 months: an opportunity if the contract book and 83–85% margins hold (you bought a trough-multiple cyclical at a discount); a slow bleed if NAND prices roll over and the "80% margin" proves to be a peak-cycle print.** Score reflects that two-sidedness.
