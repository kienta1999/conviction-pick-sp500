# Panelist C — Quality / Moat & Irreplaceability — Earnings Ballot 2026-10-25

## (1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** GOOGL
- **Runner-up:** APH
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Alphabet is the highest-irreplaceability name (9/10) that is not priced like it: Search distribution, YouTube, Gemini/TPUs and Cloud cannot be in-sourced by customers or routed around by an AI-native rival, and AI features have so far grown Search revenue (+17–19% in Q2) rather than cannibalised it. The operating engine is accelerating into the print — revenue +22% in Q1 to +24% in Q2, driven by Cloud +48% → +63% → +82% — with a $514B Cloud backlog to convert. That acceleration is not in the price: the stock is −20.4% vs its 52-week high, −6.5% in 21 days, RSI ~33, and at 20.8x forward (screen) it is the cheapest multiple in this set. Post-Q2 revisions lifted Q3 Cloud revenue estimates +10.9% and Cloud operating income +17.7%, so the bar rose on the segment that is actually delivering, not on hope.
- **Key evidence from the record:** Q2 Cloud revenue $24.8B, +82% YoY, with backlog rising to $514B (about +$50B in the quarter alone) — a contracted demand pool no substitute can replicate without Alphabet's data-centre footprint.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** low — no run into the print (ret_21d −6.5%), de-rated −20.4% from the high, 20.8x forward versus a 5-year range spanning high-teens to ~28x, and sceptical rather than crowded positioning (buybacks suspended, equity being issued). The caveat is event premium, not positioning: implied move ±6.2% is larger than the screen's 4.4% average absolute reaction, and the last two real moves were +10% and −7%.
- **The guide:** Alphabet gives no revenue or EPS guide — its guide *is* capex: FY26 $195–205B, with no clean Q4 consensus figure found in the dossier. H1 capex was $80.6B, so hitting the range requires ~$119B in H2 (~$60B/quarter, a ~33% step-up from Q2), which the run-rate supports operationally (Cloud acceleration and backlog) but guarantees negative-FCF optics. The beat-but-guided-soft loss case must be named explicitly: GOOGL can beat on operating income / ex-gain EPS (~$2.85 clean in Q2, not the $9.11 GAAP figure inflated by a $99.0B equity gain) and still fall ~7% — exactly the Q2 script — if it delivers a fourth capex raise or a hard, larger 2027 capex number without matching Cloud acceleration.
- **Plan B** — enter after a confirmed operating beat with the $195–205B capex range held (and a positive first session), because the single sentence that decides this print is unknowable before the call.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes — this is the name I would be happiest to be stuck holding for 12–18 months. Irreplaceability 9/10, net cash on the screen (net debt −$121.7B), 34–36% operating margins, and at a −15% gap (~$272) the ex-gain multiple is ~18x for a business growing revenue 24% with a $514B backlog; the 18-month risk is a capex-driven FCF drought, not franchise impairment.
- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise (operating beat + capex held) +6% to +10% / in-line (operations fine, capex language neutral) −2% to +3% / miss (Cloud deceleration and/or a fourth capex raise) −7% to −10%
- **Top risk:** A fourth capex raise, or quantified 2027 capex stepping up again, swamping a good operating quarter — compounded the same evening by the Fed decision and Microsoft's print.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
