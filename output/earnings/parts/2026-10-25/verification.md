### Claim 4 — Eliquis: guided +20–25% growth for 2026 (raised from +10–15%); US LOE 1 Apr 2028 under settlement; EU exclusivity conflict resolved
**CONFIRMED on all three, and the conflict resolves in favour of today's research (19 May 2026), with a country nuance that explains the old claim.**
- Growth guide raise: Reuters, 2026-07-30: "The company raised its Eliquis sales forecast for the year to growth of **20% to 25%, from a prior projection of 10% to 15%**. Sales of Eliquis… were $4.48 billion in the quarter, up 22%." Zacks Q2 wrap (2026-07-30) identical: "It now expects sales to increase 20-25% year over year, up from its previous forecast of 10-15% growth."
- US LOE 1 Apr 2028: Patenta.ai patent analysis (sources checked **2026-10-22**, citing the FDA Orange Book and BMS/Pfizer statements): the compound patent US 6,967,208 expires 21 Nov 2026 (pediatric exclusivity to 21 May 2027) and the formulation patent US 9,326,945 (upheld, Delaware 2020 / Federal Circuit Sep 2021) runs to 2031; "BMS and Pfizer state that under those agreements the **earliest date generic manufacturers may launch apixaban in the US is 1 April 2028**, subject to additional appeals and challenges." DrugPatentWatch's Eliquis pages corroborate: "Generic settlement dates — generally reported around April 1, 2028."
- EU — resolution: **The 19 May 2026 date is correct as the headline EU exclusivity expiry; "mid-Q4 2026" is not correct as a blanket EU date.**
  - GlobalData (Pharmaceutical Intelligence Center): "loss of its European market exclusivity on **May 19, 2026**," forecasting a −15.2% hit to global Eliquis sales in 2026 from that event.
  - DrugPatentWatch (patent-cliff analysis): "the basic composition-of-matter patent for apixaban (European Patent 1 427 415) expired on 17 September 2022, and the SPC extending it is due to expire on **19 May 2026**."
  - Patenta.ai (checked 2026-10-22, citing the EPO federated register, UK IPO and German DPMA registers): 19 May 2026 is "15 years after the first EU authorization, the normal ceiling" for the SPC — **but** Germany's SPC (12 2011 100 050.1) runs to **20 November 2026** after a pediatric extension granted in 2024, and the UK SPC has been **invalid since 6 June 2022** (patent revoked; Court of Appeal dismissed BMS's appeal in 2023). "So both dates are right, for different countries." The old "mid-Q4 2026" claim most plausibly came from the German November 2026 date being misread as EU-wide.
  - Generics already selling: BMS's own Form 10-K (FY2025, filed 2026) states for Eliquis–Europe that after the final negative UK decision "generics are now on the market there," and "One or more generics have entered the market in **Finland, Poland and Portugal** while proceedings are pending." Patenta.ai, summarising BMS reporting: "after adverse decisions in the UK and Finland, generic companies began marketing apixaban there, and… generics have entered certain other EU countries."
  - **Bottom line for the panel:** retire "EU expiry mid-Q4 2026" as a future cliff. The EU cliff's headline date (19 May 2026) is ~5 months past and already in the run-rate; residual country-level protection (e.g. Germany to Nov 2026) is a tail, not a cliff. The live Eliquis cliff framing remains the guided 2027 step-down and the US 1 Apr 2028 settlement date.
