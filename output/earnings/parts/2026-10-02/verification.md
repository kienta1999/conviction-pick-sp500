## 5. Moat's central factual claim — CONFIRMED

The dossier's central moat claim is that PEP's protection is **distribution
scale** (DSD network + integrated beverages-and-snacks system in >200
countries), with brand salience/availability friction rather than formal
switching costs.

- **pepsico.com "About PepsiCo" (Aug 25, 2026 IR release):** "PepsiCo products
  are enjoyed by consumers **more than one billion times a day in more than 200
  countries and territories**... generated **nearly $94 billion in net revenue
  in 2025**, driven by a complementary beverage and convenient foods portfolio
  that includes Lay's, Doritos, Cheetos, Gatorade, Pepsi-Cola, Mountain Dew,
  Quaker, and SodaStream... many iconic brands that generate more than
  **$1 billion each** in estimated annual retail sales."
  URL: https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/pepsico-announces-timing-and-availability-of-third-quarter-2026-financial-results
- **Zacks business description:** PepsiCo "distributes its products through
  **direct-store-delivery** and customer warehouse systems, as well as
  third-party networks."
  URL: https://zrsa.zacks.com/stock/research/snapshot/PEP.pdf
- **PepsiCo annual-report statement (via TradeAndIndustryDev, Sep 2026):**
  "DSD allows products to be **merchandised for visibility and frequent
  restocking**"; same piece cites 2025 net revenue **$93.9B**, ~306,000
  employees.
  URL: https://www.tradeandindustrydev.com/region/california/news/ca-pepsico-plan-frito-lay-distribution-warehouse-36321
- **Morningstar (Kristoffer Inton, Sep 22, 2026 via Stocktwits):** "PepsiCo's
  **strong brands, distribution network, and international growth** support its
  long-term outlook" — independent corroboration that the distribution/brand
  moat is intact even as growth has stalled.
  URL: https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/pep-stock-faces-fresh-test-deutsche-bank-flags-concerns-over-pepsi-co-s-north-america-recovery/cZMSslwRBQi
- Caveat: the dossier's sharper sub-claims (22+ billion-dollar brands, >$40B
  international, 2/3 of beverage volume) trace to vendor/secondary sources, not
  the IR language above; the IR boilerplate says only "many iconic brands...
  more than $1 billion each." The **central claim** (distribution-scale moat of
  the beverages+snacks system, protected by shelf/availability friction rather
  than switching-cost lock-in) is confirmed by primary sources. No source found
  contradicts it.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED** (central claim; granular sub-claims consistent but
rest on secondary sources).
