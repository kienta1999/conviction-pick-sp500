### EARNINGS-QUALITY FLAG — FOXA — RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN (recv_rev_divergence +0.116)
Verdict in one line: **benign, mechanical, and already explained by management — a World Cup timing bulge, not earnings running ahead of cash.**
Evidence: on the Aug 6, 2026 call, CFO Steve Tomsic said explicitly that Q4 free cash flow ($726m) "was impacted by the timing of working capital related to the World Cup, where rights payments for the tournament landed in fiscal 2026, while advertising receivables will be collected early in fiscal 2027." The mechanism is clean: Fox aired the World Cup in June–July, recognised the advertising revenue, paid the rights holder promptly, and collects from advertisers on normal 30–90 day media terms — so receivables mechanically outrun revenue at the June 30 balance-sheet date and the cash lands in Q1 FY27. The test for this print is therefore specific and falsifiable: Q1 FY27 operating cash flow should show the World Cup receivables collected (Zacks previews already expect FCF to benefit from this collection). If Q1 cash flow does NOT show that collection, the benign verdict fails and the flag becomes a real quality concern. Until then, do not treat it as a value-trap signature — it is the expected footprint of a June–July mega-event straddling a June 30 year-end.
