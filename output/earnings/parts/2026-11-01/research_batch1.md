### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10
Motion and control: hydraulics, pneumatics, fluid conveyance, flight controls, thermal management — specified into aircraft, industrial machinery and defence platforms with certification, safety and liability barriers that make switching suppliers a re-qualification project, not a purchasing decision.
