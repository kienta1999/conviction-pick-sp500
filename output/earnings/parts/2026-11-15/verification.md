## Cross-name corrections (material to the panel)

1. **The dossier's two "NOT verifiable as a single-session move" flags are both wrong.** A Q2 FY26: +16.9% on May 28, 2026 (report May 27). NTAP Q4 FY26: +22.4% on May 29, 2026 (report May 28). 2. **Reaction-measurement discipline:** three of the six reaction figures checked here were misstated because after-hours moves were mixed with next-session closes — A Q3 (+3.17% AH on Aug 26 vs +1.68% next close), NTAP Q1 (−9% AH on Sep 2 vs +2.55% next close), MU Q4 (+0.37% AH on Sep 30 vs +3.03% next close). The panel should define "reaction" once (close-to-close, next session) and apply it uniformly.
3. **Consensus-provider dispersion is the largest unflagged risk in the dossier:** MU Q1 EPS $38.20 (FMP) vs $39.37 (Finnhub); MU Q4 consensus $31.35–$31.77 across all retrieved sources vs $32.56 in the dossier; MU Q2 $42.03 (Zacks) vs $43.39 (dossier/Finnhub, undated). Single-provider consensus figures should carry the provider and date.
