### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10
Route-based uniform rental + facility services is a scale-and-density business: 1m+ customer locations, route density, laundering plants, and multi-year contracts with high switching costs (a customer does not re-tender uniforms casually). First Aid & Safety and Fire Protection add route leverage. No customer can sensibly in-source a national uniform/laundry route network; AI does not replace physical garment rental, delivery and servicing — if anything AI helps Cintas (route optimisation, inventory). The genuine threats are cyclical (employment / small-business formation drives new uniform wearers) and the UniFirst integration if it closes, not substitution. **If it gaps down 15% and I hold 18 months: opportunity, not disaster** — the business compounds at high-single-digit organic growth with record margins and a buyback; the 15% gap would put it at ~31× forward, still not cheap, but the franchise is intact. The disaster case is a recession that shrinks uniform wearers, not obsolescence.
