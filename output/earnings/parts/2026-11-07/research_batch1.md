### 5. THE GUIDE

(a) **Does VEEV guide?** Yes — granularly and conservatively. Each quarter it guides **next-quarter** total revenue, non-GAAP operating income, and non-GAAP diluted EPS, **and full-year** revenue, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP EPS, and operating cash flow. It also guides subscription-revenue growth and (until Mar 2026) billings; it stopped issuing quarterly billings guidance in Mar 2026, shifting to full-year normalized billings. A company that guides this precisely can disappoint precisely.

(b) **Forward consensus — next quarter and full year:** Q4 FY2027 (quarter ending Jan 31, 2027): **no clean published consensus found** for revenue/EPS as of Nov 7. Implied arithmetic from the FY guide: FY2027 revenue guide \$3,682–\$3,687m minus Q1 \$882.9m + Q2 \$928.0m + Q3 guide \$933.5m (midpoint) = **Q4 implied revenue ~\$937–\$942m**; FY non-GAAP EPS guide ~\$9.21 minus Q1 \$2.24 + Q2 \$2.35 + Q3 \$2.335 (midpoint) = **Q4 implied EPS ~\$2.29**. Full-year FY2027: revenue \$3,682–\$3,687m (company guide); MarketBeat's GAAP-based EPS figures (\$6.70 FY, growing to \$7.47 next year) are a different (GAAP) basis — do not mix them with the \$9.21 non-GAAP guide. FY2028: not found as a clean consensus; the company's 2030 target is a \$6bn revenue run rate.

(c) **Does the run-rate support the forward number?** Yes — comfortably. Q4 implied revenue ~\$940m is only ~+1% sequentially on Q3's ~\$933.5m and ~+12% YoY on Q4 FY2026's \$836m, *below* the 16–18% YoY run-rate — i.e., the implied Q4 is a **deceleration the business has already exceeded**, not an acceleration. Q4 implied EPS ~\$2.29 is *below* Q2's \$2.35 and Q3's ~\$2.34 — a sequential decline baked into the guide. The forward number is supportable; the risk is that "supportable" is not "exciting," and VEEV's reactions are driven by excitement, not supportability.

### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10

Veeva is the system of record for life-sciences commercial, clinical, regulatory, quality, and safety data: Vault is deeply embedded in validated, compliance-critical workflows at 1,500+ customers including most of the top 20 pharma.
