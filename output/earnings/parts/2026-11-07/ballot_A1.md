# Ballot A1 — Earnings-momentum analyst — 2026-11-07

Lens: highest probability of a beat-and-raise, judged on beat streak and surprise size, estimate revisions, and revenue acceleration into the print. All figures from the research dossier for 2026-11-07; no fresh research.

## Single-pick ballot

- **Top pick:** NTAP
- **Runner-up:** OXY
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** NTAP carries the strongest revenue-acceleration signal in the field: revenue growth ran +3% to +4% to +13% to +30% year over year into Q1 FY2027, with all-flash arrays +47%, billings +36%, and a +21% EPS beat on that print. It is also a serial raiser — four consecutive guide raises — so a beat-and-raise is the established pattern, not a hope. Crucially, that momentum is not in the price: the stock is down 28% in a month and 29.8% below its 52-week high after a downgrade and a sell-the-news September print, the only name in the field scored priced-in 4. A Q2 beat that holds the FY2027 raise has room to re-rate; the same beat at VEEV or PAYX would land on a stock already paid for it.
- **Key evidence from the record:** Q1 FY2027 EPS \$2.58 vs about \$2.12 consensus (+21%), revenue \$2.025bn vs \$1.84bn (+9.9%), with revenue growth accelerating to +30% year over year (+26% ex extra week) and billings +36% — the largest beat-plus-acceleration combination in the dossier.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** low. Price \$169.19 on Nov 6, down 28.2% in one month and 29.8% below the \$240.87 high, after Barclays downgraded NTAP to Equal Weight on Oct 14 and the September beat itself fell 8.7% next session; the market has de-crowded this name rather than chased it. The one offset is that consensus EPS \$2.60 sits at the top of management's own \$2.54–\$2.64 Q2 guide, so a merely in-range print is already expected — but a top-of-range print is not a priced-in raise.
- **The guide:** the market is carrying Q2 FY2027 guidance of revenue \$2.025–\$2.175bn and non-GAAP EPS \$2.54–\$2.64, with consensus EPS \$2.60 at the top of that range, and FY2027 guidance of revenue \$7.975–\$8.225bn and EPS \$9.73–\$10.03. The run-rate supports it as continuation, not acceleration: Q1 delivered revenue \$2.025bn and EPS \$2.58, so the Q2 midpoint of about \$2.10bn revenue (+23% year over year) and \$2.59 EPS is flat on EPS versus Q1 and does not ask for a new step-change. "Beat but guided soft" is the explicit loss case here: a Q2 beat paired with a mere FY reaffirmation — or a Q3 guide with another gross-margin trim after September's cut to 67–68% for Q2 — reads as a de facto cut from a serial raiser and is the September pattern repeating.
- **Plan A or Plan B**, with one sentence of justification. Plan B — the guide, not the beat, decides this print, and a serial raiser that merely reaffirms after a margin trim is a sell-the-news setup, so buy only after the print confirms the raise and the margin outlook.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** yes, hold 12–18 months, but only conditionally — ONTAP, the AFF installed base, and first-party cloud storage give real switching costs and 350 AI/data-lake deals in Q1 support a demand story, yet a second inventory build with decelerating billings would mark Q1 as NAND-price pass-through rather than unit demand, in which case the thesis breaks and I would not hold.
- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise roughly +10% to +15% (the May 2026 raise moved +26.3%); in-line / reaffirmation roughly -5% to -8% (the September raise still fell 8.7% on a margin trim); miss or guide cut roughly -12% to -15%.
- **Top risk:** NAND/flash cost inflation squeezes gross margin again and management reaffirms rather than raises FY2027, turning a Q2 beat into another sell-the-news print.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
