## Method note — how reactions are measured

Unless stated otherwise, a "next-session reaction" here means **report-day close to next-session close**, from Finnhub daily candles, cross-checked against TipRanks / StockAnalysis / StockInvest / CoinCodex historical-price tables. That is the standard close-to-close definition. Two other windows appear in the dossier and in press coverage and are **not** the same thing: (i) the after-hours / premarket move immediately after an AMC print, and (ii) the day-before-close to day-after-close 48-hour move (CoinCodex's convention). Where the dossier's figure matches one of those other windows instead of the close-to-close move, I say so explicitly — the figure is not invented, it is mislabelled. Weekdays were checked with `date`: Dec 1, 2026 is a Tuesday; Dec 3, 2026 is a Thursday; Nov 9, 2026 is a Monday; Nov 25 is a Wednesday; Nov 26 and Nov 19 are Thursdays.
