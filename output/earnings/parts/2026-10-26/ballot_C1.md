A −15% gap puts it near ~21x forward earnings for a serial compounder with ~111% FCF conversion, a $4.1B backlog, diversified niche monopolies and an acquisition-and-margin-expansion machine that is the real moat; the dossier's own verdict is "opportunity," and nothing in a soft print impairs the franchise — the failure mode is a valuation/raise-timing reset, not in-sourcing or substitution.
