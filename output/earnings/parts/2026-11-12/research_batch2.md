### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10
Largest US auto-parts retailer/distributor, 8,031 stores (6,863 US, 1,001 Mexico, 167 Brazil as of Aug 29, 2026), commercial (DIFM) business ~29% of sales and growing 8–10%, Mega Hub inventory network. Customers cannot in-source a parts counter; the moat is assortment + availability + speed for both DIY and professional shops. AI threat is minimal — no AI-native substitute for a physical part in hand; the real substitutes are O'Reilly, Advance/NAPA, Amazon, and dealer parts. If this gaps down 15% and I hold 18 months: **opportunity, not disaster** — an aging US car parc (average age ~13 years) is a structural tailwind, the buyback ($1.5B authorisation replenished Jun 2026; $1.6B per Aardvark) compounds EPS, and 17x forward is undemanding. The disaster case is a multi-quarter DIY demand slump, which is cyclical, not existential.
