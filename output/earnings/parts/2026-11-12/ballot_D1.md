Holding any of these through the print is paying full price for a quarter the market has already graded, and being graded again on a guide nobody can clear cleanly. The contrarian trade here is patience: the post-print dip, not the print.

- **Key evidence from the record:** The single most compelling data point is the pattern, not one print: beat-and-raise has been a sell signal across this field — ADSK fell after its last two beat-and-raises, CTAS fell 3% on its September beat-and-raise, PAYX fell 8.8% on a Q1 beat, and AZO fell 11.1% on its biggest EPS beat of the last four quarters.
