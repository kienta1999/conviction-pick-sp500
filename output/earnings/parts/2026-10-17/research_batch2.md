### 🔎 STX earnings-quality flag: RECEIVABLES_OUTRUN — explained
**The facts (FY2026 10-K / Q4 release, year ended 2026-07-03):** accounts receivable, net rose **$959M → $1,534M (+60%)** while full-year revenue rose +34% ($9.097B → $12.195B) — receivables outran revenue, hence the flag. **One-line verdict: Benign business-model effect, not the value-trap signature — receivables ballooned because Q4 shipments to a handful of hyperscalers ballooned faster (+48% YoY), DSO drifted only ~3 days, and full-year operating cash flow ($3.67B) exceeded net income ($3.18B); the flag deserves monitoring if DSO pushes past ~45 days, but cash conversion currently *refutes* "earnings ahead of cash."**
