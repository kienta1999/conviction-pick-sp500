- **Top pick:** ADSK

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Autodesk is the one name in this field where the quality case and the price case point the same way. AutoCAD, Revit and BIM are the file-format and workflow standards of AEC — a customer cannot realistically in-source them or route around them without retraining a firm and breaking model exchange — and that lock-in shows up as a 92.5% gross margin, a 41% non-GAAP operating margin in Q2 FY27 and guided FCF of ~$2.7B. It is a serial beater (8/8 EPS beats, average surprise +6.37% over the last four quarters) growing revenue 16–19%, yet it trades at 18.3x forward earnings, down 12% year-to-date and ~19% below its 52-week high — priced like a disappointment, not a compounder. The market has already punished it once for a beat-and-raise (Q1 FY27, on the MaintainX deal), so the disappointment is at least partly in the price rather than ahead of it.
- **Key evidence from the record:** 8/8 EPS beats with an average surprise of +6.37% over the last four quarters; Q2 FY27 EPS $3.30 vs $3.12 (+5.8%) and revenue $2,046M vs $2,012M (+16%), with the FY27 guide raised at the same time; revenue has grown 16–19% in each of the last four quarters.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** Low. Forward P/E 18.3x is the cheapest software multiple in the field and ~20.6x on FY27 consensus EPS; the stock is −12.34% YTD and ~19% below its $320.00 52-week high, and the +12.8% 21-day run is a rebound off a selloff, not a chase into a high. Options imply only ±3.08%, smaller than the stock's own 4.9% average absolute reaction — the options market is pricing a smaller move than history. The trap is not valuation; it is that consensus EPS ($3.07) sits near the top of management's own Q3 guide ($3.04–3.09), so a print at the low end is a miss in all but name.
- **The guide:** The market is carrying Q4 FY27 consensus EPS of $3.18 on revenue of $2.21B, and full-year FY27 consensus EPS of $12.62 on revenue of $8.32B — against company guide of EPS $12.52–12.60 and revenue $8,295–8,345M. Street FY EPS sits $0.02 above the top of guide, so Q4 needs a small raise (or a Q3 beat large enough to carry it) to square the numbers. The run-rate supports the revenue side — H1 revenue was $3,980M and Q3 guide midpoint $2,132M plus Q4 consensus $2.21B lands exactly on the guided range — but the Q4 EPS step-up from the Q3 guide ($3.04–3.09) to $3.18 needs margin, and management has flagged transaction-model headwinds and multiyear-contract annualisation. "Beat but guided soft" is the loss case here: a Q3 beat with a Q4 guide that leaves FY27 EPS below $12.62 would confirm the street is ahead of management, and the stock would likely fall despite the beat.
- **Plan A or Plan B**, with one sentence of justification. Plan B — all six research briefs returned Plan B, the reaction record is conflicted (see below), and the Q4 guide, not the quarter, decides the move, so there is no research-backed case for holding through the print.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, I would hold ADSK for 12–18 months. This is the lens's core test and ADSK passes it: mid-teens revenue growth, ~39–41% non-GAAP operating margins, ~$2.7B guided FCF, buybacks ($448M in Q1 FY27 alone), and a format-standard moat that no AI-native generative-design startup replaces — the dossier's own gap-down-15% verdict is "opportunity". The disaster case is a failed MaintainX integration or a construction downturn, not obsolescence.
- **Event scenario:** Beat-and-raise (Q3 EPS ≥$3.09 with Q4/FY27 held at or above $12.62 and MaintainX tracking its $70M H2 billings marker): +5% to +8%. In-line (Q3 near $3.07, Q4 guide merely reaffirmed inside the existing range): −3% to +1%. Miss / soft guide (Q3 near $3.04, or Q4 guide implying FY27 EPS below $12.62, or a MaintainX shortfall): −6% to −9%, roughly the stock's average absolute reaction (4.9%) to its worst recent reaction (−8.6%).
- **Top risk:** The guide, compounded by MaintainX — Q1 FY27 already showed this market will sell a beat-and-raise over this deal, Q3 is MaintainX's first quarter of contribution, and segment transparency drops this print (Autodesk stops disclosing Design-and-Make revenue), so any shortfall against the $60M H2 revenue / $70M H2 billings markers, or worse margin dilution, gets punished.
