### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — 7/10
Chromatography, mass spec and spectroscopy instruments plus the CrossLab consumables/services annuity (42% of revenue, $786M in Q3) create a razor-and-blade installed base; validated pharma workflows make switching costly but not impossible — Thermo Fisher, Danaher/SCIEX, Waters and Shimadzu all compete credibly for the same bench. AI impact is indirect (AI drug discovery → more assays → more instruments; AI in diagnostics is a tailwind, not a threat). CDMO capacity (NASD/BIOVECTRA) is a real differentiator with reshoring demand. **If it gaps down 15% and I hold 18 months: a manageable hold, not a disaster** — the business is recovering, margins expanding, balance sheet sound; but at 26x forward for 6–7% growth there is no valuation cushion, so the "opportunity" case needs the FY27 guide to confirm the recovery, not just the Q4 print.

### 8. Risks specific to THIS print
The FY27 guide is the whole event — a first FY27 EPS guide below the $6.77 consensus, or core growth guided below ~6%, would re-rate the 26x multiple. Q4 itself: ATD flattish (flagged), China +9% may not repeat (flat was the expectation three months ago), diagnostics/clinical already tracking below guide, tariff refund drops out of the YoY comparison (Q4 EPS growth +8.2% is flattered by nothing — it must be earned). Small-cap biotech funding is improving but fragile; academic/government budgets and China stimulus timing remain variable (Danaher's Q4 guide disappointment is the cautionary peer tale). The +17% screen reaction for Q2 is unverified — do not model a big post-print drift on it.

### 9. Scores
- **Beat-likelihood 6/10** — consensus sits mid-guide, ESP +0.19%, estimates flat for 30 days; Q3's beat was partly one-off (tariff refund); the miss in Q1 FY26 shows this name can miss.
- **Guide-risk 7/10** — the first FY27 guide must clear a $6.77 consensus (+9.2% EPS growth) while Q4 core growth is guided to decelerate and ATD goes flat; high bar, high multiple.
- **Priced-in 7/10** — +57% in 6 months, ~5% off the 52-week high, 26x forward for single-digit core growth; the recovery is consensus now.
- **Verdict:** A decent quarter with a dangerous guide — the print is beatable, the FY27 guide is where the money is won or lost.

### 10. Plan A or Plan B?
**Plan B — wait-for-the-number.** For A to be true: Q4 at/above the top of guide ($1.74) *and* a first FY27 guide of EPS ≥$6.77 with core growth ≥6% and no ATD/China wobble. That combination is a post-print buy, not a pre-print hold — the guide risk is the entire thesis.
