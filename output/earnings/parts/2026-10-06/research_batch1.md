# EARNINGS-QUALITY FLAGS

- **GS: LOW_CASH_CONVERSION — benign banking-model artifact.** For banks, "operating cash flow" is dominated by changes in trading assets, securities financing, and customer deposits — balance-sheet churn that has nothing to do with earnings quality. GS's earnings are driven by realized/accrued trading revenue, net interest income, and fee income; cash conversion as defined for an industrial company is meaningless here. One-line verdict: **not a red flag — this is how bank accounting works; judge GS on ROE, credit quality, and capital return instead.**
