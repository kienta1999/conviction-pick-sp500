- **Runner-up:** VEEV
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** ADSK is the one name where the market has already charged for the bad news instead of for the good news. The trap elsewhere in this field is the opposite — A, NTAP, VEEV and CTAS all need a fourth or fifth consecutive raise just to stand still.
- **Key evidence from the record:** 12/12 EPS beats and 12/12 revenue beats, with Q2 FY27 EPS of \$3.30 versus \$3.12 consensus (+5.8%), non-GAAP operating margin up to 41%, and 97% recurring revenue with net revenue retention at the top of the 100–110% range.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** Low. Down 23.6% from the high, down 17.4% year-to-date, forward P/E 17.2x, and consensus targets imply +17–27% upside — the least priced-in name in the field on price. The offset is that options imply ±9.2% against a realised ~4.5–4.9%, roughly double, and ADSK has exceeded implied in 11 of its last 17 reports, so the move itself is not cheap even if the level is.
- **The guide:** The market is carrying Q4 FY27 consensus EPS of \$3.18 on revenue of \$2.21B, inside the implied FY27 guide range (FY27 EPS \$12.52–12.60, revenue \$8.295–8.345B), while FY27 EPS consensus of \$12.62 already sits above management's guide top of \$12.60. The run-rate supports clearing that bar — but only barely: Q2 EPS beat by 5.8% and margins are expanding hard, yet billings decelerated to +10% from +18% in Q1 and RPO grew only +2% (current RPO +12%), so the demand engine is cooling even as the P&L looks strong. A small raise to just above \$12.60 is supported; a large raise is not. The loss case is explicit: beat but guided soft — a Q3 beat with a Q4 guide below \$3.18 / \$2.21B, or FY27 held at \$12.52–12.60, reads as a consensus cut and the stock falls despite the beat, exactly as it did after Q2 FY27 and Q1 FY27.
- **Plan A or Plan B**, with one sentence of justification. **Plan B** — the last two beats fell on guide/deal news, so the burden of proof for holding through the print is not met and the trade is to enter only on a confirmed beat-and-raise with a positive first session.

- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise +4–6% / in-line −3–4% / miss or soft guide −6–9%
- **Top risk:** Billings and RPO deceleration (+10% billings, +2% RPO) plus the MaintainX overhang — a \$3.6B all-cash deal that moved Autodesk from ~\$677m net cash to ~\$2.9B estimated net debt, revenue-accretive but earnings- and cash-dilutive — turning a good quarter into a soft guide.
