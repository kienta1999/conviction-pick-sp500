### CONTRADICTED (with corrected figures)

- VEEV Q2 FY27 reaction: dossier +16.4% (\$244.91 → \$285.05); correct per Finnhub daily candle is +15.2% (\$244.91 → \$282.13). The \$285.05 close appears only in Weiss Ratings; Finnhub, closelook and growin.ai agree on \$282.13.
- ADSK Q1 FY27 reaction: dossier −2.4%; correct close-to-close is −4.0% (\$240.95 → \$231.31, May 28 → May 29). The print was worse than the dossier states.
- A Q2 FY26 reaction: dossier +17.6%; correct close-to-close is +16.9% (\$115.84 → \$135.38, May 27 → May 28). The dossier's figure uses the May 26 close as its base.

### UNVERIFIED / PARTIALLY CONFIRMED

- VEEV Q4 FY27 consensus EPS \$2.31: the figure exists (InsideArbitrage, \$2.31) but Zacks — the dossier's own cited source — shows \$2.29 / \$940.75M revenue. Consequence: on Zacks, consensus equals the implied Q4 guide rather than sitting above it, so "merely honouring the FY outlook prints a touch below consensus" becomes "honouring the FY outlook merely meets consensus." The Plan A bar itself (Q4 guide at/above \$2.31 EPS / ~\$940M revenue, i.e. a fifth consecutive raise) is unchanged as a hurdle.
- Agilent Q4 consensus EPS: Zacks \$1.72 vs MarketBeat \$1.73 — a \$0.01 provider drift since the dossier's read, noted, not a contradiction.
- Agilent CrossLab "recurring and sticky": qualitative; the steady \$758–786M quarterly run-rate is consistent but does not prove stickiness.
