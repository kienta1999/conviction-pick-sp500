H1 cc EPS +3% vs a +4–6% guide is not a pessimism story, it is an arithmetic story; the 2025 record was guide-in-February, walk-back-all-year; and the three downgrades in five sessions were estimate-driven, not technical. "Cheap vs its own history" has been true all the way down from $171. Plan B exists precisely because this bear case is live, not decorative.

Priced-in risk: LOW — decade-low multiple, Hold consensus, 7-down/0-up FY26 revisions in 30 days, no run-up. Rough moves: beat-and-clean-guide +4/+6%; beat-but-qualified −2/−4%; miss/trim −5/−9%. **Plan B** — gate entry on the guide line; the downside is the guide, and the guide is only knowable after the print.

### 2. GS — de-rated beat machine facing toughest comp
Most compelling data point: 4/4 EPS beats with a **+20.5% average surprise** and beat→up 87.5%, yet the stock sits **−21.8% off its high at 12.3–12.4× forward P/E** — the record Q2'26 quarter (best in the firm's 157-year history) has been fully de-rated out of the price. Reaction record: last four +9%, +2% (day-one; drifted −3.7% day two on FICC miss), +5%, −2%; avg absolute 3.51%. Guide risk: MEDIUM — no numerical guide to cut, but Solomon's Sept 16 pre-softening ("softer" Q3 FICC, stock −4% that day) is a soft guide-down, the Q3'25→Q3'26 comp is the toughest of the cycle, and the Q1'26 pattern shows the market punishes an FICC miss even when equities prints records. Priced-in risk: LOW-MEDIUM — de-rated multiple and split analyst camp (16 of 26 at Hold), but the peak-trading question is real. Rough moves: clean beat +4/+9%; in-line/FICC-soft −2/+2%; miss with expense creep −4/−8%. **Plan B** — the beat base rate is the best in the field, but the comp and the FICC warn make pre-print conviction an extrapolation of Q2's unrepeatable peak.

Most compelling data point: it has beaten EPS on all four prints and the stock **fell on three of them** — the reaction function is set by the expense/NII guide, not the headline, and the $107.5B expense guide has disappointed three quarters running. Reaction record: last four +2%, −2%, −4%, −2% (avg +0.55%); avg absolute 2.68%, though recent prints spanned −4% to +2.5%. Guide risk: HIGH — NII was *cut* as recently as April (FY'26 NII now $105.5B), Petno has already said Q3 markets will be below Q2's record $12.1B, and the run-rate supports the $24.3 FY EPS bar only arithmetically, not with momentum. Rough moves: beat with guide held 0/−3% (the standard beat-and-fade); genuine raise +2/+4%; expense nudge or card-credit wobble −4/−7%. **Plan B** — this is the name where consensus is most wrong: the market models a compounder while the tape models a guide disappointment.
