# Panelist C — Quality / Moat & Irreplaceability — Earnings 2026-10-24

Lens rule: margins, returns on capital, balance sheet, and above all irreplaceability. Reject anything customers can in-source or a substitute — including AI-native competition — can route around. The deciding question for every name: would I be happy to be stuck holding this for 12–18 months after a bad print?

## (1) SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** HWM
- **Runner-up:** GOOGL
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Howmet is the only name here that pairs a genuine sole-source moat with a de-rated price: qualified turbine-airfoil and engine content on LEAP/GEnx-class platforms takes a decade to replicate, so no customer in-sources it and no AI-native entrant routes around a certified casting. The print should go well because the trend is the highest-quality in the field — revenue +19% YoY in Q1 to +24% in Q2, adjusted EBITDA margin expanding 28.7% → 32.0% → 32.1%, three consecutive FY guide settings raised in 2026, and GE Aerospace's Oct 20 beat-and-raise (orders +49%, commercial services backlog >$170B) confirms the demand underneath it. That quality is not in the price: the stock is −35.4% in three months, ~40% below its $310 high, RSI 19.5 — the market has already charged it for the CPP/SpaceX casting headlines, which are a next-decade story, not this order book. A quality compounder sold on a headline overhang, into a print its end-market just validated, is exactly the fallback-weighted trade.
- **Key evidence from the record:** Moat scored 9/10 in the dossier — qualified, sole-source content on LEAP/GEnx-class engines and turbine airfoils — alongside two straight FY raises (Q1 +$550M revenue baseline, Q2 +$400M revenue / +$0.33 EPS) and EBITDA margin at 32.1%.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** medium — the de-rating is real (priced-in scored 4/10, fwd P/E 29.7 is down sharply from the highs), but the trap is that consensus EPS $1.36–1.37 already sits at/above the top of management's own $1.34–1.36 Q3 guide, so a clean "meet" prints as a miss; the multiple is still premium in absolute terms, so there is no cheapness cushion if the guide merely reaffirms.
- **The guide:** Q4 2026 consensus EPS is $1.42 and FY26 consensus is $5.33–5.34, already above the $5.23–5.31 guide top — the market is carrying the next raise before it happens. The run-rate supports the revenue line (H1 $4.86B + Q3 guide ~$2.575B implies a normal seasonal Q4 of ~$2.57–2.67B against the $10.0–10.1B FY guide — continuation, not acceleration), but it does not support the EPS pre-spend: supportable on volume, heroic only in that the raise is assumed, which is why this is a quality hold, not a guide gamble.
- **Plan B**, with one sentence of justification — wait for the number, because consensus has already spent the raise and the dossier's own condition for Plan A (guide top still being the consensus, or a pre-print dismissal of the casting overhang) is not met; this is the closest name in the field to flipping to A, and a post-print entry into a 9/10 moat at a de-rated price is the better quality trade.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes — hold 12–18 months, happily. A −15% gap here is, per the dossier, an opportunity: 18 months of aftermarket and gas-turbine demand are contracted by fleet economics, not sentiment, the balance sheet is manageable (net debt/EBITDA ~1.47x on the screen), and the franchise damage case requires certified sole-source content to be displaced — a decade-long process that cannot start with one quarter.
- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise +6% to +10% / in-line (meet guide top, reaffirm) −3% to −6% / miss or any language validating the SpaceX/CPP threat −12% to −16% (options imply ±9.5%, above its 5.9% realized proxy, so the tails are honestly priced).
- **Top risk:** Management language on the call validating vertical integration — GE's ~$12B CPP acquisition or SpaceX casting turbine blades in-house — especially on gas-turbine pricing power (GT was +38% in Q2); that converts a headline overhang into a moat question, the one thing this lens cannot forgive.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
