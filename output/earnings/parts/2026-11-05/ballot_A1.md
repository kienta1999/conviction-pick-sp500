## SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** PH
- **Runner-up:** RL
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** PH is the only name in the field where the beat record, the revenue acceleration, and the price action all point the same way. It has an 8-of-8 beat streak (42 straight per SureDividend), its largest recent surprise was +11.6% in Q4 FY26, and organic growth has accelerated 5.0% → 6.6% → 6.5% → 8.0% with a record $12.8B backlog (+16% YoY) behind it. The bar has not been chased upward — consensus EPS revisions are only +0.55% in 30 days — and the stock has fallen into the print, −6.41% in a month and ~−14.9% since the Aug 6 close, 17.2% below its 52-week high. What is not in the price is the one line that matters: a Filtration-inclusive FY27 guide update, since street consensus of $35.72 already sits above the ex-Filtration guide top of $35.25.
- **Key evidence from the record:** The single most compelling data point is the combination of the Q4 FY26 print (+11.6% EPS surprise, $9.27 vs $8.31, with revenue also beating) and the accelerating organic growth into it (8.0% in Q4, Aerospace organic +13.4% with a 29.8% margin) — a beat that was driven by volume and backlog, not by a lowered bar, since revisions are flat.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** Low on price, high on the guide bar — net medium. On price it is the least crowded name in the field (priced-in 4/10, down ~15% in three months, options-implied move ~4.4% equal to the realised average of 4.4%). The trap is not the share price, it is the guide: consensus $35.72 is already above management's own ex-Filtration guide top ($35.25), and consensus for today's quarter ($8.21–8.23) sits ~2% above management's own Q1 guide of $8.07. A mere reaffirmation of ex-Filtration guidance reads as a cut, so a clean EPS beat can still fall — exactly what happened in Q3 FY26, a +4.2% beat that fell −3.0% on the day and −6.9% the day after.
- **The guide:** The market is carrying FY27 consensus EPS of $35.72 and Q2 FY27 consensus of $8.55 on $6.08B revenue. The Q1 run-rate supports the quarter (organic ~8% is a continuation), but it does NOT support FY27 $35.72 without the Filtration update — Filtration Group closed Aug 13 with ~$1.8B of FY27 sales, and that is the number the print must supply. "Beat but guided soft" is the loss case here, stated explicitly: if PH beats Q1 and only reaffirms the ex-Filtration $34.25–$35.25 range, the thesis fails on the print even though the quarter itself was good. Note also that Q4 GAAP EPS included a $0.65 tariff-refund benefit that does not repeat.
- **Plan A or Plan B:** Plan A (conditional) — hold through the print, accepting the binary, because the beat probability is the highest in the field, the bar has not risen, and the stock has already de-rated into the event; the condition is that the trade is explicitly a bet on the Filtration-inclusive guide line, not on the Q1 EPS number alone.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, I would hold it 12–18 months. The moat is 8/10 — FAA/EASA-certified aerospace parts, filtration/materials platforms, aftermarket, and a $12.8B backlog that cannot be re-sourced in a quarter — and the dossier's own verdict is that a 15% gap-down held 18 months is an opportunity. A guide disappointment is a timing/communication failure, not a broken backlog.
- **Event scenario:** Beat-and-raise (Filtration-inclusive FY27 guide above $35.72): +6% to +8% (Q4 FY26 did +7.3% on a beat plus guide initiation). In-line (beat, reaffirm ex-Filtration guidance): −3% to −5% (the Q3 FY26 beat-and-fell template). Miss, or a guide trim: −6% to −9%.
- **Top risk:** The Filtration-inclusive FY27 guide comes in at or below the $35.72 street number — a beat that is priced as a guide cut.
