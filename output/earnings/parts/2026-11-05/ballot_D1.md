## Why not NO TRADE

I considered it seriously: every name in this field carries a genuine guide-down trap (PH reaffirm = cut, RL reaffirm = cut after three raises, MNST's 55% margin hurdle, COP's Q4 arithmetic, OXY's pre-announced Q3, VYLR's litigation). But NO TRADE would pretend the six traps are equal, and they are not: PH is the only name where the price has already paid for the trap (priced-in 4/10, down ~15% in three months) while the other five are priced as if the trap does not exist. The skeptic's job is to buy the trap the market has already discounted, not to abstain from all six.
