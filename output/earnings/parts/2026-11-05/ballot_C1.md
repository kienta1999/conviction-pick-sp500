- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** Low — dossier priced-in score 4/10 (high is bad). PH has fallen ~14.9% since Aug 6, −6.41% in 1 month, and the options-implied move of ~4.4% equals the realised average absolute move of 4.4%; the stock is falling into the print, not crowded at the highs, unlike RL (+13.4% in 1 month) and MNST (+12.0% in 1 month).

"Beat but guided soft" is the explicit loss case here: a Q1 EPS beat paired with a mere reaffirmation of the ex-Filtration FY27 guide would read as a cut, since consensus already sits ~2% above management's own Q1 guide of $8.07 and above the FY27 guide top.
- **Plan A or Plan B**, with one sentence of justification. Plan A (conditional) — hold through the print, because the beat is highly likely, the priced-in risk is low, and the moat makes a bad print survivable, but only accepting that the trade is a binary on the Filtration-inclusive FY27 guide.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, I would hold PH 12–18 months. Its moat is certification-based, not sentiment-based: flight-qualified suppliers cannot be swapped or in-sourced without multi-year re-certification, the $12.8B backlog (aerospace $8.5B, +15%) and aerospace aftermarket give multi-year visibility, and the dossier itself calls a 15% gap-down held 18 months an opportunity.

- **Top risk:** The guide — no Filtration-inclusive FY27 raise, or a reaffirmation that the market reads as a cut against a consensus already above the guide top.
