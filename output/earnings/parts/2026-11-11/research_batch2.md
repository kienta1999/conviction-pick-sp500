## 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — **8/10**
AutoCAD / Revit / Fusion are the de facto file-format and workflow standards in architecture, engineering, construction and manufacturing — switching costs are extreme (decades of DWG files, trained staff, plugin ecosystems, regulatory submission formats), and the subscription/cloud transition has locked in recurring revenue (subscription revenue $1.952B in Q2, +17%). Construction is growing >20% (Q2 call), and Forma won one of the world's largest retailers for portfolio-wide planning. AI threat: AI-native design tools (generative CAD, text-to-3D) are the credible long-term substitute, and Autodesk's answer is to embed AI in its own platform (Forma, AI-assisted design) — it is *absorbing* the threat rather than being routed around, but a genuinely AI-native competitor that makes AutoCAD's UI obsolete is the tail risk. Certification/regulatory lock-in (building codes, BIM mandates) and network effects (everyone in AEC speaks DWG/Revit) are the durable protections. **If it gaps down 15% and I hold 18 months:** an opportunity, not a disaster — the franchise is intact, the balance sheet is strong ($4.16B cash), buybacks are running ($453M in Q2), and a 15% gap on a guide disappointment has historically been a buying window for this name (it recovered +9% in 48h after the Feb 2026 print). The risk is a *structural* guide cut (FY28 transaction-model headwind), not a single-quarter miss.
