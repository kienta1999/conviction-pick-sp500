## 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10
- Largest US auto-parts retailer, 8,031 stores, $20.3bn sales, dominant DIY + commercial (DIFM) distribution: 6,443 commercial programs, hub-and-spoke parts availability, and a store network that is itself the moat — a customer with a broken car needs the part *today, nearby*, which e-commerce cannot match for most SKUs. AI threat is low: AI does not change a brake pad, and AI-native competitors cannot route around a physical parts network; if anything AI diagnostics *increase* parts identification. Customers cannot in-source it (DIY *is* the customer doing it themselves; DIFM shops depend on AZO's availability). **If it gaps down 15% and I hold 18 months: opportunity, not disaster** — the business is a slow compounder (10-yr price CAGR ~16% per FinanceCharts) with buyback leverage, and a 15% gap on a no-guide name is usually a sentiment overreaction unless DIY has structurally broken.
