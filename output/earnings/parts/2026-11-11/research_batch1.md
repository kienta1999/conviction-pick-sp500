That is exactly the value-trap signature to watch, even with a benign stated cause.
- **One-line verdict per flag — INVENTORY_BUILD: PLAUSIBLY BENIGN, NOT CLEARED — strategic NAND pre-buy per the 10-Q, corroborated by +47% all-flash demand and +36% billings, but Q1 FCF fell 35% while EPS rose 66%, so Dec 1 must show inventory turns stabilizing and FCF recovering, or the flag becomes a real earnings-quality problem.**
