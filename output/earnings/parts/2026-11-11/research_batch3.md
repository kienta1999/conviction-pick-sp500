## 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10

Cintas is the largest US uniform-rental and facility-services provider: route density (a truck on a route is a local monopoly), multi-year service contracts, and deep embedding in customers' daily operations (uniforms, restroom supplies, first aid, fire protection, safety training). Switching costs are real — changing a uniform supplier disrupts every employee's workwear and the compliance/paperwork around it — and the First Aid/Fire cross-sell deepens the relationship. Scale advantages in garment processing plants and route logistics are capital-intensive to replicate. **AI angle:** AI cannot launder, deliver, or fit a uniform, inspect a fire extinguisher, or restock a first-aid cabinet — the core service is physical and route-based. AI helps Cintas (route optimization, inventory) more than it threatens it. The genuine disruptor would be a customer in-sourcing laundry — uneconomic at scale — or a low-cost regional route operator, which is the existing competitive fringe, not a new technology. If it gaps down 15% and I'm stuck for 18 months: **opportunity, not disaster** — the business compounds at ~9% organic growth with record margins and a 40-year dividend-growth culture; the 18-month risk is multiple compression (36x → 28x would be a ~25% drawdown on its own) and the UniFirst binary, not the business model.
