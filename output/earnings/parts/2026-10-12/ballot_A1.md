## PART 1 — SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** BNY
- **Runner-up:** BAC

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** BNY is the only name in the 13 with both a four-quarter EPS beat streak *and* an explicitly raised forward guide, so it offers the cleanest "beat-and-raise" two-step. Revenue has compounded at ~13% y/y for four straight quarters with ~800+ bps of positive operating leverage (Q1 pre-tax margin +833 bps y/y), and the stock has already corrected 13.4% off its highs while revisions stayed stable — the market is not priced for the flywheel to repeat a fifth time. Management already raised 2026 guidance on Jul 15 (revenue 10–11% growth, NII +12–13%), so the Q3 consensus ($2.27 EPS / $5.56B rev) is calibrated to the guide, not above it. NII and fee assets keep compounding under a Fed that just hiked (NII tailwind), giving a credible path to a raise-again print.

- **Key evidence from the record:** 4/4 EPS beats with surprises ranging +5.6% to +16.0% (avg +10.1%, beat_up 0.875); revenue ~$5.0B → $5.2B → $5.41B → $5.70B (four straight ~13% y/y quarters); raised full-year guide Jul 15 and the stock responded +5.08% — the market believes the guide. Last-four reactions: +5.1%, +2.2%, +1.9%, −2.0%.

- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** **medium**. −13.4% off the 52-week high, trading below the 50-day MA and below analyst target (~$157–162), fwd P/E 13.9 — the September run-up already corrected, so perfection is not priced. The offset: the raised guide means a mere in-line print excites nobody, and the Q3'25 precedent (beat +6.8% EPS, stock still fell 2%) proves BNY is not immune to sell-the-news. Implied earnings move not found; recent realized reactions averaged ~2.8% abs.

- **The guide:** FY'26 revenue guidance **$22.0–22.2B** (above consensus at the time of the raise), NII +12–13%, expense growth 6–7%, ~400 bps positive operating leverage. H1 revenue was $11.11B, so the guide implies H2 ≈ $10.9–11.1B (~$5.45–5.55B/quarter) — the Q3 consensus of $5.56B sits right at the top of the guided run-rate: achievable, with a modest cushion for a beat. Implied Q4 EPS ≈ $2.3, flat vs Q3 — the run-rate comfortably supports the forward number, and compounding NII supports another nudge up.

- **Plan B — wait for the number.** The earnings-momentum case is strong, but the whole regime is sell-the-peak (MS faded −4.4% on a record Q2; BNY itself fell on a Q3'25 beat). There is no pre-print catalyst that de-risks the Q3'25-style reaction, so the burden of proof for holding through is not met.

- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, hold 12–18 months. World's largest custodian with $59.4T AUC/A; custody is infrastructure that institutions cannot switch cheaply; Irreplaceability 9/10. A 15% gap down on a custody franchise compounding AUC/A is multiple compression, not franchise damage — 18 months later the fee base is larger.

- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise ≈ **+4% to +6%**; in-line ≈ **flat to +1.5%** (raised guide already, no upside hook); miss ≈ **−4% to −6%**.

- **Top risk:** Consensus revenue $5.56B sits at the very top of the guided run-rate — a quarter that merely lands in-guide with no further raise reads as a stall and gets sold like Q3'25, despite being "good."

- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
