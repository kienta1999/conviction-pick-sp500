## PART 1 — SINGLE-PICK BALLOT

- **Top pick:** BAC
- **Runner-up:** MS

- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** The bank tape is selling beats — MS faded −4.4% the session after a record Q2 with an +18% EPS beat — which means bank strength is being priced as an exit, not a hold. BAC is the cheapest admission ticket to that mis-pricing: its EPS bar was CUT 6.7% in 30 days ($1.19→$1.11) even as Q2 printed +34% y/y EPS, and the market has already baked in IB income −15.3% y/y. Meanwhile big-bank markets revenue is still forecast +17% y/y, and BAC is a serial NII-guide raiser with clean credit — so the downside the tape fears is largely in the number. At 10.4x fwd P/E and −15.8% off the high, a beat doesn't need to be a record to reprice.

- **Key evidence from the record:** 4/4 beats, Q2 EPS +34% y/y (the strongest acceleration in the bank group), and a consensus bar walked DOWN 6.7% into the print — the only name in the field where expectations were lowered while the underlying is accelerating.

- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP):** LOW. −15.8% off the 52w high, fwd P/E 10.4 (lowest of the four banks), reaction record is muted (+2/+2/−4/+4, avgabs 2.5%), and the bar was just cut. Nobody is crowded in front of this print.

- **The guide:** The market carries FY2026 at ~$4.61 EPS (MarketBeat consensus via americanbankingnews.com, early Oct 2026; Erste Group's Sep 18 note had it drifting $4.67→$4.64 — direction is DOWN, which helps the print). The only current Q4'26 point estimate I found is Zacks' $1.20 from Nov 2025 — stale, flag it. Run-rate check: Q2 printed $1.21 actual, Q3 bar ~$1.11 → carrying Q4 at ~$1.15–1.20 is consistent with FY ~$4.61; the run-rate supports it. **The guide-down case I own:** BAC's real risk is not a cut — it's a NON-raise. Management has serially raised the NII guide; if they hold it flat, the raise-streak premium compresses and this sell-the-peak tape takes −4 to −6% off even on a headline beat. A flat NII guide is the miss nobody is modelling.

- **Plan B** — even good bank prints have been faded this season (MS −4.4% on a +18% EPS beat); wait for the reaction, then buy the sell-the-news if the guide is intact.

- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes, I'd hold 12–18 months. This is a deposit-franchise thesis, not a print thesis: clean credit, disciplined expenses, 10.4x fwd P/E, and NII is a beneficiary of the Fed's Sep 16 hike to 3.75–4.00% — higher-for-longer is a bank tailwind here, not a headwind. The print is a timing catalyst; the business compounds either way.

- **Event scenario:** beat-and-raise +3 to +5% (this tape caps bank upside); in-line with intact guide ±2%; miss with flat NII guide −5 to −8%.

- **Top risk:** Capital-markets lines print worse than the −15.3% y/y IB income already baked in, AND the NII guide comes flat — a double disappointment turns a cheap multiple into a one-quarter value trap.

- **Conviction (1-10):** 6

Reaction record is muted (+2/+2/−4/+4, avgabs 2.5%), which cuts both ways: even a good print won't run, but a miss isn't a cliff. Guide risk is the non-raise case (serial NII-guide raiser holding flat); priced-in risk is the lowest in the field except LVS (10.4x fwd, −15.8% off high). Moves: beat-and-raise +3–5%, in-line ±2%, miss −5–8%. **Plan B** — let the tape react, then buy a sell-the-news if the guide holds.

**#2 MS (deep dive):** The setup reads as de-risked — bar cut 7% in 30 days ($3.16→$2.94), −11% in a month, −16.4% off the high, 4/4 beats averaging +19.1% surprise. But the reaction record contains the season's warning: record Q2 (+18% EPS beat) was faded −4.4% next session, so the tape sells bank strength on sight. Guide-risk scores 6 — the forward items (expense outlook, NII sensitivity) matter more than the headline. Priced-in risk is moderate-low (4), but the Q4'26 consensus of $2.53 is an average of only 2 estimates (MarketBeat) — thin support, so the forward number is fragile. Moves: beat +4–6%, in-line −3–+2%, miss −6–9%. **Plan B**.

**#3 CME (deep dive):** The cleanest mechanical setup in the field — perfect beat record (beat_up 1.000, 4/4), Q3 ADV already disclosed (+16%, Sept record +22%) so ~70% of the quarter is pre-visible, and crucially NO forward guide to disappoint — the guide-down case simply doesn't exist here. But the edge is thin: avg surprise is only +1.4% (meets-don't-smash), the September record is public, and the reaction record (+5/+0/+1/+0) shows even records get sold when expectations max. Priced-in risk is moderate (5): −15% off highs helps, Hold consensus means nobody is betting on it. Moves: beat +2–4%, in-line ±1.5%, miss −3–5%. **Plan B**.
