- **Top pick:** MS
- **Runner-up:** BLK
- **Thesis (3-5 sentences):** Morgan Stanley's Wealth Management franchise — record $8.9B revenue, record $148B quarterly net new assets, ~$2T IM AUM milestone — is the most AI-resilient recurring revenue on Wall Street: fiduciary, relationship-locked, and self-compounding with markets. - **Key evidence from the record:** Four straight EPS beats — +33% (Q3'25), +10.4% (Q4'25), +13.5% (Q1'26), +18–20% (Q2'26) — on accelerating revenue ($18.22B → $17.89B → $20.58B → $21.35B, +27.1% y/y in Q2), with Q2 ROTCE at 26.6% and a record WM quarter on both revenue and NNA.
- **Priced-in risk (THE TRAP): MEDIUM.** Price $187.44 sits −19.3% below the $232.25 high and below both the 50-day ($208.40) and 200-day ($202.66); positioning is light, not crowded. But the trap is Q2's precedent: a monster beat (+18–20% EPS, record everything) moved only +0.39% — "great quarter, no reaction" is the template if Q3 is merely good, and the options-implied ±4.85% sits above the name's own 3.85% average realized move, so the event move is priced for a real one.

H1 ran at $3.445/qtr — the Q4 bar sits ~13% below the run-rate, supportable even with normal seasonality. "Beat but guided soft" as a literal loss case does not exist here; the real loss case is commentary — late-quarter deal-flow slowdown (Reuters 10/8) or weak NNA quality color on the call.
- **Plan A or Plan B, with one sentence of justification:** Plan A — hold through the print, because the burden of proof is met: a de-rated price on downgrades-not-numbers, an un-chased bar, and the strongest fallback franchise in the field make the skew favorable to staying.
- **Fallback if the print disappoints:** Yes — happy to hold 12–18 months. The WM annuity (~40%+ of the revenue mix) is sticky, fee-based, and compounding; ROTCE 26.6%, a $1.15 dividend and a $20B buyback pay you to wait; and the dossier frames it plainly: a 15% gap-down on a soft capital-markets quarter is an opportunity, not a disaster, because the WM compounding engine does not break on one quarter's trading revenue.
- **Event scenario:** Beat-and-clean (WM holds, deal commentary intact): **+4 to +6%** (prior three non-Q2 prints gained 4.5–5.8%); in-line: **−1 to +2%** (Q2 template); miss or IS/NNA commentary weak: **−5 to −8%**, with the WM floor limiting the downside vs a pure trading bank.
- **Top risk:** Q2 was peak everything (record IS, record WM, record NNA) so sequential comps are hostile; >50% of the record $148B NNA came from workplace/IPO late-stage-private inflows (partially non-recurring); unconfirmed UBS combination rumors are an execution overhang; and the late-quarter multidecade-high yield spike hits the IS line harder than WM.
- **Conviction (1-10):** 7
