### 7. Moat / AI-irreplaceability — score 8/10

Auto parts retail is a logistics and availability moat: 8,031 stores, Mega Hubs, same-day commercial delivery to repair shops, and a parts-catalogue depth that online-only sellers cannot match for a mechanic who needs the part now.
