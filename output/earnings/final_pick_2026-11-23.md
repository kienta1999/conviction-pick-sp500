## Thesis

Veeva is the cleanest beat-and-raise profile in the field: eight straight EPS beats, revenue growth accelerating into the print (16% → 16% → 16.3% → 17.6%), and the guide raised four prints running. The tape has de-risked rather than chased it: down 17.5% in 21 sessions and 18.3% below its 52-week high, at a forward P/E of 25.9x on FY28 EPS versus a trailing 43.6x against a 5-year average of 52.1x, so the beat is not being paid for in advance. The panel was unanimous — all four lenses voted VEEV their top pick (Borda 40, the maximum possible), the first unanimous earnings vote of this run. One correction from Phase 3.5 verification changes the shape of the trade without changing the pick: Q4 FY27 consensus is **\$2.29 EPS / \$940.75M revenue (Zacks)**, not the \$2.35 / ~\$963M the dossier carried — the consensus sits at the guide-implied remainder, so the "mandatory raise" gap is ~\$0.01, not ~\$0.07. A guide that merely honours the remainder now *meets* consensus rather than missing it, which makes the raise — not the beat — even more clearly the whole trade. The risk is the guide bar, not the quarter: only a fifth consecutive raise clears it with margin, and a fifth raise is exactly what this management team has delivered four times running.
